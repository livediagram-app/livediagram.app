The **Inbox** sits under [Home](/help/explorer/timeline/) in the Explorer sidebar's Overview group, just above the [Timeline](/help/explorer/timeline/#the-timeline). Where the Timeline tells you what _happened_, the Inbox tells you what is still _open_: the [actions](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/) waiting on you, the ones you handed to someone else, and the [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) threads nobody has resolved yet.

## What lands here

Everything on the page comes from one of your documents, so the row always knows where it is from.

- **Assigned to You:** every open action whose assignee is you, on any document you can open. An action you assigned to yourself (the **Myself** row in the assign dialog) lives here too, so the page doubles as your to-do list across documents.
- **You Assigned:** open actions you assigned to somebody else. They are here so you can see what you are waiting _on_, not just what is waiting on you.
- **Open Comment Threads:** unresolved threads you are in. That means threads you wrote a comment in, threads where a teammate [mentioned you](/help/collaboration/comments/#mentioning-a-teammate), and threads on documents you own. A thread you were mentioned in carries a **Mentioned You** hint, and a row on your own document that you have not replied to yet carries a small **Your document** hint, so you can tell them apart.

The sections appear in that order, each with a count beside its heading, and a section with nothing in it is left out. When all three are empty the page says **Nothing waiting on you**.

Done actions and resolved threads are not listed. The Inbox is a list of what is outstanding, not a history; the [Timeline](/help/explorer/timeline/#the-timeline) and each document's own **Collaborate** panel keep the past.

## Reading a row

A row leads with a glyph for its kind (the action clipboard or the comment bubble), then the action's name or, for a thread, the element it hangs off. Under that is the action's description or the latest comment. The last line says where the row is from: a chip with the document's name (a team document's chip carries the team glyph), then the element and the tab.

On the right is the person: the assignee for an action (brand-tinted and labelled **You** when that is you), or the latest commenter for a thread, with how long ago the most recent change was beneath. Hovering the avatar names them (a **You Assigned** row reads "Assigned to" and the name) and, for a thread, says how many comments it holds.

Rows are ordered by their most recent change, newest first, within each section. Each kind of row is capped at 100, far more than anyone scrolls through.

## Opening a row

Click a row and the document opens on the right tab with the element selected, scrolled into view, and its action or comment popover already open, the same place a **Collaborate** panel row takes you inside the editor. From there you complete the action, reassign it, resolve the thread, or reply, with the editor's usual rules about who can edit what.

If the element has been deleted since the page loaded, the document still opens on that tab; there is simply nothing left to select.

## The badge

The Inbox row in the sidebar shows a count of the open actions **assigned to you**. It counts only work waiting on you, not work you handed out and not comment threads, and it shows **0** when nothing is waiting, so you can tell from any other Explorer section whether something needs you, or that you are all done.

## Guests and signing in

The Inbox works without an account, like the rest of livedocuments. Signed out, you can still assign actions to yourself and comment on your own documents, and both show up here.

**Your Inbox follows you when you sign up.** Actions you gave yourself and threads you wrote in as a guest still read as yours after you create an account, alongside the documents that move with you.

## Documents saved only in this browser

Documents saved only in This Browser (Offline Mode) show up here too: their open actions and comment threads are listed with everything else, and counted in the badge. Each of their rows carries a **This Browser** pill, because the document is kept in this browser and nowhere else. Nobody else sees those rows, and another browser or device won't list them.

<Tip>
  Clearing this browser's site data deletes a document in This Browser, and its rows go with it.
  Sync the document to keep it on livedocuments and see its actions from anywhere.
</Tip>
