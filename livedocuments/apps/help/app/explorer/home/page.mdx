**Home** is the page the [Explorer](/explorer) opens on, and the first row of its sidebar, above the [Inbox](/help/explorer/activity/). It answers the question you bring back with you, on one screen: **what was I working on?**

Home opens with a greeting across the top of the page. Under it, two pills say how many documents you have across My Work and your teams and whether anything waits in your **Inbox**; click one to go there. The search field sits right there in the greeting, with **New Document** inside it, so the first screen always offers a way to find something or start something. Beside it on a wide screen, and under it on a smaller one, tiles for an **Article**, a **Slide Deck**, an **Infographic** and a **Poster** each start a new document from that template. With **All Apps** chosen, **Start a Project** beside them makes a whole project across the apps in one press (see [Starting a Project From a Kit](/help/explorer/start-a-project/)).

Prefer a plainer Home? Press the **×** (**Hide Home Banner**) beside **Help** in the greeting's corner to hide it. Home then opens with its title and the search field in the header, like every other page, and stays that way on your other devices. Turn it back on in **Settings > Explorer** with **Show Home Banner**.

Rather skip the homepage? Turn on **Automatically Open Explorer** in **Settings > Explorer**, and opening livedocuments.app while you're signed in comes straight here.

Your first visit looks different. With nothing made yet, Home welcomes you and offers ways to start instead: the search field looks through the templates, the **Start Blank** button makes an empty document at once, and below it sit the blanks (an Illustration, a Slide Deck, an Article, an Infographic and Flashcards) under **Start Blank**, and the **Popular Templates**, each one click from a new document, with **Browse All Templates**. As a guest, **Already Have Work?** offers **Sign In**, so if you have used livedocuments before your documents come back. Once you have a document, Home shows your work instead.

Below the greeting, Home is one column of sections, each under its own heading: **New**, **Most Used**, **Recent**, your **Spaces**, then **Shared With You Recently**. A section with nothing in it is left out.

## New

**New** shows the four documents made most recently in My Work and your teams, newest first, opened or not, each saying when it was made and where it lives. A document an AI tool made for you over its [MCP connection](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/) lands here at once and wears **Made by AI** on its picture, so a batch of new work is one look away.

## Most Used and Recent

Two sections keep up to sixteen documents within reach: **Most Used**, the **eight you opened most** lately, then **Recent** directly under it, the **eight you used last**.

- **Most Used** counts how many times you opened a document over the last 14 days, so a document you open a lot over a few days rises to the top. A day you only edited or made it counts as one open. A document you have not opened in 14 days drops off, so the list stays about what you are working on now.
- **Recent** holds the documents you opened, edited or made most recently, newest first.
- **What you make is in at once**, opened or not: a new document, a duplicate, a single imported document, or one an AI tool made for you. An import of several documents at once is the exception, so a big import never pushes your work aside; those join when you first open one.
- A document is never shown twice: it appears in the first of New, Most Used and Recent it belongs to, and the next one takes its place in the others.
- A document you [hid from Recent](/help/explorer/recent/#hide-from-recent) leaves Most Used and Recent too.

On a computer or a tablet each section is cards, up to two rows of four: **Most Used** with a line under each saying how many times you opened it, then **Recent**, saying when you last used each. On a phone each section is its own strip of up to eight you scroll sideways, under its own heading, and Recent's strip ends in a **See More** tile. If every document you have opened lately is among the most used, Recent is left out until there is another.

Each document shows a picture of what is drawn on it, with its name underneath; click one to open it. A document saved only in this browser wears the **Offline** pill and takes its place like any other, counted in this browser. Documents in the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), and ones whose share link has lapsed or whose team you have left, never appear. With nothing yet to show, Recent says "The documents you open will gather here."

Opening a document is private: it counts towards your own Most Used and Recent, and nobody else ever sees that you opened it.

**See More**, at the right of Recent's heading, opens [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/): the 12 documents you opened most recently, newest first, with the same filters as the rest of the Explorer. Its breadcrumb leads back to Home.

## Shared With You Recently

Below your Spaces sit the four items most recently [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) through a link. **See All** opens Shared With Me.

## Guests and signing in

Home works without an account, like the rest of livedocuments.

**Your work follows you.** Signing up moves what you open, to your new account alongside your documents, so a week of work as a guest is still there afterwards.

## A document's History

The **History** item in a document's menu opens everything that has happened to that document: created, commented on, shared, filed. History covers the **last 30 days**; anything older is no longer listed.

<Tip>
  To step back your own recent edits on a document, use [Undo](/help/canvas/undo/) in the editor.
</Tip>
