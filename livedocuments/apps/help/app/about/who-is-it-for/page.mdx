livedocuments is for teams who make things to share and need to make them **together**. The product shines when several people are on the same document at once, building one set of pages in [real time](/help/collaboration/sharing/). That said, it works just as well for one person writing something up, and the [editor always works without signing in](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## The teams it serves

A few groups feel right at home:

- **Anyone writing something up**: briefs, reports, proposals and how-tos as [articles](/help/pages/articles/), written together with [comments](/help/collaboration/comments/) on the words.
- **People presenting**: decks built from [slide pages](/help/pages/slide-pages/) and their layouts, then [presented](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/) full screen with the laser and spotlight.
- **Marketing and communications teams** making infographics, posters, one-pagers and social posts on [infographic pages](/help/pages/infographic-layouts/), in your [themes](/help/canvas/themes/), to [export](/help/pages/exporting-pages/) or present.

## Whether you sign in or not

livedocuments is friction-free on purpose. A first-time visitor can build a real document as a [guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/) and only later create an account to sync across devices and join [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/). A signed-in user can still hand a share link to a guest who edits without any account at all.
