When you are signed in and belong to one or more [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/), the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/) reaches into them as well, so team work is as findable as your own.

## What the Teams group matches

A **Teams** group lists the teams themselves, by name. Picking one opens that team's page in the Explorer. It works the same from the editor and from the Explorer page.

## Team folders and documents

A team's work is found alongside your own rather than in a group of its own:

- **In the editor**, a team's documents are in the **Documents** group with yours. Team folders are not searched from the editor.
- **On the Explorer page**, a team's documents are among the **Items**, and its folders are in the **Folders** group with an **"in &lt;team&gt;"** suffix, so when a name appears in more than one place you can tell which team it belongs to. Picking a team folder opens the team page with that folder already open.

Each group lists up to 8 matches. See [Finding Documents and Folders](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-documents/).

## Signed-in only

Teams require an account, so the Teams group only appears when you are signed in with teams to search. Guests, and signed-in users with no teams, simply will not see it. The rest of search, your [documents and folders](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-documents/), works the same either way.

<Tip>
  Searching is often faster than expanding the Teams tree in the Explorer: type a team or folder
  name and jump straight in.
</Tip>
