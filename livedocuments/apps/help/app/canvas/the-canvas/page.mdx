The canvas is the open, scrollable surface your pages sit on in livedocuments. You can pan and zoom it freely, with the palette pinned across the top of it for adding elements and switching tools. Everything works straight away, no sign-in required, so you can [open a fresh document](/new) and start placing shapes in seconds.

## The Palette

The **Palette** is a strip pinned to the top centre of the canvas, holding everything you add to it. It starts with the canvas tool (**Select**, **Hand**, **Laser** and the other [selection modes](/help/selection-modes/)), then the palette's categories by name, each opening a panel of its elements as you rest on it, and ends with **Search** for finding any element by name. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) for the details.

In the top-left corner, beside the strip, the **menu button** opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/), with the tab's name beside it on a computer.

## Adding Elements

Most elements are added straight from the palette, whose categories sit along the strip by name. On a design or article page they are **Popular**, **Shapes**, **Write** (text and notes), **Concepts** (concept graphics, the Timeline and the Sankey), **Components** (ready-made blocks such as the Hero and the Card), **Devices**, **Icons**, **Stickers**, **Media** and **Data** (charts, meters and the Table). Rest on one, or click it, and its elements open in a panel underneath. A slide arranges its own set: see [Working with Pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/). Clicking an element arms a combined gesture: a tap drops the element at your pointer, and a drag sizes it as you go. Single-key shortcuts skip the palette altogether, such as `R` for a rectangle, `T` for text and `N` for a sticky note. See [Adding Elements](/help/canvas/the-canvas/adding-elements/) for the full walkthrough.

## Moving Around

The canvas is effectively infinite, so you are never boxed in. Use the Hand tool, a middle-click drag, two fingers on a trackpad, or hold Space and drag to pan, and `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + scroll, a pinch, or the zoom controls in the bottom-right corner to scale the view. The [Panning and Zooming](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/) article covers every way to navigate.

Each tab has its own backdrop and theme. The **Theme & canvas** button beside the zoom controls opens the **Tab Style** dialog, where you can [change the canvas background](/help/canvas/the-canvas/changing-the-background/) or [pick a theme](/help/canvas/themes/).
