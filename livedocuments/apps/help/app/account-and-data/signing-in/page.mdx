Signing in to livedocuments is entirely optional. The canvas works without an account, so there is never a sign-in wall in front of the editor. An account simply adds something useful on top: it keeps your documents with you across browsers and devices instead of being tied to one [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/), unlocks [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/), and lets you create [API tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) to call the API from your own scripts.

## Creating an account

Your livedocuments account is a **liveworkspace** account: one sign-in for livedocuments, livediagram and the other liveworkspace apps. Choosing **Sign in** at the top right of the editor or the Explorer (or opening `/sign-in` or `/get-started`) takes you to the liveworkspace sign-in page at liveworkspace.app. Enter your email and we send you a 6-digit code; type it in and you are back in livedocuments, signed in. There is no password, and there is no separate sign-up step: the first time you sign in with an email, that is your account made.

If you are already signed in to another liveworkspace app in the same browser, signing in to livedocuments takes you straight back with no code to type.

Accounts are free, with no paid tier and nothing to upgrade. After signing in from the sign-in page you land on the [Explorer](/explorer) to see your documents; after signing up you land on the new-document flow to start building. If you used the **Sign in** link in the editor or the Explorer, you go back to where you were instead.

Once you are signed in, your initial and name replace the **Sign in** link. Click it for the account menu: **Account** opens the Account category of Settings, where your name, email, [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/) and **Delete Account** live, and **Sign out** signs you out of livedocuments and returns you to the home page. Settings shows the name and email of your account; it does not edit them.

**Delete Account** removes everything you have in livedocuments, closes your liveworkspace account and signs you out. Closing it signs you out of the other liveworkspace apps too, and you can no longer sign in to them with it.

## When sign-in is not available

Sign-in is an optional capability of livedocuments. On self-hosted instances where accounts have not been configured, the sign-in pages say "Sign-in isn't enabled on this deployment" and offer **Continue as guest**, and the editor works exactly as before. If sign-in is failing on the hosted site, see [Cannot Sign In](/help/troubleshooting/cannot-sign-in/).

## Your guest documents come with you

The best part of signing up is that you keep the work you have already done. When you create an account, everything you built as a guest comes with it, so nothing is lost.

Before you sign in, new documents start [in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/), saved only in this browser. When you sign in, a **Move Documents in This Browser to Your Account?** dialog lists every one of them: every one is ticked, so press **Move** to bring them all across, or untick any you would rather keep in this browser. **Not Now** leaves them in this browser, and we only ask again when there are more. If one fails to move, it stays in this browser and a follow-up dialog names it. You can also move one any time with **Sync Document** in the Explorer or the Share dialog. Documents in This Browser belong to the browser, not to an account, so after you sign out they are offered to whoever signs in next on it.

Anything you already put on the server as a guest (a document you shared, synced, or saved to **livedocuments**) moves automatically. The migration moves your guest content, including those documents, folders, your Shared With Me list, favourites, images and saved preferences (unless your account already has its own), from your per-browser guest id over to your new account. From then on, that content is owned by your account and follows you to any browser or device where you sign in.

The process runs the first time you are signed in in a browser that holds guest work, and is safe to repeat: a second attempt simply moves nothing. Once you are signed in, the Explorer's sign-in nudge disappears.

Only the guest id in **this** browser moves. Guest documents you made in a different browser or on another device belong to that browser's guest id: open livedocuments there and sign in with the same account to bring them across too. [Documents in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) stay in their browser until you move them, because they were never on the server.
