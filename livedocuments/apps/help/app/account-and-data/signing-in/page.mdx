Signing in to livedocuments is entirely optional. The canvas works without an account, so there is never a sign-in wall in front of the editor. An account simply adds something useful on top: it keeps your documents with you across browsers and devices instead of being tied to one [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/), unlocks [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/), and lets you create [API tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) to call the API from your own scripts.

## Creating an account

Your livedocuments account is a **liveworkspace** account: one sign-in for livedocuments, livediagram and the other liveworkspace apps. Choosing **Sign in** at the top right of the editor or the Explorer (or opening `/sign-in` or `/get-started`) takes you to the liveworkspace sign-in page at liveworkspace.app. Enter your email and we send you a 6-digit code; type it in and you are back in livedocuments, signed in. Where the sign-in page offers **Continue with Google**, you can sign in with your Google account instead. There is no password, and there is no separate sign-up step: the first time you sign in with an email, that is your account made.

If you are already signed in to another liveworkspace app in the same browser, signing in to livedocuments takes you straight back with no code to type.

Accounts are free, with no paid tier and nothing to upgrade. After signing in from the sign-in page you land on the [Explorer](/explorer) to see your documents; after signing up you land on the new-document flow to start building. If you used the **Sign in** link in the editor or the Explorer, you go back to where you were instead.

Once you are signed in, your initial and name replace the **Sign in** link. Click it for the account menu: **Account** opens the Account category of Settings, where your name, email, [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/) and **Delete Account** live, and **Sign out** signs you out of livedocuments and of every other liveworkspace app in this browser, then returns you to the home page. The next time you sign in you are asked for a code or Google again, so you can pick a different account. Settings shows the name and email of your account; it does not edit them.

**Delete Account** removes everything you have in livedocuments, closes your liveworkspace account and signs you out. Closing it signs you out of the other liveworkspace apps too, and you can no longer sign in to them with it.

## When sign-in is not available

Sign-in is optional: the editor works fully as a guest. If sign-in is failing on the hosted site, see [Cannot Sign In](/help/troubleshooting/cannot-sign-in/).

## Your guest documents come with you

The best part of signing up is that you keep the work you have already done. When you sign in, whether to a new account or an existing one, everything your [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/) owns in this browser moves into the account: your documents, folders, favourites and your Shared With Me list. liveworkspace does the move, so it covers every liveworkspace app at once, and a canvas you made as a guest on livediagram comes across too. From then on that work is owned by your account and follows you to any browser or device where you sign in.

The move happens in the background as you sign in, and never holds the sign-in up. Afterwards, the next time this browser needs a guest, it starts a fresh one.

Only the guest in **this** browser moves. Guest documents you made in a different browser or on another device belong to that browser's guest: open livedocuments there and sign in with the same account to bring them across too.

If you have documents kept [in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/), which were never on the server, a **Move Documents in This Browser to Your Account?** dialog lists them when you sign in: every one is ticked, so press **Move** to bring them all across, or untick any you would rather keep in this browser. **Not Now** leaves them in this browser, and we only ask again when there are more. If one fails to move, it stays in this browser and a follow-up dialog names it. You can also move one any time with **Sync Document** in the Explorer or the Share dialog. Documents in This Browser belong to the browser, not to an account, so after you sign out they are offered to whoever signs in next on it.
