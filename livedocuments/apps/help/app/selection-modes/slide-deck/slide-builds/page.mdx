A **build** brings an element onto a slide at a step of your talk instead of with the slide. Give a slide's three
points a build each and the slide opens on its title alone; each press of **Next** brings in the next point, and only
after the last one does the deck move to the next slide.

## Giving an element a build

Right-click the element on a slide page (or select it and open its menu), open **Animation**, then **Build**:

- **Appear** cuts the element in, **Fade** fades it in, and **Rise** fades it in while it rises a short way into place.
  Hover a tile to see it play.
- **None** takes the build off: the element is on the slide from the start.

The first element you give a build comes in at step 1, the next at step 2, and so on: click through a slide's points in
the order you will talk about them and they build in that order. Select several elements first and they all come in
together, as one step.

**Order** appears once an element has a build. It lists the slide's steps by number: pick one to move the element to that
step, so it comes in together with whatever else is there, or pick **New** to give it a step of its own after the rest.

Each built element shows a small **numbered badge** on its top-left corner in the editor, so you can see the slide's order
at a glance. In Contents, a slide page with builds says how many: "Slide · In the deck · 3 builds".

Builds are set by an **Editor**. A Participant or a Viewer sees them only when they present the deck.

## Presenting with builds

| Key or control                                           | Does                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| `→`, `Space`, `Enter`, `Page Down`, a click, or **next** | Plays the slide's next build; after the last, next slide |
| `←`, `Page Up`, or **previous**                          | Takes the last build back off; with none, previous slide |
| `Home`, `End`, or a slide from the jump list             | Goes straight there, with nothing built yet              |

- **A slide with three builds presents in four steps**: first everything that does not build, then each build in turn.
  The press after the last build moves to the next slide.
- **Going back to the previous slide** shows it finished, every build in place, so pressing back keeps retracing your
  talk exactly.
- An **arrow** between two elements comes in once both of its ends are showing.
- **Auto-Advance** plays each build in turn before moving to the next slide.
- With **reduce motion** on (on your device or in Settings), each step simply appears, with no fade or movement.
- A screen reader hears each build as it plays ("Build 2 of 3").

## What builds never change

The editor, the page pictures in Contents and every export always show the **finished** slide, with every element in
place. A build only changes what a running deck shows. Presenting is your own: someone else viewing the same document
steps through the builds on their own screen.
