A tab of pages shows everything at once, which is exactly what you want for reference and exactly what you don't want when you are narrating it. A **slide deck** turns your pages into a talk: a set of slides, each one a page, presented full screen one at a time.

There is nothing to open to build one. The deck lives in **Contents**, the list of pages down the left of the canvas: the slides run in the order Contents lists them, and each page's **⋯** menu says what part it plays in the show.

## What a slide is

**A slide is a page.** It shows whatever is on that page now, framed to exactly the page, so it follows your edits without you touching the deck.

- **Every [slide page](/help/pages/slide-pages/) is already in the deck**, from the moment you make it, in page order. Move a page in Contents and its slide moves with it.
- **Any other page joins with Add to Slides**: open its **⋯** menu in Contents (or right-click its row) and press **Add to Slides**.
- **Each tab is its own show.** **Present** runs the slides of the tab you are on, in its page order. Slides on another tab are that tab's show: open it and press **Present** there.

See [Building a Deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/building-a-deck/) for hiding a slide from the run, speaker notes and the order, and [Exporting and Presenting Pages](/help/pages/exporting-pages/#turning-pages-into-slides) for how a page slide behaves.

## Presenting it

Press **Present** in the top bar and the deck opens full screen at the first slide with every part of the editor hidden. To start somewhere else, open a page's **⋯** menu in Contents and press **Present From Here**. See [Presenting](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/) for how a slide is framed, how you move through the deck, and what you can and cannot touch while it runs, and [The Presenter's Controls](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenter-controls/) for the strip in the corner, the pacing clock and the settings behind the cog. Transitions, auto-advance and the rest are also in **Settings**, under **Presenting**, so you can set them before you start rather than in front of the room.

**Starting a presentation broadcasts nothing.** Nobody else is pulled into your deck and nobody's view moves. The way you show a deck to people is the way you already show them everything else in the meeting: **share your screen**.

**The deck itself is ordinary document data**, though: a teammate who opens the document sees the same slides in Contents, hidden ones set aside, and can change them. It saves and travels exactly like the document's name does.

Changing the deck needs edit access: people on a view-only or participant [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) see no page menus in Contents. Anyone on the document, Viewers included, can present it with the **Present** button in the top bar ([Presenting](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/)).

<Tip>
  Build the deck as you would an agenda: one slide per point you intend to make, in the order you
  intend to make them.
</Tip>
