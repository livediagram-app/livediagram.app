Before anything else, the good news: **you do not need to sign in to use livedocuments**. The editor always works without an account, so a sign-in problem never blocks you from building or sharing a document. You can continue as a guest and sort the account out later. With that safety net in mind, here is how to get sign-in working.

## First, keep working

If sign-in is failing and you just want to get on with it, leave the sign-in page and head straight to a [new document](/new) or [the Explorer](/explorer). Everything works: persistence, sharing by link, and real-time collaboration. When you sign up or sign in later, the documents you built as a guest move into your account automatically, so nothing is lost, and you're offered the chance to move any documents in This Browser. See [working as a guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## Sign-in checklist

1. **Check the email code.** Sign-in uses a 6-digit code sent to your email from liveworkspace. Look in spam, and ask for a fresh code on the sign-in page if the first is slow or has expired. If you signed up with **Continue with Google**, use that again.
2. **Use the same email** you originally signed up with. A new email makes a new, empty account.
3. **Start again from livedocuments.** If the sign-in page says the sign-in had expired or did not finish, choose **Try again**. This happens when a sign-in sat open for more than ten minutes, or was started in another tab.
4. **Allow cookies for both sites.** Signing in passes between livedocuments.app and liveworkspace.app in the same tab. A browser set to block all cookies, or a privacy extension, can stop it finishing. Try an incognito window with extensions off.

## If sign-in still will not finish

If sign-in still will not complete after the checklist above, [contact us](/help/contact/) with the email address you are using and the error you see. Keep working as a guest in the meantime, your documents are safe.
