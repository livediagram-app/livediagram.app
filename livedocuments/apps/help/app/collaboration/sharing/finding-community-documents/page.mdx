The [Community](/community/) is a gallery of documents people have shared: reports, slide decks, infographics, guides, proposals, newsletters and more. You don't need an account to browse it, like a document or make your own copy.

## Searching

One search box holds everything you are looking for, written as words:

- **Plain words** search the title, description and tags, for example `quarterly report`.
- **`#tag`** finds documents with that tag. Add several, like `#pitch #startup`, and a document has to have all of them.
- **`category:pitches`** keeps to one kind of document, such as Pitches, Reports or Plans.
- **`sort:loved`** puts the most liked documents first, and **`sort:copied`** the most copied. Without one, the newest come first.
- **`is:mine`** shows only the documents you have shared (see [Your own shares](#your-own-shares)).

You can type these yourself, or use the buttons inside the right of the search box, which write the same words for you:

On smaller screens the buttons show just their icons. The cross just after your text clears the words, tags and category you searched for; the sort and **My Shares** stay as they are. Your search stays in the page address, so you can bookmark it or send it to someone.

<Tip>On a document's own page, choose any of its tags to search the gallery for that tag.</Tip>

## Your own shares

Signed in, choose **My Shares** in the search box to see only the documents you have shared. Above them, **Your Shares** adds up how many you have shared, and how many likes and copies they have had altogether, so you can see how they are doing. Each card shows its own likes and copies, and you can still search, filter and sort within them.

A document taken out of the Community after reports shows here marked **Hidden**; only you can see it. Choose it to open the document in the editor.

## Looking at a document

Open a card to see the document's page: a live preview you can scroll and page through, the author's description and its tags. **Open Document** shows it full screen. Documents in the Community are always read-only, and their comments are private.

## Liking a document

Choose the heart on a card or on a document's page to show the author you liked it. Choose it again to take your like back. Likes are kept per browser, and **Most Loved** sorts by them.

## Making your own copy

Choose **Make a Copy** on a document's page, or in the bar across the top of an open document. A copy of the whole document opens in the editor as your own document, with every tab and page, named after the post you chose. Change anything you like; the original never changes. Each person who copies a document counts once towards its copy count. You don't need an account: as a guest the copy belongs to this browser's guest identity, and it moves into your account if you [sign up](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/) later.

A document's page also lists **More Like This**: other documents in the same category to look at next.

## Reporting a document

If a document is spam, offensive, shows someone's personal information or isn't the author's to share, choose **Report** on its page. Pick a reason (**Spam**, **Offensive**, **Personal Information**, **Copyright** or **Something Else**), add a note of up to 300 characters if it helps, and send it with **Send Report**. A document reported by three different people, on different networks, is hidden automatically, for good. Nobody reviews posts by hand: the reports decide.

## Posting in the Forum

Beside the gallery, the **Forum** tab is where people using the apps share tips, show what they've made, suggest ideas, report bugs, ask questions and talk with each other. There is one Forum for every liveworkspace app. Each post has a category (Question, Tips & Tricks, Feature Request, Bug Report, Show & Tell or General) and the app it is about, shown by its logo, or **Any App**. Anyone can read it without an account; filter by category or app, or sort by **Latest Activity**, **Newest** or **Unanswered**. On a phone, the filters and sort sit behind one **Filters** button.

To post, reply or react you need to be signed in to your account (a guest can read but not post). Choose **New Post**, give it a title, pick a category and the app it is about, write your post and choose **Post**. Your name shows on everything you post. Under each post and reply, **Helpful**, **Thanks** and **Celebrate** let you say what you thought of it. On a Question, its author can choose **Mark as Answer** on the reply that solved it, so it shows first with an **Answered** mark in the list; **Unanswered** lists the Questions still waiting for one.

Every post, reply and edit is checked automatically before anyone else sees it. If the check is busy, your post shows to you alone as **Pending: being checked** until it is checked again a few minutes later; one that doesn't pass stays visible to you alone, to edit or delete. You can **Edit** or **Delete** your own posts at any time, and closing your account removes them all. Each account can write up to 20 posts and replies an hour.

If a post is spam, offensive or shows someone's personal information, choose **Report** on it. As in the gallery, a post reported by three different people, on different networks, is hidden automatically.
