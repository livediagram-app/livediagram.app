Every team has a shared library: a folder tree plus the items in it, which all joined members can see and manage. It works just like your own [My Work](/help/explorer/personal-space/), only shared. Open a team from the **Spaces** group of the Explorer sidebar to see it.

The library is liveworkspace's, so it holds items from every app: a team's folder can keep a document beside a Canvas from livediagram and a Design from livedesign. Choose **All** at the top of the page to see them all.

## What everyone can do

A team item belongs to the whole team, not just to the person who made it. Every **joined** member can:

- create a new document or folder in the library, with **Create**;
- open and edit every item;
- rename, duplicate, move and delete an item;
- make and manage its [share links](/help/collaboration/sharing/) and its share password.

Deleting a team item moves it to the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), where every member can restore it.

There are no per-folder or per-member permissions inside a team: everyone who has joined can manage everything. People with a pending invitation get nothing until they accept.

## Moving items in and out

An item lives in exactly one place: your own My Work, or one team's library. **Change Folder** in an item's menu opens a picker of every place you can file it: pick a space first (**My Work**, or one of your teams), then browse into the folder you want and press **Move Here**. Moving an item into or out of a team needs you to be a member of that team.

An item in a team library shows a **Team** badge in the Explorer, since every joined member can open it.

## When someone leaves

Membership is checked each time someone opens a team item, so a person who leaves the team, or is removed, loses access within a few minutes, even to an item they have open.

<Tip>
  Use **Create**, then **New Document**, from inside a team folder so the new document lands in the
  folder you are browsing.
</Tip>
