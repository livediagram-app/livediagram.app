A snapshot keeps the whole document as it is right now, so you can try a big change (a reorganisation, an import, a clean-up with a teammate or an AI assistant) knowing you can put everything back in one step.

## Taking a snapshot

Snapshots live in **History**. Open **Tools** in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just before Settings, and choose **History**. Press **Take Snapshot** at its foot: a **Take a Snapshot** card opens beside it, where you can give the snapshot a name (up to 80 characters), such as "Before the workshop", then press **Take Snapshot**. Without a name it is called by the time it was taken.

Each snapshot sits on History's timeline at the time it was taken, marked with a camera among the document's other changes, with its name, who took it, how long ago and its size. Someone who can take snapshots but has no timeline to read, such as an Editor on a share link, sees the snapshots on their own. The latest **five** are kept: when five are there, the card says which one taking another will remove, and the oldest goes.

## Reverting to a snapshot

Press **Revert** on a snapshot and confirm. The document goes back to exactly how it was: every tab, its folders and the slide deck. The document's name, its comments and who it is shared with stay as they are.

Reverting replaces everyone's work since that snapshot, so first it takes a snapshot of the document as it is now, marked **Before revert**. If you change your mind, revert to that one.

Everyone who has the document open sees it reload onto the reverted version straight away, so nobody keeps working on what was replaced.

## Deleting a snapshot

Press the bin on a snapshot. It is deleted straight away, without a question, and can't be brought back.

## Who can use snapshots

Editors of the whole document take, revert and delete snapshots. Participants, Viewers and people with a link to one tab don't see them. Snapshots are there on documents stored on the server: a document kept **Offline** has none, and AI assistants and scripts using an API token can't use them.

## Snapshots and Version History

[Version History](/help/tabs/version-history/) keeps earlier versions of **one tab** for you as you work. A snapshot is one you take yourself, of the **whole document** at once: every tab together. Reverting to a snapshot keeps each tab's Version History, so a tab's earlier versions are still there afterwards. **Open Version History** in History takes you to it.
