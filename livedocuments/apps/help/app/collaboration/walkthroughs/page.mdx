A walkthrough is a video of you talking someone through the document. It films this browser tab as you see it, with your voice and, if you turn it on, your camera in a round bubble in a corner. It is kept with the document for everyone who can open it, and you can share it with anyone by a public link.

## Recording a walkthrough

<Step n={1}>
  Open **Tools** in the footer, press **Walkthroughs**, then **Record Walkthrough**. The **Record a
  Walkthrough** card opens beside Tools.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick your **Microphone**. The **Camera** switch starts off, so a walkthrough records your voice
  alone; turn it on to pick a camera, show yourself in a round bubble and choose the **Bubble
  Corner**. The card remembers the switch in this browser. If the browser hasn't been allowed to
  list your devices yet, press **Allow Microphone** (or **Allow Camera**) to ask it. **Mic Level**
  moves as you speak, so you know you are heard.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  To keep something out of the video, press **Hide From Recording** now (see below). You can't
  change it once the recording starts.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Press **Start Recording** and let the browser share this tab when it asks. After a 3, 2, 1
  countdown, talk and move around the document as you would with someone beside you. A small bar
  under the palette shows the time, the level, **Discard** and **Finish**.
</Step>

<Step n={5}>
  Press **Finish** (or stop sharing from the browser's own bar). The walkthrough saves straight away
  as "Walkthrough of" the tab's name, and is listed under Walkthroughs in Tools.
</Step>

A walkthrough can run for up to ten minutes; the clock turns amber with a minute left and finishes on its own at ten. If saving fails (it is too large, or you have reached your limit), a card at the foot of the document says why and offers **Try Again** and **Download a Copy**, so the recording isn't lost. **Discard** asks first, since a discarded recording can't be recovered. Recording needs a browser that can film its own tab, such as Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox on a computer.

## Hiding elements from the recording

Press **Hide From Recording** in the card, then press each element viewers shouldn't see: a private note, an unfinished corner. Arrows attached to it go with it. **Hide Selection** hides what is already selected, and **Done** leaves the mode. Before you start you see hidden elements faded inside a dashed outline; while the tab is filmed they are hidden from your screen too, so the video never holds them. They stay on the document itself for anyone who opens it.

## Playing a walkthrough

Open **Tools** in the footer, press **Walkthroughs**, and press one to play it. It opens in a video player over the editor, with its title, who made it and when, **Full Screen**, and the usual play, seek and speed controls.

A walkthrough recorded before walkthroughs became videos still plays as it did, over the document itself: pan, zoom or press on the document to pause and look around, then **Resume**. `Space` plays and pauses, `←` and `→` skip five seconds, and `Esc` closes.

## Renaming, sharing and deleting

Each walkthrough in the list has a **⋯** menu:

- **Rename** edits its title in place (up to 120 characters).
- **Share** (on a video) turns on a **Public Link**, with **Copy Link**. Anyone with the link can watch the video without signing in; they see its title and who made it, never the document. Turn the link off and it stops working at once. **Share** is also in the video player.
- **Delete** asks first, then deletes it for everyone who can open the document.

## Who can record and manage

Editors record walkthroughs, and can rename, share and delete any of them. Everyone who can open the document can play them, and you can always rename, share or delete one you recorded. Walkthroughs aren't offered on a phone, in an embed, for a document kept in **This Browser**, or through a link to one tab.

## Privacy

Calls are never recorded. A walkthrough is only made when you press Start Recording, and the browser shows that it is sharing the tab for as long as it records. It is stored with the document for the people who can open it, and reaches anyone else only through a public link you turn on. Deleting a walkthrough, purging its document from the Trash or deleting your account deletes it. See the [privacy policy](https://www.liveworkspace.app/privacy).
