Most documents are saved to livedocuments' servers so you can reach them from any device and share them. **Offline Mode** is the opposite choice: a document saved **only in the browser you made it in**, that is never stored on the server. It suits private, local-first work, simply trying livedocuments out, or wanting a document that physically never leaves your machine.

## Creating an offline document

Offline Mode is chosen per document, so you can keep normal documents and ones kept Offline side by side.

- **If you haven't signed in**, your new documents start Offline. Nothing you try out is sent to our servers until you choose to share it, sync it, or sign in and move it to your account. Everything you make from [New Document](/new), and from the links that create a document straight away (a template from the home page, say), starts Offline. Use **Sync Document** from its Share dialog if you would rather it went to livedocuments now.
- **Once you've signed in**, new documents go to your account. To keep one only in this browser, choose **Take Offline** from its menu (see below).
- **A document made inside a folder or a team** goes to livedocuments, whoever makes it, because that is where the folder or team lives.
- The document is written to your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) and opens like any other. In the Explorer each one carries an **Offline** pill, so you always know which documents live only in this browser. Hover or focus the pill to read that clearing this browser's site data deletes the document. The rest of the Explorer lists only documents on the server, so **Offline**, a space of its own in the Explorer sidebar's **Spaces** group, is where you find them all.

<Note>
  A document kept Offline is **not synced and not backed up**. It lives only in this browser:
  clearing your site data, or the browser reclaiming storage, will delete it. Keep anything
  important on livedocuments, or export a copy.
</Note>

## What's different

A document kept Offline is a normal document: every editor feature works, including pages, shapes, arrows, tabs, themes, undo/redo, and export. Only the features that need the server are unavailable, because there's no server copy to power them:

- **Sharing** needs a document on the server. Opening **Share** on a document kept Offline tells you so and offers **Sync Document**: press it and the document uploads, and the Share options appear in the same dialog, with no reload. Embeds are unavailable until it is synced, for the same reason.
- **Live collaboration** and **teams** don't apply; a document kept Offline is yours alone. Comments still work, but only you see them.
- Images you add are stored **inside the document**, so it stays fully local.
- **Add a Tab to Another Document** is unavailable for a document kept Offline in either direction, and **Duplicate** makes another copy kept Offline rather than uploading one.
- It is listed under **Offline** in the Explorer sidebar's Spaces, in Home's Recent once you have opened it, and on the **Offline** page ([/offline](/offline), also reached from the Explorer panel), not in My Work, Recent or search, because those list what is on the server.

## Offline Folders

**New Folder** on the Offline page, or the **⋯** menu on its row in the sidebar, makes a folder that lives in this browser too, and folders can hold folders, as in My Work. **Move to Folder** on a document's menu, or a drag onto a folder, files it. A folder's menu has **Rename**, **New Subfolder**, **Move Folder** and **Delete Folder**; deleting a folder never deletes what is in it, which moves up a level. The folders are kept in this browser alone, like the documents in them.

## Archiving an offline document

**Move to Archived** works on a document kept Offline too. It never leaves this browser: it moves out of the Offline list into **Archived**, and it won't open until you move it out. Opening it shows a **Document Archived** card with **Change Folder**, which puts it back Offline (or, when you are signed in, moves it into your account). Clearing this browser's data removes it like any other document kept here.

## Moving to your account

You can change your mind at any time, from the document's **⋯** menu.

- **When you sign in** with documents kept Offline, we offer to move them to your account. Every one is ticked; untick any you want to keep in this browser, then **Move**. **Not Now** leaves them where they are, and we only ask again when there are more.

- **Sync Document** (in a document's Share dialog, signed in or not, and on each document under **Offline**, in the Explorer and on the Offline page, when you're signed in) uploads a document kept Offline to the cloud. From the Explorer, the Offline page opens and asks you to confirm. It becomes a normal document, shareable and (when you're signed in) reachable from your other devices, and the copy in this browser is removed.
- **A document in a folder keeps its place.** Sync Document offers the My Work folder with the same name (or My Work itself when there is none) and says where it will go. The folder in this browser stays, empty, until you delete it.
- **Take Offline** (on a document's menu in the Explorer and the Explorer panel) does the reverse: it downloads a cloud document into this browser and then **removes it from your account and every other device**. Because that deletes the server copy for good, we ask you to confirm first.
- **A tab it shares with other documents** (see [Add a Tab to Another Document](/help/tabs/add-to-document/)) stays in those documents, and this browser keeps its own copy. From then on the two are separate: edits on one side no longer reach the other, and syncing the document back brings your copy in as a new tab beside the original.

<Note>
  **Take Offline** is deliberate and irreversible on the server side: once a document is Offline it
  exists only in the browser you took it offline in. If it was shared, taking it offline also
  revokes the share links, and a team document leaves the team library. For the owner it is a move,
  not a delete, so it skips the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/); a teammate taking a team
  document offline sends the team's copy to the team Trash. Deleting a document kept Offline removes
  it from this browser.
</Note>
