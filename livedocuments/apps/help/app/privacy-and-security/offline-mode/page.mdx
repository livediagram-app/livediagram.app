Most documents are saved to livedocuments' servers so you can reach them from any device and share them. **Offline Mode** is the opposite choice: a document saved **only in the browser you made it in**, that is never stored on the server. It suits private, local-first work, or simply wanting a document that physically never leaves your machine.

## Creating a document in This Browser

Offline Mode is chosen per document, so you can keep normal documents and ones in This Browser side by side.

- New documents go to livedocuments, whether you are signed in or a guest. To keep one only in this browser, choose **Take Offline** from its menu (see below).
- The document is written to your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) and opens like any other. A **This Browser** badge next to its name in the editor marks it, so you always know which documents live only in this browser. Hover or focus the badge to read that clearing this browser's site data deletes the document. The rest of the Explorer lists only documents on the server, so **This Browser**, in the **More** group of the Explorer sidebar, is where you find them all.

<Note>
  A document in This Browser is **not synced and not backed up**. It lives only in this browser:
  clearing your site data, or the browser reclaiming storage, will delete it. Keep anything
  important on livedocuments, or export a copy.
</Note>

## What's different

A document in This Browser is a normal document: every editor feature works, including pages, shapes, arrows, tabs, themes, undo/redo, and export. Only the features that need the server are unavailable, because there's no server copy to power them:

- **Sharing** needs a document on the server. Opening **Share** on a document in This Browser tells you so and offers **Sync Document**: press it and the document uploads, and the Share options appear in the same dialog, with no reload. Embeds are unavailable until it is synced, for the same reason.
- **Live collaboration** and **teams** don't apply; a document in This Browser is yours alone. Comments still work, but only you see them.
- Images you add are stored **inside the document**, so it stays fully local.
- **Add a Tab to Another Document** is unavailable for a document in This Browser in either direction, and **Duplicate** makes another copy in This Browser rather than uploading one.
- It is listed only on the **This Browser** page ([/offline](/offline), from **This Browser** in the Explorer's sidebar or the Explorer panel), not in My Work, Recent or search, because the Explorer lists what is on the server.

## Moving to your account

You can change your mind at any time, from the document's **⋯** menu.

- **When you sign in** with documents in This Browser, we offer to move them to your account. Every one is ticked; untick any you want to keep in this browser, then **Move**. **Not Now** leaves them where they are, and we only ask again when there are more.

- **Sync Document** uploads a document in This Browser to the cloud. It becomes a normal document, shareable and (when you're signed in) reachable from your other devices, and the copy in this browser is removed.
- **Take Offline** does the reverse: it downloads a cloud document into this browser and then **removes it from your account and every other device**. Because that deletes the server copy for good, we ask you to confirm first.
- **A tab it shares with other documents** (see [Add a Tab to Another Document](/help/tabs/add-to-document/)) stays in those documents, and this browser keeps its own copy. From then on the two are separate: edits on one side no longer reach the other, and syncing the document back brings your copy in as a new tab beside the original.

<Note>
  **Take Offline** is deliberate and irreversible on the server side: once a document is in This
  Browser it exists only in the browser you took it offline in. If it was shared, taking it offline
  also revokes the share links, and a team document leaves the team library. For the owner it is a
  move, not a delete, so it skips the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/); a teammate taking a
  team document offline sends the team's copy to the team Trash. Deleting a document in This Browser
  removes it from this browser.
</Note>
