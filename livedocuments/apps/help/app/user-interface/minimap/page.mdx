Some canvas apps keep a zoomed-out **Map** of the whole canvas in a corner. livedocuments doesn't show one: every tab is laid out in [pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/), and the pages themselves are the overview.

## Finding Your Way Round a Tab

- **Contents** lists every page in the tab with a picture of it, its number and its name, and highlights the one you are on. Press a page to glide there. On a computer it sits as a sidebar down the left once a tab has more than one page; on a phone it is a button in the bottom-right corner. See [Contents](/help/pages/working-with-pages/#contents).
- **The page navigator** in the bottom-right corner, beside Undo and Redo, shows "2 of 5" between **Previous page** and **Next page**, each fitting that page to the screen.
- **Fit** in the bottom-right corner fits the page you are nearest. The [zoom controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/) zoom in and out.
- With nothing selected, the **arrow keys** move to the next or previous page.

## The Map Settings

[Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) still lists **Panels › Map** (**Enable Map**, **Dim Outside the View** and **Map Size**). Because a livedocuments tab always has pages, the Map never shows and these rows have no effect.
