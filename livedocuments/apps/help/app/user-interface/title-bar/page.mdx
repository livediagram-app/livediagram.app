livedocuments has **no title bar** across the top of the editor. The canvas runs all the way up, and the few controls a title bar would hold float over it in two cards, in line with the [palette strip](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/):

- **Top left**, the menu card: the livedocuments logo, the menu button that opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/), and on a computer the open tab's name.
- **Top right**, the actions card: **Copy**, **Present** and **Share** when they apply, then your account.

Both cards hide in [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/), and an [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) shows neither.

## The logo

The logo opens the Explorer on the place that holds this document, so you land beside it in its folder. On a narrow screen it shows without its wordmark. The other parts of livedocuments (**Home**, **Help** and more) are links along the foot of the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).

## The document's name and who can see it

The editor itself does not show the document's name or a visibility pill. To rename a document, or see whether it is Private, Shared or in a team, use its row in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/). Whether it is shared also shows on the **Share** button, which turns solid once the document has a live link.

## Share or Copy

The actions card holds the document's main action, and which one you get depends on whose document it is. On a computer each button shows its name beside its icon; on a phone, or when the strip needs the room, it shows the icon alone.

**Share** is for the owner (and the members of the team whose library holds it). It opens the [Share dialog](/help/collaboration/sharing/), where you create and manage links. The button turns solid once the document has a live link, so you can tell at a glance that it is shared. On a [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) document it asks you to save the document to your account first, since there is nothing on a server to share yet.

**Copy** is for everyone else who has the document open, on a share link of any role. It makes a copy of the document that belongs to you:

<Step n={1}>Click **Copy**. It reads **Copying** while the copy is made.</Step>
<Step n={2}>The editor opens your copy, named after the original with **(copy)** at the end.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Find it again later at the top level of My Work in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
</Step>

Your copy starts **Private**: no share links come with it, and nothing you do to it touches the original. If you opened the document through a link to a single tab, the copy holds just that tab. You do not need an account to copy; as a guest the copy is saved under your [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/).

## Present

While there are slides to present, a **Present** button joins the card on a computer, before **Share**. Hover it to see how many slides the deck has, and click it to start [presenting](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/). It reads **Starting** while the deck gets ready.

## Your account

At the end of the card is your account. Signed in, it shows your picture or initials; click it for a menu with your name and email, **Account** (which opens [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) on your account details, without leaving the document) and **Sign Out**. Not signed in, it shows **Sign In** instead (see [Signing In](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/)).
