There is no bar across the top of the editor: the canvas takes the whole height, and two cards float over its top corners. The **menu card** at the top left holds the logo and the menu button. The **actions card** at the top right holds **Present** when the document has a slide deck with a slide to show, **Share** or **Copy** and your account. The [palette](/help/palette/) sits between them.

On a phone the menu card joins the palette's row and the actions card shows its glyphs alone, without names; in a window too narrow for the palette and the actions card's names side by side, the actions card drops its names first. [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) and an [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) hide both cards.

## The logo

The logo at the left of the menu card opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) on the folder that holds this document. Its wordmark shows on a wide screen only.

## The menu button

The **≡** button beside the logo opens the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) under it, and pressing it again closes the panel. The current document is at the top of the panel: its name, a badge saying who can see it and its **⋯** menu. Click the name there to rename the document: `Enter` keeps the new name and `Escape` the old one.

A new document names itself until you do: while it still has its starting name (such as "Untitled Infographic" or "Untitled Article"), the words you type on the first element of its first tab, or the Title of an article there, become its name. Once you rename it yourself, your name stays.

## Present

When the document's slide deck has a slide to show, **Present** sits before Share or Copy and starts the presentation. It is not offered on a phone.

## Share or Copy

The actions card holds one main action, and which one you get depends on whose document it is.

**Share** is for the owner. It opens the [Share dialog](/help/collaboration/sharing/), where you create and manage links. The button turns solid blue once the document has a live link, so you can tell at a glance that it is shared. On a [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) document it asks you to save the document to your account first, since there is nothing on a server to share yet.

**Copy** is for everyone else who has the document open: someone on a share link of any role, or a team member opening a document a teammate owns. It makes a copy of the document that belongs to you:

<Step n={1}>Click **Copy**. It reads **Copying** while the copy is made.</Step>
<Step n={2}>The editor opens your copy, named "Copy of" and the original name.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Find it again later at the top level of My Work in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
</Step>

Your copy starts **Private**: no share links come with it, and nothing you do to it touches the original. If you opened the document through a link to a single tab, the copy holds just that tab. You do not need an account to copy; as a guest the copy is saved under your [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/).

## Your account

At the end of the actions card is your account. Signed in, it shows your initial; click it for a menu with your name and email, **Account** (which opens [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) on your account details, without leaving the document) and **Sign Out**. Not signed in, it shows **Sign in** instead (see [Signing In](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/)).
