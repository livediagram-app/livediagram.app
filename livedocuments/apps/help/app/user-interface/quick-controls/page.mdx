The **quick controls** sit at the right-hand end of the bottom bar, after the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/)'s tabs, and gather the actions you want reachable at any moment, whatever you are doing on the canvas. They stay put while the rest of the interface comes and goes with your selection, and a matching cluster appears at the bottom of the full-page [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), so the two screens don't hide their controls in different places.

## What lives here

Left to right:

- **Get Started**, only while a tour is on offer: press it for the tours on offer, such as the Pages tour or the tour of the element you have selected, and pick one to start it. See [The Pages Tour](/help/pages/pages-tour/).
- **Tools**: one button that opens a panel of the tools that work on the whole document, grouped under headings. Press a row to open that tool inside the panel, and the back bar to return to the list.
- **Settings**: the [Settings dialog](/help/user-interface/settings/): the preferences that follow you between devices, plus the **Keyboard** category with the full [shortcut reference](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) and the switch that turns shortcuts off.
- **Appearance**: click to step the [interface between Light, Dark, and System](/help/tools/dark-mode/) (System follows your device). This is the app's own chrome, not the tab's theme, which lives behind the **Style** button on each page's title bar.

## What Tools holds

Tools only lists what applies to this document and to you:

- **Search…**: opens the [search panel](/help/search-panel/) to jump to any document, tab or element, drop something onto the canvas, or run a command. `⌘K` (Ctrl+K) opens it from the keyboard too.
- **Walkthroughs**: play a recorded [walkthrough](/help/collaboration/walkthroughs/), or, as an editor, record one.
- **History**: the document's [version history](/help/tabs/version-history/) and its snapshots, on a document stored on the server.
- **Webhooks**: tell other services when the document changes, for someone who may manage them.

Signed out, **Walkthroughs**, **History** and **Webhooks** still show, with a badge, and ask you to sign in first. On a [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) document they ask you to save it to your account first.

On a computer **Get Started**, **Tools** and **Settings** show their names beside their icons (turn this off with **Button Labels** in Settings, under **Appearance**), and hovering a control shows what it does. The appearance switch is always an icon, showing the mode in force: a sun, a moon or a monitor. On a phone the bar is tight, so every control is an icon only. Inside a workspace workbench the appearance switch is not shown, because the workbench sets the look.

The Explorer's cluster also carries a link to livedocuments's source on GitHub, on a computer.

Because they are fixed in the corner rather than tied to a selection, the quick controls are the dependable starting point when you are not sure where an action lives: search, in Tools, finds both things and commands.
