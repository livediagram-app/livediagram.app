## One element selected

A single selected element has **no toolbar** floating over it, so nothing covers the page you are writing about. Everything the toolbar used to hold is in the element's menu: right-click the element (a long press on a touch screen, or **Shift+F10** with the keyboard). Its first row holds **Edit Text** (or **Add Text**), **Duplicate**, **Stack Front**, **Stack Back**, **Lock** (or **Unlock**) and **Delete**, and the row below **Add Comment** and the rest. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/).

On a Participant or view-only link the same right-click opens a short menu with what that link may do, such as **Add Comment** or **Show Comments**.

## Several elements selected

Drag a marquee or Shift-click to select several, and a toolbar floats over them with the actions that make sense across a group:

- **More** (**⋯**): the selection's menu, for styling everything at once.
- **Filter Selection** (only when the selection mixes kinds): narrows it by type, so you can marquee over a busy area and keep, say, just the arrows. See [Multi-Select](/help/canvas/selecting-and-grouping/multi-select/).
- **Duplicate**, with the arrows between the selected elements included.
- **Export**: export just these elements.
- **Lock** (or **Unlock**) and **Delete**.

The caption reads "Selected Elements" with a count.

<Figure caption="Select a mix of elements and a toolbar offers More, Filter Selection, Duplicate, Export, Lock and Delete.">
  <MultiSelectToolbar />
</Figure>

The toolbar follows your selection so the controls are always close to what they affect, and disappears the moment you deselect, handing the canvas back. Beside a selected shape or arrow, the [Quick Style panel](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/) offers the most-used colours and widths too.

In [power user mode](/help/user-interface/power-user-mode/) on a computer, **More** leaves the toolbar (right-click instead), and with Minimal Chrome on so do the caption and the bin (press `Delete` instead). Touch screens keep **More** and the bin, since they can't right-click or press `Delete`; the caption is hidden there too.

<Tip>
  To copy one element's look onto others, use the **Format** tool in the Palette's selection-mode
  dropdown (see [Format Painter](/help/selection-modes/format-painter/)).
</Tip>
