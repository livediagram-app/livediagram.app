**Export** downloads the active tab in the format you need, whether that is an image for a doc, a PDF to print, or a file to move the tab into another document.

## Opening Export

There are a few ways in, and they all open the same **Export Tab** dialog:

- The tab's menu (the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click the tab): choose **Export**.
- **Export…** in the [command palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/) (`Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `K`). This works on a view-only share link too, so viewers can download a copy.
- **Export** in the ⋯ menu at the top of the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).

To export only part of a tab, select several elements and press the **Export** button on the toolbar that appears above the selection. The dialog is then headed **Export Selection** and covers just those elements.

## Export formats

The dialog shows a card per format. A tab exports as its **pages**:

- **PDF**: every page in one PDF, or a single page, ready to print or share. Each page comes out at its own size and orientation.
- **PNG**: a high-resolution image of a page, or a **.zip** with one image per page.
- **SVG**: a crisp vector image of a page that scales to any size, or a **.zip** with one per page.
- **GIF**: one animated image of the pages, moving as they move on the canvas.
- **JSON**: a portable `.livedocuments-tab.json` snapshot that [imports](/help/tabs/import-tabs/) back with full fidelity.
- **Logo Kit**, when the tab has a logo page: an SVG, PNGs from 16 to 1024 px and a favicon in one .zip.

For the image formats, choose **All pages** or **One page**. Each page comes out as exactly its sheet, background included. See [Exporting and Presenting Pages](/help/pages/exporting-pages/) for the details.

Files are named after the document and the tab, for example `Roadmap - Overview.png`.

## Exporting a selection

**Export Selection** offers **JSON**, **Markdown**, **PNG**, **SVG** and **PDF**:

- **Markdown**: the selected elements with labels, and every connection between them, as a Markdown outline (`.md`), a readable summary rather than a full copy.
- The **text** formats (JSON and Markdown) open an editor pre-filled with the selection in that format: tweak the text and press **Copy**, or download the file (**Download .json** or **Download .md**). Your edits in that box never change the tab.
- The **image** formats (PNG, SVG, PDF) open an options screen with a preview and a download button:
  - **Isometric view** (off by default) tilts the export into the [isometric projection](/help/selection-modes/isometric-mode/).
  - **Background pattern** (on by default) paints the tab's grid, dots or texture behind the elements. Switch it off for a plain background.
  - **Hidden layers** appears only when the tab has layers hidden and the [Layers panel](/help/canvas/layers/) is switched on in Settings, and chooses whether those layers are included. It is off by default, so what you hid stays out.

**All formats** at the top of any screen takes you back to the cards.

<Tip>
  Reach for **SVG** when the image might be resized or printed large: it stays sharp at any scale,
  unlike a fixed-resolution PNG. For sharing a live, always-current view instead of a static file,
  see [Sharing and Embeds](/help/collaboration/sharing/).
</Tip>
