<ArticleLayout title="Your First Document" description="From a new document to a first page you can share, in a couple of minutes, with no account required." categoryTitle="Getting Started" categorySlug="getting-started">

The quickest way to learn livedocuments is to make something. This walkthrough takes you from a new document to a first page with text, shapes and a picture on it, ready to share. You do not need to sign in, the [editor always works as a guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## Create a new document

<Step n={1}>
  Open [a new document](/new). livedocuments makes it straight away and opens it in the editor, with
  nothing to fill in first.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  It is saved where new documents go: to your account once you have signed in, or in this browser
  while you try things out as a guest (you can [move it to your
  account](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) any time). [Default
  Folders](/help/explorer/folders/default-folders/) decide which folder it lands in.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Its first tab opens on **Start Making Pages**: **Recommended** picks first, then **Blank Pages**,
  then every [template](/help/canvas/templates/) grouped by what it is for (**Reports**, **Plans and
  Proposals**, **Team and Meetings** and more), with a search. Click one to start from its
  ready-made pages, or press the **×** to follow along from a blank page.
</Step>

Until you name it, a new document takes its name from what you write first: the words on the first element of its first tab, or an article's Title. You can rename the document any time: click its title in the header at the top of the editor and type. Renaming is instant and saved for you. Names cap at **60 characters**: typing simply stops there, which is plenty for a title and short enough to stay readable in a tab bar or a list row.

## Choose what the first page is for

Closing Start Making Pages leaves one empty page, which asks **What Is This Page For?** Choose now: a page keeps its kind once you start.

- **Infographic**: a sheet to lay out freely. Choose this to follow along.
- **Article**: a page to write on, like a document. The caret lands in its title, ready to type. See [Writing Articles](/help/pages/articles/).
- **Slide**: one slide of a deck. See [Slide Pages](/help/pages/slide-pages/).

For a short tour of your kind of page, on your own page, press Start Pages Tour in the bar at the bottom of the editor, just left of **Search**. It never starts on its own, and you can take it again whenever you like. See [The Pages Tour](/help/pages/pages-tour/).

## Add text and shapes

<Step n={1}>
  Find the **Palette**, the strip across the top of the editor. Its categories (**Shapes**,
  **Write**, **Concepts**, **Components**, **Media**, **Data** and more) sit along it by name: rest
  on one, or click it, and its elements open in a panel underneath.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick **Add Text** (in **Write**, or press `T`), then **click the page** where you want it, and
  type a heading. Click away to finish.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Rest on **Shapes** and pick a shape tile, then click the page to drop it at its default size, or
  drag on the page to draw it at the size you want. **Double-click the shape** to give it a label.
</Step>

Anything you place on a page belongs to it: whatever hangs off its edge is cut off there, on screen and in exports. Move or resize an element and it snaps to the page's edges, centre lines and margins.

## Add a picture

<Step n={1}>
  Pick **Add Image** from **Media** (or press `9`), then click the page. The image lands as an empty
  placeholder reading "No image yet".
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Press its **Select Image** button (or double-click it), and on the **Upload** tab drop a file in,
  click to browse, or paste from your clipboard. You can also paste a picture straight onto the
  page. See [Images](/help/palette/tools/images/).
</Step>

Want a picture behind everything instead? Press **Background** in the page's title bar and choose **Image**. See [Working with Pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/#backgrounds-and-patterns).

## Share it

<Step n={1}>
  Click **Share** at the top right of the editor, choose a role (**Editor**, **Participant** or
  **Viewer**), and press **Create Pass**. The link is copied for you.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Send it to whoever you like. They join the same document and see your cursor and every change
  live, even without an account. See [Sharing Your
  Document](/help/getting-started/sharing-your-document/).
</Step>

## Keep going

- **Add another page** with the **+** after the last page, and choose its kind.
- **Recolour everything** with a [theme](/help/canvas/themes/).
- **Present or export** your pages. See [Exporting and Presenting Pages](/help/pages/exporting-pages/).

<Tip>
  Made a mistake? [Undo](/help/canvas/undo/) and [Redo](/help/canvas/redo/) sit in the controls in
  the bottom-right corner (or press `Cmd/Ctrl+Z`), and the common keyboard shortcuts are in
  [Keyboard Essentials](/help/getting-started/keyboard-essentials/).
</Tip>
