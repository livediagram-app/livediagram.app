The **Polygon** tool draws clean, straight-edged outlines with exactly the corners you choose: the outline of a mark, a zone on a plan, or any irregular region that none of the fixed shapes fits. Everything it draws is a regular element you can move, resize and style.

## Where to find it

The palette strip does not show it, but the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) (`⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`) adds it: type "polygon" and pick it under **Add to canvas**.

## Placing points

<Step n={1}>Pick **Polygon**. It has no keyboard shortcut.</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Click to place each point. A preview line follows your pointer from the last point you placed.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Click the **start point** (a snap ring appears when you are close) to close the outline into a
  **filled polygon**, or **double-click** (or press `Enter`) to finish an **open line** at the last
  point.
</Step>

<Figure caption="Placing polygon points click by click, with the snap ring on the start point ready to close the outline.">
  <PolygonDraw />
</Figure>

While placing points, `Backspace` removes the last point so you can walk a mistake back without starting over, and `Escape` clears the in-progress outline (press it again to put the tool down). An open line needs at least two points and a closed polygon three: until then, double-click and `Enter` do nothing, and clicking the start point just places another point. On a touch screen, taps place points just like clicks. Points snap to the edges and centres of nearby elements, as a drawn element's corners do.

## Styling an outline

A finished outline is a regular boxed element. Its stroke colour follows the tab's theme, and a closed polygon fills with the theme's fill colour; an open line stays stroke-only. From there you can:

- Move it, resize it (the outline scales proportionally), rotate it, and lock it.
- Recolour its stroke and fill from the element's Colours controls.
- Apply the format painter, themes, and comments, exactly as with any shape.
