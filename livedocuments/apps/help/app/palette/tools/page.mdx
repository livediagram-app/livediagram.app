There is no **Tools** tab. Every element sits in a palette **category** of its own, one press away on the palette strip, so this page is a map of where each one lives.

The categories sit along the strip by name, in groups with a thin line between each. Rest on one, or click it, to see its elements.

## The categories on a page

On an article, an infographic or any other design page, the palette offers these categories, in this order:

- **Popular**: twelve tiles a page is most often built from, picked from across the categories below: Text, Square, Circle, Image, Speech Bubble, Pie, Column, Donut, Stat Row, Process, Timeline and Callout.
- **[Shapes](/help/palette/shapes/)**: the plain rectangles, circles and the rest.
- **[Write](/help/palette/write/)**: Text, Sticky Note and Pointer Note.
- **[Concepts](/help/palette/concept-graphics/)**: the concept graphics, the [Timeline](/help/palette/concept-graphics/timeline-rail/) and the [Sankey](/help/palette/concept-graphics/sankey-diagram/).
- **[Components](/help/palette/components/)**: the Hero, Header, Banner and [Stack](/help/palette/stacks/), then the Card, Badge, Step Number, Callout, Stat Row and Process, then the [Code Block](/help/palette/tools/code-blocks/) and the [Checklist](/help/palette/tools/checklists/).
- **[Devices](/help/palette/devices/)**: browser, monitor, laptop, phone, tablet, foldable and watch frames.
- **[Icons](/help/palette/icons/)** and **[Stickers](/help/palette/stickers/)**: searchable catalogues of glyphs and colour emoji.
- **Media**: [Image](/help/palette/tools/images/) (`9`) and the Tab Window, the [embeds](/help/palette/embed-elements/) (YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Figma, Google Docs and any website), and Avatar. Image, Avatar and the Hero only appear where image uploads are available.
- **[Data](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/)**: the charts and meters (Pie, Column, Line, Legend, Progress, Donut and Rating), then the [Table](/help/palette/tools/tables/).

## On other kinds of page

The palette follows the page you are working on, offering what that page is made of:

- A **slide** puts Write first, then Shapes, Media, Data, Concepts, Components (without the Header), Devices, Icons and Stickers. Its Popular adds a YouTube embed.

See [Working with Pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/) for the kinds of page.

## Elements the strip does not show

Some elements are not in any of the strip's categories, but the editor's [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) (`⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`) adds them by name, under **Add to canvas**:

- [Technology](/help/palette/technology/) icons, the full-colour marks for architecture pictures.
- The [Polygon](/help/palette/tools/drawing/), for outlines placed point by point.
- The Arrow (`A`) and the Line: see [Arrows](/help/palette/arrows/).

## Working with what you place

Each element behaves like any other once placed: select it, restyle it, multi-select it with others, and move it freely. Pick a tile and tap the canvas to drop the element at its default size, or drag to draw it at the size you want. `T` drops a text label and `N` a sticky note without opening the palette at all.

<Note>
  The palette is hidden for people with view-only access, since they can't add anything. See
  [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) for how the strip shows the categories and opens
  each one.
</Note>

## Guides for these tools

Several tools have their own walkthroughs:

- [Images](/help/palette/tools/images/) covers uploading, searching for and reusing pictures, and avatars.
- [Tables](/help/palette/tools/tables/) covers the editable grid: adding one, filling cells, and rows/columns.
- [Sticky Notes](/help/palette/tools/sticky-notes/) covers the coloured note card for short remarks.
- [Code Blocks](/help/palette/tools/code-blocks/) covers the monospace card for syntax-highlighted snippets.
- [Checklists](/help/palette/tools/checklists/) covers the card of checkable to-do rows you tick on the page.
- [Data and Chart Elements](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/) covers the **Data** category: Pie, Column and Line charts (each any of seven chart types), Legend, Progress, Donut and Rating.
- [Drawing with the Polygon](/help/palette/tools/drawing/) covers placing outlines point by point.

Arrows have their own guide too: see [Arrows](/help/palette/arrows/).
