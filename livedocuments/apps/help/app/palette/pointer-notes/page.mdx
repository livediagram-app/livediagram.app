A **Pointer Note** is a short note with a line out to a dot. Use it for a remark about one spot on a page: "stale for 60 s here", "this is where it fails", "rate limited". The dot pins to a point on a shape, so the note keeps pointing at the right place when things move.

It sits in the palette's [Write](/help/palette/write/) category, after the Sticky Note.

## Pointing it

Select the note and its dot shows a handle. Drag the handle to where the note is about:

- Let go near one of a shape's eight points (its corners and the middles of its edges) and the dot **attaches** there.
- Let go anywhere else and the dot stays where you left it.

From the keyboard, focus the handle and the arrow keys nudge the dot (hold **Shift** for bigger steps).

Once attached, moving, resizing or rotating the shape carries the note along. Moving the note itself leaves the dot on its point and the line re-runs, so you can tidy notes out of the way without losing what they point at. Copying or deleting the shape takes its pointer notes with it.

To free the dot, right-click the note and choose **Detach** in its **Pointer Note** section.

A pointer note always stays upright: it has no Rotation, since turning the note would swing its line off the point it marks.

## Styling it

A new note is amber, the colour of a remark. Its **Colours**, **Border** and text controls work as on any shape, and the line and dot take the border colour. Double-click to edit the words.

With the **Pulse** animation, the dot pulses too: a ring spreads from it, drawing the eye to the spot.
