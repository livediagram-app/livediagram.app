# Suggestions and tracked changes

Reviewing is how writing gets finished: someone proposes, the author decides. A **suggestion** is a change to an
article's writing ([Article pages](article-pages.md)) that is kept beside the text instead of changing it, until an
Editor **accepts** it (it becomes the text) or **rejects** it (it goes, the text as it was). People suggest in
**Suggesting** mode; a Participant on a **Suggest Only** link only ever suggests; an assistant's edits to an article
can arrive as suggestions too ([Pages for agents](../024-agents/pages-for-agents.md#suggestions)).

## Domain language

| Term                   | Means                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **suggestion**         | One proposed change by one author: an **insertion**, a **deletion**, a **format** change or a **paragraph break** put in or taken out (`ArticleSuggestion`). |
| **Suggesting**         | The mode, beside **Editing**, in which a person's changes to the writing are kept as suggestions.                                                            |
| **Suggest Only**       | A setting an Editor puts on a Participant link or on a person: their changes to articles are always suggestions.                                             |
| **accept**, **reject** | An Editor's decision on a suggestion: accepted, it becomes the text; rejected, it goes and the text stands as it was.                                        |
| **rejected view**      | The writing as if every suggestion were rejected: what exports, presenting, a Viewer and every agent read show.                                              |
| **change**             | An assistant's one write, whose suggestions share a change id (`g`), so they can be accepted together (**Accept All** for that change).                      |

A suggestion is never called a "revision" or a "tracked change" in the interface; the interface says **Suggestion**,
**Suggesting**, **Accept** and **Reject**.

## What a suggestion is

- **An insertion** is text put in: shown underlined in its author's colour on a soft tint of it.
- **A deletion** is text taken out but kept: shown struck through in its author's colour, its words muted. It stays
  stored until it is accepted, so it counts towards the block's 20,000 characters and the tab's 1.99 MB like any text.
- **A format change** is bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, inline code, superscript, subscript, a link, a text
  colour or a highlight put on or taken off: the text shows the new formatting with a dotted underline in its
  author's colour, and the suggestion's card says what changed ("Format: Bold, Italic"). Rejecting puts the old
  formatting back.
- **A paragraph break**: pressing Enter while suggesting splits the paragraph and marks the break as put in; deleting
  across a paragraph's start (Backspace at its start, a selection over two paragraphs) marks the break as taken out,
  leaving both paragraphs where they are. A small ¶ at the paragraph's start, underlined (put in) or struck (taken
  out) in the author's colour, shows it. Accepting a break taken out joins the paragraph onto the one before;
  rejecting a break put in does the same.
- **Every suggestion carries** its author's name and colour, when it was made, its id, and for an assistant's,
  **Generated by AI** and its change's id. Its author is the person's collab key
  ([Share roles](../013-workspace/share-roles.md#the-collab-key)), or `agent:` and the token id for an assistant.
- **One suggestion per stretch of typing**: letters typed next to your own insertion join it; a new spot starts a new
  one. Deleting your own insertion takes it out at once (it was never text). Someone else's insertion is not yours to
  delete while suggesting: the keys skip over it. Text already struck stays as it is.
- **What is not suggested**: a block's style (a heading, a list, a quote), alignment, to-do ticks, code blocks, a
  table's shape, footnotes, zones, dividers, page breaks, contents blocks and the article's style. While suggesting,
  a change to any of these is refused with a notice ("Suggesting changes words and their formatting. Switch to
  Editing to change this.", or for a Participant on Suggest Only, "Your changes here are suggestions: words and their
  formatting."), at most once every few seconds. Words in a table's cells are suggested like any words.

## Suggesting

- **The mode**: for an Editor the page toolbar opens with a mode menu, its button naming the mode in force
  (**Editing**, a pencil; **Suggesting**, a pencil with a line, in the brand tint). The menu holds **Editing**
  ("Change the writing directly") and **Suggesting** ("Your changes become suggestions to accept or reject"), then,
  under **Review**, the article's suggestion count ("6 suggestions", "No suggestions"), **Accept All** and **Reject
  All**. The mode is the person's, for every article on the tab, for as long as the editor is open; it starts at
  Editing. On a phone the button shows its glyph alone, and **Suggesting** fills it in solid brand colour so the two
  modes never look alike.
- **A Participant on Suggest Only** is always suggesting, with no mode to change: the role pill reads **Suggesting**
  ("You suggest changes; an Editor accepts or rejects them"), and a small **Comment** button shows in the page's right
  margin beside selected text (see "Comments on selected text").
- **Everyone else** writes as before: a Participant without Suggest Only writes straight in, as today; a Viewer writes
  nothing.

## Reviewing

- **The card**: with the caret inside a suggestion, or the pointer over it, a card shows under its first line (as a
  link's card does): a dot in the author's colour, their name, how long ago ("just now", "4 min ago", then the date),
  **Generated by AI** for an assistant's, then what it does ("Add "a quick test"", "Delete "slowly"", "Format: Bold",
  "Add a paragraph break", "Remove a paragraph break"), quoted text cut to 60 characters. An Editor's card has
  **Accept** (a tick) and **Reject** (a cross), and, for an assistant's change holding more than one suggestion,
  **Accept All** for that change ("Accept All 6"). Anyone else's card has no buttons.
- **Accept All** and **Reject All** (the mode menu's Review) decide every suggestion in the article at once, as one
  edit; each card's Accept and Reject decide one. Each decision is one edit and one undo step, and reaches everyone as
  any edit of the writing does, block by block ([Article pages](article-pages.md#collaboration)).
- **A held paragraph** ([Holding a paragraph](article-pages.md#holding-a-paragraph)) is decided once its writer lets
  go: Accept All and Reject All leave its suggestions and say so ("2 suggestions wait in paragraphs others are
  writing in").
- **Keyboard**: the card's buttons are focus stops; Escape closes it and puts the caret back.

## Comments on selected text

A margin note ([Comments and actions](article-pages.md#comments-and-actions)) sits beside a suggestion like any text,
so a suggestion and its discussion sit together. Anyone who may suggest may put a comment on selected text: an Editor
from the page toolbar as before, and a Participant on Suggest Only from the **Comment** button in the margin beside
the selection (⌘⌥M too). A Participant's margin note is a comment, never an action, signed with their name (the one
their cursor shows) like any comment.

## Suggest Only

- **Where it is set**: the Share dialog's **Suggestions** section (livedocuments' own section below Password
  Protection), for an Editor. It lists each Participant link (named by the tab it opens, or **Whole Document**, and
  when it was made), each with a **Suggest Only** switch, and the people who have joined through a Participant link
  (by their name), each with the same switch. A link turned on makes every Participant who opens it suggest; a person
  turned on suggests through any Participant link. Nothing else changes: the link's level is the index's, as always.
- **A Suggest Only link holds the person**: someone who opens a Suggest Only link (signed in, or a guest's browser)
  stays on Suggest Only on every later visit while that link is on, however they come back: without the link's code,
  or through another Participant link. Their row in the list shows on, "Suggest Only, from the Suggest Only link they
  opened". Turning their row off, or the link off, lets them go. Someone who also holds an Editor grant (their own
  invite or an Editor link) edits as an Editor whenever they open the document as one.
- **Assistants Suggest**: the same section's first switch, "An assistant’s edits to articles arrive as suggestions to
  accept or reject", off by default. On, every article write an assistant makes on the document is suggestions
  ([Pages for agents](../024-agents/pages-for-agents.md#suggestions)).
- **Stored on the document**, in its own room (`document.review`), never the index: the share codes, the people's
  collab keys (and names, to list them), the assistants' switch, and the people held by a Suggest Only link (their
  collab key and the link). A person is listed once they have joined as a Participant; at most 200 are kept (the most
  recent), at most 200 held, and at most 100 links are switched on.
- **Live**: a change reaches every open Participant at once; their writing switches mode from the next keystroke.

## The server is the rule

- **A Suggest Only Participant's writing** goes through the participant writing rule
  ([Share roles](../013-workspace/share-roles.md#integrity)) and then the **suggestion rule**: the article's rejected
  view after the change must be exactly the rejected view before it, every suggestion it adds must be its own (its
  collab key as author, never marked Generated by AI), and every suggestion by anyone else must be left exactly as it
  was (its text and fields). Margin note marks are the exception: a comment of its own may be put on text. A frame
  that breaks the rule is refused whole and the sender's screen reloads its copy of the tab, as any refused writing
  does. Its element changes are refused but for one: adding a margin note's comment marker.
- **Accept and reject are an Editor's**: a Participant's frame that takes away anyone else's suggestion is refused
  by the rule above; taking back its own insertion is allowed (it was never text).
- **What an agent and the API read is the rejected view**: an API token's read of a document or tab gets every article
  in its rejected view, and every agent view (`read_document`, the CLI's `article get` and views) and the Markdown
  writes it, so unaccepted text never reaches an assistant or a script. The editor's own reads keep the suggestions.

## Everywhere a page goes

- **Exports** (PDF, PNG, SVG, Word, Markdown, the MCP's preview images) show the rejected view: an article holding
  suggestions is set for the file from its rejected writing, not read off the screen.
- **Presenting** shows the rejected view's words: insertions are hidden, deletions read as plain text, and the marks,
  ¶s and cards are gone (a suggested paragraph break keeps its two paragraphs on screen). A format change keeps its new formatting while presenting (the screen draws the writing as
  it is laid out); an export sets the old.
- **A Viewer** reads the rejected view the same way: no suggestion shows.
- **Thumbnails and the Map** draw the writing as soft lines and show no text, so they read the screen as it is.
- **Find** searches the writing as it shows to the person, suggestions included; **Replace** is not offered to a
  Participant on Suggest Only.
- **Copy** takes the text as it shows (an insertion's words, a deletion's words); a paste never brings suggestion
  marks with it.
- **Version history** keeps suggestions as part of the tab, so restoring a version brings its suggestions back.

## Share roles

| Role                           | Suggestions                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Editor**                     | Suggests (Suggesting mode) or edits, accepts and rejects each or all, sets Suggest Only and Assistants Suggest in the Share dialog, comments on selected text.                                     |
| **Participant (Suggest Only)** | Suggests insertions, deletions, format changes and paragraph breaks, takes back its own insertions, comments on selected text; never accepts or rejects, never writes straight in, never replaces. |
| **Participant**                | Writes straight in, as before; sees suggestions and their cards (no buttons).                                                                                                                      |
| **Viewer**                     | Reads the rejected view; sees no suggestion.                                                                                                                                                       |

## Limits

- A suggestion's id is at most 64 characters, its author at most 64, its name at most 80; a run holds at most one
  suggestion. Suggestions count towards the article's limits like the text they mark (block, cell and tab sizes).
- `SUGGEST_ONLY_MAX_LINKS` 100, `SUGGEST_ONLY_MAX_PEOPLE` 200.

## Telemetry

- `Element · Changed · ArticleSuggesting` (the mode menu turned to Suggesting), `ArticleSuggestAccepted`,
  `ArticleSuggestRejected`, `ArticleSuggestAcceptAll`, `ArticleSuggestRejectAll`, `ArticleSuggestAcceptChange`.
- `Document · Shared · SuggestOnly` and `AssistantsSuggest` for the Share dialog's switches turned on.
- Never text, never a name.

## Non-goals

- Suggesting a block's style, alignment, a table's shape, zones, footnotes or the article's style.
- Suggestions on slides, infographics and canvas elements (an assistant's changes to those keep Show and Undo,
  [Agent changesets](../024-agents/agent-changesets.md#in-the-editor)).
- A list of every suggestion in a side panel, and replies on a suggestion itself (a margin note carries the talk).
