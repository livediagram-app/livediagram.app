# Favourites (removed)

Favourites are gone from livedocuments and every other app (owner, 2026-11-16, Work Tracker 1155). There is no star on a
document: no **Add to Favourites** or **Remove from Favourites** in its menu, no star beside its name in a row, a card or
the Explorer panel, and no **Favourites** page, sidebar row or panel section. Home's Most Used and Recent, Search and
folders are how a document is found again.

- An old `/explorer/favourites` address opens My Work, and a panel left on Favourites opens on My Work.
- A document kept only in This Browser carries no star either: an older local record's mark is ignored, and Take
  Offline and Sync move none.
- What the index keeps and why is in
  [one-explorer.md "Favourites removed"](../../../../liveworkspace/docs/specs/004-index/one-explorer.md#favourites-removed).
