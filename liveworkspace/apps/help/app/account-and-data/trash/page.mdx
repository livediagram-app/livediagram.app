When you delete a <Noun />, it is not gone straight away. It moves to the **Trash**, where it waits for **30 days**. After that it is removed for good. Until then you can bring it back with everything it had: its tabs and its share links.

<AppNote app="livediagram">That includes its slide deck and its comments.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">That includes its cards and its comments.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">That includes its comments.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">That includes its comments and history, and its stars.</AppNote>

This works the same whether you are signed in or working as a guest. The Trash is shared by every liveworkspace app, so choose **All** at the top of the page to see a deleted item from another app beside your <Noun plural />.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">The choice reads **All Apps**.</AppNote>

## Opening the Trash

Choose **Trash**, the last row of the **Library** in the Explorer sidebar's **More** group (open the Library if it is folded). It lists your deleted items, and the deleted items of the teams you belong to: any member of a team can restore its items or delete them for good, just as any member can delete them.

You can also delete a <Noun /> by dragging it onto **Trash** in the sidebar: the row reads **Drop to Trash** while you drag, the <Noun /> moves there when you let go, and dragging one of your folders there asks you first.

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  The Trash works for guests too, and on deployments without accounts. It lists everything you can restore, grouped: **Your designs** (the ones you deleted from your own space), **one group per team** you belong to (team designs go to that team's Trash, and any member can restore them or delete them for good), and **Offline** (designs kept Offline live only in this browser, so their Trash does too: open one to restore it or delete it for good). Each design shows when it was deleted and how many days it has left. One that is past its 30 days but not yet removed reads "Removed at the next clean-up": removal runs once a day, so a design usually stays for up to a day longer.

## Restoring

Choose **Restore** on a <Noun />'s row in the Trash (it is in the row's **⋯** menu too); it needs no confirmation, because it destroys nothing. The <Noun /> goes back to the folder it was in, its share links work again, and anyone you shared it with can open it as before.

<AppNote app="livedocuments,liveplanner,livehuddle">Restore is in the row's **⋯** menu.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  If that folder has been deleted since, the <Noun /> goes to the folder that held it (the root of
  **My Work**, or of its team, for a top-level folder). If you open a deleted design by its address
  and it is in your own Trash, the card that says it was deleted offers **Restore** too, with the
  days it has left.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle,livedesign">
  Deleted the wrong one a moment ago? The note that says it was deleted has an **Undo** that puts it
  straight back.
</AppNote>

## Deleting for good

Choose **Delete Forever** on a <Noun />'s row (or in its menu) to remove it now, or **Empty Trash** at the top of a group to remove everything in it: your own <Noun plural />, or one team's. While the Trash shows only <AppName />'s items, Empty Trash removes only <Noun plural />, and deleted items from other liveworkspace apps stay in the Trash; with **All** chosen, it removes every app's deleted items in that group. Both ask you to confirm, and neither can be undone.

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livehuddle">
  Empty Trash removes only <Noun plural />; deleted items from other liveworkspace apps wait in
  their own app's Trash.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  With **liveplanner** chosen at the top of the page, Empty Trash removes only plans; with **All
  Apps**, it removes every app's deleted items in that group.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  Delete Forever removes it with its history. Emptying a team's Trash empties it for the whole team.
</AppNote>

A tab that is also in another <Noun /> is never lost when a <Noun /> is deleted for good: it stays in the other <Noun />, exactly where it was.

## While Something Is in the Trash

A deleted <Noun /> disappears from your lists, Recent, search, Home and the Inbox. Its share links stop opening it, and anyone who still had it open loses access within a few minutes.

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  Anyone opening one of its share links sees that it was deleted, and anyone who still had it open
  is told so, and can no longer save changes to it.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  A deleted space disappears from your lists, Recent, search and Home (no Inbox).
</AppNote>

## Empty Work Goes to the Trash

A <Noun /> with nothing on any of its tabs, left unchanged for **30 days**, moves to the Trash on its own. Its row in the Trash says it went there because it was empty, so you can tell it apart from one you deleted, and it waits the usual 30 days there before it is removed for good. Nothing is sent to tell you: there was nothing in it to lose. Restore it like any other and it comes back as it was. It isn't moved again unless it is changed and then left empty for another 30 days.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">A plan counts as empty when it has no cards or sheets either.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,liveplanner,livedesign">
  Team <Noun plural /> go to the team's Trash, where any member can restore them.
</AppNote>

## Offline Work

[Offline <Noun plural />](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) are kept only in this browser, so they have no Trash. Deleting one from the [Offline](/offline) page asks you first, then removes it for good. They are never moved to the Trash for being empty.

<Tip>
  Taking your own <Noun /> offline is a move, not a delete, so it never goes to the Trash. (When a
  teammate takes a team <Noun /> offline, it leaves the team, so for the team it goes to the team's
  Trash.) [Deleting your account](/help/account-and-data/deleting-your-data/#deleting-your-account)
  removes your own <Noun plural /> straight away, the Trash included.
</Tip>

## For scripts and AI tools

Deleting a <Noun /> through the [API](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) also moves it to the Trash. A script using the API can delete one for good, when it asks for a permanent delete.

<AppNote app="livediagram,liveplanner,livedocuments,livedesign">
  An [AI tool](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/) that deletes a <Noun /> moves it to the Trash
  too. It can list your Trash and restore a <Noun /> from it, but it can never delete one for good.
</AppNote>
