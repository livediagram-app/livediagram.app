Signing in to <AppName /> is entirely optional. The editor works without an account, so there is never a sign-in wall in front of it. An account simply adds something useful on top: it keeps your <Noun plural /> with you across browsers and devices instead of being tied to one [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/), unlocks [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/), and lets you create [API tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) to call the API from your own scripts.

## Creating an account

Your <AppName /> account is a **liveworkspace** account: one sign-in for <AppName /> and the other liveworkspace apps. Choosing **Sign In** at the top right of the editor or the Explorer (or opening `/sign-in` or `/get-started`) takes you to the liveworkspace sign-in page at liveworkspace.app. Enter your email and we send you a 6-digit code, or continue with Google; type the code in and you are back in <AppName />, signed in. There is no password, and there is no separate sign-up step: the first time you sign in with an email, that is your account made.

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livehuddle">
  Where the sign-in page offers **Continue with Google**, you can sign in with your Google account instead of the code.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Choose **Continue with Google**, or enter your email and type in the 6-digit code we send you. **Create Account** asks for your first and last name as well; signing in with an email that has no account yet makes one too, and asks your name after the code.
</AppNote>

If you are already signed in to another liveworkspace app in the same browser, you are signed in to <AppName /> too, without a click: open the editor, the Explorer or the <AppName /> home page and your name is already at the top right (on the home page it can take a moment after the page loads). Signing out of any of them signs you out of them all.

<AppNote app="livedocuments,liveplanner">
  If you do press **Sign In**, it takes you straight back with no code to type.
</AppNote>

Accounts are free, with no paid tier and nothing to upgrade. After signing in from the sign-in page you land on the [Explorer](/explorer) to see your <Noun plural />; after signing up you land on the new-<Noun /> flow to start building. If you used the **Sign In** link in the editor or the Explorer, you go back to where you were instead.

Once you are signed in, your picture (or initials) and first name replace the **Sign In** link. Click it for the account menu (your name, **Explorer**, **Settings** and **Sign Out**): **Settings** opens Settings, whose **Profile** is where your name and colour, your time zone, your sign-in methods, your [Google Drive](/help/account-and-data/google-drive/) connection, **Sign Out** and **Delete Account** live, and the menu's own **Sign Out** signs you out of <AppName /> and of every other liveworkspace app in this browser, then returns you to the home page. The next time you sign in you are asked for a code or Google again, so you can pick a different account. In **Profile** you can change the display name and presence colour collaborators see, in every liveworkspace app; your email is shown but can't be changed there.

**Delete Account** removes everything you have in <AppName />, closes your liveworkspace account and signs you out. Closing it signs you out of the other liveworkspace apps too, and you can no longer sign in to them with it.

## When sign-in is not working

If sign-in is failing, see [Cannot Sign In](/help/troubleshooting/cannot-sign-in/). You can keep working as a guest in the meantime.

## Your Guest Work Comes With You

The best part of signing up is that you keep the work you have already done. When you create an account, everything you built as a guest comes with it, so nothing is lost.

Before you sign in, new <Noun plural /> start [in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/), saved only in this browser. When you sign in, a **Move <Noun plural capital /> in This Browser to Your Account?** dialog lists every one of them: every one is ticked, so press **Move** to bring them all across, or untick any you would rather keep in this browser. **Not Now** leaves them in this browser, and we only ask again when there are more. If one fails to move, it stays in this browser and a follow-up dialog names it. You can also move one any time with **Sync <Noun capital />** in the Explorer or the Share dialog. <Noun plural capital /> in This Browser belong to the browser, not to an account, so after you sign out they are offered to whoever signs in next on it.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Plans kept in **Browser Storage** are the ones that were never on the server.
</AppNote>

Anything you already put on the server as a guest (a <Noun /> you shared, synced, or saved to **<AppName />**) moves automatically. The migration moves your guest content, including those <Noun plural />, folders, your Shared With Me list, images and saved preferences (unless your account already has its own), from your per-browser guest id over to your new account. From then on, that content is owned by your account and follows you to any browser or device where you sign in.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  Your shape libraries move too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,liveplanner">
  liveworkspace does the move, so it covers every liveworkspace app at once, and a canvas you made as a guest on livediagram comes across too. It happens in the background as you sign in and never holds the sign-in up.
</AppNote>

<Figure caption="On sign-up, your guest work moves from the per-browser id over to your new account.">

</Figure>

The process runs the first time you are signed in in a browser that holds guest work, and is safe to repeat: a second attempt simply moves nothing. Once you are signed in, the Explorer's sign-in nudge disappears.

Only the guest id in **this** browser moves. Guest <Noun plural /> you made in a different browser or on another device belong to that browser's guest id: open <AppName /> there and sign in with the same account to bring them across too. [<Noun plural capital /> in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) stay in their browser until you move them, because they were never on the server.

<Tip>
  Build freely as a guest, then sign up when you want your <Noun plural /> everywhere. The hand-off is
  automatic, so there is no manual import to do.
</Tip>
