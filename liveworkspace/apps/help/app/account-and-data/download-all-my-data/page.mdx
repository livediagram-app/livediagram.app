When you want all of your work in one place, to keep a backup or to move on, you do not have to export <Noun plural /> one at a time. **Download All My Data** builds a single zip of everything you own, across <AppName /> and the other liveworkspace apps, and emails you a link when it is ready.

## Starting a download

You need to be [signed in](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/). A guest owns nothing that outlives their browser, so there is nothing to download until you have an account.

1. Open **Settings** in <AppName /> (or in any liveworkspace app, or on liveworkspace.app) and go to **Your Data**.
2. Choose **Download All My Data**. The row shows its progress while the zip is built, and you can close Settings and carry on working.
3. When it is ready, we email you a link, and the row shows the size and when the link lapses, with **Download** and a button to make a new one.

The link works for 3 days, and only while you are signed in as the address it was sent to. After that, make a new one from the same row. You can have one download building at a time. If one fails, the row says so and offers **Try Again**.

## What the zip holds for your <Noun plural />

Each <Noun /> you own gets its own folder, named after the <Noun /> and followed by its id, so two with the same name never collide. Inside it:

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
- **The space's file**, a `.livehuddle` file with the whole space in it. It is the same file the [Google Drive](/help/account-and-data/google-drive/) mirror writes, so you can open it with livehuddle again and use **Import as a copy** to make a new space from it.
- **A picture of each tab**, as a PNG, in tab order and numbered (`01 Agenda.png`, `02 Ideas.png`), for up to 20 tabs. A tab with nothing on it has no picture.
- **Any comments you wrote** in it, gathered with the rest in `comments.json` and `comments.csv` at the top of the zip. livehuddle has no comments of its own now, so these are only ones a space kept from before they were removed, beside your comments from the other liveworkspace apps. Each row names the space and tab, the thread, whether it is resolved, when it was written and what it said.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveknowledge">
- **The Knowledgebase's file**, a `.liveknowledge` file with the whole Knowledgebase in it, its pages included. It is the same file the [Google Drive](/help/account-and-data/google-drive/) mirror writes, so a copy of it is a backup of the whole Knowledgebase.
- **Any comments you wrote** on its pages, gathered with the rest in `comments.json` and `comments.csv` at the top of the zip, beside your comments from the other liveworkspace apps. Each row names the item, the thread, whether it is resolved, when it was written and what it said.
</AppNote>

The zip also holds a `README.txt` saying what each folder is, your profile, and the images you uploaded. If you use the other liveworkspace apps, their canvases, plans, documents, designs, Spaces and knowledgebases are in the same zip, each in a folder for its app.

## What is not in it

- **<Noun plural capital /> shared with you.** Only <Noun plural /> you own are included, and so are only the comments you wrote inside them. A <Noun /> someone else owns is theirs to download, and so are the comments you left in it.
- **Anything in the Trash.** [Restore it](/help/account-and-data/trash/) first if you want it in the zip.

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  - **Shape libraries.** livehuddle no longer offers them, and the download does not read them. Any you imported earlier stay stored until you delete your account.
</AppNote>

- **Anything over the size cap.** A download stops adding files at 2 GiB, or at a set number of items, and the README and Settings say how many were left out. A <Noun /> that cannot be read at the time is named in the README, and the download carries on without it.

<Tip>
  A download is a copy, not a deletion. Your <Noun plural /> stay where they are. To remove them, see
  [Deleting Your Data](/help/account-and-data/deleting-your-data/).
</Tip>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  For a single tab as an image, a PDF or a portable file, see [Exporting Spaces](/help/account-and-data/exporting-spaces/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveknowledge">
  For one page as Markdown, PDF or Word, or a whole Knowledgebase as a folder of Markdown, see [Exporting a Knowledgebase](/help/knowledgebase/exporting-a-knowledgebase/).
</AppNote>
