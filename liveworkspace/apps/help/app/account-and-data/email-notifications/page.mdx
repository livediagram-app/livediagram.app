## What we might send

There are four groups:

- **Tips and check-ins.** A short welcome when you first sign in, a nudge a few days later if you have not made a <Noun /> yet, a couple of getting-started notes over your first two weeks, and a friendly nudge if you have been away for about four weeks. The welcome goes straight away; everything after it can be turned off.
- **Milestones.** A small celebration when you hit a milestone: your first share link, and your tenth <Noun />. Each comes once.
- **Notifications about your work.** Each has its own switch:
  - Someone opens a <Noun /> you shared for the first time (at most one email per <Noun /> every 15 minutes). Only people signed in to an account count, and never you.
  - Someone accepts or declines an invite to a team you are an admin of.
- **Account messages.** A confirmation when you delete your account. It is tied to something you did, so it always sends.

Tips and check-ins come from liveworkspace, your account across its apps, so you get one series of tips however many apps you use. Team invitations, the note telling a team's admins that someone accepted or declined one, and the heads-up in the last week before one of your [API tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) expires come from liveworkspace rather than from <AppName /> too.

</AppNote>

**When they arrive.** Tips and check-ins, invite reminders and the API token heads-up wait for your daytime: they arrive Monday to Friday, between 9am and 9pm in your time zone. **Your Time Zone**, in **Settings**, **Profile**, is **Automatic** (the zone your browser is set to) until you pick another; until we know it, UK time is used. Everything else, like a team invite, comes straight away.

## What an email contains

Notification emails keep your work out of your inbox where they can. Nothing from the <Noun />, and never a picture of it, goes into an email beyond what is said here.

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  A joined-<Noun /> email tells you who opened which <Noun />, with a link to open it.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  A comment notification tells you who commented and on which <Noun />, with a link to open it, but not what the comment said. Two carry a little more, because the words are addressed to you: a **mention** email quotes the comment you were mentioned in (cut to about 280 characters), and an **assigned action** email names the action and includes the start of its description (up to 200 characters). Both link to the <Noun />. An assigned-action email sent to someone who can't open the <Noun /> yet leaves the <Noun /> out and links to <AppName /> instead.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,liveplanner">
  A comment notification tells you who commented and on which <Noun />, with a link to open it, but not what the comment said. A mention is the exception, because it was written to you: it quotes the comment (cut short if it is long). An assigned action carries its name and the start of its description, and names the <Noun /> only if you can open it.
</AppNote>

## Turning them off

Open **Settings** and choose **Notifications**, under the **Account** heading (the account menu's **Settings** opens Settings on **Profile**, just above it). The **Email** section has one switch per optional kind: **Someone Comments on My Work**, **Someone Mentions Me in a Comment**, **Someone Assigns Me an Action**, **Someone Responds to a Team Invite**, <AppName />'s own **Someone Joins My <Noun capital />**, **Tips and Check-Ins**, and **Milestones**. They are all on until you turn them off. Every switch but **Someone Joins My <Noun capital />** belongs to your account, so it covers your work in every liveworkspace app. Most of the switches are shared by every liveworkspace app, and say **All apps** under their description: turning one off here turns it off in the others too. The whole row is the switch, so click anywhere on it.

</AppNote>

</AppNote>

<Tip>
  Every email you can turn off carries a "Manage your notifications" link in its footer that opens
  these switches, and mail apps that show a native unsubscribe button point at the same place. Only
  the welcome email has no link.
</Tip>

The switches are saved to your account, so they apply wherever you are signed in, and the change takes effect before the next email would go out. Account messages (the invite and its reminders, the deletion confirmation, and the token-expiry reminder) are not on this list, because they confirm something rather than nudge you.

As a guest, the same place shows a short note asking you to sign in instead of the switches (on a deployment that sends email). **In-Editor Notifications**, above them, is a different thing: it controls the brief confirmations inside the editor and never sends email.

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The **In the Editor** section above the switches never sends email: **In-Editor Notifications** controls the brief confirmations inside the editor, **Join and Leave Sounds** plays a soft chime when someone arrives in the space you have open (and a softer one when they leave), and **Desktop Notifications** shows a notification on your computer when someone nudges you while the tab is in the background (your browser asks first).
</AppNote>
