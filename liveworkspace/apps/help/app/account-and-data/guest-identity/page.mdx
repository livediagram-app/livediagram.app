## Your per-browser id

The first time you visit, your browser takes a quick trip to the liveworkspace accounts site and comes back with a guest token, which it keeps in `localStorage`. The token names your guest id and lasts 45 days, renewed when you visit in its last 15 days. Everything of yours on the server as a guest (<Noun plural /> you share or sync, folders, images) is owned by that id, which is how the app knows which content to show you when you come back. The editor sends the token with each request; you never see or type it.

The trip to liveworkspace.app is a redirect you may glimpse for a moment, with nothing to fill in. liveworkspace.app remembers which guest this browser is, so a fresh token names the same guest, and a guest who comes back now and then keeps their identity.

Your guest id is liveworkspace's, so the other liveworkspace apps you use in the same browser know you as the same guest: a <Noun /> you make in <AppName /> and one you make in another liveworkspace app belong to the same guest, and both appear in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) when you choose **All Apps**.

New <Noun plural /> you make as a guest start [offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/), saved only in this browser, so nothing you try out is sent to our servers until you choose to. To share one, open **Share** and press **Sync <Noun capital />**: it uploads under your guest id and the Share options appear. A <Noun /> you create inside a folder goes to the server straight away.

Two things follow from this:

- **It is per browser, not per person.** The id lives in one browser on one device. Open <AppName /> in a different browser or on another device and you get a fresh guest id, so your earlier <Noun plural /> will not appear there.
- **It is not cross-device.** Guest content does not sync between devices, because there is no account tying them together yet.

As a guest you still get the core of <AppName />: saving, share links and real-time collaboration all work, once a <Noun /> is synced. A few things need an address or an account to attach to, so they ask you to sign in: [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/), [API tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/), [email notifications](/help/account-and-data/email-notifications/) and the [Google Drive](/help/account-and-data/google-drive/) mirror.

## What this means for your data

Because your guest content is tied to a single browser, clearing that browser's storage, or using private browsing that discards it, deletes your <Noun plural /> kept Offline and means the app can no longer recognise you as the owner of the ones on the server. Those are still there, until they are [deleted after 400 days](#how-long-guest-work-is-kept), but nothing in a fresh browser can claim them back. Treat your guest <Noun plural /> as living in that browser.

  removes the identity.
</AppNote>

Share links are the exception: anyone holding one, including you in another browser, can still open that <Noun /> with the link's role. They just won't be its owner there.

## How long guest work is kept

Your guest id stays alive for as long as you keep using any liveworkspace app in this browser. If 400 days (about 13 months) go by without a visit, the id can never be handed back to you, so everything it owns on the server is deleted for good: your <Noun plural />, your folders and your Trash. That includes <Noun plural /> you shared, so their links stop working too. <Noun plural capital /> kept Offline are not touched, because they were never on the server.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Plans kept **Offline** are not touched, because they were never on the server.
</AppNote>

To keep your work for longer, [sign in](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/). Your <Noun plural /> move into the account, and an account's <Noun plural /> are never deleted this way.

If you want your <Noun plural /> to follow you across browsers and devices, [signing in](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/) is the answer. When you sign in, whether to a new account or an existing one, the <Noun plural /> of yours on the server move into it automatically, and we offer to move the ones kept Offline too, so you do not lose the work you have already done.
