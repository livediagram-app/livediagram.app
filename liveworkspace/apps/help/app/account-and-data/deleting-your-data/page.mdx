You own your <Noun plural />, and removing one is straightforward. It helps to know how your data is scoped first, because that determines what "your data" means whether you are working as a guest or signed in.

## Deleting One Item

You can delete a <Noun /> from the [Explorer](/explorer/recent) or from the editor. Open the <Noun />'s **⋯** menu (on its row or card, or in the Current <Noun capital /> section of the Explorer panel) and choose **Delete <Noun capital />**. It goes straight away, without a question, because nothing is lost yet: a notification naming the <Noun /> ("Moved “Roadmap” to the Trash.") appears with **Undo**, which puts it back where it was. It stays for about ten seconds, longer while your pointer is over it; after that the <Noun /> is still in the Trash. Deleting the <Noun /> you have open takes you to the Explorer, where the same **Undo** appears.

Deleting a <Noun /> moves it to the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), where it waits for 30 days before it is removed for good, along with the content that belongs only to it, such as its tabs. While it waits, its share links stop working and it disappears from your lists, and you can restore it with everything it had. To remove it straight away, delete it permanently from the Trash.

A copy of a tab you added to another <Noun /> is that <Noun />'s own, so it stays there.

[<Noun plural capital /> in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) have no Trash: deleting one asks you first, then removes it for good.

If you have organised your work into [folders](/help/explorer/folders/), you can also tidy up by moving <Noun plural /> between folders or removing folders, separately from deleting the <Noun plural /> themselves.

## Deleting your account

When you are signed in, open **Settings**, choose **Profile** (under **Account**), and choose **Delete Account** under **Danger Zone**. The account menu's **Settings** opens Settings there. Because this cannot be undone, you confirm it twice: type your email address and choose **Email Me a Code**, then enter the 6-digit code we email you (the same kind of code you sign in with) and choose **Delete Account**. The code works for 10 minutes, so nobody using a computer you left signed in can delete your account without your inbox.

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  To keep a copy first, **Your Data**, also under **Account**, downloads everything you own as one zip ([Downloading Everything at Once](/help/account-and-data/exporting-documents/#downloading-everything-at-once)).
</AppNote>

Deleting your account is not only about <AppName />: it closes your liveworkspace account, which every liveworkspace app shares. It removes from the server, straight away:

- every <Noun /> you own, including anything already in the Trash, with its tabs and share links;
- your folders, images and preferences;
- your stars, your history and activity, and any [community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/) posts;
- your [API tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/), which stops any script or connected AI tool at once;
- your [Google Drive](/help/account-and-data/google-drive/) connection (liveworkspace's access is revoked at Google, and the files already in your Drive stay yours);
- your liveworkspace account itself, and with it **everything you own in every other liveworkspace app**: your canvases, plans, documents, designs and spaces in livediagram, liveplanner, livedocuments, livedesign and livehuddle, the Trash included, deleted for good the same way.

  A <Noun />'s comments are removed with it, in the Trash and from the server.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Every image you uploaded, in any liveworkspace app, goes with it, and so do your [workbench](/help/developers/workbenches/) pairings and sessions, which closes any workbench you had open.

<AppNote app="livehuddle">

</AppNote>

<Noun plural capital /> in a [team's](/help/collaboration/teams/) shared library are not deleted with you: they pass to a remaining member, so the team keeps its work. A team you were the last member of is deleted, and its <Noun plural />, which are all yours by then, are deleted with your account. If email is set up, you get a short confirmation once it is done.

Your <AppName /> data goes first, then the account. If closing the account fails, <AppName /> says so and you stay signed in, so choosing **Delete Account** again finishes it (with the same code while it lasts, or a new one). ## How your data is scoped

Where your data lives depends on how you are identified:

- **As a guest**, new <Noun plural /> start [offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/), saved only in this browser, and any you share or sync are owned on the server by your [per-browser id](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/), stored in that one browser. Your data is scoped to that browser, so clearing the browser's storage removes the app's link to your guest content, and using a different browser or device shows a fresh, empty workspace.
- **When signed in**, your <Noun plural /> are owned by your account and synced across every device where you sign in. Deleting a <Noun /> removes it everywhere, because the account is the single source of truth.

<AppNote app="livedocuments,liveplanner">
  As a guest, your new <Noun plural /> are kept in this browser until you sync them; the ones on the server are owned by your [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/), which liveworkspace gives this one browser. Clearing the browser's site data for liveworkspace.app and <code><AppName />.app</code> removes the browser's link to your guest content.
</AppNote>

A guest has no account to delete. To remove what you created as a guest, delete the <Noun plural /> you no longer want and empty the Trash. Clearing the browser's storage deletes your <Noun plural /> in This Browser, but only removes this browser's record of your guest identity: <Noun plural /> you shared or synced stay on the server, out of reach from a fresh browser, so delete them first if you want them gone.

Guest <Noun plural /> on the server are not kept forever: 400 days after your last visit, everything your guest identity owns is deleted for good, shared <Noun plural /> included ([how long guest work is kept](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/#how-long-guest-work-is-kept)).

<Tip>
  If you plan to clear your browser, sign in first if you want to keep anything. Your guest <Noun plural /> on the server move into the account on sign-up. Ones in This Browser move only if you accept the offer to move them after signing in, so do that before you clear the browser.
</Tip>
