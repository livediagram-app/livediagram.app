Your work is yours to take with you. **Your Data**, in Settings, makes one zip of everything you own across livediagram and the other liveworkspace apps, so you have a full copy whenever you want one.

## Making a Download

1. Sign in, then open **Settings** and choose **Your Data** under **Account**. It is the same in every liveworkspace app's Settings and on liveworkspace.app.
2. Press **Download All My Data**.
3. The zip is built in the background, and the row shows how far it has got. You can close Settings: we email you a link when it is ready.

When it is ready, the row shows its size and when the link stops working, with **Download** and **Make a New One**. The link works for 3 days, and only while you are signed in as the account it was made for. If a download fails, press **Try Again**.

## What Is in the Zip

- Every canvas you own, in its own folder, as a livediagram file you can bring back, with a picture of each tab.
- What you own in the other apps too: plans, documents, designs and spaces.
- The comments you wrote in your own work, as a list and a spreadsheet.
- Your profile, and every image you uploaded.
- A README that says what each folder holds and how to bring a file back.

Canvases shared with you by someone else aren't in it, and neither is anything in the Trash.

## A Team's Data

If you are an admin of a team, **Your Teams** lists it with **Download Team Data**. A team's zip holds everything the team owns and every comment in it. It holds no images, since each image belongs to whoever uploaded it, and their own download carries it.

To get a single canvas out instead, see [Exporting Diagrams](/help/account-and-data/exporting-diagrams/).
