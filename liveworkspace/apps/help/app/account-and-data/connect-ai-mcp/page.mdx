## You need an account

Connecting an AI tool is a **signed-in-only** feature, the same as [API tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) and [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/). The editor, sharing, and real-time collaboration stay account-free for guests, but driving your <Noun plural /> programmatically is an advanced, opt-in capability that needs an account.

## Connecting

In your AI tool, add a connector (an MCP server) pointing at **<code>https://mcp.<AppName />.app/mcp</code>**. [Connecting AI Tools](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/ai-clients/) has the steps for Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT and others. The first time, your tool opens a <AppName /> page titled **Connect** followed by the tool's name (you'll sign in first if you aren't already). It says which site the access will be sent to: only continue if you recognise it. Anyone can register a tool under any name, so every tool but the CLI shows **Unverified App** beside its name. Choose **Connect** to approve, or **Cancel**.

Approving creates an [API token](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) behind the scenes and hands it to your tool. From then on the connection just works. The token is an ordinary API token: it appears with your other tokens in **Settings**, **Developers**, **API Tokens** (in any liveworkspace app, or on [liveworkspace.app](https://www.liveworkspace.app/explorer?settings=tokens)), named after the tool, and you can revoke it there any time to disconnect the tool. Like all tokens, it lasts six months; reconnecting the tool then creates a fresh one.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  You get a reminder in the token's last week (by email, where email is set up).
</AppNote>

### From Claude's Connector Directory

Once <AppName /> is listed in Claude's connector directory, you won't need the address. In Claude, on the web or in the desktop app, open **Settings**, then **Connectors**, browse the directory, find **<AppName />** and choose **Connect**. The same <AppName /> approval page opens, and everything below works the same way. Until it is listed there, add it as a custom connector with the address above.

## What the AI can do

The connection gives your AI tool these tools:

- **Find <Noun plural capital />**: search your <Noun plural /> by name, across your personal library and the shared libraries of every [team](/help/collaboration/teams/) you belong to.
- **Read a <Noun />**: get a tab's contents as a text outline, and a picture of it when the AI asks for one.
- **List templates**: browse the same hand-tuned templates the editor's template starts offer.
- **Create a <Noun />**: turn a request into a new <Noun />, with one tab or several. A tab can start from a template, and the AI then fills in your content.
- **Add a tab**: add another tab to an existing <Noun />. Tabs can start from a template too.
- **Update a <Noun />**: rework a whole tab or make a small adjustment.
- **Share a <Noun />**: turn a <Noun /> into a [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) to hand to someone. Unless you ask for another level, the AI makes a **Participant** link.
- **Rename a <Noun />** and **Delete a <Noun />**: tidy your library from the AI tool, a whole <Noun /> or a single tab. Deleting a <Noun /> moves it to the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/); deleting a tab removes it for good.
- **List the Trash** and **Restore a <Noun />**: bring back something deleted by mistake, by you or by the AI.
- **Archive a <Noun />** and **Move a <Noun /> out of Archived**: put away a <Noun /> you own, so it leaves every list and its share links stop working, and bring it back when you need it. Finding <Noun plural /> can list the archived ones too.
- **List threads** and **Change threads**: read [comment threads](/help/collaboration/comments/), start one, reply, resolve and reopen threads, and edit or delete the AI's own comments. Every comment the AI writes says **Generated by AI** above it, and it can't change or delete a comment a person wrote.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">
  - **Search icons** and **List images**: find an [icon](/help/palette/icons/) by what it shows, and find a picture you uploaded to your [image gallery](/help/explorer/image-gallery/), so the AI can put them on the <Noun />.
  - **Upload images** and **Delete images**: add a picture the AI made or found (a chart, a render, a logo at a web address) to your [image gallery](/help/explorer/image-gallery/) so it can go straight onto the <Noun />, and remove pictures you no longer need. A picture the AI uploads shows **Made by AI** in the gallery. It won't remove a picture that is still on one of your <Noun plural /> unless you tell it to, because there it would turn into a broken picture.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">
  Templates are the hand-tuned [template library](/help/canvas/templates/). The comments the AI reads are a <Noun />'s threads, and it can comment on an element. With a link that only lets you view, it can read the threads but not write in them.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  - Create: ask for "a retrospective" and the AI can lay down the curated scaffold, then fill in your content.
  - Add a tab: a tab is its own canvas, for example a detail view of one part.
  - Share: the **Participant** link lets people add stickies, write and vote, but not reshape the board.
  - Some tools list each canvas tool twice, once under an older _diagram_ name marked deprecated: they do the same thing.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">

  - **Change pages** and **Write an article**: make [Pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/) pages. Ask for a deck and the AI adds slides, from the same slide layouts the page panel offers, then fills in their text; ask for an infographic and it lays one out. Ask it to write something up and it writes an [article](/help/pages/articles/), in Markdown, which flows onto as many pages as it needs when you open it. Ask for notes for a talk and it writes a stack of [flashcards](/help/pages/flashcards/), one card at a time. It can also read an article back, edit it and write it again.

<AppNote app="livedesign">

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  The connection gives your AI tool 33 tools. The ones above are `find_projects`, `read_project`, `list_templates`, `create_project`, `add_tab`, `update_project`, `share_project`, `rename_project`, `delete_project`, `list_trash`, `restore_project`, `archive_project` and `unarchive_project`. Here they work like this:

  - **List templates**: the same templates **Start Planning** offers: boards, spreadsheets, forms and dashboards.
  - **Create a plan**: ask for "a kanban board" or "a budget sheet" and the AI starts from the ready-made one, then fills in your content. It can also build a dashboard over the plan's other tabs.
  - **Add a tab**: from a template or as a dashboard.
  - **Update a plan**: rebuild a tab, such as a dashboard, or make a small change to what a tab holds.
  - **Share a plan**: the **Participant** link lets people add and move cards, write in Sheet cells, comment and vote, but not change a board's columns or settings.

  **Boards and cards**

  - **List items** (`list_items`) and **Change items** (`change_items`): read and update the [cards](/help/plan/cards/) on a plan's boards (add a task, move it to another column, change its fields). List items also names the [widgets](/help/plan/boards/#widgets) in each board's header, so the AI knows what a board already shows before it changes them. The AI names things the way your board shows them, so it says "In Progress" or "Bug" or "Severity", and liveplanner refuses a column, card type or field the plan doesn't have instead of filing a card where no board shows it. To empty a field, the AI lists it in `clear` (`"clear": ["Blocked By"]`); a field that holds several values, such as Blocked By, also clears when its value is `[]` or `null`. The AI can [flag a card](/help/plan/cards/#flag-a-card) for your attention with `"flagged": true` and take the flag off with `"flagged": false` (or `"clear": ["flagged"]`), and List items shows `"flagged": true` on every flagged card. When you [hand cards to the AI](/help/plan/cards/#hand-a-card-to-an-ai-assistant), it lists just those with `handedToMe`, takes one with the assignee `"me"`, and gives each back to you with `hand_back` when it is done. The **Work my cards** prompt asks it to do all of that in one go.
  - **Upload an image** (`upload_image`): add a picture for a plan's cards, from a public web address or the picture itself, into your Image Gallery. Change items then places it on a card's Image field or its attachments.
  - **List threads** (`list_threads`) and **Change threads** (`change_threads`): read a card's [comment threads](/help/plan/cards/#comment-threads), start a new one, reply, resolve and reopen them, and edit or delete the AI's own comments.
  - **List rules** (`list_rules`) and **Change rules** (`change_rules`): read a plan's [Automations](/help/plan/automations/#agents-and-the-cli) rules and their Audit Trail, and add, change, turn on or off, copy, delete, test and run them. The AI needs you to be an Editor of the whole plan, and a read-only connection can only list them.
  - **Add a board** (`add_board`) and **Change a board** (`change_board`): put a [board](/help/plan/boards/) on a tab, from a ready-made one (Kanban, Sprint, Bug Triage...) or with the columns you name, and later rename it, change its columns, choose which card types it shows, or add and remove the [widgets](/help/plan/boards/#widgets) in its header (a field widget by its name, such as Budget Sum).
  - **Import a board** (`import_board`): bring a Trello board's JSON export, or an Asana, Jira or Linear CSV export, in as a new Board tab or a new plan, as the editor's [Import dialog](/help/tabs/import-tabs/) does. People are matched to the plan's team by email when you are in that team, and by name otherwise. Ask for a preview first to see the columns, fields and what would be left out.
  - **A whole plan's <code>.liveplanner</code> file** is the editor's alone: the AI tools can import a board from another tool, but they can't import or export a whole plan. Use **Export** › **Whole Plan** and the Import dialog's **liveplanner Plan** row ([Importing into a Tab](/help/tabs/import-tabs/)) yourself.
  - **Change card types** (`change_card_types`): add, edit or delete the [card types](/help/plan/card-types/) a plan's boards hold, with their own fields and card templates. Deleting a type moves its cards to the Trash, as it does in the editor. Change items can then start a card from a template (`"template": "Bug Report"`), with any title or fields the AI gives winning over the template's.

  **Sheets**

  - **List sheets** (`list_sheets`), **Read a sheet** (`read_sheet`), **Change a sheet** (`change_sheet`) and **Add a sheet** (`add_sheet`): work with the [Sheets](/help/plan/sheets/) in a plan, reading cells by A1 address, setting values and formulas, formatting, sorting, and adding a new sheet from rows or CSV.

  **Forms**

  - **Add a form** (`add_form`), **Read a form** (`read_form`) and **Change a form** (`change_form`): build a [form](/help/plan/forms/) on a Form tab, from a starter or from the questions you describe, with its responses going to a board or a sheet you name, then read it back or change its questions, sections and settings. The AI can also give every response's card a field without a question (**Set Automatically**, such as a priority, an option, or a due date some days after it is sent) and fill sheet columns on every row (**Extra Columns**), and read back which ones are set and whether any no longer fits.
  - **Share a form** (`share_form`) and **Try a form** (`try_form`): publish a form's link, close it or delete the link, and fill the form in once as a test, the way the builder's Preview does, to see the card or row a response makes.
</AppNote>

In an AI tool that shows interactive results (Claude does), working on a <Noun /> also opens a small view of it beside the answer. You can switch between the <Noun />'s tabs and click to open what you see in the editor.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Creating, changing or reading a canvas opens a small view of the tab. You can pan and zoom it, and click a box to open it in the editor.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
  Creating, changing or reading a <Noun /> shows one page at a time: flip pages with the arrows in its header (or Page Up and Page Down), pan and zoom, and click an element to open it in the editor. The view is read-only: nothing you do in it changes the <Noun />. The view uses your own connection, never a share link, and draws only the page you are on, so a long flow of pages stays quick.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  Big photos are left out of the view rather than slowing it down.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Listing a plan's cards or reading a plan opens a small view of its board: each column with its card count (and its WIP limit, when it has one) and the cards in the board's order. The view is read-only: nothing you do in it changes the plan. A big board shows the first cards of each column and a **+ N more** button that opens the board in the editor, so the view stays quick. It uses your own connection.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **Editing in the view.** When the connection can edit the canvas (it isn't **Read-only access**, and you can edit the canvas yourself), the view's header shows an **Edit** button. Press it, and the view lets you make two quick changes without leaving the chat:

  - **Rename**: double-click a shape, text or sticky (or select it and press **Rename** or Enter), type, and press Enter. Escape leaves it as it was.
  - **Move**: drag a box to where you want it. With a box selected, the arrow keys nudge it 10 pixels (1 with Shift). A frame or lane brings what it holds.

  Click a box to select it; a small bar above it offers **Rename** and **Open** (the editor at that box), and says when something can only be moved in the editor (a badge, or the box it's attached to) or is **Locked**. Tab steps through the boxes, and Escape clears the selection, then leaves Edit. Each change saves as soon as you finish it, as one change in the canvas's history, and everyone with the canvas open sees it straight away. Anything bigger (adding, deleting, restyling, connecting) stays in the editor, or you can ask the AI.

  With a **Read-only access** connection, or on a canvas you can only view or take part in, there's no **Edit** button and the view stays read-only. Our server checks every change, so the view can't make one your access doesn't allow. A change you make in the view goes through the same connection, checked like any other.
</AppNote>

While it is open, the view follows the <Noun />. When you or someone else changes it, the view draws it again within a moment, and keeps your place. A small mark in its header says how it is keeping up:

- **Live**: it is following the <Noun />.
- **Reconnecting**: the connection dropped, and it is trying again.
- **Snapshot**: it has stopped following, for example because your access was removed, the connection kept dropping, or the <Noun /> moved to the Trash. It keeps showing what it last drew, and its tooltip says why and when.

To follow along, the view uses a short-lived pass that only lets it watch, never a share link.

## What a connection can and can't touch

By default the token behind a connection has full read and write access to your <Noun plural />, including the <Noun plural /> in your teams' shared libraries, so only connect tools you trust, and revoke the token if you stop using one. If you'd rather a tool could look but not change anything, switch on **Read-only access** on the approval page when you connect it: the connection can then find and read your <Noun plural /> (and list your Trash) but can't create, edit, share, rename, delete or restore anything. The block is enforced by our server, not just the tool. Its token shows a **Read-only** badge in the [API Tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) list.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  The badge reads **Read only**.
</AppNote>

Either way, a connection can't manage your teams or your account: inviting members, changing roles, and deleting things other than <Noun plural /> stay with you, signed in. It can never delete a <Noun /> for good either; that stays with you, in the Trash. (A tab it deletes is gone for good.)

Approving a connection creates a token, so it fails if you already have 20 tokens that haven't expired; revoke one in **Settings**, **Developers**, **API Tokens** first.
