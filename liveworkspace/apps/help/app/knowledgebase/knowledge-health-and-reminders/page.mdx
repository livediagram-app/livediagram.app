[Page Health](/help/knowledgebase/page-health/) is about one page. **Knowledge Health** is the same question for the whole Knowledgebase: how much of it does someone vouch for today? It comes with three emails that keep owners reviewing, and one setting that decides whether those emails reach you.

## Knowledge Health

Open the **Insights** tab in the footer and find **Knowledge Health**.

<Figure caption="Knowledge Health: one bar of Verified, Review Due and Never Verified pages, their counts, and Remind the Owners.">
  <KnowledgeHealthBar />
</Figure>

- One bar is split into **Verified**, **Review Due** and **Never Verified**, with the count of each under it.
- The counts are worked out when you open the view, under the Knowledgebase's Review Period. Under **Never**, no page is Review Due.
- A Knowledgebase with no pages reads "No Pages Yet".
- Under a Review Period of **Never**, verification is off: Knowledge Health shows the average Health Score of the pages and the pages with low health, with no bar, no counts and no **Remind the Owners**. The figures band at the top shows **Average Health** in place of **Verified**.

## Not Read in 90 Days

Below **Most Read**, **Not Read in 90 Days** lists the pages nobody opened and no agent read in the last 90 days, up to 10, the one edited longest ago first, each with when it was last edited. Press one to open it. A page made in the last 90 days is not listed: it has not had the time to be read. Past 10, the card says how many more there are. With none, it reads **Every Page Was Read**. Everyone who can open the Knowledgebase sees it.

Each Knowledgebase's verified share also shows on its card on [Home](/help/getting-started/knowledgebase-home/).

## Remind the Owners

<Step n={1}>
  In **Knowledge Health**, press **Remind the Owners**. It shows while at least one page is Review
  Due or Never Verified.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Read the toast. It says what happened in words: "Sent to 2 owners.", or why nothing was sent.
</Step>

Each owner gets one email, **Pages to Review**, listing their pages that are Review Due or Never Verified, each with its state and a link (the first 100 by name, the rest counted).

- The owners can be reminded **once a week**. For a week after a reminder, "Owners reminded" and the date stands in the button's place. A press that reached nobody does not use up the week.
- A page with no owner has nobody to remind. Set one in [Page Health](/help/knowledgebase/page-health/).
- The button is an Editor's who is signed in, and it is drawn only where the server sends email.

## The Emails

| Email                    | Sent when                                                                 | To                         |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Pages Due for Review** | Pages you own come due for review                                         | The owner of those pages   |
| **Pages to Review**      | An Editor presses **Remind the Owners**                                   | Each owner of a page to review |
| **Two Pages Disagree**   | An Editor presses **Ask Both Owners** on a conflict                       | The owners of the two pages |

- **Pages Due for Review** goes at most once a day to one owner for one Knowledgebase, and lists every page of theirs that is due. A page is told of once for each review: verify it again and you hear again when that review comes due.
- **Two Pages Disagree** is sent at most once for a conflict. See [Pages That Disagree](/help/knowledgebase/pages-that-disagree/).
- Together the three are at most **50 emails a day** for one Knowledgebase. An owner left out by that ceiling is emailed the next day.
- Each email says the Knowledgebase's Review Period ("This knowledgebase reviews pages every 90 days.").

An email goes only to someone who can still open the Knowledgebase, at the verified address of their account. A Knowledgebase kept Offline sends none, and the Knowledgebase itself keeps no email address.

## Turn the Emails Off or On

One switch covers all three emails, for your account.

<Step n={1}>
  Open **Settings** in the footer and go to **Account**, then **Notifications**.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Under **Email**, switch **Knowledgebase Reviews** off or on. It is on by default.
</Step>

With it off you get none of the three, from any Knowledgebase. The pages you own that are due still show under **Yours to Review** on Home. See [Email Notifications](/help/account-and-data/email-notifications/) for the other emails liveworkspace sends.

## Who Can Do What

| Action                                   | Editor | Participant | Viewer |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------ | ----------- | ------ |
| See Knowledge Health                     | Yes    | Yes         | Yes    |
| Remind the owners                        | Yes    | No          | No     |
| Turn your own review emails off or on    | Yes    | Yes         | Yes    |

## Good to Know

- When a reminder reaches only some owners, the toast says how many and why the rest were not reached: they have these emails turned off, have no verified address, or can no longer open the Knowledgebase.
- "Every page is verified, so there is nobody to remind." means exactly that.
