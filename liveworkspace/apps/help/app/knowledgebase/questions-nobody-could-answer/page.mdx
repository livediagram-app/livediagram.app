## How a Question Gets Here

A question is kept when:

- [Ask](/help/knowledgebase/ask/) says the pages do not cover it, or part of it.
- An agent's search finds nothing.
- An agent reports it with the MCP tool `report_gap`, or the CLI's `gap`, instead of guessing.

Questions asked in the same words (after small differences such as plurals are set aside) are one row. The row counts how many times it was asked, by people and by each named agent: "Asked 4 times by people and the Coding Agent". Nobody who asked is named or kept, only the count.

## Draft a Page for a Question

<Step n={1}>
  Press **Draft a Page** on the question's row.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  A new top-level page opens, titled from the question. Its first line reads "Draft, Not
  Verified", then the question as a heading.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  A draft written from the nearest passages follows, on built-in AI or the team's own key (see
  [AI Settings](/help/knowledgebase/ai-settings/)). Where no AI can answer, the page holds only
  the question, for you to answer.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Correct the page, take out the "Draft, Not Verified" line, and verify it. See
  [Owners and Verifying a Page](/help/knowledgebase/owners-and-verifying/).
</Step>

A draft counts as one Ask question against the limits in [Ask](/help/knowledgebase/ask/).

## Dismiss a Question

Press the dismiss button (×) at the right of a row to remove the question from the list. Use it for a question the Knowledgebase is not meant to answer.

## Good to Know

- With nothing to show, the section reads **No Unanswered Questions Yet**.
- A question is drawn as plain text: it is other people's words.
- A Knowledgebase kept Offline has no Insights, so nothing is kept for it.
