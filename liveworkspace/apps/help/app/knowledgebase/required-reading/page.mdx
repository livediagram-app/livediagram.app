Some pages everyone needs to have read: a security policy, the code of conduct, how expenses work. Mark one **Required Reading** and each person asked confirms they read it.

## Make a Page Required Reading

An Editor does this from the page's Page Info, beside the page.

1. Under **Required Reading**, press **Require Reading**.
2. Choose **Everyone**, every member of the team, or **Chosen People** and tick who.
3. Press **Require Reading**.

To change who is asked, press **Change** beside the section's heading. To stop, press **Stop Requiring** at the foot of the same panel: the requirement and everyone's confirmations go.

## Confirm You Read It

When you are asked, a **Required Reading** banner sits at the top of the page. Read the page, then press **I've Read This**. The banner goes, and Page Info says when you read it. You confirm once: pressing again changes nothing.

## See Who Has Read It

Everyone who reads the page sees how many have confirmed, such as "7 of 12 have read it", and their own state.

Editors and the page's owner also see who: **Not Yet** first, then **Read** with the date each person confirmed. Nobody else sees names.

## Remind People

Editors and the page's owner can press **Remind** to email everyone not yet read. Each person is reminded of a page at most once a week, so pressing again the next day reaches only people not yet reminded.

Reminders follow the **Knowledgebase Reviews** email setting in Settings, under Notifications, and share its limit of 50 emails a Knowledgebase a day.

## Ask Everyone Again After a Change

When you edit a Required Reading page, the bar at the bottom shows **Ask Everyone to Read Again**. Turn it on before you press **Save** and every confirmation clears, so everyone is asked again. Leave it off for a small fix, such as a typo or a link, and confirmations stay.

## In AI Knowledge

The **Required Reading** card lists every Required Reading page with how many have read it, the least read first. Press a row to open its page.

Confirming needs an account: a guest, a share link without signing in, and an agent can't confirm. A Knowledgebase kept Offline, in this browser only, has no Required Reading.
