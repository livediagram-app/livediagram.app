Verifying a page says it is right today. It will not stay right for ever, so a verified page comes **Review Due** once the Knowledgebase's **Review Period** has passed. Review Due is a prompt to read the page again, not a verdict that it is wrong.

## What Review Due Means

- A page is **Review Due** when the Review Period has passed since it was last verified.
- It shows as an amber **Review Due** pill in the row under the page's title, with "Review was due" and the date. In the tree the row shows a small amber **Due**.
- Nothing is written to the page when a review comes due. The state is worked out from the date it was verified, the Review Period and today's date each time it is shown.
- To clear it, read the page, fix what has changed, and press **Verify Again**. See [Owners and Verifying a Page](/help/knowledgebase/owners-and-verifying/).

Before it is due, the row says when it will be: "Review due" and the date.

## The Review Period

One Review Period covers every page of a Knowledgebase. A page has no period of its own.

| Choice             | A verified page comes due           |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| **Every 30 Days**  | 30 days after it was verified       |
| **Every 90 Days**  | 90 days after (the default)         |
| **Every 180 Days** | 180 days after                      |
| **Every Year**     | 365 days after                      |
| **Never**          | Never: pages do not age             |

Under **Never**, no page is ever Review Due: pages are Verified or Never Verified, and no review email is sent.

## Change the Review Period

<Step n={1}>
  On any page, press **Change** beside **Review Period** on the
  [Page Health](/help/knowledgebase/page-health/) card. It opens under the button (as a sheet on
  a phone) and says it applies to every page in this Knowledgebase.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Choose one of the five rows. Use the arrow keys to move between them.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  A longer period, or **Never**, applies at once. A shorter period that would make more pages due
  than are due now asks first: a strip under the rows says how many ("12 pages will be due for
  review with Every 30 Days."). Press **Apply** to go ahead or **Cancel** to keep the period you
  had.
</Step>

</Figure>

A toast confirms the change ("Pages are now reviewed every 30 days.") with **Undo**, which puts back the period you replaced.

## What Changing It Does

- It changes when **every** page comes due, at once, for everyone. No page is rewritten or saved again.
- Pages verified longer ago than the new period become Review Due straight away. That is why a shorter period asks first.
- An owner is emailed once for each review that comes due. Changing the period never tells an owner twice about the same review.
- The editor's own Undo (`Cmd+Z` or `Ctrl+Z`) is for writing and does not reach the Review Period. Use the toast's **Undo**, or choose the old period again.

Many pages turned Review Due at once and you did not mean it? See [Review Due on Every Page](/help/troubleshooting/review-due-everywhere/).

## Who Can Do What

| Action                        | Editor | Participant | Viewer |
| ----------------------------- | ------ | ----------- | ------ |
| See Review Due and the dates  | Yes    | Yes         | Yes    |
| Change the Review Period      | Yes    | No          | No     |

Only an Editor of the whole Knowledgebase sees **Change**. A Participant, a Viewer and someone who opened a link to one page see the period with no **Change** beside it.

## Good to Know

- The Review Period travels with the Knowledgebase: with a copy of it, its export, its Google Drive file and a snapshot.
- A Knowledgebase kept Offline keeps its period in this browser and takes it along when it is synced.
- Agents are told the period once, with the page list, and work out the same dates. No agent tool changes it.
