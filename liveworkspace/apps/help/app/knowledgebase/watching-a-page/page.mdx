Watch a page you rely on and you hear when it changes, without opening it to check. Anyone signed in who can read the page can watch it, Viewers included.

## Watch a Page

- In the page tree, open the page's menu (the three dots, or a right-click) and choose **Watch**.
- Or, beside the page, press **Watch** at the top of **Page Health**.

The button then reads **Watching**. Press it again, or choose **Unwatch** in the menu, to stop.

## Watch a Section

A page with subpages is a section. Choose **Watch This Section** in its menu, or under **Watch** beside it, and you hear about the page and every page under it, including pages added later.

A page under a section you watch shows **Watching Through** and the section's name. To stop, unwatch the section from its own page.

## What You Hear About

| Change                   | When                                                         |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Edited**               | Someone saves a change to the page (not while they type)     |
| **Verification Lapsed**  | The page's review comes due                                  |
| **Approved**, **Rejected** | Someone decides a request to approve the page              |
| **Moved**                | The page goes somewhere else in the page tree                |
| **Deleted**              | The page goes to the Knowledgebase's Trash                   |

Your own changes never tell you. Reactions, comments and verifying a page don't either.

## Where You Hear

- **Your Inbox.** Changes to a page come as one row an hour at most, under the Inbox's **Updates** filter, and stay for 14 days. They never add to the Inbox's count. Open the row to go to the page.
- **A daily email, if you want one.** Turn on **Daily Digest of Watched Pages** in Settings, under Notifications. It is off until you turn it on, and every email has a link to turn it off again.

## Who Watches

Beside **Watch**, Page Health shows how many people watch the page, for example **3 watching**. It never says who. Someone who can no longer open the Knowledgebase stops hearing about it and stops being counted.

Watching needs an account: a guest, a share link without signing in, and an agent can't watch. A Knowledgebase kept Offline, in this browser only, has no watching.
