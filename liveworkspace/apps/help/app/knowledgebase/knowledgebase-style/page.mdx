## Open Knowledgebase Style

<Step n={1}>
  Press the Knowledgebase's name at the top of the sidebar. Its menu opens.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Choose **Knowledgebase Style**. The dialog reads "Pick a look for every page of this
  knowledgebase."
</Step>

You can also find it by name in the search panel (`Cmd+K`, or `Ctrl+K` on Windows and Linux).

## Pick a Look

A look is a theme (its colours) and a font together. Each card is a small page of writing drawn in that look, named by the two, such as "Steel, Inter". The product's own theme is called **Lime**.

- Press a card to wear that look. It sets the theme and both fonts, keeps your text size, spacing and heading style, and puts the accent back on the theme's own.
- **Mashup** rolls a random theme and font.
- Seasonal looks can be shown or hidden with their switch.
- A Knowledgebase whose headings and body are in two different fonts wears no look, so no card reads as chosen.

A new Knowledgebase starts in the **Everyday** look, with the Lime theme.

## Fine-Tune

Press **Fine-tune** for two panels, each with a sample that follows your choices as you make them.

**Text:** "The typefaces, size and spacing every page is written in."

- The heading font and the body font.
- Text size: **Small**, **Medium** or **Large**.
- Line spacing: **Compact**, **Comfortable** or **Relaxed**.
- Heading style: plain, or with a rule under the largest headings.

**Colours:** "The theme, and the accent the writing takes."

- The theme, six to a page, each a small page of writing in that theme and your fonts.
- The accent: the theme's own, or one you pick. Picking a theme puts the accent back on that theme's own.

## What the Style Colours

- Headings, links (page links included), the bar beside a quote, a table's header row and the caret take the accent. It is kept readable on the page, in light and dark mode.
- **A page's Title stays in the ink** in every style: it is the page's name.
- The editor's own chrome (the sidebar, the top row, the footer's tabs, buttons) follows the theme. An accent you pick colours the writing only.
- **Review Due** stays amber, and errors stay red, in every theme.
- Drawings and charts inside a page are drawn in the Knowledgebase's theme.
- Exports (PDF, Word and Markdown) use the style.

## Good to Know

- A style change is one undoable step. Pages are not rewritten or saved again.
- There is no canvas, page background, margin or page number to style: a Knowledgebase page is a page of writing.
- An agent cannot set a page's look. The style is set for the whole Knowledgebase, by an Editor.

| See pages in the style        | Yes    | Yes         | Yes    |
