liveknowledge has three places for settings, and it helps to know which is which.

| Setting               | Belongs to                 | Holds                                                           |
| --------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Knowledgebase Settings** | One Knowledgebase, for everyone in it | The Review Period                                   |

| **Settings**          | You, on your account       | Your own preferences, such as appearance and notifications. See [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) |

This article is about the first.

## Open Knowledgebase Settings

- On a page, open [Page Health](/help/knowledgebase/page-health/) and press **Change** under **Review Period**.
- From anywhere in the Knowledgebase, open the command palette, type "Knowledgebase Settings" and press `Enter`.

The dialog opens in the middle of the window (as a sheet on a phone), titled **Knowledgebase Settings**, with the line "What every page of this knowledgebase follows."

## Reviews

The dialog has one section, **Reviews**, with one setting.

**Review Period:** how long after it was last verified a verified page comes due for review. Five choices, one chosen: **Every 30 Days**, **Every 90 Days** (the default), **Every 180 Days**, **Every Year** and **Never**.

<Step n={1}>
  Press the row you want. Use the arrow keys to move between rows.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  If the new period is shorter and would make more pages due, a strip under the rows says how many
  and offers **Apply** and **Cancel**. Any other change applies at once.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Press **Done** to close the dialog.
</Step>

What each choice means, and what happens to pages when you change it, is in [Review Due and the Review Period](/help/knowledgebase/review-due-and-the-review-period/).

## Who Can Open It

An **Editor** of the whole Knowledgebase. A Participant, a Viewer, and someone who opened a link to one page are not offered it: the **Change** link and the command are not shown to them. They still see the period on the Page Health card.

## Good to Know

- A change is for everyone, at once. It is not a preference of yours.
- If the change is refused, the period goes back as it was and a toast says why.
- The look of the pages is not here: see [Knowledgebase Style](/help/knowledgebase/knowledgebase-style/).
