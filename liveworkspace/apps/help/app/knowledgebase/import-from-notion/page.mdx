Moving from Notion? Export your pages from Notion as Markdown, and liveknowledge reads the export as a page tree.

## Export From Notion

<Step n={1}>
  In Notion, open **Settings**, then **Export**.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Choose the format **Markdown & CSV**, include subpages, and export. Notion gives you a `.zip`.
  Keep it zipped.
</Step>

## Import It

<Step n={1}>
  In liveknowledge, press the Knowledgebase's name at the top of the sidebar and choose **Import**.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Choose **A Whole Knowledgebase**, then **Notion**.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Drop the `.zip` on the drop area, or press **Choose a .zip**.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Review what was found, choose where it lands, and press the Import button, which counts the pages (such as **Import 320 Pages**). The review and
  the choices are the same for every source: see [Import From a Markdown Folder](/help/knowledgebase/import-a-markdown-folder/#import-a-folder).
</Step>

## What Comes Across

| In Notion                       | In liveknowledge                                              |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pages and their nesting         | Pages and subpages, without Notion's 32-character ids         |
| A page's title                  | Its first `#` heading, else the file name, id removed          |
| Links between pages             | Page links                                                    |
| A database                      | A table on its page                                           |
| A callout                       | A quote                                                       |
| A toggle                        | A heading with its content under it                           |
| Pictures in the export          | Pictures in the writing                                       |
| Other attachments               | Named at the foot of the page under **Attachments**           |

## Good to Know

- A toggle comes across as a heading only where Notion's export writes it as a toggle. One it writes as a plain list item stays a list item: nothing in the export tells the two apart.
- A link to a page you did not export stays an ordinary link.
- Imported pages are Never Verified, owned by nobody.
- The limits are the same for every source: 200 MB an archive, 500 pages in a Knowledgebase, 8 levels, 10 MB a picture. See [Import From a Markdown Folder](/help/knowledgebase/import-a-markdown-folder/#limits).
- The CLI imports a Notion export too: `liveknowledge import notion-export.zip --source notion --to Handbook`.
