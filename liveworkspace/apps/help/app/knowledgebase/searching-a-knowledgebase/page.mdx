Search finds pages by their words. It needs no AI and no key, and it works for everyone who can open the Knowledgebase, signed in or through a share link.

## Search This Knowledgebase

<Step n={1}>
  Press `Cmd+K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows and Linux), or press **Search** at the top of the sidebar.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Type. Results appear as you type, the pages of this Knowledgebase first, under **This
  Knowledgebase**.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Use the arrow keys to choose a result and press `Enter`. The page opens at the passage that
  matched.
</Step>

## What a Result Shows

- **Titles first, then passages.** A page whose title matches comes before a page that only mentions the words.
- Each result shows the page's title, the pages above it in the tree, a tick or **Due** if the page is verified or due for review, and the matching lines with your words marked.
- **Nothing matches?** The group still shows, reading **No Pages Match**, so you know this Knowledgebase was searched. The other groups follow.
- **Before you type,** This Knowledgebase lists the pages you opened most recently, up to eight. The list is kept in this browser.

## Everything Else

The panel is the search every liveworkspace app has, so below This Knowledgebase come your other knowledgebases, your folders and teams, commands, settings and help articles. A result from this Knowledgebase is never ranked below one from anywhere else, and the other groups never hold it up: each fills in as it answers. See [The Search Panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/).

To search every Knowledgebase you can open at once, use the field on [Home](/help/getting-started/knowledgebase-home/).

## How It Matches

Search looks at each page's title and its writing, split into passages: a heading and what is written under it. It matches words, not meaning, so search for a word the page would use. A page is searchable as soon as it is saved.

Want an answer written from the pages, not a list of them? See [Ask](/help/knowledgebase/ask/).

## Limits

- A search is at most **200 characters**.
- It returns at most **20 passages**.
- Many searches in quick succession are slowed: "Too many searches at once. Wait a moment and try again."

## Good to Know

- Every role can search: Editor, Participant and Viewer.
- A Knowledgebase kept Offline is searched in this browser, over the pages the browser already holds. A page you have not opened yet is found by its title alone.
- Agents search the same passages through the MCP tool `search_pages`. See [The liveknowledge MCP Tools](/help/developers/liveknowledge-mcp-tools/).
