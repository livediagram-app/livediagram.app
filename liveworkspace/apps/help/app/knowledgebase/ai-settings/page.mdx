Everything in it that uses one runs on **your team's own key**, from Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI, and your team pays that provider for what it uses. **AI Settings** is where an Editor adds the key. It belongs to one Knowledgebase and is the same for everyone in it.

## What Uses the Key

- [Ask](/help/knowledgebase/ask/), and drafting a page from a Knowledge Gap.
- [Describe to Set Up](/help/getting-started/describe-to-set-up/).
- The check that finds [pages that disagree](/help/knowledgebase/pages-that-disagree/).

Search, verifying, the page tree, agents connected over MCP and everything else need no key.

## Add a Key

<Step n={1}>
  Open the **Ask** or **AI Knowledge** tab in the footer and press **AI Settings** in the top row.
  Where a feature needs a key and there is none, **Open AI Settings** takes you to the same place.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Under **Provider**, choose **Anthropic (Claude)** or **OpenAI**.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Make an API key in the provider's own console (the Claude Console, or the OpenAI platform), paste
  it into the field and press **Save Key**.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Under **Model**, keep the one chosen or pick another. The list is the provider's current models,
  and the most capable is chosen to begin with.
</Step>

The key's state now reads "Key Ending" and its last four characters, and Ask works for everyone in the Knowledgebase who is signed in.

## How the Key Is Kept

- The key is sent once, over an encrypted connection, and **encrypted when it is saved**.
- It is **never shown again**, here or anywhere else. AI Settings shows only its last four characters ("Key Ending 4f2a"), and to someone who is not an Editor just "Key Added".
- The field is cleared as soon as the key is sent, whatever the answer. The key is not kept in the page, in your browser, in a log or in telemetry.
- It is used only to call the provider you chose. A Claude key is never sent to OpenAI, or the other way round.

## Replace or Remove the Key

- **Replace:** paste a new key in the field and press **Replace**. The new key takes over from that moment.
- **Remove:** press **Remove** beside the key's state and confirm **Remove the Key?** Ask stops answering for everyone in the Knowledgebase until an Editor adds a key again. Search and everything else keep working. Removing the key keeps the provider chosen.

If a key may have leaked, revoke it in the provider's console first, then replace it here.

## Change the Provider

A Knowledgebase holds one provider and one key at a time.

- With **no key saved**, choosing the other provider takes effect at once.
- With **a key saved**, it asks first ("Replace the Claude Key With an OpenAI Key?"). Confirm with **Choose Provider**. The saved key stays in use until you save a key for the new provider. That save replaces it and sets the model to the new provider's most capable one.

Everything behaves the same on either provider: the same limits, the same citations and the same words.

## Who Pays, and for What

Your team pays its provider directly, at the provider's prices, for each question asked and each structure, draft or check made. liveknowledge adds no charge. To keep spending bounded, a Knowledgebase answers at most 200 questions a day and one person asks at most 20 an hour. See [Ask](/help/knowledgebase/ask/#limits).

What is sent to the provider for a question: the question, the conversation so far, and the passages of your pages that best match it. Whether that suits your team's data is your team's decision; the provider's own terms cover what it does with it.

## Who Can Do What

| Action                                   | Editor | Participant | Viewer |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------ | ----------- | ------ |
| See the provider, the key's state and the model | Yes | Yes     | Yes    |
| Add, replace or remove the key           | Yes    | No          | No     |
| Choose the provider or the model         | Yes    | No          | No     |

A Participant and a Viewer see "Only an Editor of this knowledgebase can change these."

## Good to Know

- A key that is not the chosen provider's is refused as you save it: "That is not a Claude API key. Copy it again from the Claude Console and paste it whole."
- A Knowledgebase kept Offline has no key and no Ask: there is no server to keep the key.
- AI Settings is not part of [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), which holds your own preferences. The key belongs to the Knowledgebase.
- When Ask says the key was not accepted, see [Ask Is Not Answering](/help/troubleshooting/ask-is-not-answering/).
