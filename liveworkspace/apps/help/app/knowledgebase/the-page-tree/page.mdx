## Add a Page

<Step n={1}>Press **New Page** at the foot of the sidebar to add a page at the top level.</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  To add a page under another, hover the parent's row and press its **+**, or open its menu and
  choose **Add Subpage**. The new page becomes the parent's last subpage.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  The new page opens with the caret in its title. What you type next is its name; until then it is
  "Untitled Page".
</Step>

## Rename a Page

A page's name and its title are one thing. Type in the title at the top of the page and the row in the tree follows, a moment after you stop typing. Or rename it in the tree: double-click the row, press `F2` with the row focused, or choose **Rename** in its menu, then type and press `Enter`. `Escape` leaves the name as it was.

## Move a Page

A page always moves with its subpages.

- **Drag** a row up or down to reorder it among its siblings, or drop it on another page to make it that page's subpage. A line shows where it will land.
- **Move Up** and **Move Down** in the row's menu step it past the page before or after it.
- **Indent** puts it under the page above it. **Outdent** lifts it one level, to sit after the page it was under.

A page is never dropped inside its own subpages. A verb that cannot run stays in the menu, greyed, and says why when you hover it ("This is the first page here.", "This page is already at the top level.").

## Duplicate and Copy a Link

- **Duplicate** copies the page beside it, under the same parent, without its subpages.
- **Copy Link** copies the page's address: the Knowledgebase's, with the page at its end. Anyone who can open the Knowledgebase lands on that page.

## Delete a Page, and Undo It

<Step n={1}>Open the row's menu and choose **Delete**, the last row.</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Confirm. The dialog names the page and counts its subpages ("Move Runbooks and 3 Subpages to the
  Trash?"): deleting a page moves the pages under it to the Trash with it.
</Step>

**Undo** (`Cmd+Z`, or `Ctrl+Z` on Windows and Linux) brings the page and its subpages straight back. Later, restore them from the Trash. A link to a page in the Trash stays in the writing, struck through and reading "In the Trash"; pressing it opens the Trash at that page. See [Linking Pages](/help/knowledgebase/linking-pages/).

## The Trash

**Trash** sits at the foot of the page tree, directly above **New Page**, with a count of the pages it holds. Press it to open the Trash.

- Each page you deleted is one row, with the subpages it took, who deleted it and when, and the days it has left.
- **Restore** puts the page and its subpages back where they were, with their writing, cover, icon, owner, comments and versions. If the page it sat under is gone, it comes back at the top level and says so.
- **Delete Forever** removes one page and its subpages for good; **Empty Trash** removes them all. Both ask first and cannot be undone.
- A page waits **30 days** in the Trash, then it is removed for good. The Trash holds 500 pages at most: past that the oldest go first.

A page in the Trash is left out of search, Ask, AI Knowledge, exports and the Community, and agents cannot read it. A Knowledgebase kept Offline has no Trash: there, Undo is what brings a deleted page back.

The last page of a Knowledgebase cannot be deleted: **Delete** is greyed and says "A knowledgebase always has a page." To delete the whole Knowledgebase, see [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/).

## Fold the Tree and the Sidebar

Press a row's chevron to open or close its subpages. Which pages are open is remembered in this browser, for each Knowledgebase. **Hide Pages**, at the top right of the sidebar, collapses the sidebar to a narrow rail with **Show Pages**, **Search** and **New Page**; that is remembered in this browser too.

On a phone the tree is a sheet: press **Pages** in the top row to open it.

## Keyboard

| Key      | Does                                         |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Up, Down | Move through the rows                        |
| Right    | Open a page's subpages, then step into them  |
| Left     | Close a page's subpages, or go to its parent |
| Enter    | Open the page                                |
| F2       | Rename the page                              |
| Menu key | Open the row's menu                          |

## What a Row Shows

- A **tick** for a page that is verified, and a small amber **Due** for one whose review is due. A page never verified shows neither. See [Owners and Verifying a Page](/help/knowledgebase/owners-and-verifying/).
- The avatars of the people on that page now.

## Limits

- A Knowledgebase holds **500 pages** at most. At the limit **New Page**, **Add Subpage** and **Duplicate** are disabled and say "A knowledgebase holds 500 pages at most."
- Pages nest **8 levels** deep at most. A subpage, an indent or a drop that would go deeper is refused: "Pages nest 8 levels deep at most."

Both limits are checked again on the server, so an import or an agent cannot pass them either.

## Who Can Do What

| Action                                        | Editor | Participant | Viewer |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------ | ----------- | ------ |
| Open pages, fold the tree, copy a page's link | Yes    | Yes         | Yes    |
| Add, rename, move, duplicate and delete pages | Yes    | Yes         | No     |
| Open the Trash and restore pages              | Yes    | Yes         | No     |
| Delete a page forever, empty the Trash        | Yes    | No          | No     |

For someone who cannot edit, a row's menu holds **Copy Link** alone, and **New Page** is not shown.
