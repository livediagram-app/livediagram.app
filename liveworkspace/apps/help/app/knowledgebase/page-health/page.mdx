## Open It

- On a wide window (1,280 pixels or more, with room for the page at its full width) the two sit in a rail at the right of the page.
- On a narrower window, press **Page Info** in the top row, to the left of **Share**. It opens the same two things in a panel; press **Close Page Info**, or `Escape`, to put it away.

## On This Page

The page's headings, in order. Press one to go to it. The list follows the headings as they are written.

## What Page Health Shows

The card opens with the page's **Health Score**, with its **Score Breakdown** under it, then a status panel: **Verified**, **Review Due** or **Not Verified**, with a line on how fresh the page is. **Verify** (or **Verify Again** on a page already verified) is the button in that panel, and verifies the page there and then. See [Owners and Verifying a Page](/help/knowledgebase/owners-and-verifying/).

The rest is folded under **Page Details**, closed until you press the status panel or its chevron:

| Row                           | Shows                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**                     | The person answerable for the page, or **Nobody**                                                  |
| **Last Verified**             | The date and who verified it, or **Never**                                                         |
| **Review Due**                | The date the review comes due; **Not Verified** for a page never verified                          |
| **Review Period**             | The Knowledgebase's period, such as **Every 90 Days**, a link to change it for an Editor           |

At the foot of the rail, **Read This Week** counts how many times people (**by People**) and agents (**by Agents**) read the page in the last seven days. Agents count when they read it or are served a passage of it in a search.

When the Review Period is **Never**, verification is off: the card has no state, no **Last Verified**, no **Review Due** and no **Verify**. **Owner** and **Review Period** stay, so the period can be set again from here.

## Page Insights

The page's use shows in two places:

- Under the counts in **Read This Week**: its reads over the last 30 days, a bar a day, people and agents in two tones. Press the **by Agents** count to list which agents read the page and how often.
- **Answered From This Page**, under **On This Page**: the questions Ask answered from the page most often, up to three. A link that opens only this page has no Ask, so it is not shown there.

A page made in the last week says it is new, with its reads so far, instead of 30 days of bars from before it existed. Everyone who can open the page sees them. A Knowledgebase kept Offline does not show them.

## Change the Owner

<Step n={1}>
  Open **Page Details**, then the **Owner** list.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Choose a person, or **Nobody**. The people listed are those who can open the Knowledgebase.
</Step>

The change is saved at once. If it cannot be, the owner goes back to who it was and a toast says why, for example "The owner must be someone who can open this knowledgebase."

## The Review Period Is Not Set Here

The Review Period belongs to the whole Knowledgebase, so the card shows it, and an Editor changes it there: pressing the period itself, such as **Every 90 Days**, opens the five choices, for every page at once. See [Review Due and the Review Period](/help/knowledgebase/review-due-and-the-review-period/).

## About the Reads

- A read by a person is one opening of the page in the editor. A read by an agent is one `read_page` of the page, or one search result from it that an agent was given.
- Reads are counted by day and kept for 90 days. No reader's identity is kept, only whether it was a person or which agent it was.
- A page with many reads by agents and no verifier is worth verifying first: agents are answering from it.

## Who Can Do What

| Action                          | Editor | Participant | Viewer |
| ------------------------------- | ------ | ----------- | ------ |
| See On This Page and Page Health | Yes   | Yes         | Yes    |
| Change the owner                | Yes    | Yes         | No     |
| Verify from the card            | Yes    | Yes         | No     |
| Open the Review Period setting  | Yes    | No          | No     |

For someone who cannot change them, the rows read as text and the card has no **Verify** button.

## Good to Know

- A Knowledgebase kept Offline shows the page's state but no reads, and its owner cannot be changed.
- The reads show as a dash while they are being fetched.
