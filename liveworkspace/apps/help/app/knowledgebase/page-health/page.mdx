Beside every page are two things about it: **On This Page**, a list of its headings, and **Page Health**, a card that says who answers for the page, how fresh it is and whether anyone reads it.

## Open It

- On a wide window (1,280 pixels or more, with room for the page at its full width) the two sit in a rail at the right of the page.
- On a narrower window, press **Page Info** in the top row, to the left of **Share**. It opens the same two things in a panel; press **Close Page Info**, or `Escape`, to put it away.

## On This Page

The page's headings, in order. Press one to go to it. The list follows the headings as they are written.

## What Page Health Shows

| Row                           | Shows                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**                     | The person answerable for the page, or **No Owner**                                                |
| **Last Verified**             | The date and who verified it, or **Never**                                                         |
| **Review Due**                | The date the review comes due; **Once Verified** for a page never verified; **No Review Period** when the period is Never |
| **Review Period**             | The Knowledgebase's period, such as **Every 90 Days**, with **Change** under it for an Editor       |
| **Reads by People This Week** | How many times people opened the page in the last seven days                                       |
| **Reads by Agents This Week** | How many times agents read it or were served a passage of it in a search                           |

Under the rows, **Verify** (or **Verify Again** on a page already verified) verifies the page there and then. See [Owners and Verifying a Page](/help/knowledgebase/owners-and-verifying/).

## Change the Owner

<Step n={1}>
  Open the **Owner** list on the card.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Choose a person, or **No Owner**. The people listed are those who can open the Knowledgebase.
</Step>

The change is saved at once. If it cannot be, the owner goes back to who it was and a toast says why, for example "The owner must be someone who can open this knowledgebase."

## The Review Period Is Not Set Here

The Review Period belongs to the whole Knowledgebase, so the card shows it, and an Editor changes it there: **Change**, beside it, opens the five choices, for every page at once. See [Review Due and the Review Period](/help/knowledgebase/review-due-and-the-review-period/).

## About the Reads

- A read by a person is one opening of the page in the editor. A read by an agent is one `read_page` of the page, or one search result from it that an agent was given.
- Reads are counted by day and kept for 90 days. No reader's identity is kept, only whether it was a person or which agent it was.
- A page with many reads by agents and no verifier is worth verifying first: agents are answering from it.

## Who Can Do What

| Action                          | Editor | Participant | Viewer |
| ------------------------------- | ------ | ----------- | ------ |
| See On This Page and Page Health | Yes   | Yes         | Yes    |
| Change the owner                | Yes    | No          | No     |
| Verify from the card            | Yes    | No          | No     |
| Open the Review Period setting  | Yes    | No          | No     |

For someone who cannot change them, the rows read as text and the card has no button.

## Good to Know

- A Knowledgebase kept Offline shows the page's state but no reads, and its owner cannot be changed.
- The reads show as a dash while they are being fetched.
