A Knowledgebase is only useful, to a person or to an AI agent, while it is right. So each page can say who vouches for it and when they last did. **Verifying** a page is how you say "I have read this and it is right today."

## Verify a Page

<Step n={1}>
  Open the page and read it.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Press **Verify** in the row under the title. It is one press, with no confirmation.
</Step>

The row now shows a tick with "Verified by" and your name, and the date the review comes due. The page's row in the tree shows a tick too. If the page had no owner, you become its owner.

**Verify** is also in the page's menu in [the page tree](/help/knowledgebase/the-page-tree/) and on the [Page Health](/help/knowledgebase/page-health/) card. On a page already verified, the button reads **Verify Again**: press it after re-reading the page to start a fresh review period.

## The Three States

<Figure caption="The three states a page can be in, as the row under its title shows each.">
  <TrustStates />
</Figure>

| State              | When                                              | Shows as                                  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Verified**       | Verified, and the review is not yet due           | A tick and "Verified by" the verifier     |
| **Review Due**     | The Review Period has passed since it was verified | An amber **Review Due** pill             |
| **Never Verified** | Nobody has verified it                            | Nothing on the page; grey in the counts   |

The state shows in three places: the row under the page's title, the page's row in the tree, and the card that shows when you hover a link to the page. Agents get it with every answer too. See [Review Due and the Review Period](/help/knowledgebase/review-due-and-the-review-period/).

## The Owner

A page's **owner** is the person answerable for it being right. It is not the same as the person who made the Knowledgebase.

- Set or change the owner in **Owner** on the [Page Health](/help/knowledgebase/page-health/) card, or choose **No Owner**.
- The owner must be someone who can open the Knowledgebase.
- The owner is who gets the email when the page comes due for review, and who is asked when two pages disagree. See [Knowledge Health and Reminders](/help/knowledgebase/knowledge-health-and-reminders/).

The owner and the verifier can be different people: anyone who is an Editor can verify a page they have checked.

## Editing a Verified Page

Writing on a verified page does not clear its tick: fixing a typo should not undo someone's review. When more than a fifth of the writing has changed since it was verified, "Changed since verified" shows beside the tick until someone verifies the page again.

## Verified Pages and Agents

An agent never rewrites a page that is **Verified** or **Review Due**, even when an Editor has allowed it to write. It suggests the edit and a person accepts it. See [What Agents Can Do](/help/knowledgebase/what-agents-can-do/).

## Who Can Do What

| Action                              | Editor | Participant | Viewer |
| ----------------------------------- | ------ | ----------- | ------ |
| See a page's state and its verifier | Yes    | Yes         | Yes    |
| Verify a page                       | Yes    | No          | No     |
| Change a page's owner               | Yes    | No          | No     |

Verify needs an account, because it says who vouches for the page: an Editor who is not signed in has no **Verify** button.

## Good to Know

- A new page, an imported page and a page an agent made are all **Never Verified** with no owner.
- A Knowledgebase kept Offline has no Verify. See [Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/).
- Accepting an agent's suggested edit does not verify the page.
