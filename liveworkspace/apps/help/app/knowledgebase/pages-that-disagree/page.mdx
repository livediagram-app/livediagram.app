Two pages that say different things are worse than one page that says nothing: whoever reads the wrong one acts on it. liveknowledge looks for such pairs as pages change, and lists each under **Pages That Disagree** in the **AI Knowledge** tab of the footer.

</Figure>

## How a Disagreement Is Found

- When a page is verified, or its writing is saved, liveknowledge finds passages on other pages that match its passages and asks the model whether any pair states opposite facts. A page is checked at most once every 10 minutes.
- It runs on built-in AI, included, or on the team's own key if it added one. On a server with no built-in AI and no key, a line says so, and an Editor sees **Open AI Settings**. See [AI Settings](/help/knowledgebase/ai-settings/).
- A Knowledgebase is checked at most 20 times a day on built-in AI and 50 on its own key, and each check compares at most 5 passages.

Each pair shows the question both passages answer, the two passages side by side (stacked on a phone), and each page's trust: Verified and when, Review Due, or Never Verified.

## Settle It

<Step n={1}>
  Read both passages. Their pages' trust is a clue: a passage on a page verified last week is more
  likely right than one on a page whose review is due.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Press **&lt;Page&gt; Is Right, Fix the Other** on the side that is right (for example, **Deploying Is
  Right, Fix the Other**). The other page opens, for you to correct.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Save the correction. Once either passage changes, the pair closes by itself.
</Step>

Not sure which is right? Press **Ask Both Owners**. Each owner of the two pages gets one email, "Two Pages Disagree", with the question, both pages linked, each passage (up to 280 characters) and a link to this view. Once they are asked, "Owners asked" and the date stand in the button's place. A pair's owners are asked once.

Not a real disagreement (two pages about different things that only sound alike)? Press **Not a Conflict**. That pair never comes back.

## Ask Both Owners, in Detail

The email is one of the **Knowledgebase Reviews** emails, so it follows the same rules as [Remind the Owners](/help/knowledgebase/knowledge-health-and-reminders/): an owner who turned these emails off, or who can no longer open the Knowledgebase, is not sent one, and a Knowledgebase sends at most 50 review emails a day. The toast says what happened, for example "Sent to 2 owners.", or "Neither page has an owner to ask. Set one in Page Health." The button is shown only where this server sends email.

## Good to Know

- With nothing to settle, the section reads **No Disagreements Yet**.
- Ask uses this too: a passage on an out-of-date page that disagrees with a newer verified one is shown under **Found but Not Used**, not in the answer. See [Ask](/help/knowledgebase/ask/).
- Agents are told when a passage's page is in a disagreement, by the field `conflicts_with` in every search result.

## Who Can Do What

| Action                                          | Editor | Participant | Viewer |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------ | ----------- | ------ |
| See pages that disagree                         | Yes    | Yes         | Yes    |
| Fix the other, or mark it Not a Conflict        | Yes    | No          | No     |
| Ask both owners                                 | Yes    | No          | No     |

Asking the owners needs a signed-in Editor.
