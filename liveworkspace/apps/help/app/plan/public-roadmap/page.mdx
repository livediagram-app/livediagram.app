**Publish as Roadmap** turns one board into a live public page. Anyone with the link can open it without an account. You choose which columns, card types and fields it shows, and visitors can upvote the ideas they want most. It updates as the board changes, and **Unpublish** takes it down at once.

It shows only what you choose. People, comments and every other field never leave the plan.

## Who Can Publish

The plan's owner and the members of its team, on a plan saved to the server (a guest owner too). Someone who opened the plan from a share link, as an Editor, Participant or Viewer, doesn't see Publish as Roadmap, and neither does a plan saved only in this browser.

## Publish a Board

1. Press **Tools** in the bottom bar.
2. Choose **Publish as Roadmap**, under Collaborate. It is there once the plan has a board on any tab.
3. Pick the board: when several tabs hold boards, pick the **Tab** first (it starts on the one you're looking at), then the **Board** when that tab has several. One already published wears a green dot.
4. Choose what it shows (below), then press **Publish Roadmap**.

## Choose What It Shows

- **Title**: the page's heading, the board's title to begin with.
- **Columns**: the board's columns to show. Columns that read like triage (Under Review, Inbox, Backlog, Triage, Ideas, New, Suggestions) start hidden, so new ideas stay private until you move them.
- **Card Types**: the types the board shows, all of them to begin with.
- **Fields**: Title always shows. Add **Description** (on to begin with), **Number**, **Card Type**, **Labels**, **Start Date** or **Due Date**.
- **Voting**: whether visitors can upvote.
- **Suggest an Idea**: a form for visitors' ideas (below).

Once published, a change saves at once and keeps the link.

A card shows when it's in a chosen column and of a chosen type. Archived cards and cards in the Trash never show. A column shows its first 200 cards in the board's order, and its count says how many it holds.

An **All Cards** or archive board has no columns to choose, so it can't be published.

## What Visitors See

The roadmap is drawn in the board tab's theme: the title, **Suggest an Idea** when you chose a form, then your columns side by side (stacked on a phone), with their cards in the board's order.

Visitors never see:

- people: who a card is assigned to, any person field, who made or edited a card, or who voted;
- comments and comment threads;
- custom fields, Priority, Estimate, Checklist, attachments, linked cards or pull requests;
- the other columns, not even their names;
- cards of types you didn't choose.

## Upvotes

With **Voting** on, each card has a vote button with its count. Pressing it adds this browser's vote; pressing it again takes it back. Each browser gets one vote per card, with no account needed.

Votes land in the card's **Votes**, beside your team's own, so the card's count on the board includes them. To put the most wanted ideas first, sort the board by **Votes** in its Sort Cards setting: the roadmap follows the board's order.

A card shows its count only when its card type has **Votes** on the card. A **Roadmap** board's Projects have it from the start. Any other card type you show gets it for you: when you publish with **Voting** on, each chosen card type without Votes gets the field, on its cards, and the dialog says so under **Voting**. It is one change you can undo.

To keep things fair, votes are limited the way form responses are: a burst of votes from one network waits a minute, and a very busy roadmap asks visitors to try again in a few minutes. Reading the page is limited the same way: when one network opens it very often, the page says it is busy and loads again by itself after a minute.

## Suggest an Idea

Pick one of the plan's [forms](/help/plan/forms/) under **Suggest an Idea**, and the page offers a button that opens it. Each suggestion becomes a card in the column the form sends to.

Choose a form that sends its cards to a column the roadmap doesn't show, such as Under Review. That way you can read, edit or merge a suggestion before you move it into a shown column, and only then does it appear. A form whose cards would show on the roadmap at once can't be picked. If you later show its column, the form is let go.

## Unpublish

Open **Publish as Roadmap** from **Tools** again, pick the board, press **Unpublish**, then **Unpublish** again to confirm. The link stops working at once: visitors see "This roadmap isn't available". Your settings stay in the dialog, and publishing again makes a new link.

Moving the plan to the Trash, archiving it or deleting the board takes the roadmap down too.
