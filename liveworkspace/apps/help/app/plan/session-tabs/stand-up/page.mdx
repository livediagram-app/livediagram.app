A **Stand-up** session walks the team **person by person** through the cards they have in flight. Each person speaks to their own cards, what is stuck gets flagged on the card itself, and nobody reads out a status report in front of a big board. It is built to be quick: one press per action, and a key for each.

## Start One

On a new tab, choose **Sessions** in **Start Planning**, then **Stand-up**. **Set Up Stand-up** opens on the card types with cards in progress, and their **In-flight Stages** (In Progress, Doing, In Review, Testing, Blocked and the like, never a stage of work that is only ready to start). Then pick the People Order:

- **Board Order** (the default): people in the order their first card sits on the board.
- **Alphabetical**: by name.
- **Shuffled Daily**: a fresh order each day, the same on every screen that day, so nobody always goes first.

Cards with no assignee come last, under **Unassigned**. A Stand-up has no Order or target stage: it never moves a card on its own. A timebox here is per person. See [Set Up a Session](/help/plan/session-tabs/set-up-a-session/).

## Run It

<Figure caption="A Stand-up on Priya: her in-flight cards unfolded in the sidebar, the note strip, and Blocked, Stage and Done.">
  <StandupScene />
</Figure>

The sidebar lists one row per person, with how many cards they hold. The person the room is on is unfolded to their cards, each with its stage; press any other person to look at their cards on your screen alone. The header counts people ("2 of 4 people"), and the progress bar has a segment per person.

The action bar first says whose card it is ("Priya · card 1 of 3"), then holds:

- **Blocked** (**B**): opens **Mark Blocked**, with a short reason and chips that fill one in (Waiting on Someone, Waiting on Review, Needs a Decision, External Dependency, Unclear Requirements). **Mark Blocked** flags the real card, saves "Blocked: {reason}" as a comment on it, and keeps the room on the card.
- **Stage**: a picker of the card's stages. Choosing one moves the real card there, so "it's in review now" is one press.
- **Done** (**D**): the person is finished. Each of their cards not decided yet is recorded as discussed, and the room moves to the next person.

Above it, anyone who may comment can type a note into **Add a note to …** and press **Add Comment** (or Enter). Notes land on the card in the day's stand-up thread ("Stand-up, 28 Nov"), so a card keeps one thread per day rather than a comment for every remark.

**J** and **K** move between cards, across people. **Undo Last** (**U**) takes back the latest step, one at a time: a Done, a Blocked (the flag comes off; its comment stays, as said) or a Stage move (the card goes back to its own place).

## When Everyone's Done

**Session Complete** counts the cards Discussed and Flagged, then lists the **Blockers**: every card marked blocked in the stand-up, with its person and reason. Press one to open it; **Back to Summary** returns. With none, it reads "No blockers today".

Each step is in the card's [Activity](/help/plan/card-activity/): "Sam discussed this in a Stand-up session", or "Sam flagged this in a Stand-up session: Waiting on the API keys".

## Who Can

The facilitator (an Editor) runs it: Done, Blocked and Stage are theirs. Participants and Editors can add notes from the strip and edit the card as on a board. Viewers watch, and have no note strip.
