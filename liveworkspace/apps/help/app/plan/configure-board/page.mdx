Configure Board is where an Editor sets up a [board](/help/plan/boards/) that fills its tab. It suggests ready-made layouts drawn from the cards already on the board, and gathers every part of the set-up in one place, so you can try a new way of seeing the work in one press and take it back with one **Undo**.

## Open Configure Board

On a Board tab, press **Board** (the cog) in the bottom-right corner, joined to the left of **Style**. Configure Board rises from the button; on a phone it opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen. Nothing is dimmed behind it, because every change shows on the board straight away. Press **Board** again, press outside it or press Escape to close it.

## Suggested for This Board

Each suggestion is a small drawing of the layout, its name, a line on what it does and, when the board's cards give one, why it suits them ("5 of 8 cards have someone on them"). The layout the board has now is marked. Pick one and the board takes it at once.

**Simple Columns** (no rows, cards in the order you put them) and **Compact Overview** (Minimal cards, no rows) always come first. The rest are the ones your card types can show, best fit first:

- **By Person**: a row for each person, soonest due first.
- **Most Urgent First** and **Deadlines First**: highest priority first, or soonest due first.
- **By Card Type**, **By Priority** and **By Label**: a row for each.
- **Quick Wins First** and **Biggest First**: by estimate, smallest or largest first.
- **Starting Soon**, **Newest First** and **Recently Changed**: by start date, or by when cards were made or last changed.
- **Your own fields**: a choice or checkbox field gives a row for each value (**By Stage**), a number field a largest-first sort (**Highest Seats First**) and a date field a soonest-first one (**Earliest Renewal First**).

At most nine are offered. A suggestion with nothing to show, such as By Person when nobody is assigned, is left out once the board holds five cards or more.

A suggestion sets the board's swimlanes, its sort and its card size together. It never changes the columns or the cards, so trying one is always safe.

## Fine-Tune

Below the suggestions, a tile for each part of the set-up says how it stands now ("4 columns", "Priority, then Due Date"):

- **Columns**: the board's columns, their colours, WIP limits and which count as done.
- **Swimlanes**: what the rows group by.
- **Sort Cards**: the order of the cards in every column.
- **Supported Cards**: which card types the board shows and takes.
- **Card Layout**: **Minimal** or **Detailed** cards.

A tile opens that part's controls in a popover beside it. These are the same settings described in [Boards](/help/plan/boards/#the-board-s-own-settings). Press the tile again, press outside or press Escape to close the popover and stay in Configure Board. An All Cards or Archive board has no Columns tile, and an Archive board no Supported Cards.

**Create Form**, at the top beside the close button, starts a [form](/help/plan/forms/) whose responses arrive as cards on this board. It isn't shown on a phone.

## Who Can

Only Editors see the **Board** button. Participants and Viewers see the board as it is set up. Every change is shared with everyone on the plan, and each one is a single step of [Undo](/help/user-interface/undo/).

A board in a [Dashboard](/help/plan/dashboards/) slot is set up from the cog in its header instead.
