On a Board, Sheet, Form or Dashboard tab, the **AI** button sits in the bottom-right corner, just left of the Trash and **Card Types**. It opens the same question box as **Ask AI** in [Tools](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/), working on the tab you have open. Ask it something ("Which cards have no assignee?"), or tell it what to change ("Add a total under each column of numbers"), and it does it for you.

## Ask or Tell AI

1. Open the tab and press **AI** (the sparkle).
2. Type a question or an instruction, or press one of the suggestions under **Try**. Press Enter to send; Shift and Enter make a new line.
3. The answer appears under your words. When AI changed the tab, a **Changes** box lists each change with a tick. If one couldn't be made, it is the last line, with a warning and why; the changes before it are kept.

Follow up in the same box: AI remembers the last few turns. **New Chat** starts again. Your conversation stays with the tab while the plan is open.

## What It Can Change

- **A board**: add cards (with their type, column and fields), change a card's fields, move cards between columns, and put cards in the Trash. Name a card by its number, such as #12.
- **A sheet**: fill or change cells, rows and columns, write formulas from a description, add totals, sort, filter, freeze, tidy and format, draw a chart from the data, start a sheet from scratch, and explain or fix a formula.
- **A form**: add, reword, reorder or remove questions and sections, make questions required, and change the form's heading and description. Where responses go stays in Form Settings.
- **A dashboard**: add and remove rows and slots, make a slot wider or narrower, change a row's height, move a slot, and put a note or a Board or Sheet tab in a slot.

AI can only do what you could do by hand on the tab, and every change syncs to everyone on the plan straight away.

## Undo

Each answer that changed the tab has an **Undo** button under it, while it is the newest answer. It takes back everything that turn changed. You can also use the editor's own Undo, a step at a time.

## Who Can Use It

- **Editors** can ask, and AI makes any of the changes above.
- **Participants** can ask, and AI can add, change and move cards and fill in cells, as they can by hand.
- **Viewers** can ask questions, and AI changes nothing.
- You need to be signed in. Without an account, the panel asks you to sign in.

## Which AI Answers

The pill in the box says which AI answers, and it is the same choice as Ask AI's: your own or your team's [AI key](/help/account-and-data/ai-keys/), or built-in AI. On built-in AI each turn counts as one of your 30 built-in questions a day, shared with Ask AI. On your own key there is no daily limit.

Only the open tab and your words are sent to the AI, and they aren't kept.
