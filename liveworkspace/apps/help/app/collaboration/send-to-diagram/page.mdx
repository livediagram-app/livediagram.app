A process, a system or a team written as prose is easier to follow as a picture. **Send to Diagram** asks AI to read the words you picked and draws them in livediagram as boxes and arrows, laid out for you. The words stay as they are; a small chip after them opens the diagram.

You need to be signed in. Reading the page is enough to send from it: a Viewer can send too. Sending is not offered to a guest, in an embed, for a knowledgebase saved only in this browser (Offline), or on Community.

## Sending

<Step n={1}>
  Pick the words: a sentence, a list, a few paragraphs, up to 8,000 characters. With nothing picked,
  the paragraph or list item the caret is in is sent. While you edit, press **Send to Diagram** on
  the text toolbar or the page toolbar, beside **Send to Plan**. If you can only read the page, pick
  the words and press the **Send to Diagram** pill that shows under them.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Choose **Where**: a **New Diagram** of your own, or a **New Tab** in a diagram you can edit (your
  most recent come first; search for the rest).
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Choose a **Kind**: **Auto** lets the AI pick, or ask for a **Flowchart**, a **Sequence** (who
  sends what to whom, in order), a **Mind Map**, an **Org Chart**, an **Architecture** diagram or a
  **Timeline**. Check the **Name**.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Press **Preview**. The AI reads the words and the picker shows a sketch of what it found, with how
  many boxes and arrows. Nothing is written yet: press **Draw Again** for another try, or change the
  kind and preview again.
</Step>

<Step n={5}>
  Press **Make Diagram** (or **Add Tab**). livediagram lays the diagram out and it opens in a new
  browser tab. Its caption says where the words came from and links back to them.
</Step>

## Sending again

Words you have drawn show a chip after them, with livediagram's logo and the diagram's name. Click it to open the diagram on its tab. You see a chip only for a diagram you can open.

Send the same words again and the picker opens on **Update**: **Update Tab** redraws that tab from the words as they read now. **New Diagram** and **New Tab** are still there, so the earlier diagram is only replaced when you mean it.

## Which AI draws it

The knowledgebase's own AI key answers first, then its team's, then yours, as Ask does. Without one, liveknowledge's built-in AI draws it. Built-in AI draws up to 10 diagrams a person a day; anyone may draw 20 an hour, and a knowledgebase 100 a day. The words go to that AI alone, and are never kept.

<Tip>
  If the AI finds no diagram in the words, pick a kind: a list of steps reads best as a Flowchart,
  and a list of people with their managers as an Org Chart.
</Tip>
