A team's **Audit Log** answers the questions an admin gets asked: who shared this, who joined, who removed that member, who added that webhook. It is the same log in every liveworkspace app, <AppName /> included, and only the team's Admins can see it.

## Where to Find It

Open your team in the Explorer: it's a space under **Spaces** in the sidebar. Below the team's card, its library is a section headed **Audit Log**, marked "Admins only · kept a year". It starts closed; open it to read the log. Members don't see the section at all, and if you stop being an Admin it tells you the log is for Admins only.

The **Audit Log** is for Admins and holds only team-level events, never what happens inside a <Noun />: no edits or content, just names and ids.

## What It Records

- **People:** an invitation sent, someone joining (by email or the invite link), an invitation declined, a role changed between Admin and Member, someone removed or leaving, and the team renamed.
- **Work:** a share link made (and its level, or a public link for a Community post) or removed, a <Noun /> moved into or out of the team, sent to the Trash, restored, or deleted for good (including an owner's Take Offline).
- **Webhooks:** a webhook added to or removed from one of the team's <Noun plural />, with the address it sends to and its kind (Slack, Microsoft Teams or signed JSON).

Each row says who did it and what, the <Noun /> or person it was about, which app it came from and when. Rows are grouped by day, newest first; hover a time to see it in UTC. Someone whose account has closed shows as **Deleted account**. API tokens and connected apps belong to a person, not a team, so they never show here.

## Filtering

Above the list are four filters, and you can combine them:

- **Person:** who did it. The team's members are listed by name, and anyone else the log names (someone who has since left) is marked "not in the team".
- **Event:** one kind of event, grouped into People, Work and Webhooks.
- **From** and **To:** whole days, both included.

**Clear Filters** puts everything back. The list shows 50 events at a time; **Show More** reads the next 50.

## Exporting as CSV

**Export CSV** saves every event your filters match as one CSV file named after the team and the day, ready for a spreadsheet. Each row has the time in UTC, the event, the app, who did it, whom it was about, the <Noun /> and the detail, each with its id. A very large export (more than 200,000 events) saves the newest 200,000 and asks you to narrow the dates for the rest. The button is off when nothing matches. In a browser that can save straight to a file (Chrome and Edge), it asks where to save first and writes the file as it reads, so even the largest export stays light on memory.

## How Long It Is Kept

Each event is kept for a year from when it happened, then deleted. When someone closes their liveworkspace account, their name is taken out of the log and their rows show as Deleted account.
