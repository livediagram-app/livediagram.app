A team is a named group of signed-in people. Teams belong to your liveworkspace account, so a team is the same in every liveworkspace app, and you make and run them in the Explorer, wherever it shows: the Explorer page and the editor's Explorer panel. Each member has a role, either Admin or Member, and every team gets a shared library that all members can open and edit. Teams are not a paid tier: <AppName /> is free and open source, so the roles are about who may manage the team, not about unlocking features.

## What a Team Is

A team has a name (up to 80 characters) and a list of members. To create one, choose **New Team**, the **+** at the right of the Explorer sidebar's **Spaces** heading (in the editor's Explorer panel, the last row on the rail under Spaces): give the team a name and, if you like, an organisation, and the new team opens. The person who creates a team becomes its first Admin.

While you work, you can also choose **Change Folder** on a <Noun />'s menu, then the **New Team** tile: name the team, and <AppName /> makes it and files the <Noun /> straight into it.

A team is not tied to a single owner, so it carries on even if its creator later leaves.

Teams need a signed-in account because they are keyed to your account identity and invites are matched by email. <AppName /> itself always works without an account, and a share link can still be handed to a guest who edits without signing in.

In the Explorer sidebar, every team you have joined is a space under **Spaces**, beside My Work. Open a team to see its view: the team's card at the top, with its members and their roles, and the shared library below.

A team you join shows up here once your sign-in next refreshes, within 15 minutes (the Explorer reads your teams from it). Signing out and back in brings it at once.

## Roles and Invites

There are two roles. **Admins** can invite people, change roles, rename the team, remove members, and delete the team, all from the team's card. **Members** can see the team and its members, work in the shared library, and leave. A team always keeps an Admin. See [Roles and Invites](/help/collaboration/teams/roles-and-invites/) for the details.

The last remaining Admin cannot step down, or leave while anyone else is in the team, so promote another member first, or delete the team. If you are the only member, leaving deletes the team.

Admins add people by email address at the foot of the team's card, or share a join link with **Invite by Link**. When someone invites you, the invitation shows under **Invites** in the Explorer sidebar, with a count, where you can **Accept** or **Decline** it.

## Leaving or Deleting a Team

- Leaving a team (**Leave Team** on its card's ⋯ menu) takes you out.
- Deleting a team (Admins only, **Delete Team** on the same menu) removes the team and its member list for everyone.

Either way, you land back in My Work with a short message saying so.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Canvases you created in the team library stay with the team (another member, the longest-standing Admin, takes them over), and if you have one of its canvases open, you lose access straight away. When a team is deleted, nobody can open its canvases any more, and none of them move back to My Work, so move any you want to keep out of the team first.
</AppNote>
