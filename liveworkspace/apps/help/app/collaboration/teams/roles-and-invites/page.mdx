Teams belong to your liveworkspace account, so a team is the same in every liveworkspace app, <AppName /> included. You make and run them in the Explorer of <AppName />: **New Team**, the **+** at the right of the sidebar's **Spaces** heading (the last row under Spaces on the editor's Explorer panel rail), makes one, and a team's page runs it: its **People** tab (or **Members** in the editor's Explorer panel). Everything here needs a signed-in account.

## Admin and Member

The person who creates a team is its first **Admin**. On the team's **People** tab, Admins can:

- rename the team (**Edit Team** on its ⋯ menu),
- invite people by email, one or several at once,
- resend an invitation, or invite someone again once theirs has ended,
- change a member's role,
- remove a member or withdraw an invite,
- turn on a join link (the **Invite Link** card),
- delete the team.

**Members** can see the team and its member list, work in the shared library, and leave the team.

A team always keeps an Admin: the last Admin can't step down or leave while others remain. Alone in a team, leaving deletes it. **Leave Team** and **Delete Team** are on the team's ⋯ menu (**Leave Team** is also under the cards at the side of People); afterwards you land in My Work.

The **Invite Link** card on People gives a join link that works for 7 days: anyone signed in who opens it joins as a Member. **Turn On Link**, then **Copy** it, **Make a New Link** (the old one stops working), or **Turn Off** the link. **Copy Link** at the top of the team's page copies it too.

## Inviting by Email

<Step n={1}>
  Open your team in the Explorer (or press **Invite People** at the top of its page). Under **Invite
  by Email**, type or paste one or more addresses (separate them with commas or spaces), choose
  Member or Admin, and press **Send Invite**. If an address can't be invited, it stays in the box
  with the reason, and the rest are still sent.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  They get an email from liveworkspace with a link to the Explorer's **Invites**, where the
  invitation waits. They don't need an account yet: it waits until they sign up with that address.
  Signed in to <AppName />, they also see it under **Invites** in the Explorer sidebar, with a
  count.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  They press **Accept** (which opens the team) or **Decline** on the invitation's card in Invites.
  Accepting takes a signed-in account whose verified email address is the one invited. The email's
  own decline link works without an account.
</Step>

Until they answer, the invitation waits under **Waiting to Join** on People, saying who sent it and when it ends. An invitation ends 28 days after it was sent. **Resend** emails it again and starts the 28 days over (once every 10 minutes at most); **Withdraw** takes it back. Once it has ended or been declined, **Invite Again** sends a new one, and **Remove** clears it from the list.

Once they've joined, the team shows up in their Explorer when their sign-in next refreshes, within 15 minutes; signing out and back in brings it at once.
