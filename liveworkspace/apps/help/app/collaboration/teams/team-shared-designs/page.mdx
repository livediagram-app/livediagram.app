Every team has a shared library: a folder tree plus designs that all joined members can see and manage. It works just like your personal folder tree, only shared. It is the **Library** tab of the team's page in the Explorer.

## What everyone can do

A team design belongs to the whole team, not just to the person who made it. Any **joined** member can:

- create a new design or folder in the library,
- open and edit every design,
- rename, duplicate, and delete a design,
- re-folder a design within the team, or move it to another team they belong to,
- make and manage its [share links](/help/collaboration/sharing/) and its share password.

Deleting a team design moves it to the team's Trash, which every joined member can see in the Trash (the last row of the Explorer sidebar's **Library**) and restore from. See [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/).

Joined membership grants edit access to every design in the library. There are no per-folder or per-member permissions inside a team: everyone who has joined can manage everything. People with a pending, un-accepted invite get nothing until they accept.

Members open a design directly from the library and don't need a link. A share link is for people outside the team.

## How designs are placed

A design lives in exactly one place: your personal tree, or one team's library. Within a team, a design sits either in a folder or at the team's root, listed beside its folders.

- **Moving a design into a team** files it in that team's library and removes it from your personal lists. Only the design's owner can move a personal design into a team, and only into a team they have joined.
- **Moving a design out of a team** into your own personal library makes you its owner, since personal folders are tied to their owner. You need to be a member of that team.
- **Moving a design to another team** needs you to be a member of both teams.
- **Re-foldering within the team** can be done by any joined member.

**Change Folder** opens a browser of every place you can file a design: pick a space first (**My Work**, or one of your teams), then browse into the folder you want, so you can file a design anywhere you have access from one place. A design already in a folder opens the picker where it is, that folder selected. On a team's own page the picker opens on that team, with My Work and your other teams one step back, at **Choose a Space**, so a team design can always be moved back to your personal tree.

## When someone leaves

When a member leaves the team or is removed, the designs and folders they created in the library stay with the team, and the remaining members run them. The person who left loses access within a few minutes, even to a design they have open.

## When a team is deleted

Deleting a team removes it and its member list for everyone, so nobody can open its designs afterwards. They are not moved back to anyone's My Work, so move any you want to keep out of the team first. The team's Library pictures and gallery are deleted for good by a daily clean-up.

A design in a team library wears a **Team** badge in the Explorer instead of **Private**, and the line under the Share dialog's title starts with **Team**, since every joined member can open it. If you also share that design with a link, the Explorer badge reads **Shared** as normal and the Share dialog adds the number of live passes: share links and team membership are separate, independent grants.

<Tip>
  Press **New**, then **New Design**, from within a team folder so the fresh design lands in the
  folder you are browsing rather than at the root of your My Work.
</Tip>
