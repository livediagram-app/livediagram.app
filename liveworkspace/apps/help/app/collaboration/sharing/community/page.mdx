## Sharing It

You need to be signed in to share a <Noun /> to the Community, so every <Noun /> there has a real author. A guest still sees the Share dialog's **Community** tab, with a note that sharing needs an account and a **Sign In to Share** button. Browsing, liking and copying need no account.

<Step n={1}>Open the **Share** dialog on your <Noun />.</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Open the dialog's **Community** tab, beside **Passes**, and choose **Share to Community**.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Give it a title (3 to 80 characters), a description of what it shows and how someone could reuse
  it (20 to 500 characters), a **Category** and up to five **Tags**, then choose **Share to
  Community**.
</Step>

You can also share from the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), for the <Noun plural /> you own. The dialog shows a preview of the card people will see. Once it is shared, a confirmation offers **Copy Link** to copy the Community page's address, and **View Post** to open it.

## What People See

A Community <Noun /> always shows your **latest saved version**, so your edits appear there too. Visitors see it read-only:

- They can look around and make their own copy with **Make a Copy**. Their copy is theirs; it never changes yours.
- They do not join your <Noun /> live, so you won't see strangers' cursors, and you won't get an email for each visitor.
- Private details stay private (see below).

Each card shows how many people liked the <Noun /> and how many made a copy.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Visitors can pan, zoom and switch tabs. **Comments stay private.** They are not shown to Community visitors.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">

The designs people like most also reach **Start Making Pages**: each category's three most liked designs of the past month (with at least five likes in that time) show after its templates, marked **Community**, so a well-loved design can be someone else's starting point. See [Templates](/help/canvas/templates/#starting-from-a-template).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">

A document people really take to is shown beyond the gallery too: the three most liked in each category over the past month, each with at least five likes in that time, appear on [Start Making Pages](/help/canvas/templates/) after that category's templates, marked **Community**, so anyone starting a new document can begin from a copy of it.

Opened from the Community, a <Noun /> is read-only for you too, so you can see it exactly as visitors do and never change it by accident from the gallery. The bar across the top offers **Edit Your <Noun capital />**, which opens your own <Noun />.

## Editing or Removing It

Open the **Share** dialog again, on its **Community** tab. It shows your post with its category, likes and copies, and three buttons:

- **View Post** opens its Community page in a new tab.
- **Edit Listing** changes the title, description, category, tags or **Share Anonymously**. Choose **Save Changes** when you are done.
- **Remove From Community** asks you to confirm, then takes it out of the gallery straight away. Its likes and copy count go with it; copies people already made stay theirs. Choose **Keep It** to back out.

## If Your Post Is Hidden

Anyone can report a post. When three different people, on different networks, report yours, it is taken out of the Community automatically and for good: nobody reviews posts by hand, so the reports decide. The **Community** tab of the **Share** dialog then says **Hidden after reports**, and it shows in [My Shares](/help/collaboration/sharing/finding-community-documents/#your-own-shares) marked **Hidden**. Only you can see it. A hidden post can't be edited or removed, so the same <Noun /> can't be shared again, but your <Noun /> itself is untouched and you can still set a share password on it.

## Things That Can't Be Shared

- A <Noun /> in a **team library** belongs to the team, so the Share dialog has no **Community** tab for it.
- A <Noun /> whose first tab is empty can't be shared until you add something to it.
- A <Noun /> in the **Trash** disappears from the Community until you restore it.
