Some <Noun plural /> need a sign-off. **Request Approval** asks the people you name to approve a <Noun />, each answering **Approve** or **Request Changes**. The <Noun /> keeps saying where it stands after it is approved.

Think of a decision record, a policy or a review. The <Noun /> wears a badge saying where it stands.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Think of a project plan, a budget, a roadmap, before the work starts. A plan is approved as a whole: its boards, Sheets, Dashboards and Forms together.
</AppNote>

## Asking for Approval

<Step n={1}>
  Open the **Share** dialog on the <Noun /> and choose its **Approval** tab.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Tick the people who should approve it under **Approvers**. The list holds your team's members,
  then the <Noun />'s owner and anyone who came in through a share link that opens every tab; each
  shows how they reach it (Team, Owner or Link). Once there are more than eight, a **Filter people**
  field appears.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Add a **Note** about what to look at, and a **Due** day if you hope for an answer by then. The due
  day is shown, never enforced: when it passes, the banner says **Overdue**.
</Step>
<Step n={4}>
  Tick **Save this version as a snapshot** to keep the <Noun /> as it is now, then press **Request
  Approval**. The snapshot is named "Sent for approval" and the request points at it, so you can
  come back to exactly what was asked about ([Snapshots](/help/collaboration/snapshots/)).
</Step>

Each approver gets an email, "Ada asked you to approve Q3 Plan", with your note, the due day and a button that opens the <Noun />. It goes to people who have **Someone Assigns Me an Action** switched on in their [email notifications](/help/account-and-data/email-notifications/), since an approval asks something of them as an assigned action does. You can name up to 10 people, never yourself, and a request is limited to 10 a day on one <Noun />.

Only an Editor of the whole <Noun /> can ask. The tab is not there for <Noun plural /> kept in **This Browser**, in an embed, or opened through a link that covers one tab.

## Approving or Requesting Changes

An approver opens the <Noun /> and sees a banner across the top of the page: who asked, the note and the due day.

- **Approve** records their approval.
- **Request Changes** opens a field in place, "What needs to change?". Write what you would like different and press **Send**; it stays off until there is a note.

Once they have answered, the banner says so ("You approved this. Waiting on 2 more.") and offers **Change Decision**, so an approver who asked for changes can approve once they are made, and the other way round. Only people named on the request can answer, and a Viewer can: approving is the one write a Viewer has.

## Where It Stands

The badge is the first thing in the card at the top right of the <Noun />, before **Share** and your account. It is there for everyone who can open the <Noun />, and for an Editor of the whole <Noun /> it opens the Approval tab. Hover it to see who asked, the due day and who has and hasn't answered. On a phone it shows its symbol alone.

The **Approval** tab shows the same state at its top, with who asked and when, and the due day.

<Figure caption="The four states a request can be in.">
  <ApprovalBadges />
</Figure>

- **Awaiting 1 of 3**: waiting on answers; the numbers say how many have approved.
- **Changes Requested**: at least one approver asked for changes.
- **Approved**: everyone approved and the <Noun /> has not changed since.
- **Changed Since Approval**: the <Noun /> was saved after it was approved. This can take **up to a minute** to show after the first save, because an edit reaches the badge when the editor next reports its changes, at most once a minute. A rename, a move or a share does not count as a change.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  A change here is a card moved or a cell typed in.
</AppNote>

The page reads the approval when you open the <Noun />, again when you return to the browser tab (at most every 30 seconds) and after your own changes. Someone else's answer shows on the next read; nothing is pushed live.

## Cancelling or Asking Again

With a request open, the Approval tab lists each approver with their answer (**Approved**, **Changes Requested** or **Waiting**) and their note. **Cancel Request** asks you to confirm, then ends it. **New Request** opens the form again and replaces the open one, so a <Noun /> never has two. A request waits on everyone named, even a person who has since lost access: cancel it and ask again without them.

## History and the Audit Log

Every step is written to the <Noun />'s **History**: Approval Requested, Approved (Approved by everyone on the last one), Changes Requested, and Approval Cancelled (Approval Asked Again when a new request replaced it), each with the note beneath. It covers the last 30 days. On a team's <Noun />, the same events are copied to the team's [Audit Log](/help/collaboration/teams/audit-log/), kept for a year, so who asked whom and who approved is a record the team can look back on.

## Who Can Do What

- **Editors of the whole <Noun />** ask and cancel.
- **Named approvers** approve or request changes, whatever their level: Editor, Participant or Viewer.
- Guests, Community visitors, embeds, <Noun plural /> in This Browser, and AI assistants or scripts using an API token see no approval.

Editors also see the badge, and everyone else who can open the <Noun /> sees the badge only.
