Sharing turns a <Noun /> into something other people can open. A share link lets anyone with the URL join your <Noun /> in real time, with everyone's presence and edits flowing between them. You can protect a link with a password, give it an expiry date, or embed the <Noun /> in another page. None of this needs the other person to sign in: a share link can be handed to a guest who edits without an account, and you can create links as a guest too.

<AppNote app="liveknowledge">
  In liveknowledge, a Knowledgebase's pages are what the Share dialog calls tabs: a link opens every page unless you [scope it to one](/help/collaboration/sharing/one-tab/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  Everyone who joins is walking, talking and seeing each other live.
</AppNote>

## Share Links

The dialog draws every link as a **pass**, a ticket that lets whoever holds it in. Each pass carries a role, which sets what its holder can do:

- An **Editor** pass lets the visitor change the <Noun /> with you, live.
- A **Participant** pass lets them take part without reshaping the <Noun />.
- A **Viewer** pass lets them watch along.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">
Under **Issue a pass**, press the role card and pick what the pass lets people do from the three roles it unfolds, then press **Create Pass**. The link is created and copied in one go, ready to paste. The role is printed on the coloured band across the pass's top.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
A **Viewer** pass lets them watch, pan and zoom, follow along and point with the laser, and nothing else: no [comments](/help/collaboration/comments/) or changes. Hand out a Participant pass to anyone who should chime in.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livehuddle">
  The line under the dialog's title always tells you who can get in right now: **Private** while only you can open it, **Team** while it sits in a team's library, **Shared** once any pass is live, and **Public** while it is listed in the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The line under the dialog's title always tells you who can get in right now: **Private** while only you can open it, **Shared** once a pass is live on a canvas outside a team, **Team** while it sits in a team's library (with a count of any live passes), and **Public** while it is listed in the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/).

A **Participant** pass lets them take part without reshaping the board. They add stickies, text and images, grow mind maps with **Tab**, **Enter** or **Add Child** and **Add Sibling**, add steps and connectors to an existing flowchart, undo and redo their own changes, write on anything (a table's cells included) except a behaviour element such as a poll, move and resize stickies and images, recolour stickies, swap an image's picture, cast dots, answer [session tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/), [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) and send reactions. They can delete what they added and any mind-map node, nothing else of other people's, and they cannot change the tab's mode, add or remove tabs, move or restyle other people's shapes and frames (mind-map nodes aside), spin a picker, press Bring Focus or hold the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) baton. Their palette has one category, **Participate**, holding just what they can add, and the selection modes they need: Select, Hand, Laser, Spotlight, Isometric and Zen, plus Avatar while they walk (entered from an Avatar button on the board). It is the pass to hand a team for a retrospective or a workshop.

A **Viewer** pass lets them watch, pan and zoom, follow along and point with the laser, and nothing else: no [comments](/help/collaboration/comments/), votes, [polls](/help/collaboration/session-tools/polls/) or changes. Hand out a Participant pass to anyone who should chime in.

## Controlling Access

Two self-serve controls let you bound who gets in and for how long:

- A [share password](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/) makes every link prompt for a password before it opens.
- A [share link expiry](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-link-expiry/) gives a link a lifetime (a week, a month, or six months). When it runs out the link stops working, but stays in your dialog so you can extend or delete it.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The canvas's owner, and the members of its team, never need the password.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  The document's owner, and the members of its team, never need the password.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The space's owner, and the members of its team, never need the password.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">The design's owner always bypasses the password.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  At the foot of the dialog, **Password Protection** puts one password on every link to the plan.
</AppNote>

These are independent: you can use either, both, or neither. They are layered on top of the link role, not a replacement for it. [Share Link Security](/help/privacy-and-security/share-link-security/) explains what a link does and does not protect.

## Embedding

An [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) puts a live copy of a <Noun /> inside another page, like a wiki, a doc, or Notion. Where an `<iframe>` isn't allowed, like a GitHub README, use a [live image](/help/collaboration/sharing/live-image/) from the pass's **Live image** menu instead. Both are copied from a pass's **Embed** and **Live image** menus.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The embed honours the link's role: an Editor pass embeds an editable canvas, while a Participant or Viewer pass embeds a read-only one with pan and zoom. Either way it hides the editor chrome and updates as the canvas is edited. Each pass's **Embed** menu offers **Copy embed URL** for the bare URL and **Copy iframe** for a ready-made snippet to paste.

## Sharing with Everyone

Proud of a <Noun />? [Share it to the Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/), the public gallery where anyone can find it, view it read-only and make their own copy. Looking for a starting point instead? [Find <Noun plural /> in the Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/finding-community-documents/) by searching their words and tags. The Share dialog's **Community** tab, beside **Passes**, publishes it, edits its listing and takes it down again. It is there for your own <Noun />, not a team <Noun />; a guest sees a note and **Sign In to Share** there instead.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  While a plan is listed, the editor's header and the Explorer show it as **Public**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  **Asking for Sign-Off.** The Share dialog's **Approval** tab asks named people to approve the
  document, and a badge in the header shows where their answers stand. See [Request
  Approval](/help/collaboration/sharing/request-approval/).
</AppNote>

## Stopping Sharing

To stop a link working, press the bin (**Revoke link**) on its pass and confirm with **Revoke**. Anyone viewing through it is sent out of the <Noun /> straight away, and the URL stops resolving. A revoked link can't be brought back: create a new pass if you change your mind, which gives a new URL.

<Note>
  A [<Noun /> kept Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) lives only in your browser, so
  there is nothing on the server to share yet. Opening **Share** on one shows a gate instead of the
  link controls, with a button that uploads it to your account first.
</Note>
