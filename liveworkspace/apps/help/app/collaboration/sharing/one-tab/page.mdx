A share link normally opens the whole <Noun />, every tab of it. Sometimes you only want to show one. A link scoped to one tab does exactly that, without copying the tab into a new <Noun />.

## Scoping a New Link

When a <Noun /> has more than one tab, the **Share** dialog gains an **Opens** dropdown just above **Valid**. Leave it on **All tabs** for an ordinary link, or pick the tab you want to share, then **Create Pass**. The role still applies to a one-tab link.

You can make as many links as you like, each scoped differently: one tab to one person, another tab to someone else, and an All tabs link for your team. A link opens one tab or all of them; it can't open a chosen handful.

## What the Visitor Sees

Someone opening a one-tab link lands straight on that tab. The other tabs stay in their tab bar as **Not shared** tabs, so they know the <Noun /> has more, but without names, and nothing happens when they press one (hovering says "This tab isn't shared with you").

The other tabs are private, not just hidden. Their content never reaches the visitor's browser: not their names, not their live edits. With an **Editor** pass the visitor can change their tab, but not rename, add, reorder or delete tabs, or rename the <Noun />.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments">
  Their shapes and comments never reach the browser either. The visitor also can't lock tabs, use
  tab folders, or open the slide deck (a deck spans tabs).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">

  tab folders.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  Their pieces, and the people walking there, never reach the browser either. The visitor also can't
  use tab folders.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">

  lock tabs.
</AppNote>

The scope follows the link everywhere it goes: an [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) of the link shows its tab only, and a [live image](/help/collaboration/sharing/live-image/) of the link always shows its tab, so its menu has no **Tab** picker.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle">

</AppNote>

</AppNote>

## Changing a Link's Tabs

On a <Noun /> with more than one tab, every pass in the Share dialog shows which tabs it opens, beside **Opens**. Change it there at any time: widen a one-tab link to **All tabs**, narrow an All tabs link to one tab, or move it to another tab. The link's URL stays the same, and anyone using it right now reloads into the new choice.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Every pass in the Share dialog's **Passes** list has a tab menu, and the change takes effect at
  once.
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  Deleting the tab a link is scoped to deletes that link too. Anyone using it leaves the <Noun />,
  just as if you had revoked it.
</Tip>
