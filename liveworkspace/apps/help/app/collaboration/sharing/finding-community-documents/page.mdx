## Searching

One search box holds everything you are looking for, written as words:

- **Plain words** search the title, description and tags.
- **`#tag`** finds <Noun plural /> with that tag. Add several and a <Noun /> has to have all of them.
- **`category:name`** keeps to one kind of <Noun />.
- **`sort:loved`** puts the most liked <Noun plural /> first, and **`sort:copied`** the most copied. Without one, the newest come first.
- **`is:mine`** shows only the <Noun plural /> you have shared (see [Your own shares](#your-own-shares)).

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  For example `checkout flow`, `#aws #event-driven`, and `category:planning` for a subject such as
  Planning, Meetings or Software.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  For example `cafe logo`, `#logo #minimal`, and `category:websites` for a kind of design such as
  Branding, Websites or Photos.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  For example `quarterly report`, `#pitch #startup`, and `category:pitches` for a kind of document
  such as Pitches or Reports.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  For example `team pod`, `#standup #remote`, and `category:office` for a kind of space such as
  Office, Campus or Recreation.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  For example `sprint board`, `#scrum #retro`, and `category:finance`. The categories are the

  and People, plus Something Else.

You can type these yourself, or use the buttons inside the right of the search box, which write the same words for you:

<Step n={2}>

<Step n={3}>

## Your Own Shares

Signed in, choose **My Shares** in the search box to see only the <Noun plural /> you have shared. Above them, **Your Shares** adds up how many you have shared, and how many likes and copies they have had altogether, so you can see how they are doing. Each card shows its own likes and copies, and you can still search, filter and sort within them.

A <Noun /> taken out of the Community after reports shows here marked **Hidden**; only you can see it. Choose it to open the <Noun /> in the editor.

## Liking a Post

Choose the heart on a card or on a post's page to show the author you liked it. Choose it again to take your like back. Likes are kept per browser, and **Most Loved** sorts by them.

## Making Your Own Copy

Choose **Make a Copy** on a post's page, or in the bar across the top of an open <Noun />. A copy of the whole <Noun /> opens in the editor as your own <Noun />, named after the post you chose. Change anything you like; the original never changes. Each person who copies a <Noun /> counts once towards its copy count. You don't need an account: as a guest the copy belongs to this browser's guest identity, and it moves into your account if you [sign up](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/) later.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">The copy has every tab.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">The copy has every tab and page.</AppNote>

A post's page also lists **More Like This**: other <Noun plural /> in the same category to look at next.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  You can also start from the Community without leaving the editor. On an empty tab, choose
  **Inspiration** in **Start Planning**: after the templates come the plans people liked most this
  month, up to three from each category, each marked **Community**. Searching Start Planning finds
  them by their titles too. Choosing one makes your own copy, just as **Make a Copy** does.
</AppNote>

## Reporting a Post

If a <Noun /> is spam, offensive, shows someone's personal information or isn't the author's to share, choose **Report** on its page. Pick a reason (**Spam**, **Offensive**, **Personal Information**, **Copyright** or **Something Else**), add a note of up to 300 characters if it helps, and send it with **Send Report**. A <Noun /> reported by three different people, on different networks, is hidden automatically, for good. Nobody reviews posts by hand: the reports decide.

<AppNote app="livediagram">The report dialog is titled **Report This Canvas**.</AppNote>

## Posting in the Forum

Beside the gallery, the **Forum** tab is where people using the apps share tips, show what they've made, suggest ideas, report bugs, ask questions and talk with each other. There is one Forum for every liveworkspace app. Each post has a category (Question, Tips & Tricks, Feature Request, Bug Report, Show & Tell or General) and the app it is about, shown by its logo, or **Any App**. Anyone can read it without an account; filter by category or app, or sort by **Latest Activity**, **Newest** or **Unanswered**. On a phone, the filters and sort sit behind one **Filters** button.

To post, reply or react you need to be signed in to your account (a guest can read but not post). Choose **New Post**, give it a title, pick a category and the app it is about, write your post and choose **Post**. Your name shows on everything you post. Under each post and reply, **Helpful**, **Thanks** and **Celebrate** let you say what you thought of it. On a Question, its author can choose **Mark as Answer** on the reply that solved it, so it shows first with an **Answered** mark in the list; **Unanswered** lists the Questions still waiting for one.

Every post, reply and edit is checked automatically before anyone else sees it. If the check is busy, your post shows to you alone as **Pending: being checked** until it is checked again a few minutes later; one that doesn't pass stays visible to you alone, to edit or delete. You can **Edit** or **Delete** your own posts at any time, and closing your account removes them all. Each account can write up to 20 posts and replies an hour.

If a post is spam, offensive or shows someone's personal information, choose **Report** on it. As in the gallery, a post reported by three different people, on different networks, is hidden automatically.
