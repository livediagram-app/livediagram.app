A share link is a standing invitation: anyone holding the URL keeps the link's role for as long as it lives. A lifetime bounds that, so a link stops working on its own without you having to remember to revoke it. It complements the [share password](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/) as a second self-serve control.

## Choosing a Lifetime

When you create a link in the **Share** dialog, the **Valid** control sets its lifetime, right beside **Create Pass**. You can pick:

- **Forever**, the default: the link lasts until you revoke it.
- **1 week**
- **1 month**
- **6 months**

The countdown starts when you press **Create Pass**. Pick the shortest span that fits, like a week for a workshop group or a month for a review. Each pass in the dialog's **Passes** list shows how long it has left, like "6d left", or **Forever**.

## What Happens When a Link Expires

When a link's lifetime runs out, it stops opening the <Noun /> everywhere at once: the share URL, any [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) and [live image](/help/collaboration/sharing/live-image/) made from it, and the realtime room. People who already have the <Noun /> open are not thrown out at the exact moment it expires. If you need someone out straight away, revoke the link instead.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  People who already have the plan open lose access within a few minutes.
</AppNote>

## Expired Links: Extend or Delete

A link that has run out moves to the dialog's **Expired** list. Press **Extend** on it, named for the lifetime the link was created with (**Extend 1 week**, say), to open it again for another round of that lifetime, counted from now. The link keeps its URL and moves back up to your live passes, so anyone who already has it can use it again.

To get rid of an expired link for good, press its bin. A live pass is stopped with its bin (**Revoke link**).

  Visitors see the same not-found page as for a revoked link. People who already have the canvas open lose access the next time they load the page or their changes are saved.

  No warning or email is sent before a link expires: the countdown chip in the Share dialog is where to see how long a link has left.

  Expired links move into an **Expired** section below your live passes, greyed out with an **Expired** stamp beside the struck-through URL. **Delete** removes one straight away, without a confirmation. The Expired section only appears when there is something in it.

  A link's lifetime can't be changed once it is created, and a link set to **Forever** can't be given an expiry later. To change either, revoke the link and create a new one with the lifetime you want (the new pass has a new URL).
</AppNote>
