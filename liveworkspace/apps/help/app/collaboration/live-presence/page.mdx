## Live Cursors

You see everyone else's pointer move around the <Noun />, each labelled with their name in their own colour. Cursors are tied to a tab, so you only see the cursors of the people looking at the same tab as you. When someone moves to a different tab, their cursor follows them there.

If a cursor or its name is in the way of what you want to see, move your pointer onto it: it fades out while your pointer is there and comes back when you move on. It never blocks a click, so you can click straight through it. On a touch screen there is no pointer to hover, so cursors stay put.

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  Someone walking in Avatar mode shows their character instead, with no cursor, so the cursors you
  see are Editors building the Space (Participants and Viewers always walk).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Other people's cursors show only on a Dashboard tab. Board and Sheet tabs show no one's pointer:
  the card rings and cell outlines described below do that job there.
</AppNote>

## Who Is Here, and Where

Each person is given a colour and a name. Signed-in people show their account name; everyone else gets a friendly auto-generated name like "Curious Otter". You can see who is around in two places:

- **On each tab**, small initials show who is currently looking at that tab, so you can tell at a glance where everyone is working.
- A coloured ring around someone shows whether they are **active**, **away** (after five quiet minutes), or have gone **offline**. Hover an avatar for their name and status.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">The initials sit on each tab in the tab bar.</AppNote>

## Collaborators

Click any avatar on a tab, or the **+N** badge when a tab is busy, to open **Collaborators**. It lists everyone in the <Noun /> grouped by the tab they are on, so you can see where the whole room is at once rather than hovering tab by tab. The person you clicked is highlighted.

- Your own tab is marked **You're Here**. Every other tab has a **Go to Tab** button that takes you straight there.
- Each person shows their name, their role when it is known, whether they are online, away or offline, and how long ago they were last active.
- **Hide Participant Cursors**, at the foot of Collaborators, stops showing everyone else's cursor and name on your screen, for when a busy session gets distracting. Avatars and Follow stay, and others still see your cursor. It is the same switch as in **Settings**, under **Collaborate**, and it is remembered in this browser only.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  **Follow** beside someone makes your view follow theirs (it changes to **Stop Following** while
  you do). See [Following someone](/help/canvas/follow-along/) for how it works. The buttons to hand
  out the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) role live here too, on each person's row.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The switch sits in **Settings** › **Panels**, under **Collaborate**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  The switch sits in **Settings** › **Panels**, in its **Collaborate** section. Because cursors show
  only on a Dashboard, the switch is there only while you are on a Dashboard. On a Board, Sheet or
  Form tab it is hidden, and your choice is kept for when you come back.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
  **Follow** beside someone makes your view follow theirs (it changes to **Stop Following** while
  you do). See [Following someone](/help/canvas/follow-along/) for how it works.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
**Nudge** beside someone gets their attention: their screen plays a chime and shows "Sam nudged you" with a **Go to Sam** button that takes them to you. See **Nudging Someone** below.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Hearing People Come and Go.** When someone arrives in the Space you have open, you hear a soft chime, and a softer one when they leave. It plays even while the Space is on another browser tab, so you can get on with other work and still hear when someone comes in to talk to you.

- You hear the chime when you arrive too, so you know you're in. Someone reloading or briefly losing their connection is silent.
- When several people arrive together you hear one chime, not one each.
- Browsers keep a page silent until you have clicked or typed on it once, so click anywhere in the Space after opening it.
- Turn it off in **Settings** › **Account** › **Notifications**, with **Join and Leave Sounds**. **Do Not Disturb** quiets it too.

**Nudging Someone.** Press **Nudge** beside someone in **Collaborators** to get their attention. You can nudge each person once every two minutes: the button reads **Nudged** until then, and its tooltip counts down to when you can nudge again. Editors and Participants can nudge; Viewers can't, but anyone can be nudged.

When someone nudges you:

- You hear a chime, louder than the join chime, even with the Space on another tab.
- A note says who nudged you, with a **Go to** button (such as **Go to Sam**) that switches to their tab and centres your view on them.
- If the Space is on a tab in the background, your computer shows a desktop notification, "Sam nudged you in Weekly Standup". Clicking it brings the tab back and goes to them.

Desktop notifications need your browser's permission, which is only ever asked when you press something: the **Desktop Notifications** switch in **Settings** (beside **Join and Leave Sounds**), or the one-time offer the first time you nudge or are nudged. If your browser blocks notifications, Settings says so, and the sound and the note still work. On **Do Not Disturb** a nudge still shows its note, silently and with no desktop notification.
</AppNote>

## Seeing What Others Are Working On

When someone selects something, you can tell, and it is gently locked for everyone else, so two people can't fight over the same thing at once.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">
  A small disc with their initials appears on the element's top-left corner, so you always know what a teammate is working on. While an Editor or Participant has it selected, that element is locked for everyone else. A Viewer's selection only shows the disc. If they select several elements, every one of them is locked, not just the one they clicked. Letting go does not free an element quite at once: while the change they just made is still being saved (a moment, and never more than about 10 seconds if the save keeps failing) it stays locked, so nobody's edit is lost. Otherwise it frees up again as soon as they click away. When you zoom far out, the discs hide until you zoom back in.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  What you see depends on the tab:

- **On a Board**, a card someone has open or is dragging carries a ring in their colour and their first name, so you know a teammate is in it. Cards they move and fields they change update on your screen as they go.
- **On a Sheet**, each other person's selected cells are outlined in their colour, with their name on a small tag. A cell someone is typing in has a thicker outline. Two people typing in different cells never overwrite each other.
- **On a Dashboard**, you see everyone else's pointer move, labelled with their name in their colour. If someone's pointer or name is in the way, move your pointer onto it: it fades out until you move on, and a click goes straight through it.

Someone who joins late sees what is already held: the cards others have open and the cells they have selected.

**Held while someone else has it.** When an Editor or a Participant selects something, it is gently **held** for everyone else. It wears the holder's colour as an outline and you can't select, drag or edit it until they let go. A few details:

- **A whole selection is held.** If they select several things at once, every one of them is held, not just the one they clicked.
- **A Viewer holds nothing.** A Viewer's selection only shows who is looking.
- **Letting go isn't quite instant.** While the change they just made is still being saved, it stays held so nobody's edit is lost: a moment, and never more than about 10 seconds if the save keeps failing. Otherwise it frees up as soon as they click away, switch tab or leave.
- **It is a guard, not a guarantee.** The hold keeps collisions rare, but it can't stop two people racing within the same instant, or a script using an [API token](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/), which never sees it. If two people do change the same thing, the last change to arrive wins.

If something stays held for good, its holder has probably wandered off with it still selected: ask them to click away. See [Real-Time Collaboration Problems](/help/troubleshooting/collaboration-issues/).

</AppNote>

## Freeing an Element Someone Left Selected

The lock clears by itself when the person clicks away, switches tab or leaves. What it can't fix on its own is someone who is still connected but has wandered off with something selected, leaving an element nobody else can touch.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  liveplanner has no menu to free a hold: ask the person holding it to click away.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle,livedesign,livedocuments">
  When that happens, it can be freed:

<Step n={1}>Right-click the locked element.</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  A small **In use** menu opens, with one row per person holding it, such as "Release Jae's Hold".
</Step>
<Step n={3}>Choose that row. The element frees up for everyone straight away.</Step>

**What the holder sees.** As the menu says: "They keep their work; it just stops being theirs to edit." Their selection is dropped, and if they were typing into the element, that editing ends too, but the element itself is not changed. A short notification tells them their hold was freed, so it doesn't look like a glitch. Nobody else in the room is told anything; the element simply stops looking locked.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  **Who can do it.** While someone is the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/), only they
  can. While nobody is facilitating, any Editor can. Participants and Viewers can't free a hold: for
  them, right-clicking a locked element opens nothing, as it does for an Editor while somebody else
  is facilitating. The holder's notification reads "The facilitator freed an element you were
  holding".
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">

  else holds it.
</AppNote>

## Agents at Work

An AI agent or a script working on a <Noun /> with an [API token](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) can show itself while it works. It appears as an avatar of its own, named after its owner ("Webber's Agent") in their colour and marked with a small star, on the tab it last read or changed:

- **What it is doing** shows in its hover card, on its row in Collaborators, and in what a screen reader announces. Everyone sees it, its owner included.
- **It leaves on its own** a few minutes after it last read or changed the <Noun />, so a finished or stopped agent never lingers.

Agents are listed in Collaborators, but they are not counted as people.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  **What it is looking at** is outlined with a thin ring in its owner's colour. The ring never locks
  anything: you can keep working on those elements. Agents can't be followed, and never hold the
  facilitator role.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
  **What it is looking at** is outlined with a thin ring in its owner's colour. The ring never locks
  anything: you can keep working on those elements. Agents can't be followed.
</AppNote>

## Who Can See Presence

Live presence works for everyone in a shared <Noun />, including guests and people opening a [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) to view, not just signed-in editors. A private <Noun /> you have not shared has no presence, since it is just you, except for your own agent while it works on it.

<Tip>
  Names and colours are chosen automatically, and a colour is nudged if two people happen to get the
  same one, so everyone stays easy to tell apart.
</Tip>
