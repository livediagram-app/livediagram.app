Some things are quicker said than written. **Start a Call Here** opens a livehuddle video call about the item you have open, in a new browser tab, so the item stays where it is in its own tab. Everyone who opens the item sees the call is on and can join it. When you are done, the call's summary goes to a knowledgebase and its actions to a plan, and the item lists the call under **Meetings**.

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  A Space is already a place to meet, so it has no Call button of its own. A call started from a
  diagram, a plan, a document, a design or a knowledgebase opens here, as a Space of its own, and
  this article's **In the call** and **Wrapping up** sections are what you see in it.
</AppNote>

## Starting a call

<Step n={1}>
  Press **Call** in the editor's top-right corner (on a phone, open **Apps** and tap **Call**).
  Nothing starts yet: a **Start a Call** popover asks where to meet.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Under **Where to Meet**, your usual choice is already picked: **A New Space**, or your Calls
  Space if you chose one in Settings. Pick another of your Spaces to meet there this time; it
  changes only this call, never your setting. With many Spaces, **Find a Space** narrows the list.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Press **Start Call** (or Enter). A new tab opens on the call, and you are in it. A new Space is
  made for the call and named after the item: a room built for meeting round it, with chairs facing
  a big wall [Screen](/help/palette/screen/) with the item on it, live for everyone in the room who
  can open it. **Cancel**, Escape or a click outside the popover closes it with nothing started.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Everyone else with the item open sees a green **In Call** pill with how many people are in it, and
  **Join**. Join brings up the call in its own tab, or the tab you already have it in.
</Step>

You need to be signed in to start a call. Anyone who can view the item can join it, a guest through a share link included, who joins as a guest. Calls are not offered in an embed, for an item saved only in this browser (Offline), or on Community.

## Choosing where your calls open

By default each item gets a Space of its own for its call. To meet in a Space you already use, open **Settings**, choose **Calls** under Account, and pick a Space under **Calls Open In**. The list shows the livehuddle Spaces you or your teams own, each with its picture. The choice is kept on your account, so it applies in every app and on every device.

From then on, **Call** has that Space picked when it asks where to meet, and everyone who joins from the item lands there too. You can still pick **A New Space** or another Space for one call. A few things to know:

- If a call about the item is already on, the button reads **Join** and takes you straight in, with nothing to choose.
- A Space holds one item's call at a time. If yours is in a call about something else, the new call gets a Space of its own and a note says so.
- People who join from the item get into the Space through a call link that stops working a week after your latest call there. You can stop it sooner from the Space's **Share**.
- If the Space is deleted, archived or you can no longer use it, Call goes back to a new Space each time, and **Settings › Calls** shows the choice as **Unavailable** until you pick another.

Choose **A New Space for Each Call** to go back to the default.

## In the call

The Space's top-right card leads with the item: its app's logo and name. **Open Item** brings back the item's tab (or opens it). **Invite** picks teammates to ask in: each gets an entry in their Inbox under **Calls**, whose **Join Call** takes them straight in while the call is on. Anyone in the call who is signed in can invite, unless they reached the Space by a view-only link: a Viewer watches and invites no one.

Calls are never recorded. A notice says so when you first open the call.

## Wrapping up

Whoever started the call has **Wrap Up** in the same card.

<Step n={1}>
  Check the **Summary**. It is drafted from the Space's notes: its stickies and text, floor by floor,
  with headings for **Decisions** and **Actions**. **Write With AI** rewrites it from the notes when
  AI is on for you; your draft stays if AI can't help.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick where it goes under **Send To**: a knowledgebase you can edit. The one connected to the item
  is suggested, else your team's newest. Press **Send Summary**: it becomes a page there, with **Open
  Page**.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Press **Send Actions to Plan** to make a liveplanner card from each sticky on the floor, each linked
  back (see [Send to Plan](/help/collaboration/send-to-plan/)).
</Step>

## When the call ends

A Space made for a call is there for that call alone. Once everyone has left and nobody has come back for two minutes, the call has ended and the Space goes to your **Trash**, usually within a quarter of an hour. Anything drawn on it goes with it, so wrap up before you leave: the summary and the actions live in their knowledgebase and plan, and the item keeps the call under **Meetings**. Rejoin within the two minutes and the call and its Space carry on. The next **Call** makes a new Space.

To keep a Space made for a call, rename it, move it to a folder or a team, archive it, or choose it as your Calls Space; you can also restore it from the Trash for 30 days. Your Calls Space and every Space you made yourself are never deleted this way.

## Meetings

Open **Tools** and choose **Meetings** (under Collaborate) to see the item's calls, newest first: when each started and how long it ran, who was in it, and its summary and plan when you can open them. A call that is still on has **Join**.
