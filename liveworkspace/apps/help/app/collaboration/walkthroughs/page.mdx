A walkthrough is a recording of you talking someone through the <Noun />. It films your browser tab as a video, with your voice and, if you leave it on, your camera (shown as a round bubble in a corner). Whoever plays it watches the video, and you can share it by a public link.

## Recording a Walkthrough

<Step n={1}>
  Open **Tools** in the footer, press **Walkthroughs**, then **Record Walkthrough**. The set-up opens
  beside Tools.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick your **Microphone**. The **Camera** switch starts off, so a walkthrough records your voice
  alone and the browser asks only for the microphone; turn it on to pick a camera, and choose which
  corner its round bubble sits in. The set-up remembers your choice in this browser. If the browser
  hasn't been allowed to list your devices yet, press **Allow Microphone** (or **Allow Camera**) to
  ask it. **Mic Level** moves as you speak, so you know you are heard.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Press **Start Recording**. Your browser asks which to share: choose this tab. After a 3, 2, 1
  countdown, talk and move around the <Noun /> as you would with someone beside you; the tab is
  filmed as you see it, and the browser shows its own "sharing this tab" bar. Press **Finish** when
  you are done, or stop sharing from the browser's bar. **Discard** throws the recording away.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Finishing saves it at once as "Walkthrough of" and the tab's name, with a progress card while it
  uploads. It is listed under Walkthroughs in Tools for everyone who can open the <Noun />. To
  rename it, press its **⋯** and then **Rename**.
</Step>

A walkthrough can run for up to ten minutes; the bar turns amber with a minute left and finishes on its own at ten. With subscriptions on, a tier sets the length instead (5 minutes on Free, 30 on Pro and Pro for Teams, the better of yours and the tier of whoever owns the <Noun />), and Free keeps five walkthroughs. If saving is refused (it is too large, or you have reached your limit), the card says why and offers **Download a Copy** so the recording isn't lost. A browser that can't share a tab, such as Safari, says so in the set-up and records nothing.

## Hiding Elements From the Recording

Press **Hide From Recording** in the set-up, then press each element viewers shouldn't see: a private note, an unfinished corner. **Hide Selection** hides what is already selected, and **Done** or `Esc` leaves the mode. While you choose, you see hidden elements faded inside a dashed outline. Once recording starts they are hidden on your own screen too, so the video never holds them. They stay on the <Noun /> itself for anyone who opens it.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  What a press picks depends on the tab: an element on a plan tab (a card on a board drawn there is
  the card), a card on a **Board** tab, a widget on a **Dashboard** tab. **Hide Selection** also
  hides the cards you have selected on a board. A **Sheet** or **Form** tab has nothing to hide, so
  the set-up leaves Hide From Recording out there.
</AppNote>

## Playing a Walkthrough

Open **Tools** in the footer, press **Walkthroughs**, and press one to play it. It opens in a player over the <Noun /> with its title, who recorded it and when, and the browser's own controls to play, seek and change speed. **Full Screen** fills your screen, and **Close** puts you back where you were.

Walkthroughs recorded before screen recording came in are replays, not videos: they play over the <Noun /> itself, following the presenter's pointer, pans, zooms and tab changes (and, in livediagram and livedocuments, slide steps), with their camera bubble or a chip with their name in the corner. Pan, zoom or press on the <Noun /> and a replay pauses, leaving the view with you; **Resume** puts the view back where the recording is. `Space` plays and pauses, `←` and `→` skip five seconds, `Esc` closes, and the bar has a scrubber and a speed of 1x, 1.5x or 2x. If the <Noun /> has changed since, a line says so and it still plays over the <Noun /> as it is now.

## Sharing a Walkthrough

Press **Share** in the player, or **⋯** and then **Share** on its row in Tools, and turn on **Public Link**. Press **Copy Link** and send it: anyone with the link watches the video without signing in, and sees its title and who made it, never the <Noun /> itself. Turn **Public Link** off and the link stops working at once. Replays have no public link.

## Who Can Record and Delete

Editors record walkthroughs and can rename, share and delete any of them. Everyone who can open the <Noun /> can play them, and anyone can rename, share or delete one they recorded. Walkthroughs aren't offered on a phone, in an embed, for a <Noun /> kept **Offline**, or through a link to one tab.

## Privacy

Calls are never recorded. A walkthrough is only made when you press Start Recording, and it is stored with the <Noun /> for the people who can open it, and for anyone you give its public link. Deleting a walkthrough, purging its <Noun /> from the Trash or deleting your account deletes it. See the [privacy policy](https://www.liveworkspace.app/privacy).
