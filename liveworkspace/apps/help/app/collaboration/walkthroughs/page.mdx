A walkthrough is a recording of you talking someone through the <Noun />. It keeps your voice, your camera if you leave it on (shown as a round bubble in a corner), your pointer, and every pan, zoom and tab change you make. Whoever plays it watches it over the <Noun /> itself, not in a video, so they can pause, look around and resume.

## Recording a Walkthrough

<Step n={1}>
  Open **Tools** in the footer, press **Walkthroughs**, then **Record Walkthrough**. The recording
  dock opens at the bottom of the <Noun />.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick your **Microphone**. The **Camera** switch starts off, so a walkthrough records your voice
  alone and the browser asks only for the microphone; turn it on to pick a camera and show yourself
  in a round bubble. The dock remembers your choice in this browser. If the browser hasn't been
  allowed to list your devices yet, press **Allow Microphone** (or **Allow Camera**) to ask it. The
  level meter shows the microphone hears you.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Press **Start Recording**. After a 3, 2, 1 countdown, talk and move around the <Noun /> as you
  would with someone beside you. Press **Finish** when you are done.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Give it a **Title** and press **Save**. It is listed under Walkthroughs in Tools for everyone who
  can open the <Noun />.
</Step>

A walkthrough can run for up to ten minutes; the bar turns amber with a minute left and finishes on its own at ten. If saving is refused (it is too large, or you have reached your limit), the dock says why and offers **Download a Copy** so the recording isn't lost.

## Hiding Elements From the Recording

Press **Hide From Recording** in the dock, then press each element viewers shouldn't see: a private note, an unfinished corner. **Hide Selection** hides what is already selected, and **Done** or `Esc` leaves the mode. You still see hidden elements, faded inside a dashed outline; people playing the walkthrough never do, and presses and pointer moves over them are left out of what is stored. They stay on the <Noun /> itself for anyone who opens it.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  What a press picks depends on the tab: an element on a plan tab (a card on a board drawn there is the card), a card on a **Board** tab, a widget on a **Dashboard** tab. **Hide Selection** also hides the cards you have selected on a board. A **Sheet** or **Form** tab has nothing to hide, so the dock leaves Hide From Recording out there.
</AppNote>

## Playing a Walkthrough

Open **Tools** in the footer, press **Walkthroughs**, and press one to play it. The view follows the recording, fitted to your screen, with the presenter's pointer and, in the corner, their camera bubble, or a small chip with their name when they recorded without the camera.

- **Pause, look around, resume.** Pan, zoom or press on the <Noun /> and it pauses, leaving the view with you. **Resume** puts the view back where the recording is and plays on.
- **Keys.** `Space` plays and pauses, `←` and `→` skip five seconds, `Esc` closes. The bar also has a scrubber and a speed of 1x, 1.5x or 2x.
- **Edited since recording.** If the <Noun /> has changed since, a line says so and it still plays over the <Noun /> as it is now.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Recorded while you present the slide deck, a walkthrough replays the deck too: it starts the presentation at the slide you were on, moves through the slides as you did, and leaves it when you left it.
</AppNote>

Closing puts you back where you were.

## Who Can Record and Delete

Editors record walkthroughs and can delete any of them. Everyone who can open the <Noun /> can play them, and anyone can delete one they recorded. Walkthroughs aren't offered on a phone, in an embed, for a <Noun /> kept in **This Browser**, or through a link to one tab.

## Privacy

Calls are never recorded. A walkthrough is only made when you press Start Recording, and it is stored with the <Noun /> for the people who can open it. Deleting a walkthrough, purging its <Noun /> from the Trash or deleting your account deletes it. See the [privacy policy](https://www.liveworkspace.app/privacy).
