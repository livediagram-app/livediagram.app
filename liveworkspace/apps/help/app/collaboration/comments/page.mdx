Comments let you have a conversation about the work without changing it. Leave a question or a piece of feedback where it belongs, and resolve it once it has been dealt with. They are the lightweight way to review work, agree on changes, and keep a record of why something is the way it is.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  There are two places a comment can live: on a **card**, and on a **cell** of a Sheet. Boards,
  Dashboards and Forms carry no comments of their own: the conversation lives on the cards they
  show.
</AppNote>

## Leaving a comment

A comment is attached to the thing it is about, so the reader sees exactly what you are talking about.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">
Because a thread lives on the element rather than inside its content, you can leave feedback on a draft without nudging anyone's shapes or arrows out of place.
</AppNote>

    its menu and press **Add Comment**. Every boxed element can carry a thread; arrows cannot. On a
    Participant link there is no right-click menu: select the element and press **Add Comment** on its
    toolbar.

**Commenting on a Sheet cell.** On a Sheet, a comment is about a single cell. Right-click the cell and choose **Insert Comment** (or press Ctrl+Alt+M, ⌘⌥M on a Mac), type your note and press **Comment**. While a tab has cell comments, a **Collaborate** button appears in the bottom-right cluster with a count of the threads still open. ## Reacting to a comment

Sometimes a reply is too much: you agree, you love it, or you just want to say you've seen it. React instead, with one of six emoji: 👍 Thumbs Up, 👎 Thumbs Down, 😄 Laugh, 🎉 Hooray, ❤️ Heart and 👀 Eyes.

- **Add Reaction** is the smiley with a plus beside the bin, which appears when you hover a comment (on a touch screen it is always there). The six open under the comment's text: press one to leave it.
- Each emoji in use shows as a **chip** under the comment, with how many people left it. Yours are highlighted. Press a chip to add yours, or press it again to take yours back. You can leave several different emoji on one comment, each once, and nobody can take away anyone else's.
- **See who reacted** by hovering a chip (or holding a finger on it): "You, Ana and Sam reacted".

Reactions appear for everyone on the <Noun /> as they are left, and stay with the comment. A resolved thread keeps its reactions but takes no new ones until it is reopened. Editors and Participants can react: someone on a **Viewer** link sees the reactions and who left them, but can't add any.

  panel](/help/palette/collaborate/comment-panels/) on the canvas shows each comment's reactions

</AppNote>

## Mentioning a teammate

Type **@** in a comment to tag someone on your team. A list of teammates appears as you type: use the arrow keys and **Enter** (or click) to pick one, and their handle, like `@priya-kaur`, goes into the comment. **Esc** closes the list and leaves what you typed.

A mention does two things for the person you tag:

- The thread appears on their [Inbox](/help/explorer/activity/), even if they never commented.
- They get an email quoting your comment, unless they have turned off **Someone Mentions Me in a Comment** in their [email notifications](/help/account-and-data/email-notifications/).

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">The thread on their Inbox is marked **Mentioned you**.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">For a card, the email's button opens that card.</AppNote>

You can mention the members of the team whose library holds the <Noun />, as long as you are a member of that team too, including people who have been invited but not joined yet. On a personal <Noun /> there is nobody to mention; move it into a team library first. You need to be signed in to mention anyone.

## Resolving comments

Once a comment has been actioned, resolve its thread to clear it from the working view while keeping the record. A resolved thread has no reply box, and **Reopen** brings it back.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">
Every open thread on a tab is also listed in the [Collaborate panel](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/#the-collaborate-panel), the **Collaborate** button in the bottom-right corner with a count of what is still open. Its **Resolved** view holds the threads you have resolved.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Press **Resolve** at the top of the thread. It becomes a green **Resolved** badge, the reply box is hidden, and the element's comment icon goes away. To reopen the thread, find it under **Resolved** in the [Collaborate panel](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/#the-collaborate-panel) and press **Reopen** beside the badge. Resolving keeps the canvas focused on what still needs attention rather than on questions that are already settled.
</AppNote>

Use comments to:

- **Request changes** during a review without editing someone else's work directly.
- **Ask a question** on the exact thing it is about.
- **Capture a decision** so the reasoning is not lost once the <Noun /> changes.

## Comments from an AI assistant

An AI assistant you connect to your <Noun plural /> (see [Connect an AI tool](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/)) can read the comment threads, reply, and resolve or reopen them as it works through what they ask. Its comments appear under the name and colour of the person whose access it uses, with a small **Generated by AI** mark above each one, so you can always tell them apart. A comment it changed later shows **Edited** beside the time. It never edits or deletes a comment a person wrote, and anything it resolves can be reopened like any other thread.

## Comments and collaboration

<AppName /> is multiplayer, so comments are part of how a group works together. New comments appear
for everyone in the <Noun /> as they are posted. A collaborator can answer a question where it was
asked, and a reviewer can leave feedback for the author to pick up later.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  A reviewer leaves feedback for the owner to pick up later. Open threads you are part of are
  gathered on your [Inbox](/help/explorer/activity/), across every plan.
</AppNote>

Because <AppName /> works without signing in, a guest you hand a share link to can read and join the conversation too. Commenting needs a **Participant** or **Editor** share link: someone on a **Viewer** link can read the threads and see their reactions, but not add to them, react, resolve them or delete anything.

A few things to know:

- **Comments aren't part of undo.** Pressing undo never takes back a comment, yours or anyone else's.
- **Owners can be emailed.** When someone comments on a <Noun /> you own, you can get an email, controlled by **Someone Comments on My Work** in your [email notifications](/help/account-and-data/email-notifications/).
- **Comments stay private in the Community.** A <Noun /> [shared to the Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/) never shows its comments to visitors, and a copy made from it leaves them out.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **Comments stay with their card:** a card keeps its thread when it moves between columns or
  boards. A copy of a Sheet starts without its cell comments.
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  Keep each comment to one point. A single, specific question is far easier to resolve than a long
  thread covering five unrelated things.
</Tip>
