While a [vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/) is running, press the **Vote** button to open the **Vote** panel above it. While casting is open the button counts the dots you have left; after that a dot on it means the vote is still on (amber during the results walkthrough). On a desktop the panel stays open while you vote: press the button again to put it away, and it closes by itself when the vote is cleared.

For anyone who can cast dots (an Editor or a Participant), the top of the panel shows your own dots while casting is open, like "2 of 3 dots left".

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Under that, small chips list the rules the vote runs under: the dots each person has, **One per item** when stacking is off, and **Cursors hidden** or **Counts hidden** while those privacy switches are in force.
</AppNote>

## While Voting Is Open

It shows how far the room has got: how many people have finished ("2 of 5 finished"), dots cast against dots available with a progress bar, and a row per person who has started voting, with their spent dots as filled pips and the rest hollow, ending in how many they have left. Below them a line counts who hasn't started, like "1 person hasn't voted yet".

The tallies on the <Noun /> tell you what is winning; this tells you whether anyone is still deciding, which is the thing that decides when to press **End vote**.

The buttons at the bottom follow the vote along: **End vote** while it is open, then **Show results** or **Clear** once it has ended, then **Clear vote** after the reveal. They are only there for whoever started the vote; everyone else sees a note in their place.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The buttons are also there for the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/); everyone else sees "Only the person who started this vote, or the facilitator, can end it." in their place.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  The note says only the person who started the vote can end it, or, during the results walkthrough, that the host is walking through the results.
</AppNote>

## Why the Rows Have No Names

Voter rows aren't labelled with names. Dots aren't recorded against people in a way the app can turn back into a name, so the panel shows the shape of the room rather than a register: all-filled rows are done, part-filled rows are who you are waiting on.

## After the Reveal

The panel switches to the [ranked list](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-results/) the moment you choose **Show results**.
