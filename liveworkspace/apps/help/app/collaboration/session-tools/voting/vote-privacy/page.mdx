A [vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/) is only honest if nobody can read the room before it closes. Two switches sit above **Start vote**, and both apply to everyone on the tab, not just to you.

## Hide Cursors

**Hide cursors** is **on by default**. While the vote is open, nobody sees anyone else's pointer, so the group cannot watch each other drift toward a favourite.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Nobody sees anyone's laser trail either. You can still see **who** is in the room: the initials on the tab stay put throughout, and an element someone else has selected still shows their name, so you know why it is locked. Cursors come back the moment you choose **End vote**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  A Board tab never shows other people's pointers in the first place (see [Live Presence](/help/collaboration/live-presence/)), so on a board this switch changes nothing you can see. You can still see **who** is in the room: the initials on the tab stay put throughout, and a card someone has open still carries their name.
</AppNote>

## Hide Running Counts

**Hide running counts** is **off by default**. Turn it on for a blind vote: while it runs, each tally shows only **your own** dots, so you can still see and retract what you spent, but nobody can watch a total climb and pile on.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The tally is the pill on each element.

  <Figure caption="In a blind vote the pill shows your own dots, never the room's total.">
    <DiagramVoteBlindTally />
  </Figure>
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  The tally is the counter on each card.

  <Figure caption="In a blind vote a card's counter shows your own dots, never the room's total.">
    <PlannerVoteBlindTally />
  </Figure>
</AppNote>

**Show results** reveals the real tallies and the winners together.

## Both Are Fixed for the Life of the Vote

There is no way to turn them off halfway through, which is the point: end the vote and start another if you want different rules.
