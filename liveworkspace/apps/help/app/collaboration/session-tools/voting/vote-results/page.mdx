**Show results** starts a guided walkthrough of everything that received dots in a [vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/), most votes first.

## Stepping Through the Picks

The current pick pulses an amber highlight, while the top of the [Vote panel](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-panel/) shows where you are and how many dots it got ("Top result 1 of 4 · 5 votes"), with **Previous** and **Next** buttons to step through in order. The panel opens above the **Vote** button for whoever runs the walkthrough, and the button's dot turns amber while it runs.

The walkthrough is shared: the person who started the vote drives it, and everyone else follows to the same pick. Their Vote panel shows the same readout without the buttons, and says "The host is walking through the results." That is the point of reviewing together, and it means "everyone look at this one" actually works.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Whoever is facilitating can drive it too, and everyone else's canvas follows to the same element.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">Everyone else sees the same card highlighted.</AppNote>

## The Ranked List

The [Vote panel](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-panel/) switches to the **ranked list** at the same moment: every item that got a dot, most votes first, with the winners flagged.

If you started the vote, click any row to move the room straight to that item instead of stepping through in order. Everyone else sees the same list and follows along.

<AppNote app="livediagram">If you are facilitating, you can click a row too.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">Each card is listed by its title and number.</AppNote>

## Finishing

On the last pick, Next becomes **Done**. Pressing it ends the walkthrough and clears the whole vote session: the tally pills, the highlights and the Vote panel all go together, leaving the <Noun /> clean for whatever comes next.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">Each card keeps the dots it gathered as its **Votes**.</AppNote>
