## Spending and Taking Back

Press **+** to add one of your dots and **−** to take one back. Stacking several dots on one item is allowed, each counting separately, unless the vote was started with **One each** under **Dots per item**: then each of your dots must go on a different item. For anyone who can cast (an Editor or a Participant), the **Vote** button keeps count: its badge shows how many dots you have left, and the Vote panel above it says so in words, like "2 of 3 dots left".

The counter starts at zero, so you can always see which items are in play and how many dots each has. **−** greys out when none of the dots on it are yours, and **+** once your budget is spent (or, under **One each**, once you have a dot on that item), rather than disappearing, so the buttons stay where your pointer expects them.

Once the vote ends the buttons go and the counter becomes a plain tally, since at that point it is a result to read rather than something to change.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  The rest of the card keeps working during a vote: click it to open its panel, or drag it to
  another column.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **From the Keyboard.** Press **F6** until the canvas is focused, then **Tab** to walk its
  elements. An element somebody else has selected is skipped until they move on. With a votable
  element selected, press **+** (or **=**) to add one of your dots and **−** to take one back. A
  screen reader hears what happened and how many dots you have left, like "Dot added to 'Idea'. 2 of
  3 dots left", or why nothing changed, like "No dots left." The same rules apply as for a click:
  your budget, **One each**, and the vote's layer. When no vote is open, or the element can't take a
  dot, the keys type into its label as usual.
</AppNote>

## What Can Take a Dot

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **Votable** elements are shapes, sticky notes, and images. Section backdrops (frames), text, freehand drawings, tables, arrows, and annotations are not votable, so you vote on the ideas, not the scaffolding around them. Nor are elements that do something when pressed (mode buttons, session buttons, reveal zones, pickers and reaction pads), or comment and action panels. Readouts (stat rows, charts, progress bars and rings, legends and keys) and checklists are left out too: they show what the board says or what to do next, not an idea to rank. If the vote is [limited to one layer](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-layers/), only that layer's elements count.

If the vote was started with **Only items with text**, a blank sticky note or shape takes no dot: it is dimmed while the vote runs, and the elements that can take one are ringed. Type into a blank note mid-vote and it joins in straight away. Images have no text, so they sit a text-only vote out. A note that already holds dots keeps them, and its counter, even if its text is cleared.

Casting needs an Editor or Participant role: someone on a Viewer share link sees the tallies but can't add a dot.

Non-votable elements show no counter and still select and move normally. Pressing a votable element casts a dot rather than selecting it.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  The **cards** take the dots. The board itself takes none, so you can still work the board during the vote. A card shown on two boards is one card, with one count. A card face down on a board that hides writing takes no dots until it is revealed.

When the host ends the vote, each card keeps the dots it gathered as its **Votes**, which the card's ▲ count and a board's **Top Voted** widget read afterwards.

</AppNote>
