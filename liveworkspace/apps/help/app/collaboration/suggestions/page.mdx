Sometimes the thing in front of you would do more in another app: a retro's actions belong in a plan, a decision belongs in the knowledgebase. When <AppName /> sees that, a small **suggestion** appears at the bottom of the screen, says what it would do, and does it when you press its button. Your content goes with it, and a link back to where it came from.

## What you might see

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **Make These a Plan.** Three or more stickies on the tab are actions: open assigned actions, or
  stickies in a lane headed **Actions**, **Action Items**, **Next Steps** or **To Do**. **Send to
  Plan** opens Send to Plan on exactly those stickies, one card each, and you pick the plan.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **Write It Up.** You move a card to a done column and its description holds a decision ("we
  decided", "agreed", "going with"), or the card is a **Milestone**. **Write Page** writes the card
  as a page in your team's knowledgebase, with a link back to the card.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  **Add to Knowledgebase.** The article you have open explains a process: numbered steps, **Step**
  headings, or a **How to** heading with a list. **Add Page** copies the article into your team's
  knowledgebase as a page, with a link back. The article stays where it is.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveknowledge">
  **Bring In Your Diagrams and Docs.** The knowledgebase is new: nothing connected yet and only a
  few pages. **Connect Them** connects your three latest diagrams and three latest documents, so Ask
  can answer from them too. Nothing is copied.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Send to a Plan.** You leave a call in the Space and the wall you met on has stickies. **Send to
  Plan** opens Send to Plan on every sticky there, one card each, and you pick the plan.
</AppNote>

A page written to the knowledgebase goes to the one connected to this <Noun />, else your team's newest, else your own. It is written as you, starts unverified, and a message says where it went, with **Open Page**.

## When they appear

- **One at a time**, and never over another notice at the bottom of the screen.
- **Never while you work**: not while you type, drag or draw, nor for a moment after.
- **Once per <Noun />**: pressing a suggestion's button or its **×** means it never comes back for this <Noun />.
- **Only for what you can do**: suggestions show to an Editor who is signed in. A Participant or a Viewer never sees one, nor does an embed, an Offline <Noun /> or a Community copy.

## Turning them off

- **Don't Suggest This Again**, under a suggestion's words, turns that one off everywhere.
- **Settings › Getting Started › Suggest Other Apps** turns every suggestion off, in every app at once. Turning it back on does not bring back the ones you turned off one by one.

What you dismissed is remembered in this browser.
