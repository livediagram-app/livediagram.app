## Mouse and Keyboard

A mouse and keyboard unlock the fastest ways to work:

- **Keyboard shortcuts** for picking tools and running common actions, like undo, copy, duplicate, and zoom. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) for the full list.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  - **Hold Shift** while resizing to keep an element's proportions, and **Shift-click** to add or
  remove elements from a selection. - **Drag a box** across empty canvas to select several elements
  at once. - **Hold Space and drag**, drag with the middle mouse button, or scroll (two fingers on a
  trackpad) to move around; hold Ctrl (or Cmd) and scroll, or pinch on a trackpad, to zoom.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  - **Drag** cards between columns, drag the edges of a sheet's columns and rows to resize them, and
  drag across cells to select a range. - **Keyboard shortcuts** for undo and redo, search, Zen mode,
  moving cards and working in a sheet. See [Keyboard
  Essentials](/help/getting-started/keyboard-essentials/). - **Scroll** with the mouse wheel or two
  fingers on a trackpad to move through a long column, a big sheet or a dashboard.
</AppNote>

## Right-Click and Hover

Right-click any element, tab, or empty space to open its menu of formatting and actions. Resting the pointer on a button for half a second shows a tooltip naming it; some controls explain themselves at once with a hover card. Keyboard focus shows either straight away, and Escape hides it.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign,livehuddle">
  See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign,livehuddle">
  In those menus, resting the pointer on a colour, border or preset previews it on the selection
  before you commit, which touch screens can't do.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livehuddle">
  The tooltip shows on an icon-only button (a button that shows its name, a close X or a colour
  swatch has none).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Right-click a card, a cell, a sheet's row or column header, or a board's empty space to open its
  menu, and use a tab's **⋯** for the tab's own. A button with its name on it needs no tooltip.
</AppNote>
