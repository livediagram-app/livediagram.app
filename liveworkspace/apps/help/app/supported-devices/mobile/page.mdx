## A Layout Built for Touch

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  On a phone the editor keeps the same [layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) as on a computer, fitted to the width. Along the top, a small card holds the menu button (for the Explorer), and beside it the palette's strip: the categories as icons (swipe them sideways for the rest), and **Search** at the end. Tap a category to open its elements across the width of the screen. The tabs and quick controls run along the bottom.

A few panels are simplified to fit: they stay where they are rather than being dragged around (they start collapsed; tap a panel's header to expand or collapse it), and the zoom controls' − and + buttons give way to a single **Fit** button, since you pinch to zoom.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The strip starts with the selection mode; a tab's mode is chosen on its start, see [Editor
  Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/#on-a-phone). **Layers** (and **Collaborate**, once the tab has a
  comment or an action) open as pop-overs above their buttons in the bottom-right corner. The
  [Map](/help/user-interface/minimap/) is hidden.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  **Collaborate**, once the tab has a comment or an action, opens as a pop-over above its button in
  the bottom-right corner. The [Map](/help/user-interface/minimap/) is hidden.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  **Collaborate**, once the tab has a comment or an action, opens as a pop-over above its button in
  the bottom-right corner. [Contents](/help/pages/working-with-pages/#contents) is always a button
  rather than a sidebar.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The strip starts with the Edit | Walk toggle (its icons alone). The
  [Map](/help/user-interface/minimap/) is hidden.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  The editor keeps the same parts as on a computer, fitted to the width: the plan's name and **Share** along the top, the work in the middle, the bottom-right controls, and the tabs along the bottom with **Search** and **Settings** as icons at the end.

- **Boards** show one column a screen, centred, with a sliver of its neighbours on either side: swipe sideways and the board settles on the next column, one at a time. A short or slow nudge springs back, so swipe over a quarter of the way across, or flick. Scrolling down a column keeps you in that column, even when your thumb drifts sideways, and stays where you leave it. Tap a column's empty space (not a card or a button) and it glides to the centre. Cards are drawn at their smallest size so a column shows more of them, and the board's header leaves out its widgets to make room for the title. A card opens in a panel that rises from the bottom of the screen.
- **Dashboards** stack their slots one under another, in reading order, and you scroll down through them. To reorder slots, use **Move Up** and **Move Down** in a slot's settings.
- **Sheets** keep their grid, with a toolbar that packs its buttons tighter and folds the rest into **More**.
- **Forms** fill the width of the screen, and you build and answer them just as on a computer.

Menus open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen; drag it down to close it.

</AppNote>

## Held Sideways

Turn your phone sideways and the screen gets short, so the header along the top and the tabs along the bottom fold away and the work fills the screen. To reach Share, the <Noun />'s name or your tabs, tap the small arrow button in the top-right corner, **Show Header and Tabs**: both bars come back, and the same button (**Hide Header and Tabs**) folds them away again. Turn the phone upright and they are back as usual.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  The palette strip, Undo and Redo, and Fit stay where they are. The menu button and the
  [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) go away completely, as there is no room for them; turn
  the phone upright to use them. Held upright, an empty tab opens zoomed out a little (to 60%) and a
  tab with content opens fitted to it, so you land on a useful overview of the canvas, and when you
  add a new element it slides into view automatically.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">Layers stays where it is too.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  The [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) needs the phone upright.
</AppNote>

## Touch Gestures

Your fingers do the work a mouse would on a computer:

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  - **Pinch** with two fingers to zoom in and out. - **Double-tap** empty canvas to zoom in on that
  spot. It moves to the middle of the screen at twice the zoom (at least 100%), so a small view
  becomes readable in one go. **Double-tap again** to zoom back out to the whole tab, then
  double-tap wherever you want to go next. - **Double-tap** an element to edit it. If it is too
  small on your screen to work on, the first double-tap zooms in on it so you can see it properly;
  double-tap it again to edit. - **Tap** to select, and **drag** an element to move it. - **Press
  and hold** an element for half a second (a long press) to open its menu, the touch version of a
  right-click. On a phone menus open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen; drag it down to close
  it.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  **Drag** empty canvas to move around: a phone starts on the **Hand** tool. To select several
  elements, switch the selection mode to **Select**: then dragging across empty canvas draws a
  selection box.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Drag** one finger across empty canvas to move around. Tap **Walk** at the start of the strip
  (the footprints) to walk the Space as your character, and the pointer arrow beside it to go back
  to building. See [Edit and Walk](/help/selection-modes/select/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  - **Tap** a card to open it, or a button to press it. - **Drag** a card to move it to another
  column or up and down its column: hold it still for a moment, then move. A finger that moves
  straight away scrolls the board, whether it starts on a card or on empty space. - **Press and
  hold** a card for half a second to open its menu (when you can edit), the touch version of a
  right-click. - **On a sheet**, tap a cell to select it and drag to scroll. Hold a finger still on
  a cell until it grabs, then drag to select a range; hold and let go without moving to open the
  cell's menu.
</AppNote>

**Press and hold** a button that shows only an icon to see its name. Letting go does not press it.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  Drag handles (an arrow's ends, for instance) have a little extra invisible padding on touch, so
  they are easier to hit accurately with a fingertip.
</AppNote>

## What Works Differently on a Phone

A phone has a small screen, no mouse pointer and usually no keyboard, so a few things are not available or work differently:

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  - **Spotlight** and the **Slide Deck** (the presenting tools) are reserved for larger screens. -
  **The Map** and **UI Scale** are desktop only. - **Quick Style** (the small style panel beside a
  selection) does not show on a phone; every style is still in the element's menu.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  - **Spotlight** is reserved for larger screens. - **The Map** and **UI Scale** are desktop only. -
  **Quick Style** (the small style bar beside a page's title bar) is not a bar on a phone: its
  choices are under **Selected Element** in the page's **Appearance**, and every style is still in
  the element's menu.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  - **Spotlight** and presenting a **Slide Deck** are reserved for larger screens. - **UI Scale** is
  desktop only.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  - **UI Scale** and the **Trash** button in the bottom-right corner are left out on a phone. (While
  the session tools are showing, the card tools fold into a **More card tools** menu, and Trash is
  in it.) - **Board widgets** aren't shown, and a quick filter set with one on a wider screen stands
  down, so no cards are hidden without you seeing why.
</AppNote>

- **Keyboard shortcuts** need a keyboard: without one, the on-screen controls do everything the shortcuts do. Pair a keyboard and they work as on a computer.
- **Hover previews** (styles previewing as you rest on them) need a hovering pointer, so on touch a tap simply applies the change.

<Tip>
  A [tablet](/help/supported-devices/tablet/) with more room, or a
  [computer](/help/supported-devices/desktop/), is more comfortable for a long or detailed session.
  A phone is perfect for quick changes and following along live.
</Tip>
