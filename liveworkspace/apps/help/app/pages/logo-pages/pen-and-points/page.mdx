The **Pen** draws the deliberate line a mark needs: a clean curve, a custom outline traced over a sketch, the contour of a letter. It works the way pens in other vector tools do, and every point stays editable afterwards with **Edit Points**.

## Drawing with the Pen

On a [logo page](/help/pages/logo-pages/), open **Draw** on the palette and pick **Pen** under **Pens**. Then:

- **Click** to place a corner point.
- **Press and drag** to place a smooth point, pulling out its curve as you drag.
- Hold **Alt** (Option on a Mac) while dragging to break the curve's mirror, so only the outgoing handle follows.
- Hold **Shift** to keep the line, or a handle, to 45° steps.
- Hold **Space** while dragging to move the point you are placing instead of its handle.
- **Backspace** removes the last point you placed and keeps drawing; **Undo** steps back one point at a time while you draw.

To finish, click the first point to close the shape, or press **Enter** or **Escape** (or double-click) to leave it open. With the Pen in hand, clicking the end of an open path carries on drawing from there. With **Show Guides** on, a click lands on a guide within reach.

## Editing points

Double-click a path, press **Enter** with it selected, or press **Edit Points** on its selection toolbar. Every point shows: corners as squares, smooth points as circles. A toolbar floats over the path.

- **Drag** a point to move it, or a handle to reshape its curve. Arrow keys nudge selected points by 1 px, Shift and an arrow by 10 px.
- **Shift-click** points to select several, or drag a box around them.
- **Click a segment** to add a point there without changing the curve; **drag a segment** to bend it.
- **Double-click** or **Alt-click** a point to switch it between corner and smooth.
- On the toolbar, set the selected points to **Corner**, **Mirrored** (both handles in one line, equal length) or **Aligned** (in one line, lengths free), delete a point, and **Close Path** or **Open Path** (opening cuts the path at the selected point). `J` closes too.
- Press **Done**, **Escape** or click outside the path to finish.

Every gesture is one undo step. On a phone or tablet, drag points and handles with your finger, and long-press a point to bring the toolbar beside it.

## Other paths

**Edit Points** works on any path, not just the Pen's. A shape made by **Combine** is edited one outline at a time, with the others shown dashed; the shape counter at the start of the toolbar ("Shape 1 of 3") moves to the next, and **Simplify** smooths an outline with many small corners. On a pencil or marker drawing, **Edit Points** first tidies it into a path, then opens its points.

A locked path hides **Edit Points** until you unlock it.
