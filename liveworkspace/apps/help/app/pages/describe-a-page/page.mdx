When you already know what a page should say, **Describe a Page** saves you picking a layout and typing over its samples. Say what the page is about and what it should say, and AI makes the next page of the tab for you: it picks a layout that suits your words and writes them into it.

It works for every kind of page, and is named for the tab's kind:

- **Describe a Slide** on a slide tab, and **Describe a Page** on infographic pages: AI picks one of the kind's ready-made layouts (64 for slides, 92 for infographics) and fills its title, text, lists and figures.
- **Describe an Article**: AI writes a new article, with its title and its writing, after the article you are in.
- **Describe a Card** on flashcards: AI writes the card's words.

## Before You Start

- You need to be signed in and an **Editor** of the tab. Participants, Viewers and people on a share link without an account do not see the button.
- It runs on your team's AI key, or your own (see [AI Keys](/help/account-and-data/ai-keys/)). Without one, it runs on built-in AI, included, and each page counts as one of your 30 built-in AI questions a day.

## Make a Page

1. Open **Contents** and press the sparkle button at its foot, right after **Add Slide** (or **Add Page**, **Add Article**, **Add Card**).
2. Describe the page in your own words, up to 500 characters: what it is about, and some of what it should say. The examples above the box (**Agenda**, **Key Figures** and **Compare Plans** on a slide tab, for instance) fill it with words you can change.
3. Press **Make Slide** (or **Make Page**, **Make Article**, **Make Card**), or Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter on a Mac).

The new page lands right after the page you are on, the same size and background as your other pages, and Contents marks it. A message names the layout AI picked, such as "Added a slide from the Agenda layout". It is one change: **Undo** takes it away.

It usually takes a few seconds. If it takes longer, the dialog says it is still working: a model that thinks first can take up to a minute. **Cancel** stops it, and nothing is added.

## When It Can't

- If your words don't describe a page, the dialog says so and keeps them for you to change.
- If your key is not accepted, the line under the box says so, with a button to fix the key in Settings.
- When you have used your built-in AI questions for the day, the dialog says so: try again tomorrow, or add a key of your own.
- A tab holds up to 100 pages. At the limit the button is disabled, as Add Page is.

## Privacy

Only your description goes to the AI that makes the page, with the layouts' own names and samples: nothing else of your document is sent. Your words and the AI's reply are not logged.
