## What it does

Drag the slider down and every panel becomes translucent, so the canvas behind them stays visible while you work near an edge. The percentage next to the slider shows the current setting, from 100% (fully solid, the default) down to 30%, in steps of 5%. The panels change when you let go of the slider.

<Figure caption="At a lower opacity the panels turn translucent, so the canvas behind them stays visible.">
  <PanelOpacity />
</Figure>

So a translucent panel never gets in your way, each panel snaps back to fully opaque the moment you hover over it or focus something inside it, then fades back once you move away. You always get a clear, readable panel exactly when you are using it. On a phone or tablet, where there is no hover, panels stay fully opaque.

## Where it applies

Panel Opacity affects every panel: the corner panels (the Map and the tool panels), the panels that open as popovers from a button, and the [palette strip](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/). Buttons are not panels, so the menu button and the buttons in the bottom-right corner stay fully opaque, and so do menus and dialogs.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The popover panels are Layers, Collaborate, and the Poll and Vote panels from the Session buttons. The [Quick Style panel](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/) is covered too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  The popover panel is the Collaborate panel. The [Quick Style bar](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/) and the card of actions in the top-right corner are covered too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  The popover panel is the Collaborate panel. The Quick Style bar beside a selected element is covered too, and so is the card at the top right that holds Copy, Present and Share, which fades with the strip so the two read as one row.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The popover panel is Collaborate, the one that opens from a button.
</AppNote>

The [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) is the one panel that always stays fully opaque, whatever the setting. Its menus open beside it, so a translucent Explorer would fade just as you used one of them.

## Resetting it

Drag the slider back to 100% to make the panels solid again. The setting is saved with your other [settings](/help/user-interface/settings/). When you are signed in it follows you to your other devices; as a guest it stays with this browser.
