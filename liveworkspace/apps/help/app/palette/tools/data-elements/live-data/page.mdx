A chart or a table in your <Noun /> can take its numbers from one of your [liveplanner](https://www.liveplanner.app) plans and keep in step with it, so a report, a slide or a status board never goes stale. Move a card on the plan's board and the chart that counts its columns changes wherever the <Noun /> is open. It stays an ordinary element, drawn in your theme and styled as usual: only its numbers come from the plan.

## What a chart or table can show

- **Sheet Range**: a range of one of the plan's Sheets, typed as in a spreadsheet (such as `B2:E9`). The first row names each series and the first column labels each slice, bar, point or row.
- **Board Counts**: the cards on one of the plan's boards, counted by **Column**, **Label**, **Assignee** or **Priority**. Narrow them with **Only Cards With** a priority, a label, an assignee or a card type, such as the high priority bugs per column. Cards in the Trash are never counted.
- **Dashboard Metric**: one of the numbers the plan's Dashboards work out, such as **Completion**, **Workload** or **Top Voted**.

## Link a chart or table

<Step n={1}>
  Right-click the chart (**Tools**, then **Data**) or the table (**Data**) and choose **From a
  Plan…**.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Pick one of your plans (search them with **Search your plans**), then the **Source**: a Sheet
  Range, Board Counts or a Dashboard Metric, and what it reads.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Check the **Preview**. When the source has more than one series, press a series to choose which
  feed the chart and in what order; a pie, column or bar chart draws one series, so pressing another
  draws it instead.
</Step>
<Step n={4}>Press **Link**. The element takes the plan's numbers in one undoable step.</Step>

A linked table fills its cells from the plan, with a header row, and is read only while it is linked, since the next update would write over a typed cell. A chart keeps its chart type, palette, legend and animation for you to set.

## Staying in step

- **Live, not polled.** While the <Noun /> is open, the plan tells it when a Sheet or a card changes, and the element reads again within a couple of seconds. Each source is read once however many elements show it.
- **Where it comes from.** While a linked element is hovered or selected, a line under it says "Live from Launch Plan · updated 2 min ago", and a small dot at its corner is green while the numbers are live.
- **Open Plan** in its **Data** section opens the plan in liveplanner, for anyone. **Update** reads it again now, **Change…** picks another source and **Unlink** keeps the last numbers and turns it back into an element you edit by hand.

## Freeze

**Freeze** keeps the numbers as they are now and stops reading the plan, for a report that should show the figures as of a day. The line says "From Launch Plan · frozen 12 Nov". **Unfreeze** reads the plan again and follows it from then on.

## Who sees what

- The numbers are saved in the <Noun />, so everyone who can open it sees them. Someone who can open the plan sees its numbers as they are now; someone who can't sees the numbers last saved, and the line says "saved" rather than "updated".
- Linking needs read access to the plan, with your own account: a share link or a public link to the plan is not enough.
- Only Editors can link, update, freeze, change or unlink. Participants and Viewers see the element, its line and Open Plan.
- Exports, Offline copies and Community copies draw the saved numbers and carry no link, so they never name a plan their reader may not open.

<Tip>
  A plan's board renamed or a column added shows the next time the <Noun /> opens, or when you press
  **Update**.
</Tip>
