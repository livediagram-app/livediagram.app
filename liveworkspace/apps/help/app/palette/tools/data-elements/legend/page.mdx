A legend is a small card of rows, each one a coloured dot and a label. It's the piece that turns a colour-coded <Noun /> into one somebody else can read: which colour is which team, which status, which environment.

</Figure>

## Adding one

<Step n={1}>
  Open the **Data** category on the palette strip, or type "legend" into the [search
  panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/).
</Step>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The **Data** category is on the palette strip in [Workshop](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/); in Diagram mode, use the search panel.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  A logo page has no Data category, so use the search panel there.
</AppNote>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick **Legend**, then tap the canvas to drop it, or drag to draw it at a size. It starts with
  three rows, "First", "Second" and "Third".
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Move, resize, recolour, or lock it like any other element. Its background and text colours come
  from **Colours** under **Appearance**, the same as every other card.
</Step>

## Editing the colours and labels

Right-click the legend and open **Legend**. Each row gives you:

- A round **colour chip**: click it to pick that row's colour.
- A **label** field: type what the colour means.
- A **×** to remove the row.

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  A legend keeps at least one row.
</AppNote>

**Add Row** puts another row on the end (up to 40). A new row takes the next colour from the legend's palette automatically, so you only need to set a colour when you want a specific one. Nothing on the legend itself is clickable: all its editing happens here.

Under the rows, the **Text Size** slider (6 to 48 pixels) sets how big the labels are. It's the same scale a chart's own key uses, so a legend beside a chart can match it. Below it, **Palette** picks the colours every uncoloured row takes, with a reset back to the default.

## Matching a chart

A row you never colour takes the next colour in the same order a chart's uncoloured marks do, from the same ladder a chart uses: your theme's chart colours by default. So a legend dropped beside a chart already matches it, first row to first slice. A legend's **Presets** under **Appearance** restyle its card, not its dots.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The order is the legend's own **Palette** if you set one, otherwise your tab theme's chart colours, otherwise the built-in set. If you gave the chart a palette of its own, pick the same one under the legend's **Palette**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  If the chart uses one of the palettes (**Vivid**, **Ocean**, **Forest**, **Sunset**, **Berry**, **Earth**, **Grey** or **Contrast**), pick the same one from the **Palette** tiles at the foot of the **Legend** section, and the two stay in step: every row you haven't coloured by hand follows it. Its reset goes back to the theme's colours.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  The **Palette** tiles at the foot of the Legend section recolour every row you haven't coloured by hand, with the same eight palettes a chart offers (**Vivid**, **Ocean**, **Forest**, **Sunset**, **Berry**, **Earth**, **Grey** and **Contrast**), plus a reset back to the theme's colours. Give a legend and a chart the same palette and they stay in step.
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  A legend is a key, not a chart: it holds colours and words, and no values. If you want the numbers
  as well, hovering a slice or bar on the chart itself shows its value.
</Tip>
