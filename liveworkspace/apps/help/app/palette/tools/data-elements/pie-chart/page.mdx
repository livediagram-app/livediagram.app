A pie chart shows how a total divides into parts. Each slice is sized by its value, coloured from your active theme, and named in a key, so a reader sees the breakdown at a glance. You build the chart from a short list of label and value rows, with no spreadsheet required.

## Adding one

<Step n={1}>
  Open the **Data** category on the palette strip, or type "pie" into the [search
  panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/).
</Step>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The **Data** category is on the palette strip in [Workshop](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/); in Diagram mode, use the search panel.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  A logo page has no Data category, so use the search panel there.
</AppNote>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick **Pie**, then tap the canvas to drop it, or drag to draw it at a size. It lands with sample
  slices so it looks finished straight away.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>Move, resize, or lock it like any other element.</Step>

## Editing the data

Right-click the chart, open **Tools**, then **Data**. It edits inline, one row per slice, and each row has:

- A recolourable **swatch** to set that slice's colour.
- A **label** for the slice name.
- A **value** that sizes the slice.

Remove a slice with its **×**, or add one with **+ Add Slice**; the chart updates as soon as you leave a field. New slices default to variants of the active theme, so the chart reads as part of your <Noun /> out of the box, but you can override any slice's colour with its swatch.

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  To keep the slices in step with a spreadsheet instead, choose **Link to Sheet…** under the rows and pick a range of a liveplanner Sheet: the chart then takes its numbers from the sheet and updates when the sheet changes. See [Linking to a sheet](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/bar-and-line-charts/#link-a-chart-to-a-liveplanner-sheet).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">

## Legend, palette and animation

- **Chart**, also in **Tools**, has a **Legend** placement picker: **Off**, **Top**, **Left**, **Right**, or **Below** (the default). Picking a side shows the key there, and **Off** hides it. While the key is on, the **Text Size** slider (6 to 48 pixels) sets how big its labels are. The key names each slice; hovering a slice always shows a readout with its label and value, whether or not the key is on.
- **Show Values**, under the legend, prints each slice's number inside it, as a percentage when the values add up to 100.
- **Appearance** holds the chart's **Presets**: eight palettes (**Vivid**, **Ocean**, **Forest**, **Sunset**, **Berry**, **Earth**, **Grey** and **Contrast**) that recolour every slice you haven't coloured by hand, including slices you add later. A chart has no Colours or Border controls: its colour lives in its slices.
- **Animation**, then **Chart**, carries chart-specific animations: **Grow**, **Pop**, **Spin** and **Pulse**. Grow and Pop play once as an entrance, while Spin and Pulse loop. Once you pick one, a speed row (**Slowest**, **Slow**, **Normal**, **Fast**) and a **Repeat** toggle appear. In livedesign its **Shape** section moves the whole chart instead.

<AppNote app="livediagram">

**Chart type and linked data.**

**Chart Type**, first in **Tools**, turns the pie into a **Doughnut** (the middle left open) or into a column, bar, line, area or scatter chart, keeping its size, colours and legend. See [Bar and Line Charts](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/bar-and-line-charts/#chart-types).

Instead of typing the slices in, choose **Link to Sheet…** under the rows in **Data** to take them from a range of a liveplanner Sheet. The chart then keeps in step with the sheet. See [Link a Chart to a liveplanner Sheet](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/bar-and-line-charts/#link-a-chart-to-a-liveplanner-sheet).

</AppNote>

<Tip>
  Because slice colours default to your theme, changing the tab's theme restyles the chart to match
