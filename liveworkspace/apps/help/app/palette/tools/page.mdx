**Which categories each mode offers**

The palette is laid out per editor mode, so not every category appears everywhere:

- **Diagram** (the default): Shapes (with the BPMN notation), Write (pages, text, notes, annotations, pointer notes, the Callout, the Comment panel and the Action card), Draw (the pens and arrows), **Diagrams** (mind nodes, org charts, flowcharts, sequence diagrams, tables, entities and the C4 model's elements), **Concepts** (the concept graphics, the Timeline among them, and the Sankey), **Layout** (lanes, frames, stacks, boundaries, the diagram key, cards, badges and step numbers, then Image and Avatar), Icons and Tech. **Library** (your shape libraries) joins Common once you have [imported a shape library](/help/explorer/shape-libraries/), and **Event Storming** joins Structure on an [Event Storming board](/help/canvas/event-storming-boards/).
- **[Workshop](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/)**: Shapes, Write (text, notes and pointer notes, then tables and checklists), Draw (the pens and arrows), Layout (as in Diagram, with the Timeline), then the session kit, **Ask**, and **Session**, which holds Tools, Record, React, Selection Mode and Navigate, then Icons (with a Stickers side) and Data. It leaves out what a session does not need: Page and Annotation from Write, the Shape Pen and Polygon from Draw, and the Diagrams, Concepts and Tech categories.
- **[Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/)** swaps the palette for the whiteboard's own pens and shapes.

Anything a mode leaves out is still one search away: the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) (`⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`) can add any palette element by name, whatever the mode.

**Diagrams** (Diagram mode) holds the structures that grow as you type: **Mind Node** (Tab adds a child, Enter a sibling), **Org Chart**, **Flowchart**, **Sequence Diagram**, **Table** (an editable grid of cells), **Entity**, and the C4 model's elements. **Layout** (both modes) holds what a diagram or board is arranged in: **Lane** (a titled band that carries its steps), **Frame** (a labelled box that groups a section, `F`), **Stack** and **Boundary**, then the **Diagram Key**, **Card**, **Badge** and **Step Number**, and at its end **Image** and **Avatar**. The **Timeline** is a [concept graphic](/help/palette/concept-graphics/timeline-rail/), in **Concepts** (in Workshop, which has no Concepts, it sits in Layout).

**Write** holds the wordy elements: **Page** is a paper-sized sheet you write into, **Text** drops a free-standing label (`T`), **Note** is a coloured sticky note card (`N`), **Annotation** is a small marker that holds a note, and **Pointer Note** is a note with a line out to the spot it is about. See [Write Elements](/help/palette/write/). The coloured Event Storming workshop stickies live in [their own category](/help/palette/event-storming/), which appears on an Event Storming board.

**Draw** holds the gesture tools: **Freehand** (`P`) sketches a stroke and keeps it exactly as drawn, the **Shape Pen** (`6`) turns a rough sketch into a real shape, the **Highlighter** lays one wide translucent marker stroke, **Polygon** places straight-edged outlines point by point, **Arrow** (`A`) drops a connector with a pointer at its end that you position by hand, and **Line** drops the same without the pointer. See [Drawing and Sketch](/help/palette/tools/drawing/) and [Highlighter](/help/palette/tools/highlighter/).

**On no strip.** A few pieces are not in any category: the strip's Search and the search panel add them in any mode. They are the page blocks (**Hero**, **Header**, **Banner**, **Stat Row** and **Process**; Hero needs image uploads, like Image and Avatar), the **Code** block, the device frames (Browser, Monitor, Laptop, Phone, Tablet, Foldable and Watch) and the embeds. The **Checklist** is in Workshop's Write; in Diagram mode, search for it.

**Data** (Workshop) holds the charts and meters: Pie, Column, Line, Legend, Progress, Donut and Rating. See [Data and Chart Elements](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/).

**Collaborate** (Workshop) is the elements that do something when somebody interacts with them, or that ask the room something, in six groups: **Ask** on the strip (dot vote, poll, quiz, estimate card, temperature check, idea box, Q&A board), and inside **Session**: **Tools** (timer, stopwatch, reveal zone, done check, picker), **Record** (comment panel, action panel, agenda, decision record, roll call), **React** (the five reaction pads), **Selection Mode** buttons, and **Navigate** (portal, bring focus, link card). See [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/) and [Collaborate Elements](/help/palette/collaborate/).

**Pictures and embeds.** **Image** (`9`) and **Avatar** sit at the end of Layout, and only appear where image uploads are available. The [embeds](/help/palette/embed-elements/) for YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Figma, Google Docs, any website, and the **Live Embed** (a canvas, plan, page or design from our other apps, shown live) are added from the strip's Search or the search panel.

**The categories**

The palette opens on **Popular**: Sticky note and Text, then, behind a divider, Room, Meeting Table, Chair, Bring Focus and Avatar Pad. Popular is the palette's landing page rather than a category on the strip; every other category is one level deep.

Anything you cannot see is still one search away: the strip's Search and the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) (`⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`) add any palette element by name.

**Write** holds the words and pictures: **Text** drops a free-standing label (`T`), **Note** is a coloured sticky note card (`N`) and **Image** places a picture you upload, such as your logo. See [Write Elements](/help/palette/write/).

**Rooms** holds what a floor is laid out with. It opens on **Build**: the [Room](/help/palette/virtual-office/), the Room Builder, the Corridor, the Door and the Window, the pieces a room is made with. The Room, Corridor and Door each open their types beside the panel, and so does the Rug. Under **Floor** come the [Fence](/help/palette/fences/), the [Stage](/help/palette/virtual-office/stage/), the Stage Curtains, the Podium, the Office Pod, the Phone Booth, the Nap Pod, the Reception Desk, the Rug, the Pillar and the Column.

**Seats** holds what people sit at: the [Chair, Desk and Sofa](/help/palette/chairs/), and the [Meeting Table, Long Table, Bean Bag, Reading Nook, Hot Desk and Standing Desk](/help/palette/virtual-office/).

**Perks** holds what makes a Space feel like a place, in three groups: **Office** (the Plant, Bookshelf, Printer, Copier, Lockers, Coat Rack and Umbrella Stand), **Kitchen** (the Water Cooler, Coffee Machine, Vending Machine, Kitchen Counter, Kitchen Sink, Fridge, Microwave and Kettle) and **Restroom** (a whole Restroom, or its Toilet Cubicle, Washbasin and Hand Dryer). See [Virtual Office Elements](/help/palette/virtual-office/).

**Play** holds somewhere to unwind, in four groups: **Bar** (the Bar, Bar Stool, Drinks Cabinet and Drinks Fridge), **Games** (the Ping-Pong Table, Pool Table, Foosball Table, Arcade Machine and Dartboard), **Gym** (the Treadmill and Weight Bench) and **Lounge** (the Jukebox, Fireplace and Pet Bed).

**Garden** holds the outdoor pieces, in five groups: **Plants** (the Outdoor Plant, Tree and Grass), **Paths and Fences** (the Garden Path and Garden Fence), **Furniture** (the Bench, Picnic Table, Hammock and Garden Umbrella), **Features** (the Fountain, Pond, Swimming Pool and Campfire) and **Parking** (the Car Park and Bike Rack).

**Meet** holds what a meeting room and a team need: the [Screen](/help/palette/screen/), the [Room Sign](/help/palette/room-sign/), the Notice Board, World Clock, Calendar Wall, Suggestion Box and Team Photo Wall, and the [Timer and Stopwatch](/help/collaboration/session-tools/timer/).

**Travel**, set apart on the strip, holds what takes people somewhere: the [Portal](/help/palette/tools/portals/), [Bring Focus](/help/palette/behaviour/bring-focus/), the [Avatar Pad](/help/palette/avatar-pads/), the [Directory](/help/palette/directory/), the Signpost, the [Elevator](/help/palette/elevator/), the [Stairs](/help/palette/stairs/) and the [Avatar Button](/help/palette/behaviour/mode-buttons/).

**Stickers** are for decoration: die-cut colour emoji and word badges. They aren't on the strip: open **Search** (press **S**) and type a name, like "heart" or "approved". See [Stickers](/help/palette/stickers/).

There is no **Media** category: pictures come from **Write**'s Image, and a Space does not add link cards. A link card already in a Space still shows, moves and deletes, and its address can still be changed.
