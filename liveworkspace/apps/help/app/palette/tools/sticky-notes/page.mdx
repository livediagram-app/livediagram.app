A sticky note is a small coloured card for a short piece of text: an idea, a question, a label, a brainstorm item. It is the digital version of the paper sticky, and it behaves like any other canvas element once placed.

## Adding one

<Step n={1}>
  Open the **Write** category on the palette strip, or press `N` to skip the palette.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Pick **Note**, then tap the canvas to drop a note (200 &times; 200 by default, or the size of the
  element you had selected), or drag to draw it at the size you want.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>Double-click the note to type.</Step>

## Writing on a note

A note holds several lines of text and wraps it as you type. While you are editing, **Enter** starts a new line, and **Escape** or clicking away keeps what you typed. Text that outgrows the note is clipped rather than spilling out, so resize the note to show the rest, or set the text size to **Scale** so the text shrinks to fit.

## Colours

A sticky note keeps its own colour whatever the [theme](/help/canvas/themes/): it lands in a pale amber, like a paper note, and changing the tab's theme never recolours it, because a note's colour is usually your own shorthand (ideas in yellow, risks in pink) rather than part of the <Noun />'s palette.

To recolour one, right-click it and open **Appearance**.

Its **Presets** are a pad of twelve note colours, each with a readable ink for its paper: **Classic**, **Lemon**, **Peach**, **Rose**, **Lilac**, **Sky**, **Mint**, **Teal**, **Slate**, **Paper**, **Charcoal** and **Ink**. **Colours** sets the paper and text by hand if none of them fits.

## Working with them

Stickies move, resize and lock like everything else. Scatter a wall of them for a brainstorm, select several at once to restyle or move them together, or group them so the cluster travels as one.
