The editor lays its tools out the same way on every device, a computer, a tablet or a phone: the palette is one strip across the top of the canvas, and the Explorer opens from a menu button in the top-left corner.

## What it does

The palette is a single strip pinned to the top centre of the canvas. From left to right it holds:

<AppNote app="livediagram">

- **Selection mode**: Select, Hand, Eraser, Laser and the rest.
- **The categories**, by name: in Diagram mode **Shapes**, **Write**, **Draw**, **Diagrams**, **Concepts**, **Layout**, **Icons** and **Tech**. Rest the pointer on one, or click it, and its elements open in a panel underneath. Pictures and avatars (**Image** and **Avatar**) sit at the end of **Layout**.
- **Library**, a button just before Search, once you or your team have saved shapes to place: it opens your shape libraries.
- **Search** (the magnifier, or press **S**), last on the strip, to find any element, icon or sticker by name.

</AppNote>

- **Library**: the shapes and components you and your teams saved, to place on any page in any of the apps (see [The Library](/help/palette/library/)). It opens in a panel of its own under its button.
- **Search** (the magnifier, or press **S**), last on the strip, to find any element, icon or sticker by name.

## The categories

Each category on the strip opens a panel of its elements under its name:

- **Rest the pointer on a category** and its panel opens; slide along the strip and the panel follows to each one you point at. Move away from both the strip and the panel and it closes. A click or a tap opens it too.
- **Click an element to add it, or drag it onto the canvas.** The panel closes once you have used one, so the canvas is clear. The strip at the bottom of the panel says what the element under your pointer does.
- With the keyboard: the **Left** and **Right** arrows move along the strip, **Down** or **Enter** opens a category, the arrows move through its elements, and **Escape** closes it.

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
**Each kind of page.** The strip offers what the page you are working on is made of:
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
- **Its elements come in named groups**, such as **Basics**, **Flowchart** and **More Shapes** in Shapes. Shapes shows them as pictures; the other categories as cards with a line on what each one is for. A tile's keyboard shortcut shows beside its name.
- **Card** and **Badge** open their presets in a second panel beside the first, which stays open behind it.
- **Icons**, **Tech** and **Session** list their groups down the left, with the open group's entries on the right and a search box across the top that looks through every group. Each reopens on the group you last had open. In Workshop mode, an **Icons | Stickers** switch beside Icons' search box turns it to the full-colour stickers and back, and remembers your choice; a search finds both, the icons first, then the stickers, each under its own heading.

  The names on the strip are one word each, and the editor modes offer different categories: **Write** holds the words (Text, notes and comments) and **Draw** the pens and lines (Freehand, Shape Pen, Highlighter, Polygon, Arrow and Line), and in Workshop mode **Ask** puts a question to the room while **Session** holds the timers, reveals, records, reactions, selection-mode buttons and the links that take people somewhere. On a window too narrow for the names, the strip shows the categories' icons alone.

## Search

**Search** finds an element without knowing which category holds it. Before you type, it shows the kinds of element already on your tab, so the ones you are using are one click away. The × at the end of the box clears it. Click one to add it, or press Enter to add the best match.

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
Type a name, or a word you would use for it ("database" finds the cylinder and a database icon), and matching tiles, icons and stickers appear in one list, the best name matches first.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
Type a name, or a word you would use for it ("database" finds the cylinder, a database icon and the database brands), and matching tiles, icons, stickers and technology marks appear in one list, the best name matches first; where two match equally well, the tiles your current editor mode offers lead.

Each editor mode keeps its palette to the tools that suit it, so a Diagram has no charts and a page has no pens. Search still finds those: they wait below, under **Elements From Other Modes**, closed until you open it, and every one of them works in any mode. That includes Draw mode's **Marker 1, 2 and 3**, in the colours and widths you gave them there: pick one and draw, and press Escape to put it down.

A few elements are on no mode's strip: the **Code** block, the [embeds](/help/palette/embed-elements/) (YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Figma, Google Docs, Website and Live Embed), the [device frames](/help/palette/devices/) and the [page blocks](/help/palette/components/) (Hero, Header, Banner, Stat Row and Process). Search lists them with your mode's own elements, in every mode, so typing a name is how you add one.

Search is not on an Event Storming board, where the notation is the palette, nor for a Participant, whose strip holds only the Participate tiles, and Draw mode shows its own dock instead of the strip. Wherever the strip's Search isn't shown, **S** keeps its old meaning and picks the Select tool.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">

## The order of the tiles

Each category keeps its own order: adding something never moves it, and its tiles always come in the same order.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  Its groups of related tiles (such as the desktop and the mobile devices, or the charts and the
  meters) always come in the same order.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  An Event Storming board's strip opens on its notation, and a Participant's on **Participate**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">A Participant's strip opens on **Participate**.</AppNote>

## What else the strip shows

- **Viewers** who can't edit have no strip, since there is nothing to add; the menu button still shows.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  On an Event Storming board the strip shows the notation's tiles instead of the two pickers, led by
  **Add from Photo** where it is available.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  **Zen mode** hides the strip and the menu button with the rest of the editor's chrome.
</AppNote>

## On a phone

On a phone the strip is narrower:

- The **menu button** sits in a card of its own at the left of the strip.

<AppNote app="livediagram">

- The **categories** show their icons only, a little further apart. Swipe them sideways to reach the rest; the selection mode and Search stay put. A category's panel opens across the width of the screen.
- **Layers** and **Collaborate** open above their buttons in the bottom row, as on a computer. Pinch to zoom; the **Fit** button is there too.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">

- The **categories** keep their names. Swipe them sideways to reach the rest, with the **Library** button at the end of the row; the selection mode and Search stay put. A category's panel opens across the width of the screen.
- **Collaborate** opens above its button in the bottom row, as on a computer. Pinch to zoom; the **Fit** button is there too.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">

- The **categories** show their icons only, a little further apart. Swipe them sideways to reach the rest; the selection mode and Search stay put. A category's panel opens across the width of the screen.
- **Collaborate** opens above its button in the bottom row, as on a computer. Pinch to zoom; the **Fit** button is there too.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">

- The **categories** keep their names, a little further apart, since the strip swipes sideways to reach the rest; the Edit | Walk toggle and Search (their icons alone) stay put. A category's panel opens across the width of the screen.
- **Collaborate** opens above its button in the bottom row, as on a computer. Pinch to zoom; the **Fit** button is there too.

</AppNote>

A phone held sideways counts as a phone too.
