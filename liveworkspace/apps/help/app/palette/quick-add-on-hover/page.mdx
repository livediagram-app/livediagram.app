When you select an element, a **+** button appears on each of its sides. Clicking one opens the quick-add menu, a strip of actions for that side: **Duplicate** (copy the element to that side, connected), **Arrow** (drag out an arrow) and **Text** (add a label beside it, with no connector). Quick-Add on Hover changes how that menu opens, so you can reach it without a click.

Some elements put their own actions on the **+**.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  A mind node offers **Add Child**, **Add Sibling** and **Duplicate**, a flowchart step **Add Step**
  and **Add Branch**, an entity **Add Related Entity** and **Add Field**, a table **Add Row** and
  **Add Column**, and a timeline **Add Milestone**.
</AppNote>

</AppNote>

## What it does

With the setting on, **hovering** a **+** opens its menu straight away, and the menu stays open while your pointer is over the + or the menu itself. Move away and it closes on its own after a moment, long enough to cross the gap between the two. When you are adding several connected elements in a row, it saves a click each time.

<AppNote app="livediagram">

<Figure caption="With Quick-Add on Hover on, resting the pointer on a + opens its menu, no click needed.">
  <QuickAddHoverMenu />
</Figure>

</AppNote>

The **+** buttons still only appear on the element you have selected, and clicking a + still works exactly as before, so turning this on only adds the hover shortcut; it never takes the click away. Dragging a **+** still skips the menu and starts an arrow from that side, either way.

Hovering needs a mouse or trackpad. On a touch screen there is no hover, so you tap the **+** as usual.

## Turning it on

It is **off by default**, since menus that open on hover can feel twitchy if you didn’t ask for them. Switch on **Quick-Add on Hover** in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), under **Editor**, in its **Mouse** section.

<AppNote app="livedesign">
