A **Card** is a single node with slots: a title, a muted meta line, a body, an icon and a row of tags. Use it wherever a node has more to say than its name: a service with what it runs on, a persona with their role, a feature with its status.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  In Diagram and Workshop mode it sits in the palette's **Layout** category ([Build
  Elements](/help/palette/build/)).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  It sits in the palette's [Components](/help/palette/components/) category.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  It is also in **Elements** on a wireframe page. Its tile opens six presets to start from:
  **Service** (an icon, a title and meta line, a body and tags), **Compact** (an icon with a title
  and meta line on one row), **Panel** (a header band with a badge), **Person** (an initial, a name
  and role), **Accent** (a coloured edge) and **Callout**. Each is an ordinary card, so you can
  switch any slot on or off afterwards.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  The Card tile also opens a few presets to start from: **Service**, **Compact**, **Panel**,
  **Person**, **Accent** and **Callout**.
</AppNote>

A new card shows every slot filled in, so you can see what each one is and switch off the ones you don't need.

## The slots

From the top:

- **Header Band**: a coloured band across the top that carries the title and meta line, for a card that reads as a titled panel.
- **Media**: at the top left, an icon on a **Badge** (a filled disc), on a **Tile** (a soft rounded square), or the title's **Initial**. Drop an icon from the palette onto a card to set it; a card with no media turns Tile on.
- **Title** and **Meta Line**: the name, and a quieter line beside it for the product, runtime, team or date.
- **Body**: the card's ordinary label. Double-click to edit it with the full text toolbar.
- **Tags**: up to six small chips along the bottom. A tag that doesn't fit the width is hidden until the card is wide enough again.

Select a card, then click its title, meta line or a tag to type in place. Enter keeps the change and Escape puts it back.

Right-click and open **Tools**, then **Card**, to switch **Header Band**, **Title** and **Meta Line** on or off and pick the **Media**. **Tools**, then **Tags**, adds, reorders or removes tags. **Add Tag** is also on the ring round a selected card.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The tags' colours are set at the bottom of the same **Card** section.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  The **Tags** section sits beside **Card** and adds, reorders and removes tags.
</AppNote>

Everything else is an ordinary element: resize it and the text re-wraps, recolour it from **Colours**, connect arrows to it, and animate it like any shape.

## Callouts are cards

The **Callout** tile places a card with a badge, a heading and a body: a soft note box for a tip or a warning. It is a card underneath, so you can add tags or a meta line to it like any other.

<AppNote app="livediagram">The tile is in Diagram mode's **Write** category.</AppNote>

## Accent edge

Any card, rectangle or stadium can carry an **accent edge**: a coloured strip down its left side, or across its top or bottom. Use it to colour-code them by team or status without filling the whole box.

<AppNote app="livediagram">A mind-map node can carry one too.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">The rectangle is the **Square** shape there.</AppNote>

<Figure caption="An accent edge on the left, the top and the bottom.">
  <AccentEdges />
</Figure>

Right-click, open **Appearance**, then **Border**, and pick the side under **Accent Edge**. **Edge Colour** sets its colour, and **Match Border** makes it the border's own colour again. The format painter copies it along with the border.

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  **Colour by tag.** Give a tag a colour once and every card on the tab carrying that tag follows
  it: the tag's chip is tinted, and the card draws an accent edge in that colour (on the left,
  unless it has an edge side of its own). Retag a card and its colour follows; change a tag's colour
  and every card with it changes. Right-click a card with tags, open **Tools**, then **Card**, and
  pick a colour for any of its tags under **Tag Colours**. **Clear** beside it removes the colour. A
  card takes the colour of its first coloured tag, and a card with its own **Edge Colour** keeps it.
  A tab holds colours for up to 12 tags, and tags match whatever their capitals.
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  For a node with a name and one quiet line under it, you don't need a card: type both lines in a
  rectangle, then pick **Title and Caption** from the text toolbar's paragraph menu. The first line
  turns bold and the rest turns into a smaller, muted caption.
</Tip>
