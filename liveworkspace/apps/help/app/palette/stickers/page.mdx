Every sticker lands the way a real one would: on a **die-cut white plate** with a soft shadow, so it reads as something stuck on top of the canvas rather than drawn into it. It arrives straight; tilt it yourself from the **Rotation** section of its right-click menu if you want the scrapbook look.

## How a sticker behaves

Stickers behave nothing like ordinary shapes or text:

- A sticker **keeps its own colours whatever the theme**. A thumbs up stays yellow, an APPROVED badge stays green, however you restyle the <Noun />.
- A sticker **takes no text**. There is no caption under it and no editor when you double-click, because it says what it says in its own artwork.
- A sticker is **always its own element**. Dragging one onto a shape drops a sticker on top; it never folds into that shape's label.

## The eleven groups

Stickers are filed in eleven groups; Search looks across all of them at once.

- **Badges**: the word pills, coloured by what they mean: APPROVED, DONE, SHIPPED, BLOCKED, REJECTED, URGENT, AT RISK, WIP, ON HOLD, IN REVIEW, NEEDS REVIEW, P0, P1, OWNER?, DISCUSS, OPEN QUESTION, NEW, IDEA, MUST HAVE, MVP, NICE TO HAVE, QUICK WIN, TO DO, DRAFT, PARKED, LATER, OUT OF SCOPE, and more.
- **Reactions**: responding to somebody else's work: thumbs up and down, clapping, raised hands, a heart, fire, 💯, eyes.

## Finding one

Search by what you mean, not by the emoji's official name. The catalogue matches on synonyms, so "celebrate" finds the party popper, "wip" finds both the WIP badge and the roadworks, and "thanks" finds the folded hands. A badge also answers to the word printed on it. Search always runs across **every** group, so you never have to guess where something was filed.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  Search looks through the icons too: the stickers it finds come after them, under their own
  **Stickers** heading.
</AppNote>

Stickers turn up in the editor's searches: the palette strip's [Search](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/#search) and the [Search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) can both add one to the canvas.

## Placing one

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">

<Step n={1}>
  Rest on **Icons** on the palette strip, or click it, and pick **Stickers** in the switch beside
  its search box. Icons remembers the side you last chose.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick a group from the list down the side, or type in the search box to look across all eleven at
  once. Stickers reopens on the group you last had open.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Click a sticker to add it to the canvas, or drag it out and drop it where you want it.
</Step>

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">

<Step n={1}>Open **Search** on the palette strip (the magnifier, or press **S**).</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Type what you mean, like "thumbs up", "blocked" or "party". Matching stickers appear with the
  other results.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>Click a sticker to add it to the canvas, or press Enter to add the best match.</Step>

</AppNote>

<Step n={4}>
  Drop a **BLOCKED** or **AT RISK** badge over the step it applies to. A sticker always sits on top
  as its own element, so you can move it away again without touching what is underneath.
</Step>

A sticker behaves like any other element once it is on the canvas: move it, resize it, rotate it, and it exports with the rest of the <Noun />. What it will not do is take text or take your theme's colours, by design, so its right-click menu has no Colours, Border, Presets or Text controls. Delete it like anything else, or press **Undo** straight after placing it.

<AppNote app="livediagram">You can put it on a layer.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">You can bring it to the front or send it to the back.</AppNote>

<Tip>
  Stickers work especially well for a quick reaction round on a shared <Noun />: drop a handful of
  thumbs and hearts near the ideas that landed, then move on. Because a sticker is just an element,
  everything you place is still there when you come back to the <Noun />.
</Tip>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  For a burst of confetti that everyone sees and then fades, use a [Reaction
  Pad](/help/palette/behaviour/reaction-pads/) instead.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  <Note>
    An emoji placed as an icon (from an imported document, say) stays a plain tinted glyph, not a
    die-cut sticker. Stickers come from here.
  </Note>
</AppNote>
