A **Picker** chooses at random when you press it. It lives in the **Tools** group of the palette's **Session** category, one of Workshop mode's [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/) categories.

## What it picks from

Either one of the people currently in the room, or one of the options you write. Right-click the Picker and open **Picker**: choose **People Here** (the default) or **A List**, and a list gets an **Options** box, one entry per line, up to 24 of them. A list with nothing in it shows "Nothing to pick from".

**People Here** means whoever is in the room at the moment you press it, so it can only pick somebody who is actually there. Alone, it picks you.

Give the Picker a label to say what it is choosing ("Who demos?"); the label sits small in the top corner, with a count beside it: how many people are here, or how many options there are.

## Rolling it

Press **Spin**. The names scroll past behind the window like a slot reel and slow to a stop on the highlighted line over about a second and a half. People appear with their own avatar as they go by. Once it has landed the button reads **Spin Again**. With **Reduce motion** on, the answer fades in instead of scrolling.

**Everyone watches the same roll**, not just whoever pressed it: the spin plays on every screen in the room and every one of them lands on the same name. That is the point of rolling on the canvas rather than saying a name out loud.

Only people with edit rights can spin it. On a Participant or Viewer share link the Picker shows its result as it lands, with no button to press.

While somebody holds the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) role, the room's answer is theirs to land. Other editors can still spin, but their roll plays on their own screen only and isn't saved.

A drag moves the Picker rather than rolling it, so you can rearrange the canvas without setting it off.

## Remove once drawn

For "everyone gets a turn" (stand-up order, who presents next, working down a list of questions), right-click the Picker, open **Picker** and turn on **Remove Once Drawn**. Each name it lands on then leaves the pool, so nobody is picked twice:

- The count reads "5 of 8 left", and on a big enough card the names already drawn are listed under the reel, struck through.
- Everyone in the room sees the same pick and the same names left, whoever pressed it.
- When every name has been drawn the Picker says so ("Everyone's had a turn", or "Every option is drawn") and **Spin** becomes **Reset**.
- **Reset** (the round arrow beside **Spin**) puts every name back and starts a new round. Like spinning, only editors can reset, and the facilitator while somebody holds the role.

Turning the setting on or off also starts a fresh round.

<Tip>Handy for "who demos next" without anyone having to volunteer.</Tip>
