<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **Run commands with ⌘K.** You don't need to remember where every button lives. Press `⌘K` (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux) in any plan, type a few letters of what you want, and pick it. The same box finds plans, tabs, settings and help, and runs commands too.

**A few to try.**

- `new` and **Create New Tab** adds a tab and opens **Start Planning** on it, ready for a board, sheet, dashboard or form.
- `rename` offers **Rename Tab** and **Rename Plan**.
- `export` opens the tab's **Export…**, for a CSV, an image, a PDF or JSON.
- `share` opens **Share Plan**.
- `zen` hides the editor's chrome so only your board or sheet is on screen; `Z` or `Escape` brings it back.
- `keyboard` opens Settings on its **Keyboard** category, where you can switch shortcuts off.
- `dark` finds the **Theme** setting, so you can switch between light and dark.

- On the left, the **selection mode** (what your pointer does), shown as a grid in three groups. **Edit**: **Select** (`V`), **Hand** (`H`), **Eraser** (`E`) and the [Format](/help/tips-and-tricks/format-painter/) painter. **Present**: **Laser** (`K`) and **Spotlight**. **Preview**: **Isometric** (`I`) and **Zen** (`Z`). Spotlight is desktop only, and everything after Hand except Zen stays greyed out until the canvas has something on it. The [Slide Deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/) isn't here: it lives in **Tools** in the bottom bar. See [Selection Modes](/help/selection-modes/).
- Then the **categories**, by name. Rest the pointer on one, or click it, and its elements open in a panel underneath, in named groups; click one to add it or drag it onto the canvas. In Diagram mode they are **Shapes**, **Write** (text, notes and comments), **Draw** (the pens and arrows), **Diagrams** (mind maps, flowcharts, tables and more), **Concepts**, **Layout** (lanes, frames, cards and badges, then images and avatars), **Icons** and **Tech** (full-colour technology marks), plus **Library**, last, once you have [imported a shape library](/help/explorer/shape-libraries/).

  [Workshop mode](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/) has no Diagrams, Concepts or Tech: after **Layout** come **Ask** and **Session** (the tools to time, record, react, switch modes and move around), the interactive elements for workshops, then Icons (with the **Stickers** on its Stickers side) and **Data**.

**Search** (the magnifier, or `S`) finds any element by name. Each mode has its own set: Workshop adds **Stickers**, the **Data** charts and its session kit. An [Event Storming board](/help/canvas/event-storming-boards/) opens on its notation and has no Search. See [where each one lives](/help/palette/tools/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  **Adding elements.** Pick a tile and it arms a **tap-or-drag** gesture: click a page to drop the element at its natural size, or drag on the page to size it as you place it. You can also drag a tile straight onto a page.

If a boxed element is already selected when you click to drop another, the new one inherits its width and height, so you can chain similarly-sized elements quickly. (A circle or diamond takes a square the size of the larger side.)

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **Adding elements.** Pick a tile and it arms a **tap-or-drag** gesture: click the canvas to drop the element at its natural size, or drag on the canvas to size it as you place it. You can also drag a tile straight onto the canvas. An annotation is the exception: it drops at the centre of the visible canvas, ready to move.

If a boxed element is already selected when you click to drop another, the new one inherits its width and height, so you can chain similarly-sized nodes quickly. (A circle or diamond takes a square the size of the larger side, and a selected frame or lane never passes its size on.)

</AppNote>
