<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  **The shortcut reference.** Run **Keyboard Shortcuts** from [search](/help/search-panel/) (`⌘K`)
  and [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) opens on its **Keyboard** category. Below the
  on/off switch, **All Shortcuts** lists every binding the editor knows, in collapsible groups by
  purpose, so you never have to memorise the full set. You can also get there yourself: open
  **Settings** from the sliders icon in the editor's bottom bar and pick **Keyboard**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  That is the way in for a Participant or a Viewer, whose search doesn't list the **Keyboard Shortcuts** command.

- `V` to **Select** (the default on desktop), with `S` kept working as a legacy alias where the [palette strip](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/)'s **Search** isn't shown; where it is, `S` opens Search.
- `H` for the **Hand** (pan) tool, the default on touch viewports.
- `F` to draw a **Frame**, the section backdrop that carries what you draw inside it.
- `K` for the **Laser** pointer, for drawing attention while you share your screen.
- `Z` to toggle [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) on and off. `Escape` exits it too, once nothing is selected (the first press clears the selection).

The reference in Settings stays available in every role. On a view-only link, only the keys that change nothing still work: `V` (or `S` or `1`), `H`, `K` and `Z`, zoom and zoom to fit, search, `Tab` to step through elements, and `Escape`. Everything that edits is switched off. A [Participant](/help/collaboration/sharing/) sits in between: they can type into labels, delete, undo and redo, but of the keys that add elements only `T` (or `8`), `N` and `9` work, and copy, cut, duplicate, select all, lock, layer order and nudging stay off.
</AppNote>

  **Everywhere in the editor.**

| Keys     | Does                                                                                                                   |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `⌘K`     | Open [search and commands](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/) (`⌘.` does the same)                  |
| `⌘Z`     | [Undo](/help/user-interface/undo/)                                                                                     |
| `⌘⇧Z`    | [Redo](/help/user-interface/redo/) (Ctrl Y works too)                                                                  |
| `Z`      | Turn [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) on or off                                                                       |
| `Escape` | Close what is open, or leave Zen mode                                                                                  |
| `F6`     | Move to the next part of the editor: header, the tab's content, the controls, the tab bar (Shift for the previous one) |

On a view-only link, `⌘K`, `Z` and `Escape` still work, since they change nothing. Undo and redo are for people who can edit, and for a Participant, who undoes and redoes their own changes.

A key typed into a text box is just a letter: a `z` typed into a card's title never switches on Zen mode.

**On a board.** Move to a card with `Tab`, and while it has focus the board takes these keys:

| Keys               | Does                                         |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Arrow keys         | Move between cards                           |
| `Enter` or `Space` | Open the card                                |
| `⇧←` / `⇧→`        | Move the card to the previous or next column |
| `⇧↑` / `⇧↓`        | Move the card up or down its column          |
| `N`                | Add a card to the card's column              |
| `Delete` / `⌫`     | Move the card to the Trash                   |

Each move is read out to a screen reader ("#12 moved to In Progress, position 2 of 4"). See [Boards](/help/plan/boards/) and [Cards](/help/plan/cards/).

**In a sheet.** A [Sheet](/help/plan/sheets/) works the way spreadsheets usually do:

| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| `Enter` / `Tab`    | Move down / right (Shift goes back)                                                 |
| `F2`, or just type | Edit the cell                                                                       |
| `Delete` / `⌫`     | Clear the selected cells                                                            |
| `⌘B` / `⌘I` / `⌘U` | Bold / italic / underline                                                           |
| `⌘F` / `⌘⇧H`       | Find / find and replace                                                             |
| `⌘D` / `⌘R`        | Fill down / fill right                                                              |
| `⌘;` / `⌘⇧;`       | Today's date / the time now                                                         |
| `Alt↓`             | Open the cell's dropdown                                                            |
| `⌘+` / `⌘-` / `⌘0` | Zoom the sheet in / out / back to 100%                                              |

## Turning shortcuts off

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  Shortcuts are handy, but they are not for everyone: if you dictate into the browser, or share it

<AppNote app="livedesign,livehuddle">
  Turning them off switches off the editor's shortcuts, not just the single letters: the tool keys,
  `Delete`, `Escape`, and the chords such as `⌘Z` and `⌘C` all stop, and the browser's own behaviour
  for those keys comes back. A few keep working because they are part of how the canvas is driven
  rather than shortcuts: pasting with `⌘V` (it goes through the browser's own paste), `Space` + drag
  to pan, and holding `⌘` to show the palette's badges. Use the on-screen controls (undo and redo
  sit in the bottom-right corner) while they are off. This choice is saved in your browser and,
  unlike most preferences, deliberately stays per device rather than following you across sign-in:
  that way you can keep shortcuts on where you have a full keyboard and off on a device where you
  don't.
</AppNote>
