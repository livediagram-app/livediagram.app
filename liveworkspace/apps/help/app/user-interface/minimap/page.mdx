<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Some canvas apps keep a zoomed-out **Map** of the whole canvas in a corner. <AppName /> doesn't show one: every tab is laid out in [pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/), and the pages themselves are the overview.

**Finding your way round a tab.**

- **Contents** lists every page in the tab with a picture of it, its number and its name, and highlights the one you are on. Press a page to glide there. On a computer it sits as a sidebar down the left once a tab has more than one page; on a phone it is a button in the bottom-right corner. See [Contents](/help/pages/working-with-pages/#contents).
- **The page navigator** in the bottom-right corner, beside Undo and Redo, shows "2 of 5" between **Previous page** and **Next page**, each fitting that page to the screen. On a stack of pages (flashcards, or any tab you stack), the page in front has its own **Previous** and **Next** instead.
- **Fit** in the bottom-right corner fits the page you are nearest. The [zoom controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/) zoom in and out.
- With nothing selected, the **arrow keys** move to the next or previous page.

**The Map settings.** [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) still lists **Panels › Map** (**Enable Map**, **Dim Outside the View** and **Map Size**). Because a <AppName /> tab always has pages, the Map never shows and these rows have no effect.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  **Navigating with it.**

- **Tap** anywhere on the Map to re-centre the canvas on that spot.
- **Drag** across it to pan continuously: the view box follows your pointer.
- **Scroll** on the Map to zoom the canvas in or out, centred where you scroll.

The view box updates live as you pan and zoom by any means, so the Map doubles as a constant "you are here" indicator.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The shapes use their real outlines (a circle reads as a circle), and arrows show as connecting
  lines, so the overview is a faithful miniature rather than a grid of blocks.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Elements on a hidden [layer](/help/canvas/layers/) drop out of the Map too, so it always matches
  the canvas.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The elements use their real outlines (a round rug reads as round), so the overview is a faithful
  miniature rather than a grid of blocks.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  **When it shows, and turning it off.** The Map appears once a tab has **four or more elements** to
  map, and it sits in the bottom-left corner. It's a desktop convenience, so it stays hidden on
  phones where the canvas is already edge-to-edge.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">It steps aside in [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/).</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  Like the other corner panels, it can be collapsed from its header or dragged to another corner (see [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/)).

Its settings live in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), under **Panels › Map**:

- **Enable Map**: switch it off to hide the Map, and on again to bring it back.
- **Dim Outside the View**: shades the part of the Map outside what you are looking at, so the view box stands out. On by default.
- **Map Size**: **Short**, **Medium** (the default) or **Tall**.

  <Tip>
    On a big canvas, scroll on the Map to zoom straight into a far corner, instead of panning all the way there and zooming separately.
  </Tip>
</AppNote>
