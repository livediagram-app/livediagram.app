<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livehuddle">
  Select something on the canvas and a **toolbar** appears next to it, holding the quick actions for what you have selected. It is contextual: it shows up only when there is a selection, and its contents change to match. A small caption above it names the selection, for example "Selected Square".
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Select several elements on the canvas and a **toolbar** floats over them, holding the quick actions for the group. One selected element has no toolbar: it uses its right-click menu instead (see below).
</AppNote>

## One element selected

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  From left to right:

  - **More** (**⋯**): opens the element's full menu, the same one a right-click opens, with its colours, borders, text styling and the rest. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/).
  - **Add Text** (or **Edit Text** once the element has some), and **Edit Points** on a path (drawn with the Path tool, or made by Combine), to adjust its points and curves. On a pencil or marker drawing, **Edit Points** first tidies it into a path (as **Tidy** does), then opens its points.
  - **Duplicate**, and beside it **Save to Library**, which keeps the element in your [Library](/help/palette/library/) (your own or a team's) to place again in any <Noun />, in any app that can draw it.
  - **Bring to Front** and **Send to Back**.
  - **Lock** (or **Unlock**) and **Delete**.

  Which buttons show depends on the element: an image has no text to add, a design kept only in [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) has no Library to save to, and a locked element hides **Edit Points**, and its **Delete** is greyed out until you **Unlock** it. To comment on an element, open **More** and choose **Add Comment** under its **Resources**. On a link that can't edit, the toolbar holds just a comments button instead (and a right-click opens a [short element menu](/help/user-interface/context-menus/)): **Add Comment** on a Participant link, so you can still join a discussion, and **Show Comments** on a view-only link, only on an element that already has comments, to read them (embeds show no comments).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  A single selected element has **no toolbar** floating over it, so nothing covers the page you are writing about. Everything the toolbar used to hold is in the element's menu: right-click the element (a long press on a touch screen, or **Shift+F10** with the keyboard). Its top row holds **Save to Library**, with **Delete** at the right, and its last section, **Resources**, holds **Assign Action** and **Add Comment**; duplicate with **Cmd/Ctrl+D**, lock with **Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+L**, and reorder from its **Layer** section. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/).

  On a Participant or view-only link the same right-click opens a short menu with what that link may do, such as **Add Comment** or **Show Comments**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  From left to right:

  - **More** (**⋯**): opens the element's full menu, the same one a right-click opens, with its colours, borders, text styling and the rest. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/).
  - **Add Text** (or **Edit Text** once the element has some).
  - **Rotate Left** and **Rotate Right**, on a piece of furniture that turns, such as a chair or a desk: a quarter turn each way.
  - **Duplicate**.
  - **Format Painter**: takes this element's style, ready to paint onto the next elements you press. See [Format Painter](/help/selection-modes/format-painter/).
  - **Bring to Front** and **Send to Back**.
  - **Delete**.

  Which buttons show depends on the element: an image has no text to add, and only furniture turns. An element from a feature <AppName /> no longer has keeps **Delete** alone.

  The toolbar is an Editor's. On a Participant or Viewer link, selecting an element shows no toolbar, and a right-click opens a [short element menu](/help/user-interface/context-menus/#on-a-participant-or-view-only-link) instead.
</AppNote>

## Several elements selected

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livehuddle">
  Drag a marquee or Shift-click to select several, and the toolbar switches to the actions that make sense across a group:
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Drag a marquee or Shift-click to select several, and a toolbar floats over them with the actions that make sense across a group:
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  - **More** (**⋯**): the selection's menu, for styling everything at once.
  - **Filter Selection** (only when the selection mixes kinds): narrows it by type, so you can marquee over a busy area and keep, say, just the arrows. See [Multi-Select](/help/canvas/selecting-and-grouping/multi-select/).
  - **Duplicate**, with the arrows between the selected elements included, and **Save to Library**, to keep the whole selection in your Library.
  - **Combine** (on a [logo page](/help/pages/logo-pages/), when the shapes can be combined): unite, subtract, intersect or exclude them into one shape.
  - **Export**: export just these elements.
  - **Lock** (or **Unlock**) and **Delete**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  - **More** (**⋯**): the selection's menu, for styling everything at once.

  - **Duplicate**, with the arrows between the selected elements included.
  - **Save to Library**: keep the selection as one item in a [shape library](/help/explorer/shape-libraries/).
  - **Export**: export just these elements.
  - **Lock** (**Unlock** when every selected element is locked) and **Delete**.
</AppNote>

The toolbar follows your selection so the controls are always close to what they affect, and disappears the moment you deselect, handing the canvas back.
