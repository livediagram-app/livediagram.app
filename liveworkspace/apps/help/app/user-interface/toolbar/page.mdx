<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livehuddle">
  Select something on the canvas and a **toolbar** appears next to it, holding the quick actions for
  what you have selected. It is contextual: it shows up only when there is a selection, and its
  contents change to match. A small caption above it names the selection, for example "Selected
  Square".
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Select several elements on the canvas and a **toolbar** floats over them, holding the quick
  actions for the group. One selected element has no toolbar: it uses its right-click menu instead
  (see below).
</AppNote>

## One element selected

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livehuddle">From left to right:</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
- **More** (**⋯**): opens the element's full menu, the same one a right-click opens, with its colours, borders, text styling and the rest. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/).
- **Add Text** (or **Edit Text** once the element has some), and **Edit Points** on a path (drawn with the Path tool, or made by Combine), to adjust its points and curves. On a pencil or marker drawing, **Edit Points** first tidies it into a path (as **Tidy** does), then opens its points.
- **Duplicate**, and beside it **Save to Library**, which keeps the element in your [Library](/help/palette/library/) (your own or a team's) to place again in any <Noun />, in any app that can draw it.
- **Bring to Front** and **Send to Back**.
- **Lock** (or **Unlock**) and **Delete**.

Which buttons show depends on the element: an image has no text to add, a design kept only [Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) has no Library to save to, and a locked element hides **Edit Points**, and its **Delete** is greyed out until you **Unlock** it. To comment on an element, open **More** and choose **Add Comment** under its **Resources**. On a link that can't edit, the toolbar holds just a comments button and, on an element with words, **Send to Plan** instead (and a right-click opens a [short element menu](/help/user-interface/context-menus/)): **Add Comment** on a Participant link, so you can still join a discussion, and **Show Comments** on a view-only link, only on an element that already has comments, to read them (embeds show no comments).

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  A single selected element has **no toolbar** floating over it, so nothing covers the page you are writing about. Everything the toolbar used to hold is in the element's menu: right-click the element (a long press on a touch screen, or **Shift+F10** with the keyboard). Its top row holds **Save to Library**, with **Delete** at the right, and its last section, **Resources**, holds **Assign Action** and **Add Comment**; duplicate with **Cmd/Ctrl+D**, lock with **Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+L**, and reorder from its **Layer** section. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/).

On a Participant or view-only link the same right-click opens a short menu with what that link may do, such as **Add Comment** or **Show Comments**.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
- **More** (**⋯**): opens the element's menu, the same one a right-click opens, with its size and its own settings. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/).
- **Duplicate**.
- **Delete**.

To rename a piece, double-click it and type; a Room's **Room name** is in its settings (**More**, then **Room**). To turn a piece of furniture, open **More** and pick a way in its own section (see [Turning Furniture](/help/palette/virtual-office/turning-furniture/)). An element from a feature <AppName /> no longer has keeps **Delete** alone.

The toolbar is an Editor's. On a Participant or Viewer link, selecting an element shows no toolbar, and a right-click opens a [short element menu](/help/user-interface/context-menus/#on-a-participant-or-view-only-link) instead.

</AppNote>

## Several elements selected

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livehuddle">
  Drag a marquee or Shift-click to select several, and the toolbar switches to the actions that make
  sense across a group:
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Drag a marquee or Shift-click to select several, and a toolbar floats over them with the actions
  that make sense across a group:

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  - **More** (**⋯**): the selection's menu, for styling everything at once. - **Filter Selection**
  (only when the selection mixes kinds): narrows it by type, so you can marquee over a busy area and
  keep, say, just the arrows. See [Multi-Select](/help/canvas/selecting-and-grouping/multi-select/).
  - **Duplicate**, with the arrows between the selected elements included, and **Save to Library**,
  to keep the whole selection in your Library. - **Combine** (on a [logo
  page](/help/pages/logo-pages/), when the shapes can be combined): unite, subtract, intersect or
  exclude them into one shape. - **Export**: export just these elements. - **Lock** (or **Unlock**)
  and **Delete**.
</AppNote>

The toolbar follows your selection so the controls are always close to what they affect, and disappears the moment you deselect, handing the canvas back.

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  For a selected shape or arrow, the [Quick Style bar](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/) beside the
  page's title bar offers the most-used colours and widths too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Beside a selected shape or arrow, the [Quick Style panel](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/) offers
  the most-used colours and widths too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">

  colours and widths too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  While you drag, resize or reshape the selection it steps aside, and comes back at the new place
  when you let go.
</AppNote>
