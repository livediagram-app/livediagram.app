A **context menu** is the menu you get by right-clicking, or by pressing and holding for half a second on touch. Each one is scoped to whatever you clicked, so it only ever offers what makes sense there. On a phone the menus open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen.

## On an element

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments">
  The element menu is where an element is **styled and described**. It is built from collapsible
  sections, and which ones appear depends on what you clicked.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The element menu is where an element is **styled and set up**. It is built from collapsible
  sections, with **Appearance** and **Tools** opening to the side, and which ones appear depends on
  what you clicked.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  Appearance and Animation (when it has text) open to the side. Along its top, a slim bar holds
  **Clear Styles** at the right, while the element has a style to clear. Below it, for a shape:
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Appearance, Animation (when it has text), Text and Resources open to the side. Along its top, a
  row of buttons holds the element kind's own verbs, such as **Add Child** and **Add Sibling** on a
  [mind map](/help/palette/mind-maps/) node or **Add Step** and **Add Branch** on a
  [flowchart](/help/palette/flowcharts/) step, with **Save to Library** and **Clear Styles** at the
  right. Below it, for a shape:
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  **Appearance** opens to the side. Along its top, one row of buttons: **Save to Library** at the
  left (keep the element to place again in any <Noun />; not on a locked element), and **Delete** at
  the right. Below them, for a shape:
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  For a shape:

- **Clear Styles**, the eraser in a slim bar across the top of the menu, while the element has a style to clear: one press puts it back to the tab's theme.
- **Arrange**: **Bring to Front**, **Send to Back** and its opacity.
- **Size** and **Rotation**.
- **Appearance**: the shape's **Presets**, **Colours** (text, background and border, with **Reset to Theme**), **Border**, **Shadow** and **Finish**, and, once it has text, a **Text** flyout of its own with font, size, padding, alignment and markers.

Images and links add a section of their own (**Image**, **Link**), and the Space's pieces gather their own settings under **Tools**: which kind a piece is, a chair's facing, a room's walls and floor, a portal's destination, the Stage, a Screen's address and more.

</AppNote>

On a desktop pointer, the style controls **preview on hover**: rest the pointer on a colour, border, preset, rotation, marker, alignment, or size tile and the selection shows the change live; it only sticks when you click, and moving away reverts it. Comparing looks costs nothing.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments">The same goes for a shape tile.</AppNote>

## On empty canvas

Right-click empty canvas and you get the **tab menu** (below) with a labelled **Paste** row straight under the quick actions, since a right-click on empty space is usually "paste what I copied". Pasted elements land just offset from where they were copied, selected and ready to drag into place.

## On a tab

Right-click a tab in the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/), or use its **⋯** button, for the tab menu:

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments">
  - A row of quick actions: **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Paste**, **Side by Side** (**Close Side by
  Side** while a split is open; not on a phone or with only one tab), **Lock Tab** (or **Unlock
  Tab**), and **Delete**, which asks you to confirm and is unavailable while the tab is locked or
  when it is the <Noun />
  's only tab.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  - A row of quick actions: **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Paste**, **Side by Side** (**Close Side by
  Side** while a split is open; not on a phone or with only one tab) and **Delete**. Delete asks
  nothing, since **Undo** brings the tab back, and is unavailable when it is the space's only tab.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
  - Then full-width rows, in groups split by a line: **Organise in Tab Folder** (a [tab
  folder](/help/tabs/tab-folders/)) and **Add to <Noun capital />
  ** (link it into another of your <Noun plural />, so it stays one live tab and edits show in both
  places); **Import**, **Export** and **Clear**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  - Then full-width rows, in groups split by a line: **Organise in Tab Folder** (a [tab
  folder](/help/tabs/tab-folders/)) and **Add to Another Canvas** (link it into another of your
  canvases, so it stays one live tab and edits show in both places); **Import**, **Export** and
  **Clear**. A tab's [editor mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/) is not in this menu: it is the switch
  beside the menu button at the top left.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  - Then full-width rows, in groups split by a line: **Organise in Tab Folder** (a [tab
  folder](/help/tabs/tab-folders/)) and **Add to Another Space** (link it into another of your
  spaces, so it stays one live tab and edits show in both places); **Attendance**, who was in the
  tab and when (see [Attendance](/help/collaboration/live-presence/tab-attendance/)); **Import**,
  **Export** and **Clear**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  A connected tab, one that stands for another item (in <AppName /> or another app) and opens it,
  has a shorter menu: **Organise in Tab Folder** and **Remove Tab** (the other item is left as it

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">

## On a Participant or view-only link

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram">
  Right-click an element (a long press on a touch screen) and you get a short menu with only what
  your link lets you do: **Add Comment** (or **View Comments**) and **Edit Text** on a Participant
  link, **Show Comments** on a view-only link where the element already has comments, and **Open
  Link**, **View Note** and **Copy Text** wherever the element has them. There is no tab menu on
  these links.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Right-click an element (a long press on a touch screen) and you get a short menu with only what your link lets you do: **Add Comment** (or **View Comments**) and **Edit Text** on a Participant link, **Show Comments** on a view-only link where the element already has comments, **Open Link** on a link card or a video, and **Copy Text** wherever the element has words. There is no tab menu on these links.

With the keyboard, select an element (press `Tab` on the canvas) and press **Shift+F10**, or the menu key, to open its menu.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  Right-click an element (a long press on a touch screen) and you get a short menu with only what
  your link lets you do: **Copy Text** wherever the element has text. Empty canvas has no menu on
  these links. A Participant's tab menu holds **Attendance** alone; a Viewer has no tab menu.
</AppNote>

Context menus close as soon as you pick an action or click elsewhere, and `Escape` closes them too.
