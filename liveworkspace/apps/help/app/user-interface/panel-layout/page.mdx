<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments">
  Everything around the canvas is a toolbar, a **panel** or a fixed control. <AppNote app="livehuddle">

<AppNote app="liveplanner">

Around it sit a few fixed pieces of chrome, the same on a computer, a tablet and a phone: a card in each top corner, the tab's own header, a cluster of buttons in the bottom-right corner, and the tab bar along the bottom.
</AppNote>

## What sits where

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  - **Top-left**: the **menu button**, which opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) (your designs and folders) under it, with the tab's name beside it on a computer.
  - **Top centre**: the [palette strip](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/), for picking a selection mode and adding things to the canvas.
  - **Top-right**: a card with **Share** (or **Copy** on someone else's design) and your account, with **Prototype** first on a wireframe tab (see [The Top Corners](/help/user-interface/title-bar/)). The tool panels, when they are in use, sit below it.
  - **Down the left**: **Contents**, the list of pages, when it is docked as a sidebar (Settings › Editor › **Contents**).
  - **Bottom-right**: a fixed cluster of buttons: Undo and Redo, the page navigator (on a computer, while the tab has two pages or more and Contents is not docked), **Contents** (unless it is docked as a sidebar), **Photo** while a photo page is in focus, **Inspect** and **Flow View** on a wireframe tab, **Collaborate** (while the tab has a comment or an action), the **Theme & canvas** button, and the [zoom controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/). Their panels open as popovers over their buttons, and close when you press the button again. On a phone the whole cluster shares one row.
  - **Along the bottom**: the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/), with the [quick controls](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/) (Tools, Settings and the appearance switch) at its right-hand end.
  - **Next to a selection**: the [toolbar](/help/user-interface/toolbar/).
  - **Beside a page's title bar**: the [Quick Style bar](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/), for a selected shape or arrow.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">

  - **Top centre**: the [palette strip](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/), for picking a selection mode and adding things to the canvas. In [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/) its dock takes the strip's place.
  - **Top-right**: a card with **Share** (or **Copy**), **Present** and your account (see [The Top Corners](/help/user-interface/title-bar/)), and below it the tool panels, when they are in use.
  - **Bottom-left**: the [Map](/help/user-interface/minimap/), on a computer or tablet, once a tab has a few elements.
  - **Bottom-right**: a fixed cluster of buttons, left to right: Undo and Redo, the [session tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/) (**Timer**, **Vote** and **Poll**, in Workshop mode), **Collaborate** (while the tab has a comment or an action), then one strip of **Tidy** (in Diagram mode, for Editors; see [Layout Cleanup](/help/tools/layout-cleanup/)), **Layers** and the **Theme & canvas** button (named **Style** on a wide screen; none in Draw mode), and last the [zoom controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/). Their panels open as popovers over their buttons, and close when you press the button again. On a phone the whole cluster shares one row.
  - **Along the bottom**: the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/), with the [quick controls](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/) (Get Started when a tour is on offer, Tools with Search, Settings and appearance) at its right-hand end.
  - **Next to a selection**: the [toolbar](/help/user-interface/toolbar/), and the [Quick Style panel](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/) for a shape or arrow.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  **Put panels in the corner you want.** With a mouse or trackpad, the tool panels can be dragged by their header to any corner of the screen. On a touch screen, tapping a panel's header collapses or expands it instead. Snap guides appear in the four corners while you drag, and the nearest one highlights to show where the panel will land; drop it away from every corner and it stays right where you left it. Panels sharing a corner stack neatly and reflow when one of them is hidden.
</AppNote>

## On a phone

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  On a phone the menu button sits in a card of its own at the left of the strip, and the strip's tiles swipe sideways. Tap a panel's header to collapse or expand it. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/#on-a-phone).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  On a phone the menu button (and the mode switch, as an icon) sits in a card of its own at the left of the strip, the top-right card shows icons alone, and the strip's tiles swipe sideways. Tap a panel's header to collapse or expand it. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/#on-a-phone).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  On a phone the menu button sits in a card of its own at the left of the strip, the strip's tiles swipe sideways, and the Share card meets the right edge of the screen with its buttons as icons. Tap a panel's header to collapse or expand it. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/#on-a-phone).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  On a phone the bottom-right cluster stays on one row, its buttons sharing the width there is, and shows icons without their names. The Trash button is left off a phone, except while the session tools are showing: then the card strip folds into one **More card tools** button, whose menu includes **Trash**. The top-left card drops the logo and the top-right card shows its buttons as icons, and a phone held sideways folds the tab bar away on its own, with a handle at the top right to bring it back.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  [Panel Opacity](/help/palette/panel-opacity/) fades every panel so the canvas shows through, and **UI Scale** under **Appearance** makes them bigger or smaller (on a computer).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **UI Scale** under **Appearance** in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) makes the panels and corner buttons bigger or smaller on a computer, and **Panel Opacity** under **Panels** lets what is behind a panel show through.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">

    Want even less on screen? [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) (`Z`) hides every panel and leaves just the canvas and the zoom controls.
  </Tip>
</AppNote>
