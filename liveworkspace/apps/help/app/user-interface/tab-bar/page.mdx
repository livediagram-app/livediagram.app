## What you can do here

- **Switch** between tabs by clicking one. A small stack of initials avatars on a tab shows who else is on it right now.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Each tab also shows a small icon for what fills it.

- **Add** a new tab with the **+** button straight after the last tab. It arrives numbered ("Tab 2", "Tab 3"), wearing the current tab's theme, and opens on **Start Planning**, where you pick a board, sheet, form or dashboard for it. See [Planning Basics](/help/plan/planning-basics/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  - **Add** a new tab with the **+** button straight after the last tab. It arrives numbered ("Tab
  2", "Tab 3"), wearing the current tab's theme, background and font, on **Start Making Pages**, so
  you can begin from a [template](/help/canvas/templates/) or close it to leave the tab blank.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  - **Add** a new tab with the **+** button straight after the last tab. It arrives numbered ("Tab
  2", "Tab 3"), wearing the current tab's theme, background and font, in the same mode, on that
  mode's start, so you can begin from a [template](/help/canvas/templates/) or close it to leave the
  tab blank.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  - **Add** a new tab with the **+** button straight after the last tab. It arrives numbered ("Tab
  2", "Tab 3"), wearing the current tab's theme, background and font, opening on **Start a Space**,
  so you can begin from a [template](/help/canvas/templates/) or close it to leave the tab blank,
  with an [Avatar Pad](/help/palette/avatar-pads/) where people arrive.
</AppNote>

- **Rename** a tab by double-clicking the active one, or from its menu.
- **Reorder** tabs by dragging them along the bar; a caret shows where the tab will land.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  - **Manage** a tab from its menu: right-click any tab (it becomes the active one), or click the
  **⋯** button on the active tab. The menu holds **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Paste**, **Lock Tab**
  and **Delete**, plus **Organise in Tab Folder**, **Add to <Noun capital />
  ** (link it into another <Noun />
  ), import, export and more. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/#on-a-tab).
</AppNote>

- **Group** related tabs into one-level folders with **Organise in Tab Folder** when a <Noun /> grows. See [Tab Folders](/help/tabs/tab-folders/).

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  A [connected file](/help/tabs/connected-files/)'s tab gets a shorter menu: **Remove Tab**, **Organise in Tab Folder** and, if the connection has broken, **Connect Again**.

On a Participant or Viewer link, or a [link to a single tab](/help/collaboration/sharing/one-tab/), the bar still switches tabs, but there is no **+** button, no renaming and no tab menu.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">

On a Participant or Viewer link, or a link to a single tab, the bar still switches tabs, but there is no **+** button, no tab menu, and tabs cannot be renamed or dragged.

</AppNote>
