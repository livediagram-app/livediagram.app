The **quick controls** sit at the right-hand end of the bottom bar, after the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/)'s tabs, and gather the actions you want reachable at any moment, whatever you are doing on the canvas.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram">
  They stay put while the rest of the interface comes and goes with your selection.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livehuddle">
  They stay put while the rest of the interface comes and goes with your selection, and a matching
  cluster appears at the bottom of the full-page [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), so the
  two screens don't hide their controls in different places.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  - **Get Started**: shown while a guided tour is on offer, such as the tour of the kind of page you are on, or of a complex element you have selected. Press it to pick a tour and start it. Once you have taken a tour it leaves the button; **Getting Started** in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/#getting-started) offers it again.
  - **Tools**: one menu for the tools that work on the whole <Noun />, in groups:
    - **Search…** opens the [search panel](/help/search-panel/) to jump to any <Noun />, tab, or element, to drop something onto the canvas, or to run a command. `⌘K` (Ctrl+K) opens it from the keyboard too.
    - **Webhooks**, if you can manage the <Noun />'s webhooks: tell [other services](/help/collaboration/webhooks/) when the <Noun /> changes.
    - **Walkthroughs**: record a [walkthrough](/help/collaboration/walkthroughs/) of the <Noun />, or play one.
    - **History**, unless you opened the <Noun /> through a share link: [who changed what and when](/help/collaboration/history/), with the <Noun />'s [snapshots](/help/collaboration/snapshots/) pinned on its timeline.

    History, Webhooks and Walkthroughs need you signed in, and a <Noun /> kept [Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) saved to your account first: until then their rows carry a **Sign In** or **Sync Design** badge, and pressing one asks you to do that.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">

<AppNote app="livehuddle">

Pick one to start it. See [The Receptionist](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/avatar-tour/).
  - **Tools**: a toolbox that opens a panel of the tools that work on the whole <Noun />, grouped under small headings. What it holds depends on what you may do here:
    - **Search…**, to open the [search panel](/help/search-panel/) and jump to any <Noun />, tab, or element, drop something onto the canvas, or run a command. `⌘K` (Ctrl+K) opens it straight from the keyboard.

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  **What Tools holds.** Tools only lists what applies to this <Noun /> and to you:

- **Search…**: opens the [search panel](/help/search-panel/) to jump to any <Noun />, tab or element, drop something onto the canvas, or run a command. `⌘K` (Ctrl+K) opens it from the keyboard too.
- **Walkthroughs**: play a recorded [walkthrough](/help/collaboration/walkthroughs/), or, as an editor, record one.
- **History**: the <Noun />'s [version history](/help/tabs/version-history/) and its snapshots, on a <Noun /> stored on the server.
- **Webhooks**: tell other services when the <Noun /> changes, for someone who may manage them.

Signed out, **Walkthroughs**, **History** and **Webhooks** still show, with a badge, and ask you to sign in first. On an [Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) <Noun /> they ask you to save it to your account first.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **Tools.** **Tools** lists, under small headings, every tool you may use on this plan. Which rows you see depends on your role and on whether the plan is saved to an account:

- **Find**: **Search…** opens the [search panel](/help/search-panel/) to jump to any plan, tab or card, or to run a [command](/help/tips-and-tricks/command-palette/). `⌘K` or `⌘.` (Ctrl+K or Ctrl+. on Windows and Linux) opens it straight from the keyboard.
- **Automate**: **Automations…** ([Automations](/help/plan/automations/)), **Email to Board** ([Email to Board](/help/plan/email-to-board/)) and **Webhooks**.
- **Collaborate**: **Walkthroughs** ([Walkthroughs](/help/collaboration/walkthroughs/)), **Subscribe to Calendar** ([Calendar Feed](/help/plan/calendar-feed/)) and **Publish Roadmap** ([Public Roadmap](/help/plan/public-roadmap/)).
- **Versioning**: **History**, who changed what and when, with [Snapshots](/help/collaboration/snapshots/) in it.

Most tools open inside the popover, with a back bar at the top to return to the list, so you can hop from one to the next. **Search…** and **Automations…** close Tools and open their own window, which is what the ellipsis on their names says. Escape steps back a level, then closes Tools. A small amber dot on the button means an automation rule has paused itself.

On a phone, Tools (and Get Started) opens as a sheet across the foot of the screen instead of a popover.

**Tools that need an account or a synced plan.** A tool that needs you signed in, or needs the plan saved to <AppName />, still shows in Tools so you can find it. Small badges beside its name say what it needs first: **Sign In**, then **Sync Plan** for a plan kept Offline. Pressing it opens a short step in its place: **Sign In** takes you to sign in and brings you back with the tool open, and **Sync Plan** saves the plan to <AppName /> and carries straight on to the tool. Automations, Email to Board, Subscribe to Calendar, History and Webhooks work this way. A tool your role can't use stays out of the list.
