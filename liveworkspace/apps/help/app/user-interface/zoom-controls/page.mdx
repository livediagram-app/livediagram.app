## The controls

- **Zoom out** / **Zoom in**: step 10% at a time.

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  - **The percentage button**: shows the current level. **Click it to fit**: it frames the page
  nearest the middle of the screen, the quickest way to get your bearings after panning around.
  **Hover it** for a popover of preset levels (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, 125% and 150%, plus **Fit to
  screen**) and pick one to jump straight there (100% is true size). With the keyboard, `Enter` fits
  and the arrow keys open the presets.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  - **The percentage button**: shows the current level. **Click it to fit the whole canvas** in view
  at once, the quickest way to get your bearings after panning around. **Hover it** for a popover of
  preset levels (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, 125% and 150%, plus **Fit to screen**) and pick one to jump
  straight there (100% is true size).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  - **The percentage button**: shows the current level. **Click it to fit the page you are nearest**
  to the screen, the quickest way to get your bearings after panning around. **Hover it** for a
  popover of preset levels (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, 125% and 150%, plus **Fit to screen**) and pick one
  to jump straight there (100% is true size).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  - **The percentage button**: shows the current level. **Click it to go back to 100%**. **Hover
  it** for a popover of preset levels (50%, 75%, 100%, 125% and 150%) and pick one to jump straight
  there.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle">Zoom runs from 10% to 500%.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  On a [photo page](/help/pages/photo-pages/) you can zoom in further, far enough to see its single
  pixels.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  To fit everything on the tab at once, run **Fit to Screen** from the [command
  palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  With the [Isometric](/help/selection-modes/isometric-mode/) tool in hand, an orbit button joins
  the cluster: drag it to turn the view, or click it to reset the angle. On a Participant or Viewer
  link the cluster also carries a **Zen mode** button.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  A sheet zooms from 50% to 200%. The column letters, row numbers and cells grow and shrink
  together, while the toolbar and formula bar keep their size, and everything works as it does at
  100%. Zoom is your own view: it is never saved or shared, and the sheet opens at 100% next time.
</AppNote>

## From the keyboard and trackpad

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  | Keys | Does | | ------------------------ | --------------------------------- | | `⌘+` / `⌘-` |
  Zoom in / out | | `⌘0` | Reset the zoom to 100% | | `⇧1` | Frame the page nearest the middle | |
  `⌘` + scroll, or a pinch | Zoom in or out |
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  | Keys | Does | | ------------------------ | --------------------------------- | | `⌘+` / `⌘-` |
  Zoom in / out | | `⌘0` | Reset the zoom to 100% | | `⇧1` | Zoom to fit everything on the tab | |
  `⌘` + scroll, or a pinch | Zoom in or out |
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  - `⌘` `+` and `⌘` `-` (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) step 10% larger or smaller, and `⌘` `0` puts the
  sheet back to 100%. - Hold `⌘` (Ctrl) and scroll, or pinch on a trackpad, to zoom smoothly within
  the same range. - With the percentage button focused, the Up or Down arrow opens the preset
  levels.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments">
  `⌘` is Ctrl on Windows and Linux. A plain two-finger scroll pans rather than zooms, and so do the
  [Hand tool](/help/selection-modes/hand/), `Space` + drag, and a middle-button drag.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  `⌘` is Ctrl on Windows and Linux. A plain two-finger scroll pans rather than zooms, and so do
  `Space` + drag, a middle-button drag, and a one-finger drag on a touch screen.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  Zoom never changes the <Noun />, so it works on a view-only link too. See [Pan and
  Zoom](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/).
</AppNote>

## On a Phone

On a phone the zoom buttons and the percentage are dropped to save space: pinch to zoom, and a dedicated **Fit** button stands in for fit-to-screen.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">The **Fit** button puts the sheet back to 100%.</AppNote>

## In Zen Mode

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments,liveplanner">
  In [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) everything else hides but the zoom controls stay, joined by
  an **Exit zen mode** button.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">Any session tools in use stay too.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  On a Participant or Viewer link, a **Zen mode** button also sits at the end of the zoom controls,
  so you can enter it from there.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  <AppName /> has no zen mode: the zoom controls are always in the corner.
</AppNote>
