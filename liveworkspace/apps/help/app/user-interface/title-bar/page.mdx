<AppNote app="livediagram">

On a phone the menu button and the mode switch (its icon alone) move into a card of their own at the left of the strip.

**Top right.** The card at the top right holds, from left to right:

- **Copy**, for anyone who cannot share this canvas (see below).
- **Present**, while the canvas's [slide deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/) has a slide to show. It starts the presentation. Not on a phone. See [Presenting](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/).
- **Share**, for those who can share the canvas (see below).
- **Your account** (see below).

When the strip needs the room, the card drops its words and keeps its icons. On a phone it shows icons alone.

**Present.** While there are slides to present, a **Present** button joins the card on a computer, before **Share**. Hover it to see how many slides the deck has, and click it to start [presenting](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/). It reads **Starting** while the deck gets ready.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **The top left.** The **logo** opens the Explorer on the place that holds this plan (its folder, its team's library, Offline or Shared With Me), so you land beside it. On a narrower screen it shows the mark without the wordmark, and on a phone the logo is left off to make room.

Beside it, the **menu button** (three lines) opens the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) under it: your plans and folders, with links to the full Explorer, Help and more along its foot. Press it again to close it.

When a board or a Sheet fills its tab, or the tab is a Dashboard, its name rides in the same card, right after the menu button.

**Renaming the plan.** With no title bar, the plan's name is not shown above your work. To rename the plan, open the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) from the menu button and choose **Rename** on the plan's ⋯ menu. Names are capped at 60 characters. Only people who can change the plan's structure can rename it: its owner, a member of its team, and anyone on an Editor link.

</AppNote>

## Share or Copy

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments,liveplanner">
  The card holds one main action, and which one you get depends on whose <Noun /> it is.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">Which button you get depends on whose space it is.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  On a computer each button shows its name beside its icon; on a phone, or when the strip needs the
  room, it shows the icon alone.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  **Share** is for the owner, and for a member of the team a [team
  design](/help/collaboration/teams/team-shared-designs/) belongs to, opening it from the team
  rather than through a share link.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  **Share** is for the owner, and for members of the team whose library holds the <Noun />.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  **Share** is for the owner (and the members of the team whose library holds it).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **Share** is for the owner (and, on a team plan, the team's members).
</AppNote>

It opens the [Share dialog](/help/collaboration/sharing/), where you create and manage links. The button turns solid once the <Noun /> has a live link, so you can tell at a glance that it is shared. On an [Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) <Noun /> it asks you to save the <Noun /> to your account first, since there is nothing on a server to share yet.

<AppNote app="livehuddle">In the Share dialog you also choose who can see the space.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Hovering the solid button reads **Shared**. While a [Form](/help/plan/forms/) tab is open, **Share
  Form** takes Share's place: it publishes the form and copies its link.
</AppNote>

**Copy** is for everyone else who has the <Noun /> open: someone on a share link of any role. It makes a copy of the <Noun /> that belongs to you:

<Step n={1}>Click **Copy**. It reads **Copying** while the copy is made.</Step>

<Step n={2}>The editor opens your copy, named after the original with "(copy)" at the end.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Find it again later at the top level of My Work in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
</Step>

Your copy starts **Private**: no share links come with it, and nothing you do to it touches the original. If you opened the <Noun /> through a link to a single tab, the copy holds just that tab.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments,livehuddle,liveplanner">
  You do not need an account to copy; as a guest the copy is saved under your [guest
  identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  On a design from the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/), Copy is in the Community
  bar along the top instead.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Someone who opened a canvas from the
  [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/finding-community-documents/) does not get **Copy** here.
  A slim Community bar offers **Make a Copy** instead, which works the same way.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  Someone who opened a space from the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/finding-community-documents/) does not get **Copy** in this card. A slim bar under the top of the canvas offers **Make a Copy** instead, which works the same way.

On a phone the card meets the right edge of the screen and its buttons show their icons alone. Share and Copy wait while the welcome screen of a new space is open, and the whole card is hidden inside an [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/).

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  On a phone, and whenever the card would crowd the tab's own toolbar, its buttons show their icons
  without their names.
</AppNote>

## Your account

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  At the end of the card is your account. Signed in, it shows your picture (or your initials) and
  first name.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  Signed in, your account shows your picture or initials and your first name.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  At the end of the card is your account. Signed in, it shows your picture or initials.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  At the far right is your account. Signed in, it shows your initial.
</AppNote>

Click it for a menu with your name and email, **Account** (which opens [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) on your account details, without leaving the <Noun />) and **Sign Out**. Not signed in, it shows **Sign In** instead (see [Signing In](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/)).

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  **The design's name and who can see it.** The editor does not show the design's name: the menu card at the top left shows the open tab's name instead. To rename the design, use **Rename** on its **⋯** menu in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/). Names are capped at 60 characters.

Who can see a design (Private, Shared, Team, Public in the Community, or Offline) shows as a pill on the design in the Explorer, and the [Share dialog](/help/collaboration/sharing/) is where the owner changes it.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **Where the canvas name went.** The canvas's name is not shown above the canvas. The [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/) names each tab, and the canvas's own name shows in the Explorer and in your browser's tab. Rename a canvas from the Explorer.

All of these cards hide in [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/), and an [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) shows none of them.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  **The document's name and who can see it.** The editor itself does not show the document's name or
  a visibility pill. To rename a document, or see whether it is Private, Shared or in a team, use
  its row in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/). Whether it is shared also shows on the
  **Share** button, which turns solid once the document has a live link.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Where the title bar's parts went.**

- **The logo** sits in its own card at the top left. Click it to open the Explorer on the folder that holds this space.
- **The space's name** is no longer shown in the editor; the tab bar names the tabs. To rename the space, use **Rename** on its ⋯ menu in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).
- **Who can see the space** is in the Share dialog, behind **Share**.
- **Your role** is no longer shown: the link you opened the space with sets it.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **When the corners are hidden.** [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) hides both cards, and an
  [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) never shows them.
</AppNote>
