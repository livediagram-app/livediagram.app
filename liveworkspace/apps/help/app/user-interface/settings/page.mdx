**Settings** opens from the **Settings** button (the sliders icon) in the [quick controls](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/) at the end of the bottom bar, in the editor and in the Explorer. It holds the preferences that are about _you_ rather than about any particular <Noun />, which is why there is one set of them and not one per <Noun />.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  Signed in, the dialog's header has a **
  <AppName />
  ** / **All** switch. **All** shows every setting your liveworkspace account keeps, in one list of
  categories; **
  <AppName />
  ** shows only the ones that apply to <AppName />. Under **All**, to the left of each setting are
  the logos of the apps it applies to, or the liveworkspace logo when it applies to every app, so
  you can see when changing it here changes it in another app too; the **
  <AppName />

  under three headings: **Account** (**Profile**, with **Name and Colour**, **Time Zone**, **Sign-in
  Methods**, **Where You’re Signed In**, **Sign Out** and **Cloud Sync**, then **Notifications**,
  **Privacy**, **Accessibility** and **Your Data**), **Apps** (the app's own categories) and
  **Developers** (**API Tokens**). A guest sees this app's settings only.
</AppNote>

The dialog lists its categories down the left, with a **Search settings** field above them, and shows one category at a time on the right. Type into the search to find a setting by name or by a word it is about ("transparency" finds **Panel Opacity**). On a phone, the list and the category take turns: tap a category to open it, and go back for the list.

## Keyboard

- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: the switch that turns the editor's keyboard shortcuts on and off. Off means all of them, chords like undo included, not just the single-letter keys. Saved on this device only (see below).
- **All Shortcuts**: the full shortcut reference, in collapsible groups. The **Keyboard Shortcuts** command in [search](/help/search-panel/) opens Settings straight on this category. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Accessibility

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle">

## Getting Started

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  The Pages tour has one tour for each kind of page: **Pages Tours** switches them all at once, with **Wireframe Tour**, **Logo Tour** and **Photo Tour** beneath it. Tours never start on their own: you start one from **Get Started** in the bar at the bottom of the editor, just left of **Tools**, which lists the tours on offer. A switch here turns itself off once you've started, finished or skipped that tour. Switch one back on and close Settings while you're in Pages, and the tour starts straight away. See [The Pages Tour](/help/pages/pages-tour/).

Below them, **Show Me** is closed until you open it, and says how many are on. Some elements, such as the [Sankey Diagram](/help/palette/concept-graphics/sankey-diagram/), have a tour of their own: select one and **Get Started** offers its tour. **Show Me on Every Element** switches them all; beneath it, one switch per element (such as **Sankey Diagram**, **Timeline** and **Stack**) shows whether you've taken that one. Once you've taken one, it leaves the button until you switch it back on here.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Watch It Built has a section per mode, **Watch It Built in Diagram**, **Watch It Built in Draw** and **Watch It Built in Workshop**, each closed until you open it and saying how many of its topics are on. Inside is the mode's switch over all its topics, then a switch per topic. Tours never start on their own: each one starts from the **Get Started** button at the start of the controls at the right of the bottom bar, just left of **Tools**. A topic's switch turns itself off once you have watched it, and a mode's switch once you have watched all its topics. Switch one back on and close Settings to watch again: the topic list opens straight away if you are in that mode, or the button is there when you get to it. See [Watch It Built](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/), [Watch It Built in Draw](/help/canvas/draw-mode/draw-tour/) and [Watch It Built in Workshop](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/workshop-tour/).

Some elements, such as the [Sankey Diagram](/help/palette/build/sankey-diagram/), have a tour of their own, on the same **Get Started** button while one is selected. **Show Me on Every Element** switches them all; beneath it, one switch per element that has a tour (**Sankey Diagram**, **Layer Stack**, **Pyramid**, **Funnel**, **Cycle**, **Hub and Spoke**, **Onion**, **Chevrons**, **Timeline**, **Mind Map**, **Flowchart**, **Org Chart**, **Sequence**, **Chart**, **Stack**, **Entity**, **Lane**, **Boundary** and **Diagram Key**) shows whether you have taken that one.
