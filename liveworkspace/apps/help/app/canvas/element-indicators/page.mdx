<ArticleLayout path="canvas/element-indicators" title="Element Indicators" description="The quiet marks that tell you an element carries something you can't otherwise see, and how to change or hide them." categoryTitle="Canvas" categorySlug="canvas">

Some of what an element carries is invisible on the <Noun />: an [action](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/) waiting on someone, a [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) thread. **Element indicators** are the small icons that give these away. They read like metadata printed on a card: quiet until you look for them, and one click opens whatever they point to.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  An element can also carry a [link](/help/canvas/links/) it jumps to and a
  [note](/help/canvas/notes/) behind it, and these get indicators too.
</AppNote>

## What They Show

An element shows an indicator for each of these it has, always in this order, starting with its link and note where the app has them:

- **Action**: the element has an open assigned action. Hovering it reads **Assigned to** and the person's name. A completed action stops showing.
- **Comments**: the element has unresolved comments, with the count beside the icon. Resolving the last thread clears it.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  The first two, **Link** and **Note**, come before Action. **Link**: the element links to another
  tab, another <Noun /> or a web address. Hovering it shows a card naming the destination. **Note**:
  the element has a note.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  On a wireframe, a link to another screen (or Back) shows a screen icon instead, with the word
  **Screen** in the Footer style; a screen link whose screen is gone shows in red as a **Broken
  link**.
</AppNote>

Comments come last because they are the most frequent and the most urgent, and the far end is the first place your eye lands.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  A few elements show these things on their own face instead, so they carry no indicator for them:
  an [annotation](/help/canvas/annotations/)'s marker is its note, a link card is its link, a
  comment panel shows its own comments, and an action panel its own action. A **mind map root** can
  also carry two commands, **Edit Outline** and **Tidy Map**. They work a little differently; see
  [Mind Map Commands](#while-an-element-is-selected) below.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  A few elements show these things on their own face instead, so they carry no indicator for them:
  an [annotation](/help/canvas/annotations/)'s marker is its note, and a link card's link is its
  face.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  A margin note on an article page shows its comments or its action on its own face, so it carries
  no indicators at all.
</AppNote>

## Choosing a Style

Open [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), go to **Editor** and pick an option for **Element Indicators**. It is your own preference, so it changes what you see and nobody else's view.

- **Top** (the default) prints the icons inside the element along its top, in the element's own text colour at half strength, so they suit any fill, theme or style.
- **Footer** puts a row along the inside of the element's bottom edge, the way a task card shows its details, with the assignee's initials on a small coloured disc beside **Action**, then the comment count. When the words don't fit, the row drops them and keeps the icons, disc and count.
- **Off** draws no indicators at all. Everything they open is still in the element's menu (see [With Indicators Off](#with-indicators-off)).

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  In Footer, **Link** and **Note** each get a word beside their icon too.
</AppNote>

## Where They Sit

Indicators always sit **inside** the element's outline, never across it:

- On a box-like element (a rectangle, rounded rectangle, pill, card or panel), Top sits at the **top-right**.
- On a round or pointed shape (a circle, diamond, hexagon, cloud, triangle, trapezoid, star or actor), Top is **centred** along the top, since there is no corner for it.
- Footer starts at the bottom-left of a box, and is centred on the same round or pointed shapes.

<AppNote app="livediagram">A mind node counts as a box-like element too.</AppNote>

They look for a spot clear of the element's label and icon first. When there isn't one, the label shifts just far enough to make room (down under Top, up over Footer), and a label set to scale to fit shrinks a little instead.

On an element too small, thin or spiky to hold them (a small pill, a progress bar, a star, an actor, a small diamond), the icons gather in a small **chip on the element's edge** instead, half in and half out, drawn in the element's own fill.

## Using Them

Each indicator is a button. Click it to:

- **Action**: open the action, showing who it is assigned to.
- **Comments**: open the comment thread.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  **Link** follows the link, to the other tab, the other <Noun />, or the web page in a new tab.
  **Note** opens the note.
</AppNote>

Hovering the element brings its indicators up a little, and the one under the pointer to full strength. From the keyboard, each indicator is reachable with **Tab** and shows a focus ring.

## While an Element is Selected

Selecting an element brings its own handles and **+** buttons onto the same edges, so its indicators **shrink and fade away** while it is selected and come back when you deselect it. Nothing else moves: the label stays exactly where it was. To reach the action or comments on a selected element, deselect it or use its menu.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  Its toolbar comes onto the same edges too, so a link or note is reachable only by deselecting or
  from the menu.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **Mind Map Commands.** A mind map root with at least one child carries **Edit Outline** (edit the
  whole map as a list) and **Tidy Map** (lay the map out neatly again). These are things you **do**,
  not things the root **has**, so they stay hidden until you **hover** the root. Their space is kept
  free so nothing shifts when they appear. In Top they lead the cluster, and in Footer they end the
  row. They aren't shown while the root is selected, on a root with no children, on a [locked
  tab](/help/tabs/locking-tabs/), on a hidden or locked active [layer](/help/canvas/layers/), or to
  a Viewer or Participant. Both are always in the root's **Mind Map** menu section too, which is the
  way in from the keyboard or on a touch screen. See [Edit
  Outline](/help/palette/mind-maps/edit-outline/).
</AppNote>

## With Indicators Off

Turning indicators **Off** hides them on every element. Nothing becomes unreachable:

- **Assign Action** and **Add Comment** sit under **Resources**, the last category of the element's menu; every open thread and action is also listed in the **Collaborate** panel.

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Under **Resources**, a row the element already has is tinted and reads **View Action** or **View
  Comments**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  Links and notes are under **Resources** too, and a row the element already has is filled in and
  marked **Added**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  A mind map root's commands no longer appear on hover; **Edit Outline** and **Tidy Map** are in the
  root's **Mind Map** section.
</AppNote>

## When They Don't Show

- **Zoomed far out**: below 40% zoom, indicators hide, along with the padlock on locked elements and the avatars of collaborators who have an element selected. An overview is for shape, not detail. See [Pan and Zoom](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/).
- **Exports**: indicators are editor chrome, so PNG and SVG exports and <Noun /> thumbnails never include them.
- **Viewers and Participants** still see actions and comments, and can open each one to read it.
- **Embeds**: a <Noun /> [embedded](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) in another page shows no indicators at all, whether the embed is for viewing or editing and whatever style you chose. Open it in <AppName /> to see them.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **Community**: a visitor viewing a canvas in the
  [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/) sees no comment counts, since there is no
  comments panel for them to open. The other indicators still show.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  Viewers and Participants also still see links and notes, and can open each one to read it. Viewers
  and Participants get no mind map commands.
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) hides the editor's panels and bars, but indicators are part of
  the elements, so they stay. Set **Element Indicators** to **Off** too for the cleanest possible
  <Noun />.
</Tip>

## On a Phone or Tablet

Indicators work the same on a touch screen: tap one to open what it points to. See [Mobile](/help/supported-devices/mobile/).

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  There is no pointer to hover with, so a mind map root's commands don't appear; long-press the root
  and use its **Mind Map** section instead.
</AppNote>

<Note>
  Element indicators are separate from the other marks an element can wear. The padlock on a [locked
  element](/help/canvas/locking/) and the avatars of collaborators who have it selected are not
  affected by this setting.
</Note>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The tally on an element during a [vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/) is not affected
  by this setting either.
</AppNote>
