The canvas is effectively infinite, so nothing on it is ever cramped. Panning moves your view across the surface, and zooming changes how much of it you see at once. There are several ways to do each, so you can use whichever suits your device.

## Panning

Panning slides the canvas under your view without moving any elements.

- **Two fingers on a trackpad, or the scroll wheel.** Scrolling over the canvas pans it, in any direction.
- **Middle-click drag.** Hold the middle mouse button and drag to pan from any tool, even over elements, so you never have to stop editing to move around. If you would rather keep the middle button for your browser, turn off **Middle-Mouse Pan** in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) under **Editor**.
- **Hold Space.** Holding Space and dragging pans while keeping your current tool, which is handy mid-edit.
- **Touch.** Pinch with two fingers to zoom.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  **Hand tool.** Pick the [Hand tool](/help/selection-modes/hand/) from the palette's canvas tool
  menu (shortcut `H`), then drag on the canvas to scroll around. It is the tool a phone starts on.
  Drag with the Hand tool to pan on a touch screen.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">Drag one finger across empty canvas to pan on a touch screen.</AppNote>

When you pan, background patterns track the pan offset and tile seamlessly, so the texture stays continuous however far you travel.

## Zooming

The view zooms from 10% up to 500%, around your pointer when you use a gesture.

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  On a photo page it can zoom in further, until each pixel of the photo is large enough to work on.
</AppNote>

- **[Zoom controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/).** The bar in the bottom-right corner has **Zoom out** and **Zoom in** buttons (10% at a time) either side of the current percentage. Hover the percentage for a menu of fixed levels from 25% to 150%, or click it to fit your work to the screen. On a phone the step buttons give way to a **Fit** button, and you pinch to zoom.
- **Gestures.** Pinch on a trackpad or touch screen, or hold `Cmd` / `Ctrl` while you scroll.
- **Keyboard.** `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `+` zooms in, `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `-` zooms out, `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `0` resets to 100%, and Shift + `1` zooms to fit.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livehuddle">
  The percentage fits everything on the tab to the screen.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  The percentage, like Shift + `1`, fits the page nearest the middle of your view to the screen.
</AppNote>

Zooming in lets you place and label small elements precisely; zooming out gives you the whole canvas at a glance, which is useful when tidying up a large layout or before presenting.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  On a big board, the [Map](/help/user-interface/minimap/) shows where you are and lets you jump
  around.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  On a tab of many pages, [Contents](/help/pages/working-with-pages/#contents) lists every page so
  you can jump straight to one.
</AppNote>

## Pointing Things Out

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  When you are walking others through the canvas, two canvas tools help direct attention without changing the layout. Both live in the canvas tool menu.

- **[Laser](/help/selection-modes/laser/)** (shortcut `K`) emits a glowing trail that follows your pointer and fades after about a second, shown live to others. While it is on, the **Laser** panel sets the pen's **Width**, **Colour**, **Trail** (how long it lasts) and **Effect**, and everyone sees the pen as you set it. With a mouse, a click-drag still pans.
- **[Spotlight](/help/selection-modes/spotlight/)** dims the whole canvas except a pool of light around your cursor, a local view aid that draws the eye to one area. Left-click grows the light, right-click shrinks it. While it is on, the **Spotlight** panel sets its **Size**, **Dim**, **Edge** and **Shape** (**Circle**, or **Wide** to light a row). It is desktop-only, and only you see it.

Spotlight never changes the canvas: while it is active the canvas ignores clicks, so nothing can be selected or moved by accident. You can still pan with a middle-click drag, Space, or a trackpad.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  When you are walking others through the canvas, [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/)
  lets you walk up to the thing you are talking about, and everyone sees your character get there.
  To bring the whole room to where you are looking, ask them to [follow
  you](/help/canvas/follow-along/).
</AppNote>
