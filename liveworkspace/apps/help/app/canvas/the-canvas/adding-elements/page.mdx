Every element on the canvas starts from the palette. The palette organises its contents into categories so it stays compact however much it holds, and adding an element is usually a single tap.

## Picking a Category

The palette is the strip across the top of the canvas. Its categories sit along it by name: rest the pointer on one, or click it, and its elements open in a panel underneath, in named groups, with a line on what each one does.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  In Diagram mode the categories are:

- **Shapes**: the common nodes, such as square, circle, diamond, cylinder, parallelogram, hexagon, canvas and stadium.
- **Write**: the words, text, sticky notes, pages, annotations and pointer notes, then comments and action cards.
- **Draw**: what you make with a stroke or a drag, the pencil, shape pen, highlighter, polygon, arrows and lines.
- **Diagrams**: the structures that grow as you type, such as mind nodes, org charts, flowcharts, sequence diagrams, tables and entities.
- **Concepts**: ideas laid out as one element, such as a pyramid, a funnel, a cycle, a timeline and the Sankey.
- **Layout**: what a diagram is arranged in and labelled with, such as lanes, frames, stacks, boundaries, a diagram key, cards, badges and step numbers, then its pictures: an uploaded image or a circular avatar.
- **Icons**: a searchable set of single-colour icons, its groups down the side, that you can drop on the canvas or onto a shape.
- **Tech**: full-colour cloud and infrastructure marks for system-architecture diagrams.

Other modes arrange their own set. [Workshop mode](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/) adds **Ask**, **Session** and **Data**, and gives **Icons** a Stickers side. Your saved shapes, and those of any [shape library](/help/explorer/shape-libraries/) you have imported, are behind the **Library** button beside the strip's **Search**. A Participant on a shared link gets one category instead, **Participate**, with sticky notes, text and an image to add to the board.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  On a blank page the categories are:

- **Popular**: twelve elements a page is most often built from, each also in one of the categories below.
- **Shapes**: the common shapes, such as square, circle, diamond, cylinder, parallelogram, hexagon and speech bubble.
- **Write**: the words, text, sticky notes and pointer notes.
- **Concepts**: ideas laid out as one element, such as a pyramid, a funnel and a cycle, then the Timeline and the Sankey.
- **Components**: themed blocks such as the Hero, Header, Banner and Stack, and the **Devices** row of browser, laptop, phone and other frames for showing a screen, then the Card, Badge, Step, Callout, Stat and Process, then the Code Block and the Checklist.
- **Icons**: a searchable set of single-colour icons, its groups down the side, that you can drop on the canvas or onto a shape. Its **Icons | Stickers** switch turns it to the full-colour stickers that decorate a page, and its search finds both.
- **Media**: an uploaded image and the embeds.
- **Data**: charts and meters, then the Table.

A wireframe and a logo page each arrange their own set, offering what that kind of page is made of, and a photo page holds the photo tools: see [Working with Pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/). A Participant on a shared link gets one category instead, **Participate**, with sticky notes, text and an image to add to the page.

</AppNote>

- **Icons**: a searchable set of single-colour icons, its groups down the side, that you can drop on the canvas or onto a shape. Its **Icons | Stickers** switch turns it to the full-colour stickers that decorate a page, and its search finds both.

## Dropping or Drawing an Element

Almost every tile arms a combined gesture when you click it.

<Step n={1}>
  Click a tile in the palette. It stays pressed to show it is armed, and a banner along the top of
  the canvas says what comes next ("Click to drop or drag to draw" and the element's name, "Tap" on
  a phone), with a Cancel button.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Tap an empty spot on the canvas to drop the element at its natural size on that point.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Or, instead of a single tap, drag out a box and release to create the element at that size. Both
  corners snap to nearby elements as you drag.
</Step>

While a tile is armed you can even start the drag on top of another element: the new one is drawn over it rather than selecting it. Press Escape (or Cancel on the banner) to disarm a tile without adding anything. Text lands ready for typing; everything else lands selected, ready to move or restyle. An **Image** lands as an empty placeholder: press its **Select Image** button when you are ready to add the picture.

If a boxed element is already selected when you tap a tile, the new one inherits its width and height (a drag sets its own size), so you can chain together similarly sized elements quickly.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  Circles and diamonds are an exception: they are inherently square, so they snap back to a square
  using the larger inherited dimension.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">A selected frame or lane never passes its size on.</AppNote>

You can also **drag a tile straight from the palette onto the canvas** instead of tapping. As you drag, a translucent preview of the element follows your cursor at its real size, so you can see exactly where, and how big, it will land before you let go.

## Adding from the Keyboard

Single keys arm the most common elements without opening the palette: `T` for text and `N` for a sticky note. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) for the full list. With one labelled element selected, a letter key types into its label instead. You can also **double-click empty canvas** to drop a text element and start typing straight away.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  More keys: `R` for a rectangle, `O` for an oval, `D` for a diamond, `C` for a cylinder, `G` for a
  parallelogram, `A` for an arrow and `9` for an image.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">`F` adds a frame.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livehuddle">
  The keys add nothing for a Participant or a Viewer.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">[Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/) has keys of its own.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  A Participant's keys add only what its **Participate** palette holds (`N`, `T` and `9`), and a
  Viewer's add nothing.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **Annotations land in the middle.** An [annotation](/help/canvas/annotations/) is the one element
  without the draw gesture. It is a fixed-size marker, so there is no size to choose: it lands at
  the centre of the visible canvas, already selected, so you can drag it where you want.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  Icons can be added to a shape rather than as standalone elements: drag an icon tile onto a shape,
  or click an icon while a shape is selected, and it attaches beside that shape's label.
</AppNote>
