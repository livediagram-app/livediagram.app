## The Palette

The **Palette** is a strip pinned to the top centre of the canvas, holding everything you add to it. It starts with a tool at its left, then the palette's categories by name, each opening a panel of its elements as you rest on it, and ends with **Search** for finding any element by name. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) for the details.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  The strip starts with the canvas tool (**Select**, **Hand**, **Laser** and the other [selection
  modes](/help/selection-modes/)).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">Beside Search, the **Library** button holds your saved shapes.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The strip starts with the [Edit | Walk toggle](/help/selection-modes/select/) (build the board, or
  walk it as your character). Switch to **Walk** and the strip gives way to the avatar palette,
  while Undo, Redo and the **Theme & canvas** button step aside until you switch back to **Edit**.
</AppNote>

In the top-left corner, beside the strip, the **menu button** opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/), with the tab's name beside it on a computer.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  A tab's mode (**Diagram**, **Draw** or **Workshop**) is set with the mode switch beside the menu
  button, or on an empty tab's start: see [Editor
  Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/#choosing-a-mode).
</AppNote>

## Adding Elements

Most elements are added straight from the palette, whose categories sit along the strip by name. Rest on one, or click it, and its elements open in a panel underneath. Clicking an element arms a combined gesture: a tap drops the element at your pointer, and a drag sizes it as you go. Single-key shortcuts skip the palette altogether, such as `T` for text and `N` for a sticky note. See [Adding Elements](/help/canvas/the-canvas/adding-elements/) for the full walkthrough.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  `R` is a single-key shortcut for a rectangle too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  In Diagram mode they are **Shapes**, **Write** (text, notes and comments), **Draw** (the pens and
  arrows), **Diagrams** (mind nodes, flowcharts, tables), **Concepts** (pyramids, funnels, cycles
  and the Sankey), **Layout** (lanes, frames, cards, and images), **Icons** and **Tech**. Each mode
  offers its own set: **Stickers** (the Icons category's Stickers side), **Data** (charts) and
  **Session** belong to [Workshop mode](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/), for example.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  On a blank page they are **Popular**, **Shapes**, **Write** (text and notes), **Concepts**
  (concept graphics, the Timeline and the Sankey), **Components** (ready-made blocks such as the
  Hero and the Card, and the device frames), **Icons** (icons and stickers), **Media** and **Data**
  (charts, meters and the Table). A wireframe and a logo page each arrange their own set, and a
  photo page holds the photo tools: see [Working with Pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  On a design page they are **Shapes**, **Write** (text and notes), **Concepts** (concept graphics,

</AppNote>

## Moving Around

The canvas is effectively infinite, so you are never boxed in. Hold Space and drag, use a middle-click drag or two fingers on a trackpad to pan, and `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + scroll, a pinch, or the zoom controls in the bottom-right corner to scale the view. The [Panning and Zooming](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/) article covers every way to navigate.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">The Hand tool pans too.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">On a touch screen, drag one finger to pan.</AppNote>

Each tab has its own backdrop and theme. The **Tab Style** dialog is where you can [change the canvas background](/help/canvas/the-canvas/changing-the-background/) or [pick a theme](/help/canvas/themes/).

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livehuddle">
  The **Theme & canvas** button beside the zoom controls (named **Style** on a wide screen) opens
  it.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  The **Style** button on a page's title bar (top right of every page) opens it.
</AppNote>
