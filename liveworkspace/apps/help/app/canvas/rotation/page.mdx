## Snap to a Preset Angle

Right-click an element and open the **Rotation** category. It offers eight preset angles, every 45° around the circle: **0°, 45°, 90°, 135°, 180°, 225°, 270°,** and **315°**. Each tile shows the orientation, and with a mouse, hovering a tile previews it on the element before you click. Choosing one snaps the element to that exact angle, which keeps a row of tilted elements perfectly consistent. Picking **0°** sets it back upright. Angles are measured clockwise.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livehuddle">
  You can also press the **⋯** button on the element's selection toolbar.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">On a touch screen, use a long press.</AppNote>

<Figure caption="The Rotation category: eight 45° tiles, with 45° chosen.">
  <RotationCategory />
</Figure>

Select several elements and the multi-selection menu has the same **Rotation** category, turning every one of them that can rotate to the angle you pick.

## Rotate from the Search Palette

With a single element selected, open the search palette (**Cmd+K** or **Cmd+.**, **Ctrl** on Windows and Linux) and type **rotate** for quick **Rotate 90°**, **Rotate 180°**, **Rotate 270°**, and **Reset Rotation** actions: the keyboard-only route to the same result, without opening the menu. They set the angle rather than add to it, so **Rotate 90°** twice still leaves the element at 90°.

<Note>
  Rotation uses these fixed 45° steps rather than a free-drag handle, so tilted elements stay
  consistent with one another and any angle is easy to reproduce. Each change is one undo step.
</Note>

## Good to Know

- Rotation turns an element about its centre, and is a per-element property, so different elements on the same tab can sit at different angles. Copy, paste and duplicate keep the angle.
- Text, sticky notes and images can be rotated.
- A rotated element keeps its resize handles, turned with it. Dragging one grows the element along its own tilted sides while the opposite corner or edge stays put. It doesn't snap to other elements while it is tilted.
  <AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
    Shapes, freehand strokes and tables can be rotated too. Arrows have no Rotation category: move
    their ends instead, and arrows attached to a rotated element stay on its outline as it turns.
  </AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments">Videos and link cards can be rotated as well.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">So can paths.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  Rooms and the Space's other pieces can be rotated, apart from furniture. **Furniture turns a
  quarter at a time, if at all.** Chairs, desks, sofas, tables and the other pieces drawn from the
  room's camera always stand upright, so they have no Rotation category. A piece with a front to
  face (a chair, a desk, a sofa, a bookshelf, a coffee machine and the like) turns instead: select
  one and press **Rotate Left** or **Rotate Right** on its selection toolbar (the search palette has
  the same two actions) to turn it to face another way. Select several and the same buttons turn
  each piece about its own centre. A piece with no front, such as a plant, has no turn at all.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  [Annotation](/help/canvas/annotations/) markers don't rotate: they are round note markers of a
  fixed size, so they have no Rotation category or **Rotate** actions, and a multi-selection's
  Rotation leaves them upright. [Pointer notes](/help/palette/pointer-notes/) don't rotate either:
  their line is pinned to a point, and turning the note would swing it away. They stay upright in
  the same way.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  [Pointer notes](/help/palette/pointer-notes/) don't rotate: their line is pinned to a point, and
  turning the note would swing it away. Nor do an article's margin notes. They have no Rotation
  category or **Rotate** actions, and a multi-selection's Rotation leaves them upright.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  A piece that moved to livehuddle with Avatar mode (a chair, a fence, a virtual office piece) shows
  on an older board as a **Moved to livehuddle** card, and has no Rotation category. Notes on an
  [event storming board](/help/canvas/event-storming-boards/) have no Rotation category: their tilt
  is part of the notation.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  The **Bounce** and **Wobble** [animations](/help/canvas/animations/) compose with an element's
  rotation, so a tilted shape still bobs or wiggles around its angle.
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  Use a preset angle when you want several elements to share the exact same tilt; reach for the
  search palette's **Rotate** actions when your hands are already on the keyboard.
</Tip>
