A blank start is freeing, but sometimes you would rather start with structure already in place. Templates give you a ready-made <Noun /> as a starting point, so you can begin from something shaped roughly like what you need and adapt it, instead of building everything from nothing.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">

## Starting from a Template

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  When you [create a new canvas](/new), it is made there and then, saved where new canvases go (see [Default Folders](/help/explorer/folders/default-folders/)), and asks nothing first. The empty tab opens on a start, where you choose what you are making (Diagram, Draw or Workshop): **Start Diagramming**, **Start Drawing** or **Start a Workshop**: its **Recommended** templates first, then every template of that mode in groups beside them, with a search. Click a template and the tab fills with it; from there it is an ordinary canvas, to move, edit, add to and delete from as on any canvas.

Every new tab, and every tab you empty, offers the same start. Close it with its **×** to begin from a blank canvas; while the tab is still empty, **Show Templates** in the strip under the palette brings it back. Picked the wrong mode? The mode switch at the top of the start's list changes it while the tab is empty. In Draw the start is **Start Drawing**, with the pen already in hand, and since Draw has a dock rather than the palette, **Show Templates** sits in a pill at the top of the canvas.

The search at the top of a start looks in every mode, not just the tab's own. Type "sailboat" on Start Drawing and the Sailboat retrospective turns up under **In Other Modes**, in a Workshop block, its card saying **Opens in Workshop**: click it and the tab switches to Workshop with the template on it. Each mode shows up to six matches, with **Show All** for the rest, and pressing the down arrow in the search box takes you to the first card it found.

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  When you [create a new design](/new), it is made there and then, saved where new designs go (see [Default Folders](/help/explorer/folders/default-folders/)), and its empty tab opens on **Start Making Pages**: its **Recommended** templates first, then **Blank Pages**, then every template grouped by what it is for (**Branding**, **Shopping**, **Websites**, **Work and Productivity** and the rest), with a search. Click a template and the tab fills with its pages; from there it is an ordinary design, to move, edit, add to and delete from as on any tab.

Every new tab, and every tab you empty, offers the same start. Close it with its **×** to begin from a blank page; while the tab is still empty, **Show Templates** in the strip under the palette brings it back.

To see one kind of page only, use **All Pages** at the top of the categories and choose **Logo**, **Wireframe** or **Photo**. Choose **Wireframe** and only the templates for app and website screens are left, in every category and in the search; a category with none of that kind steps out of the list. Community designs show only under **All Pages**. The start opens on **All Pages** every time.

The search at the top of the start, **Find a template**, finds a template by its name, its description, its category, or the everyday words people use for it: try "logo", "brand", "dashboard", "travel" or "checkout". Every word you type has to match, and pressing the down arrow in the search box takes you to the first card it found.

**From the Community.**

After a category's templates, **Start Making Pages** can show up to three designs people shared to the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/) under that category, marked **Community**: the most liked of the past month, each with at least five likes in that time, with who made it beneath. Click one and you get your own copy of that design to start from, and it opens; the shared design never changes. **Recommended** and **Blank Pages** show templates only, the counts beside each category count only its templates, and the search finds a Community design by the words of its title.

</AppNote>

**Find a template** finds a template by its name, its description, its category, or the everyday words people use for it: try "1:1", "retro", "erd", "vsm", "pestel" or "onboarding". Every word you type has to match.

Three blanks lead the **Popular** shelf, one for each way of working, and sit apart from the categories since starting from nothing is not a kind of diagram: **Blank Diagram** (an empty canvas), **Blank Whiteboard** (a board in [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/) for free drawing without distractions) and **Blank Workshop** (an empty tab in [Workshop mode](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/), to run a retro, town hall or workshop with timers, votes and polls to hand). After the blanks, Popular shows a few of the templates most people start from; choose a mode and Popular changes to that mode's favourites, its blank first.

**Finding templates for a mode.**

Every template opens in one mode: **Diagram**, **Draw** or **Workshop**, and each mode's start lists its own. To browse another mode's, switch the tab's mode with the switch at the top of the start's list while the tab is empty, or just search: a template from another mode shows which mode it opens in.

- **Draw** templates are drawn by hand, ready to draw over: **Sketchnote**, **Rich Picture**, **Comic Strip**, **Doodle Warm-Up**, **Paper Prototype**, **Journey Doodle**, **Pre-Mortem** and **Idea Garden**.
- **Workshop** templates are sessions to run with your team: **Blank Workshop**, **Retrospective**, **Start, Stop, Continue**, **Mad, Sad, Glad**, **4Ls Retrospective**, **Sailboat Retrospective**, **Town Hall Q&A**, **Lean Coffee**, **Crazy 8s**, the quizzes and games (**Pub Quiz**, **Icebreaker Questions**, **Two Truths and a Lie**, **Would You Rather** and **Find Someone Who Bingo**), **Meeting Agenda**, **Workshop Board**, **Brainstorm Board**, the boards that come with a dot vote, **Affinity Map**, **Prioritization Matrix** and **Risk Matrix**, the boards a team fills in together in a session (**Incident Postmortem**, **Objectives Planner**, **Roadmap**, **Sprint Planning**, **RACI Matrix**, **User Story Map**, **Business Model Canvas**, **Stakeholder Map**, **Value Proposition Canvas**, **Opportunity Solution Tree**, **User Persona** and **Interview Synthesis**), and the sticky-note boards a group fills in together, such as **SWOT**, **PESTLE Analysis**, **KWL Chart**, **Empathy Map** and **User Journey**. See [Workshop Mode](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/).
- Every other template is a **Diagram** template.

Each one arrives as real elements rather than a picture: a Gantt chart's bars are shapes you drag, a mind map's branches are nodes you can rearrange. Nothing in a template is locked or special.

**What a template tells you.**

Every template but the blanks carries a [note](/help/canvas/notes/) on its title saying how to use it: a Workshop template's note is a run sheet for the facilitator, step by step with timings; a template you present (a roadmap, a pyramid, an objectives tree) has a talk track in the order to tell it; and a diagram's says how to read it and how to grow it. Open the note from the indicator on the title.

Workshop templates come ready to run. Their timer and vote are on the board, each beside a line saying when to press it, under **Run the session** to the right of the board when the board itself has no room for them; press one and it starts for everyone (see [Session Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/)). Diagrams that show something moving, such as a request through an architecture or data through a data-flow diagram, animate their lines in the direction it travels.

</AppNote>

## Changing the Theme

A new <Noun /> starts on the Default theme, and a template picked on a start takes the tab's current theme. Once you are on the canvas, open the **Tab Style** dialog and try a different [theme](/help/canvas/themes/): it recolours the whole template at once.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livehuddle">
  Click the **Theme & Canvas** button in the bottom-right corner (named **Style** on a wide screen)
  to open it.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">Click the **Style** button on a page's title bar to open it.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  **Clearing the example content.** Some templates arrive filled in with examples that show how they are used. While the tab still holds them, a bar under the palette says **Example content shows how this works.** with two buttons:

- **Clear Example Content** empties the example notes, checklist rows and ticks, table cells and chart figures, and keeps the rest: every element stays where it is and looks as it did, with its headings and titles.
- **Keep It** keeps the examples as your own.

Either one is a single step, so **Undo** brings the examples back. Only an Editor sees the bar.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The examples are things like the Eisenhower Matrix's notes in each quadrant, a retro's notes and

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">

</AppNote>

## Making It Your Own

A template is just a head start, not a constraint. Everything in it is fully editable: rename and recolour the elements, restyle the whole thing with a different theme, or strip out the parts you do not need. Because the <Noun /> works the same as any other, you can also add [tabs](/help/tabs/using-tabs/), invite others, and share it once you have shaped it into what you want.

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  You can also add rooms and furniture from the palette's **Rooms**, **Seats** and **Perks**
  categories, and move the walls.
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  Templates pair naturally with themes: pick a template that fits your content, then try a few
  themes from the Tab Style dialog to find the look you want. Double-click a theme to apply it and
  close the dialog in one go.
</Tip>
