## Selecting More Than One

The quickest way to grab several elements is the marquee: drag across an empty patch of canvas and a selection rectangle picks up every element that sits entirely inside it. You can also build a selection click by click with Shift, or press `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `A` to select everything on the tab. Either way, the selection becomes a single thing you can move, copy or restyle in one go. See [Marquee and Multi-Select](/help/canvas/selecting-and-grouping/multi-select/) for every way to gather a selection.

## Copying a Look

Once you have styled one element just right, the [format painter](/help/selection-modes/format-painter/) copies that look onto others in a couple of clicks, so a whole <Noun /> can share one consistent style without re-setting each colour and border by hand.
