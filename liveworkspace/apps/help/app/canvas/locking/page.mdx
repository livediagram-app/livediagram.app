## Locking and Unlocking

Select an element and press `Cmd+Shift+L` (`Ctrl+Shift+L` on Windows and Linux). Locking is a normal edit: it syncs to collaborators and **Undo** reverses it.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  You can also click the **Lock** button in the element's floating toolbar. The button reads **Unlock** while it's locked, so the same control toggles it back, and so does the shortcut. Select several elements first and the button locks or unlocks the whole selection at once.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  You can also open the search palette (`Cmd+K`) and run **Lock / Unlock Selection**; run either again to unlock it. Select several elements and the toolbar floating above them has a **Lock** button too, reading **Unlock** when every one of them is locked; the shortcut locks or unlocks the whole selection at once.
</AppNote>


A locked element shows a small **padlock** badge on its top-left corner, selected or not, so it's clear at a glance which parts of the canvas are protected (arrows show no badge, and the badge hides when you zoom far out). Only Editors can lock and unlock.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  Viewers and Participants don't get the Lock button.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  For Viewers and Participants the shortcut does nothing.
</AppNote>


## What Locking Protects

A locked element:

- **Can still be selected**, so you can always reach it to unlock it.
- **Can't be moved**: dragging its body does nothing.
- **Can't be resized**: its resize handles are hidden entirely, so there's nothing to grab.
- **Can't be deleted**: Delete and Backspace skip it, and a multi-select delete keeps the locked members while removing the rest. Deleting a shape that an arrow connects to also leaves a locked arrow in place.
- **Can still be labelled**: double-click to edit its text. Locking protects an element's position and existence, not its content.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  Its toolbar's Delete is disabled.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  **Delete** in its right-click menu is disabled.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The [eraser](/help/selection-modes/eraser/) passes over it.
</AppNote>

**Arrows** lock the same way: a locked arrow's endpoint handles can't be dragged, and it can't be moved or deleted until you unlock it. Double-clicking a locked arrow doesn't open its text.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  Use **Edit Text** on its selection toolbar instead.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Select it and press `Space` instead.
</AppNote>


<Note>
  This per-element lock is different from the brief lock you see in a live session, where everything
  someone else has selected, one element or many, is held for them until they move on and their change is saved. See [live
  presence](/help/collaboration/live-presence/) for that one.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Lock a finished background before you start arranging the busy part of a <Noun />, so the
  foundation stays put while you work on top of it. To freeze a whole tab at once, see
  [locking a tab](/help/tabs/locking-tabs/).
</Tip>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Lock a finished frame or legend the same way. To protect a whole slice of the canvas, [lock its layer](/help/canvas/layers-visibility-and-locking/#locking-a-layer).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  Lock a finished picture or heading the same way. To protect a whole slice of the canvas, see [Layers](/help/canvas/layers/).
</AppNote>
