## What a Theme Covers

A theme sets the canvas look together with the fill, border, and text colours of your elements, so the <Noun /> reads as one coherent style. Sticky notes keep their own note colours, and switching themes leaves your [fonts](/help/canvas/text-and-fonts/) untouched.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  A theme sets the canvas background and pattern together with the fill, border, and text colours of
  your elements, so the diagram reads as one coherent style. Most themes paint every element the
  same colours; some are cleverer, tinting each branch of a hierarchy a different hue, or colouring
  each shape kind by its meaning (the **UML** theme makes decisions amber and data stores purple,
  for example).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  A theme sets the canvas background and pattern together with the fill, border, and text colours of
  your elements, so the page reads as one coherent style. Most themes paint every element the same
  colours; some are cleverer, tinting each branch of a hierarchy a different hue.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  A theme sets the canvas colour (and, for most themes, a floor) together with the fill, border, and
  text colours of your elements, so the board reads as one coherent style. Most themes paint every
  element the same colours; the multicolour ones give each element the next hue of a palette.
</AppNote>

A theme belongs to one tab, so the tabs of one <Noun /> can each wear a different one. A new <Noun /> starts on Default, and a tab you add starts on the theme of the tab you added it from.

## Light and Dark, for Everyone

Every theme has a light half and a dark half. Which half you see is your own [appearance](/help/tools/dark-mode/): on **Default**, light chrome shows the off-white canvas with the familiar blue elements and dark chrome the blue-slate canvas with grey ones, and every other theme turns the same way, keeping its own hues.

Nothing is saved when the half changes: a colleague in light chrome sees the light half of the very same tab while you work in the dark one. A colour someone picked by hand is drawn as it is for everyone.

## Browsing and Applying

You change a tab's theme from the **Tab Style** dialog, which opens on its home. At the top are the **Looks** (from **Everyday** and **Boardroom** to colourful ones like **Spectrum** and **Daydream**), each setting the theme, the [finish](/help/canvas/finish/), the tab font and the canvas pattern (or floor) together in one click, led by a **Mashup** card that rolls a random mix of all four each time you press it, and a dropdown beside them narrows the looks to one finish. To pick a theme on its own, press **Colours** under **Fine-tune**: every theme sits in one list, nine to a page. Click one to apply it. See [Changing the Theme](/help/canvas/themes/changing-theme/) for the full walkthrough.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">

  zoom controls (named **Style** on a wide screen).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Open it with the **Style** button on a page's title bar (top right of every page; on an article
  page, **Style** then **Tab Style**).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">

</AppNote>

## Beyond a Single Colour

[Multicolour themes](/help/canvas/themes/multicolour-themes/) go past the one-colour look.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Multicolour themes give each branch of a mind map or hierarchy its own hue, the way a rainbow mind
  map does.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  Multicolour themes give each branch of a hierarchy of connected shapes its own hue.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  Multicolour themes give each element the next hue of a palette, so a wall of notes reads as
  colourful at a glance.
</AppNote>

## Quick Emphasis

For tweaks to a single element rather than the whole tab, its right-click menu restyles it, so a key shape can stand out while still matching the overall look.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  The [style presets](/help/palette/shapes/style-presets/) in the right-click menu offer one-click
  colour and border variations derived from the active theme.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  Its [element menu](/help/canvas/colours-borders-opacity/) restyles it, so a key note can stand out
  while still matching the overall look.
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  Every control in the Tab Style dialog applies as you click, with no Apply or Cancel, so you can
  try themes on the canvas behind the dialog until one feels right. Undo takes a theme change back
  if you change your mind later.
</Tip>
