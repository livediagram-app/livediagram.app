## Where Your Work Lives

<Noun plural capital /> are saved through a single Cloudflare Worker API. The browser never talks to storage directly.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  - **Durable storage** is one Cloudflare Durable Object per canvas, reached only through the API: the canvas's tabs, comments and the images it places are stored there. Where it is filed, its folders, share links and its History events (the last 30 days) are kept by liveworkspace's index, the shared library behind every liveworkspace app.
  - **Real-time state** (who is in the room, live cursors, in-flight edits) is handled by the same Durable Object, the canvas's room. It also keeps a working record of collaborative input (answers, ideas, ticks, votes, comments and live polls) in its own storage, so that saves made at the same moment can be merged.
  - **Images** you add are kept by liveworkspace's media service, and a cached picture of each canvas, used for its thumbnail and live image, in Cloudflare R2 object storage; both are served back through the same API. Location and camera details are stripped from a JPEG before it is stored.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">

## What Is Stored, and Who Can Read It

A <Noun /> is owned by an identity. That identity is either a signed-in account or, for guests, a guest identity that liveworkspace gives your browser, which is the same in every liveworkspace app. Only the owner, people holding a valid [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/), and the members of the [team](/help/collaboration/teams/) whose library the <Noun /> is in can read a <Noun />. Opening a <Noun /> you cannot access returns a plain "not found", so its contents and name are never revealed.

  A canvas you [share to the Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/) is public until you remove it.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Calls, cameras and screens.** What you say, show on camera or share from your screen in a Space is never stored. It travels from your browser through Cloudflare's realtime service to the people who can hear you, and nowhere else: livehuddle's server sets up each connection but never receives the sound or picture, and nothing is recorded or transcribed.

  - **Only when you choose.** The microphone opens the first time you unmute (it starts muted), the camera when you turn it on, and a screen when you pick it, each after your browser asks. A muted microphone is released after 15 seconds with nobody else on your tab, and a screen share carries no sound.
  - **A preview stays with you.** A camera or screen turned on with no call joined shows only to you, marked **Only You**; nothing is sent.
  - **Who receives it.** The people in earshot, or in the same room, hear you and see your camera and screen. From a stage, the whole tab hears you, and sees you only if the stage shows cameras. Everyone on the tab can see that you are talking, never what you say.
  - **Never peer to peer.** Your browser connects to Cloudflare, not to anyone else's browser, so other people never see your IP address.
  - **Your character's moves, messages, status, raised hand and knocks** go live to the people in the Space and are never stored.

  The [Privacy Policy](https://www.liveworkspace.app/privacy#livehuddle-calls) says the same, formally.
</AppNote>

## Staying in Control

- **Guest by default.** You can build and share <Noun plural /> without ever creating an account. See [working as a guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).
- **Keep it in the browser.** [Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) keeps a <Noun /> off the server entirely.
- **Share on your terms.** Add a [share password](/help/privacy-and-security/share-link-security/) to a <Noun /> or an expiry to a share link, and revoke links at any time.
- **Delete what you own.** Deleting a <Noun /> moves it to the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/) for 30 days, then removes it with its tabs, comments and share links; you can delete it permanently from the Trash sooner. Signed-in users can [delete their whole account](/help/account-and-data/deleting-your-data/#deleting-your-account), which removes their personal <Noun plural />, folders, tokens and preferences straight away.
- **Opt out of analytics.** The anonymous usage events have a switch in Settings. See [What We Collect](/help/privacy-and-security/what-we-collect/).
