Most <Noun plural /> are saved to <AppName />'s servers so you can reach them from any device and share them. **Offline Mode** is the opposite choice: a <Noun /> saved **only in the browser you made it in**, that is never stored on the server. It suits private, local-first work, simply trying <AppName /> out, or wanting a <Noun /> that physically never leaves your machine.

## Creating in This Browser

Offline Mode is chosen per <Noun />, so you can keep normal <Noun plural /> and ones in This Browser side by side.

- **If you haven't signed in**, your new <Noun plural /> start in This Browser. Nothing you try out is sent to our servers until you choose to share it, sync it, or sign in and move it to your account. Everything you make from [New <Noun capital />](/new), and from the links that create a <Noun /> straight away (a template from the home page, say), starts in This Browser. Use **Sync <Noun capital />** from its Share dialog if you would rather it went to the server now.
- **Once you've signed in**, new <Noun plural /> go to your account. To keep one only in this browser, choose **Take Offline** from its menu (see below).
- **A <Noun /> made inside a folder or a team** goes to the server, whoever makes it, because that is where the folder or team lives.
- The <Noun /> is written to your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) and opens like any other. In the Explorer, the place these live is called **Browser Storage**, and each one carries a **Browser Storage** pill, so you always know which <Noun plural /> live only in this browser. Hover or focus the pill to read that clearing this browser's site data deletes the <Noun />. The rest of the Explorer lists only <Noun plural /> on the server, so **Browser Storage**, a space of its own in the Explorer sidebar's **Spaces** group, is where you find them all.

## What's Different

A <Noun /> in This Browser is a normal <Noun />: every editor feature works, including tabs, themes, undo/redo, and export. Only the features that need the server are unavailable, because there's no server copy to power them:

- **Sharing** needs a <Noun /> on the server. Opening **Share** on a <Noun /> in This Browser tells you so and offers **Sync <Noun capital />**: press it and the <Noun /> uploads, and the Share options appear in the same dialog, with no reload. Embeds are unavailable until it is synced, for the same reason.
- **Live collaboration** and **teams** don't apply; a <Noun /> in This Browser is yours alone. Comments still work, but only you see them.
- Images you add are stored **inside the <Noun />**, so it stays fully local.
- **Add a Tab to Another <Noun capital />** is unavailable for a <Noun /> in This Browser in either direction, and **Duplicate** makes another copy in This Browser rather than uploading one.
- It is listed under **Browser Storage** in the Explorer sidebar's Spaces, in Home's Recent once you have opened it, and on the **Browser Storage** page ([/offline](/offline), also reached from the Explorer panel), not in My Work, Recent or search, because those list what is on the server.

## Folders in Browser Storage

**New Folder** on the Browser Storage page, or the **⋯** menu on its row in the sidebar, makes a folder that lives in this browser too, and folders can hold folders, as in My Work. **Move to Folder** on a <Noun />'s menu, or a drag onto a folder, files it. A folder's menu has **Rename**, **New Subfolder**, **Move Folder** and **Delete Folder**; deleting a folder never deletes what is in it, which moves up a level. The folders are kept in this browser alone, like the <Noun plural /> in them.

## Archiving in This Browser

**Move to Archived** works on a <Noun /> in This Browser too. It never leaves this browser: it moves out of the Browser Storage list into **Archived**, and it won't open until you move it out. Opening it shows a **<Noun capital /> Archived** card with **Change Folder**, which puts it back in This Browser (or, when you are signed in, moves it into your account). Clearing this browser's data removes it like any other <Noun /> kept here.

## Moving to Your Account

You can change your mind at any time, from the <Noun />'s **⋯** menu.

- **When you sign in** with <Noun plural /> in This Browser, we offer to move them to your account. Every one is ticked; untick any you want to keep in this browser, then **Move**. **Not Now** leaves them where they are, and we only ask again when there are more.
- **Sync <Noun capital />** (in a <Noun />'s Share dialog, signed in or not, and on each <Noun /> under **Browser Storage**, in the Explorer and on the Browser Storage page, when you're signed in) uploads a <Noun /> in This Browser to the cloud. From the Explorer, the Browser Storage page opens and asks you to confirm. It becomes a normal <Noun />, shareable and (when you're signed in) reachable from your other devices, and the copy in this browser is removed.
- **A <Noun /> in a folder keeps its place.** Sync <Noun capital /> offers the My Work folder with the same name (or My Work itself when there is none) and says where it will go. The folder in this browser stays, empty, until you delete it.
- **Take Offline** (on a <Noun />'s menu in the Explorer and the Explorer panel) does the reverse: it downloads a cloud <Noun /> into this browser and then **removes it from your account and every other device**. Because that deletes the server copy for good, we ask you to confirm first.
- **A tab it shares with other <Noun plural />** (see Add a Tab to Another <Noun capital />) stays in those <Noun plural />, and this browser keeps its own copy. From then on the two are separate: edits on one side no longer reach the other, and syncing the <Noun /> back brings your copy in as a new tab beside the original.
