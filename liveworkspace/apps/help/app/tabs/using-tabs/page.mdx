A <Noun /> is a collection of **tabs**. A new <Noun /> usually starts with one tab, and you add more whenever a piece of work deserves its own space. The tab bar sits along the bottom of the editor, and the active tab fills the area above it.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  A <Noun /> is not a single page: each tab has its own elements, layout, theme, and background.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
  Each tab has its own pages, elements, layout, theme, and background.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Each tab holds exactly one thing: a **Board** of cards in columns, a **Sheet** of cells and
  formulas, a **Dashboard** of metrics and charts, or a **Form** whose answers become cards.
</AppNote>

## What a tab is

Each tab is a self-contained <Noun /> inside the <Noun />. A tab carries its own elements, its own theme and background pattern, and an optional lock, all independent of the other tabs around it. Common ways people split work across tabs:

- An **Overview** tab with the high-level picture, and detail tabs behind it.
- One tab per system, team, or phase of a project.
- A scratch tab for ideas you are not ready to file yet.

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
  Each tab is a self-contained row of [pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/) inside the <Noun />.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Common ways to split a plan across tabs: a **Board** for the day-to-day work and a **Dashboard**
  beside it that sums it up; a **Sheet** for the budget or the numbers behind the plan; a **Form**
  for requests, whose answers land as cards on a board. The cards on a plan's boards belong to the
  plan rather than to one tab, so two boards can show the same cards, and a Dashboard can chart
  them. See [Planning Basics](/help/plan/planning-basics/). The small icon at the start of a tab's
  name shows what it holds.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Each tab also has its own [mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/) (Diagram, Draw or Workshop), the same
  for everyone on it, chosen on its start while it is empty. The small icon at the start of a tab's
  name shows it.
</AppNote>

## Adding a tab

Press the **+** button at the end of the tab bar, or run **Create New Tab** from the [command palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/) (`Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `K`). The new tab:

- is named `Tab 2`, `Tab 3` and so on, after the number of tabs in the <Noun />;
- takes the theme, background and font of the tab you were on, so it matches the work around it;
- starts on its own start, so you can begin from a template or close it to keep the tab blank.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  It opens in the same mode as the tab you were on: made from a Workshop tab it is a Workshop tab,
  and from a Draw tab a Draw tab. It starts on that mode's own start, such as **Start Drawing** or
  **Start a Workshop**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">It starts on **Start Making Pages**.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  It starts on **Start a Space**, with an [Avatar Pad](/help/palette/avatar-pads/) where people
  arrive.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  It takes the look of the tab you were on, and opens on **Start Planning**, where you pick what it
  holds: a board, sheet, form, visualisation or dashboard, from a template or blank. Picking one
  usually names the tab too, such as "Kanban" for a Kanban board.
</AppNote>

**Duplicate** in the tab menu makes a copy instead: it is named after the original with "copy" on the end, sits right after it, and is a separate tab with its own content from then on.

## Naming and reordering tabs

Tabs start with default names like `Tab 1` and `Tab 2`, and you can rename any of them:

- **Double-click** the active tab's name to edit it inline. Enter or clicking away saves, Escape cancels, and an empty name keeps the old one.
- **Rename** in the tab menu (and **Rename Tab** in the command palette) opens the same editor.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign,livehuddle">
  **Auto-rename on first edit:** while a tab still has its default name, editing the label of its
  first element renames the tab to match. This only happens once, so later edits leave a renamed tab
  alone.
</AppNote>

Tab names are capped at **60 characters**. Runs of spaces and line breaks collapse to one, and anything longer is cut at the last whole word with an ellipsis, so a paragraph typed into a sticky can't turn into an unreadable tab.

Drag a tab left or right to reorder it. A blue bar shows where it will land: drop on the left half of a tab to land before it, the right half to land after it. Reordering only moves the tab in the list, it never touches the content on any tab.

Drag a tab up off the bar to the **right edge** of the screen instead, and it opens beside the tab you're on: see [Side by Side Tabs](/help/tabs/side-by-side/).

## The tab menu

Click the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click any tab (which switches to it first), to open its menu. On a phone it opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign,livehuddle">
  Right-clicking an empty spot on the <Noun /> opens the same menu, with **Paste** moved to a row of
  its own at the top.
</AppNote>

Along the top sits a row of quick actions: **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Paste** (greyed out until you have copied something), **Side by Side** (on a large enough window; **Close Side by Side** while two tabs are open), **Lock Tab** and **Delete**. Below them, everything else is a full-width row, in groups split by a line:

- **Organise in Tab Folder** and **Add to Another <Noun capital />**.
- **Import**, **Export**, and **Clear**, which removes every element from the tab after a confirmation (undo brings them back; it is greyed out on an empty tab).

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The quick actions have no **Lock Tab**, and the menu has an
  **[Attendance](/help/collaboration/live-presence/tab-attendance/)** row: who was in the tab, when
  they joined and left, and how long they stayed. It isn't offered in a space kept
  [Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
  **Side by Side** is offered once the <Noun /> has another tab to pair it with. The row reads **Add
  to <Noun capital />
  ** rather than **Add to Another <Noun capital />
  **.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  There is no row for adding the tab to another plan: a tab belongs to one plan (see below).
  **Import…** opens the [Import dialog](/help/tabs/import-tabs/): a CSV into a Board or Sheet tab, a
  tab's JSON, or a board from Trello, Asana, Jira or Linear. **Clear** empties the tab after a
  confirmation (Undo brings it back).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign,livehuddle">
  To tidy a tab's layout, search for **Auto Layout** or **Auto-align** in the [command
  palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/). See [Cleaning Up a
  Tab](/help/tabs/tab-cleanup/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  In Diagram mode you can also press **Tidy** in the bottom-right corner. The [session
  tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/) (timer, vote and poll) are not in this menu: they have
  their own buttons in the bottom bar.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The [session tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/) are not in this menu: a Timer or Stopwatch
  is a piece you place in a room.
</AppNote>

## Deleting a tab

Choose **Delete** in the tab menu. The tab goes straight away, and **Undo** brings it back. Once you reload or leave the <Noun /> it is gone, unless another <Noun /> also holds it, so export or duplicate anything you might want later. A few things to know:

- A <Noun /> always keeps at least one tab, so Delete is greyed out on the last one.
- Links on other tabs that pointed at the deleted tab are removed with it.
- If the tab is also in another <Noun />, it is only removed from this one.

<AppNote app="livediagram,liveplanner,livedocuments,livedesign">
  A [locked tab](/help/tabs/locking-tabs/) can't be deleted until you unlock it.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Delete asks first only when it does something Undo can't take back: a [form](/help/plan/forms/)'s
  tab deletes the form's link with it, and a board or sheet that forms send their responses to stops
  them taking responses. Links on other tabs are not mentioned in liveplanner.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **Tabs in more than one plan.** A tab belongs to one plan. Tabs used to be addable to another plan
  from the tab menu, but a plan's tabs show its cards, and a card is kept in the plan it came from,
  so a tab carried into another plan couldn't take its cards with it. Tabs that were already shared
  between plans stay shared: an edit in either plan shows in both, and deleting the tab in one plan
  only removes it from that one. To reuse a tab's layout somewhere new, [export
  it](/help/tabs/export-tabs/) and [import it](/help/tabs/import-tabs/) into the other plan.
</AppNote>

## Share links

On a share link for a Viewer or a Participant, and on any link scoped to one tab, you can still click between tabs, but the **+** button, the tab menu, renaming and drag-to-reorder are all hidden. Only an Editor with a link to the whole <Noun /> changes its tabs.

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  A Participant's tab menu (and that of anyone on a link scoped to one tab, other than a Viewer)
  holds **Attendance** alone; a Viewer gets no tab menu, and the **+** button, renaming and
  drag-to-reorder are hidden.
</AppNote>

## More with tabs

Each tab also carries its own menu of actions, covered in their own guides:

- **[Tab Folders](/help/tabs/tab-folders/)** group related tabs under one named chip so a long tab bar stays readable.
- **[Side by Side Tabs](/help/tabs/side-by-side/)** puts two tabs on screen at once, each live, with the toolbars following you between them.
- **[Connected Files](/help/tabs/connected-files/)** adds a tab that opens a file from any liveworkspace app: a plan, a document, a design or another <Noun />.
- **[Importing](/help/tabs/import-tabs/)** and **[Exporting](/help/tabs/export-tabs/)** move a tab's contents in and out as a file.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign,livehuddle">
  **[Cleaning Up a Tab](/help/tabs/tab-cleanup/)** tidies a tab's layout in one click.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  **[Linking Across Tabs](/help/tabs/linking-tabs/)** turns any element into a jump point to another
  tab.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  **[Add a Tab to Another <Noun capital />
  ](/help/tabs/add-to-document/)** shares a tab into a different <Noun />: it is the same tab in
  both places, not a copy, so edits appear everywhere it lives.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  **[Add a Tab to Another Design](/help/tabs/add-to-design/)** shares a tab into a different design:
  it is the same tab in both places, not a copy, so edits appear everywhere it lives.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,liveplanner,livedocuments,livedesign">
  **[Locking a Tab](/help/tabs/locking-tabs/)** makes a whole tab read-only.
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  A shared reference tab such as a glossary can live in more than one <Noun /> at once with Add to
  Another <Noun capital />. Editing it in one place updates it everywhere.
</Tip>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Lots of tabs? Press `⌘K` and type part of a tab's name to jump straight to it, or group them into
  [folders](/help/tabs/tab-folders/). In liveplanner a tab belongs to one plan, so a shared
  reference tab as above does not apply.
</AppNote>
