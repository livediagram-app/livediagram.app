## Locking and unlocking a tab

Open the tab's menu (the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click the tab) and press the padlock button, **Lock Tab**, in the row of quick actions along the top. The same button reads **Unlock Tab** afterwards (and shows as pressed), so it toggles the tab back. A locked tab shows a small **padlock** before its name in the bar, so you can tell at a glance which tabs are frozen.

The lock is saved with the tab, so it applies to everyone who opens it, not just you. Anyone with edit access can unlock it again.

<AppNote app="livediagram,liveplanner,livedocuments,livedesign">
  **When to use it**
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">
  **What a locked tab protects**

While a tab is locked, everything on it is read-only:

- Elements **can't be moved, resized, restyled, or edited**, and nothing new can be added or pasted onto the tab.
- The padlock before the tab's name says the whole tab is locked, so its elements don't each show one. An element that also carries its own lock keeps its padlock.
- An image still takes a picture: **Select Image** fills an empty one and **Change Image** swaps it. Only the picture changes; the image stays where it is.
- The **Undo** and **Redo** buttons are greyed out on the tab.
- In the tab menu, **Import** and **Clear** are greyed out (an import started from the command palette is refused), the [layout tidiers](/help/tabs/tab-cleanup/) are off (the Tidy panel greys them out and says the tab is locked, and the command palette leaves them out), and the tab itself **can't be deleted** until you unlock it.

Locking clears your current selection. You can still look around, pan and zoom, follow links, and **rename**, **duplicate**, or **export** the tab from the same menu. Duplicating gives you an editable copy if you want to keep building from a locked original.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  The [eraser](/help/selection-modes/eraser/) skips everything on a locked tab, and links on it
  still take you to their [linked tabs](/help/tabs/linking-tabs/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **What a locked tab protects.**

- The tab **can't be deleted** until you unlock it.
- **Clear** is greyed out in the tab menu, and in the [Import dialog](/help/tabs/import-tabs/) the tab's own row (its CSV or JSON) is greyed out with the reason. A board from another tool can still come in, as a new tab.
- A **Dashboard** can't be changed: its rows, slots and what they show stay put, and you see it as a Viewer does, without its settings or editing.
- The **Undo** and **Redo** buttons are greyed out on the tab.

You can still **rename**, **duplicate** or **export** the tab from the same menu, and move it into a folder. Duplicating gives you an unlocked copy if you want to keep building from a locked original.

**What a lock leaves alone.** The cards on a board and the cells of a sheet belong to the plan, not to one tab, so a lock doesn't freeze them: people can still add, edit and move cards on a locked Board tab, and type into a locked Sheet. A lock protects the tab, not the work flowing through it.

To stop someone changing anything at all, share a [view-only link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) instead.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">
- Freeze a signed-off or presented tab so a later edit can't disturb it.
- Protect a reference or template tab you copy from but never change.
- Hand a <Noun /> to collaborators with the finished tabs locked, leaving only the working tabs open.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
- Freeze a Dashboard you've set up for a team or a review, so its layout can't be knocked out of place.
- Protect a tab you rely on from being deleted or cleared by accident.
- Hand a plan to collaborators with its set-up tabs locked, leaving the work on them free to move.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">
  To protect just a few elements rather than a whole tab, lock them individually instead. See
  [locking elements](/help/canvas/locking/). To stop someone editing anything at all, share a
  [view-only link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) instead.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Need a fresh start from a locked tab? **Duplicate** it from the tab menu: the copy is unlocked and
  ready to change.
</AppNote>
