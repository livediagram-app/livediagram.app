## Tidying from the command palette

Open the [command palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/) (`Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `K`) and type **Auto-align**. The tidiers appear under **Actions**:

- **Auto-align to Grid** snaps the current positions and sizes onto a tidy grid, straightening rows and columns while keeping your overall arrangement. Reach for it when the layout is basically right but a little ragged.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  In Diagram mode, the quickest way is the **Tidy** button in the bottom-right corner, just left of
  Layers. It shows each layout style as a small picture, and resting the mouse on one previews it on
  the canvas before you click.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign">
  You can also type **Auto Layout**, which lists these under **Actions**: **Auto Layout (tidy up)**
  recomputes positions from the arrows, laying the diagram out from scratch based on how its
  elements connect, in the arrangement that best fits its current rough flow; reach for it when you
  want the structure rebuilt rather than nudged. **Auto Layout: Flowchart (down)**, **Auto Layout:
  Flowchart (right)**, **Auto Layout: Tree** and **Auto Layout: Mindmap** run Auto Layout in that
  particular style: a flowchart running down or to the right, a tree with parents centred over their
  children, or a mindmap radiating from a central topic.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  You can also type **Auto Layout**: **Auto Layout (tidy up)** recomputes positions from the arrows,
  laying the shapes out from scratch based on how its elements connect; **Auto Layout: Layered
  (down)**, **Auto Layout: Layered (right)**, **Auto Layout: Tree** and **Auto Layout: Radial** run
  it in layers running down or to the right, a tree with parents centred over their children, or
  radially. To straighten just one infographic or slide page, press **Tidy** (the broom) in that
  page's title bar instead; see [Working with Pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/).
</AppNote>

## More on layout

These are the [Layout Cleanup](/help/tools/layout-cleanup/) tools. See that guide for the details of how [Auto-Align](/help/tools/layout-cleanup/auto-align/) decides where things go.

The tidiers are for Editors: they aren't offered to a Participant or on a view-only share link.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">
  [Auto Layout](/help/tools/layout-cleanup/auto-layout/) has its own guide too. A [locked
  tab](/help/tabs/locking-tabs/) can't be tidied until you unlock it.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">The Tidy panel says so when the tab is locked.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  livehuddle has only Auto-align (no Auto Layout), and has no tab lock. Tidying is for Editors: it
  isn't offered on a Viewer's or a Participant's share link.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">
  **Tip:** try **Auto-align** first, it is the gentler of the two and keeps your layout
  recognisable. Fall back to **Auto Layout** when a <Noun /> needs rebuilding from its connections.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Tip:** Auto-align is safe to run often: it never moves anything far, and running it again on a
  tidy tab changes nothing.
</AppNote>
