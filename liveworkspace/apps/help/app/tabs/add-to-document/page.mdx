## Adding a Tab Elsewhere

<Step n={1}>Open the tab's menu (the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click the tab).</Step>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  <Step n={2}>
    Choose **Add to Another <Noun capital />
    **.
  </Step>
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  <Step n={2}>Choose **Add to Document**.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  In the dialog that opens, pick the destination. Each <Noun /> shows as a tile with a preview of
  its <Noun />, and the filter box at the top narrows a long list. A message confirms when the tab
  has been added.
</Step>

The option is greyed out when there is nowhere to add the tab to: if this is your only <Noun />, until you create another, and in a [<Noun /> kept Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/). It is greyed out too on a <Noun /> you don't own, such as one someone shared with you as an Editor, since a shared tab has to live in <Noun plural /> of your own. It isn't offered to Viewers or Participants, or on a link shared for [one tab](/help/collaboration/sharing/one-tab/). If the tab is already in 20 <Noun plural />, adding it to another fails with a message that it could not be added.

## One Tab, Shared

Adding a tab this way doesn't make a copy: it is the same tab in both places. Edit its elements, restyle it, or rename it from either <Noun /> and the change lands in the other as well, a few seconds after you stop editing. If the other <Noun /> is already open in another window, reload it to see the latest edits. If the tab is edited in both <Noun plural /> at once, the later edit is the one both keep.

Two things stay per-<Noun />, so each <Noun /> can organise the shared tab its own way: the tab's position in the tab bar, and which [tab folder](/help/tabs/tab-folders/) (if any) it sits in.

Removing the tab from one <Noun /> doesn't touch the others; the tab and its contents are only deleted for good when you remove it from the last <Noun /> containing it. Deleting a whole <Noun /> works the same way: while it waits in the Trash its shared tabs stay in step, and once it is deleted for good they stay, with their contents, in every other <Noun /> they are in. Taking a <Noun /> offline tells you first how many of its tabs are shared: they stay in the other <Noun plural />, and the copies in this browser stop sharing edits with them.

<Tip>
  Prefer an independent copy you can tailor per project? Use **Duplicate** in the same menu first,
  then add the duplicate to the other <Noun />.
</Tip>
