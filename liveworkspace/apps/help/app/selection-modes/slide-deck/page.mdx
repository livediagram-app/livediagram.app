A finished <Noun /> shows everything at once, which is exactly what you want for reference and exactly what you don't want when you are narrating it. A **slide deck** turns it into a talk: a set of slides, presented full screen one at a time.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  You build the slides yourself, each showing the elements you chose. Open **Slide Deck** from **Tools** in the bottom bar: it is in the **Collaborate** section. You can also type "slide deck" into the [command palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/), which opens Tools there. Slide Deck is **desktop only**: a phone screen has no room for the deck list and the canvas side by side, so it isn't offered there. A deck you built on a desktop browser is kept, ready for the next time you open the canvas on one.

  Opening it does **not** start presenting. It opens the **Slide Deck** page inside Tools, where you build and check the deck; starting is a deliberate second act. Unlike other tools, Tools stays open while you select elements on the canvas for a slide; close it with its close button or `Esc`.
</AppNote>

## What a slide is

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **A slide is a set of elements you chose, from one tab.** It is not a region of the canvas, not a saved camera position, and not a layer.

  - **The deck spans tabs, a slide doesn't.** Slide 1 can come from one tab, slide 2 from another, and slide 3 from the first one again, but each slide draws from a single tab, which is what gives it a backdrop and a coordinate space to be framed in.
  - **An element can be on as many slides as you like.** Membership is a list, not a split, so a title that belongs on every slide simply goes on every slide.
  - **Slide order is the deck's own.** It has nothing to do with tab order or with which element sits in front of which, and reordering the deck never restacks anything on the canvas.
  - **Slides point at elements, they never copy them.** Restyle a shape and every slide it appears on shows the new one. Delete it and the slides quietly skip it; undo the delete and it is back on all of them.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  **A slide is a page.** It shows whatever is on that page now, framed to exactly the page, so it follows your edits without you touching the deck.

  - **Every [slide page](/help/pages/slide-pages/) is already in the deck**, from the moment you make it, in page order. Move a page in Contents and its slide moves with it.
  - **Any other page joins with Add to Slides**: open its **⋯** menu in Contents (or right-click its row) and press **Add to Slides**.
  - **Each tab is its own show.** **Present** runs the slides of the tab you are on, in its page order. Slides on another tab are that tab's show: open it and press **Present** there.

  See [Building a Deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/building-a-deck/) for hiding a slide from the run, speaker notes and the order, and [Exporting and Presenting Pages](/help/pages/exporting-pages/#exporting) for how a page slide behaves.
</AppNote>

## Building a deck, in short

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  <Step n={1}>
    Open **Tools** in the bottom bar and pick **Slide Deck**. A new deck starts empty, because a
    guessed deck is one you have to read and prune before you can trust it.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2}>On the canvas, select what you want on the first slide.</Step>
  <Step n={3}>
    Press **New slide** at the bottom of the Slide Deck. It counts your selection back to you, "New
    slide from 3 elements", and the slide takes the tab you are on.
  </Step>
  <Step n={4}>
    Move to the next thing you want to talk about, select it, and press the button again.
  </Step>

  **Arrows come along on their own.** An arrow with both ends on the slide is included automatically, so you can build a slide by dragging a box round four shapes without hand-picking the connectors between them.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Slide pages are in the deck already; any other page joins with **Add to Slides** on its **⋯** menu in Contents.
</AppNote>

Everything else about building, the slide menu, editing what is on a slide, hiding one from the run, reordering, notes and time budgets, is in [Building a Deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/building-a-deck/).

## Presenting it

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Press **Present** at the bottom of the Slide Deck (or the **Present** button in the top bar) and the deck opens full screen at slide 1 with every part of the editor hidden. Its badge counts the slides that will run, and it stays greyed out until the deck has at least one slide that isn't hidden. See [Presenting](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/) for how a slide is framed, how you move through the deck, and what you can and cannot touch while it runs, and [The Presenter's Controls](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenter-controls/) for the strip in the corner, the pacing clock and the settings behind the cog.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Press **Present**, in the buttons at the top right of the canvas, and the deck opens full screen at the first slide with every part of the editor hidden. To start somewhere else, open a page's **⋯** menu in Contents and press **Present From Here**. See [Presenting](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/) for how a slide is framed, how you move through the deck, and what you can and cannot touch while it runs, and [The Presenter's Controls](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenter-controls/) for the strip in the corner, the pacing clock and the settings behind the cog. Transitions, auto-advance and the rest are also in **Settings**, under **Presenting**, so you can set them before you start rather than in front of the room.
</AppNote>

**Starting a presentation broadcasts nothing.** Nobody else is pulled into your deck and nobody's view moves. The way you show a deck to people is the way you already show them everything else in the meeting: **share your screen**.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **The deck itself is ordinary canvas data**, though: a teammate who opens the canvas sees your slides in their own Slide Deck panel, and can edit them. It saves and travels exactly like the canvas's name does.

  Building a deck needs edit access. People on a view-only or participant [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) can open the Slide Deck from Tools to see the slides, open each one and present the deck, but they get no **New slide** button and no slide menus, and the command palette does not offer them Slide Deck.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  **The deck itself is ordinary document data**, though: a teammate who opens the document sees the same slides in Contents, hidden ones set aside, and can change them. It saves and travels exactly like the document's name does.

  Changing the deck needs edit access: people on a view-only or participant [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) see no page menus in Contents. Anyone on the document, Viewers included, can present it with the **Present** button at the top right of the canvas ([Presenting](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/)).
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  Build the deck as you would an agenda: one slide per point you intend to make, in the order you
  intend to make them.
</Tip>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Put the same title element on every slide and the room always knows which diagram they are looking at.
</AppNote>
