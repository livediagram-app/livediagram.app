Select is the ordinary pointer: the tool for picking elements, moving them, resizing them and editing their text. It is where you spend most of your time on a desktop.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments">
  Every other tool hands you back here when you are done. Choose it from the tool picker at the start of the palette strip, or press **`V`**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **It is called Edit here.** If you can edit a Space, the palette strip starts with a small toggle in two halves: **Edit** (a pointer arrow) and **Walk** (footprints). It is your one switch between building the board and being in it. On a computer each half shows its icon and its name; on a phone, the icons alone.

  - **Edit** is plain selecting and building: pick elements, move them, resize them, type into them, and add new ones from the palette's categories.
  - **Walk** puts you in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/): you walk the Space as your character, talk to whoever is near, sit at a desk and react.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Switching between them.** While you build, Edit is on. Press **Walk** and your character appears, and the strip becomes the avatar palette (your character's look, Actions and React, with the call controls on a Call dock at the bottom). The same toggle sits at the very start of that strip with Walk on, so pressing **Edit** brings the building palette straight back.

  On a keyboard, **`W`** walks, from Edit. **`E`** goes back to Edit, and so do **`V`** and **`Esc`**. While you walk, `W` is Wave (one of your character's actions), so `W` only starts walking from Edit. You can also type "Edit" or "Walk" in the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/).

  While **Start a Space** is open, the Walk half is greyed out: pick a template, or close Start a Space, to walk. Edit always works. **Participants and view-only visitors are always walking**, so they never see the toggle. See [Avatar Mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments">
  **The tool picker.** The palette strip starts with the tool picker, showing the tool you are holding, then the palette's categories. Open the tool picker and the tools are laid out as tiles in three bands:

  - **Edit**: **Select**, **Hand**, **Eraser** and **Format**, the tools that work on the canvas.
  - **Present**: **Laser** and **Spotlight**, the tools for pointing things out to an audience.
  - **Preview**: **Zen**, which hides the editor's chrome rather than changing tool.

  The current tool is tinted, and a tool with a keyboard shortcut shows its letter in the tile's corner. Every tool after Hand, apart from Zen, needs something on the canvas to act on, so those tiles are greyed out on an empty tab. On a phone, Spotlight is left out of the picker.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  A Participant's picker holds only Select, Hand, Laser, Spotlight and Zen.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">

The [Slide Deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/) is not a tool here: it lives in **Tools** in the bottom bar. A Participant's picker leaves out Eraser and Format, and on an Event Storming board an Editor's palette has no picker, since its notation is the palette there.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">

## What you can do

In Select mode the pointer interacts directly with your elements:

- **Click** an element to select it. Click an empty patch of canvas, or press `Esc`, to clear the selection.
- **Shift-click** to add elements to the selection, or take them out again.
- **Drag a marquee** from empty canvas to select many elements at once. Only elements that sit entirely inside the box are picked up. See [Multi-select](/help/canvas/selecting-and-grouping/multi-select/).
- **Drag** an element to move it, or press the arrow keys to nudge the selection by 1 pixel (10 with `Shift`).
- **Drag its handles** to resize.
- **Double-click** an element to edit its label. With one element selected you can also just start typing: the first key opens the label and replaces what was there.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments">
  You can still pan while holding Select: drag with the middle mouse button (unless it is turned off in Settings), hold `Space` and drag, or scroll with two fingers on a trackpad. See [Pan and Zoom](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Moving around the canvas.** There is no separate tool for panning. In Edit, or while you walk, hold `Space` and drag, scroll with two fingers on a trackpad, drag with the middle mouse button (unless it is turned off in Settings), or, on a phone or tablet, drag one finger across empty canvas. See [Pan and Zoom](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/).

  **Removing things.** Select elements and press `Delete` or `Backspace`, or press Delete on the selection toolbar above them. [Undo](/help/canvas/undo/) brings back anything you removed by mistake.

  **Copying a look.** Select an element and press **Format Painter** on its selection toolbar. See [Format Painter](/help/selection-modes/format-painter/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments">
  **When Select is not the starting tool.** Select is the starting tool on a desktop. A few places start you somewhere else, because a drag there should move the view rather than an element. **On a phone**, the editor opens on [Hand](/help/selection-modes/hand/), so a one-finger drag pans the canvas. **An embedded <Noun />** always opens on Hand.

  **Returning here.** Select is your home base. Choosing a different tool changes what a click or drag does, so coming back to Select restores normal selection and editing. `Esc` takes you straight back from [Format](/help/selection-modes/format-painter/).

  View-only visitors have no palette, but they can still press `V` to select an element and read it. They cannot move or edit it.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  In [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/) the palette holds Draw's own tools instead of the tool picker. `V` still picks Draw's Select tool and `H` still picks Hand. `Esc` also takes you straight back from [Isometric](/help/selection-modes/isometric-mode/) (Isometric returns to Hand on a phone).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments">
  <Tip>
    Press `V` to jump straight back to Select without opening the tool picker. If a single element is
    selected, press `Esc` first: with one element selected, a letter key types into its label instead.
  </Tip>
</AppNote>
