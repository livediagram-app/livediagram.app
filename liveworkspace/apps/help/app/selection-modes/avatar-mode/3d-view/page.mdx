Every Space is 3D, always: the floor lies back with the tab's background on it, the walls of rooms, corridors, pods and fences stand up with their doorways and windows, furniture stands on the floor, and every character stands upright. There is no flat view and nothing to switch on. It changes nothing on the board and nothing anyone else sees.

## Arriving

When you open a Space walking, or travel to another tab, the camera lands on your character, close enough to see who is round you. Switching from Edit to Walk on the tab you're on glides in.

## Turning the Camera

Turn the camera a quarter turn round your character to see the back and sides of the room and the pieces in it: a bookcase shows its back from behind, a sofa its end from the side. Use the left and right of the view dial at the bottom right, **Turn Left** and **Turn Right** on a phone, or press **`[`** and **`]`**, in Edit or Walk.

- The arrow keys still steer by the screen: up always walks away from the camera.
- Clicking the floor walks there, and dragging pans the way your hand moves, whichever way the camera faces.
- Room names and text on the floor turn to stay readable.
- The turn is yours alone and is not remembered.

## Walls and Rooms

- **Rooms open up as you come near.** Walk up to a room, or stand inside one, and its walls on your side fade, so you can see what is going on before you choose to walk in.
- **A wall between you and the camera fades** too, so your character is never hidden.
- **Each room's name hangs over its doorway**, on a small sign above the door, where no picture or screen on the wall can cover it. A room with no door keeps its name on its back wall.
- **Pieces hung on a wall stay on it**: a Notice Board, Clock, Screen or Room Sign placed against a room's back wall hangs upright on that wall.

## Pieces You Can Use

In 3D, a piece with a control of its own **glows softly under the pointer**, so you can tell what does something: an Arcade's **Insert Coin**, a **Notice Board**, the office dog and a **Reception Desk**'s bell. The bubbles that open as you walk up to a piece stand upright over it, as name chips do.

## Building in 3D

Press **Edit** and the Space stays 3D: pieces you place, drag or draw land on the floor under the pointer, whichever way the camera faces. Everything you can do while walking still works: sitting, opening doors, using pieces, speaking, reactions and calls.

Everyone has the 3D View, because it only changes your own screen. On a phone a tap walks and a drag pans.
