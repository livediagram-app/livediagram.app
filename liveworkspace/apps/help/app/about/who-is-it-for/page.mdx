<AppNote app="livediagram">
  livediagram is for teams who think visually and need to do that thinking **together**. A
  single-player diagramming tool is not the point. The product shines when several people are on the
  same canvas at once, building one shared diagram in [real time](/help/collaboration/sharing/).
  That said, it works just as well for one person sketching something out, and the [canvas always
  works without signing in](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  liveplanner is for people who plan work **together**. It shines when a team shares one plan and
  everyone sees the same boards and sheets change in [real
  time](/help/collaboration/live-presence/). It works just as well for one person keeping track of
  their own work, and it [always works without signing in](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  livedocuments is for teams who make things to share and need to make them **together**. The
  product shines when several people are on the same document at once, building one set of pages in
  [real time](/help/collaboration/sharing/). That said, it works just as well for one person writing
  something up, and the [editor always works without signing
  in](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveknowledge">
  liveknowledge is for teams who need one place where what they know is written down and can be
  **trusted**. Every page has an owner and says when it was last verified, so people, and the AI
  agents working beside them, can tell a current answer from a stale one. Several people can write
  in a Knowledgebase at once, in [real time](/help/collaboration/live-presence/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  livedesign is for teams who design things and need to make them **together**. The product shines
  when several people are on the same design at once, building one set of pages in [real
  time](/help/collaboration/sharing/). That said, it works just as well for one person sketching a
  mark on their own, and the [editor always works without signing
  in](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  livehuddle is for people who want to be in the room **together**, even when they are not. It
  shines when several people are in the same Space at once, walking round it as their characters and
  seeing each other in [real time](/help/collaboration/sharing/). It works just as well for one
  person setting up a Space before the meeting, and the [Space always works without signing
  in](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).
</AppNote>

## The People It Serves

A few groups feel right at home:

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  - **Engineering teams** sketching architecture, sequence flows, and system designs. Use
  [shapes](/help/palette/shapes/) and [arrows](/help/palette/arrows/), [technology
  icons](/help/palette/technology/) for cloud and infrastructure marks, [sequence
  diagrams](/help/palette/sequence-diagrams/) and [entities](/help/palette/entities/) for flows and
  data models, and [tabs](/help/tabs/using-tabs/) to keep related views together. - **Product and
  design teams** mapping user journeys, information architecture, and brainstorms with
  [shapes](/help/palette/shapes/), [text and fonts](/help/canvas/text-and-fonts/), and
  [themes](/help/canvas/themes/). - **Cross-functional groups** in workshops, planning sessions, and
  retrospectives, where everyone needs to contribute at once and see each other's input live.
  [Workshop mode](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/) puts timers, dot votes and polls in front of whoever
  runs the session, and [BPMN](/help/palette/bpmn/) shapes map a business process step by step.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  - **Software and product teams** running sprints, backlogs, bug triage and roadmaps on
  [boards](/help/plan/boards/), with [card types](/help/plan/card-types/) for stories, tasks and
  bugs, a [Gantt chart](/help/plan/visualisations/) of what's coming, pull requests [linked from
  GitHub](/help/plan/connect-github/) and a [public roadmap](/help/plan/public-roadmap/) customers
  can vote on. - **Project and operations teams** keeping a launch, a content calendar or a weekly
  plan moving, with [dashboards](/help/plan/dashboards/) that show where the work stands at a
  glance. - **People running sessions** such as retros and planning meetings, where everyone adds
  cards at once, then votes on what matters with [session
  tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/). - **Anyone who lives in spreadsheets**:
  [Sheets](/help/plan/sheets/) handle budgets, timesheets and contact lists, with formulas, beside
  the boards they relate to. - **Anyone collecting requests or feedback**: a
  [Form](/help/plan/forms/) turns each answer into a card or a sheet row, and a board can take
  [requests by email](/help/plan/email-to-board/). - **Anyone tired of routine updates**:
  [Automations](/help/plan/automations/) move, assign, flag and announce cards for you when
  something changes.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  - **Anyone writing something up**: briefs, reports, proposals and how-tos as
  [articles](/help/pages/articles/), written together with [comments](/help/collaboration/comments/)
  on the words. - **People presenting**: decks built from [slide pages](/help/pages/slide-pages/)
  and their layouts, then [presented](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/) full screen with
  the laser and spotlight. - **Anyone giving a talk**: a stack of
  [flashcards](/help/pages/flashcards/) to speak from, each card saying how long it takes to say, so
  a speech, a toast or a pitch fits its slot. - **Marketing and communications teams** making
  infographics, posters, one-pagers and social posts on [infographic
  pages](/help/pages/infographic-layouts/), in your [themes](/help/canvas/themes/), to
  [export](/help/pages/exporting-pages/) or present.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveknowledge">
  - **Teams writing down how they work**: handbooks, runbooks, onboarding and how-tos as
  [pages](/help/knowledgebase/the-page-tree/), each with an owner who keeps it current. - **Anyone
  answering the same question twice**: [Ask](/help/knowledgebase/ask/) answers from your pages,
  citing each one, on your team's own AI key. - **Teams working with AI agents**: [connect an
  agent](/help/knowledgebase/connecting-an-agent/) and it reads verified pages first and suggests
  edits for a person to accept. - **Teams moving from another wiki**: bring pages in from
  [Notion](/help/knowledgebase/import-from-notion/),
  [Confluence](/help/knowledgebase/import-from-confluence/) or a [Markdown
  folder](/help/knowledgebase/import-a-markdown-folder/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  - **Brand and marketing teams** drawing marks, wordmarks and lockups on [logo
  pages](/help/pages/logo-pages/), then [exporting](/help/pages/exporting-pages/) them as PNG, SVG
  or PDF. - **Product and design teams** sketching screens on [wireframe
  pages](/help/pages/wireframe-pages/), each framed by its device, with
  [comments](/help/collaboration/comments/) on the details, then linking them into a [clickable
  prototype](/help/pages/wireframe-pages/prototypes/) and handing them to developers with
  [Inspect](/help/pages/wireframe-pages/inspect/). - **Anyone touching up pictures**: retouching,
  cutting out and colour-correcting photos on [photo pages](/help/pages/photo-pages/), in layers,
  then exporting them as PNG, JPEG, WebP or PSD. - **People walking others through** a design on a
  call: [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) clears the screen they share, and the laser and spotlight
  point things out.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  - **Remote and hybrid teams** who want a place to bump into each other. A [Virtual Office or a
  Coworking Space](/help/canvas/templates/) gives everyone a desk to sit at, a
  [status](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) to show whether they are free, and a meeting table to
  gather round. - **Anyone hosting a meeting**, from a stand-up in a Team Pod or a one-to-one in a
  quiet room to an all-hands in an All-Hands Hall. The [session
  tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/) put a Timer or a Stopwatch in the room for whoever runs
  it, and whoever holds the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) baton is the room's one
  host. - **Communities and event hosts** who want a place for a group to gather, walk up to each
  other and say hello, with [reactions](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) for the applause.
</AppNote>

## Whether You Sign In or Not

<AppName /> is friction-free on purpose. A first-time visitor can start something real as a
[guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/) and only later create an account to sync across
devices and join [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/). A signed-in user can still hand a share link
to a guest, who joins without any account at all.
