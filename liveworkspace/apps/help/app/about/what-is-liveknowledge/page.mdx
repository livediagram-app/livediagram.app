liveknowledge is a web app where a team keeps what it knows. You make a **Knowledgebase**, write its **pages** together, and say who vouches for each one. Then anyone on the team, and any AI agent the team connects, can find an answer and see how far to trust it.

## Pages in a Tree

A Knowledgebase is pages of writing, arranged in a tree in the sidebar. A page can have subpages, up to 8 levels deep, and a Knowledgebase holds up to 500 pages.

- **Writing**: one continuous page with headings, lists, tables, a table of contents, footnotes and margin notes, and pictures, charts and drawings placed in the writing. See [Writing on a Page](/help/knowledgebase/writing-on-a-page/).
- **The page tree**: add, nest, move, rename, duplicate and delete pages. See [The Page Tree](/help/knowledgebase/the-page-tree/).
- **Links**: type `[[` to link one page to another, and see what links to a page under **Linked From**. See [Linking Pages](/help/knowledgebase/linking-pages/).
- **Embeds**: show a diagram, a plan, a document, a design or a Space from the other liveworkspace apps inside a page. See [Embedding From Other Apps](/help/knowledgebase/embedding-from-other-apps/).

## Pages You Can Trust

A Knowledgebase is only useful while it is right, so every page says who vouches for it and when they last did.

- A page has an **owner**, and an Editor presses **Verify** to say the page is right. See [Owners and Verifying a Page](/help/knowledgebase/owners-and-verifying/).
- A verified page comes **Review Due** after the Knowledgebase's Review Period, 90 days unless you change it. See [Review Due and the Review Period](/help/knowledgebase/review-due-and-the-review-period/).
- **Page Health** shows a page's owner, its dates and how often people and agents read it. See [Page Health](/help/knowledgebase/page-health/).

## Finding an Answer

- **Search** finds pages and passages by their words, this Knowledgebase's first. It needs no AI and works for everyone. See [Search](/help/knowledgebase/searching-a-knowledgebase/).
- **Ask** answers a question in plain words from your own pages and cites the passage behind each claim. It runs on built-in AI, included, or on your team's own Claude or OpenAI key if you add one. See [Ask](/help/knowledgebase/ask/) and [AI Settings](/help/knowledgebase/ai-settings/).

## A Knowledgebase for Agents Too

AI agents read a Knowledgebase through its MCP server, its command line or an API token, and every answer they get carries the page's verified state. A new connection can read, search and suggest edits; an Editor decides whether one may write. The **AI Knowledge** view shows which agents are connected, what they read, the questions nobody could answer and the pages that disagree. See [Connecting an Agent](/help/knowledgebase/connecting-an-agent/) and [What Agents Can Do](/help/knowledgebase/what-agents-can-do/).

## What liveknowledge Is Not

liveknowledge has one kind of page: writing. It has no slides, infographics or flashcards, no presenting, and no paper sizes while you write; those are [livedocuments](https://www.livedocuments.app/)' job. Sharing is for a whole Knowledgebase or one page of it by link, not page by page for named people.

## Free and Open Source

liveknowledge is **MIT-licensed** and **free for everyone**, with every feature on the free plan. Search needs no AI, and Ask and Describe to Set Up run on built-in AI, included with daily limits. Your team's own Claude or OpenAI key is optional.
