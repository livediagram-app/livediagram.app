## Create a Share Link

<Step n={1}>
  Open the <Noun /> you want to share and click **Share** at the top right of the editor, or choose
  **Share** from the <Noun />'s **⋯** menu in the Explorer. Only the <Noun />'s owner (or a member
  of the team it belongs to) can issue passes, so someone who joined through a link sees **Copy**
  (Make a Copy) in its place, to duplicate the <Noun /> into their own files.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Under **Issue a pass**, click the role card to choose a role. **Valid** sets how long it lasts:
  Forever, 1 week, 1 month or 6 months. In a <Noun /> with several tabs, **Opens** can limit the link
  to one tab.
</Step>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The roles are **Editor** (draws with you in real time, the default), **Participant** (adds stickies, writes and votes, but can't reshape the board) and **Viewer** (watches, pans and zooms; can't comment, vote or change a thing).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  The roles are **Editor** (draws with you in real time, the default), **Participant** (edits text and comments, but can't add, move or restyle anything) and **Viewer** (watches, pans and zooms; can't comment or change a thing).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The role card starts on **Participant** (always walking: walks, talks and uses the pieces, but adds nothing to the Space), the pass most meetings want; press it to pick **Editor** (changes the Space with you in real time) instead.
</AppNote>

<Step n={3}>
  Click **Create Pass**. The link is copied for you straight away, ready to send to whoever you want
  in the <Noun />; each pass in the list also has its own copy button.
</Step>

If you are a guest, check the name under **Sharing as** at the foot of the dialog first (or press the shuffle button beside it for another): that is the name peers see on your cursor and comments. A <Noun /> saved to **This Browser** has to be on <AppName /> before it can be shared: **Share** then offers **Sync <Noun capital />**, which moves it there in place.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  This Browser is where a guest's new canvases go.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Syncing moves it to your account in place.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The name shows on your cursor and character. A Space saved to **This Browser** has nothing to share yet: **Share** opens on **This Space Is in This Browser**, and **Sync Space** puts it on livehuddle's servers (guest or signed in) and shows the share options in its place.
</AppNote>

## What Happens When They Open It

Someone opening your link for the first time enters the password if you set one, picks the name others will see (a signed-in visitor keeps their account name), and then lands in the <Noun />:

<Figure caption="Everyone in a shared work sees the others' live cursors and a presence stack of who is here.">
  <LiveCursors />
</Figure>

- **Live cursors and selections.** Everyone sees where everyone else is and what they have selected.
- **Simultaneous editing.** On an Editor pass, you both change the <Noun /> at once. To avoid clashes, everything one person has selected, one element or many, is gently locked for the others while they hold it, and for a moment after they let go, until their change is saved.
- **No sign-in required.** A guest can join straight away, the editor never blocks them with a sign-in wall. See [Guest vs account](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Characters, cursors and selections.** Everyone sees where everyone else is standing and pointing, and what they have selected. Walking in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/), each person shows their name and status over their character. A guest walks in straight away.
</AppNote>

## Managing and Ending Access

Back in the Share dialog you can manage links over time:

- **Multiple passes.** Issue several, with different roles. They are listed under **Passes**.
- **Revoke** a pass to cut off access through it. Expiring passes show a countdown, and expired ones move under **Expired**, where they can be extended or deleted. See [Share Link Expiry](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-link-expiry/).
- **Password Protection.** Protect every pass with one shared password; everyone opening a link must enter it first, embeds included. See [Share Passwords](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/).

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments">
  For example, a Viewer pass for stakeholders and an Editor pass for collaborators.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  For example, a Participant pass for the team and an Editor pass for whoever sets up the Space.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments">
  **Approval.** Signed in, the dialog's **Approval** tab asks the people you name to approve the <Noun />. See [Request Approval](/help/collaboration/sharing/request-approval/).
</AppNote>

**Community.** The dialog's **Community** tab, beside **Passes**, publishes the <Noun />, once you're signed in, to the public [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/) gallery. A <Noun /> in the Community can't have a password, and a password-protected one can't be published.

<Tip>
  Want to put the <Noun /> on a web page or in a doc? Each pass can also be copied as an embed (**Copy
  iframe**) or, while no password is set, as a live image that stays up to date. A Participant pass
  embeds look-only; an Editor pass embeds an editable <Noun />. See
  [Embeds](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/).
</Tip>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments">
  A Viewer pass embeds look-only too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  A legacy Viewer pass embeds look-only as well.
</AppNote>
