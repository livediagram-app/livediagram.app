## Everyday Editing

A few shortcuts cover the actions you repeat most:

- **`Cmd/Ctrl+Z`** undoes, and **`Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z`** (or **`Ctrl+Y`**) redoes. The same controls sit in the bottom-right corner.
- **`Cmd/Ctrl+K`** opens [search](/help/search-panel/), which also runs commands.
- **`Delete`** or **`Backspace`** removes the selection.
- **`Escape`** closes an open dialog or clears the selection.
- **`Enter`** confirms a dialog.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  On the canvas:

  - **`Cmd/Ctrl+C`**, **`X`** and **`V`** copy, cut and paste through your system clipboard, and **`Cmd/Ctrl+D`** duplicates the selection. See [Copy and Paste](/help/tips-and-tricks/copy-and-paste/).
  - **Tab** (with the canvas focused) selects the next element, **Shift+Tab** the previous, so you can walk the canvas without the mouse, and Tab past the last element moves on out of the canvas. Selection changes are announced to screen readers.
  - **`Escape`** also cancels an in-progress text edit.
  - In a text or sticky label **`Enter`** starts a new line, and clicking away finishes the edit.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **Double-click** a shape or arrow to edit its text label, or select it and press **Space**, or simply start typing to replace the label. In a shape label **`Enter`** starts a new line; on an arrow label **`Enter`** finishes and **`Shift+Enter`** starts a new line, and in a mind node's label **`Enter`** adds the next node while **`Shift+Enter`** starts a new line.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  **Double-click** a shape or arrow to edit its text label, or select it and press **Space**, or simply start typing to replace the label. In a shape label **`Enter`** starts a new line; on an arrow label **`Enter`** finishes and **`Shift+Enter`** starts a new line.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Double-click** a sticky note or text to edit its label, or select it and press **Space**, or simply start typing to replace the label. **`F6`** jumps between the editor's regions (header, palette, canvas, controls and tabs), and **`Shift+F6`** goes back.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  - **`Cmd+K`** (or **`Cmd+.`**) also finds plans, tabs, cards, settings and help articles. Pick a card and it opens on the tab that shows it. See [Command Palette](/help/tips-and-tricks/command-palette/).
  - Undo puts a moved card back and brings a trashed card back. See [Undo](/help/user-interface/undo/).
  - **`Escape`** also closes an open menu or card, or leaves Zen mode.
  - **`F6`** moves focus between the parts of the screen (the header, the tab's content, the bottom-right controls and the tab bar), and **`Shift+F6`** moves back.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments,liveplanner">
  **`Z`** turns [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) on and off, hiding all the toolbars and panels for distraction-free work.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  **Switching tools.** The canvas tools each have a single-key shortcut, matching the conventions in tools like Figma and Excalidraw. They work while nothing that takes a label is selected; with one such element selected (a shape, a sticky, an arrow), a letter key starts typing its label instead.

  - **`V`**: Select tool (click, shift-click, and marquee-select elements).
  - **`S`**: Search the palette strip for any element to add.
  - **`H`**: Hand tool (drag empty canvas to pan around).
  - **`K`**: Laser pointer (a glowing trail your collaborators see).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  - **`P`**: Freehand (sketch a stroke, kept as drawn).
  - **`F`**: Frame (draw a section backdrop that carries its contents).
  - **`E`**: Eraser (press, or hold and drag, to delete elements).

  The number row works too: **`1`** Select, **`2`** Square, **`5`** Arrow, **`7`** Pencil, **`8`** Text, **`0`** Eraser and more. [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/) has its own keys, shown on each tool in its dock: there **`P`** picks the Path tool, **`F`** the Fill tool, **`1`** to **`3`** pick the pens, **`S`** opens Shapes, and **`K`** does nothing.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  Letters add the common elements: **`T`** Text (a Paragraph), **`R`** Square, **`O`** Circle, **`D`** Diamond, **`N`** Sticky Note, **`F`** Frame and **`A`** Arrow, and **`P`** picks the Pencil. The number row works too: **`1`** Select, **`2`** Square, **`3`** Diamond, **`4`** Circle, **`5`** Arrow, **`8`** Text and **`9`** Image.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">

  - **`E`**: Edit (click, shift-click, and marquee-select elements). **`V`** does the same.
  - **`W`**: Walk, as your character.
  - **`S`**: Search the palette strip for any element to add.

  Some keys add an element: **`T`** Text and **`N`** a sticky note. **`8`** adds Text too.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  **Moving the view.** You will pan and zoom constantly, and you do not need to leave your tool to do it:

  - **Hold Space and drag** to pan from anywhere.
  - **Middle-click and drag** to pan as well (switch this off with **Middle-Mouse Pan** in Settings if you need the button for something else).
  - **`Cmd/Ctrl` + `+`** and **`Cmd/Ctrl` + `-`** zoom in and out, **`Cmd/Ctrl+0`** resets to 100%, and **`Shift+1`** zooms to fit everything on the tab.
  - **Pinch** on a trackpad or touch screen to zoom; the on-screen zoom buttons sit in the bottom-right of the canvas.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **While walking.** In [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) a few keys belong to your character instead:

  - **Arrow keys** walk, and **`Space`** hops and waves a flag.
  - **`Enter`** opens a speech bubble over your head; **`Enter`** again sends it.
  - **`W`** waves, and **`J`**, **`S`**, **`C`** and **`D`** play the other moves; **`1`** to **`5`** throw a reaction around you.
  - **`Shift+3`** turns the 3D View on or off.
  - **Undo** and **Redo** rest while you walk, so a stray key never changes the Space behind you.
  - **`E`**, **`V`** or **`Escape`** stops walking.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **On a board.** Press **Tab** until a card is focused, then:

  - **Arrow keys** move between cards.
  - **Enter** (or **Space**) opens the card.
  - **Shift+Left** and **Shift+Right** move the card to the previous or next column; **Shift+Up** and **Shift+Down** move it within its column.
  - **N** adds a card to the focused card's column.
  - **Delete** moves the card to the Trash, where you can restore it (an Editor's).
  - **Shift+Space** adds the card to a selection, or takes it out. With several selected, **Shift+Left** and **Shift+Right** move them all a column and an Editor's **Delete** trashes them all; **Escape** clears the selection.

  More in [Boards](/help/plan/boards/).

  **On a sheet.** Sheets use the keys you know from other spreadsheets:

  - **Arrow keys** move between cells; hold **Shift** to select a range, and **Cmd** to jump to the edge of the data.
  - **Tab** and **Enter** move right and down (with **Shift**, left and up). Start typing to replace a cell, or press **F2** to edit what's in it.
  - **Delete** or **Backspace** clears the selected cells.
  - **`Cmd+C`**, **`Cmd+X`** and **`Cmd+V`** copy, cut and paste, and **`Cmd+Shift+V`** pastes values only.
  - **`Cmd+B`**, **`Cmd+I`** and **`Cmd+U`** set bold, italic and underline.
  - **`Cmd+D`** and **`Cmd+R`** fill down and right, **`Cmd+F`** finds in the sheet and **`Cmd+Shift+H`** finds and replaces.
  - **`Cmd+;`** enters today's date, and **`Cmd+Shift+;`** the time now.
  - **`Cmd++`**, **`Cmd+-`** and **`Cmd+0`** zoom the sheet in, out and back to 100%.

  More in [Sheets](/help/plan/sheets/).
</AppNote>

</Tip>
