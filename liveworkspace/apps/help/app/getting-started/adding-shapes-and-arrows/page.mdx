## Adding Shapes

The **Palette**, the strip across the top of the editor, holds everything you can add. It starts with the selection mode (Select, Hand and the rest), then the palette's categories by name. Rest the pointer on a category, or click it, and its elements open in a panel underneath. See [where each one lives](/help/palette/tools/) for the full map.

<Step n={2}>

## Connecting With Arrows

The fastest way to draw an arrow is **quick-connect**:

<Figure app="livediagram" caption="Click a selected shape's + to open its options, then drag Arrow onto a second shape to pin an arrow.">
  <QuickConnect />
</Figure>

<Step n={1}>Select a shape. Small **+** buttons appear on its sides.</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Drag a **+** onto another shape, or click it to open its options and drag **Arrow** onto another
  shape. <AppName /> draws an arrow pinned to both, so the arrow follows whenever you move either
  shape. On a phone, tap **Arrow**, then tap the target shape.
</Step>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The options are **Duplicate**, **Arrow**, **Pencil** and **Text**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  The options are **Duplicate**, **Arrow** and **Text**.
</AppNote>

<Step n={3}>
  To draw a free connector instead, press `A` and drag between any two points.
</Step>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  You can also pick **Add Arrow** (in the **Draw** category) to start the same connector.
</AppNote>

**Duplicate** in the same options drops a copy of the shape beside it. Turn on **Quick-Add on Hover** in Settings and the options open as soon as you hover a **+**. See [Quick-Add on Hover](/help/palette/quick-add-on-hover/).

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments">
  The copy comes with no arrow between them, so you can wire it up however you like.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  The copy is connected to the original, the quickest way to grow a process one step at a time.
</AppNote>

## Styling Arrows

**Right-click** an arrow (long-press on touch) to open its formatting:

- **Line**: **Straight**, **Curved** or **Angled** (an L-shaped elbow); the pattern (Solid, Dashed, Dotted, Dash-dot, Long dash or Dash-dot-dot); and the thickness, Thin, Medium, Thick or Extra thick.
- **Pointer**: the arrowhead shape, size, and which ends carry a head.
- **Label:** double-click an arrow to type a label. It sits on the middle of the line, with the line broken open behind it, and wraps onto more lines when it is long. Press **Shift+Enter** for a line break of your own.
- **Pass Behind Boxes:** on by default, so a long arrow crossing an unrelated shape ducks under it instead of drawing a line over its label. Turn it off per arrow from the **Line** section if you want the old always-on-top look.

Drag an arrow's line to bend it where you grabbed it, and use the handles on curved and angled arrows to shape the path exactly. See [Arrow Styles](/help/palette/arrows/arrow-styles/).

<Tip>
  Working in a flow? Drop a shape, label it, quick-connect to the next spot, and repeat. Because a
  new shape can inherit the selected one's size, chained nodes stay tidy without fiddling.
</Tip>
