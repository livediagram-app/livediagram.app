## What You Get as a Guest

As a guest you have the full editing experience. Your new <Noun plural /> start in this browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)), saved automatically, so they are there when you come back on the same device. Sync one, from its Share dialog, and it is saved to <AppName />, owned by a guest identity that liveworkspace gives this browser. The same guest is you in every liveworkspace app.

- **The full editor:** nothing is held back (the account tools below aside).
- **Persistence** for your <Noun plural />, in this browser ([sign in](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/) to sync across devices).
- **Sharing and collaboration:** open share links as an Editor, Participant or Viewer, and mint your own share links so others can join (Share offers **Sync <Noun capital />** first for a <Noun /> in this browser).
- **Real-time presence** on any shared session.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **The full canvas:** every shape, arrow, drawing tool, theme, and template.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
  **The full editor:** every kind of page, every element, theme and template.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">**The full canvas:** every element, theme, and template.</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **Everything in the editor:** boards, sheets, forms, visualisations and dashboards, every template
  and every theme (the account tools below aside).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livehuddle">
  **Your settings** are saved against the same per-browser id. See [Guest
  Identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,liveplanner">
  **Your library** in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/): folders and the <Noun plural />{' '}
  shared with you. See [Guest Identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/).
</AppNote>

The one trade-off: your guest identity lives in your current browser. Clearing your browser data, or switching to another device, means that guest library does not follow you.

## What an Account Adds

Creating a free account (at `/get-started`, with your liveworkspace account, confirmed with a code we email you; see [Signing In](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/)) adds the things that only make sense once you have an identity:

- **Cross-device sync.** Your <Noun plural /> travel with your account, so they appear on every device you sign in to.
- **A persistent identity.** Your display name comes from your profile rather than being set per session.
- **[Teams](/help/collaboration/teams/).** Join or create a team to work in a shared space with teammates.
- **[Email notifications](/help/account-and-data/email-notifications/)** on deployments that send email.
- **Publishing to the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/)**, the public gallery of shared work.
- **[API tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/)** for your own scripts.

## The Sign-In Reminder

While you work as a guest in the editor, a notification at the bottom of the screen reminds you that an account is there, in one line: **Sign in to get Teams, AI & even more powerful features.** It has one button and a close:

- **Sign In** takes you to the sign-in page, and brings you back where you were afterwards.
- **×** dismisses it.

**When it appears.** It waits until you have been in a <Noun /> for about five minutes, so it never greets you the moment you open one, and it stays out of embedded <Noun plural />. The [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) never shows it; there, the **Sign In to Use Teams** card under Spaces is the way in.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments,liveplanner">
  It also stays out of [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/).
</AppNote>

**Dismissing it** hides it in this browser, and it doesn't come back. It is only a reminder: nothing stops working if you ignore it.

It never appears once you are signed in.

## Moving From Guest to Account

You do not lose anything by signing up. When you create your account, or sign in to one, in the same browser, everything you built as a guest **moves into your account** automatically: your <Noun plural />, folders and your Shared With Me list, in every liveworkspace app at once. Your guest settings carry over to a new account; when you sign in to an account that already has settings, the account's settings are kept. The work you have already done carries over rather than being left behind.

<Noun plural capital /> still in **Offline** stay there until you choose to move them: after you
sign in, **Move Offline <Noun plural capital /> to Your Account?** lists them, so you can press
**Move** to bring them across, or **Not Now** to leave them where they are. See [Offline
Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/).

The dialog lists them all ticked: untick any you want to keep in this browser before you press **Move**. **Not Now** is remembered until you make another, and **Sync <Noun capital />** on any of them moves it by hand.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">

</AppNote>

The two paths also coexist forever: a signed-in user can hand a share link to a guest, who edits the same <Noun /> with no account at all.
