This walkthrough takes you from nothing to a small Knowledgebase with a few pages, a link between them and a share link for your team.

## Make a Knowledgebase

<Step n={1}>
  Open [a new Knowledgebase](/new). It is made there and then, called "Untitled Knowledgebase",
  with one page, **Start Here**. There is no template to pick and nothing to fill in first.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  The caret is already in the page's title. Type a title and the page takes it as its name in the
  sidebar, a moment after you stop typing.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  To name the Knowledgebase itself, press its name at the top of the sidebar, type a name and press
  **Enter**. **Escape** leaves the name as it was.
</Step>

<Figure caption="A new Knowledgebase: one page, Start Here, with the caret in its title, and Describe to Set Up above New Page while it is empty.">
  <NewKnowledgebase />
</Figure>

Where it is saved depends on whether you are signed in:

- **Signed in**, it is saved to your account, in **Private**, so it is on every device you sign in on.
- **As a guest**, it is kept **Offline**, in this browser only.

Search and writing work either way. **Verify**, **Ask**, **AI Knowledge** and sharing need the Knowledgebase to be saved to an account. See [Guest vs Account](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/) and [Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/).

## Add Pages

<Step n={1}>
  Press **New Page** at the foot of the sidebar to add a page at the top level. It opens with the
  caret in its title.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  To put a page under another, hover the parent's row and press its **+** (**Add Subpage**). The
  new page is added as the parent's last subpage.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Drag a row to move it, or open its **Page Options** menu for **Move Up**, **Move Down**,
  **Indent** and **Outdent**.
</Step>

Rather build the structure in one go? While the Knowledgebase is still empty, **Describe to Set Up** in the sidebar proposes a whole tree of pages from a description. See [Describe to Set Up](/help/getting-started/describe-to-set-up/). Already have your writing somewhere else? See [Import From a Markdown Folder](/help/knowledgebase/import-a-markdown-folder/).

## Write and Link

Press in a page and write. The formatting controls are in the row above the page; type `/` on an empty line for the list of things you can insert. See [Writing on a Page](/help/knowledgebase/writing-on-a-page/).

To link to another page, type `[[` and start typing its title, then press `Enter` on the page you want. The link follows the page if it is renamed or moved. See [Linking Pages](/help/knowledgebase/linking-pages/).

## Say a Page Is Right

When a page says what you want it to say, press **Verify** in the row under its title. The page shows a tick and your name, and it comes due for review 90 days later unless the Knowledgebase's Review Period says otherwise. See [Owners and Verifying a Page](/help/knowledgebase/owners-and-verifying/).

## Share It

Press **Share** at the right of the top row and choose who can open the Knowledgebase and what they can do: an **Editor** writes and verifies, a **Participant** reads and comments, and a **Viewer** reads. See [Sharing](/help/collaboration/sharing/) and [Roles in a Knowledgebase](/help/knowledgebase/knowledgebase-roles/).

## Take a Tour

Press **Get Started** in the footer for six short tours of liveknowledge, each a few steps on the real controls: **Your First Page**, **Organise Your Knowledgebase**, **Trust a Page**, **Ask Your Knowledgebase**, **Connect an AI Agent** and **Share and Work Together**. A tour you have taken gets a tick, and the button goes once you have taken them all. You only see the tours and steps for what you can do here: a Viewer has no tour of writing a page, and Ask and Connect need you signed in and online. To take one again, switch it back on in **Settings**, **Getting Started**.

## Good to Know

- A Knowledgebase holds up to **500 pages**, nested up to **8 levels** deep.
- A page's name and its title are one thing: renaming the page in the sidebar rewrites its title.
- The three tabs in the footer are the Knowledgebase's views: **Pages**, **Ask** and **AI Knowledge**.
