<AppNote app="livehuddle">

## Moving Around

You will pan and zoom constantly. There are a few ways to do each:

- **Pan** by holding the **Space** bar and dragging, or by **middle-click dragging**. On a trackpad, a two-finger drag pans too.
- **Zoom** with the zoom buttons in the bottom-right of the canvas, `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + scroll, `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `+` and `-`, or pinch-zoom on a trackpad or touch screen. `Shift+1` zooms to fit everything on the tab.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  You can also pan by switching to the **Hand** tool (shortcut `H`) and dragging on empty canvas, and middle-click dragging works from any tool.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  When you let go, the view glides onto the page you are on, so you are never left between pages (see [Working with Pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/#pages)).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  The pattern background tiles endlessly as you move, so you never run out of canvas.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  Space and middle-click panning work from anywhere, and on a phone or tablet a one-finger drag on empty canvas pans.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  On a phone-sized screen the Hand tool is the default, so a drag moves the view; on a larger screen the default is the Select tool.
</AppNote>

See [Pan and Zoom](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/) and [Zoom Controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/).

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  **Select versus Hand.** The selection-mode dropdown at the top of the Palette holds the canvas tools (see [Selection Modes](/help/selection-modes/)). The two you will use most:

  - **Select** (shortcut `V`) is for working with elements: click to select (click again to deselect), shift-click to add to the selection, and drag on empty canvas to draw a marquee that selects everything inside it.
  - **Hand** (shortcut `H`) is for moving the view: drag on empty canvas to scroll around without touching any elements.

  Middle-click panning works from any tool, so you rarely need to leave Select just to move the view a little.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Edit versus Walk.** If you can edit the Space, the palette strip starts with a two-part toggle (see [Edit and Walk](/help/selection-modes/select/)):

  - **Edit** (shortcut `E`) is for working with elements: click to select (click again to deselect), shift-click to add to the selection, and drag on empty canvas to draw a marquee that selects everything inside it.
  - **Walk** (shortcut `W`) puts you in the Space as your character, to walk up to people and talk.

  Panning works the same in both, so you never need to leave Edit just to move the view a little.
</AppNote>

## Around the Canvas

A few things sit over the canvas (see [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/)):

- **Palette**, the strip across the top, adds what you build with. See the [Palette](/help/palette/) guides.
- **Explorer**, behind the menu button in the top-left corner, is your library of <Noun plural /> and folders. See [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
- **Corner panels**, such as the Map, can be dragged by their header to another corner and collapsed to a banner.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The palette adds shapes, arrows, icons, and tools. A **mode switch**, right of the menu button (and at the top of an empty tab's start), chooses whether the tab works in Diagram, Draw or Workshop, whatever is on it. See [Editor Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/). The tool panels are corner panels too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  The palette adds text, shapes, icons, pictures, charts and more.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The palette adds sticky notes, text, furniture, rooms and stickers.
</AppNote>

Around them sit the fixed controls: the **tab bar** along the bottom, with **Tools** (Search and the tools that work on the whole <Noun />), **Settings** and the light/dark switch at its right-hand end, and a cluster in the bottom-right corner with Undo and Redo and the zoom controls. **Right-click menus** carry per-element formatting, and the tab menu carries tab-wide tools.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The tab bar also has **Get Started** (while a tour is on offer). The bottom-right cluster adds the [session tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/) (timer, vote and poll in Workshop), **Tidy** (in Diagram), **Layers** and **Style** (the **Theme & canvas** button).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  **Tools** also holds History. The bottom-right cluster adds the **Theme & canvas** button (labelled **Style** when the buttons show their names).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  The tab bar also has **Get Started** (while a tour is on offer). Each page's title bar has a **Style** button that opens the tab's look.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Share** and your account sit in a card at the top right. The bottom-right cluster adds the **Theme & canvas** button. While you walk, Undo, Redo and Theme & canvas step aside: the corner shows the **3D View** switch, and the call's microphone, camera and screen-share buttons sit in a dock beside it.
</AppNote>

On a phone the layout is the same: the menu button sits in its own card left of the strip, the strip's tiles swipe sideways, and tapping a panel's header collapses it, so the canvas stays readable.

## Light and Dark

The editor has its own appearance switch at the right-hand end of the bottom bar: click it to step through **Light**, **Dark** and **System** (which follows your device). This recolours the editor's interface around your canvas, and the canvas too: every [theme](/help/canvas/themes/) has a light and a dark side, and you see the one that matches your appearance. The theme itself, which recolours the canvas content, is set from the theme button. See [Dark Mode](/help/tools/dark-mode/).

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The theme button is the **Style** (Theme & canvas) button in the bottom-right corner.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livehuddle">
  The theme button is **Theme & canvas**.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  The theme button is the **Style** button on a page's title bar.
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  Lost on a big canvas? Zoom out with the bottom-right controls to get your bearings, then zoom back
  in on the part you need.
</Tip>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  For total focus, try [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) to hide all the toolbars and panels.
</AppNote>
