Watch It Built is the tour of [Diagram mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/). Instead of pointing at the controls one by one, a cursor called **Example** shows you how something is done, on your own canvas: you pick a topic, and it builds, styles or tidies with the editor's real palette, toolbar, menus and dialogs, so what you see is exactly what you will press. Each topic takes 10 to 25 seconds, and nothing it does is saved. (Until November 2026 it was one replay, and before that a stepped Diagram Tour and the Welcome Tour.)

## Starting It

It never starts or pops up on its own. On a Diagram tab you can edit, press **Get Started** (the play button) in the bar along the bottom of the editor, first of the buttons at its right-hand end, left of **Tools**. On a phone the button shows only its play icon. It doesn't appear on a view-only canvas, on a locked tab, in an embed, for someone taking part rather than editing, or while the tab's **Start Diagramming** templates are open.

A press opens the **Get Started** panel above the button, "Quick tutorials to get you going.", listing the Diagram topics, each with its length (on a phone it rises from the foot of the screen). Press one to watch it. A topic you've watched shows a **Done** tick, and you can press it to watch it again. The close button, **Esc** or a click outside closes the panel and leaves the button where it is.

The button stays until you've watched every Diagram topic. After that it offers another tour you haven't taken, and you can bring it back from Settings (see below).

## The Topics

| Topic             | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **The Basics**    | Drops two shapes from the palette, joins them with an arrow from the ring round the first, names one "Check stock", then right-clicks it and picks the **Success** colour from the element menu. |
| **Look and Feel** | Opens **Style** in the bottom-right corner, picks two looks for the whole tab in turn, then a new canvas background. Your tab keeps its own look when it ends.                                   |
| **Style a Shape** | Right-clicks a shape and goes through the element menu's **Appearance**: a thicker, dashed **Border**, a **Shadow**, then a handwritten **Font**.                                                |
| **Find Anything** | Opens **Search**, types "database" and drags the result onto the canvas, then does the same for "decision".                                                                                      |
| **Tidy**          | Five joined steps land a little off, then **Tidy** in the bottom-right corner and its **Smart** Auto Layout glide them into a clean flow.                                                        |

## While It Plays

- **Your work is safe.** Your own elements fade back while Example works, and come back exactly as they were when it ends. Nothing Example draws or changes is saved, sent to anyone you're working with, or added to undo. A look or layout it shows is never applied to your tab.
- **Panels it opens close again.** Example opens the editor's real menus and panels with their own buttons, and closes each one as it moves on.
- **The player** shows Example's line for the chapter, a **Pause** / **Play** button (**Replay** once the topic has played through), the topic's chapters with a progress line each, and **Your Turn**. Press a chapter's name to watch it again from its start.
- **Keys**: **Space** pauses and plays, the **left** and **right arrow** keys step between chapters, and **Esc** ends it.
- **Your Turn** ends it, hands the canvas back to you and opens the **Get Started** list again, so you can pick the next topic or close it. Once a topic has played through and there are more, **More Topics** beside it does the same. **Esc**, or switching to another tab or mode, ends it and closes everything.
- **Reduced motion**: if your system or the editor's **Reduce Motion** setting asks for less movement, nothing glides. Each chapter shows how it ends, and the play button reads **Next** to step to the next one (and **Done** on the last).
- **On a phone** Example is a soft touch dot instead of an arrow, and the lines talk about taps and long-presses instead of clicks.

## Watching Again

Every topic stays in the panel with its **Done** tick, so as long as the button is there you can watch any of them again. Once you've watched them all, bring the button back from Settings:

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Getting Started**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Open **Watch It Built in Diagram** and switch **Watch It Built** back on to offer every Diagram
  topic again, or switch on just the topics you want under it.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Close Settings. In Diagram mode the topic list opens straight away; otherwise the **Get Started**
  button is there the next time you work in Diagram.
</Step>

Draw and Workshop have topics of their own, on the same button: see [Watch It Built in Draw](/help/canvas/draw-mode/draw-tour/) and [Watch It Built in Workshop](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/workshop-tour/). When you have a complex element selected, such as a Sankey diagram or a flowchart step, its own tour is on offer too: **Get Started** then lists every tour on offer, the element's first (**Sankey Tour**, for example), with **Watch It Built** below it while you still have topics to watch.
