## Moving Around

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign,livehuddle">
  **Reaching the canvas.** The canvas is a focus stop in the normal `Tab` order, labelled
  **"Canvas"**. Tab to it from the page and you'll see a focus ring (only when navigating by
  keyboard, so mouse users see nothing new). The [Palette](/help/tips-and-tricks/command-palette/)
  strip and the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) behind the menu button stay reachable by
  the keyboard too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  The panels such as the [Map](/help/user-interface/minimap/) are reachable by the keyboard as well.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
  The panels such as [Contents](/help/pages/working-with-pages/#contents) are reachable by the
  keyboard as well.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  Because `Tab` on the canvas walks its elements, **`F6`** jumps between the editor's regions
  instead: the header controls (Share and your account), the palette strip, the canvas, the
  bottom-right controls and the tab bar, in screen order, and round again. **`Shift`+`F6`** goes
  back the other way. A region that isn't on screen is skipped, and while a dialog is open F6 stays
  inside it.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign,livehuddle">
  **Walking the elements.** Once focus is on the canvas:

- **`Tab`** selects the **next** element, **`Shift`+`Tab`** the **previous**, in the order they're stacked on the canvas. The canvas scrolls each selected element into view as you go.
- There's **no wrap and no trap**: pressing `Tab` past the last element (or `Shift`+`Tab` before the first) moves focus out of the canvas to the next thing on the page, so you can always keyboard your way onward.
- The **selection ring is the focus indicator**: there's one highlighted element, not two competing outlines.
- Elements **locked by someone else** are skipped, the same as when you click.

This works even in read-only views, since it's just navigation. It is part of the editor's keyboard shortcuts, so it pauses while a dialog is open, and it is off if you have switched **Keyboard Shortcuts** off in Settings.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Elements on a hidden or locked [layer](/help/canvas/layers/) are skipped too (arrows are selected
  without scrolling). On a [mind map](/help/palette/mind-maps/) node or a flowchart step you
  selected yourself, `Tab` grows it instead (a child node, or the next step), and `Enter` adds a
  sibling. One you reached by `Tab` is walked past, so `Tab` never traps you on it. Walking also
  pauses while a slide deck is presenting.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **Getting round the editor.** Every button, tab and field is in the normal **Tab** order, with a focus ring when you navigate by keyboard (mouse users see nothing new). To skip a long run of controls, press **`F6`** to jump to the next part of the screen (the header, the work in the middle, the bottom-right controls and the tab bar) and **`Shift+F6`** to go back. Open dialogs keep focus inside them until you close them with **Escape**.

**`Cmd+K`** (**`Ctrl+K`** on Windows and Linux) opens [search](/help/search-panel/), which reaches plans, tabs, cards, settings and commands from the keyboard.

**Working a board.** Tab to a card on a board, then use the **arrow keys** to move between cards. **Enter** (or **Space**) opens the card's panel, **Shift** with the arrow keys moves the card to another column or up and down its column, **N** adds a card to its column, and **Delete** moves it to the Trash. **Shift+Space** adds the focused card to a selection (or takes it out), so **Shift+Left** and **Shift+Right** move every selected card a column at once; **Escape** clears the selection.

Screen readers hear every move, for example "#12 Fix login moved to In Progress, position 4 of 4", and when a card goes to the Trash. On a sorted board, **Shift+Up** and **Shift+Down** move nothing and say why. Viewers can move between and open cards, but not change them; Participants can add and move cards, and moving one to the Trash is an Editor's. See [Boards](/help/plan/boards/).

**Working a sheet.** A sheet takes the keys of any spreadsheet: the arrow keys move between cells, **Shift** with them selects, **Enter** and **Tab** move down and across, typing replaces a cell, and **F2** edits it. **Alt+Down** opens a cell's dropdown, and the **menu key** (or **Shift+F10**) opens the cell's menu. See [Sheets](/help/plan/sheets/).

</AppNote>

## Hearing What Changes

Every element carries a name, so a screen reader can describe it. A polite live region announces changes as they happen. These announcements are separate from the on-screen notifications, so you'll hear them even if you've turned notifications off. **Undo** and **Redo** are announced too (`Undid the last change`, `Redid the last change`).

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">
  Names read like `Square "Login"`, `Sticky note`, or `Arrow "yes"`.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments">
- **Selection:** `Selected 'Login'` for a labelled element (or `Selected a Square` when it has no label; tables and images are always named by their type). An arrow is named by its type, its label and the labelled elements it joins, such as `Selected an Arrow 'yes' from 'Login' to 'Home'`. Several selected give a short summary (`Selected 2 Squares & an Arrow`), and clearing the selection says `Selection cleared`.
- **Editing:** deleting an element (`Deleted 'Login'`) is announced too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
- **Selection:** `Selected 'Login'` for a labelled element (or `Selected a Square` when it has no label; arrows, tables and images are always named by their type, such as `Selected an Arrow`), a short summary when several are selected (`Selected 2 Squares & an Arrow`), or `Selection cleared`.
- **Editing:** deleting an element (`Deleted 'Login'`) is announced too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  Names read like `Sticky note "Agenda"`, `Text`, or `Image`.

- **Selection:** `Selected 'Agenda'` for a labelled element (or `Selected a Sticky note` when it has no label; images are always named by their type, such as `Selected an Image`), a short summary when several are selected (`Selected 2 Sticky notes & an Image`), or `Selection cleared`.
- **Editing:** deleting an element (`Deleted 'Agenda'`) is announced too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **Mode:** switching editor mode with `Shift`+`D` announces the new one, for example `Draw mode`.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  Pictures carry the words a screen reader says for them too. Right-click an image and fill in **Alt
  Text**; it goes with the picture into SVG, PowerPoint and Word exports. See [Alt
  text](/help/palette/tools/images/#adding-an-image).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **Undo** and **Redo** (**`Cmd+Z`** and **`Cmd+Shift+Z`**) work from anywhere in the editor.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign,livehuddle">
  **Editing from the keyboard.** With an element selected by keyboard, the usual keyboard verbs
  apply: arrow keys nudge it, `Delete` / `Backspace` removes it, `Space` edits its label, and
  `⌘`/`Ctrl`+`Z` undoes. The editing verbs are disabled in read-only views and while you're typing
  in a field. `⌘`/`Ctrl`+`K` opens [search](/help/search-panel/), which reaches <Noun plural />,
  tabs, elements and commands from the keyboard. See [Keyboard
  Essentials](/help/getting-started/keyboard-essentials/) and the full [keyboard
  shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) reference.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The editing verbs are also disabled while you walk as your character.
</AppNote>

## Reducing Motion

If animation is uncomfortable, turn on **Reduce Motion** in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), under **Accessibility**. It turns off the editor's decorative animations and transitions so panels and popovers appear instantly instead of sliding or popping. Your device's own reduce-motion preference is always respected as well, and the setting follows you to your other devices.

## Larger Controls

If the panels and buttons are too small to read or hit comfortably, **UI Scale** in Settings, under **Appearance**, makes the panels, the toolbar and the corner buttons smaller or bigger (80% to 120%), with a separate scale for each underneath (the toolbar's goes up to 140%). Desktop only.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  On a Sheet tab, the zoom controls in the bottom-right corner make the cells themselves bigger, from 50% to 200%.

**Turning shortcuts off.** If you dictate into your browser, or another tool needs the keys, switch off **Keyboard Shortcuts** in Settings, under **Keyboard**. It is saved on this device only.

</AppNote>
