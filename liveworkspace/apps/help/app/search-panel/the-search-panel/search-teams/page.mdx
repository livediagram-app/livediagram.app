When you are signed in and belong to one or more [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/), the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/) reaches into them as well, so team work is as findable as your own.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  The panel lists your teams in a **Teams** group, matched by name. Picking one opens that team's library in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/team-spaces/). Your teams come from your sign-in, so a team you joined a moment ago in another liveworkspace app appears once your sign-in next refreshes.
</AppNote>

## What the Teams group matches

A **Teams** group lists the teams themselves, by name. Picking one opens that team's page in the Explorer.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  It works the same from the editor and from the Explorer page.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The group shows up to 8 matches, and with an empty box it lists your teams, so you can open search and arrow straight to one. The team's page is the [Explorer](/help/explorer/team-spaces/)'s.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  In the editor the group shows up to 8 matches.
</AppNote>

## Team folders and work

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  A team's work is found alongside your own rather than in a group of its own:

  - **In the editor**, a team's <Noun plural /> are in the **<Noun plural capital />** group with yours. Team folders are not searched from the editor.
  - **On the Explorer page**, a team's <Noun plural /> are among the **Items**, and its folders are in the **Folders** group with an **"in &lt;team&gt;"** suffix, so when a name appears in more than one place you can tell which team it belongs to. Picking a team folder opens the team page with that folder already open.

  Each group lists up to 8 matches.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  See [Finding Designs and Folders](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-designs/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  See [Finding Your Work and Folders](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-documents/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  A team's canvases are not in the Teams group, and not in **Canvases** either, which is for your own canvases. Once you type, they appear in the **Every App** group with livediagram's logo, alongside canvases shared with you and work in the other liveworkspace apps. Picking one opens the canvas. See [Finding Your Work and Folders](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-documents/).

  The editor's search does not list folders. **Search** on the Explorer page does: its **Folders** group includes your teams' folders, each tagged **in &lt;team&gt;**, and picking one opens the team's page with that folder open.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **In the editor,** a team's spaces are not listed under Teams: they are in the **Spaces** group, beside your own. See [Finding Your Work and Folders](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-documents/).

  **On the Explorer page,** a team's work is spread across the groups it belongs in: **Teams** matches your teams by name; **Folders** lists team folders beside your own, each marked **"in &lt;team&gt;"** so you can tell which team it belongs to at a glance, and picking one opens the team's page with that folder already open; **Items** finds a team's spaces by name, with everything else you can open. Each group lists up to 8 matches, so type a little more of a name to narrow a long list. Folders are read as the panel opens, so a folder made while it is open shows the next time you open it.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  On the Explorer page, the search panel's **Folders** group also finds your teams' folders, and its **Items** group finds the plans in their libraries. In the editor, a team's plans that the Plans group misses show under **Every App** once you type. The search field at the top of the [Explorer page](/help/explorer/explorer-page/#searching) searches every library you can reach too.
</AppNote>

## Signed-in only

Teams require an account, so team results only appear when you are signed in with teams to search. Guests, and signed-in users with no teams, simply will not see them. The rest of search works the same either way.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  <Tip>
    Searching is often faster than expanding the Teams tree in the Explorer: type a team or folder
    name and jump straight in.
  </Tip>
</AppNote>
