## Opening and closing search

- **Keyboard:** press `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `K`, or `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `.`. Both open the same panel. (`K` is the usual command-palette shortcut; `.` is the original binding, kept because browsers reserve `Cmd` + `T` for a new browser tab.)
- **Button:** open **Tools** and choose **Search…**, its first row.
- **Explorer panel menu:** the Explorer panel's **⋯** menu also has a **Search** row.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments,livehuddle,liveplanner">
  The shortcuts work in the editor and on the Explorer page alike. While you are typing in a text box (such as the Explorer's filter box) they are left to that box; in the editor they are off when you turn off keyboard shortcuts in Settings.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments,liveplanner">
  They also work in [zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) and in view-only <Noun plural />, and are left alone while a dialog is open.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  On the Explorer page the keyboard shortcuts setting does not turn them off.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The shortcuts work in [zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) and in view-only canvases. While you are typing in a text box they are left to that box, and they are off while a slide deck is running and when you turn off keyboard shortcuts in Settings.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  They also work in view-only spaces.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  **Button:** in the editor, **Tools** is near the right-hand end of the bottom bar. On the Explorer page the **Search** button is in the bottom bar. On a phone each button is its icon alone.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **Button:** **Tools** is near the right-hand end of the bottom bar. The Explorer page's bottom bar has Tools too, with Search in it. See [Quick Controls](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **Button:** click **Tools** in the bottom bar, then **Search…**, its first row. On a phone the Tools button is its toolbox icon alone.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  **Button:** in the editor, open **Tools** in the bottom bar and choose **Search…**, the first row. On the Explorer page, click **Search** in the bottom bar, just before Settings. On a phone it is the magnifier alone.
</AppNote>

The panel pops up near the top of the screen and blurs what is behind it so you can focus on the query. Press `Esc`, click the **Esc** chip at the end of the search box, or click outside the panel to close it.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  On a touch screen the chip is a **×**.
</AppNote>

## What search covers

Type a few letters and matches appear, grouped by what they are. Matching is a simple, case-insensitive "contains" match on the name, so `flow` finds "Checkout flow" and "Workflow". What is in scope depends on where you open it.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  In plans, `plan` finds "Sprint plan" and "Planning".
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Actions, settings and help articles also match on related words, and when you type several words each one only has to appear somewhere, so "export png" finds **Export…**.
</AppNote>

## Beyond navigation

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  In the editor the panel does more than jump around:

  - **[Add to the canvas](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/):** search the palette and drop a shape or icon straight onto the canvas.
  - **[Create a new tab](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-create-tab/):** an action result that spins up a fresh tab.
  - **[Run any command](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/):** type an action's name (undo, export, settings) and run it. The panel doubles as a command palette on `⌘K`.

  These only appear once you have typed something, and only inside a <Noun />.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments">
  Commands include auto layout, and you can also pick up a canvas tool.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  Commands include auto-align, and you can also switch between Edit and Walk.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  In the editor the panel does more than jump around:

  - **[Create a new tab](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-create-tab/):** an action result that adds a fresh tab and opens Start Planning on it.
  - **[Run a command](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/):** type an action's name (undo, rename, export, settings) and run it. The panel doubles as a command palette on `⌘K`.

  These only appear once you have typed something, and only inside a plan.
</AppNote>

Two more groups help you find your way:

- **Jump to a setting:** a **Settings** group matches the rows of the [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) dialog, by name and by the words people use for them, so "dark mode" finds **Theme**. Each result says which category it lives in ("in Appearance"), and picking it opens Settings on that row with the row highlighted.
- **Find the help for it:** a **Help** group lists matching articles from this help centre, so the explanation of a feature is reachable from the same box as the feature. Articles match on their search synonyms too. Picking one opens the article in a new browser tab, so you do not lose your place.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  On the Explorer page, signed in, the Settings group lists the sections of your account's Settings and each app's settings instead, and the Help group offers the Explorer's own articles.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  In the editor, "transparency" finds Opacity.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  On the Explorer page, signed in, the Settings group matches the sections of Settings instead (an **Account Settings** section, or an app's own, such as **liveplanner Settings**) and opens Settings there. "kanban" finds Boards.
</AppNote>

## Picking a result

Results are ordered by group, and the very first match is preselected, so for the common case you can just type and press `Enter`. Otherwise click a row, or press the `↑` and `↓` arrow keys to move the highlight and `Enter` to pick it. Picking a result takes you exactly where it points. The panel closes as it goes.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  That means opening a <Noun /> or folder, switching to a tab, selecting an element, or jumping to a team.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  That means opening a canvas, switching to a tab, selecting an element, or opening a team's page in the Explorer.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  That means opening a plan or folder, switching to a tab, or jumping to a team.
</AppNote>

## Read-only views

Search only navigates, so it works in a view-only <Noun /> too: you can still find and jump to tabs. The command palette keeps only the actions that do not change the <Noun />.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livediagram,livedocuments,livehuddle">
  You can still find and jump to elements. **Add to canvas** results are not offered there. See [The Command Palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/#in-read-only-views).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  See [The Command Palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/#in-read-only-views).
</AppNote>
