## First, Find Them

1. **Check the right tab.** Each tab stores its own content. If a tab looks empty, confirm you are on the tab you actually edited. See [tabs](/help/tabs/using-tabs/).
2. **Check the right <Noun />.** It is easy to have two similar <Noun plural /> open. Open [your <Noun plural />](/explorer/recent) and confirm you are in the one you edited.
3. **Check the right identity.** Guest work is tied to the browser that made it. If you switched browsers or devices, you are looking at a different guest identity's <Noun plural />. If you just signed in, your guest <Noun plural /> move into your account; if they have not appeared, reload, and if they still do not show, [contact us](/help/contact/) with the report from **Copy Diagnostics**. See [working as a guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).
4. **Reload the page.** A refresh reloads your saved <Noun /> and clears any temporary display issue.

## How Saving Works

Changes are saved automatically after you make them, per tab: to the server, not just to your browser. There is no manual "save" to forget. Because saving is per tab, editing one tab never disturbs another, and an empty, never-loaded tab is prevented from overwriting a tab that still has content.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle">
  A <Noun /> kept [Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) is saved to this browser's
  storage rather than the server.
</AppNote>

There is no "saved" indicator to watch, because saving is the normal state. What you will see is a failure: if a save cannot reach the server, a message at the bottom of the screen says **Couldn't save your changes. Check your connection.** (If your browser is offline it says **You're offline. Keep this tab open to save when you reconnect.**) Autosave keeps trying, so reconnect and leave the tab open until your changes go through. Other messages mean retrying will not help on its own:

- **Your sign-in has lapsed**: sign in again to keep the changes.
- **You can no longer edit this**: the share link was revoked or you were removed from the team, so autosave has stopped. Export a copy of the tab to keep your recent work.

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livehuddle">
  - **This tab is too big to save**: the connection is fine, but the tab holds more than one tab can
  store. Move some of it to another tab and it saves again.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,liveplanner">
  While autosave keeps trying, a banner at the top of the screen says so.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Changes to cards and Sheet cells made without a connection wait in this browser instead, even
  through a reload, and go as soon as you are back: the banner at the top counts them while they
  wait. If one of them can no longer be made (someone deleted that card meanwhile), it is undone
  with a message, and the rest still save. One kind is never undone: if deleting cards or moving
  them to the Trash would take too many cards off a board at once, or empty it, the server pauses
  it, the cards stay hidden on your screen, and a banner at the top says how many cards were about
  to be removed. Nothing is gone yet: open the list on the banner to see which cards, choose **Keep
  Cards** to bring them back, or choose the Remove button, which names the count (Remove 11 Cards),
  to go ahead.
</AppNote>

**"A new version of <AppName /> is ready".** When <AppName /> is updated while you have a <Noun /> open, and the update changes how <Noun plural /> are stored, a small notice at the bottom of the screen asks you to reload. Nothing happens until you choose: **Reload** first waits until your latest changes are saved, then reloads, and if they cannot be saved yet it tells you so and stays put. 1. **Press Undo** (**Cmd/Ctrl+Z**, or the undo button at the bottom-right) to step back your most recent changes, one at a time.
2. **Press Redo** (**Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z**, or **Ctrl+Y**) if you went back too far.
3. Undo covers your own recent steps in this session. It is cleared when you reload, and it never reaches into someone else's edits.

A deleted <Noun /> is not gone either: it waits in the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/) for 30 days until you restore it. A <Noun /> left empty for 30 days moves there on its own, so look there too.

If an AI tool or a script changed the <Noun />, the note that appears with its change has an **Undo** that puts it back, separately from your own undo history.

<AppNote app="livedesign">

  open **Tools** in the bottom bar and press **History** (signed in, on a design saved to your
  account). It shows who changed what and when and, for an Editor of the whole design, its
  [snapshots](/help/collaboration/snapshots/): **Revert** to one to put the design back as it was
  then. Take a snapshot before a big change so there is one to go back to.
</AppNote>

  undo note.
</AppNote>

## What Collaboration Does and Doesn't Protect

**Two people editing different things on the same tab is safe.** Edits travel as changes to individual elements and merge one by one, so your move of one item cannot overwrite somebody's edit to another. That was not always true, and it is the single most common shape of "my change vanished".

**Two people editing the _same_ thing is the case to know about.** There the last edit to arrive wins. In practice the editor heads this off by showing something as locked while another editor has it selected (every element of a multi-selection, and for a moment after they let go, until their change is saved), so you can see that it is spoken for before you start.

If a connection drops, the editor **catches up on reconnect** rather than reloading: it asks for what it missed and applies it. Your viewport, your selection and your undo history survive, so a wobbly connection no longer costs you your place. If it has been away too long to catch up, it re-fetches the <Noun />, still without a page reload.

If you have checked the right tab, <Noun />, and identity, reloaded, and the change is still missing, [contact us](/help/contact/) with the <Noun /> URL and roughly when you made the edit.
