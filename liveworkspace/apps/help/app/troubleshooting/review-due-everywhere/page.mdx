Yesterday the tree was full of ticks. Today most rows read **Due** and the pages wear an amber **Review Due** pill. Nothing has happened to the pages: the Knowledgebase's **Review Period** was made shorter.

## Why It Happens

A page is Review Due when the Review Period has passed since it was verified, and that is worked out each time the page is shown. Shorten the period from **Every 180 Days** to **Every 30 Days** and every page verified more than 30 days ago is due at once. No page was changed, and no verification was lost.

## Check the Period

Open any page's [Page Health](/help/knowledgebase/page-health/) card and read **Review Period**. If it is shorter than you expected, an Editor changed it.

## Put It Back

<Step n={1}>
  As an Editor, press **Change** under **Review Period** on the Page Health card to open
  **Knowledgebase Settings**.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Choose the period you had before. A longer period applies at once, and the pages that were only
  due under the shorter one go back to Verified.
</Step>

If you made the change a moment ago, the toast that confirmed it has **Undo**, which does the same.

## Or Keep the Shorter Period

If the shorter period is what the team wants, the pages really are due under it.

- Each owner sees their own under **Yours to Review** on [Home](/help/getting-started/knowledgebase-home/), oldest first, with **Still Right** to verify one without leaving Home.
- An Editor can press **Remind the Owners** in [Knowledge Health](/help/knowledgebase/knowledge-health-and-reminders/) to email every owner their list.
- Owners are emailed once for each review that came due, not once more because the period changed.

## Pages That Should Never Age

For a Knowledgebase whose pages do not go stale (a glossary, an archive), choose **Never**. No page is then Review Due, and no review email is sent.

## Still Wrong?

- A page shows **Review Due** though it was verified this week: check the date under **Last Verified** on its Page Health card. Verifying an older copy, or a page with a similar name, is the usual cause.
- You cannot see **Change**: only an Editor of the whole Knowledgebase can change the period. See [Roles in a Knowledgebase](/help/knowledgebase/knowledgebase-roles/).
