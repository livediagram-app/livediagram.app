## Sign-In Checklist

1. **Check the email code.** Sign-in uses a 6-digit code sent to your email from liveworkspace. A code lasts 10 minutes and allows three tries. Look in spam, and ask for a fresh code on the sign-in page if the first is slow, has expired or has run out of tries.
2. **Use the same email** you originally signed up with. A new email makes a new, empty account.
3. **Signed up with Google?** Choose **Continue with Google** on the sign-in page. Signing in with Google on an address that already has an account joins that account, as long as Google has verified the address.
4. **Start again from <AppName />.** If the sign-in page says the sign-in had expired or did not finish, choose **Try Again**. This happens when a sign-in sat open for more than ten minutes, or was started in another tab.
5. **Allow cookies for both sites.** Signing in passes between the app's own address and liveworkspace.app in the same tab. A browser set to block all cookies, or a privacy extension, can stop it finishing. Try an incognito window with extensions off.

If sign-in still will not complete after the checklist above, [contact us](/help/contact/) with the email address you are using and the error you see. Keep working as a guest in the meantime, your <Noun plural /> are safe.
