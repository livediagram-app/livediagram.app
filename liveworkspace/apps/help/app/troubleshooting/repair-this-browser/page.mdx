## What a Repair Clears

- Settings kept only in this browser, such as panel positions and light or dark appearance. Preferences saved to your account, such as panel opacity, come back on the next load.
- Tips and banners you dismissed in this browser, which may show again once.
- The cached list of recent <Noun plural />, which reloads from the server.
- Saved share-link passwords, so a protected link asks for its password again.

## What a Repair Always Keeps

- **Your <Noun plural />.** <Noun plural capital /> stored on the server stay there, and a repair never touches them.
- **Your guest identity.** If you use <AppName /> without an account, the <Noun plural /> you made belong to an identity saved in this browser. A repair keeps it, so your <Noun plural /> stay in your list.
- **<Noun plural capital /> in This Browser.** These exist only in this browser, so a repair leaves them alone.
- **Your sign-in.** A repair never signs you out.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  **Changes waiting for the connection.** Card and Sheet changes you made while offline that have not reached liveplanner yet are kept, and still save when you reconnect.
</AppNote>

## Copy Diagnostics

**Copy Diagnostics** copies a short report about this browser: whether storage and cookies work, whether the browser's database opens, and whether the browser is up to date. Paste it into a message to [support](/help/contact/) so we can see what is going on. It never includes what is in your <Noun plural />, and it carries only the start of a guest id. From the editor it also includes the <Noun />'s address and, if you are signed in, your account id.

When a <Noun /> fails to load, the editor offers the same two buttons on its error screen while you are online, and its report also says how far the load got.
