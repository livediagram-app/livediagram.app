## Nothing Was Imported

| What the dialog says           | Why                                                     | What to do                                                         |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **This Archive Is Too Large**  | It is over 200 MB                                       | Split it into smaller folders and import them one at a time        |
| **Too Many Pages**             | A Knowledgebase holds 500 pages, counted with the ones already there | Import fewer pages, or import into a new Knowledgebase  |
| **No Pages Found**             | The archive holds no pages of that source's kind        | Check it is the right export, then **Choose Another**              |

A Confluence export that holds no HTML pages says "Export the space as HTML and try again."

## Pages Left Out at the Review

The review lists, under **Left Out or Changed**, what will not come across. Nothing is imported until you press Import, so this is the time to fix the archive and choose it again.

| Group                          | What happens                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Pages Could Not Be Read**    | Left out. Links to them stay as they were written                  |
| **Pages Over 5 MB**            | Left out. Links to them stay as they were written                  |
| **Files Outside the Archive**  | Left out: their paths lead outside it                              |
| **Files Not Used**             | No page shows or links to them, so they are left out               |
| **Pages Moved Up**             | Kept. Pages nest 8 levels deep, so deeper ones sit at the last level |
| **Pages With a Title Already Used** | Both kept. A link written by title goes to the first          |

## Pages That Failed While Importing

At the end, the dialog says "318 of 320 Pages Imported" and names each page that failed, with why:

- **It is larger than a page may be.** Split the file into two, and import it again.
- **It could not be saved.** The connection or the Knowledgebase stopped answering. Import that page again, or paste it in with **Import**, then **Into This Page**.
- **The Knowledgebase filled up** while the import ran. Make room, then import the rest.

A failed page's subpages land under the page above it, so nothing below it is lost.

If saving stops answering altogether (the connection drops), the import stops rather than pile up pages that are not saved, and says so. What was added keeps saving.

## Pictures and Attachments

- **Pictures Not Found:** the archive does not hold them. Each page keeps the picture's description.
- **Pictures Over 10 MB:** left out. Each page keeps the picture's description.
- A picture that could not be uploaded is named at the end. Its page keeps its description: add the picture to the page by hand.
- **Attachments Named:** files other than pictures are not stored. Each is listed by name at the foot of its page, under **Attachments**.

## Bring the Rest In

Import only the pages that were left out, **Under a Page of This Knowledgebase**: zip them on their own, then import that. The pages already in are not touched.
