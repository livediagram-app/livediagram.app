## Pair a Workbench Once

A workbench opens your <Noun plural /> through your [CLI](/help/developers/cli/) sign-in, and only after you have allowed it. The first time, the tool asks you to approve it, or you can pair it yourself:

<AppNote app="livediagram">

```bash
livediagram workbench pair --origin https://127.0.0.1:5175 --name "Acme Editor"
```

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">

```bash
liveplanner workbench pair --origin https://127.0.0.1:5175 --name "Acme Editor"
```

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">

```bash
livedocuments workbench pair --origin https://127.0.0.1:5175 --name "Acme Editor"
```

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">

```bash
livedesign workbench pair --origin https://127.0.0.1:5175 --name "Acme Editor"
```

</AppNote>

The command prints a link and, in a terminal, opens it in your browser: a page headed **Allow this workbench?**, asking **Allow Acme Editor at `https://127.0.0.1:5175` to open your <Noun plural /> with the token <AppName /> CLI?** Check the address: it is what <AppName /> enforces, while the name is only a label. Choose **Allow**, and the command finishes by itself. **Don't allow** pairs nothing.

Pairing belongs to one CLI sign-in and one address. If someone obtained your token, it would still open nothing in a tool you never allowed.

## Open Your Whole Workspace

A workbench can also keep your Explorer beside your code: Home, your folders, your teams' <Noun plural />, the Inbox, Recent and the Trash, with every <Noun /> opening in the same view and a **Back** to where you were. **New <Noun capital />** makes the <Noun /> right there. Your account stays out of it: no Settings, no API tokens, no team administration.

The whole workspace is a second approval, even for a tool you already paired:

<AppNote app="livediagram">

```bash
livediagram workbench pair --origin https://127.0.0.1:5175 --name "Acme Editor" --workspace
livediagram folder ls
livediagram workbench open --workspace --folder Minigames --origin https://127.0.0.1:5175 --json
```

The page asks **Allow Acme Editor at `https://127.0.0.1:5175` to open your whole workspace in livediagram, and to create, move, share and trash canvases and folders in it, with the token livediagram CLI?** A token made read-only gives a workspace that browses and reads. `livediagram folder create <name> [--in <folder>]` makes a folder for a project and prints its id.

</AppNote>

## Talk to Your Agent About a Selection

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedocuments,livedesign">

Select elements, then type your message. The workbench adds a short block to it, naming the <Noun />, the tab and each selected element, for example:

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">

Select something on the tab, such as a board or a sheet, then type your message. The workbench adds a short block to it, naming the plan, the tab and what you selected, for example:

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livediagram">

```text
[livediagram] "Home screen" › tab "Wireframe" (doc 3h9x2a, tab 0b34, rev 41)
selected: button 146b "Play", frame e4a8 "Game grid"
read: livediagram tab view 3h9x2a --tab 0b34 --view show --ref 146b
```

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">

```text
[liveplanner] "Sprint 14" › tab "Board" (project 4cd7082b-…, tab 8bf8, rev 41)
selected: plan-board 0933
read: liveplanner tab view 4cd7082b-… --tab 8bf8 --view show --ref 0933
```

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">

```text

```

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">


</AppNote>

## Stop or Unpair

<AppNote app="livediagram">

A workbench session lasts a working day and renews itself while the tool is open. To stop a tool for good, go to **Settings**, then **Developers**, **API Tokens**: it lists your **Paired workbenches**, each with what it may open (**One canvas at a time** or **Whole workspace**), and **Unpair** (which asks first) turns their open diagrams read-only straight away, until you allow the tool again. Revoking the token does the same for every tool it paired.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">

A workbench session lasts a working day and renews itself while the tool is open. To stop a tool for good, go to **Settings**, then **Developers**, **API Tokens**: **Paired workbenches** lists each tool by name and address, with what it may open (**One plan at a time** or **Whole workspace**), and **Unpair** turns its open plans read-only straight away. Revoking the token, in the same place, does the same, within a few minutes, for every tool it paired.

</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">

A workbench session lasts a working day and renews itself while the tool is open. To stop a tool for good, go to **Settings**, then **Developers**, **API Tokens**: it lists your **Paired workbenches**, each with what it may open (**One <Noun /> at a time** or **Whole workspace**), and **Unpair** turns their open <Noun plural /> read-only straight away. Revoking the token does the same for every tool it paired.

</AppNote>
