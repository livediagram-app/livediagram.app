The **Image Gallery** lives under **Library** in the Explorer sidebar's More group and collects every image you have uploaded. It is the companion to placing images on the <Noun />: the <Noun /> is where you use them, and the gallery is where you manage them. Pictures you pick from the image search land here too.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Images you bring in with an import (draw.io, Microsoft Whiteboard, Excalidraw) land here too.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">

## What it shows

Each image appears as a card with its name, its size in pixels and on disk, and a badge saying where it is used:

- **Used in 2 <Noun plural />** (or however many) means a <Noun /> of yours still shows it. When something of yours in another liveworkspace app shows it too, the badge counts **items** and names each one's app. Click the badge to list them, each a link that opens it.
- **Not used** means nothing of yours, in any liveworkspace app, points at it, so it is usually safe to delete.
- **Couldn’t check** means liveworkspace could not look up where the image is used just now, so it says neither. Open the gallery again to retry. These images are never listed under **Unused**.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  **In Library** means an item in the [Library](/help/palette/library/) holds the image, so it counts as used for as long as the item is kept.
</AppNote>

To add an image, drop it, paste it, or click the **Drop, paste, or click to upload an image** area at the top. It uses the same checks as uploading from the <Noun />: one image can be up to 10 MB, and an image you already have is not stored twice.

## Finding unused images

To see only the images nothing uses, press **Unused** at the top of the gallery, just left of **Help**. The cards that stay are the ones with a **Not used** badge (a picture in something of yours in the Trash still counts as used), ready to review and delete; press **All** to see every image again. Deleting a card while **Unused** is on takes it out straight away. When every image is in use, the gallery says so and offers **Show All Images**. The switch appears once your gallery has images.

## Deleting images

Press the bin on a card to delete an image and reclaim the space. The confirmation tells you what will happen: if the image is still used in one or more <Noun plural />, it warns that those <Noun plural /> will show a broken picture; otherwise it says the image will be removed for good. Either way, a deleted image cannot be brought back.

## Automatic cleanup of unused images

You do not have to keep on top of this by hand. Once a day, liveworkspace removes uploads that are **both** more than **30 days old** and **used by nothing** in any of its apps, freeing the storage automatically. The 30-day window is deliberately generous: an image you uploaded recently, or one anything still references, is always left alone, so a picture you have not placed on the <Noun /> yet is never swept out from under you. An image in use stays put no matter how old it is.

## Who has a gallery

Everyone does, signed in or not. A guest's gallery belongs to their browser, like their <Noun plural />, and moves to their account when they [sign up](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/). The gallery is also reachable from the editor's Explorer panel, on the **Image Gallery** tab of its **Library** section.

A gallery holds up to 500 images and 1 GB in total, and it is the same gallery in every liveworkspace app you sign in to. If images are turned off for a deployment, the gallery says "Images are not enabled here." rather than failing silently.

<Tip>
