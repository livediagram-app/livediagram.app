Microsoft has retired Whiteboard for personal accounts: boards became read-only in September 2026, and the remaining legacy boards were deleted on **16 October 2026** (work and school accounts keep Whiteboard in Teams and on the web). **Import from Microsoft Whiteboard** brings a board you exported across into a tab, so you can keep working on it: every pen stroke stays a stroke, and sticky notes, text, shapes, lines and images come with it. Import into a [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/) tab to keep drawing on the board with its pressure and colours as they were.

Everything happens in your browser: the import needs no Microsoft account and makes no call to Microsoft.

## What you need

The import reads a **board export**: a folder per board holding the board's edit history, the way the Whiteboard web app syncs it. (Getting that export out of Whiteboard happens outside livediagram.) A board folder contains:

- `manifest.json`, `session.json` and `changes.json`, which every board needs
- `metadata.json`, which holds the board's title and dates
- an `objects` folder with the board's images

Other files beside them (such as a screenshot) are ignored. You can pick **one board folder**, a **folder holding many board folders**, or a **.zip** of either. One pick can be up to 512 MB.

## Importing

A board goes into an empty tab, from the tab's start:

1. Make a [new canvas](/new), or add a tab, so you are on an empty tab's start (**Start Diagramming**, **Start Drawing** or **Start a Workshop**).
2. Choose **Import** on the start's rail, then **Microsoft Whiteboard** under **A Board from Another Tool**.
3. In the **Import from Microsoft Whiteboard** dialog, press **Choose a folder** or **Choose a .zip**, or drop either onto the panel.

A single board imports straight away. When there are several, the panel lists them, newest first, with the date each was last edited and how many items it holds. **The first ticked board in the list goes into this tab**; untick the others, since any other board you leave ticked is named in the report as left out ("Only one board goes into a tab"). To bring in more boards, import each into a tab of its own.

The board takes the place of the tab's start: the tab keeps its name, and nothing in your other tabs or canvases changes. You need to be able to edit the canvas, and the tab must not be [locked](/help/tabs/locking-tabs/).

## How boards come across

In a Draw mode tab:

- **Pen strokes** become marker strokes with their pressure, so thick and thin stay as you drew them.
- **Colours** follow your board: black ink on a light board, and white ink on a dark board, become your whiteboard's own ink, so they read on either background. Other colours keep their hue.
- **Highlighter** strokes stay highlighter strokes, and arrowheads drawn on pen strokes come with them.
- **Sticky notes**, **text** and **shapes** keep their position, size and text. Lines keep their arrowheads, width and dashes, and shapes you drew by hand and Whiteboard tidied keep their outline.
- **Images** are saved to your [image gallery](/help/explorer/image-gallery/) like any imported image.
- The board's **background** (plain, dots or grid, light or dark) carries over.

In a Diagram or Workshop tab, ink comes in as pencil lines, and colours keep their exact values (black ink follows the tab's theme).

A few things change on the way, and the import says how many of each:

- **Rainbow ink** is drawn in pink, and **galaxy ink** in violet.
- **Tables** become rectangles, with the ink inside them kept.
- **Strokes drawn in a dark board's own colour**, which you could never see on it, are left out.
- **Images missing from the export** are left out, and the report counts them.
- Anything Whiteboard keeps that livediagram doesn't recognise is skipped and counted, never fatal.

Comments and reactions on a board are not imported.

## When a board fails

A board whose files can't be read is named in the report with its reason. If the whole pick holds no boards, the panel says "No Microsoft Whiteboard boards found. Pick a board folder or its .zip."
