The **Explorer panel** is the Explorer inside the editor. Press the **menu button** (☰) in the top-left corner of the <Noun /> and it opens under the button; press the button again, click anywhere outside it or press Escape, and it closes. While it is open, the rest of the window dims so the panel stands out; on a computer or tablet it opens wide and tall, nearly the height of the window, and on a phone it fills the screen below the button. It reads the same library as the [full-page Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), so you can browse your library, catch up on what happened and open another <Noun /> without leaving the one you are on.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">

</AppNote>

## What the panel shows

- **The current <Noun />** at the top: its name, its visibility badge (**Private**, **Shared**, **Team**, **Public** or **Offline**) and its **⋯** menu. **Click the name to rename it** in place: Enter saves, Escape keeps the old name. A <Noun /> saved only in this browser shows here too, with the **Offline** badge; its **⋯** menu offers **Open**, **Sync <Noun capital />** (when you are signed in) and **Delete <Noun capital />**, which removes it for good.
- **The rail** down the left lists every section, grouped as on the Explorer page: **Overview** (Home, Inbox, Shared With Me), **Spaces** (My Work, each of your teams, then Offline while this browser keeps any <Noun plural />, and **New Team** last when you are signed in) and **More** (Library, Archived and Trash).
- **The pane** on the right shows the section you pick, a page at a time; **Show more** loads the next page. **Home** shows the <Noun plural /> you were last in, and the **Inbox**, the **Library** and the **Trash** open right there too. Choosing an Inbox row shows it in place of the list, with **Back** to return, so you can reply or resolve without leaving the <Noun />. **My Work** and each team list their folders and <Noun plural />; a folder opens right there in the pane, and picking a <Noun /> opens it in the editor, while an item from another liveworkspace app opens on that app's site. The pane's header holds the section's title and **New Folder** (**New Subfolder** inside a folder), which makes a folder in My Work or the team you are looking at, with a team's **Members** beside it.
- The panel opens on **Home** and then remembers the section you last picked.
- **Offline**, last among the spaces, shows how many <Noun plural /> are saved only in this browser and opens their page ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)). It appears only while there is at least one (or one of its folders). It lists all of its <Noun plural />, whichever folder holds them.
- Along the foot are <AppName />'s other places: **Home**, **Explorer**, **Community** (when it is on) and **Help**.

It is the same data as the full page: a rename or a move shows in both at once. Each <Noun /> has the same menu as on the Explorer page, from its **⋯** button or a right-click, so **Change Folder**, **Share** and the rest work here too. **History** and **Webhooks** are left out of the panel's menus: for the <Noun /> you are in, find them under **Tools** in the footer. The panel's menu adds **Take Offline**, which moves a <Noun /> into this browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)); **Sync <Noun capital />** moves one back to your account. Drag one of your own <Noun plural /> onto a folder in the pane, or onto **My Work** or a team in the rail, to file it there; rest it on a section in the rail for a moment and that section opens.

In **Change Folder**, **New Team** makes a team on the spot and files the <Noun /> into it.

## When you will see it

- **In the editor**, to jump to another <Noun /> without going back to the library. Zen mode hides the menu button with the rest of the chrome.
- **On a phone**, the panel opens under the menu button across the screen: the sections become a row of chips over the pane.
- **When a <Noun /> can't be opened**, it stays available beside the error screen, so you can pick another <Noun />.

Like everywhere else in the Explorer, the panel works whether or not you are signed in.
