A **shape library** is a named set of shapes you reuse: a UML preset, your team's icons, a notation of your own. Each library is yours, kept with your canvases, and its shapes are ready to place on any tab from the palette's **Library**.

## Making a library

Shape libraries come from draw.io. In draw.io, a library is the set of shapes you keep in a scratchpad or save as a library file. Import the file with [Import from draw.io](/help/explorer/drawio-import/) on the Explorer page, on its own or together with diagrams and folders:

- **Each library file becomes its own library**, named after the file. Importing two libraries gives you two; they are never merged.
- If you already have a library with that name, the new one is called "Team icons (2)", so neither is lost.
- Shapes keep their order, titles and look, connections included. Pictures inside them are saved to your [image gallery](/help/explorer/image-gallery/).
- Shapes that can't be read are left out, and the report says how many.

You can have up to 100 shape libraries, each holding up to 1,000 shapes (a bigger draw.io library keeps its first 1,000). A library must also fit in the same space as one tab, just under 2 MB; a larger one is left out and named in the report. A shape library comes from an import. To keep shapes from the canvas, select them and use [Save to Library](/help/palette/library/) instead.

## Placing a shape

Press the **Library** button on the palette, beside Search. On a canvas saved to your account it opens the shared Library: your shape libraries sit under **Personal**, each a folder named after the library, beside anything you saved with **Save to Library**, and your teams' libraries follow. Because the Library is shared across liveworkspace, signed in you can place these shapes in the other apps that read it too. **Search the library** finds shapes by their title, or by the library's name.

On a canvas kept only in this browser, the button shows your shape libraries alone, each in its own section, newest first, with a **Search my shapes** box; there it appears once you have a library with at least one shape. Each shape shows a small preview with its title (or "Shape 1", "Shape 2" when it has none).

- **Click a shape** to place it in the middle of the view, or **drag it** to where you want it. Placing a shape closes the Library; open it again to place the next, which lands beside the last instead of on top of it, so a run of placements lines up left to right.
- It arrives selected, ready to move, and one undo takes it back.
- What you place is ordinary shapes and connections: edit them like anything else.

With the keyboard, Tab to a shape and press Enter or Space to place it in the middle of the view.

The Library isn't offered on a view-only tab or in [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/), whose dock replaces the palette. The palette loads your libraries when the editor opens, so after importing or changing a library, reopen the canvas to see the change.

## Managing your libraries

The Explorer's **Shape Libraries** page, under **Library** in the sidebar's More group after the Image Gallery, lists your libraries, newest first, with how many shapes each holds and a preview of its first shapes. The editor's Explorer panel shows them too, on the **Shape Libraries** tab of its **Library** section.

- **Rename** a library in place: type the new name and press Enter, or Escape (or click away) to keep the old one. Names must be unique among your libraries, up to 120 characters.
- **Delete** a library after confirming. Its shapes leave the palette's Library; canvases where you placed them keep them.
- **Show shapes** lists every shape in a library, each with its own **Delete**. Deleting a single shape takes effect straight away, without a confirmation; deleting the last shape leaves an empty library until you delete that too.

Deleting a library or a shape never changes a canvas: what you placed is ordinary elements.

To add more libraries, use the draw.io button in **Import from** at the top right of this page. Diagrams picked alongside libraries land in My Work as canvases of their own ([Importing draw.io files](/help/explorer/drawio-import/)).

Renaming or deleting a shape from the palette's Library first copies that whole library into your Personal Library, and the change applies to the copy there. The library on this page stays as it was.

<Tip>
  Shape libraries belong to you, signed in or not. If you start as a guest and later sign up, your
  libraries come with you.
</Tip>
