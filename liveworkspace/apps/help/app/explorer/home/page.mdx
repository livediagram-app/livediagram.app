**Home** is the page the [Explorer](/explorer) opens on, and the first row of its sidebar. It answers the question you bring back with you, on one screen: **what was I working on?**

<AppNote app="livediagram,liveplanner,livedocuments,livedesign">

Home opens with a greeting across the top of the page. Under it, two pills say how many <Noun plural /> you have across My Work and your teams and whether anything waits in your **Inbox**; click one to go there. The search field sits right there in the greeting, with **New <Noun capital />** inside it, so the first screen always offers a way to find something or start something. With **All Apps** chosen, the tiles come from every app, and **Start a Project** beside their heading opens liveworkspace.app, where one press makes a whole project's files across the apps (a canvas and a plan, say) in a new folder, each one connected to the others.

## New

**New** shows the four <Noun plural /> made most recently in My Work and your teams, newest first, opened or not, each saying when it was made and where it lives. A <Noun /> an AI tool made for you lands here at once and wears **Made by AI** on its picture, so a batch of new work is one look away.

## Most Used and Recent

Two sections keep up to sixteen <Noun plural /> within reach: **Most Used**, the **eight you opened most** lately, then **Recent** directly under it, the **eight you used last**.

- **Most Used** counts how many times you opened a <Noun /> over the last 14 days, so a <Noun /> you open a lot over a few days rises to the top. A day you only edited or made it counts as one open. A <Noun /> you have not opened in 14 days drops off, so the list stays about what you are working on now.
- **Recent** holds the <Noun plural /> you opened, edited or made most recently, newest first.
- **What you make is in at once**, opened or not: a new <Noun />, a duplicate, a single imported board, or one an AI tool made for you. An import of several <Noun plural /> at once is the exception, so a big import never pushes your work aside; those join when you first open one.
- A <Noun /> is never shown twice: it appears in the first of New, Most Used and Recent it belongs to, and the next one takes its place in the others.
- A <Noun /> you [hid from Recent](/help/explorer/recent/#hide-from-recent) leaves Most Used and Recent too.

On a computer or a tablet each section is cards, up to two rows of four: **Most Used** with a line under each saying how many times you opened it, then **Recent**, saying when you last used each. On a phone each section is its own strip of up to eight you scroll sideways, under its own heading, and Recent's strip ends in a **See More** tile. If every <Noun /> you have opened lately is among the most used, Recent is left out until there is another.

Each <Noun /> shows a picture of what is drawn on it, with its name underneath; click one to open it. A <Noun /> saved only in this browser wears the **Offline** pill and takes its place like any other, counted in this browser. <Noun plural capital /> in the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), and ones whose share link has lapsed or whose team you have left, never appear. With nothing yet to show, Recent says "The <Noun plural /> you open will gather here."

Opening a <Noun /> is private: it counts towards your own Most Used and Recent, and nobody else ever sees that you opened it.

**See More**, at the right of Recent's heading on a computer or tablet (and the last tile of the strip on a phone), opens [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/): the 12 <Noun plural /> you opened most recently, newest first, with the same filters as the rest of the Explorer. Its breadcrumb leads back to Home.

## Shared With You Recently

Below your Spaces (Workspaces in livehuddle) sit the four items most recently [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) through a link. **See All** opens Shared With Me.

## Guests and signing in

Home works without an account, like the rest of <AppName />.

**Your work follows you.** Signing up moves what you open, to your new account alongside your <Noun plural />, so a week of work as a guest is still there afterwards.

## History

The **History** item in a <Noun />'s menu opens everything that has happened to that <Noun />: created, commented on, shared, filed. History covers the **last 30 days**; anything older is no longer listed.
