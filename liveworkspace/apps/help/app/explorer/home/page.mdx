Below the greeting, Home has two columns once there is room: **Recent** on the left, a timeline of what you made and opened, and a rail on the right with **Most Used**, your **Spaces** and **Shared With You Recently**. A section with nothing in it is left out, heading and all, so Home never lists empty sections; it comes back the moment it has something, with no need to reload. ## Recent

**Recent** is a timeline, newest first, under a heading for each day (**Today**, **Yesterday**, a weekday, then the date). Each row starts with its time and reads what happened and where: **Made · Marketing**, say, or **Opened · My Work**.

- **What you made lately**: the four <Noun plural /> made most recently in My Work and your teams, opened or not, each marked with a spark. A new <Noun />, a duplicate, a single imported board or one an AI tool made for you lands here at once, and one an AI tool made wears **Made by AI** after its name. An import of several <Noun plural /> at once is the exception, so a big import never pushes your work aside; those join when you first open one.
- **What you opened lately**: up to eight more <Noun plural /> you opened, edited or made most recently, each marked with a ring.

**See More**, at the right of Recent's heading, opens [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/): the 12 <Noun plural /> you opened most recently, newest first, with the same filters as the rest of the Explorer. Its breadcrumb leads back to Home.

## Most Used

**Most Used** ranks the **eight <Noun plural /> you opened most** lately, numbered from the top. Each row has a small chart of the last 14 days (each square a day, today on the right) and how many times you opened it ("12 opens").

- It counts how many times you opened a <Noun /> over the last 14 days, so a <Noun /> you open a lot over a few days rises to the top. A day you only edited or made it counts as one open. A <Noun /> you have not opened in 14 days drops off, so the list stays about what you are working on now.
- A <Noun /> is never shown twice: one you made lately stays in Recent's made rows, one in Most Used is left out of Recent's opened rows, and the next one takes its place.
- A <Noun /> you [hid from Recent](/help/explorer/recent/#hide-from-recent) leaves Most Used and Recent too.

Click any row to open the <Noun />. A <Noun /> saved only in this browser wears the **Offline** pill and takes its place like any other, counted in this browser. <Noun plural capital /> in the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), and ones whose share link has lapsed or whose team you have left, never appear.

Opening a <Noun /> is private: it counts towards your own Most Used and Recent, and nobody else ever sees that you opened it.

## Shared With You Recently

In the rail, below your Spaces (Workspaces in livehuddle), sit the four items most recently [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) through a link. **See All** opens Shared With Me.

## Guests and signing in

Home works without an account, like the rest of <AppName />.

**Your work follows you.** Signing up moves what you open, to your new account alongside your <Noun plural />, so a week of work as a guest is still there afterwards.

## History

The **History** item in a <Noun />'s menu opens everything that has happened to that <Noun />: created, commented on, shared, filed. History covers the **last 30 days**; anything older is no longer listed.
