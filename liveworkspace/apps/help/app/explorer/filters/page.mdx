Every list of <Noun plural /> in the [Explorer](/explorer) can be narrowed. Type a few words into the search field in the top bar, the one that reads “Search your work”, or pick a **filter chip** under the page title. Both edit the same search, so a chip you pick shows up in the field as a pill, and a filter you type lights its chip.

Words match a <Noun />'s name or the name of the folder it is in, ignoring capitals. With several words, a <Noun /> has to match all of them. Accents count, so "cafe" does not find "Café".

## Where it works

- **My Work, a folder and a team.** The search stays inside the place you are in, and reaches into every folder below it: the list shows each matching <Noun /> once, with the folder it lives in beside its name. Folders step aside while a search is set and come back when you clear it.
- **Recent, Shared With Me and Search results** gather <Noun plural /> from everywhere, so they add a **Space** chip: My Work, Shared With Me, or one of your teams.
- **Browser Storage** narrows its own list in this browser. It offers no **Made by AI** chip, since a <Noun /> kept only here records no maker.
- **Home, Inbox, Archived and Trash** have no list of <Noun plural /> to narrow. Typing in the field there opens **Search results**, every <Noun /> you can open, narrowed by what you typed.

## The chips

| Chip       | Keeps <Noun plural /> that                                                                      |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Opens in   | Open in the apps you pick (on All Apps only)                                              |
| Made by AI | An AI tool made for you; they also carry a **Made by AI** badge wherever they are listed |
| Edited     | Were last edited today, in the last 7, 30 days or 12 months, or this year                |
| People     | You own (Me), or someone else owns (Others)                                              |
| Space      | Live in My Work, are shared with you, or are in a team (gathered lists only)             |

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  liveplanner has one more chip, **Template**: it keeps plans that were made from a Retrospective or Kanban template.
</AppNote>

Click a chip to open its list and tick values; **Any** at the top unsets it. **Made by AI** has only one value, so it is a simple on/off toggle. A chip can hold several values: picking Me and Others under People keeps both. Different chips narrow together: Made by AI and Edited keeps what an AI tool made for you that was edited in the last 7 days. **Clear** at the end of the row empties the search.

While the switch at the top right of the page is on **All Apps**, showing every app's items, the **Opens in** chip narrows them to the apps you pick; on **<AppName />**, showing <AppName />'s own, it is not offered.

## Typing filters

The field takes filters as words too, the way the chips write them:

- `made-by:ai` keeps what an AI made.
- `opens-in:canvas,document` keeps what opens in livediagram or livedocuments.
- `edited:7d` keeps what you edited in the last 7 days; `today`, `30d`, `12m` and `this-year` work too.
- `people:me` or `people:others`, and `space:mine` or `space:shared`, work the same way. A team is offered by name as you type `space:`.

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  `template:retrospective,kanban` keeps both template families.
</AppNote>

As you type, suggestions appear under the field: use **Down** and **Up** to move through them, **Enter** or **Tab** to take one, and **Esc** to close the list. When the whole list is loaded, a suggestion marked **No matches** would leave nothing in it on its own. A finished filter becomes a pill in the field, with a **×** to remove it; **Backspace** at the start of the words selects the last pill, and a second **Backspace** removes it.

A word that looks like a filter but isn't one, such as `colour:red` or `edited:yesterday`, is searched as plain text, and the Explorer says so under the chips, so a typo never hides <Noun plural /> silently. A **Space** filter typed on My Work, a folder or a team does nothing there, because the place you are in already sets the space; the note under the chips says so. The search reads at most 512 characters.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livehuddle,livedocuments">
  `template:retrospective` is searched as plain text too, since there is no Template filter in these apps.
</AppNote>

When the filters leave nothing, the list says so and offers **Clear Filters**. A screen reader hears how many <Noun plural /> are left once the list has loaded; while more pages are still to come, it says "or more".

## Share a search

The search lives in the page's address, so copying the link shares exactly what you are looking at. Typing changes the address in place rather than adding a step to Back for every letter. Moving between Recent, Shared With Me and Search results keeps your search; opening a folder or a team starts afresh. A search is not remembered for next time.

</Tip>
