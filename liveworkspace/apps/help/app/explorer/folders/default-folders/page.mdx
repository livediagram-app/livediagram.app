A **default folder** is where your new <Noun plural /> go when you create one without choosing a place. With none set, they land in My Work. Your defaults are your own: teammates choose theirs. The liveworkspace index keeps them for your account or guest identity, so they follow you to any browser, and it files each new <Noun /> by them.

## Setting a default from a folder

Open a folder's menu (its **⋯** button, or right-click the folder) and choose **Use as Default for…**. Under **New <Noun plural /> that open as**, tick what this folder should collect.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  - **Diagrams**: new canvases that open in Diagram mode.
  - **Drawings**: new canvases that open in Draw mode.
  - **Workshops**: new canvases that open in Workshop mode.

  That is the whole list: every new canvas goes by the mode it opens in, so an Event Storming board or a retrospective lands with the rest of that mode's canvases.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments,livedesign">
  Tick **<Noun plural capital />**. There is one default, for every new <Noun />.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The one entry is **<Noun plural capital />**: every new space.
</AppNote>

Each entry points at one folder, so ticking it here moves it from wherever it was; unticking it sends those <Noun plural /> back to My Work. One folder can hold several entries.

**My Work** has the same menu: a ticked entry there means those <Noun plural /> land in My Work. Choosing it when unticked clears that default.

You can use a team's folders too, as long as you are a member of the team. A team's own top level cannot be a default: create inside the team to put a <Noun /> there.

## Seeing your defaults

A default folder shows a small mark after its name, in the sidebar, the Explorer's lists and cards, and the editor's Explorer panel. Hover it to read what it collects, such as "Default folder for new <Noun plural />".

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  The hover text reads, for example, "Default folder for new diagrams and drawings".
</AppNote>

## When You Create Something

**New <Noun capital />** never asks where a <Noun /> goes: it is filed in your default folder, or in My Work when you have none.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  It is filed in the default folder for the mode it opens in (**Diagrams**, **Drawings** or **Workshops**), or in My Work when that has none.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  It is filed in your **<Noun plural capital />** default folder, or in My Work when you have none.
</AppNote>

- Starting from inside a folder or a team keeps you there: that folder wins over your defaults.
- To file one <Noun /> somewhere else, use **Change Folder** on it afterwards.
- As a guest it works the same way, and your folders and defaults move to your account when you sign in.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  A template picked on a new canvas's start screen counts too: pick a Workshop template such as a Retrospective on a canvas you just made, and the canvas goes to your **Workshops** default folder, with a note saying where. A canvas you made inside a folder stays there.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">
  While you try things out as a guest, new designs are saved in this browser instead ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)), so they have no folder until you move them to your account.
</AppNote>

## In Settings

**Settings > Explorer > Default Folders** (**Where New <Noun plural capital /> Go**) shows the folder each entry sends new <Noun plural /> to, or My Work. **Change** picks a folder; **Clear** sends those <Noun plural /> back to My Work.

If a default folder has been deleted, or belongs to a team you have left, the row says "A folder you can no longer reach", and those <Noun plural /> go to My Work instead. Deleting a folder that is one of your defaults warns you first.

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  The row reads "A folder you can no longer reach, using My Work".
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livehuddle">
  The row also says where those spaces go instead: your **Spaces** default folder, or My Work.
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  Defaults only decide where a new <Noun /> starts. Moving a <Noun /> later, or changing a default,
  never moves <Noun plural /> you already have.
</Tip>
