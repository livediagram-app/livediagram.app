Zen mode is a distraction-free focus mode for the editor. It hides the chrome around your work so only the work itself remains, which is ideal for reading, sharing your screen, or thinking without the toolbars in the way. It is purely a view setting: it changes nothing in the <Noun /> and is not synced to anyone else, so each person can focus independently.

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  Only the canvas remains (in Workshop mode the Session strip stays, so a facilitator can still run
  the session, and so does the Following banner while you follow someone).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  Only the canvas remains (the Following banner stays while you follow someone).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">Only the board, sheet, dashboard or form on the tab remains.</AppNote>

## What It Hides and Keeps

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  When zen mode is on, the editor hides the header, the tab bar, the palette strip, the menu button,
  the inspector and the panels such as Layers and the minimap, the Explorer, the Collaborate panel,
  your role badge, the empty-canvas prompt, and the undo/redo dock.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign">

  the inspector and the panels such as the minimap, the Explorer, the Collaborate panel, your role
  badge, the empty-canvas prompt, and the undo/redo dock.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedocuments">
  When zen mode is on, the editor hides the Share and account card at the top right, the tab bar,
  the palette strip, the menu button, the inspector and the panels such as the Explorer and the
  Collaborate panel, the empty-canvas prompt, and the bottom-right buttons beside the zoom controls
  (Undo and Redo, the page navigator, Contents and Collaborate).
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  When zen mode is on, the editor hides the [cards in the top
  corners](/help/user-interface/title-bar/) (the logo, the menu button and the Explorer at the top
  left; **Share** or **Copy** and your account at the top right), the [tab
  bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/) and its quick controls, and the buttons in the bottom-right
  corner: **Undo** and **Redo**, **Trash**, **Types**, the comments button, **Board** and **Style**.
</AppNote>

What stays is the part you came for:

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  - the **canvas and everything on it**, with selection, alignment guides, laser trails, and remote
  cursors all working as normal, - the **zoom controls** in the bottom-right, so you can still zoom
  and fit, - an **Exit zen mode button on those controls**, shown while you are in zen mode, so
  there is always a visible way out.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  - the **tab's content**, a board with its own header, a sheet, a dashboard or a form, with
  presence and everything else working as normal, - the [session
  tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/) (**Timer** and **Vote**) while a session is running, so
  whoever runs it still can, - on a Sheet tab, the [zoom
  controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/), - an **Exit zen mode** button in the bottom-right
  corner, so there is always a visible way out.
</AppNote>

You can still work as usual (if your role allows), and keyboard shortcuts keep working too. Zen mode is available to everyone, including view-only visitors, since focusing changes nothing. It also only ever affects your own screen: the people you are working with keep their chrome.

## Turning It on and Off

You can turn zen mode on from the keyboard or the command palette:

- **The keyboard.** Press **Z**. It never fires while you are typing into a label or text field, and it is listed under Tools in the shortcut reference (the **Keyboard** category in **Settings**). If you have switched keyboard shortcuts off there, use the command palette instead.
- **The command palette.** Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux) and run "Enter zen mode". See [The Command Palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/).

<AppNote app="livediagram">
  A third way is **the canvas-tool dropdown**: open the **Selection Mode** picker at the left of the
  toolbar and choose **Zen**, in the Preview group under Isometric. It acts immediately and leaves
  your current tool selected. Viewers and Participants also have a Zen button on the zoom controls.
  On a Draw tab and on an Event Storming board there is no picker, so use **Z** or the command
  palette.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="livedesign,livedocuments">
  A third way is **the canvas-tool dropdown**: open the **Selection Mode** picker at the left of the
  toolbar and choose **Zen**, in the Preview group. It acts immediately and leaves your current tool
  selected. Viewers and Participants also have a Zen button on the zoom controls.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  Participants and Viewers on a shared link have a **Zen mode** button in the bottom-right corner.
</AppNote>

To come back, use the **Exit zen mode** button, or press **Z**, or **Escape** (if something is selected, the first Escape clears the selection). Zen mode is not remembered: reloading the page brings the chrome back.

<AppNote app="livediagram,livedesign,livedocuments">
  The button is the compress icon on the zoom controls.
</AppNote>

<AppNote app="liveplanner">
  The button is in the bottom-right corner, and you can also run **Exit zen mode** from the command
  palette.
</AppNote>

<Tip>
  Reach for zen mode when you share your screen on a call: your work fills the view with nothing to
  distract from what you are showing.
</Tip>
