## How it fits

- The CSS 3D view still runs underneath: its stage transform is the camera, its floor is the ground, its pieces' boxes
  take presses. The WebGL canvas sits over the floor and under the overlay, reading the stage's computed transform each
  frame, so the scene and the floor never drift.
- The static scene rebuilds only when the board's elements, the walls, the roofs or the palette change. Each frame
  moves the camera, poses each character's rig, plays moments (lifting the piece out of the merged mesh by zeroing its
  vertex range), and fades the walls in front of your feet and any wall or roof between your head and the camera.
- Shader warm-up (`shader-warmup.ts`): `laterMaterials()` (stand-ins for the materials the scene makes later: a
  moment's glow and lit parts, a poster's picture, a desk's screen, a figure's props and marked parts, a dog's coat, a
  selection's lines) are made with the renderer, compiled with `compileAsync` (`prewarm`, once) as the first frame
  starts, lit as the scene is, and kept until the renderer goes (three frees a program once no material uses it). The
  sun's shadows are `PCFShadowMap` from the start (three swaps `PCFSoftShadowMap` for it at its first draw), so the
  programs compiled ahead of that draw are the ones it uses. Each frame, before drawing, `ShaderWarmup.mayDraw` walks the scene and the ink
  pass's quad for a material it has not seen at its version; if there is one it runs `compileAsync` on both and, only
  when that made new programs (`renderer.info.programs` grew), skips drawing until they are ready: up to
  `FIRST_HOLD_MS` (6 s) before the first frame, `LATER_HOLD_MS` (0.4 s) after. The first frame drawn sets
  `data-real-3d-ready` on the canvas, which `space-3d.css` fades in over 180 ms from transparent (at once under reduced
  motion). The shadow map's own depth programs still compile on the first frame (three gives no way to warm them).
- Measured on the Coworking Space (2026-11-25, block models): 2.4 ms a frame median, 5.1 ms p95; three.js's used parts
  133 KB gzipped, loaded only when Real 3D is on. With the smooth models, the Company Campus holds about 613,000
  triangles; frame time held the browser's cap, not yet measured on a mid laptop or a phone.

## Palette portraits

Derived from [Palette portraits](../3d-engine.md#palette-portraits).

- **Request**: `{ look: CharacterLook; crop: 'full' | 'action' | 'head' | 'stage'; move: AvatarReactionKind | null;
width; height }` in CSS px, rendered at `min(PORTRAIT_MAX_RATIO, devicePixelRatio)`. `portraitKey` is
  `gameLookKey(gameLook(look))#move#crop#WxH` in device px, so options that wear the same parts share one image.
  `full` for the Gender, Clothes and Build tiles, `head` for Hair, `action` for the Actions cards, `stage` (room for a hop)
  for the preview card's still and player alike, so the figure keeps its size between them.
- **Scene**: a plain `THREE.Scene` in canvas coordinates (y down) as the live scene's, `createLook` for the sky and
  sun (its sun's shadow off, the shadow map off, the catcher hidden), `ColorManagement` off and
  `LinearSRGBColorSpace` output, transparent clear. The live view's projection mirrors canvas y; a portrait mirrors
  its image left to right instead (`copyOut`), because a mirrored parent breaks `GameFigure`'s posing (it reads its
  world turn, which a negative scale corrupts). No ink pass (its depth maths is the stage's projection, not a
  camera's); the image carries the pass's grade as a CSS filter (`PORTRAIT_GRADE`).
- **Figure**: a `GameFigure` of the look's pack (awaiting `loadGamePack`), kept dressed per `gameLookKey` (an LRU of
  `PORTRAIT_FIGURE_CAP`, disposed oldest out and with the engine), since dressing clones the pack; the settle steps
  ease out whatever pose the last image left. A still settles by `PORTRAIT_SETTLE_STEPS`
  updates of `PORTRAIT_SETTLE_DT`; its pose is `portraitMove(move)`: `behaviourStill`'s arms, legs and facing (yaw by
  facing as the scene's `YAW`).
- **Camera**: perspective, `PORTRAIT_FOV_DEG` vertical, from the front (canvas +y) turned `PORTRAIT_TURN_DEG` and
  raised `PORTRAIT_PITCH_DEG`, looking at the crop's centre, backed off until both the crop's height and width fit.
- **Renderer**: one `WebGLRenderer` on a detached canvas of `PORTRAIT_BUFFER_PX` square (antialiased, pixel ratio
  1), each image drawn into its bottom-left viewport and scissor, taken with `createImageBitmap` (the frame's only
  readback cost), then off the frame drawn mirrored to a 2D canvas of its size, `toBlob` to PNG, `URL.createObjectURL` into the store. Context loss or a throw drops the engine; every queued
  request is stored as `PORTRAIT_FAILED`.
- **Queue**: one `requestAnimationFrame` pump renders at least one queued key a frame, and another only while the
  time spent plus the last image's time stays within `PORTRAIT_FRAME_BUDGET_MS`; a key
  queued, pending or stored is never queued twice. With nothing queued and no player for
  `PORTRAIT_IDLE_RELEASE_MS`, the renderer is disposed and its context lost on purpose.
- **Store**: `PORTRAIT_CACHE_CAP` images, oldest out (its object URL revoked); `subscribePortraits` tells each
  `FigurePortrait` (`useSyncExternalStore`) when one arrives.
- **Player**: `playPortrait` makes its own `GameFigure`, and each animation frame poses it from
  `reactionPose(move, t mod (reactionDuration + PORTRAIT_LOOP_REST_MS))` (standing once `done`), lifts it by
  `liftInSpritePx(lift)`, renders and copies into the card's canvas; stop disposes it. Under reduced motion the card
  shows the still image instead.
- **Fallback**: `PORTRAIT_FAILED` shows the tile's sprite; the empty state is a rounded placeholder of the tile's size.
- **Tests**: `figure-portrait.test.ts` (key sharing and splitting, frames, moves, lift, the store's cap and revoke),
  `AvatarPalette.test.tsx` (every look tile and the preview card ask for the portrait of the look they show).
