# Quiz and game templates

> **Gone from livehuddle** (Work Tracker #423, part 3): not part of livehuddle's meeting product ([Not in livehuddle](../002-project-scope/not-in-livehuddle.md)). livediagram keeps them, and [its spec there](../../../../livediagram/docs/specs/012-collaboration/quiz-and-game-templates.md) is the one to read and change.

The Pub Quiz, Icebreaker Questions, Two Truths and a Lie, Would You Rather and Find Someone Who Bingo templates
(`pub-quiz`, `icebreaker-questions`, `two-truths-and-a-lie`, `would-you-rather`, `people-bingo`) left the catalogue
with their builders and previews. A link, an agent or a seed naming one is answered with Blank Space, as any unknown
kind is (`packages/templates/src/retired-templates.test.ts`), and a document named "Untitled Pub Quiz" still reads as
unnamed ([Templates by mode](../007-editor/templates-by-mode.md#the-catalogue)).
