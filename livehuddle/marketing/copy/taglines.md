# Taglines

Short one-liners grouped by character budget, for directory listings, app store
fields, social bios, and the like. Pick the longest one that fits the field's
limit. Counts in brackets are exact character counts including spaces.

Every line is built from [`facts.md`](facts.md). Don't add a claim that isn't
there, and never one about talking by voice or video (see "Do not claim"). No
em dashes. The pitch: **Meet your team in a Space.**

## Ultra-short, <= 30 characters

For tight bios, badges, app subtitles.

- Meet your team in a Space. [26]
- Your team's virtual office. [27]
- Open a link, walk in. [21]
- A place for your team to meet. [30]
- A virtual office from a link. [29]
- Meet in rooms, not in a grid. [29]

## Short, <= 40 characters

Classic "tagline" slot on most directories.

- Meet your team in a Space, free. [32]
- Stand-ups, all-hands, one Space. [32]

## Medium, <= 60 characters

Listing headlines, Open Graph titles, search-result titles.

- Meet your team in a Space. Free, open source, no sign-up. [57]
- A virtual office your team walks round, from one link. [54]
- Walk your team into a Space: rooms, desks and a timer. [54]
- A free, open-source virtual office in your browser. [51]

## Long, <= 80 characters

Hero subheads, Twitter/X header copy, longer headline fields.

- Meet your team in a Space: walk in from a link, gather round the table. [71]
- A free, open-source virtual office. No sign-up, nothing to install. [67]
- Stand-ups in a Team Pod, all-hands in a hall, one free Space for it all. [72]

## Long, <= 100 characters

Meta-description-adjacent fields, longer banner copy.

- Meet your team in a Space: characters, rooms, sticky notes and a timer on the wall. No sign-up. [95]
- A free, open-source virtual office your team walks round from one link, in any browser. [87]

## Pick-list by use

| Field type                  | Use the version at |
| --------------------------- | ------------------ |
| App subtitle / badge        | <= 30              |
| Directory tagline           | <= 40              |
| OG title / listing headline | <= 60              |
| Hero subhead                | <= 80              |
| Long banner / meta-ish      | <= 100             |
