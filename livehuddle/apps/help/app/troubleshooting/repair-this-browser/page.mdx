If a canvas opens for a colleague but not for you, something saved in your browser may be in the way. A repair clears the settings and caches livehuddle keeps in this browser, then you open the canvas again. It works from this page even when the editor itself will not start.

<BrowserRepairTool />

## What a repair clears

- Settings kept only in this browser, such as panel positions and light or dark appearance. Preferences saved to your account, such as panel opacity, come back on the next load.
- Tips and banners you dismissed in this browser, which may show again once.
- The cached list of recent canvases, which reloads from the server.
- Saved share-link passwords, so a protected link asks for its password again.

## What a repair always keeps

- **Your canvases.** Canvases synced to the server stay there, and a repair never touches them.
- **Your guest identity.** If you use livehuddle without an account, the canvases you made belong to an identity saved in this browser. A repair keeps it, so your canvases stay in your list.
- **Canvases in This Browser.** These exist only in this browser, so a repair leaves them alone.
- **Your sign-in.** A repair never signs you out.

## Copy Diagnostics

**Copy Diagnostics** copies a short report about this browser: whether storage and cookies work, whether the browser's database opens, and whether the browser is up to date. Paste it into a message to [support](/help/contact/) so we can see what is going on. It never includes what is in your canvases, and it carries only the start of a guest id. From the editor it also includes the canvas's address and, if you are signed in, your account id.

When a canvas fails to load, the editor offers the same two buttons on its error screen while you are online, and its report also says how far the load got.

## If it still does not load

Work through [A Canvas Will Not Load](/help/troubleshooting/document-not-loading/). If you are a guest, [signing in](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/) moves your canvases to an account, which also rules out anything saved in this browser.
