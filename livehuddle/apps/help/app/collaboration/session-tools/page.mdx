Session tools are the light tools a meeting needs, and the one it needs most is a clock. In livehuddle the clock lives in the room itself: place a **Timer** or a **Stopwatch** from the palette's **Meet** category, and everyone in the Space sees it tick in sync.

## Time in every room

Because they're pieces rather than a button in the toolbar, every room can keep its own time: a stand-up corner with a 15 minute Timer, a meeting room with a Stopwatch, a focus booth with a 25 minute Timer. Each runs on its own.

- **Timer and Stopwatch**: place one, then start, pause, reset or set it with the buttons under it. See [Timer and Stopwatch](/help/collaboration/session-tools/timer/).

To point everyone at the same spot before you start, press [Bring Focus](/help/palette/behaviour/bring-focus/).

## Who can do what

Anyone with an **Editor** or **Participant** pass can run a Timer or Stopwatch, so whoever is talking can keep time. A **Viewer** watches it tick. Placing and moving the pieces is an Editor's.

## What does and does not get recorded

Starting, pausing and resetting don't go on [Undo](/help/canvas/undo/): they're not the kind of thing you'd want to Ctrl+Z. A [space in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) has no live room to share them with, but its pieces still run for you.

<Tip>
  Share a Participant pass with everyone who should take part, and keep an Editor pass for whoever
  arranges the space. The roles do the gating for you.
</Tip>
