The **Timer** and the **Stopwatch** are pieces you place in a room, like a Screen or a World Clock. A Timer counts down ("you have ten minutes"); a Stopwatch counts up ("let's see how long this takes"). Put one on the wall of each meeting room, and every room can keep its own time.

## Placing one

Both are in the palette's **Meeting** category, beside the Screen, Room Sign, Notice Board and World Clock, and search finds them too. Pick one and click where it goes, as with any other piece. Move, resize, lock or delete it like any piece; it keeps its shape.

## Running it

Under each piece sit its own buttons. Pressing them never selects or moves the piece, and in Avatar mode they work without walking over.

- **Start** sets it going, **Pause** stops it where it is, and **Reset** puts it back to the start.
- On a Timer, **Set** opens its length: tap **5**, **10**, **15** or **25 min**, or type the minutes (1 to 120) and use **−** and **+**, then press **Set Timer**. A new length starts a new round.
- When a Timer reaches **0:00** its display turns red and flashes three times (no flash if you've asked your device for less motion), its word reads **Time's Up**, and **Restart** runs it again.

The word under the time says where it is: **Ready**, **Running**, **Paused** or **Time's Up**. A Stopwatch runs until you pause it.

## The chime

A Timer plays a short, gentle chime when it reaches zero. While you're walking in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/), you hear the chime of a Timer in the room you're standing in; otherwise you hear every Timer on the tab. It plays once, on time, and never late: open the tab after a Timer ran out and you see 0:00 in silence. Your browser only lets a page play sound once you've pressed something on it.

## Who can run it

Anyone with an **Editor** or **Participant** pass can start, pause, reset and set a Timer or Stopwatch, even while someone is the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/). A **Viewer** sees the time tick but gets no buttons. Placing, moving and deleting the pieces is an Editor's.

## How it stays in sync

Rather than sending a tick every second, each screen works the time out from its own clock and the moment the piece was started. Everyone sees the same time, people who join late or reload see it straight away, and moving the piece never stops it. Starting or pausing is not something [Undo](/help/canvas/undo/) takes back. A downloaded picture of the tab shows the time as it was when you saved it.

<Note>
  The time is shared but not second-perfect across machines: tiny differences between device clocks
  are fine for keeping a meeting together.
</Note>
