Once you are talking in a call, you can turn on your camera or share a screen. Either one shows as a **bubble just above your character's head** to the people near you, and anyone watching a bubble can press it to see it large.

Cameras and screens ride the call, so start with [Proximity Audio](/help/collaboration/proximity-audio/): walk into the Space (you join the call by walking). The **Camera** and **Share** buttons sit beside it, on the **Call dock**, the small card at the bottom centre of the canvas.

<Note>
  Calls need a Space synced to the server. In a Space kept in **This Browser**, there is no call;
  the microphone, camera and screen there only preview to you.
</Note>

## Turn on your camera

<Step n={1}>Walk into the Space, then press **Camera** on the Call dock.</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Your browser asks for the camera the first time. Allow it, and the button turns on: the people
  near you now see you in a bubble above your character.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>Press **Camera** again to turn it off. Your browser's camera light goes out.</Step>

Your own bubble shows above your own head, mirrored, so you can see what others see; they see it the right way round. The camera stays on while you walk around alone, as it is your choice to make, but nothing is sent to anyone out of earshot. Leaving the call, Avatar mode or the tab turns it off.

On a computer the **Camera** and **Share** buttons are named beside their icons; on a phone they show only their icons. Hover one to see its state, such as **Camera: Off**. If the camera's says **Camera: Blocked**, your browser refused the camera. Allow it from the address bar, then press the button to try again.

## Share your screen

Sharing a screen is an Editor's to do, so the **Share** button only appears for Editors.

<Step n={1}>
  Press **Share** on the Call dock. Your browser's own picker opens: choose a whole screen, a window
  or a browser tab.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  The people near you see it as a bubble above your head, beside your camera's when both are on.
  While you share, a pulsing **Sharing** marker and **Stop Sharing** join the Call dock, after the
  call buttons. Your look's buttons stay where they are.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Press **Stop Sharing** on the Call dock to stop. Stopping it from your browser's own control works
  too, as does leaving the call. Either way, **Sharing** and **Stop Sharing** leave the dock and
  **Share** comes back.
</Step>

</Figure>

A shared screen carries no sound of its own: voices stay the call's only sound. It is tuned for reading text, so slides, documents and code come through clearly.

## Bubbles, and who sees them

Cameras and screens follow the same rule as voices: **you see the bubbles of the people you hear**, and nobody else sees yours. That is the people in earshot, and in a [room](/help/collaboration/proximity-audio/#rooms-are-private) only those on your side of its walls, so a meeting's cameras and shared screens stay inside them. Walk away and a bubble goes; walk back and it returns.

- **Off-screen bubbles are not shown.** If someone in earshot is outside the part of the board you can see, their bubble waits until you scroll them into view.
- **A hidden tab shows none.** While your browser tab is hidden, cameras and screens pause and voices carry on. They come back when you return.
- **Up to six cameras and two screens at once**, the nearest. Anyone else you hear shows just their character.

### When people stand close

People talking stand close together, so their bubbles would cover each other. They never do:

- **Bubbles slide apart sideways** into one row, in the same left-to-right order as their people, with a small gap between them. A crowded row stays centred on its group, and a bubble with room to spare stays right above its own head. Someone's camera and screen move together.
- **A small tail** in each person's colour points down from their bubbles to their head, so a bubble nudged aside still says whose it is.
- **No jitter.** Two people's bubbles swap sides only once they have clearly walked past each other, and the swap is a short slide, never a jump.
- **Speech bubbles rise from above them**, so what someone types never covers their video.

## Maximise a bubble

Press any bubble to **maximise** it. It opens into a large panel over the canvas, with the person's name in a bar above the picture and a **Restore** button. The board stays live, softly dimmed behind it, and a maximised camera switches to a sharper picture for you alone.

Press **Restore**, press **Esc**, or click the dimmed board to put it back. One bubble is maximised at a time: maximising another restores the first. If its person walks out of earshot, turns it off or leaves, the panel closes by itself. On a phone the panel fills the width of the screen.

## Who can do what

| What you can do                        | Editor | Participant | Viewer |
| -------------------------------------- | ------ | ----------- | ------ |
| See the bubbles of the people near you | Yes    | Yes         | Yes    |
| Maximise and restore a bubble          | Yes    | Yes         | Yes    |
| Turn on a camera                       | Yes    | Yes         | No     |
| Share a screen                         | Yes    | No          | No     |

Guests on an Editor or Participant link can turn on a camera without signing in, just as they can speak. A Viewer link listens and watches.

## Video Paused

Video and screen sharing can be paused while audio carries on. The camera and share buttons then give way to one calm **Video Paused**: a camera or share that was on stops, every bubble closes, and you keep talking as before.
