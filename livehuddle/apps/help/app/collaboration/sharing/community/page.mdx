The [Community](/community/) is a public gallery of spaces people are proud of. Anyone can browse it, filter it by category and tag, open a space read-only, like it, and make their own copy to start from. Sharing a space there is a way to show your work and to help someone else get going.

## Sharing a space

You need to be signed in to share a space to the Community, so every space there has a real author. The Share dialog shows its **Community** tab only to a signed-in owner; a guest sees just **Passes**. Browsing, liking and copying need no account.

<Step n={1}>Open the **Share** dialog on your space.</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Open the dialog's **Community** tab, beside **Passes**, and choose **Share to Community**.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Give it a title (3 to 80 characters), a description of what it shows and how someone could reuse
  it (20 to 500 characters), a **Category** and up to five **Tags**, then choose **Share to
  Community**.
</Step>

You can also share from the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/): choose **Share** in a space's **⋯** menu, and the same dialog opens with its **Community** tab, for the spaces you own.

The dialog shows a preview of the card people will see. Once it is shared, a confirmation offers **Copy Link** to copy the Community page's address, and **View Post** to open it.

Good tags are the words someone would search for: the tools, the method, the subject. Each tag is 2 to 24 characters. They help people find your space through the filters on the Community page.

Your space is shared anonymously by default: it shows as Anonymous, with no name or picture. Turn off **Share Anonymously** in the same dialog to show your name instead.

## What people see

A Community space always shows your **latest saved version**, so your edits appear there too. Visitors see it read-only:

- They can pan, zoom and switch tabs, and make their own copy with **Make a Copy**. Their copy is theirs; it never changes yours.
- They do not join your space live, so you won't see strangers' cursors, and you won't get an email for each visitor.
- **Comments stay private.** They are not shown to Community visitors.

Each card shows how many people liked the space and how many made a copy.

The month's most liked spaces in each category (at least five likes in the past 30 days, three at most) also show in [Start a Space](/help/canvas/templates/), after that category's templates, marked **Community**, so someone starting a new space can begin from yours.

Opened from the Community, a Space is read-only for you too, so you can see it exactly as visitors do and never change it by accident from the gallery. The bar across the top offers **Edit Your Space**, which opens your own Space.

## Editing or removing it

Open the **Share** dialog again, on its **Community** tab. It shows your post with its category, likes and copies, and three buttons:

- **View Post** opens its Community page in a new tab.
- **Edit Listing** changes the title, description, category, tags or **Share Anonymously**. Choose **Save Changes** when you are done.
- **Remove From Community** asks you to confirm, then takes it out of the gallery straight away. Its likes and copy count go with it; copies people already made stay theirs. Choose **Keep It** to back out.

<Tip>
  A space in the Community can't also have a [share

  set, **Share to Community** is unavailable. Remove one to use the other.

## If your post is hidden

Anyone can report a post. When three different people, on different networks, report yours, it is taken out of the Community automatically and for good: nobody reviews posts by hand, so the reports decide. The **Community** tab of the **Share** dialog then says **Hidden after reports**, and it shows in [My Shares](/help/collaboration/sharing/finding-community-documents/#your-own-shares) marked **Hidden**. Only you can see it. A hidden post can't be edited or removed, so the same space can't be shared again, but your space itself is untouched and you can still set a share password on it.

## Things that can't be shared

- Spaces in a **team library** belong to the team, so the Share dialog has no **Community** tab for them.
- A space whose first tab is empty can't be shared until you add something to that tab, even if
  other tabs have content.
- A space in the **Trash** disappears from the Community until you restore it.
