In a Space you talk by walking up to people. Out on the open floor you hear the characters near yours and nobody else: a voice fades as its person walks away, and sits to your left or right as they stand. Step into a room and it becomes a call of its own, with walls nobody hears through. Someone too far away to hear still shows that they are talking, so you can choose to walk over and join in.

Audio rides [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/): only a walking character has a place on the board, so only someone walking hears or is heard. People in a Space can also turn on a camera or share a screen; see [Cameras and Screen Sharing](/help/collaboration/cameras-and-screen-sharing/).

<Note>
  You can only talk in a Space that's saved online. A Space saved only in your browser (it shows
  **Offline** at the top) can't have anyone else in it, there is no call there. You can still try
  your microphone on its own: the dock's microphone (**Mic: Only You**) shows only you your own
  talking bars and sends nothing. Save the Space online to talk with people.
</Note>

## Hear the People Near You

<Step n={1}>
  Walk into the Space: a Space from the templates opens with you walking, arriving on its **Avatar
  Pad**; otherwise press **Walk** at the start of the palette strip. The call's controls sit on the
  **Call dock**, a small card at the bottom centre of the canvas, while you walk.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  That is all: there is nothing to join. Walking where there is a call connects you by itself, and
  you hear the people near you at once. If your browser held the sound back, it starts on your next
  press or key. If your link lets you listen but not speak, you listen the same way.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Your microphone starts **muted** and is not opened, so your browser does not ask for it yet. Press
  **Mic** to unmute; the browser asks the first time (allow it to be heard). The Call dock also
  shows **Camera** and **Share** (Share sends a screen for Editors only). In a wide window each
  button is named beside its icon; in a narrow one and on a phone they are icons alone, and hovering
  or pressing one tells you what it does. While it connects the dock reads a greyed **Connecting…**;
  if it cannot, **Retry** (press to try again). To stop hearing people, set **Do Not Disturb**.
</Step>

Audio only works on a Space other people can reach. A Space saved in this browser alone has nobody to talk to, so there is no call, only a microphone preview. Leaving Avatar mode, switching to another tab or closing the page also leaves the call.

## Who You Hear

You hear people on the same tab, and only while you both walk. Somebody sitting in a chair is heard from where the chair is. Anywhere you stand, on the open floor or inside a room, you hear the people within earshot:

- **Closer is louder.** A voice is full volume when you are right next to its person, and fades smoothly to silence as they walk out of range. There is a little give at the edge, so somebody standing on the boundary does not flicker in and out.
- **Left is left.** A person standing to your left is heard more in your left ear, which helps when a few people talk at once.
- **Walking away leaves a conversation.** There is nothing to hang up: walk over to another group to join theirs.

### Your Hearing Ring

While you talk, a soft ring in your colour lies on the floor round your character, showing how far your voice carries: anyone inside it can hear you. It ripples while you talk and fades a moment after you stop. Walls block sound, so inside a room the ring stops at the room's walls, and a room you stand outside is cut out of it. On a stage, where the whole tab hears you, it doesn't show.

Only you see your ring. To hide it, open [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) and switch off **Audio Proximity Ring** under **Collaborate**. Nothing else about your microphone or the call changes.

## Rooms Are Private

A room in a Space (an Office Room, with its walls and doorway) keeps a meeting in it private:

- **Inside a room, you hear the people near you**, just as on the open floor: closer is louder, and someone across a big room is out of earshot. Gather round to talk.
- **Walls block sound.** Nobody outside hears anyone inside, however close they stand to the wall or the door, and nobody inside hears anyone outside. Two rooms side by side don't hear each other through their shared wall.
- **The doorway is the line.** You are in a room once your feet are past the line of its walls. Standing in the doorway with your feet on the corridor side, you are still in the corridor; one step in, you are in the meeting.
- **A room inside a room** is private from the one around it, so a phone booth drawn inside an open-plan room is a quiet corner of its own.

In the **Virtual Office** template, the meeting rooms, the phone booths and the kitchen are each walled off from the open floor between them. The **Coworking Space** and the **All-Hands Hall** are each one big room, where you hear your neighbours, not the whole room.

## Your Microphone

The first button on the Call dock is your microphone. It starts muted. Press it to unmute (the browser asks for the microphone the first time), and again to mute. Unmuted, it stays open even when you are alone. When muted, nothing is sent at all. Its look tells you at a glance whether anyone hears you, and hovering it names the state in words:

<Figure caption="The microphone's states, as the Call dock shows them.">
  <MicStates />
</Figure>

In a wide window the button is named beside its glyph; in a narrow one and on a phone it shows only its glyph. Hover it to see its state, such as **Mic: Live**.

- **Live**: somebody hears you: anyone in earshot on your side of the walls. The button fills from its foot, white, as you speak, so you can see how loud you are and that you are being heard.
- **Nobody Near**: your microphone is on, but nobody hears you (the button still fills, paler). People further off, or on the other side of a wall, still see that you are talking.
- **Muted**: nobody hears you. Press to unmute.
- **Mic Blocked**: your browser refused the microphone. Allow it from the address bar, then press the button to try again. You still hear everyone.
- **Mic Off**: you have just unmuted and the microphone is still opening.
- **Muted** and alone on the tab for 15 seconds: the microphone is released (your browser's recording light goes out), and opens again when you unmute.
- **Mic: Only You**: where there is no call (a Space kept **Offline**, or calls off), pressing the microphone opens it on your own screen only, so you can see your talking bars move. Nothing is sent.

### Muting from Another Tab

You don't have to go back to the Space to mute. Press [**Pop Out**](#pop-out-the-mini-view) at the end of the Call dock and the small window that stays on top carries your microphone at its foot, and your camera while it is on. In Chrome and Edge, the browser's own media controls also show a microphone and a camera button while you are in a call. Each press does exactly what the Call dock does, and the dock follows. There is no hang up button: you leave a call by walking away.

## Pop Out: The Mini View

You will often leave the Space's tab to work in another one, and lose sight of who just walked over, who is talking and who is knocking. **Pop Out** is the Call dock's last button (after Share while you share, with a divider before it). It opens the **Mini View**: a small window that stays on top of your other tabs and apps, so the Space's people stay in sight.

<Step n={1}>
  Press **Pop Out** at the end of the Call dock. The Mini View opens at about 320 by 400 pixels, in
  the light or dark look the app had when it opened, and you can drag it and resize it where you
  like. The button turns solid and reads **Popped Out**.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Work in another tab or app. The window keeps showing who is on the Space's tab, you first, then
  everyone else. While you are in a call, the people you hear lead under **Near You** and the rest
  follow under **In the Space**. Each row shows the person's face, their name (yours reads **You**),
  their status if they have one, a raised hand, and three bouncing bars with a ring round their face
  while they talk.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  To go back, press **Back to Space** at the top of the window (or any row, or its header): the
  Space's tab comes to the front. To put the window away, press its close button, or press **Popped
  Out** on the dock.
</Step>

<Figure caption="The Call dock ends with Pop Out. The Mini View it opens shows the cameras of the people you hear, then lists the people near you first, then everyone else in the Space, with Back to Space at the top and your microphone and camera at the foot.">
  <MiniView />
</Figure>

- **Knocks reach you here too.** When somebody knocks, a line at the top reads "Sam is knocking" with **Let In** and **Not Now**. Answering here or on the Space's tab answers once for both.
- **Your microphone and camera** sit at the foot of the window when you may speak, and press just as the Call dock's do. A Viewer has none, and while a call connects there is nothing to press yet.
- **It keeps up while the Space's tab is hidden.** Arrivals, who is talking, hands and knocks stay current.
- **Cameras come with it.** The cameras of up to four of the nearest people you hear show as tiles, two to a row, even while the Space's tab is hidden. Shared screens are not shown there. Press a tile to go back to the Space.
- **Anyone can open it,** Editors, Participants and Viewers alike, because who is here is everyone's to see. There is one Mini View for each tab, and it never opens by itself. It closes when you close it, or leave the Space.

### Which Browsers Offer It

Pop Out needs the browser's Picture-in-Picture window for web pages:

- **Chrome and Edge** (version 116 or later) and **Firefox** (version 151 or later) show it.
- **Desktop Safari and phones** do not offer it, so the Call dock shows no Pop Out and nothing else changes.

## Seeing Who Is Talking

When someone talks, three small bars in their colour bounce above their name. You see them for everyone on your tab, not just the people you hear, even through a room's walls: the bars only say that somebody is talking, never what is said, so a closed meeting room reads like glass.

- **People you don't hear** have calm, faint bars, fading further with distance, so the nearest conversations stand out.
- **People you hear** have brighter bars, so you can see who is coming through.
- **Your own bars** show above your own head while you talk, so you can tell you are coming through.

Hover or tap someone's bars to see "Sam is talking, walk over". Press them and your character walks towards Sam, stopping a short way off so you do not bump into them. This works while you are walking; otherwise the bars only show.

<Figure caption="Sam is in earshot and heard. Priya is further off: her bars are faint, and pressing them walks you over.">
  <Earshot />
</Figure>

Only a rough level of each person's voice is sent to show the bars, never the sound itself: nobody out of earshot receives any audio. Nothing said in a call is recorded or stored anywhere.

## Speaking from a Stage

A Space can hold a **Stage**: a raised platform with its name on a plate at the front. Whoever stands on it, and may speak from it, is heard by everyone on the tab, in any room or none, at full volume. Several people on a stage hear each other as one room does. While you speak from it unmuted, an **Everyone Can Hear You** badge shows beside **On Stage** on your screen, so you know the whole tab hears you.

While someone speaks from a stage, everyone else in its room (or, for a stage on the open floor, everyone else on the tab) is the **audience**: the stage does not hear them, and they hear each other only by distance, so neighbours can whisper without the whole hall hearing. Walls still stand, so a meeting room elsewhere on the tab stays its own call. Step off the stage and you are back in your room's call straight away.

## Speaking from a Podium

Click a **Podium** and your character walks round to stand behind it, facing the room. While you stand there, your voice carries **three times as far** as usual, so a whole hall can hear a talk without anyone gathering round. Walls still block it: nobody outside the room hears you, however close they are. Your hearing ring grows to match while you talk. Step away (click anywhere else, or press an arrow key) and your voice is back to its usual reach. Only your voice carries further: you hear everyone else as you always do.

On a stage, the stage already reaches the whole tab, so the podium adds nothing there; it never lets you speak from a stage you otherwise couldn't.

## Raise Your Hand

In a stage's audience, the stage does not hear you, but you can ask to speak.

- **Raise Hand** appears on the Call dock while you are in a stage's audience. Press it and a small amber hand shows beside your character, where everyone can see it. Press **Lower Hand** to put it down.
- **The people on the stage see how many hands are up**, as a count on their Call dock. They, and any Editor, can press the hand beside your character to **Let Speak**.
- **Once you are let speak**, the hand turns into a green microphone and your strip reads **Speaking**: everyone in the Space hears you, as if you were on the stage. Your camera stays with the people who would see you anyway.
- **Your turn ends** when you press **Lower Hand**, when the stage presses your microphone again (**Done**), or when you walk out of the room. Leaving audio or the Space puts your hand down too.

If you stand on a stage that only lets Editors speak, a short line over the Call dock says so: you are heard as the audience is, and you can raise your hand like anyone else.

## Do Not Disturb

Need to concentrate? Press **Status** on the palette at the top while you walk and choose **Do Not Disturb**. A red dot with a white bar shows above your character, so people know not to expect an answer.

- **No voice reaches you**, not even from a stage, and no one's camera or screen. Other people's talking bars disappear for you.
- **You can still be heard** if you speak, so turning to a neighbour does not need the status cleared. Your microphone keeps its own mute.
- While you are in a call, the Call dock shows **Do Not Disturb**; press it to turn it off. It lasts for this visit only, like every status.

## Who Can Speak

| What you can do                   | Editor | Participant  | Viewer |
| --------------------------------- | ------ | ------------ | ------ |
| Hear the people near you          | Yes    | Yes          | Yes    |
| Speak from a stage                | Yes    | When allowed | No     |
| Speak, mute and unmute            | Yes    | Yes          | No     |
| Show your talking bars to others  | Yes    | Yes          | No     |
| See who is talking, and walk over | Yes    | Yes          | Yes    |
| Raise and lower a hand            | Yes    | Yes          | No     |
| Let a raised hand speak           | Yes    | From a stage | No     |
| Set Do Not Disturb                | Yes    | Yes          | Yes    |

A stage lets Editors speak from it, and Participants too when it is set to; anyone else standing on it is heard as the audience is. Guests speak too, wherever their [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) is an Editor or Participant one: audio needs no sign-in. A Viewer link listens: the Call dock shows **Listening**, with headphones, rather than a microphone.

## When Calls Are Paused

Calls can be paused for a while across livehuddle. The button then reads **Calls Paused**, calmly rather than as an error, and everything else in the Space carries on. Press it to try again. If it says **Audio Unavailable**, audio could not start; press it to try again.

<Tip>
  Wear headphones if you can: hearing a voice on your left or right works best with one ear on each
  side.
</Tip>
