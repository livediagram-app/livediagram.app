Live presence shows who is in a space right now. **Attendance** remembers it: who came into a tab, when they joined, when they left and how long they stayed. Use it after a meeting to see who came to the stand-up in the Team Pod, or who dropped into the Lobby while you were away.

## Opening it

Open the tab's menu (the **⋯** on the open tab, or right-click the tab) and choose **Attendance**. It lists the visits to that tab, newest first, grouped by day: **Today**, **Yesterday**, then the date.

Each row shows:

- the person's picture or initials, and their name;
- **You** on your own visits, **Guest** for someone who isn't signed in, and **Here Now** while they are still in the tab;
- when they joined and when they left, for example "Joined 14:02 · Left 14:47";
- on the right, how long they stayed: "45 min", "1 hr 5 min" or "Under a minute".

If nobody has been in the tab yet, it says so.

## What counts as a visit

- A visit starts when someone opens the tab, and ends when they move to another tab or leave the space. Having the tab open side by side counts too.
- **One person, one visit.** Someone in the same tab on their laptop and their phone is one visit; it ends when the last of them leaves.
- **A reconnect isn't a leave.** If someone's connection drops, they reload, or they hop to another tab and back within two minutes, their visit carries on.
- Agents working in a space aren't counted.

## Who can see it

|                            | Editor | Participant | Viewer |
| -------------------------- | ------ | ----------- | ------ |
| Is counted when in the tab | Yes    | Yes         | Yes    |
| Opens a tab's Attendance   | Yes    | Yes         | No     |

A link that opens only one tab shows only that tab's Attendance. A space saved only in This Browser has no Attendance, since nobody else can be in it.

## How long it's kept

- Visits are kept for **90 days** after they end, then deleted.
- A tab keeps its latest **500 visits**; a new one past that removes the oldest.
- Deleting a tab deletes its Attendance, and deleting a space deletes all of it.
- Attendance is never part of a tab's content: it isn't in exports, copies, versions or the Drive copy of a space.

## If someone closes their account

Their visits stay, so a meeting's head count stays right, but their name becomes "Deleted account" and their picture goes.
