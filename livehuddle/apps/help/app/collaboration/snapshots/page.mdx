A snapshot keeps the whole Space as it is right now, so you can try a big change (a reorganisation, an import, a clean-up with a teammate or an AI assistant) knowing you can put everything back in one step.

## Taking a Snapshot

Snapshots live in **History**. Press **Tools** in the bar along the bottom of the editor, then **History**. Press **Take Snapshot** at the foot of History, give the snapshot a name if you like, such as "Before the all-hands", and press **Take Snapshot** again. Without a name it is called by the time it was taken.

Each snapshot sits on History's timeline at the time it was taken, marked **Snapshot**, with its name, who took it, how long ago and its size. The latest **five** are kept: when five are there, the take box says which one taking another will remove, and the oldest goes.

## Reverting to a Snapshot

Press **Revert** on a snapshot and confirm. The Space goes back to exactly how it was: every tab and its folders, with any tab made since removed and any removed since brought back. The Space's name and who it is shared with stay as they are; calls, attendance and the characters walking around are not part of a snapshot.

Reverting replaces everyone's work since that snapshot, so first it takes a snapshot of the Space as it is now, marked **Before revert**. If you change your mind, revert to that one.

Everyone who has the Space open sees it reload onto the reverted version straight away, so nobody keeps working on what was replaced.

## Deleting a Snapshot

Press the bin (**Delete snapshot**) on a snapshot. It goes at once, with no question, and a deleted snapshot can't be brought back.

## Who Can Use Snapshots

Editors of the whole Space take, revert and delete snapshots. Signed in, an Editor sees them on History's timeline; an Editor who came in through a share link, with no History to read, sees the snapshots on their own. Participants, Viewers and people with a link to one tab don't see them. Snapshots are there on Spaces stored on the server: a Space kept **Offline** has none, and AI assistants and scripts using an API token can't use them.
