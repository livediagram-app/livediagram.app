A snapshot keeps the whole Space as it is right now, so you can try a big change (a reorganisation, an import, a clean-up with a teammate or an AI assistant) knowing you can put everything back in one step.

## Taking a snapshot

Press **Snapshots** (the camera) in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just before Settings. Give the snapshot a name if you like, such as "Before the workshop", and press **Take Snapshot**. Without a name it is called by the time it was taken.

Each snapshot lists its name, who took it, how long ago and its size. The latest **five** are kept: when five are there, the dialog says which one taking another will remove, and the oldest goes.

## Reverting to a snapshot

Press **Revert** on a snapshot's row and confirm. The Space goes back to exactly how it was: every tab, its folders and the slide deck. The Space's name, its comments and who it is shared with stay as they are; calls and who was there are not part of a snapshot.

Reverting replaces everyone's work since that snapshot, so first it takes a snapshot of the Space as it is now, marked **Before revert**. If you change your mind, revert to that one.

Everyone who has the Space open sees it reload onto the reverted version straight away, so nobody keeps working on what was replaced.

## Deleting a snapshot

Press the bin on a snapshot's row and confirm. A deleted snapshot can't be brought back.

## Who can use snapshots

Editors of the whole Space take, revert and delete snapshots. Participants, Viewers and people with a link to one tab don't see them. Snapshots are there on Spaces stored on the server: a Space kept in **This Browser** has none, and AI assistants and scripts using an API token can't use them.
