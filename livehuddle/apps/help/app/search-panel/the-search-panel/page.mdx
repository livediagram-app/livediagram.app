The **search panel** is the fastest way to get anywhere in livehuddle. It is one box that searches across everything you can reach and, when you are in the editor, can act on the canvas too. The scope adapts to where you are, so the same panel finds a space from the Explorer and an element on the canvas.

## Opening and closing search

- **Keyboard:** press `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `K`, or `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `.`. Both open the same panel. (`K` is the usual command-palette shortcut; `.` is the original binding, kept because browsers reserve `Cmd` + `T` for a new browser tab.) They work in the editor and on the Explorer page alike, including in view-only spaces. While you are typing in a text box (such as the Explorer's filter box) they are left to that box, and in the editor they are off when you turn off keyboard shortcuts in Settings.
- **In the editor:** open **Tools** in the bottom bar and choose **Search…**, the first row. The Explorer panel's **⋯** menu also has a **Search** row.
- **On the Explorer page:** click **Search** in the bottom bar, just before Settings. On a phone it is the magnifier alone.

The panel pops up near the top of the screen and blurs what is behind it so you can focus on the query. Press `Esc`, click the **Esc** chip at the end of the search box, or click outside the panel to close it.

## What search covers

Type a few letters and matches appear, grouped by what they are. Matching is a simple, case-insensitive "contains" match on the name, so `flow` finds "Checkout flow" and "Workflow". What is in scope depends on where you open it.

**In the editor**, the panel searches:

- **Spaces:** your [spaces](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-documents/), your own and those in your teams' libraries.
- **Teams:** when you are signed in, the [teams](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-teams/) you belong to, by name.
- **Every App:** once you type, what you can open in the other liveworkspace apps (and any of this app's the other groups miss, such as a space shared with you), each marked with its app's logo and name in the app's colour. Picking one opens it in its own app.
- **Tabs and Elements:** the [tabs and labelled elements](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-tabs-and-elements/) of the space you have open.

The results come in this order: **Spaces**, **Teams**, **Every App**, **Tabs**, **Elements**, then **Actions**, **Add to canvas**, **Settings** and **Help**. With an empty box, the panel lists your spaces, teams, and the space's tabs and labelled elements, so you can open it and arrow straight to something. Most groups show at most 8 results (12 for Elements, 10 for Actions and Add to canvas, 6 for Settings and Help), so keep typing to narrow a long list.

**On the Explorer page**, the panel searches **Items** (what you can open in every app, or only livehuddle's while the Explorer is showing just this app), **Folders** (yours and your teams', a team's marked "in" its name), **Teams**, **Help** and **Settings**, at most 8 of each. Nothing is listed until you type.

## Beyond navigation

In the editor the panel does more than jump around:

- **[Add to the canvas](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/):** search the palette and drop a shape or icon straight onto the canvas.
- **[Create a new tab](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-create-tab/):** an action result that spins up a fresh tab.
- **[Run any command](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/):** type an action's name (undo, auto-align, export, settings) and run it, or switch between Edit and Walk. The panel doubles as a command palette on `⌘K`.

These three only appear once you have typed something, and only inside a space. Two more groups work everywhere, in the editor and on the Explorer page:

- **Jump to a setting:** a **Settings** group matches the rows of the [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) dialog, by name and by the words people use for them, so "dark mode" finds **Theme**. Each result says which category it lives in ("in Appearance"), and picking it opens Settings on that row with the row highlighted.
- **Find the help for it:** a **Help** group lists matching articles from this help centre, so the explanation of a feature is reachable from the same box as the feature. Articles match on their search synonyms too, so "transparency" finds Opacity. Picking one opens the article in a new browser tab, so you do not lose your place.

In the editor the Help group always sits last, so a help article never steals the `Enter` from somewhere you were trying to go.

## Picking a result

Results are ordered by group, and the very first match is preselected, so for the common case you can just type and press `Enter`. Otherwise click a row, or press the `↑` and `↓` arrow keys to move the highlight and `Enter` to pick it. Picking a result takes you exactly where it points: opening a space or folder, switching to a tab, selecting an element, or jumping to a team's page in the Explorer. The panel closes as it goes.

## Read-only views

Search only navigates, so it works in a view-only space too: you can still find and jump to tabs and elements. **Add to canvas** results are not offered there, and the command palette keeps only the actions that do not change the canvas. See [The Command Palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/#in-read-only-views).
