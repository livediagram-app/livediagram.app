When you are signed in and belong to one or more [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/), the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/) reaches into them as well, so team work is as findable as your own.

## In the editor

The **Teams** group matches the teams you belong to by name, up to 8. Picking one opens that team's page in the Explorer.

A team's spaces are not listed under Teams in the editor: they are in the **Spaces** group, beside your own. See [Finding Spaces and Folders](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-documents/).

## On the Explorer page

On the [Explorer page](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), a team's work is spread across the groups it belongs in:

- **Teams** matches your teams by name. Picking one opens the team's page.
- **Folders** lists team folders beside your own, each marked **"in &lt;team&gt;"** so you can tell which team it belongs to at a glance. Picking one opens the team's page with that folder already open.
- **Items** finds a team's spaces by name, with everything else you can open.

<Figure caption="On the Explorer page: a team's space under Items, its folder marked with the team, and the team itself.">
  <SearchTeams />
</Figure>

Each group lists up to 8 matches, so type a little more of a name to narrow a long list. Folders are read as the panel opens, so a folder made while it is open shows the next time you open it.

## Signed-in only

Teams require an account, so team results only appear when you are signed in and belong to a team. Guests, and signed-in users with no teams, simply will not see them. The rest of search, your [spaces and folders](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-documents/), works the same either way.

<Tip>
  Searching is often faster than expanding the Teams tree in the Explorer: type a team or folder
  name and jump straight in.
</Tip>
