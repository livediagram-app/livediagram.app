Wherever you open the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/), it covers your own library, so you never have to remember which folder a space is in. Matching is on the name only, and any part of it will do: `board` finds "Sprint board" and "Onboarding".

## Spaces, in the editor

The **Spaces** group matches your spaces by name (a space you never named is listed as "Untitled Space"): your own, and those in the libraries of the [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/) you have joined. Pick one and it opens, the quickest way back into a space without going through the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/). The editor reads up to 100 of your spaces once the space you are in has loaded, and search filters those.

With an empty box the group lists your spaces straight away, so you can open search and arrow down to one.

## Shared with you, and other apps

A space someone shared with you is not in the Spaces group. Once you type, the **Every App** group below it lists what you can open anywhere in liveworkspace that the groups above missed: a space shared with you, and your work in the other apps, each marked with its app's logo and name. Picking one opens it on its own link, in its own app.

## On the Explorer page

On the [Explorer page](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), search has two groups for your library:

- **Items** matches what you can open by name: in every app, or only livehuddle's while the Explorer is showing just this app. Each row carries its app's logo. An archived one is marked **Archived**, and picking it opens the Archived view on it.
- **Folders** matches your [folders](/help/explorer/folders/) by name, and your teams' folders too, each marked "in" its team. Picking one opens it in the Explorer.

Nothing is listed there until you type.

## Limits

Each group lists up to 8 matches. If what you want is not among them, type a little more of its name.

<Tip>
  Search is owner-scoped: a guest finds the spaces their own browser owns; signing in brings your
  account's spaces into scope. A space saved only in [This
  Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) is not on the server, so search does not find
  it: open it from the Explorer's This Browser list. Search only looks at names, never inside other
  spaces, so to find a word on a space, open that space and search there.
</Tip>
