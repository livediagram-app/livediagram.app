Avatar mode drops a small pixel character onto the canvas and lets you walk it around. Instead of waving a cursor at the part of the canvas you are describing, you walk over to it, so the room follows a character moving between places. Press **Walk** on the Edit | Walk toggle at the very start of the palette strip, or press `W` while you are editing. A [Selection Mode button](/help/palette/behaviour/mode-buttons/) on the board starts it too, and so does typing "walk" in Search. A space made from a Spaces template (Virtual Office, Coworking Space, Team Pod, All-Hands Hall and the rest) opens with you already walking, arriving on its [Avatar Pad](/help/palette/avatar-pads/), in the middle of the Space.

## The Edit | Walk toggle

If you can edit a space, the toggle at the start of the strip is Avatar mode's switch. While you build, **Edit** is on; press **Walk** and the strip becomes the avatar palette, with the same toggle at its very start, Walk on. Press **Edit** there (or `E`, `V` or `Esc`) and the building palette comes back. While **Start a Space** is open, Walk is greyed out: pick a template, or close Start a Space, to walk. See [Edit and Walk](/help/selection-modes/select/).

Every Space from the templates has an Avatar Pad, and so does every blank tab you start (Blank Space, or closing **Start a Space**). Whenever the board has one, you start on the pad, beamed in.

## Walking

**Click anywhere on the canvas and the character walks there**, in a straight line, facing the way it is going. Click somewhere else while it is still walking and it turns and heads for the new spot instead. Walk onto a [Chair, Desk or Sofa](/help/palette/chairs/) and the character sits; press an arrow key or double-click to stand. A [Fence](/help/palette/fences/) stops it: characters cannot walk through one, nor through a room's walls (only its doorway). Walk up to a water cooler or a coffee machine and your character gets a drink; see [Virtual Office](/help/palette/virtual-office/).

**The arrow keys steer it directly**: hold one and the character walks that way for as long as you hold it, and holding two walks a diagonal. Release and it stops where it stands. On a touchscreen, tapping is the way to move it.

**Changing tab ends Avatar mode.** The character belongs to the board you are walking round, so switching to another tab (from the tab bar, the Explorer, a portal or by following someone) puts you back in Edit, and the character disappears for everyone. Press **Walk** again to walk on the new tab. Participants and view-only visitors are always walking, so they keep walking on the new tab.

When the character ends up standing on an element, that element gets a soft ring around it, so your audience can see exactly which box you have arrived at. The canvas scrolls to follow the character when it gets close to the edge of the view, and the zoom is left alone.

## Jump and wave a flag

Press **`Space`** and the character hops, waving a small flag on the way up and for a beat after it lands. It is there for the moment in a walkthrough when you want to say "this bit here" with a little more emphasis than a ring around a box, and it works well for a round of applause at the end of a review.

One press is one hop: holding the key down will not pogo, and pressing again mid-air does nothing until the character is back on the ground.

While you walk you are in the room rather than editing it, so **Undo**, **Redo** and **Tab Style** step out of the bottom-right corner, and Cmd/Ctrl+Z does nothing. Leave Avatar mode and they come straight back.

## Talk, show your camera, share your screen

The call's buttons sit at the **left end of the avatar palette**, just after the Edit | Walk toggle (an Editor's alone) and before your look: **Join Audio** (or **Listen In**), then once you are in your microphone, **Camera**, **Share** and **Leave**. On a phone they stay put while the rest of the palette swipes. See [Proximity Audio](/help/collaboration/proximity-audio/) and [Cameras and Screen Sharing](/help/collaboration/cameras-and-screen-sharing/).

## Make the character yours

While you walk, **the palette at the top of the screen dresses your character** instead of building the board. Its buttons on the left are your look, each opening a panel of pictures of your own character wearing each choice:

- **Gender**: a large portrait of your character, a **Randomise** button for a whole new look, and **Male** or **Female**.
- **Clothes**: sixteen outfits: T-shirt, stripes, jumper, hoodie, vest, suit, dress, skirt, polo, flannel, overalls, lab coat, Hawaiian, varsity, turtleneck, or apron.
- **Hair**: fourteen styles: short, buzz, curly, long, ponytail, bun, mohawk, bald, pigtails, afro, spiky, bob, braid, or top knot. At the bottom, **Hair Colour** has a row of swatches: black, dark brown, brown, auburn, ginger, blonde, platinum, grey and white, plus blue, pink, purple and green for something bolder. The styles above are drawn in the colour you pick.
- **Status**: how you are (see below), and your **Jukebox Music** switch.

While you [share your screen](/help/collaboration/cameras-and-screen-sharing/), **Gender**, **Clothes** and **Hair** step aside to make room for **Sharing** and **Stop Sharing**, and come back when the share ends.

Point at a button (or tap it) to open its panel, and pick anything: the character on the canvas changes as you go, and the panel stays open so you can try the next. Stop walking and the usual palette comes straight back.

**The first time you use the mode you get a random character** (a random gender, outfit, hairstyle and natural hair colour, at the regular size), so you are not the same figure as everyone else by default. From then on it is yours: **your choices are remembered in this browser**, so the character you build is the one waiting the next time you enter the mode, in this space or any other.

Press anywhere outside a panel, or press `Esc`, to close it. The avatar palette is there on a phone too (swipe it sideways), and for everyone who walks, view-only visitors included.

**Right-clicking the character** on the canvas is a shortcut for switching gender without opening the palette. Right-clicking anywhere else does nothing while you are in this mode (the usual context menus stay shut so a menu cannot interrupt a walkthrough).

There is no shirt colour choice, on purpose: the character's shirt takes **your participant colour** (the same colour as your cursor and your name in the tab bar), so on a shared space it is always obvious which character is yours.

## Actions and React

On the right of the avatar palette are two more buttons, each a panel of one-click actions.

**Actions** makes the character perform on the spot:

- **Jumping jacks**: five of them.
- **Wave**: a raised hand, waving.
- **Spin**: two turns on the spot.
- **Cheer**: arms up, bouncing.
- **Dance**: a side-to-side sway.

They are quick on purpose (a gesture, not a cutscene), and everyone else on a shared space sees the performance too. Starting one stops whatever walk was in progress, so the character performs where it stands.

**React** throws a burst around your character: **Confetti**, **Sparkles**, **Hearts**, **Applause** or **Fireworks**. The burst plays on your own screen.

On a keyboard, each action is a single key, so you can perform without opening the palette. Each action shows its key:

- **Actions**: `J` for jumping jacks, `W` to wave, `S` to spin, `C` to cheer and `D` to dance.
- **React**: `1` to `5` throw Confetti, Sparkles, Hearts, Applause and Fireworks, in the palette's order.

The keys do nothing while you are typing a message or anything else. `W` waves here, rather than starting a walk, and `E`, `V` and `Escape` still take you out of Avatar mode.

## Show your status

Open **Status** on the avatar palette and pick one to let people know how you are, without a word. Each status shows on your character too, the way headphones go on when you're focusing. Point at one to try it on in the big preview beside the menu before you pick it.

**Availability**

- **Available**: a green sparkle twinkles by your head.
- **Need Help**: a yellow question mark bobs over your head, so people know to come over.
- **Away**: your character fades and a little "Zz" floats up.
- **Do Not Disturb**: a red no-entry sign by your head. No voices or speech bubbles reach you, and you can still be heard if you speak.

**Activity**

- **Focusing**: your character puts on headphones.
- **In a Meeting**: a headset with a microphone.
- **Out to Lunch**: a sandwich in your hand, with a bite every few seconds.

**Mood**

- **Angry**: steam puffs out of your ears, your face goes red and your brows come down.
- **Celebrating**: a party hat, with confetti falling around you.
- **Thinking**: a thought bubble, its dots lighting up one by one.
- **Feeling Sick**: a green face, an ice pack on your head and a bead of sweat.

Everyone on the space sees it, next to your name. It lasts until you change it or leave walking, and it is never saved. If you've turned on reduced motion, the steam, sparkle and the rest hold still.

## Say something

Press **`Enter`** and a speech bubble opens above your character's head with a text box in it. Type up to **50 words** and press **`Enter`** again (or the send button) and the bubble lets go of your head and floats slowly upward, swaying a little, until it fades away. Press **`Esc`** to cancel instead.

Everyone on the tab sees your bubble float up from where you were standing. Each bubble has a border in the speaker's colour and their name in small type under the message, so you can tell who said what even after they've walked off. Longer messages stay up longer, up to about ten seconds.

You can keep walking while you type: the bubble follows your character until you send it. There's a short pause of a second and a half between one message and the next.

On a phone, or if you'd rather not use the keyboard, press **Say** at the right end of the avatar palette: type in the box and press **Send**.

Messages aren't saved anywhere. Once a bubble has floated away it's gone, for everyone.

Speaking is for people who can edit or take part in the space. Someone with a view-only link sees everyone's bubbles but can't send one.

## The canvas is read-only while you walk

Every click means "walk over there", so nothing on the canvas can be selected, moved, resized, or edited while Avatar mode is on, and the element shortcuts stay dormant. That is deliberate: you can click around the canvas freely mid-presentation without any chance of nudging a shape out of place.

**To edit again, stop walking.** Press **Edit** at the start of the avatar palette, and the building palette comes back. Press `E`, `V` or **`Esc`** to put the character away too.

Panning still works the usual ways while walking: hold `Space` and drag, middle-mouse drag, a two-finger trackpad scroll, or the zoom controls.

## Availability

Avatar mode is available on desktop and touch. If you can edit a space, the Edit | Walk toggle at the start of the strip is its switch: press **Walk** to start walking on any board, and **Edit** to stop. A board's Avatar button, or searching for **Walk**, starts it too. **Participants and view-only visitors are always walking**: their space opens with their character on it, they never see the toggle, and nothing takes them out of the mode. A space with an Avatar Pad (or an Avatar Button) opens walking once each visit, for everyone, unless the pad is all it holds yet: a blank tab opens on your usual tool, so you can build; an editor who leaves the mode stays out until they come back. An embedded space never opens walking.

While you walk as an avatar, press Start Avatar Tour in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just left of **Search**, for a short [Avatar Tour](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/avatar-tour/) of all this.

<Tip>
  Everyone on a shared space can walk around at the same time, and you can see each other's
  characters. See [Walking Together](/help/selection-modes/walking-together/).
</Tip>

To make a board a place to walk round, drop an **Avatar Button** on the canvas (the palette's **Travel**
category; Spaces made before the Avatar Pads carry one called **Walk In**). It reads "Switch to Avatar", so one click puts a visitor in
the mode, and the character appears right at the button they pressed, or on the board's
[Avatar Pad](/help/palette/avatar-pads/) when it has one. Pressing it again while in the
mode puts them back in Edit. See [Selection Mode Buttons](/help/palette/behaviour/mode-buttons/).

Portals work here too: walk your character into a [Portal](/help/palette/tools/portals/) and you come
out of the one it is linked to, even on another tab.
