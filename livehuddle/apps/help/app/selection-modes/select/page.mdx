If you can edit a Space, the palette strip starts with a small toggle in two halves: **Edit** (a pointer arrow) and **Walk** (footprints). It is your one switch between building the board and being in it. On a computer each half shows its icon and its name; on a phone, the icons alone.

- **Edit** is plain selecting and building: pick elements, move them, resize them, type into them, and add new ones from the palette's categories.
- **Walk** puts you in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/): you walk the Space as your character, talk to whoever is near, sit at a desk and react.

## Switching between them

While you build, Edit is on. Press **Walk** and your character appears, and the strip becomes the avatar palette (your character's look, Actions and React, with the call controls on a Call dock at the bottom). The same toggle sits at the very start of that strip with Walk on, so pressing **Edit** brings the building palette straight back.

On a keyboard:

- **`W`** walks, from Edit.
- **`E`** goes back to Edit. **`V`** and **`Esc`** do too.

While you walk, `W` is Wave (one of your character's actions), so `W` only starts walking from Edit.

You can also type "Edit" or "Walk" in the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/).

While **Start a Space** is open, the Walk half is greyed out: pick a template, or close Start a Space, to walk. Edit always works.

**Participants and view-only visitors are always walking**, so they never see the toggle. See [Avatar Mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/).

## What you can do in Edit

- **Click** an element to select it. Click an empty patch of canvas, or press `Esc`, to clear the selection.
- **Shift-click** to add elements to the selection, or take them out again.
- **Drag a marquee** from empty canvas to select many elements at once. Only elements that sit entirely inside the box are picked up. See [Multi-select](/help/canvas/selecting-and-grouping/multi-select/).
- **Drag** an element to move it, or press the arrow keys to nudge the selection by 1 pixel (10 with `Shift`).
- **Drag its handles** to resize.
- **Double-click** an element to edit its label. With one element selected you can also just start typing: the first key opens the label and replaces what was there.

## Moving around the canvas

There is no separate tool for panning. In Edit, or while you walk:

- Hold `Space` and drag.
- Scroll with two fingers on a trackpad.
- Drag with the middle mouse button (unless it is turned off in Settings).
- On a phone or tablet, drag one finger across empty canvas.

See [Pan and Zoom](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/).

## Removing things

To remove elements, select them and press `Delete` or `Backspace`, or press Delete on the selection toolbar above them. Locked elements are kept. [Undo](/help/canvas/undo/) brings back anything you removed by mistake.

## Copying a look

To copy one element's style onto others, select it and press **Format Painter** on its selection toolbar. See [Format Painter](/help/selection-modes/format-painter/).

<Tip>
