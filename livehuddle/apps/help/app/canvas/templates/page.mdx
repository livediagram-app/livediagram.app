A blank canvas is freeing, but a meeting needs a place: rooms to walk into, a table to sit round, somewhere for everyone to arrive. Templates give you a ready-made Space as a starting point, so you can begin from somewhere shaped roughly like your team and adapt it, instead of building every room from nothing.

## Starting from a Template

When you [create a new space](/new), the space is made there and then, saved where new spaces go (see [Default Folders](/help/explorer/folders/default-folders/)). The empty tab then opens on **Start a Space**, on its **Recommended** templates, with the catalogue beside them and a search. Click a template and the tab fills with it; from there it is an ordinary canvas, to move, edit, add to and delete from as on any canvas.

After each category's templates, Start a Space shows up to three Spaces people shared to the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/) under that category, the month's most liked (each with at least five likes in the past 30 days), marked **Community** with who made them. Choose one to make your own copy of it and open it; the tab you started from is left as it was. They show in a search too, by their titles. **Recommended** and the counts beside each category are templates only.

Every new tab, and every tab you empty, offers the same start. Close it with its **×** to begin from a blank space: the tab gets an [Avatar Pad](/help/palette/avatar-pads/), a little below the middle, where people arrive, just as **Blank Space** gives it. If you take the pad away again and the tab is empty, **Show Templates** in the strip under the palette brings the start back.

Every one of the twenty-five Spaces is made to walk round in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/). The Hotel and Suites and the Co-working Tower are buildings with a tab for each floor: ride a named [Elevator](/help/palette/elevator/) between them, or in the Co-working Tower take the [Stairs](/help/palette/stairs/) a floor up or down. The **Homes** have a tab for each floor too, **Downstairs** and **Upstairs** (and the City Townhouse's **Roof Terrace**), joined only by the Stairs in the hall, standing in the same place on every floor.

The **Campus** Spaces and the **Venues** are whole sites: every room has a door onto a garden path or the open floor and a Room Sign beside it, and a **Directory** just inside the gate or the front doors lists every room with **Walk There**, so you can find your way from the gate to the bar without a map. The **Games Rooms** are built round the pieces you can play: walk up to an [Arcade Machine](/help/palette/virtual-office/) and press it for Moon Mail, or sit at a Ping-Pong Table to play whoever sits opposite.

**Find a template** finds a template by its name, its description, its category, or the everyday words people use for it: try "office", "garden", "drinks", "arcade", "house" or "all hands". Every word you type has to match.

**Blank Space** leads the **Popular** shelf, apart from the categories since starting from nothing is not a kind of Space: a tab to furnish yourself, holding only an [Avatar Pad](/help/palette/avatar-pads/) a little below the middle where people arrive, with a Timer or Stopwatch to place and Bring Focus to hand (see [Session Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/)). Closing **Start a Space** with its **×** starts from the same blank tab and pad. After it, Popular shows the Spaces most people start from.

Each template arrives as real elements rather than a picture: a room's walls, a table's chairs and the sticky notes on the wall are elements you drag, and an [Avatar Pad](/help/palette/avatar-pads/) in the middle of the Space (its busiest room, its lobby or its lawn) is where everyone arrives as an avatar. A Space with floors has one on every floor, by the lifts. Nothing in a template is locked or special.

## Changing the Theme

A new space starts on the Default theme, and a template picked on a start takes the tab's current theme. Once you are on the canvas, click the **Theme & canvas** button in the bottom-right corner to open the **Tab Style** dialog and try a different [theme](/help/canvas/themes/): it recolours the whole template at once.

## Making It Your Own

A template is just a head start, not a constraint. Everything in it is fully editable: add rooms and furniture from the palette's **Rooms**, **Seats** and **Perks** categories, move the walls, restyle the whole thing with a different theme, or strip out the parts you do not need. Because the canvas works the same as any other, you can also add [tabs](/help/tabs/using-tabs/), invite others, and share it once you have shaped it into what you want.

<Tip>
  Templates pair naturally with themes: pick a Space that fits your team, then try a few themes from
  the Tab Style dialog to find the look you want. Double-click a theme to apply it and close the
  dialog in one go.
</Tip>
