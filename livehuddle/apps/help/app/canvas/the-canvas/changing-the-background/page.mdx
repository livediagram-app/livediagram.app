Each tab is a floor you walk around on, and each has its own: the floor itself, a canvas colour, a strength and a size, all stored per tab. You change them from the **Tab Style** dialog, a focused modal that gives these controls plenty of room.

## Opening the Dialog

Click the **Theme & canvas** button in the bottom-right corner of the editor, beside the zoom controls (it is there on every screen size, phones included). The **Tab Style** dialog opens on its home; press **Canvas** under **Fine-tune** to open the floor controls, and the **Tab Style** bar across the panel's top to return. You can also run **Open Canvas Options** from the [command palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/), which opens the dialog straight on the Canvas panel.

The button is only there for people who can edit the tab, so it does not appear for a Participant or a Viewer on a shared link, or in an embed.

Every control applies to the active tab as you click, so the canvas updates behind the dialog. There is no Apply or Cancel step: the dialog is an editor, not a wizard, and closing it with the X, Escape, or a click outside it simply dismisses it. Each change is an ordinary edit, so undo takes it back.

## The Canvas Panel

The Canvas panel gathers the floor controls:

- **Floor** is a carousel of fifteen choices, each tile drawn in the floor itself (nine to a page on a wider screen, six on a phone). **None** leaves the plain canvas colour. Then the indoor floors: **Carpet**, **Carpet Tiles**, **Wood Floor**, **Parquet**, **Tiles**, **Marble**, **Terrazzo** and **Concrete**; and the outdoor ones: **Decking**, **Stone Paving**, **Cobbles**, **Grass**, **Sand** and **Mud**. Hover a tile to see what it is for.
- **Colours** offers a **Canvas** swatch: the colour under the floor, which is all you see with None and shows through a floor below full strength. It opens the colour picker: the theme's palette, the house colours, the colours already used in the canvas, and **Add a custom colour** (four coloured dots) for one of your own.
- **Strength** is a slider with a percentage readout that fades the floor into the canvas colour, from the full floor at 100% to the plain canvas at 0%.
- **Size** is a slider (50% to 200%) that scales the floor, so the planks, tiles or cobbles read larger or smaller. None has nothing to scale.

A floor brings its own colours (grass is green, oak is honey), in a light version and a dark one: a dark canvas, such as a theme's dark half when you use dark mode, gets the dark floor. Every floor is kept quiet, so furniture, avatars and text stay easy to read on top of it, and it stays put under your pieces as you pan and zoom.

## The Other Panels

The same dialog also hosts the **Colours** panel, where every theme, your own custom ones included, sits in one list to recolour the whole tab. Picking a theme repaints the canvas colour to match, but keeps any canvas colour, floor or strength you set yourself, the same way it keeps hand-set element colours. Learn more in [Themes](/help/canvas/themes/). The **Text** panel sets the tab's default typeface and text size, and the twenty-nine **Looks** on the dialog's home set the theme, finish, font and floor together in one click; see [Choosing Fonts](/help/canvas/text-and-fonts/choosing-fonts/).

<Tip>
  The floor is per tab, so different tabs in one space can carry different floors, for instance
  carpet tiles for the office and grass for the garden out the back. A new tab starts with the
  floor, colour and strength of the tab you added it from.
</Tip>
