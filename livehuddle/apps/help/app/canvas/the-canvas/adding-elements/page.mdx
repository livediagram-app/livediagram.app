Every element on the canvas starts from the palette. The palette organises its contents into categories so it stays compact however much it holds, and adding an element is usually a single tap.

## Picking a Category

The palette is the strip across the top of the canvas. Its categories sit along it by name: rest the pointer on one, or click it, and its elements open in a panel underneath, in named groups, with a line on what each one does. The categories are:

- **Write**: the words, text and sticky notes.
- **Frame**: the frame, a labelled box that groups a room's notes.
- **Navigate**: what people walk round and through, such as portals, Bring Focus, chairs, desks, sofas, fences, Avatar Pads, link cards, and the office pieces (rooms, meeting tables, plants and more).
- **React**: the five Reaction Pads.
- **Selection Mode**: the buttons that hand whoever presses them a different tool.
- **Icons**: a searchable set of single-colour icons, its groups down the side, that you can drop on the canvas or onto an element.
- **Stickers**: colour emoji and word badges.
- **Media**: an uploaded or searched-for image, and the link card.

The palette opens on **Popular**, the elements a meeting reaches for most. See [Where each tool lives](/help/palette/tools/) for the full map. A Participant on a shared link gets one category instead, **Participate**, with sticky notes, text and an image to add to the board.

## Dropping or Drawing an Element

Almost every tile arms a combined gesture when you click it.

<Step n={1}>
  Click a tile in the palette. It stays pressed to show it is armed, and a banner along the top of
  the canvas says what comes next ("Click to drop or drag to draw" and the element's name, "Tap" on
  a phone), with a Cancel button.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Tap an empty spot on the canvas to drop the element at its natural size on that point.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Or, instead of a single tap, drag out a box and release to create the element at that size. Both
  corners snap to nearby elements as you drag.
</Step>

While a tile is armed you can even start the drag on top of another element: the new one is drawn over it rather than selecting it. Press Escape (or Cancel on the banner) to disarm a tile without adding anything. Text lands ready for typing; everything else lands selected, ready to move or restyle. An **Image** lands as an empty placeholder: press its **Select Image** button when you are ready to add the picture.

If a boxed element is already selected when you tap a tile, the new one inherits its width and height (a drag sets its own size, and a selected frame never passes its size on), so you can chain together similarly sized notes quickly.

You can also **drag a tile straight from the palette onto the canvas** instead of tapping. As you drag, a translucent preview of the element follows your cursor at its real size, so you can see exactly where, and how big, it will land before you let go.

## Adding from the Keyboard

Single keys arm the most common elements without opening the palette: `T` for text, `N` for a sticky note, `F` for a frame and `9` for an image. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) for the full list. With one labelled element selected, a letter key types into its label instead. The keys add nothing for a Participant or a Viewer. You can also **double-click empty canvas** to drop a text element and start typing straight away.

<Tip>
  Icons can be added to an element rather than standing alone: drag an icon tile onto a sticky note,
  or click an icon while one is selected, and it attaches beside that note's label.
</Tip>
