Every element on the canvas starts from the palette. The palette organises its contents into categories so it stays compact however much it holds, and adding an element is usually a single tap.

## Picking a Category

The palette is the strip across the top of the canvas. Its categories sit along it by name: rest the pointer on one, or click it, and its elements open in a panel underneath, in named groups, with a line on what each one does. The categories are:

- **Write**: the words, text and sticky notes.
- **Rooms**: what a floor is laid out with. It opens on **Build**, the Room, the Room Builder and the Door, then the stages, fences, office pods and rugs.
- **Seats**: what people sit at: chairs, desks, sofas and meeting tables.
- **Perks**: what makes it feel like a place: plants, bookshelves, water coolers and coffee machines.
- **Play**: somewhere to unwind: the bar and its stools, the games, the jukebox and the fireplace.
- **Garden** and **Meet**: the outdoors and what a meeting room needs.
- **Travel**: what takes people somewhere: portals, Bring Focus, Avatar Pads, the Directory, Signposts, Elevators and the Avatar Button.
- **Stickers**: colour emoji and word badges, found through **Search** (press **S**) rather than on the strip.

The palette opens on **Popular**, the elements a meeting reaches for most. See [Where each tool lives](/help/palette/tools/) for the full map. A Participant on a shared link has no palette: they walk the space as their avatar and add nothing to it.

## Dropping or Drawing an Element

Almost every tile arms a combined gesture when you click it.

<Step n={1}>
  Click a tile in the palette. It stays pressed to show it is armed, and a banner along the top of
  the canvas says what comes next ("Click to drop or drag to draw" and the element's name, "Tap" on
  a phone), with a Cancel button.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Tap an empty spot on the canvas to drop the element at its natural size on that point.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Or, instead of a single tap, drag out a box and release to create the element at that size. Both
  corners snap to nearby elements as you drag.
</Step>

While a tile is armed you can even start the drag on top of another element: the new one is drawn over it rather than selecting it. Press Escape (or Cancel on the banner) to disarm a tile without adding anything. Text lands ready for typing; everything else lands selected, ready to move or restyle. An **Image** lands as an empty placeholder: press its **Select Image** button when you are ready to add the picture.

If a boxed element is already selected when you tap a tile, the new one inherits its width and height (a drag sets its own size), so you can chain together similarly sized notes quickly.

You can also **drag a tile straight from the palette onto the canvas** instead of tapping. As you drag, a translucent preview of the element follows your cursor at its real size, so you can see exactly where, and how big, it will land before you let go.

## Adding from the Keyboard

Single keys arm the most common elements without opening the palette: `T` for text and `N` for a sticky note. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) for the full list. With one labelled element selected, a letter key types into its label instead. The keys add nothing for a Participant or a Viewer. You can also **double-click empty canvas** to drop a text element and start typing straight away.
