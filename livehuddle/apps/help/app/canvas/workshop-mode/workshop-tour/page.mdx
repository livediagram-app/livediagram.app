The Workshop tour shows you where everything for running a session lives. Like the [Diagram Tour](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/), it works on the real editor rather than screenshots: it spotlights each part with a highlight ring and a short explanation. It adds nothing to your canvas, takes under a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## Starting the Tour

The tour never starts or pops up on its own. When you're in [Workshop mode](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/) on a tab you can edit, press Start Workshop Tour in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just left of **Search**. On a phone the button shows only its play icon.

The button doesn't appear on a locked tab, in an embed, or for someone who can only view or take part. If you have a complex element selected, the button offers that element's tour instead; select nothing to get the Workshop tour back. It stays there after you've taken the tour, so you can press it again whenever you like.

## What It Covers

1. **Collaborate**: **Ask** and **Session**, the palette's session kit after Layout: Ask holds votes, polls and quizzes, and Session the tools, records, reactions, selection modes and places to go. See [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/). The rest of the palette is the [Diagram Tour](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/)'s.
2. **Run the Room**: the **Timer**, **Vote** and **Poll** buttons in the bottom-right corner, which run for everyone without placing anything. See [Session Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).
3. **Bring People In**: the **Share** button, where a Participant link lets people add notes, vote and answer without changing the rest. Left out where there is no Share button.

Each step has **Next**, **Back** and **Skip tour**. The last card, **You're ready to run a workshop**, links to the [Workshop mode](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/) article and ends the tour with **Start**. If you leave Workshop mode or switch tab mid-tour, it ends as skipped.

## Replaying the Tour

Press Start Workshop Tour again, or start it from Settings:
