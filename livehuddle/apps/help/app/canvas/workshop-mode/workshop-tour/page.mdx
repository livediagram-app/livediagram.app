The Workshop tour shows you where everything for running a session lives. It works on the real editor rather than screenshots: it spotlights each part with a highlight ring and a short explanation. It adds nothing to your canvas, takes under a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## Starting the Tour

The tour never starts or pops up on its own. On a tab you can edit, press Start Workshop Tour in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just left of **Search**. On a phone the button shows only its play icon.

The button doesn't appear on a locked tab, in an embed, or for someone who can only view or take part. If you have a complex element selected, the button offers that element's tour instead; select nothing to get the Workshop tour back. Once you've taken the tour (finished or skipped it), it leaves the button, which then offers another tour you haven't taken or isn't shown. To take it again, switch it back on in Settings (see below).

## What It Covers

1. **Collaborate**: **Ask** and **Session**, the palette's session kit after Layout: Ask holds votes, polls and quizzes, and Session the tools, records, reactions, selection modes and places to go. See [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/).
2. **Run the Room**: the **Timer**, **Vote** and **Poll** buttons in the bottom-right corner, which run for everyone without placing anything. See [Session Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).
3. **Bring People In**: the **Share** button, where a Participant link lets people add notes, vote and answer without changing the rest. Left out where there is no Share button.

Each step has **Next**, **Back** and **Skip tour**. The last card, **You're ready to run a workshop**, links to the [Workshop mode](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/) article and ends the tour with **Start**. If you switch tab mid-tour, it ends as skipped.

## Replaying the Tour

Once taken, the tour stays off the button until you switch it back on in Settings:

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Tours**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>Switch **Workshop Tour** back on.</Step>

<Step n={3}>Close Settings. On a tab you can edit, the tour starts straight away.</Step>
