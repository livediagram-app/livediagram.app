**Workshop mode** is how every tab in livehuddle works, made for running a session with your team: a retrospective, a town hall, a lean coffee, a planning workshop or a quick icebreaker. It puts the tools that run a room in front of you (timers, dot votes, polls, reveals, quizzes, stickers).

It is on for everyone and on every tab: there is nothing to switch or turn on in Settings. It used to be called **Facilitate mode**.

## What Workshop Mode Gives You

- **The palette** offers Shapes, **Write** (text, notes, tables and checklists), **Draw** (the pens and arrows) and **Layout** (frames, lanes, timelines, cards and badges), then the session kit: **Ask** (votes, polls, quizzes, estimates and the Q&A board) and **Session**, which holds **Tools**, **Record**, **React**, **Selection Mode** and **Navigate** down its side, from the timer and reveal to the agenda, the reactions and the places Avatars walk to. Session's search looks through all of them. After them come Icons, **Stickers**, Media and **Data**. Write and Draw keep what a session uses, leaving out Page and Annotation; search still adds either. See [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/).
- **The bottom-right corner** gains the **Timer**, **Vote** and **Poll** buttons, right after Undo and Redo. See [Session Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).
- **Everything else** works as you would expect on a canvas: selection, arrows, text, sticky notes, snapping, undo and shortcuts.

## Starting a Session

- **A new canvas:** the empty tab opens on **Start a Workshop**: the session templates grouped by the same [categories](/help/canvas/templates/) as every start, such as **Retrospectives**, **Meetings & Workshops**, **Virtual Spaces**, **Quizzes & Games** and **Strategy**, with **Recommended** first. **Blank Workshop** (or the **×**) leaves one empty tab.
- **Session templates:** the retrospectives (Retrospective, Start, Stop, Continue, Mad, Sad, Glad, 4Ls and Sailboat), Town Hall Q&A, Lean Coffee, Crazy Eights, Meeting Agenda, the Workshop Board and the Brainstorm Board, the boards that come with a dot vote (Affinity Map, Prioritization Matrix and Risk Matrix), so the vote is there to run from the Session strip, the boards a team fills in together in a session (Incident Postmortem, Objectives Planner, Roadmap, RACI Matrix, User Story Map, Business Model Canvas, Stakeholder Map, Value Proposition Canvas, Opportunity Solution Tree and User Persona), and the sticky-note boards a group fills in together, such as SWOT, PESTLE Analysis, KWL Chart, Empathy Map and User Journey. So are the quizzes and games: a Pub Quiz of timed [quiz](/help/palette/collaborate/quizzes/) rounds with a scoreboard, Icebreaker Questions spun on [pickers](/help/palette/behaviour/pickers/), Two Truths and a Lie and Would You Rather on live polls, and Find Someone Who Bingo. So are the spaces to walk round together in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/): the Virtual Office, a Coworking Space, a Team Pod and an All-Hands Hall.

<Tip>
  Share a Participant pass with everyone in the session, so they can vote, answer polls and add
  sticky notes, and hand the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) baton to whoever is
  running it, so only they start and end the tools.
</Tip>

## Take the Tour

Press Start Workshop Tour in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just left of **Search**, for a short [Workshop tour](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/workshop-tour/): the Collaborate categories, the Session strip and Share. It never starts on its own, and once you've taken it, it leaves the button until you switch it back on in **Settings**, under **Tours**.
