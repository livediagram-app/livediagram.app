An **Avatar Pad** is the entrance to a board in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/). While a tab has one, everybody who starts walking appears standing on the pad, rather than wherever they happened to be looking, so a group arriving for a session all starts in the same place.

It lives in the palette's **Travel** category, one of the [meeting elements](/help/palette/behaviour/), beside the [Portal](/help/palette/tools/portals/), Bring Focus and the Directory, and search adds one too.

## Every Space starts with one

You rarely need to add the first one yourself:

- Every [template](/help/canvas/templates/) has an Avatar Pad in the middle of the action, so you arrive where the people are: in the middle of the Virtual Office's corridor, in the Team Pod between the desks and the table, in the aisle of a hall facing its stage, on a garden's lawn, in a building's lobby, with a line beside it saying what to do once you arrive. A Space with floors has one on every floor too, by the lifts, so changing floor from the tabs still lands you somewhere sensible.
- A blank tab gets one when you start it blank: choose **Blank Space** on **Start a Space**, or close it with its **×**. The pad sits a little below the middle of the view, so the middle stays free to build in. A tab holding nothing but its pad opens on your usual tool rather than walking, since there is nothing to walk round yet.
- A new tab still opens on **Start a Space** first; the pad arrives with whatever you choose there.

## Setting up an entrance

1. Add an Avatar Pad and put it where people should arrive: by a welcome note, in the lobby of a [Virtual Office](/help/palette/virtual-office/), at the start of a tour. Or move the one the Space started with.
2. Press **Walk**, or click the pad itself. Your character beams in on the pad, and the view moves to it if it was somewhere else on the board. Participants and view-only visitors are always walking, so they arrive on it as they come in, and a Space with a pad and something to walk round opens walking for everyone.

## Arriving

- Every time someone goes into Avatar mode on the tab, they start on the pad, even if they were walking somewhere else a minute ago.
- They arrive in a beam of light in their own colour, with a few sparks rising out of it. Everybody on the tab sees it, so you can tell who has just walked in.
- If the board has **several pads**, you start on the one nearest the middle of your view, so a big board can have an entrance per area.
- Placing, moving or deleting a pad never moves anyone already walking about. It only changes where people arrive next time.
- With **reduced motion** turned on, there is no beam: your character simply appears on the pad.

On a board without a pad, a character starts just below the [Selection Mode button](/help/palette/behaviour/mode-buttons/) you pressed (Spaces made before the pads carry one called **Walk In**), or in the middle of your view.

## Who can do what

- **Editors** place, move, resize and delete pads.
- **Participants** and **Viewers** cannot change a pad, but they start on it and see everyone arrive, the same as an Editor.

## The look

The pad is drawn in the same chunky pixel style and colours as the characters themselves: a round platform with a ring of cyan light set into it that shimmers gently. It has no label and no **…** on its face. It lies on the floor, behind furniture, and characters walk over it freely. Otherwise it is an ordinary element: move, resize (it keeps its shape), rotate, lock, copy and export.
