An **Avatar Pad** is the entrance to a board in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/). While a tab has one, everybody who starts walking appears standing on the pad, rather than wherever they happened to be looking, so a group arriving for a session all starts in the same place.

It lives in the palette's **Travel** category, one of the [meeting elements](/help/palette/behaviour/), beside the [Portal](/help/palette/tools/portals/), Bring Focus and the Link card, and search adds one too.

## Setting up an entrance

1. Add an Avatar Pad and put it where people should arrive: by a welcome note, in the lobby of a [Virtual Office](/help/palette/virtual-office/), at the start of a tour.
2. Add a [Selection Mode button](/help/palette/behaviour/mode-buttons/) set to Avatar, so people have a way in. Anyone can pick Avatar from the Selection Mode menu too, but the button is the door people see, and a space with one opens walking.
3. Press the button. Your character beams in on the pad, and the view moves to it if it was somewhere else on the board.

## Arriving

- Every time someone goes into Avatar mode on the tab, they start on the pad, even if they were walking somewhere else a minute ago.
- They arrive in a beam of light in their own colour, with a few sparks rising out of it. Everybody on the tab sees it, so you can tell who has just walked in.
- If the board has **several pads**, you start on the one nearest the middle of your view, so a big board can have an entrance per area.
- Placing, moving or deleting a pad never moves anyone already walking about. It only changes where people arrive next time.
- With **reduced motion** turned on, there is no beam: your character simply appears on the pad.

On a board without a pad, a character starts just below the button you pressed.

## Who can do what

- **Editors** place, move, resize and delete pads.
- **Participants** and **Viewers** cannot change a pad, but they start on it and see everyone arrive, the same as an Editor.

## The look

The pad is drawn in the same chunky pixel style and colours as the characters themselves: a round platform with a ring of cyan light set into it that shimmers gently. It has no label and no **…** on its face. It lies on the floor, behind furniture, and characters walk over it freely. Otherwise it is an ordinary element: move, resize (it keeps its shape), rotate, lock, copy and export.
