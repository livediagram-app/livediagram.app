The editor lays its tools out the same way on every device, a computer, a tablet or a phone: the palette is one strip across the top of the canvas, and the Explorer opens from a menu button in the top-left corner.

## What it does

The palette is a single strip pinned to the top centre of the canvas. From left to right it holds:

- **Selection mode**: **Build** (Select, Hand), **Edit** (Eraser, Format) and **Interact** (Avatar).
- **The categories**, by name, with a thin line between each group: **Write**; **Rooms**, **Seating**, **Amenities**, **Bar**, **Recreation**, **Garden** and **Meeting**; **Travel**; then **Stickers**. Rest the pointer on one, or click it, and its elements open in a panel underneath.
- **Search** (the magnifier, or press **S**), last on the strip, to find any element or sticker by name.

The palette opens on **Popular**, its landing page: the elements a meeting reaches for most, from sticky notes to a room and a meeting table. Popular has no name on the strip; every category there is one level deep.

In the top-left corner, in a card of their own, sit the **menu button** and the tab's name. The menu button opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) as a popover under it, and clicking it again closes it.

**Collaborate** and **Tidy Up** open just above their buttons in the bottom row, and close when you click the button again. Every other panel (the Map and the tool panels) docks in a corner of the canvas, and you can drag it to another (see [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/)). Undo and Redo stay in the bottom-right corner.

Only one of the strip's menus is open at a time: opening the selection mode, a category, More or Search closes whichever other was open, and a click anywhere outside closes it.

## The categories

Each category on the strip opens a panel of its elements under its name:

- **Rest the pointer on a category** and its panel opens; slide along the strip and the panel follows to each one you point at. Move away from both the strip and the panel and it closes. A click or a tap opens it too.
- **Its elements come in order**, and a long category splits them into named groups. Most categories show them as cards with a line on what each one is for; Stickers as pictures. A tile's keyboard shortcut shows beside its name.
- **Click an element to add it, or drag it onto the canvas.** The panel closes once you have used one, so the canvas is clear. The strip at the bottom of the panel says what the element under your pointer does.
- **Stickers** lists its groups down the left, with the open group's entries on the right and a search box across the top that looks through every group. It reopens on the group you last had open.
- With the keyboard: the **Left** and **Right** arrows move along the strip, **Down** or **Enter** opens a category, the arrows move through its elements, and **Escape** closes it.

The names on the strip are short: **Write** holds the words (Text and sticky notes), **Rooms** what a floor is laid out with (it opens on **Build**: the Room, the Room Builder and the Door), **Seating** what people sit at, **Amenities** the plants, shelves and drinks, **Bar**, **Recreation**, **Garden** and **Meeting** the rest of a Space's pieces, and **Travel** what takes people somewhere (portals, Bring Focus, Avatar Pads, the Directory and Elevators). See [Where each tool lives](/help/palette/tools/) for the full map. On a window too narrow for the names, the strip shows the categories' icons alone.

## Search

**Search** finds an element without knowing which category holds it. Before you type, it shows the kinds of element already on your tab, so the ones you are using are one click away. Type a name, or a word you would use for it ("coffee" finds the Coffee Machine and the coffee icons), and matching tiles, icons and stickers appear in one list, the best name matches first; where two match equally well, the tiles on the strip lead. Search finds only what the palette offers. The × at the end of the box clears it. Click one to add it, or press Enter to add the best match.

A Participant has no strip at all: they walk the space and add nothing to it. Wherever the strip's Search isn't shown, **S** keeps its old meaning and picks the Select tool.

## The order of the tiles

Each category keeps its own order: adding something never moves it, and its tiles always come in the same order.

## What else the strip shows

- **Viewers** who can't edit have no strip, since there is nothing to add; the menu button still shows.

## On a phone

On a phone the strip is narrower:

- The **menu button** sits in a card of its own at the left of the strip.
- The **categories** show their icons only, a little further apart. Swipe them sideways to reach the rest; the selection mode and Search stay put. A category's panel opens across the width of the screen.
- **Collaborate** opens above its button in the bottom row, as on a computer. Pinch to zoom; the **Fit** button is there too.

A phone held sideways counts as a phone too.
