The **Frame** category holds what you lay a board _out_ with: the frame. It sits on the palette strip after Write. Everywhere else in the palette you are adding something to the canvas; here you are deciding how the canvas is arranged.

## What the category contains

- A **Frame** is a labelled box that groups a section of the canvas: the notes from one room, one topic of a meeting, or one team's corner of a Space. It draws behind its contents and carries them when you move it. Press `F` to add one. See [Frames](/help/palette/build/frames/).

## Working with frames

A frame behaves like any other element once placed: select, restyle, lock, and move it freely, and it can carry a [link](/help/canvas/links/), a note, or a [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) like anything else on the canvas.

Because a frame holds other work, two habits pay off. Put the frame down **first** and fill it afterwards, so the things inside are captured by it as you go. And when you move one, check what came with it: a frame carries everything whose centre sits inside it, which is usually what you want and occasionally a surprise. Resizing a frame never moves its contents.

<Tip>
  Reach for frames at the start of a meeting: lay out one per topic, name each, then drop the sticky
  notes into the frame they belong to.
</Tip>
