The **Write** elements are the ones whose content is _yours_: words, and pictures. Everywhere else in the palette you place a piece of furniture or a room and then maybe label it; here what you write or bring is the point, and the element is just what carries it.

The two writing elements differ mostly in **whose words they are**. A Text label is the board's own structure: a title, a heading, the name of a room. A Sticky note is somebody's contribution: an idea, a question, a point to raise. An **Image** is the picture that makes a Space yours: your logo on the reception wall, a team photo by the door, a floor plan by the lifts.

## What the category contains

- A **Text** element is a free-standing label with no box around it: just words on the canvas. `T` drops one. Use it for titles, headings, and anything that labels a _region_ rather than an element. See [Text and Fonts](/help/canvas/text-and-fonts/).
- A **Sticky note** is a coloured note card, the one that reads as somebody's contribution rather than the author's structure. `N` drops one. It is what brainstorms are made of. See [Sticky Notes](/help/palette/tools/sticky-notes/).
- An **Image** places a picture you upload. Pick it, then click the canvas for a default size or drag out the size you want: the image lands as an empty frame, and its **Select Image** button (or a double-click) opens the picker, where you upload a file, reuse one from your gallery or search for an openly licensed picture.

<Note>
  There is a third way to attach words, and it is not in this category: any element can carry a
  [note](/help/canvas/notes/) or a [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) of its own. Those belong
  to the element they are on and move with it.
</Note>

## Pasting or dropping a picture

You don't need the palette to add a picture. Copy one (a screenshot, or an image from a web page) and press `⌘V` (`Ctrl+V` on Windows) on the canvas, or drag an image file from your computer onto the canvas. It uploads to your image gallery and lands as an image of its own shape, in the middle of the screen for a paste or where you let go for a drop. PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF pictures work: a picture in another format says which ones to use, and any other file is turned away with a short note.

Adding a picture is for editors: on a Participant or view-only [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) there is no palette and a paste or a drop does nothing, but everybody in the Space sees the pictures that are there.

## Rows, not pictures

Text and Note are both rectangles with words in them, so at tile size they would look nearly alike. In their [palette panel](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) each Write element is a row with a one-line description instead: "a free-standing text label" against "a coloured note card" is what you are actually choosing between.

## Working with them

Each behaves like any other element once placed: select, restyle, rotate and move it freely, and each can carry a [link](/help/canvas/links/) like anything else on the canvas.

Text styling is shared with the rest of the canvas rather than special to this category: font, size, weight and alignment all come from the same controls that style any label. See [Text and Fonts](/help/canvas/text-and-fonts/).

<Tip>
  `T` and `N` drop a text label and a sticky note without opening the palette at all, which is the
  fast way through a brainstorm: type, `N`, type, `N`.
</Tip>
