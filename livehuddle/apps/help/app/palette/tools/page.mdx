The palette's tools are grouped into **categories**, each one press away on the palette strip. This page is a map of where each tool lives.

The categories sit along the strip by name, in three bands with a thin line between each: **Common** (Write), **Meet** (Travel, Seating, Rooms, Amenities, Selection Mode) and **Decorate** (Icons, Stickers, Media). Rest on one, or click it, to see its elements.

## The categories

The palette opens on **Popular**: Sticky note, Text, Image and Link card, then, behind a divider, Room, Meeting Table, Chair, Bring Focus and Avatar Pad. Popular is the palette's landing page rather than a category on the strip; every other category is one level deep.

Anything you cannot see is still one search away: the strip's Search and the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) (`⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`) add any palette element by name.

## Where each tool lives

**Write** holds the wordy elements: **Text** drops a free-standing label (`T`) and **Note** is a coloured sticky note card (`N`). See [Write Elements](/help/palette/write/).

**Travel** holds what takes people somewhere: the [Portal](/help/palette/tools/portals/), [Bring Focus](/help/palette/behaviour/bring-focus/), the [Avatar Pad](/help/palette/avatar-pads/) and the [Link card](/help/canvas/links/link-cards/).

**Seating** holds what people sit at: the [Chair, Desk and Sofa](/help/palette/chairs/), and the [Meeting Table](/help/palette/virtual-office/).

**Rooms** holds what a floor is laid out with: the [Fence](/help/palette/fences/), and the [Room, Stage and Rug](/help/palette/virtual-office/).

**Amenities** holds what makes a Space feel like a place: the [Plant, Bookshelf, Water Cooler and Coffee Machine](/help/palette/virtual-office/).

**Icons** and **Stickers** are for decoration: a searchable catalogue of single-colour glyphs, and die-cut colour emoji and word badges. See [Icons](/help/palette/icons/) and [Stickers](/help/palette/stickers/).

**Media** covers pictures and other people's pages: **Image** (`9`), with uploads, the gallery and image search, and the **Link card**. Image only appears where image uploads are available.

Each behaves like any other element once placed: select it, restyle it, multi-select it with others, and move it freely. Pick a tile and tap the canvas to drop the element at its default size, or drag to draw it at the size you want.

<Note>
  The palette is hidden for people with view-only access, since they can't add anything. See
