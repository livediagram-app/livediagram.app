The palette's tools are grouped into **categories**, each one press away on the palette strip. This page is a map of where each tool lives.

The categories sit along the strip by name, with a thin line between each group: **Write**; then **Rooms**, **Seats**, **Perks**, **Play**, **Garden** and **Meet**; then **Travel** on its own. Rest on one, or click it, to see its elements.

## The categories

The palette opens on **Popular**: Sticky note and Text, then, behind a divider, Room, Meeting Table, Chair, Bring Focus and Avatar Pad. Popular is the palette's landing page rather than a category on the strip; every other category is one level deep.

Anything you cannot see is still one search away: the strip's Search and the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) (`⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`) add any palette element by name.

## Where each tool lives

**Write** holds the words and pictures: **Text** drops a free-standing label (`T`), **Note** is a coloured sticky note card (`N`) and **Image** places a picture you upload, such as your logo. See [Write Elements](/help/palette/write/).

**Rooms** holds what a floor is laid out with. It opens on **Build**: the [Room](/help/palette/virtual-office/), the Room Builder, the Corridor, the Door and the Window, the pieces a room is made with. The Room, Corridor and Door each open their types beside the panel, and so does the Rug. Under **Floor** come the [Fence](/help/palette/fences/), the [Stage](/help/palette/virtual-office/stage/), the Stage Curtains, the Podium, the Office Pod, the Phone Booth, the Nap Pod, the Reception Desk, the Rug, the Pillar and the Column.

**Seats** holds what people sit at: the [Chair, Desk and Sofa](/help/palette/chairs/), and the [Meeting Table, Long Table, Bean Bag, Reading Nook, Hot Desk and Standing Desk](/help/palette/virtual-office/).

**Perks** holds what makes a Space feel like a place, in three groups: **Office** (the Plant, Bookshelf, Printer, Copier, Lockers, Coat Rack and Umbrella Stand), **Kitchen** (the Water Cooler, Coffee Machine, Vending Machine, Kitchen Counter, Kitchen Sink, Fridge, Microwave and Kettle) and **Restroom** (a whole Restroom, or its Toilet Cubicle, Washbasin and Hand Dryer). See [Virtual Office Elements](/help/palette/virtual-office/).

**Play** holds somewhere to unwind, in four groups: **Bar** (the Bar, Bar Stool, Drinks Cabinet and Drinks Fridge), **Games** (the Ping-Pong Table, Pool Table, Foosball Table, Arcade Machine and Dartboard), **Gym** (the Treadmill and Weight Bench) and **Lounge** (the Jukebox, Fireplace and Pet Bed).

**Garden** holds the outdoor pieces, in five groups: **Plants** (the Outdoor Plant, Tree and Grass), **Paths and Fences** (the Garden Path and Garden Fence), **Furniture** (the Bench, Picnic Table, Hammock and Garden Umbrella), **Features** (the Fountain, Pond, Swimming Pool and Campfire) and **Parking** (the Car Park and Bike Rack).

**Meet** holds what a meeting room and a team need: the [Screen](/help/palette/screen/), the [Room Sign](/help/palette/room-sign/), the Notice Board, World Clock, Calendar Wall, Suggestion Box and Team Photo Wall, and the [Timer and Stopwatch](/help/collaboration/session-tools/timer/).

**Travel**, set apart on the strip, holds what takes people somewhere: the [Portal](/help/palette/tools/portals/), [Bring Focus](/help/palette/behaviour/bring-focus/), the [Avatar Pad](/help/palette/avatar-pads/), the [Directory](/help/palette/directory/), the Signpost, the [Elevator](/help/palette/elevator/), the [Stairs](/help/palette/stairs/) and the [Avatar Button](/help/palette/behaviour/mode-buttons/).

**Stickers** are for decoration: die-cut colour emoji and word badges. They aren't on the strip: open **Search** (press **S**) and type a name, like "heart" or "approved". See [Stickers](/help/palette/stickers/).

There is no **Media** category: pictures come from **Write**'s Image, and a Space does not add link cards. A link card already in a Space still shows, moves and deletes, and its address can still be changed.

Each behaves like any other element once placed: select it, restyle it, multi-select it with others, and move it freely. Pick a tile and tap the canvas to drop the element at its default size, or drag to draw it at the size you want.

<Note>
  The palette is hidden for Participants and Viewers, since they can't add anything. See [Toolbar
  Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) for how the strip shows the categories and opens each one.
</Note>
