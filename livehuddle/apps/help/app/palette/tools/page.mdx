The palette's tools are grouped into **categories**, each one press away on the palette strip. This page is a map of where each tool lives.

The categories sit along the strip by name, in groups with a thin line between each: **Common**, **Structure**, **Decorate** and **Dynamic**, plus the session kit. Rest on one, or click it, to see its elements.

## The categories on the strip

The strip holds **Popular**, then Shapes, **My shapes**, Write (text, notes, tables and checklists), Draw (the pens and arrows), **Layout** (lanes, frames, stacks, boundaries, the Timeline, the board key, cards, badges and step numbers), then the session kit, **Ask**, and **Session**, which holds Tools, Record, React, Selection Mode and Navigate, then Icons, Stickers, Media (Image and Avatar) and Data. **Library** (your shape libraries) joins once you have [imported a shape library](/help/explorer/shape-libraries/). See [Workshop Mode](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/) for how a session uses them.

Anything the strip leaves out is still one search away: the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) (`⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`) can add any palette element by name.

## Where each tool lives

**Layout** holds the elements you lay a board out with, the ones that hold other work: **Lane** (a titled band that carries its steps), **Frame** (a labelled box that groups a section, `F`), **Stack** and **Boundary**, then the [Timeline](/help/palette/timeline-rail/) (milestones along a line), the board key, cards, badges and step numbers. See [Build Elements](/help/palette/build/).

**Write** holds the wordy elements: **Text** drops a free-standing label (`T`), **Note** is a coloured sticky note card (`N`), **Table** is an editable grid of cells and **Checklist** a list to tick off. The **Page** (a paper-sized sheet you write into) and the **Annotation** (a small marker that holds a note) are added from Search.

**Draw** holds the gesture tools: **Freehand** (`P`) sketches a stroke and keeps it exactly as drawn, the **Highlighter** lays one wide translucent marker stroke, **Arrow** (`A`) drops a connector with a pointer at its end that you position by hand, and **Line** drops the same without the pointer. See [Drawing and Sketch](/help/palette/tools/drawing/) and [Highlighter](/help/palette/tools/highlighter/).

**Components** (on no palette strip; the strip's Search and the search panel add them) holds the ready-made pieces in three groups: **Layout** (Hero, Header, Banner and the Stack; Hero needs image uploads, like Image and Avatar), **Page Blocks** (Card, Badge, Step Number, Callout, Stat Row, Process) and **Code and Lists** (Code, Checklist). The Card, Badge, Step Number and Stack are on the strip too, in **Layout**, and the Checklist in **Write**.

**Data** holds the charts and meters: Pie, Bar, Line, Legend, Progress, Donut and Rating. See [Data and Chart Elements](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/).

**Collaborate** is the elements that do something when somebody interacts with them, or that ask the room something, in six groups: **Ask** on the strip (dot vote, poll, quiz, estimate card, temperature check, idea box, Q&A board), and inside **Session**: **Tools** (timer, stopwatch, reveal zone, done check, picker), **Record** (comment panel, action panel, agenda, decision record, roll call), **React** (the five reaction pads), **Selection Mode** buttons, and **Navigate** (portal, bring focus, chair, desk, sofa, fence, link card, and the virtual office: room, meeting table, plant, bookshelf, water cooler, coffee machine, rug). See [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/) and [Collaborate Elements](/help/palette/collaborate/).

**Media** covers pictures and other people's pages: **Image** (`9`) and **Avatar**; the [embeds](/help/palette/embed-elements/) for YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Figma, Google Docs and any website are added from the strip's Search or the search panel. Image and Avatar only appear where image uploads are available.

Each behaves like any other element once placed: select it, restyle it, multi-select it with others, and move it freely. Pick a tile and tap the canvas to drop the element at its default size, or drag to draw it at the size you want.

## Guides for these tools

Several tools have their own walkthroughs, listed below and linked here:

- [Drawing and Sketch](/help/palette/tools/drawing/) covers the Freehand pen and how strokes are smoothed.
- [Highlighter](/help/palette/tools/highlighter/) covers the translucent marker and its colours and widths.
- [Images](/help/palette/tools/images/) covers uploading, searching for and reusing pictures, and avatars.
- [Pages](/help/palette/tools/pages/) covers the paper-sized sheet for prose.
- [Tables](/help/palette/tools/tables/) covers the editable grid: adding one, filling cells, and rows/columns.
- [Sticky Notes](/help/palette/tools/sticky-notes/) covers the coloured note card for short annotations.
- [Code Blocks](/help/palette/tools/code-blocks/) covers the monospace card for syntax-highlighted snippets.
- [Checklists](/help/palette/tools/checklists/) covers the card of checkable to-do rows you tick on the canvas.
- [Portals](/help/palette/tools/portals/) covers linking two portals and travelling between them, including across tabs.
- [Data and Chart Elements](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/) covers the **Data** category: Pie, Column and Line charts (each any of seven chart types), Legend, Progress, Donut and Rating.
- [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/) covers the **Collaborate** categories' interactive elements: Selection Mode buttons, Reveal zones, Done checks, Pickers, Bring Focus, the Timer and Stopwatch, and Reaction pads.

Arrows are deep enough to deserve their own guide: see [Arrows](/help/palette/arrows/).
