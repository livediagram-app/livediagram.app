A **Room Sign** is the plaque beside a meeting room's door. It shows the room's name and whether anyone is in there, and lets you knock.

It lives in the palette's **Meet** category, one of the [meeting elements](/help/palette/behaviour/), beside the [Screen](/help/palette/screen/), and search adds one too.

## Placing it

Put the sign beside a room's door, just outside the room (or inside it). It belongs to the nearest room or [Office Pod](/help/palette/virtual-office/) within a short step. Its name is the room's name, so rename the room and the sign follows. A sign too far from any room reads **No Room**.

## What it says

- **Free** (green): nobody is inside.
- **In Use** (amber), with how many people are inside ("In Use · 3") and, underneath, for how long ("12 min"). People in an Office Pod inside the room are counted on the pod's own sign.
- **Do Not Disturb** (red): an Office Pod whose door is shut, because both its seats are taken.

How long counts from when you first saw the room in use, so if you join while a meeting is already going it starts at **Just now**.

## Opening the sign

Press a sign while you walk and it opens as a door panel, as long as there is nothing to knock on (the room is free, or you are already inside):

- The room's name, with a coloured chip saying **Free**, **In Use** or **Do Not Disturb**.
- **Inside**, how many people are in there, and **In Use For**, how long.
- **Who's Inside**: each person's initials and name, with you first. An empty room says **Nobody's Inside**.
- At the bottom, **Walk In** walks your character just inside the room's doorway, and **Knock** knocks while the room is in use. When one can't be used, it stays greyed out with the reason beside it, such as "You're inside this room" or, at a closed Office Pod, "Its door is shut".

A sign that isn't near a room says **Not Beside a Room** instead.

## Knocking

While a room is In Use or Do Not Disturb, the sign shows a **Knock** button to anyone walking in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) who isn't already inside. Walking up to a closed Office Pod's door offers you a **Knock** too.

- Everyone inside sees **"Sam is knocking"** with **Let In** and **Not Now**. Anyone set to Do Not Disturb isn't shown knocks.
- You hear back: **"Sam let you in"** or **"Sam says Not Now"**. A room's doorway is always open, so in a room you simply walk in.
- At a closed Office Pod, **Let In** stands up whoever pressed it, so a seat is free and the door slides open for you.
- You can knock once every 10 seconds. Knocks are never saved.

## Who can do what

- **Editors** place, move and delete a Room Sign.
- **Everyone**, Editors, Participants and Viewers, sees it and can open it, and anyone walking can knock, answer a knock and walk in from it.

## The look

A small plaque with a coloured stripe down its left edge that matches its status, light on a light theme and slate on a dark one. It has no label of its own and no **…** on its face, and characters walk past it freely.
