These pieces turn a space into an office your team walks round in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/). With [chairs, desks and sofas](/help/palette/chairs/), [fences](/help/palette/fences/) and speech bubbles, you can lay out a reception, desks, a meeting room, a kitchen and a lounge, then sit, talk and grab a coffee together.

They're spread over the palette's Space categories, all [meeting elements](/help/palette/behaviour/): the Room, Room Builder, Corridor, Door, Stage, Stage Curtains, Podium, Office Pod, Phone Booth, Nap Pod, Reception Desk, Rug, Pillar and Column in **Rooms**, the Meeting Table, Bean Bag, Reading Nook, Hot Desk and Standing Desk in **Seats**, the Plant, Bookshelf, Printer, Copier, Lockers, Coat Rack, Umbrella Stand, Water Cooler, Coffee Machine, Vending Machine, Kitchen Counter, Kitchen Sink, Fridge, Microwave, Kettle, Restroom, Toilet Cubicle, Washbasin and Hand Dryer in **Perks** (under **Office**, **Kitchen** and **Restroom**), the Screen, Room Sign, Notice Board, World Clock, Calendar Wall, Suggestion Box, Team Photo Wall, Timer and Stopwatch in **Meet**, the Signpost in **Travel**, the Bar, Bar Stool, Drinks Cabinet, Drinks Fridge, Ping-Pong Table, Pool Table, Foosball Table, Arcade Machine, Dartboard, Treadmill, Weight Bench, Jukebox, Fireplace and Pet Bed in **Play** (under **Bar**, **Games**, **Gym** and **Lounge**), and the Outdoor Plant, Tree, Grass, Garden Path, Garden Fence, Bench, Picnic Table, Hammock, Garden Umbrella, Fountain, Pond, Swimming Pool, Campfire, Car Park and Bike Rack in **Garden** (under **Plants**, **Paths and Fences**, **Furniture**, **Features** and **Parking**). Like all furniture, every piece is drawn standing up, the way you'd see it walking in.

## Room

Four walls round a floor, with a doorway and the room's name on its back wall.

- **Walls block**: characters walk round a room and in through its doorway, never through a wall.
- **The doorway** is in the front wall to start with. Right-click the room and open **Room** to move it to the **Top**, **Right**, **Bottom** or **Left** wall, or choose **None** for a closed room you can only reach through a [Portal](/help/palette/tools/portals/).
- **The name** is the room's label: double-click to rename it "Kitchen" or "Meeting Room 2".
- A new room goes **behind everything** on the tab, so furniture you've already placed stands on its floor. Resize it to any shape; the walls stay the same thickness.
- **Room types**: click the Room in **Rooms** and its types open beside it: the plain **Room**, a small **Office**, a long **Boardroom**, an **Open Plan L**, a **Kitchen** with a corner cut off and an octagonal **Lounge**. Each places at its own size, with a doorway already in a wall. The shaped ones are drawn rooms, so they resize in proportion like one you draw yourself.

## Room Builder and Door

When four walls won't do, draw your own room: an L-shaped open plan, a kitchen with a corner cut off, a reception with a slanted front.

- **Room Builder**: pick it in **Rooms**, then click to place each corner. Every wall snaps to 45 degrees from the last corner (hold **Cmd** or **Ctrl** to place a corner freely). Click the first corner, double-click, or press **Enter** to close the room. **Backspace** takes the last corner back and **Escape** starts again. On a phone, tap to place corners and tap the first one to close.
- The room you draw is a Room like any other: its name, its floor colour, walls characters walk round (sliding along a slanted one), and a call of its own. It resizes in proportion, so slanted walls stay at 45 degrees.
- **Door**: drag a Door onto any wall, of a drawn room, a plain one or a corridor, and it snaps on and turns to the wall's angle, a slanted wall included. Characters walk through it; drag it along the wall to move it. A room takes as many doors as you like, and moving the room carries them with it.
- **Door types**: click the Door in **Rooms** and pick the **Door**, a wider **Double Door**, a glass **Sliding Door** or a **Revolving Door**. Each opens and shuts the same way, and moves when it does.
- **Locked doors**: lock a door (right-click it and choose **Lock**) and it shows a padlock, its leaves shaded, so everyone can see at a glance it isn't one to move.

## Corridor

A hallway that joins your rooms: walls down its long sides and open ends, so characters walk along it and out of either end. Click the Corridor in **Rooms** to pick a **Straight** run, a **Corner**, a **T-Junction** or a **Crossroads**, and rotate it to turn it the way you need. Doors snap to its walls just as they do to a room's. A corridor goes behind everything like a room, but it isn't a call: people in it hear whoever is near them.

## Stage

A raised platform for an all-hands. Whoever stands on it is heard by everyone on the tab, not just the people near them. It goes behind everything, like a room, and never gets in anyone's way. Right-click it and open **Stage** to choose who may speak from it and whether their camera shows. See [Stage](/help/palette/virtual-office/stage/).

## Meeting Table

A table with six chairs round it. Walk your character in and it sits on the seat nearest where you came from, facing the table. The people sitting there show in a pill above it.

## Plant and Bookshelf

Scenery that makes an office feel lived in. Characters walk round a plant's pot and along the front of a bookshelf, never through them.

## Printer, Copier, Lockers, Coat Rack and Umbrella Stand

The everyday kit of an office floor: a desktop **Printer** with a page in its tray, a floor-standing **Copier**, a bank of **Lockers**, a **Coat Rack** with a coat and a scarf on its hooks and an **Umbrella Stand** by the door. Characters walk round their bases.

## Water Cooler and Coffee Machine

Walk your character up to one and it **gets a drink**: a cup of water or a steaming coffee, held in its hand for a minute, with a sip every few seconds. The machine bubbles or drips as it pours. Everyone else sees your cup too, and you keep it if you sit down. Nothing is saved: the cup is gone when you leave Avatar mode.

## Vending Machine

A tall machine of snacks and cans for a break room, its sign and window lit. On a dark canvas it glows, throwing a soft light on the floor in front of it. Characters walk round its base. It doesn't hand out a snack: for a drink, use the water cooler or the drinks fridge.

## Kitchenette

A **Kitchen Counter** and a **Kitchen Sink** are the same size, with the same worktop, so placed side by side they read as one run of units. Stand a **Fridge** at the end, and put a **Microwave** and a **Kettle** on the counter: drop them over the worktop and they sit on it. Characters walk along the front of the units and round the fridge; the microwave and kettle never get in the way.

## Restroom

The **Restroom** tile places a whole restroom in one go: a room named Restroom with its door, two **Toilet Cubicles**, a **Hand Dryer** and two **Washbasins** with mirrors, plus a [room sign](/help/palette/room-sign/) by the door outside. It's one change, so one undo takes it all away, and every piece can be moved or deleted on its own. Each piece is in the Restroom group too, to build your own. Characters walk round the cubicles and basins; the hand dryer is on the wall.

## Stage Curtains and Podium

Dress a stage for an all-hands. **Stage Curtains** are red velvet drapes under a scalloped pelmet: lay them over a stage and they frame it, open in the middle so whoever stands on it shows through. Characters walk between and in front of them. A **Podium** is a lectern with a microphone for the speaker to stand behind; characters walk round its foot.

## Pillar and Column

Structure for a big hall or a lobby: a plain square **Pillar**, or a classical stone **Column** with a scrolled capital. Characters walk round their bases.

## Swimming Pool, Car Park and Bike Rack

A **Swimming Pool** with lanes and a ladder, for a hotel roof or a beach. Stretch it to any size; characters stop at the water's edge and walk round on its deck. A **Car Park** is three bays with two cars parked (place a few side by side for a longer row), and a **Bike Rack** two stands with a bike locked to each, for where people arrive at a campus. Characters walk round them.

## Bean Bag and Reading Nook

Relaxed seating for a breakout corner. Walk onto one to sit, just like a [chair](/help/palette/chairs/).

- **Bean Bag**: a slouchy seat for one. Place a few together for a chill-out corner.
- **Reading Nook**: a wingback armchair on a rug, with a side table of books and a floor lamp. One seat, in the armchair. On a dark canvas the lamp casts a warm pool of light.

## Office Pod

A booth for two, for a private chat. Walk in through its doorway and your character sits on a bench. Once both seats are taken, its glass door **slides shut** for everyone, and nobody else can walk in until one of you stands up. With calls on, the two of you hear each other and nobody outside hears you, door open or shut.

## Phone Booth

A booth for one, for a call of your own. Sit in it and you step out of the room's audio: nobody outside hears you, and you don't hear them. Its lamp lights up and your status shows **Busy** while you're inside; stand up and you're back in the room, with your own status again. For a private chat between two, use an Office Pod.

## Nap Pod

A cocoon lounger for a quiet moment. Walk in and your character lies back; while you rest, everyone sees your status as **Focusing**. Get up and it goes back to what it was.

## Reception Desk

A front desk with a bell, for the way into a Space. Stand at its counter and a **Welcome** bubble shows above it, with a **Ring Bell** button. Ring it and everyone in the desk's room (or near it, if it stands in no room) hears a ding, sees the bell ring and is told who rang. You can ring it once every five seconds. People with Do Not Disturb on don't hear it. Editors, participants and viewers can all ring it.

## Hot Desk and Standing Desk

- **Hot Desk**: a desk anyone can take. Sit at it and its name card shows your name until you get up.
- **Standing Desk**: a tall desk with a monitor. Walk up to it and your character works standing, facing the screen.

## Bar and Bar Stool

A bar counter with bottles behind it. Characters stand at it and walk round its ends. Put **Bar Stools** in front of it: walk onto one to sit, facing the bar.

## Drinks Cabinet and Drinks Fridge

These work like the water cooler: walk up to one and your character gets a **juice** from the cabinet or a **soda** from the fridge, for a minute.

## Garden

- **Outdoor Plant** and **Tree**: characters walk round the planter and the trunk, and pass in front of a tree's leaves.
- **Garden Path**: stepping stones that lie on the ground under everything. Stretch it to any length; the stones stay round.
- **Grass**: a patch of lawn that lies on the ground under everything, like the path. Stretch it to any width and height to lay out a lawn or a park; characters walk across it.
- **Garden Fence**: a low picket fence that blocks walking just like a [fence](/help/palette/fences/).
- **Bench**, **Picnic Table** and **Hammock**: walk onto one to sit. A bench seats two side by side, a picnic table six facing the table, a hammock one.
- **Garden Umbrella**: a striped parasol to stand beside or over a picnic table. Only its base gets in the way.
- **Fountain** and **Pond**: water you walk round. Stretch a pond to any size.
- **Campfire**: a fire in a ring of stones to gather round.

## Notice Board

A cork board for the wall. Click **Notes** on it (or click the board while you're walking) to read every note, newest first, and pin your own: a short title, up to 280 characters and a colour. The board shows the four newest notes.

- **Editors** and **participants** pin notes and unpin their own. Editors can unpin any note.
- **Viewers** read the notes.

## World Clock

A wall clock with a dial for each time zone. A new clock shows your own time. Right-click it and open **World Clock** to add up to six zones, such as London or New York, each with its own label; the clock shows the first three. It moves on once a minute.

## Calendar Wall

A month planner for the wall. It shows this month on your own clock, with today circled in red and a small tab on each day that has notes. Click **Calendar** on it (or click it while you're walking) to open the month: step back and forward with the arrows, press **Today** to come back, and pick a day to read its notes and pin your own with **Pin to** that day. Notes are the Notice Board's: a short title, up to 280 characters and a colour, and up to 50 on a calendar.

- **Editors** and **participants** pin notes and unpin their own. Editors can unpin any note.
- **Viewers** read the notes.

## Suggestion Box

A box for ideas with no name on them. Click **Suggest** on it (or click it while you're walking), write up to 500 characters and press **Drop It In**. The note keeps only its words and the day it was dropped: no name, no time and no link, and it never appears on anyone's screen as it arrives.

- **Editors** read the notes (newest first, with **Refresh** to fetch new ones) and **Remove** them. A box holds up to 200.
- **Participants** drop notes in, and never read any, their own included.
- **Viewers** see the box but can't drop a note in. Give people a participant link if you want their ideas.
- **Lock** the box to close it: it takes no more notes, and Editors can still read it.

The notes stay with the Space, never in the box itself, so they don't travel with exports, copies or templates. A Space saved in This Browser has nobody to collect from, so its box asks you to share the Space first.

## Team Photo Wall

A wall of eight picture frames showing the people in the Space right now, you first, each as the character they walk as. Rest on a frame to see who it is. With more than eight people, the last frame shows how many more. Empty frames wait for people to arrive; the wall never lists anyone who isn't here.

## Signpost

A wooden signpost in **Travel** whose arrows name the three rooms, office pods or stages nearest to it, each pointing the way. Click an arrow to walk there (it starts walking for you if you aren't already). While walking, a click anywhere on the signpost takes the top arrow. With no rooms on the tab yet, its arrows stay blank.

## Play

- **Jukebox**: plays background music to everyone standing in its room (or near it, outside a room). Right-click it and open **Jukebox** to paste a link to an audio stream. Nobody hears anything until they press **Play** on the jukebox once; after that, **Mute** on the jukebox or **Jukebox Music** under **Status** on the avatar palette turns it off for you.
- **Ping-Pong Table**: two seats, one at each end. When both are taken, a little rally plays between them.
- **Pool Table** and **Foosball Table**: a pool table with the balls racked and a cue on the cloth, and a foosball table with its rods and players. They're for the look of a games room: characters walk round them, and there's no game to play yet.
- **Arcade Machine**: a glowing cabinet. Walk up to it and it asks you to insert a coin.
- **Dartboard**: a board with three darts in it, to hang on a wall. Characters walk past it.
- **Fireplace**: a fire with a flicker, to gather round in a lounge.
- **Treadmill** and **Weight Bench**: walk onto the treadmill and its belt runs, or sit at the bench to lift. While you do, your status shows **On a Break**.
- **Pet Bed**: a dog bed, and the office dog that goes with it. It naps in its bed and now and then trots round its room, the same way on every screen. Click it to pat it. With reduced motion on, it stays asleep in its bed.

A status a piece sets is only shown while you're there: if you pick a status yourself while sitting, yours wins, and standing up always brings back your own.

## Rug

A rug on the floor, in any colour you like, to mark out a lounge or a reception. It goes behind everything, like a room, and never gets in anyone's way. Click the Rug in **Rooms** to pick its type: the **Oval**, a **Round** rug, a **Bordered** one, a long **Runner**, a **Striped** one, a **Persian**, a **Kilim** or a **Doormat**. Its pattern is drawn to whatever size you stretch it to.

## Who can do what

- **Editors** place, move, rename and recolour every piece, draw rooms with the Room Builder, place doors, set a room's doorway, choose who speaks from a stage, set a world clock's zones and a jukebox's music.
- **Everyone** walking in Avatar mode, including someone on a view-only link, walks through doors, sits at tables, in pods and on stools, takes drinks, reads the notice board and hears the jukebox.
