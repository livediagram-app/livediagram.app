In a Space you usually hear the people near you, and walls keep each room private ([Proximity Audio](/help/collaboration/proximity-audio/)). An all-hands needs something else: one person heard by the whole Space. A **Stage** is that. Walk your character onto it and everyone on the tab hears you, in any room or none, as clearly as if they were standing beside you.

## Adding a stage

<Step n={1}>Open **Rooms** on the palette strip.</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick **Stage**, beside the Room, among the office pieces. It goes in behind everything, like a
  room or a rug, so chairs you have already placed stand in front of it.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Resize it to any width. Its name sits on a plate on its front edge: double-click to rename it
  "Main Stage" or "Demo Corner".
</Step>

The **All-Hands Hall** template comes with a stage already standing at the front of the hall, rows of seats facing it and a Bring Focus button for the presenter ([Templates](/help/canvas/templates/)).

## Speaking from the stage

<Figure caption="A speaker on the stage is heard by everyone on the tab: the seats in the hall, and someone in the corridor outside.">
  <StageHall />
</Figure>

- **Stand on its top.** Your feet must be on the stage itself; the front edge you see, and the outline round it, count as off it. Walk off and you are back in the room's own call straight away.
- **Everyone on the tab hears you**, at full volume and centred, wherever they are. Two speakers on a stage, or on two stages, hear each other as one room does.
- **The audience is muted to the stage.** While someone speaks from it, everyone else in the stage's room is its audience: the stage does not hear them, and they hear each other only by distance, so neighbours can whisper without the whole hall hearing. The walls still stand: a meeting room next door keeps its own call.
- **Somebody in the audience can ask to speak** with **Raise Hand**, and the stage can **Let Speak** ([Proximity Audio](/help/collaboration/proximity-audio/)).
- **Your microphone reads Live** while anyone else walks the tab, because they all hear you.

## Who Can Speak and Show Cameras

<Figure caption="Right-click a stage and open its Stage section to choose who may speak from it and whether their camera shows.">
  <StageSettings />
</Figure>

Right-click the stage and open its **Stage** section:

- **Who Can Speak**: **Editors** (the default), or **Editors and Participants** for a panel, a demo from the floor or a show-and-tell. Anyone else can still stand on the stage; they are heard as the audience is, and a short line above the call strip tells them only Editors speak from it.
- **Show Cameras**: when it is on, everyone on the tab sees a speaker's camera and shared screen, not just the people near them ([Cameras and Screen Sharing](/help/collaboration/cameras-and-screen-sharing/)). When it is off, the stage is heard but not seen. It is off on a new stage, and on in the All-Hands Hall.

Select several stages to set them all at once.

## Who can do what

| What                                   | Editor | Participant                           | Viewer |
| -------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Place, move, rename and set up a stage | Yes    | No                                    | No     |
| Speak from a stage                     | Yes    | When it lets Editors and Participants | No     |
| Hear a stage                           | Yes    | Yes                                   | Yes    |
| Raise a hand in its audience           | Yes    | Yes                                   | No     |
