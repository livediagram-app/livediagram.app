livehuddle is for teams who want to be in the room **together**, even when they are not. It shines when several people are in the same Space at once, walking round it as their characters, talking and working on one shared board in [real time](/help/collaboration/sharing/). It works just as well for one person setting up a board before the meeting, and the [Space always works without signing in](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## The teams it serves

A few groups feel right at home:

- **Remote and hybrid teams** who want a place to bump into each other. A [Virtual Office or a Coworking Space](/help/canvas/templates/) gives everyone a desk to sit at, a [status](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) to show whether they are free, and a meeting table to gather round.
- **Facilitators** running retros, workshops and planning sessions, where everyone needs to contribute at once and see each other's input live. [Session tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/) put timers and polls in front of whoever runs the session, and whoever holds the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) baton steers the session.
- **Anyone hosting a crowd**, from a stand-up in a Team Pod to an all-hands in an All-Hands Hall, with the [avatar's Reactions](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) for the applause.

## Whether you sign in or not

livehuddle is friction-free on purpose. A first-time visitor can open a Space as a [guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/) and only later create an account to sync across devices and join [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/). A signed-in user can still hand a share link to a guest who walks in and joins without any account at all.
