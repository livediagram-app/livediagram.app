livehuddle is for people who want to be in the room **together**, even when they are not. It shines when several people are in the same Space at once, walking round it as their characters and seeing each other in [real time](/help/collaboration/sharing/). It works just as well for one person setting up a Space before the meeting, and the [Space always works without signing in](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## The people it serves

A few groups feel right at home:

- **Remote and hybrid teams** who want a place to bump into each other. A [Virtual Office or a Coworking Space](/help/canvas/templates/) gives everyone a desk to sit at, a [status](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) to show whether they are free, and a meeting table to gather round.
- **Anyone hosting a meeting**, from a stand-up in a Team Pod or a one-to-one in a quiet room to an all-hands in an All-Hands Hall. The [session tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/) put a Timer or a Stopwatch in the room for whoever runs it, and whoever holds the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) baton is the room's one host.
- **Communities and event hosts** who want a place for a group to gather, walk up to each other and say hello, with [reactions](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) for the applause.

## Whether you sign in or not

livehuddle is friction-free on purpose. A first-time visitor can open a Space as a [guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/) and only later create an account to sync across devices and join [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/). A signed-in user can still hand a share link to a guest who walks in and joins without any account at all.
