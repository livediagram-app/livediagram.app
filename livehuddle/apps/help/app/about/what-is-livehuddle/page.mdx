livehuddle is a web app where a team **meets in a shared Space**. You open a **Space**, a place in the browser laid out like an office or a hall, and everyone who joins walks in as a little character: they walk to the room they want, sit round a table, say hello in a speech bubble and show at a glance whether they are free. Everyone sees everyone else's moves as they happen.

## A Space to meet in

A Space is a floor plan of rooms and furniture. It holds one or more tabs, each a floor of its own. Start from one of the four Spaces, a **Virtual Office**, a **Team Pod**, a **Coworking Space** or an **All-Hands Hall**, and it opens with you walking, in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/). Or start from a **Blank Space** and furnish it yourself. See [Templates](/help/canvas/templates/).

- **Characters** you walk with a click or the arrow keys, dress how you like, and sit at a desk or round a table. See [Avatar Mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/).
- **Rooms and furniture**: rooms with walls and a doorway, meeting tables, desks, sofas, a water cooler and a coffee machine. See [Virtual Office](/help/palette/virtual-office/).
- **Portals** between tabs, so a Space can have a lobby, a meeting room and a breakout floor. See [Portals](/help/palette/tools/portals/).
- **Presence**: everyone's character, cursor and name, and a status on each character (such as Available, Focusing or Away), so you can see who is free to talk. See [Walking Together](/help/selection-modes/walking-together/).

## Talking in a Space

- **Speech bubbles**: press `Enter` while walking and what you type floats up over your character for everyone to read.
- **Reactions**: wave, cheer or dance on the spot, or press a [Reaction Pad](/help/palette/behaviour/reaction-pads/) for a burst of confetti or applause.
- **Comments** pinned where they matter, with replies and mentions, for anything that should outlast the meeting. See [Comments](/help/collaboration/comments/).

Calls by proximity, where you hear the people near you, are built in but not switched on everywhere yet. Where they are off, the bubbles, reactions and comments carry the conversation. See [Proximity Audio](/help/collaboration/proximity-audio/).

## Keeping a meeting moving

A few light tools help whoever is running the meeting, and nothing more:

- **Timer** and **Vote** in the bottom bar: a shared countdown to keep an item short, and a quick dot vote to decide together. See [Session Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).
- **The facilitator**: one person takes the role, so the room has one host and the timer and the vote answer to them. See [Facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/).
- **Bring Focus** offers everyone a jump to one spot, and **Follow** takes your view wherever someone else's goes. See [Bring Focus](/help/palette/behaviour/bring-focus/) and [Follow Along](/help/canvas/follow-along/).
- **Laser** and **Spotlight** point the room at one thing while you talk it through. See [Laser](/help/selection-modes/laser/) and [Spotlight](/help/selection-modes/spotlight/).

On the walls there is room for light content: sticky notes, text, images, link cards, frames, icons and stickers, for the notes a meeting leaves behind and for decoration. livehuddle has no diagramming or drawing tools; those are livediagram's job.

## Built for teams

livehuddle is for people who would rather be in the room together: remote and hybrid teams keeping a place to bump into each other, anyone hosting a stand-up, a one-to-one or an all-hands, and communities that want somewhere to gather. The unit of value is the group in the room, not the single author.

To make that frictionless, a Space **always works without signing in**. You can open a Space, set it up, and hand the link to a colleague who walks straight in, no account required. See [Sharing Your Space](/help/getting-started/sharing-your-document/).

## Free and open source

livehuddle is **MIT-licensed** and **free for everyone**. There is no paid tier and no plan to introduce one. It runs at livehuddle.app, and the whole codebase is open for anyone to read. Every feature is available to every user. See [Why use livehuddle?](/help/about/why-livehuddle/).
