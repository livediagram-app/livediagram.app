**Home** is the page the [Explorer](/explorer) opens on, and the first row of its sidebar. It answers the question you bring back with you, on one screen: **what was I working on?**

Home opens with a greeting across the top of the page. Under it, a pill says how many spaces you have across My Work and your teams; click it to go there. The search field sits right there in the greeting, with **New Space** inside it, so the first screen always offers a way to find something or start something. Home also shows **What you can make**: a Virtual Office, a Team Pod, a Coworking Space and an All-Hands Hall, each a click away from a new space laid out that way.

Prefer a plainer Home? Press the **×** beside **Help** in the greeting's corner to hide it. Home then opens with its title and the search field in the header, like every other page, and stays that way on your other devices. Turn it back on in **Settings > Explorer** with **Show Home Banner**.

Rather skip the homepage? Turn on **Automatically Open Explorer** in **Settings > Explorer**, and opening livehuddle.app while you're signed in comes straight here.

Your first visit looks different. With nothing made yet, Home welcomes you and offers ways to start instead: the search field looks through the templates, **Start Blank** makes an empty space at once, and below it sit a blank and the most popular templates, each one click from a new space. If you have used livehuddle before and see nothing, **Sign In** brings your spaces back. Once you have a space, Home shows your work instead.

Below the greeting, Home is one column of sections, each under its own heading: **New** (what was made most recently), **Most Used**, **Recent**, your **Workspaces**, then **Shared With You Recently**. A section with nothing in it is left out.

## Most Used and Recent

Two sections keep up to sixteen spaces within reach: **Most Used**, the **eight you opened most** lately, then **Recent** directly under it, the **eight you used last**.

- **Most Used** counts how many times you opened a space over the last 14 days, so a space you open a lot over a few days rises to the top. A day you only edited or made it counts as one open. A space you have not opened in 14 days drops off, so the list stays about what you are working on now.
- **Recent** holds the spaces you opened, edited or made most recently, newest first.
- **What you make is in at once**, opened or not: a new space, a duplicate, a single imported board, or one an AI tool made for you. An import of several spaces at once is the exception, so a big import never pushes your work aside; those join when you first open one.
- A space is never shown twice: if a recent one is also among the most used, it stays in Most Used, and the next most recent takes its place in Recent.

On a computer or a tablet each section is cards, up to two rows of four: **Most Used** with a line under each saying how many times you opened it, then **Recent**, saying when you last used each. On a phone each section is its own strip of up to eight you scroll sideways, under its own heading, and Recent's strip ends in a **See More** tile. If every space you have opened lately is among the most used, Recent is left out until there is another.

Each space shows a picture of what is drawn on it, with its name underneath; click one to open it. A space saved only in this browser wears the **Browser Storage** pill and takes its place like any other, counted in this browser. Spaces in the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), and ones whose share link has lapsed or whose team you have left, never appear. With nothing yet to show, Recent says "The spaces you open will gather here."

Opening a space is private: it counts towards your own Most Used and Recent, and nobody else ever sees that you opened it.

**See More**, at the right of Recent's heading, opens [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/): the 12 spaces you opened most recently, newest first, with the same filters as the rest of the Explorer. Its breadcrumb leads back to Home.

## Shared With You Recently

Below your Workspaces sit the four items most recently [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) through a link. **See All** opens Shared With Me.

## Guests and signing in

Home works without an account, like the rest of livehuddle.

**Your work follows you.** Signing up moves what you open, to your new account alongside your spaces, so a week of work as a guest is still there afterwards.

## A space's History

The **History** item in a space's menu opens everything that has happened to that space: created, commented on, shared, filed. History covers the **last 30 days**; anything older is no longer listed.

<Tip>
  To step back your own recent edits on a space, use [Undo](/help/canvas/undo/) in the editor.
</Tip>
