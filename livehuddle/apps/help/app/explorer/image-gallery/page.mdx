The **Image Gallery** lives under **Library** in the Explorer sidebar's More group and collects every image you have uploaded. It is where you manage the pictures behind your profile and the images already in your Spaces. Images that come in with an imported tab, and pictures you pick from the image search, land here too.

## What it shows

Each image appears as a card with its name, its size in pixels and on disk, and a badge saying where it is used:

- **Used in 2 spaces** (or however many) means a space of yours still shows it. When something of yours in another liveworkspace app shows it too, the badge counts **items** and names each one's app. Click the badge to list them, each a link that opens it.
- **Not used** means nothing of yours, in any liveworkspace app, points at it, so it is usually safe to delete.
- **Couldn’t check** means liveworkspace could not look up where the image is used just now, so it says neither. Open the gallery again to retry. These images are never listed under **Unused**.

A picture an AI assistant added through an app's MCP connection carries a **Made by AI** chip on its card, the same chip an AI-made file shows in the Explorer.

To add an image, drop it, paste it, or click the **Drop, paste, or click to upload an image** area at the top. It uses the same checks as uploading from the canvas: one image can be up to 10 MB, and an image you already have is not stored twice.

## Finding unused images

To see only the images nothing uses, press **Unused** at the top of the gallery, just left of **Help**. The cards that stay are the ones with a **Not used** badge (a picture in something of yours in the Trash still counts as used), ready to review and delete; press **All** to see every image again. Deleting a card while **Unused** is on takes it out straight away. When every image is in use, the gallery says so and offers **Show All Images**. The switch appears once your gallery has images.

## Deleting images

Press the bin on a card to delete an image and reclaim the space. The confirmation tells you what will happen: if the image is still used in one or more spaces, it warns that those spaces will show a broken picture; otherwise it says the image will be removed for good. Either way, a deleted image cannot be brought back.

## Automatic cleanup of unused images

You do not have to keep on top of this by hand. Once a day, liveworkspace removes uploads that are **both** more than **30 days old** and **used by nothing** in any of its apps, freeing the storage automatically. The 30-day window is deliberately generous: an image you uploaded recently, or one anything still references, is always left alone, so a picture you have not placed on the canvas yet is never swept out from under you. An image in use stays put no matter how old it is.

## Who has a gallery

Everyone does, signed in or not. A guest's gallery belongs to their browser, like their spaces, and moves to their account when they [sign up](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/). The gallery is also reachable from the editor's Explorer panel, on the **Image Gallery** tab of its **Library** section.

A gallery holds up to 500 images and 1 GB in total, and it is the same gallery in every liveworkspace app you sign in to. If images are turned off for a deployment, the gallery says "Images are not enabled here." rather than failing silently.

<Tip>
  The **Not used** badge checks what is yours in every liveworkspace app, not other people's spaces.
  The daily cleanup will eventually clear unused uploads for you, but deleting one yourself reclaims
  the space straight away rather than waiting out the 30-day window.
</Tip>
