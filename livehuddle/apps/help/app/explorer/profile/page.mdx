Your account lives in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), under **Profile**. When you are signed in, the account button at the top right of the editor (your initial, with your first name) opens a menu with your name and email, **Account** and **Sign Out**. Pick **Account** and Settings opens on the right place. You can also open Settings from its sliders icon in the bottom bar and choose **Profile** in the list. Your profile lives in Settings alongside the rest of your preferences, so there is one place to look.

## What it shows

**Profile** opens on your identity card: the same initial as the account button (or your Google picture), your name, your email address, and the date you joined. As a guest, the card says you are working as a guest and offers to sign in. Below it, in order:

- **Name and Colour**: your **Display name** (leave it empty to show the name you signed up with) and the colour people see on your cursor and avatar in every app. When you sign in with Google, a switch chooses whether your Google picture shows. Each change saves as you make it.
- **Time Zone**: the zone your account keeps for the emails that can wait (tips, check-ins, reminders and expiry warnings). They arrive on weekdays between 09:00 and 21:00 in that zone. It starts on **Automatic**, which follows the time zone of the browser you use. To set it yourself, press the zone and search for your city, region or offset (such as GMT+1), then pick it; it is saved at once and stays until you choose **Automatic** again.
- **Sign-in Methods** (an emailed code, and Google) and **Where You’re Signed In**, each browser signed in to your account, with a way to sign out of the others.
- **Google Drive**, when the deployment offers it, connects a Drive folder that mirrors your spaces; see [Google Drive](/help/account-and-data/google-drive/).
- **Your Account**, with **Sign Out**, which signs you out of every liveworkspace app in this browser.

**Notifications** sits beside Profile in the list, and **API Tokens** under **Developers**; see [API Tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/). Your deleted spaces are in the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), in the Explorer's Library.

## Email notifications

**Notifications** has an **Email** section with a switch for every optional email: **Someone Joins My Space**, **Someone Responds to a Team Invite**, **Tips and Check-Ins**, and **Milestones**. The whole row is the switch, so click anywhere on it to flip it. The switches are saved to your account and apply wherever you sign in. See [Email Notifications](/help/account-and-data/email-notifications/) for what each kind of email contains and when it sends.

The email switches only appear when the deployment can send mail and you are signed in. A guest sees a note asking them to sign in to choose their emails, since without an address the switches could never apply. The same page also holds **In-Editor Notifications**, which works for everyone.

## Danger zone

Profile ends with a **Danger Zone**: **Reset All Settings** puts every setting back to its default in every liveworkspace app (your profile, API tokens, webhooks and sign-ins stay), and the **Delete Account** button closes the account. **Delete Account** opens a confirmation dialog that asks you to type your email address, a deliberate speed bump before anything irreversible happens, and then the 6-digit code it emails you, so only someone who can read your inbox can delete the account. Confirming permanently removes your spaces (the Trash included), folders and the rest of your data from the server, then deletes the account itself and signs you out. This cannot be undone. For deleting a single space, or how guest data is scoped, see [Deleting Your Data](/help/account-and-data/deleting-your-data/).

## Signed-in feature

Deleting removes the data held against a verified account, so it needs one: the row is still shown to guests, saying it is only available once you are signed in, with a link to sign in, rather than hiding. A guest's spaces belong to their browser, and [Deleting Your Data](/help/account-and-data/deleting-your-data/) covers clearing those.
