The full-page [Explorer](/explorer) is your library: a sidebar on the left to move around, and the page you picked on the right, with its folders and items. Opening `/explorer` takes you to **Home**. (For the compact version inside the editor, see the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).)

The Explorer is shared by every liveworkspace app. It lives once, on liveworkspace.app, and opens at livehuddle's own `/explorer` too, so a folder can hold a Space beside a Document from livedocuments and a Design from livedesign. Its header shows the liveworkspace logo and then livehuddle's; click livehuddle's to go back to livehuddle. Your folders, favourites, sharing and Trash are the same whichever app you open it from.

## livehuddle or All Apps

At the top right of most pages, a two-way switch chooses what the lists show:

- **livehuddle** shows only livehuddle's spaces.
- **All Apps** shows every item from every app. Each row then carries its app's name, and the lists say "files" rather than "spaces".

Coming from livehuddle, the Explorer opens on **livehuddle**, and your choice holds as you move from page to page. Opening an item from another app takes you to that app's site to open it.

## Making things

**New Space** sits in the page header wherever a new space has an obvious home: Recent, Favourites and search results put it in My Work, outside any folder, and on Home it sits inside the search field. On My Work, inside a folder and in a team's library, the button becomes **Create**, offering **New Space** and **New Folder** (**New Subfolder** inside a folder). New always makes a space here, whichever filter is on; to make something in another app, open the Explorer from that app.

Pages that are not a place an item lives (the Inbox, Shared With Me and the Trash) have no New button.

## Overview

- **Home** is what the Explorer opens on ([Home](/help/explorer/home/)): the spaces you use most, what was shared with you recently.
- **Inbox** lists the [actions and comment threads](/help/explorer/activity/) waiting on you, with a badge counting the open actions assigned to you.
- **Shared With Me** gathers every item other people have [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) through a link, kept apart from your own library, with how many there are.

## Spaces

This group is the places your work lives, yours and your teams', not a list of your Spaces themselves.

- **My Work** is [your own library](/help/explorer/personal-space/). Beneath it sit the folders you create; opening it lists those folders, then the items you have not filed. Its own menu (its **⋯** button or a right-click) holds **New Folder** and **Use as Default for…** ([Default Folders](/help/explorer/folders/default-folders/)).
- **Each team** you belong to sits beside it, with its member count, and expands to the team's folders ([Team Spaces](/help/explorer/team-spaces/)).
- **Browser Storage** holds the spaces saved only in this browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)). It sits after your teams, with how many spaces there are, and shows only while this browser keeps at least one space or one of its own folders. Its page shows its folders, then its spaces, newest first, each opening in the editor. **New Folder** on its page, or the **⋯** menu on its row in the sidebar, makes a folder that lives in this browser too, and folders can hold folders, as in My Work. **Move to Folder** on a space's menu, or a drag onto a folder, files it. A folder's menu has **Rename**, **New Subfolder**, **Move Folder** and **Delete Folder**; deleting a folder never deletes what is in it, which moves up a level. When you are signed in each space has **Sync Space** to move it to your account; one in a folder goes to the My Work folder of the same name (or My Work itself when there is none), and its folder stays here, empty, until you delete it. A space's menu offers **Take Offline** to do the reverse. On liveworkspace.app, which can't read this browser's storage, Browser Storage is instead a link under More to the app's own Browser Storage page.
- **Invites** appears while a team invitation waits for you.
- **New Team**, when you are signed in, makes a team in place ([Teams](/help/collaboration/teams/)). If you are not signed in, a **Sign in to access Teams** card sits here instead.

## More

- **Library**, folded until you open it, expands to **Recent** (everything by when you last opened it, [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/)), **Favourites** (what you starred), and the **Image Gallery** (every image you have uploaded, with where each is used, [Image Gallery](/help/explorer/image-gallery/)), then **Archived** and, last, the **Trash**.
- **Archived**, in the Library, holds the spaces you have put away ([Archived](#archived)).
- **Trash**, the Library's last row, holds deleted items for 30 days, ready to restore ([Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/)). While you drag an item, the Trash row wakes up as a place to drop it. If the Library is folded, rest the drag on its row for half a second and it opens, so you can reach the Trash.

Which rows you expand is remembered in this browser. Opening a page opens the rows that hold it: its space and every folder down to it, or the Library for Recent, Favourites, Archived and the Trash (and the Library's other pages). It only ever opens rows, so a row you fold stays folded until you go to another page.

## Moving around with the keyboard

The sidebar is a single stop when you press Tab. Then the **arrow keys** move up and down, **Right** opens a row and **Left** closes it (or moves to its parent), **Home** and **End** jump to the first and last rows, **Enter** or **Space** opens the row, and typing a letter jumps to the next row starting with it. On a folder, or on My Work, **Shift+F10** (or the **Menu** key) opens its menu.

## Searching

The search field at the top of the page narrows the list you are on by the titles of your items and the names of their folders. On a page with no list of its own, such as Home or the Inbox, typing opens a **Search results** page across the whole library. The chips under each list's title, and typed filters like `made-by:ai` or `edited:7d`, narrow it further: [Search and Filter Spaces](/help/explorer/filters/) covers them. Search does not look inside an item's content.

## List, Card and Details

Every list of items (My Work, a folder, a team, Recent, Favourites and search results) can be shown three ways, switched with the **List view**, **Card view** and **Details view** buttons at the top right of the page. Cards are what you get until you choose otherwise. Shared With Me and the Trash keep a fixed list of their own. [List, Card and Details Views](/help/explorer/list-and-card-views/) covers them.

## An item's menu

Each row and card has a **⋯** button (or right-click it) with what you can do to that item: **Open**, **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Change Folder**, **Move to Archived**, **Share**, **Add to Favourites**, **History**, **Webhooks**, **Hide from Recent**, **Open Team** for a team's item, **Take Offline**, and **Delete Space** to move it to the Trash. What appears depends on your access: someone who reaches an item through a link cannot rename, move, share or delete it, and its menu offers **Remove from Shared With Me** instead.

**Webhooks** posts what happens to this space to a Slack or Microsoft Teams channel, or to a service you run. It is the space's own: what you set up there applies to this space only, and each of your other spaces has its own, up to 10 each. Choose **Add Webhook**, pick where it posts, paste the address (the dialog shows how to get one), name it, then tick what it should send (none are ticked at first) and **Who Did It**: **Anyone**, or **Only Me** for just your own changes. Each webhook has **Send Test**, **Pause**, **Delete**, **Edit Events** and its **Recent Deliveries**. You need to be signed in and able to edit the whole space: its owner, someone in its team, or someone with an Editor link to every tab.

**Duplicate** makes a copy named after the original with "(copy)" on the end, every tab included, and puts it in your library.

## Archived

**Move to Archived** in a space's menu puts it away without deleting it. Only its owner can archive it: on a team's space, that is the person who made it.

An archived space is hidden from every list except **Archived** and search results. It can't be opened, edited or shared, and opening it says so instead (from the Explorer, or from its address in the editor). It keeps its preview, and you can still rename it from its menu. Archiving it also removes, for everyone:

- its share links, for good, so people who had them lose access;
- its place in a team, so teammates no longer see it;
- its favourites, its spot in Recent and Home, and its threads and actions in the Inbox.

To work on it again, choose **Change Folder** from its menu (or from the card the editor shows) and pick a folder, My Work or a team. **Archived** is listed under your spaces in Change Folder too. The space comes back with no links and no favourites, so share it again if you need to.

A space kept only in **Browser Storage** can be archived too. It stays in this browser, and **Change Folder** moves it back to Browser Storage (or, signed in, into your account). See [Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/).

## On a phone

The sidebar folds away on a small screen. A bar under the header shows the name of the page you're on, with the menu icon beside it, and stays at the top as you scroll. Tap anywhere on the bar to open the **Menu** across the whole screen, then pick a page, or press the close button, to close it again.
