The palette is the strip across the top of the canvas. It holds the canvas tools and every category of thing you can add to a diagram, so it is your main launchpad while you build.

## The two dropdowns

The **menu button** and the tab's name sit just left of the strip. The strip itself starts with the selection mode, then the categories:

- On the left, the **selection mode** (what your pointer does), shown as a grid in three groups. **Edit**: **Select** (`V`), **Hand** (`H`), **Eraser** (`E`) and the [Format](/help/tips-and-tricks/format-painter/) painter. **Present**: **Laser** (`K`), **Spotlight** and [Slide Deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/), with **Avatar** shown only while you are walking (an Avatar button on the board is its way in). **Preview**: **Isometric** (`I`) and **Zen** (`Z`). Spotlight and Slide Deck are desktop only, and most of these stay greyed out until the canvas has something on it. See [Selection Modes](/help/selection-modes/).
- Then the **categories**, by name. Rest the pointer on one, or click it, and its elements open in a panel underneath, in named groups; click one to add it or drag it onto the canvas. They are **Shapes**, **Write** (text, sticky notes, tables and checklists), **Draw** (the pens and arrows) and **Layout** (lanes, frames, timelines, cards and badges), then **Ask** and **Session**, the interactive elements for [workshops](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/), then Icons, **Stickers**, Media and **Data**, plus **My shapes** once you have [imported a shape library](/help/explorer/shape-libraries/).

**Search** (the magnifier, or `S`) finds any element by name, the technology brand icons included. See [where each one lives](/help/palette/tools/).

## Adding elements

Pick a tile and it arms a **tap-or-drag** gesture: click the canvas to drop the element at its natural size, or drag on the canvas to size it as you place it. You can also drag a tile straight onto the canvas. An annotation is the exception: it drops at the centre of the visible canvas, ready to move.

If a boxed element is already selected when you click to drop another, the new one inherits its width and height, so you can chain similarly-sized nodes quickly. (A circle or diamond takes a square the size of the larger side, and a selected frame or lane never passes its size on.)

A tile with a keyboard shortcut shows its key in the corner on the strip; in the More and Search grids, hold `⌘` (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) to show them. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## More about the strip

The strip stays pinned to the top of the canvas on every device; on a phone its categories swipe sideways. It steps aside in Zen mode and on a view-only link. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) for the categories, Search and the order of the tiles, and [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/) for where everything else sits.

Looking for commands rather than elements? `⌘K` opens search with its actions; see [the Command Palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/).
