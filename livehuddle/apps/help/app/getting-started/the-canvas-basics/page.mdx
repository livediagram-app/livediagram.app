The [canvas](/help/canvas/the-canvas/) is the floor each tab of a Space is laid out on. It fills most of the editor, with the palette and a few panels on top for adding rooms, furniture and notes and for navigating your library. This article covers moving around it so the rest of livehuddle feels natural.

## Moving around

You will pan and zoom constantly. There are a few ways to do each:

- **Pan** by holding the **Space** bar and dragging, or by **middle-click dragging**, from anywhere. On a trackpad, a two-finger drag pans too, and on a phone or tablet a one-finger drag on empty canvas.
- **Zoom** with the zoom buttons in the bottom-right of the canvas, `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + scroll, `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `+` and `-`, or pinch-zoom on a trackpad or touch screen. `Shift+1` zooms to fit everything on the tab. The pattern background tiles endlessly as you move, so you never run out of canvas.

See [Pan and Zoom](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/) and [Zoom Controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/).

## Edit versus Walk

If you can edit the Space, the palette strip starts with a two-part toggle (see [Edit and Walk](/help/selection-modes/select/)):

- **Edit** (shortcut `E`) is for working with elements: click to select (click again to deselect), shift-click to add to the selection, and drag on empty canvas to draw a marquee that selects everything inside it.
- **Walk** (shortcut `W`) puts you in the Space as your character, to walk up to people and talk.

Panning works the same in both, so you never need to leave Edit just to move the view a little.

## Around the canvas

A few things sit over the canvas (see [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/)):

- **Palette**, the strip across the top, adds sticky notes, text, furniture, rooms and stickers. See the [Palette](/help/palette/) guides.
- **Explorer**, behind the menu button in the top-left corner, is your library of spaces and folders. See [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
- **Corner panels**, such as the Map and the tool panels, can be dragged by their header to another corner and collapsed to a banner.

Around them sit the fixed controls: the **tab bar** along the bottom, with Search, Settings and the light/dark switch at its right-hand end, and a cluster in the bottom-right corner with Undo and Redo, the **Theme & canvas** button, and the zoom controls. **Right-click menus** carry per-element formatting, and the tab menu carries tab-wide tools.

On a phone the layout is the same: the menu button sits in its own card left of the strip, the strip's tiles swipe sideways, and tapping a panel's header collapses it, so the canvas stays readable.

## Light and dark

The editor has its own appearance switch at the right-hand end of the bottom bar: click it to step through **Light**, **Dark** and **System** (which follows your device). This recolours the editor's interface around your canvas, and the canvas too: every [theme](/help/canvas/themes/) has a light and a dark side, and you see the one that matches your appearance. The theme itself, which recolours the canvas content, is set from the **Theme & canvas** button. See [Dark Mode](/help/tools/dark-mode/).
