<ArticleLayout title="Your First Space" description="From a new Space to a team gathered round the board in a couple of minutes, with no account required." categoryTitle="Getting Started" categorySlug="getting-started">

The quickest way to learn livehuddle is to open a Space and walk round it. This walkthrough takes you from a new Space to a board your team can meet at. You do not need to sign in, the [Space always works as a guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## Open a Space

<Step n={1}>
  Open [a new Space](/new). The **New Space** window opens: "A place for your team to meet, plan and
  run sessions." There is nothing to choose yet.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  It shows what **Create** makes: a **Blank Space** that opens on its start, with templates and the
  session tools ready. Under "Templates on its start" it names the first Virtual Spaces waiting
  there (Virtual Office, Coworking Space and Team Pod), and sessions to run.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Under that, **Sync Space to Server** says where the new Space will be saved. It starts on once you
  have signed in (saved to your account, on every device) and off while you try things out as a
  guest (saved in this browser only). Flip it before you create if you want the other place; you can
  also [move a Space](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) later. When you start from a folder
  or a team, the Space goes there and the switch is not shown.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Press **Create**. The Space is called "Untitled Space" and [Default
  Folders](/help/explorer/folders/default-folders/) decide which folder it lands in. **Skip** makes
  the same blank Space with its start closed, **Open Existing Space** opens the
  [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) instead, and the × (or **Esc**) backs out without making
  anything.
</Step>

<Figure caption="The New Space window: the Blank Space that Create makes, the templates waiting on its start, and where it will be saved.">
  <NewSpaceWizard />
</Figure>

<Step n={5}>
  The empty board opens on **Start a Space**: the **Virtual Spaces** first (Virtual Office,
  Coworking Space, Team Pod and All-Hands Hall), then the session
  [templates](/help/canvas/templates/) by category, with a search. Pick **Team Pod** to follow
  along, or close it with the × to start from an empty board.
</Step>

You can rename the Space later: click its title in the header at the top of the editor and type.
Renaming is instant and saved for you. Names cap at **60 characters**: typing simply stops there,
which is plenty for a title and short enough to stay readable in a tab bar or a list row.

## Walk in

A board from the Spaces opens with you **walking**: you are in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/), as a little character standing just below the board's **Walk In** button.

<Step n={1}>
  **Click anywhere on the floor** and your character walks there, or hold the **arrow keys** to
  steer it. Walk onto a chair or a desk and it sits down; press an arrow key to stand.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Press **`Enter`** to say something: a speech bubble opens over your head, and everyone in the
  Space sees it float up. Press **`Space`** to hop and wave a flag.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Your character is yours to dress: the **Avatar** button beside Undo and Redo opens its outfit,
  hair and size. Its shirt is always your participant colour, so everyone can tell which one is you.
</Step>

<Figure caption="Walking in Avatar mode: the character has walked over to the part of the board being talked about, which is ringed.">
  <AvatarMode />
</Figure>

To stop walking and work on the board, pick **Select** from the Selection Mode menu at the left of the palette, or press `V`. The board's **Walk In** button (or `W`) puts you back on your feet.

## Put something on the board

<Step n={1}>
  Find the **Palette**, the strip across the top of the canvas. **Popular** holds what a session is
  most often run with: a square, a sticky note, text, a frame, an arrow and an image, then the
  session kit.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  **Click the Sticky note tile**, then **click the board** where you want it: it drops there, ready
  to type into. Or drag the tile straight onto the board. Drop a few for the team's ideas.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  When the room is ready to decide, start a **Vote** or a **Timer** from the Session strip, and
  everyone's dots and the countdown appear for the whole room. See [Session
  Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).
</Step>

<Figure caption="Drag a tile from the Palette onto the board to drop it, and it lands selected.">
  <AddingElements />
</Figure>

## Invite the team

You now have a Space with something on the board. From here:

- **Share the link.** Hand a colleague a link and their character walks into the same Space. See [Sharing Your Space](/help/getting-started/sharing-your-document/).
- **Run the session** with the [Session tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/): a timer and a quick vote.
- **Move and multi-select.** Drag to reposition; drag on an empty part of the board to marquee-select several elements at once, or Shift-click to add one at a time.

<Tip>
  Made a mistake? [Undo](/help/canvas/undo/) and [Redo](/help/canvas/redo/) sit in the controls in
  the bottom-right corner (or press `Cmd/Ctrl+Z`), and the common keyboard shortcuts are in
  [Keyboard Essentials](/help/getting-started/keyboard-essentials/).
</Tip>
