<ArticleLayout title="Your First Space" description="From a new Space to a team gathered round the meeting table in a couple of minutes, with no account required." categoryTitle="Getting Started" categorySlug="getting-started">

The quickest way to learn livehuddle is to open a Space and walk round it. This walkthrough takes you from a new Space to a room your team can meet in. You do not need to sign in, the [Space always works as a guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## Open a Space

<Step n={1}>
  Open [a new Space](/new). The **New Space** window opens. There is nothing to choose yet.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  It shows what **Create** makes: a **Blank Space** that opens on its start. Under "Templates on its
  start" it names the Virtual Spaces waiting there (Virtual Office, Coworking Space and Team Pod).
  All-Hands Hall is there too.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Under that, **Sync Space to Server** says where the new Space will be saved. It starts on once you
  have signed in (saved to your account, on every device) and off while you try things out as a
  guest (saved in this browser only). Flip it before you create if you want the other place; you can
  also [move a Space](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) later. When you start from a folder
  or a team, the Space goes there and the switch is not shown.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Press **Create**. The Space is called "Untitled Space" and [Default
  Folders](/help/explorer/folders/default-folders/) decide which folder it lands in. **Skip** makes
  the same blank Space with its start closed, **Open Existing Space** opens the
  [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) instead, and the × (or **Esc**) backs out without making
  anything.
</Step>

<Figure caption="The New Space window: the Blank Space that Create makes, the templates waiting on its start, and where it will be saved.">
  <NewSpaceWizard />
</Figure>

<Step n={5}>
  The empty tab opens on **Start a Space**: the four **Virtual Spaces** (Virtual Office, Coworking
  Space, Team Pod and All-Hands Hall), with a search. See [Templates](/help/canvas/templates/). Pick
  **Team Pod** to follow along, or close it with the × to start from an empty floor.
</Step>

You can rename the Space later: click its title in the header at the top of the editor and type.
Renaming is instant and saved for you. Names cap at **60 characters**: typing simply stops there,
which is plenty for a title and short enough to stay readable in a tab bar or a list row.

## Walk in

A Space from the templates opens with you **walking**: you are in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/), as a little character standing just below the Space's **Walk In** button.

<Step n={1}>
  **Click anywhere on the floor** and your character walks there, or hold the **arrow keys** to
  steer it. Walk onto a chair or a desk and it sits down; press an arrow key to stand.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Press **`Enter`** to say something: a speech bubble opens over your head, and everyone in the
  Space sees it float up. Press **`Space`** to hop and wave a flag, or **`W`** to wave.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Your character is yours to dress: the **Avatar** button beside Undo and Redo opens its outfit,
  hair and size, and its **Status**, so everyone can see whether you are free to talk. Its shirt is
  always your participant colour, so everyone can tell which one is you.
</Step>

<Figure caption="Walking in Avatar mode: the character has walked over to the part of the Space being talked about, which is ringed.">
  <AvatarMode />
</Figure>

To stop walking and change the Space, pick **Select** from the Selection Mode menu at the left of the palette, or press `V`. The Space's **Walk In** button, or **Avatar** in the same menu, puts you back on your feet.

## Put a note on the wall

<Step n={1}>
  Find the **Palette**, the strip across the top of the canvas. **Popular** holds what a Space is
  most often furnished with: a sticky note, text, a frame, an image and a link card, then a room, a
  meeting table, a chair, Bring Focus and an Avatar Pad.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  **Click the Sticky note tile**, then **click the canvas** where you want it: it drops there, ready
  to type into. Or drag the tile straight onto the canvas. Drop one by the meeting table for the
  day's notes.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  To keep the meeting short, start a **Timer** from the bottom bar, or a **Vote** when the room
  needs to decide: the countdown and everyone's dots appear for the whole room. See [Session
  Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).
</Step>

<Figure caption="Drag a tile from the Palette onto the canvas to drop it, and it lands selected.">
  <AddingElements />
</Figure>

## Invite the team

You now have a Space with a room to meet in. From here:

- **Share the link.** Hand a colleague a link and their character walks into the same Space. See [Sharing Your Space](/help/getting-started/sharing-your-document/).
- **Host the meeting.** Take the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) role so the timer and the vote answer to you, and press a [Bring Focus](/help/palette/behaviour/bring-focus/) button to bring everyone to one spot.
- **Move and multi-select.** Drag to reposition; drag on an empty part of the canvas to marquee-select several elements at once, or Shift-click to add one at a time.

<Tip>
  Made a mistake? [Undo](/help/canvas/undo/) and [Redo](/help/canvas/redo/) sit in the controls in
  the bottom-right corner (or press `Cmd/Ctrl+Z`), and the common keyboard shortcuts are in
  [Keyboard Essentials](/help/getting-started/keyboard-essentials/).
</Tip>
