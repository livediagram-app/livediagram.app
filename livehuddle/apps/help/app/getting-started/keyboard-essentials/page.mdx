You can do everything in livehuddle with the mouse, but a few keyboard shortcuts make working on the [canvas](/help/canvas/the-canvas/) much faster. This is the short list worth committing to memory. For the complete reference, see [keyboard shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Edit and Walk

The [Edit | Walk toggle](/help/selection-modes/select/) at the start of the palette has a key for each side. Like every single-key shortcut, they work while nothing with a label is selected; with one labelled element selected, a letter key starts typing its label instead.

  <KeyboardEssentials />
</Figure>

- **`E`**: Edit (click, shift-click, and marquee-select elements). **`V`** does the same.
- **`W`**: Walk, as your character.
- **`S`**: Search the palette strip for any element to add.

Some keys add an element: **`T`** Text and **`N`** a sticky note. **`8`** adds Text too.

To remove elements, select them and press **`Delete`** or **`Backspace`**.

## Moving the view

You will pan and zoom constantly, in Edit or while you walk:

- **Hold Space and drag** to pan from anywhere.
- **Middle-click and drag** to pan as well (switch this off with **Middle-Mouse Pan** in Settings if you need the button for something else).
- **`Cmd/Ctrl` + `+`** and **`Cmd/Ctrl` + `-`** zoom in and out, **`Cmd/Ctrl+0`** resets to 100%, and **`Shift+1`** zooms to fit everything on the tab.
- **Pinch** on a trackpad or touch screen to zoom; the on-screen zoom buttons sit in the bottom-right of the canvas.

## Everyday editing

A few shortcuts cover the actions you repeat most:

- **`Cmd/Ctrl+Z`** undoes, and **`Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z`** (or **`Ctrl+Y`**) redoes. The same controls sit in the bottom-right corner of the canvas.
- **`Cmd/Ctrl+C`**, **`X`** and **`V`** copy, cut and paste through your system clipboard, and **`Cmd/Ctrl+D`** duplicates the selection. See [Copy and Paste](/help/tips-and-tricks/copy-and-paste/).
- **`Delete`** or **`Backspace`** removes the selection.
- **`Cmd/Ctrl+K`** opens [search](/help/search-panel/), which also runs commands.
- **Double-click** a sticky note or text to edit its label, or select it and press **Space**, or simply start typing to replace the label.
- **Tab** (with the canvas focused) selects the next element, **Shift+Tab** the previous, so you can walk the canvas without the mouse, and Tab past the last element moves on out of the canvas. Selection changes are announced to screen readers.
- **Escape** cancels an in-progress text edit, closes an open dialog, or clears the selection.
- **Enter** confirms a dialog. In a text or sticky label it starts a new line, and clicking away finishes the edit.

## While walking

In [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) a few keys belong to your character instead:

- **Arrow keys** walk, and **`Space`** hops and waves a flag.
- **`Enter`** opens a speech bubble over your head; **`Enter`** again sends it.
- **`W`** waves, and **`J`**, **`S`**, **`C`** and **`D`** play the other moves; **`1`** to **`5`** throw a reaction around you.
- **`E`**, **`V`** or **`Escape`** stops walking.

<Tip>
  The editor has a built-in shortcut reference: open **Settings** from the sliders icon at the
  bottom-right and pick **Keyboard** to see the full list any time without leaving the canvas.
  Holding `Cmd` (or `Ctrl`) also shows each palette tile's shortcut on the tile itself.
</Tip>
