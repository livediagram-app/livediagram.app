livehuddle is multiplayer from the moment someone walks in. Sharing is just creating a link and handing it over: whoever opens it joins the same Space, walks round it as their character, and sees everyone else live. The person you share with does not need an account. For the full details see [Sharing](/help/collaboration/sharing/).

## Create a share link

<Step n={1}>
  Open the space you want to share and click **Share** at the top right of the editor, or choose
  **Share** from the space's **⋯** menu in the Explorer. Only the space's owner can issue passes, so
  someone who joined through a link sees no **Share** button.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Under **Issue a pass**, the role card starts on **Participant** (always walking in, talks and
  sends reactions, but adds nothing to the Space), the pass most meetings want; press it to pick
  **Editor** (changes the Space with you in real time) instead. **Valid** sets how long it lasts:
  Forever, 1 week, 1 month or 6 months. In a space with several tabs, **Opens** can limit the link
  to one tab.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Click **Create Pass**. The link is copied for you straight away, ready to send to whoever you want
  in the space; each pass in the list also has its own copy button.
</Step>

If you are a guest, check the name under **Sharing as** at the foot of the dialog first (or press the
shuffle button beside it for another): that is the name peers see on your cursor and character. A space
saved to **This Browser** has to be synced to your account before it can be shared.

## What happens when they open it

Someone opening your link for the first time enters the password if you set one, picks the name others will see (a signed-in visitor keeps their account name), and then lands in the Space:

<Figure caption="Everyone in a shared space sees the others' live cursors and a presence stack of who is here.">
  <LiveCursors />
</Figure>

- **Characters, cursors and selections.** Everyone sees where everyone else is standing and pointing, and what they have selected. Walking in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/), each person shows their name and status over their character.
- **Simultaneous editing.** On an Editor pass, you both change the space at once. To avoid clashes, an element one person has selected is gently locked for the others while they hold it.
- **No sign-in required.** A guest walks in straight away, the Space never blocks them with a sign-in wall. See [Guest vs account](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## Managing and ending access

Back in the Share dialog you can manage links over time:

- **Multiple passes.** Issue several, with different roles, for example a Participant pass for the team and an Editor pass for whoever sets up the Space. They are listed under **Passes**.
- **Revoke** a pass to cut off access through it. Expiring passes show a countdown, and expired ones move under **Expired**, where they can be extended or deleted. See [Share Link Expiry](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-link-expiry/).
- **Password Protection.** Protect every pass with one shared password; everyone opening a link must enter it first, embeds included. See [Share Passwords](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/).
- **Community.** The dialog's **Community** tab, beside **Passes**, publishes the space, once you're signed in, to the public [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/) gallery. A space in the Community can't have a password, and a password-protected one can't be published.

<Tip>
  Want to put the space on a web page or in a doc? Each pass can also be copied as an embed (**Copy
  iframe**) or, while no password is set, as a live image that stays up to date. A Participant pass
  (or a legacy Viewer pass) embeds look-only; an Editor pass embeds an editable space. See
  [Embeds](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/).
</Tip>
