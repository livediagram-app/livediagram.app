The **quick controls** sit at the right-hand end of the bottom bar, after the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/)'s tabs, and gather the actions you want reachable at any moment, whatever you are doing on the canvas. They stay put while the rest of the interface comes and goes with your selection, and a matching cluster appears at the bottom of the full-page [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), so the two screens don't hide their controls in different places.

## What lives here

Three controls, left to right, with **Get Started** ahead of them while there is something to show you:

- **Get Started**, only while a tour or the Receptionist is on offer: a play button that opens a short list of what is on offer, such as **Ask Receptionist** while you walk on a tab with the Receptionist, or a **Portal Tour** while a portal is selected. Pick one to start it. See [The Receptionist](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/avatar-tour/).
- **Tools**: a toolbox that opens a panel of the tools that work on the whole space, grouped under small headings. What it holds depends on what you may do here:
  - **Search…**, to open the [search panel](/help/search-panel/) and jump to any space, tab, or element, drop something onto the canvas, or run a command. `⌘K` (Ctrl+K) opens it straight from the keyboard.
  - **History**, with your [Snapshots](/help/collaboration/snapshots/) in it, and **Webhooks**. Signed out, both are listed and ask you to sign in.

  Choosing a tool opens it inside the panel, with a back button to the list (Search closes Tools and opens the search panel). `Escape` steps back, then closes Tools.

- **Settings**: the [Settings dialog](/help/user-interface/settings/): the preferences that follow you between devices, plus the **Keyboard** category with the full [shortcut reference](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) and the switch that turns shortcuts off.
- **Notifications**: the bell, just right of Settings, with no label. Notices slide into it as they leave, and a dot on it means one left before you saw it. Press it to open **Notifications**, a read-only list of every notice you received, newest first, with how long ago. **Clear** empties it. The list is kept in this browser for each space (the last 50), and the full-page Explorer's bar has its own bell and list.
- **Appearance**: click to step the [interface between Light, Dark, and System](/help/tools/dark-mode/) (System follows your device). Its icon shows the mode in force, and hovering it names it. This is the app's own chrome, not the board's theme, which lives behind the **Theme & Canvas** button in the bottom-right corner.

The Explorer's cluster holds Tools with **Search…** alone, and a link to livehuddle's source on GitHub, on a computer.

On a computer, Get Started, Tools and Settings show their names beside their icons (switch **Button Labels** off in **Settings › Appearance** for icons alone). On a phone the bar is tight, so the controls are icons only, and Get Started and Tools open as sheets from the bottom of the screen.

Because they are fixed in the corner rather than tied to a selection, the quick controls are the dependable starting point when you are not sure where an action lives: search finds both things and commands.
