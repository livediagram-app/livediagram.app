Select something on the canvas and a **toolbar** appears next to it, holding the quick actions for what you have selected. It is contextual: it shows up only when there is a selection, and its contents change to match. A small caption above it names the selection, for example "Selected Square".

<Figure caption="With one shape selected, the toolbar floats beside it with More, Add Text, Duplicate, Format Painter, stacking order and Delete.">
  <ContextualToolbar />
</Figure>

## One element selected

From left to right:

- **More** (**⋯**): opens the element's full menu, the same one a right-click opens, with its colours, borders, text styling and the rest. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/).
- **Add Text** (or **Edit Text** once the element has some).
- **Duplicate**.
- **Format Painter**: takes this element's style, ready to paint onto the next elements you press. See [Format Painter](/help/selection-modes/format-painter/).
- **Bring to Front** and **Send to Back**.
- **Delete**.

Which buttons show depends on the element: an image has no text to add. On a link that can't edit, a right-click opens a [short element menu](/help/user-interface/context-menus/) instead.

## Several elements selected

Drag a marquee or Shift-click to select several, and the toolbar switches to the actions that make sense across a group:

- **More** (**⋯**): the selection's menu, for styling everything at once.
- **Filter Selection** (only when the selection mixes kinds): narrows it by type, so you can marquee over a busy area and keep, say, just the sticky notes. See [Multi-Select](/help/canvas/selecting-and-grouping/multi-select/).
- **Duplicate**.
- **Export**: export just these elements.
- **Delete**.

The caption reads "Selected Elements" with a count.

<Figure caption="Select a mix of elements and the toolbar switches to More, Filter Selection, Duplicate, Export and Delete.">
  <MultiSelectToolbar />
</Figure>

The toolbar follows your selection so the controls are always close to what they affect, and disappears the moment you deselect, handing the canvas back.

<Tip>
  To copy one element's look onto others, select it and press **Format Painter** on this toolbar,
  then press each element you want to paint (see [Format
  Painter](/help/selection-modes/format-painter/)).
</Tip>
