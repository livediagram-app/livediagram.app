A **context menu** is the menu you get by right-clicking, or by pressing and holding for half a second on touch. Each one is scoped to whatever you clicked, so it only ever offers what makes sense there. On a phone the menus open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen.

## On an element

The element menu is where an element is **styled and set up**. It is built from collapsible sections, with **Appearance** and **Tools** opening to the side, and which ones appear depends on what you clicked. For a shape:

- **Clear Styles**, the eraser in a slim bar across the top of the menu, while the element has a style to clear: one press puts it back to the tab's theme.
- **Arrange**: **Bring to Front**, **Send to Back** and its opacity.
- **Size** and **Rotation**.
- **Appearance**: the shape's **Presets**, **Colours** (text, background and border, with **Reset to Theme**), **Border**, **Shadow** and **Finish**, and, once it has text, a **Text** flyout of its own with font, size, padding, alignment and markers.

Images and links add a section of their own (**Image**, **Link**), and the Space's pieces gather their own settings under **Tools**: which kind a piece is, a chair's facing, a room's walls and floor, a portal's destination, the Stage, a Screen's address and more.

The everyday verbs live elsewhere: **Duplicate**, stacking order and **Delete** are on the [selection toolbar](/help/user-interface/toolbar/), and cut, copy and paste are on the keyboard (see [Copy and Paste](/help/tips-and-tricks/copy-and-paste/)). Right-click a multi-selection and you get the same kind of menu for the whole group.

On a desktop pointer, the style controls **preview on hover**: rest the pointer on a colour, border, preset, rotation, marker, alignment, or size tile and the selection shows the change live; it only sticks when you click, and moving away reverts it. Comparing looks costs nothing.

## On empty canvas

Right-click empty canvas and you get the **tab menu** (below) with a labelled **Paste** row straight under the quick actions, since a right-click on empty space is usually "paste what I copied". Pasted elements land just offset from where they were copied, selected and ready to drag into place.

## On a tab

Right-click a tab in the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/), or use its **⋯** button, for the tab menu:

- A row of quick actions: **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Paste**, **Side by Side** (**Close Side by Side** while a split is open; not on a phone or with only one tab) and **Delete**. Delete asks nothing, since **Undo** brings the tab back, and is unavailable when it is the space's only tab.
- Then full-width rows, in groups split by a line: **Organise in Tab Folder** (a [tab folder](/help/tabs/tab-folders/)) and **Add to Another Space** (link it into another of your spaces, so it stays one live tab and edits show in both places); **Attendance**, who was in the tab and when (see [Attendance](/help/collaboration/live-presence/tab-attendance/)); **Import**, **Export** and **Clear**.

A tab connected from another app has a shorter menu: **Organise in Tab Folder**, **Attendance** and **Remove Tab**, plus **Connect Again** when the file it shows can no longer be opened.

Auto-align, the layout tidier, is in the [command palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/) rather than in this menu. See [Layout Cleanup](/help/tools/layout-cleanup/).

A tab's theme and canvas background are not in this menu: they are behind the **Theme & Canvas** button in the bottom-right corner. See [Themes](/help/canvas/themes/). A Timer or Stopwatch is run from the buttons under it, not from this menu. See [Timer and Stopwatch](/help/collaboration/session-tools/timer/).

## On a Participant or Viewer link

Right-click an element (a long press on a touch screen) and you get a short menu with only what your link lets you do: **Copy Text** wherever the element has text. Empty canvas has no menu on these links. A Participant's tab menu holds **Attendance** alone; a Viewer has no tab menu.

Context menus close as soon as you pick an action or click elsewhere, and `Escape` closes them too.
