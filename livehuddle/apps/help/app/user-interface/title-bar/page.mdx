There is no bar across the top of the editor: the canvas takes the whole height, and two cards float over its top corners. The **menu card** at the top left holds the logo and the menu button. The **actions card** at the top right holds **Share** or **Copy** and your account. The [palette](/help/palette/) sits between them.

On a phone the menu card joins the palette's row and the actions card shows its glyphs alone, without names; in a window too narrow for the palette and the actions card's names side by side, the actions card drops its names first. an [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) hides both cards.

## The logo

The logo at the left of the menu card opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) on the folder that holds this space. Its wordmark shows on a wide screen only.

## The menu button

The **≡** button beside the logo opens the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) under it, and pressing it again closes the panel. The current space is at the top of the panel: its name, a badge saying who can see it and its **⋯** menu. Click the name there to rename the space: `Enter` keeps the new name and `Escape` the old one.

A new space names itself: while it still has its starting name (such as "Untitled Space"), the first label you type on its first tab becomes its name. A name you gave it yourself is never replaced.

## Share or Copy

The actions card holds one main action, and which one you get depends on whose space it is.

**Share** is for the owner, and for members of the team whose library holds the space. It opens the [Share dialog](/help/collaboration/sharing/), where you create and manage links. The button turns solid blue once the space has a live link, so you can tell at a glance that it is shared. On a [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) space it asks you to save the space to your account first, since there is nothing on a server to share yet.

**Copy** is for everyone else who has the space open: someone on a share link of any role. It makes a copy of the space that belongs to you:

<Step n={1}>Click **Copy**. It reads **Copying** while the copy is made.</Step>
<Step n={2}>The editor opens your copy, named "Copy of" and the original name.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Find it again later at the top level of My Work in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
</Step>

Your copy starts **Private**: no share links come with it, and nothing you do to it touches the original. If you opened the space through a link to a single tab, the copy holds just that tab. You do not need an account to copy; as a guest the copy is saved under your [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/).

Someone who opened a space from the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/finding-community-documents/) does not get **Copy** in the title bar. A slim bar under the title bar offers **Make a Copy** instead, which works the same way.

## Your account

At the end of the actions card is your account. Signed in, it shows your initial; click it for a menu with your name and email, **Account** (which opens [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) on your account details, without leaving the space) and **Sign Out**. Not signed in, it shows **Sign in** instead (see [Signing In](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/)).
