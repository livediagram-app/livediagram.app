## Share or Copy

Which button you get depends on whose space it is.

**Share** is for the owner, and for members of the team whose library holds the space. It opens the [Share dialog](/help/collaboration/sharing/), where you create and manage links and choose who can see the space. The button turns solid once the space has a live link, so you can tell at a glance that it is shared. On a [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) space it asks you to save the space to your account first, since there is nothing on a server to share yet.

**Copy** is for everyone else who has the space open: someone on a share link of any role. It makes a copy of the space that belongs to you:

<Step n={1}>Click **Copy**. It reads **Copying** while the copy is made.</Step>
<Step n={2}>The editor opens your copy, named "Copy of" and the original name.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Find it again later at the top level of My Work in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
</Step>

Your copy starts **Private**: no share links come with it, and nothing you do to it touches the original. If you opened the space through a link to a single tab, the copy holds just that tab. You do not need an account to copy; as a guest the copy is saved under your [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/).

Someone who opened a space from the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/finding-community-documents/) does not get **Copy** in this card. A slim bar under the top of the canvas offers **Make a Copy** instead, which works the same way.

On a phone the card meets the right edge of the screen and its buttons show their icons alone. Share and Copy wait while the welcome screen of a new space is open, and the whole card is hidden inside an [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/).

## Your account

At the right of the card is your account. Signed in, it shows your picture or initials and your first name; click it for a menu with your name and email, **Account** (which opens [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) on your account details, without leaving the space) and **Sign Out**. Not signed in, it shows **Sign In** instead (see [Signing In](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/)).

## Where the title bar's parts went

- **The logo** sits in its own card at the top left. Click it to open the Explorer on the folder that holds this space.
- **The space's name** is no longer shown in the editor; the tab bar names the tabs. To rename the space, use **Rename** on its ⋯ menu in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).
- **Who can see the space** is in the Share dialog, behind **Share**.
- **Your role** is no longer shown: the link you opened the space with sets it.
