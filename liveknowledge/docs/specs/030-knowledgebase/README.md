# Knowledgebase

What makes liveknowledge a knowledgebase, for people and for AI. Everything here is built on the article engine the fork
brought from livedocuments ([Article pages](../007-editor/article-pages.md)) and on the platform every app shares.

- [Knowledgebases and pages](knowledgebases-and-pages.md): the domain language, a knowledgebase, its page tree and the continuous page.
- [Reading and editing a page](editing.md): pages open to read; Edit opens one shared live draft, Save or Cancel.
- [Links and embeds](links-and-embeds.md): page links, backlinks and other apps' items inside a page.
- [Verification](verification.md): owners, verified pages and reviews that come due.
- [Approval](approval.md): Pending Approval, and other members approving or rejecting a page.
- [Comments on a page](page-comments.md): comment threads at the foot of every page.
- [Reactions on a page](page-reactions.md): any emoji from any signed-in reader, Viewers included, beside Edit; no notifications.
- [Search and Ask](search-and-ask.md): the search index, and answers with sources from a team's own Claude or OpenAI key.
- [AI Knowledge](ai-knowledge.md): what agents read, what nobody could answer, pages that disagree, suggested edits.
- [Knowledgebase Home](knowledgebase-home.md): the page a person lands on.
- [Publish to the Web](publishing.md): the knowledgebase, or one section, as a public, read-only site.
- [Describe to set up](describe-to-set-up.md): no start screen, and a page structure proposed from a description.

Blueprints: [index](blueprints/README.md).
