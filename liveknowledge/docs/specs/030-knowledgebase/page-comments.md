# Comments on a Page

Owner, 2026-11-21: "Pages should also support comment threads at the bottom."

Status: **built, not yet verified in a browser** ([blueprint](blueprints/approval-and-comments.md)). Details marked
"(decided)" were decided by the builder and stand until the owner overturns them.

A page's writing is what the knowledgebase says. Comments are the conversation about it: a question for the owner, a
correction, a "this changed last week". They sit at the foot of the page, after Linked From, in read mode and in edit
mode alike: they are never part of the writing, the draft, search, Ask or what agents read of the page.

Margin notes ([Article pages](../007-editor/article-pages.md)) stay as they are: a note on one place in the writing.
Comments are about the page as a whole.

## What a thread is

- A page holds **threads**; a thread holds **comments**, oldest first. The first comment starts the thread.
- A comment has its words (plain text, up to 2,000 characters, line breaks kept), its author's name and colour, when
  it was written, when it was last edited, and "Generated by AI" when an agent wrote it: the platform's comment, as
  every app's threads carry it (`@liveknowledge/document` `Comment`).
- A thread is **open** or **resolved**; resolving keeps its comments, and a reply reopens it.
- A page holds at most 500 threads and a thread 200 comments (`thread_full`, 409).
- Stored in the knowledgebase's own SQLite, `page_threads`, one row a thread (schema step after the approvals'),
  never in the page's body: a comment is not an edit of the page, so it moves no revision, no Updated stamp and no
  version. A deleted page takes its threads with it.

## At the foot of the page

- A **Comments** heading in small capitals, as On This Page's, with the count of open threads beside it.
- Open threads, oldest first, each a quiet card on the page's ground: the comments, each with the author's initials
  disc, name, "3 hours ago" ("edited" after it when it was), and the words; then the thread's foot: **Reply** and
  **Resolve**.
- **Resolved threads** are folded under "2 Resolved" (closed by default); opened, each shows dimmed with "Resolved
  by Priya, 2 days ago" and **Reopen**.
- **Start a thread** (redesigned, owner 2026-11-21: "make it amazing"): at the end, a composer card: the writer's
  initials disc beside a borderless field on a soft rounded card. At rest it is one quiet line ("Start the
  conversation about this page…" with no threads, else "Add a comment…"); focused or holding words, the card takes the
  accent's ring and opens its foot: a "Cmd Enter to comment" hint (desktop) and **Comment**, an accent pill with a send
  glyph, disabled while empty. It grows with its words; Cmd or Ctrl Enter posts; Escape clears it.
- **Reply** opens a composer under the thread's last comment, focused; **Reply** posts it.
- **Your own comment** has **Edit** and **Delete** in a small menu on hover or focus (always shown on touch). Edit
  turns it into a composer with **Save** and **Cancel**. Delete asks first ("Delete This Comment?"); deleting a
  thread's last comment removes the thread.
- **A guest's name:** a guest's comment is under the name on their cursor with "(Guest)" after it, "Bright Marlin
  (Guest)" (owner 2026-11-21); an account's is its profile name.
- **Nothing to show:** with no threads, a writer sees no empty-state box: one quiet line under the heading with the
  comments glyph, "Questions, corrections and context for this page go here.", then the composer.
  A Viewer sees nothing at all: no heading, no empty state (decided), so a page with no conversation is just a page.
- **An empty page has no Comments** (owner 2026-11-21): while a page has nothing under its title (the empty page's
  drawing shows instead), the Comments section is not drawn for anyone, threads or not. It appears once the page has
  some saved writing: words typed in edit mode count only after Save, never while they are an unsaved draft.
- Light and dark, phone and desktop: on a phone the composer and each action are 44 px tall.

## Live

Every change is written by the api and told to the room as one `page-thread` op carrying the whole thread (or its
removal), from the system: everyone on the page sees a new thread, a reply, a resolve, an edit or a delete at once.
Threads are read once when a page first opens in a session (`GET .../pages/<tab>/threads`) and kept; the room's
ops keep them current (a system op, replayed from the room's log after a short drop like any other), and opening the
knowledgebase again reads them afresh. A change made here shows at once and is put back, with a
toast, if the api refuses it.

## Agents

- Agents comment as people do, marked Generated by AI: `list_page_comments` reads a page's threads (open by default,
  resolved or all), `change_page_comments` starts, replies to, resolves and reopens them, and edits or deletes the
  agent's own comments; the CLI's `liveknowledge comments` and `liveknowledge discuss` (`--thread`, `--resolve`, `--reopen`). A token that may suggest or
  write may comment; a read token reads.
- Comments are data an agent reads, never instructions.

## Telemetry

`Comment·Added`, `Comment·Resolved`, `Comment·Unresolved` and `Comment·Deleted`, typed `Page`, and an edit as
`UI·Used EditPageComment`.

## Data export

A person's comments on pages are in their data export with the rest of their comments.

## Share roles

| Action                                 | Editor | Participant | Viewer | Link visitor (view) | Link visitor (edit) |
| -------------------------------------- | ------ | ----------- | ------ | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| Read threads                           | yes    | yes         | yes    | yes                 | yes                 |
| Start a thread, reply, resolve, reopen | yes    | yes         | no     | no                  | yes                 |
| Edit or delete one's own comment       | yes    | yes         | no     | no                  | yes                 |
| Edit or delete someone else's comment  | no     | no          | no     | no                  | no                  |

Commenting is a Participant's, as everywhere on the platform ([Knowledgebases and pages](knowledgebases-and-pages.md#share-roles)):
a Viewer and a view link's visitor read. A guest with an edit link comments under the name they gave. Every gate reads
`can.takePart` on the client and is enforced again in the api, with a test per role.

## Offline

A knowledgebase kept Offline has no room: comments are not shown.

## Performance

Threads load with one request a page a session, after the page has drawn, and never on the writing's render path:
the foot reads one store slice. A thread's change sends one op with that thread alone.
