# Knowledgebase blueprints

Follow the references below only as needed; never upfront.

- ./continuous-page.md - when implementing or changing the knowledgebase page: one page kind and how a page is made, the continuous size and its height, where the page sits, how the view frames and scrolls it, what is not offered while writing, and the doors closed on slides and infographics
- ./page-surface.md - when implementing or changing how a page stands on screen: the choice to keep the canvas engine and lock it, the lock (the column, its side room, the scale on a narrow window), the locked viewport store and the native scroll container, what is painted for a page, the doors closed on zoom and panning, and what is not built
- ./page-tree.md - when implementing or changing the page tree: `Tab.parent` and how it is read, the limits, the tree functions (rows, ancestors, add, move, indent, outdent, delete), and the list of what is not built yet (summaries, the room's check, the sidebar, the breadcrumb, share roles)
- ./knowledge-ui.md - when implementing or changing what the editor shows of a page's knowledge: the typed client for the knowledge routes, page links in the writing, Linked From, embeds from other apps, the verification meta row, the right rail and Page Health, and search
- ./ask-ui.md - when implementing or changing the switch between Pages, Ask and AI Knowledge, the view in the address, the surface a view is drawn on, AI Settings (the provider, the team's own key and the model) or the Ask view (the conversation, citations, Sources, Knowledge Gaps, recent questions, its states and failures)
- ./home.md - when implementing or changing the Knowledgebase Home: its routes and who sees it, the list and the summaries it reads, the search across knowledgebases, the hero's illustration, the sections and their loading states, and what it hands the editor (a question for Ask, Import)
- ./mcp-connect-ui.md - when implementing or changing the Connect view: the fourth tab and its gates, the six apps and their steps (shared with Build with AI), the access rows per role, the bounded status checks and their cost, Already Connected and Other Ways In
- ./ai-knowledge-ui.md - when implementing or changing the AI Knowledge view: its one read and how an action re-reads it, each section (agents, pages that disagree, unanswered questions, agents' suggested edits, Knowledge Health, Always in Context, What an Agent Sees), their empty states and who may do what
- ./describe-to-set-up.md - when implementing or changing Describe to Set Up: who is offered it and when a knowledgebase counts as empty, the sidebar button and Describe to Add Pages, the dialog's three steps and states, the suggestion route's client, how the ticked pages are written through the Import write path, and what is not built
- ./editing.md - when implementing or changing read mode and edit mode: the draft flow and its record, the lens the editor edits through, Save, Cancel and Discard, who is editing, what the room withholds from a reader, what reads the saved page only, and the upkeep that drops old drafts
- ./approval-and-comments.md - when implementing or changing Approval or Comments on a Page: the shared rule in @liveknowledge/knowledge, the field and the page_approvals and page_threads tables, the approval and threads routes and their room op, the agents' tools and commands, the editor's meta row, rail, tree dot, Home section and the threads at a page's foot, and their performance
- ./publishing.md - when implementing or changing Publish to the Web: the capability check, who may publish, the scope and page addresses, the published snapshot and its refresh, the site address registry, the routes, the public site's pages, caching, limits and the leak tests
- ./page-reactions.md - when implementing or changing Reactions on a Page: the emoji check and bounds in @liveknowledge/api-schema, the page_reactions table, the reactions route, its rate limit and its room op, and the editor's store, capsule and picker in the page's floating corner
- ./suggested-edits-ui.md - when implementing or changing agents' suggested edits on a page or in the Inbox: the one health read and the live op, the view store, the writing's plugin and how a passage is found, the pill, popover, Show in Page, rail and tree dot, Accept and Dismiss, the outbox push, and their limits
