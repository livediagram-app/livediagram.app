livediagram runs in your browser, and it can also be installed like an app, on a phone or a computer. Signed in, you can then turn on phone notifications, so you hear about a mention or an assigned action without keeping a tab open.

## Installing livediagram

Use your browser's own install option, such as the install button in the address bar on a computer, or Add to Home Screen on a phone. The installed app opens on the Explorer, so your canvases are the first thing you see.

On iPhone and iPad, install it first if you want notifications: tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, and open livediagram from your Home Screen.

## Turning On Phone Notifications

1. Sign in, on the device that should get them.
2. Open **Settings**, then **Notifications** under **Account**.
3. In the **Phone** section, press **Turn On**. Your browser asks for permission first.

One device is enough: notifications from every liveworkspace app reach it. The devices you have turned on are listed there, and you can remove one.

## What You Hear About

The **Phone** section has a switch for each kind, all on until you turn them off:

- **Someone Comments on My Work**
- **Someone Mentions Me in a Comment**
- **Someone Assigns Me an Action**
- **Replies to Threads I Commented In**

A notification says who did it and on which canvas, never what they wrote. Tap it to open the canvas at that comment thread or action. You aren't notified about your own changes, or while you already have the canvas open.

If Settings says notifications are **Blocked**, allow them for livediagram in your browser's site settings, then come back. For the same events by email, see [Email Notifications](/help/account-and-data/email-notifications/).
