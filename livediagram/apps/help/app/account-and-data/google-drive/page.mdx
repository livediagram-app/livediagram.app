Google Drive sync belongs to your **liveworkspace** account, so it is the same in livediagram and every other liveworkspace app, and one connection covers them all. When you are signed in, each of your canvases becomes a `.livediagram` file in a **liveworkspace** folder in your Drive, in folders that match yours. Renames, moves and deletions sync both ways, and it keeps up on liveworkspace's servers whether or not livediagram is open.

To connect, open **Settings**, choose **Account**, and find **Google Drive** under **Cloud Sync**. In the Explorer, a small cloud by each canvas's Updated time says whether it is synced.

**[Read the full Google Drive guide on liveworkspace.app](https://liveworkspace.app/help/google-drive)**: connecting, what is synced and how often, the Explorer's marks, folders liveworkspace can't see, opening files from Drive with **Open with**, switching Google account and disconnecting.

<Tip>
  Only your own work is synced: canvases in a team, canvases shared with you and canvases kept only in this
  browser stay out of Drive.
</Tip>
