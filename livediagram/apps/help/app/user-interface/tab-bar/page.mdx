A canvas can hold many boards, and the **tab bar** is how you move between them. Each tab is its own canvas; the bar runs along the bottom of the editor and shows them in a row, so the whole canvas is one click wide. The [quick controls](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/) (Search, Settings and the appearance switch) sit at its right-hand end.

</Figure>

## What you can do here

- **Switch** between boards by clicking a tab. A small stack of initials avatars on a tab shows who else is on it right now.
- **Add** a new tab with the **+** button straight after the last tab. It arrives numbered ("Tab 2", "Tab 3"), wearing the current tab's theme, background and font, in the same mode, on that mode's start, so you can begin from a [template](/help/canvas/templates/) or close it to leave the tab blank.
- **Rename** a tab by double-clicking the active one, or from its menu.
- **Reorder** tabs by dragging them along the bar; a caret shows where the tab will land.
- **Manage** a tab from its menu: right-click any tab (it becomes the active one), or click the **⋯** button on the active tab. The menu holds **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Paste**, **Lock Tab** and **Delete**, plus **Organise in Tab Folder**, **Add to Another Canvas** (link it into another canvas), import, export and more. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/#on-a-tab).
- **Group** related tabs into one-level folders with **Organise in Tab Folder** when a canvas grows. See [Tab Folders](/help/tabs/tab-folders/).

On a view-only link the bar still switches tabs, but there is no **+** button and no tab menu.

For the depth, see [Tabs](/help/tabs/): this bar is just the control surface for them.
