The **quick controls** sit at the right-hand end of the bottom bar, after the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/)'s tabs, and gather the actions you want reachable at any moment, whatever you are doing on the canvas. They stay put while the rest of the interface comes and goes with your selection.

## What lives here

From left to right:

- **Get Started**, first in the cluster, only while a guided tour is on offer for your mode or the selected element (Watch It Built for the mode, a Sankey tour on a selected Sankey Diagram, and so on). Press it to see the tours on offer, and pick one to start it. See [Watch It Built](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/).
- **Tools**: one menu for the tools that work on the whole canvas, grouped under **Find**, **Automate**, **Collaborate** and **Versioning**. **Search…** is always first: it opens the [search panel](/help/search-panel/) to jump to any canvas, tab, or element, to drop something onto the canvas, or to run a command (`⌘K`, or Ctrl+K, opens it from the keyboard too). The rest appear when they apply, such as **Slide Deck** (on a computer), **Walkthroughs**, **Webhooks**, **History** and, for an Editor, **Editing Mode**, which previews the canvas as a Participant or a Viewer would see it. Most tools open inside the menu, with a back bar to the list.
- **Settings**: the [Settings dialog](/help/user-interface/settings/): the preferences that follow you between devices, plus the **Keyboard** category with the full [shortcut reference](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) and the switch that turns shortcuts off.
- **Appearance**: click to step the [interface between Light, Dark, and System](/help/tools/dark-mode/) (System follows your device). It shows the setting in force as an icon, and hovering it names it. This is the app's own chrome, not the diagram's theme, which lives behind the **Theme & canvas** button in the bottom-right corner.

The full-page Explorer's bottom bar has its own **Search**, **Settings** and **Appearance**.

On a computer, Get Started, Tools and Settings show their names beside their icons (turn this off with **Button Labels** in Settings), and hovering a control shows what it does. On a phone the bar is tight, so the controls are icons only.

Because they are fixed in the corner rather than tied to a selection, the quick controls are the dependable starting point when you are not sure where an action lives: Search, in Tools, finds both things and commands.
