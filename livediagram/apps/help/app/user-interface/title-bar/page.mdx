There is no bar across the top of the editor: the canvas takes the whole height, and two cards float over its top corners. The **menu card** at the top left holds the logo, the menu button and the mode switch. The **actions card** at the top right holds **Present** when the canvas has a slide deck with a slide to show, **Share** or **Copy** and your account. The [palette](/help/palette/) sits between them.

On a phone the menu card joins the palette's row and the actions card shows its glyphs alone, without names; in a window too narrow for the palette and the actions card's names side by side, the actions card drops its names first. [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) and an [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) hide both cards.

## The logo

The logo at the left of the menu card opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) on the folder that holds this canvas. Its wordmark shows on a wide screen only.

## The menu button

The **≡** button beside the logo opens the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) under it, and pressing it again closes the panel. The current canvas is at the top of the panel: its name, a badge saying who can see it and its **⋯** menu. Click the name there to rename the canvas: `Enter` keeps the new name and `Escape` the old one.

A new canvas names itself: while it still has its starting name (such as "Untitled canvas", "Untitled Whiteboard" or "Untitled Workshop"), the first label you type on its first tab becomes its name, in every mode. A name you gave it yourself is never replaced.

## The mode switch

In the same card, right of the menu button, the **mode switch** names the tab's mode (**Diagram** or **Draw**) and opens a menu to change it for everyone on the tab. See [Editor modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/). It shows only to someone who may edit the tab, never on a locked tab or an event-storming board.

## Present

When the canvas's slide deck has a slide to show, **Present** sits before Share or Copy and starts the presentation. It is not offered on a phone.

## Share or Copy

The actions card holds one main action, and which one you get depends on whose canvas it is.

**Share** is for the owner, and for members of the team whose library holds the canvas. It opens the [Share dialog](/help/collaboration/sharing/), where you create and manage links. The button turns solid blue once the canvas has a live link, so you can tell at a glance that it is shared. On a [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) canvas it asks you to save the canvas to your account first, since there is nothing on a server to share yet.

**Copy** is for everyone else who has the canvas open: someone on a share link of any role. It makes a copy of the canvas that belongs to you:

<Step n={1}>Click **Copy**. It reads **Copying** while the copy is made.</Step>
<Step n={2}>The editor opens your copy, named "Copy of" and the original name.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Find it again later at the top level of My Work in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
</Step>

Your copy starts **Private**: no share links come with it, and nothing you do to it touches the original. If you opened the canvas through a link to a single tab, the copy holds just that tab. You do not need an account to copy; as a guest the copy is saved under your [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/).

Someone who opened a canvas from the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/finding-community-documents/) does not get **Copy** in the title bar. A slim bar under the title bar offers **Make a Copy** instead, which works the same way.

## Your account

At the end of the actions card is your account. Signed in, it shows your initial; click it for a menu with your name and email, **Account** (which opens [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) on your account details, without leaving the canvas) and **Sign Out**. Not signed in, it shows **Sign in** instead (see [Signing In](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/)).
