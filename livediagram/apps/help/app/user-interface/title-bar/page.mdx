## Top Left

- **The logo** opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) on the place that holds this canvas. On a smaller screen it shows without its wordmark.
- **The menu button** opens the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) under it, with your canvases and folders, and closes it again.
- **The editor mode switch** sits beside the menu button and shows the tab's [editor mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/) (Diagram, Draw or Workshop). Pick another mode from it. Only an Editor gets it, and not on a locked tab or an [Event Storming board](/help/canvas/event-storming-boards/).

On a phone the menu button and the mode switch (its icon alone) move into a card of their own at the left of the strip.

## Top Right

The card at the top right holds, from left to right:

- **Copy**, for anyone who cannot share this canvas (see below).
- **Present**, while the canvas's [slide deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/) has a slide to show. It starts the presentation. Not on a phone. See [Presenting](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/).
- **Share**, for those who can share the canvas (see below).
- **Your account.** Signed in, it shows your picture or initials and your first name; click it for a menu with your name and email, **Account** (which opens [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) on your account, without leaving the canvas) and **Sign Out**. Not signed in, it reads **Sign In** (see [Signing In](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/)).

When the strip needs the room, the card drops its words and keeps its icons. On a phone it shows icons alone.

## Share or Copy

**Share** is for the owner, and for members of the team whose library holds the canvas. It opens the [Share dialog](/help/collaboration/sharing/), where you create and manage links. The button turns solid once the canvas has a live link, so you can tell at a glance that it is shared. On an [Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) canvas it asks you to save the canvas to your account first, since there is nothing on a server to share yet.

**Copy** is for everyone else who has the canvas open: someone on a share link of any role. It makes a copy of the canvas that belongs to you:

<Step n={1}>Click **Copy**. It reads **Copying** while the copy is made.</Step>
<Step n={2}>The editor opens your copy, named after the original with "(copy)" at the end.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Find it again later at the top level of My Work in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
</Step>

Your copy starts **Private**: no share links come with it, and nothing you do to it touches the original. If you opened the canvas through a link to a single tab, the copy holds just that tab.

Someone who opened a canvas from the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/finding-community-documents/) does not get **Copy** here. A slim Community bar offers **Make a Copy** instead, which works the same way.

## Where the Canvas Name Went

The canvas's name is not shown above the canvas. The [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/) names each tab, and the canvas's own name shows in the Explorer and in your browser's tab. Rename a canvas from the Explorer.

All of these cards hide in [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/), and an [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) shows none of them.
