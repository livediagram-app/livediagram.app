A **Timeline** is a row of milestones along one line, read left to right toward its arrow. Each milestone is a dot on the line with **when** it happens and **what** it is, and they take turns above and below the line so even long names have room. It is the shape for a roadmap, a release plan, a project's phases or a company's history, and it lays itself out: add a milestone and the rest space out evenly.

## Adding one

<Step n={1}>Open the **Concepts** category on the palette strip.</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Pick **Timeline**, then tap the canvas to drop it, or drag to draw it at a size. It lands with
  four milestones to write over.
</Step>

## Writing the milestones

- **Select the timeline, then click a name** to type over it. **Enter** keeps it and **Escape** puts it back.
- **Click a when** (the small coloured line nearest the dot) to change it: a date, a quarter, a sprint. Clear it and the milestone shows its name alone.
- **Add a milestone** from any of the timeline's **+** buttons: **Add Milestone** puts a new one at the end, up to eight.
- **Reorder or remove them** in its menu: right-click the timeline, open **Tools**, then **Rows**. Each row has its name and its when, arrows to move it, and a cross to remove it (two always stay).

## Its look and its motion

The dots shade along the timeline from the theme's colour, the line and the whens are in that colour, and the names are in the tab's ink, so a new [theme](/help/canvas/themes/) restyles it at once. Under **Animation**, then **Concept**, **Build** brings the milestones in from left to right and **Pulse** lights each in turn.

With one selected, press Start Timeline Tour in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just left of **Settings**, for a short tour of it.

<Tip>A milestone with no when shows just its name, ready for you to add when it happens.</Tip>
