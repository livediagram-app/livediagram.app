An **Entity** is a title bar over a list of fields, each a name with an optional type and key. It is the box at the heart of an entity-relationship diagram and a UML class diagram alike, and the place a data model grows: entities add fields where they stand and join one another field to field, with the cardinality on each end.

## Take the tour

The first time you select an entity, a small **Show Me** toolbar appears beside its toolbar. Press **Play** for a short walkthrough on your own entity: its title, its fields and keys, editing them in place and in the Fields menu, and joining entities. Press the cross if you would rather not; either way it is not offered again. To be offered it again, open **Settings**, then **Tours**, open **Show Me** and switch **Entity** back on.

## Finding it

In Diagram mode, **Entity** is in the palette's **Diagrams** category, after the Table. In any mode you can also add it by name from the editor's [Search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/).

## Adding one and filling it in

<Step n={1}>
  Add an **Entity**. It arrives titled "Entity" with two example fields: `id`, the primary key, and
  `name`, both typed `string`.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>Double-click its title bar to rename it: the title is an ordinary element label.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Double-click a field to edit it where it stands: the name, then **Tab** to the type. **Enter**
  saves and moves to the next field, and past the last one it adds a new field, so you can type a
  whole table without reaching for the mouse. **Escape** keeps what you typed, and a field you empty
  is removed.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Press the small key at the start of the field you are editing to give it a key: **PK** (primary
  key), **FK** (foreign key), **PK FK** (both, for a join table) or **UK** (unique). Each key shows
  as a glyph beside the field.
</Step>

The box grows and shrinks with its fields, so a new field is never hidden below the bottom. You can also edit every field at once: right-click the entity and open **Tools**, then **Fields**, for a key button, a name, a type and a **×** per field, with **Add Field** at the foot.

An entity holds up to 40 fields, each name and type up to 80 characters. The type is optional: leave it blank for a rough sketch or an attribute list where the type does not matter.

## Growing a model

Select an entity and press the **+** on any side:

- **Add Related Entity** puts a new entity on that side, already joined to this one. It comes with its own `id` primary key and a foreign key named after this entity (`customer_id` beside "Customer"), and its title is ready to type.
- **Add Field**, on the bottom **+**, starts a new field at the end.

## Joining fields

Hover a field of the selected entity and a small dot appears on each side of it. Drag from a dot to a field of another entity: the line runs from field to field and stays on those fields as you move the entities. Dropping a line on an entity's title joins the whole entity instead.

A line joining two entities becomes a relationship as you let go, with crow's-foot ends: **one** at the table being pointed to, **many** at the end on a foreign key. Select it and press **Cardinality** in its toolbar to choose another pair: One to One, One to Many, Many to One, Many to Many, Zero or One to Many, or One to Zero or Many.

## Modelling with them

- **Data models.** One entity per table, one field per column, with its type and key, and a relationship from each foreign key to the table it points at. The **Database Schema** template is a worked example.
- **Class diagrams.** Put attributes and methods in the same list; a method reads naturally as `render(): void` in the name column.
- **Relationships** are ordinary [arrows](/help/palette/arrows/), so every arrow style applies; the arrowhead styles also include the hollow triangle (inheritance) and the filled and hollow diamonds (composition and aggregation) UML uses.

The title bar grows with the title's text size, so a larger title never collides with the divider under it.

<Tip>

</Tip>
