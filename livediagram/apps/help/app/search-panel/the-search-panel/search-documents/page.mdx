Whenever you open the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/) in a canvas, it covers your library too, so you never have to remember which folder a canvas is in. Matching is on the name only, and any part of it will do: `board` finds "Sprint board" and "Onboarding".

## Canvases

The **Canvases** group matches your own canvases by name (a canvas you never named is listed as "Untitled canvas"). Pick one and it opens, the quickest way back into a canvas without going to the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).

A canvas saved only in this browser (see [Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)) carries a **This Browser** label in the results, so you can tell it apart from one saved to your account.

## Shared and team canvases: Every App

Canvases someone shared with you, and canvases in your teams' libraries, are not in the Canvases group. Once you type, they appear in the **Every App** group, marked with livediagram's logo and name, alongside what you can open in the other liveworkspace apps. It lists only what you can open, and nothing in the Trash. Picking a shared canvas opens it on its share link, with whatever access the owner granted; picking a team canvas opens it like your own.

## Folders

The editor's search does not list folders. To find a folder, use **Search** on the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) page (or `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `K` there): its **Folders** group matches your folders and your teams' folders by name, and picking one opens it.

## Limits

The Canvases and Every App groups each list up to 8 matches. If what you want is not among them, type a little more of its name. With an empty box the panel lists your canvases (with your teams and, in a canvas, its tabs and elements), so you can open it and arrow straight down to one.

<Tip>
  Search only looks at names: it does not look inside other canvases, so to find a word on a canvas,
  open that canvas and search there. A guest finds the canvases their own browser owns; signing in
  brings your account's canvases into scope.
</Tip>
