When you are signed in and belong to one or more [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/), the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/) reaches into them as well, so team work is as findable as your own.

## The Teams group

The **Teams** group matches the teams you belong to by name, up to 8 of them. Picking one opens that team's page in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/team-spaces/). With an empty box the group lists your teams, so you can open search and arrow straight to one.

## Team canvases

A team's canvases are not in the Teams group, and not in **Canvases** either, which is for your own canvases. Once you type, they appear in the **Every App** group with livediagram's logo, alongside canvases shared with you and work in the other liveworkspace apps. Picking one opens the canvas. See [Finding Canvases and Folders](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-documents/).

## Team folders

The editor's search does not list folders. **Search** on the Explorer page does: its **Folders** group includes your teams' folders, each tagged **in &lt;team&gt;**, and picking one opens the team's page with that folder open.

## Signed-in only

Teams require an account, so the Teams group only appears when you are signed in with teams to search. Guests, and signed-in users with no teams, simply will not see it. The rest of search works the same either way.

<Tip>
  Searching is often faster than expanding the Teams tree in the Explorer: type a team's name and
  jump straight to its page.
</Tip>
