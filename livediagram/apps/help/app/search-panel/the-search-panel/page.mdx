The **search panel** is the fastest way to get anywhere in livediagram. It is one box, opened from inside a canvas, that searches your canvases and teams and that canvas's tabs and elements, and can act on the canvas too. (The [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) page has a search panel of its own, over your items, folders, teams, help and settings; this article is about the one in the editor.)

## Opening and closing search

- **Keyboard:** press `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `K`, or `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `.`. Both open the same panel. (`K` is the usual command-palette shortcut; `.` is the original binding, kept because browsers reserve `Cmd` + `T` for a new browser tab.) They work in [zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) and in view-only canvases. While you are typing in a text box they are left to that box, and they are off while a slide deck is running and when you turn off keyboard shortcuts in Settings.
- **Tools:** click **Tools** in the bottom bar, then **Search…**, its first row. On a phone the Tools button is its toolbox icon alone.
- **Explorer panel menu:** the Explorer panel's **⋯** menu also has a **Search** row.

The panel pops up near the top of the screen and blurs what is behind it so you can focus on the query. Press `Esc`, click the **Esc** chip at the end of the search box (a **×** on a touch screen), or click outside the panel to close it.

## What search covers

Type a few letters and matches appear, grouped by what they are. Matching is a simple, case-insensitive "contains" match on the name, so `flow` finds "Checkout flow" and "Workflow". Actions, settings and help articles also match on related words, and when you type several words each one only has to appear somewhere, so "export png" finds **Export…**.

- **Your [canvases](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-documents/)**, by name.
- **Your [teams](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-teams/)**, when you are signed in.
- **Every App:** once you type, what else you can open anywhere in liveworkspace: canvases shared with you, your teams' canvases, and items in the other liveworkspace apps. Each row carries its app's logo and name in the app's colour, and picking one opens it in its own app.
- **The [tabs and elements](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-tabs-and-elements/)** of the canvas you have open, including text inside table cells.

With an empty box, the panel lists what it can browse (your canvases, your teams, and the canvas's tabs and labelled elements), so you can open it and arrow straight to something. Most groups show at most 8 results (12 for Elements, 10 for Actions and Add to canvas, 6 for Settings and Help), so keep typing to narrow a long list.

The results come in this order: **Canvases**, **Teams**, **Every App**, **Tabs**, **Elements**, then **Actions**, **Add to canvas**, **Settings** and **Help**.

## Beyond navigation

In the editor the panel does more than jump around:

- **[Add to the canvas](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/):** search the palette and drop a shape or icon straight onto the canvas.
- **[Create a new tab](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-create-tab/):** an action result that spins up a fresh tab.
- **[Run any command](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/):** type an action's name (undo, auto layout, export, settings) and run it, or pick up a canvas tool. The panel doubles as a command palette on `⌘K`.

These three only appear once you have typed something. So do two more groups:

- **Jump to a setting:** a **Settings** group matches the rows of the [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) dialog, by name and by the words people use for them, so "dark mode" finds **Theme**. Each result says which category it lives in ("in Appearance"), and picking it opens Settings on that row with the row highlighted.
- **Find the help for it:** a **Help** group lists matching articles from this help centre, so the explanation of a feature is reachable from the same box as the feature. Articles match on their search synonyms too, so "transparency" finds Opacity. Picking one opens the article in a new browser tab, so you do not lose your place.

The Help group always sits last, so a help article never steals the `Enter` from somewhere you were trying to go.

## Picking a result

Results are ordered by group, and the very first match is preselected, so for the common case you can just type and press `Enter`. Otherwise click a row, or press the `↑` and `↓` arrow keys to move the highlight and `Enter` to pick it. Picking a result takes you exactly where it points: opening a canvas, switching to a tab, selecting an element, or opening a team's page in the Explorer. The panel closes as it goes.

## Read-only views

Search only navigates, so it works in a view-only canvas too: you can still find and jump to tabs and elements. **Add to canvas** results are not offered there, and the command palette keeps only the actions that do not change the canvas. See [The Command Palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/#in-read-only-views).
