Teams belong to your liveworkspace account, so a team is the same in every liveworkspace app, livediagram included. You run them on [liveworkspace.app](https://liveworkspace.app/account/teams); livediagram shows your teams, their members, their shared documents and the invitations waiting for you, and you can make a team from its Move dialog. Everything here needs a signed-in account.

## Admin and Member

The person who creates a team is its first **Admin**. On liveworkspace.app, Admins can:

- rename the team,
- invite people by email,
- change a member's role,
- remove a member or withdraw an invite,
- delete the team.

**Members** can see the team and its member list, work in the shared library, and leave the team.

A team always keeps an Admin: the last Admin can't step down or leave while others remain. Deleting a team gives its documents back to their owners (see [Team Shared Documents](/help/collaboration/teams/team-shared-documents/#when-a-team-is-deleted)).

In livediagram, open a team in the Explorer and choose **Manage on liveworkspace.app** at the top of its page to get there.

## Inviting by email

<Step n={1}>
  On [liveworkspace.app](https://liveworkspace.app/account/teams), open your team and enter the
  person's email address.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  They get an email from liveworkspace with a link to their teams page, where the invitation waits.
  They don't need an account yet: it waits until they sign up with that address. Signed in to
  livediagram, they also see it under **Invites** in the Explorer sidebar, with a count.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  They accept or decline on liveworkspace.app (**Answer on liveworkspace.app** in Invites goes
  there). Accepting takes a signed-in account whose verified email address is the one invited.
</Step>

Once they've joined, the team shows up in livediagram within a few minutes, usually at once.
