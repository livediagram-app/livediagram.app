A walkthrough is a video of you talking someone through the canvas. It films this browser tab as you see it, with your voice and, if you turn it on, your camera in a round bubble in a corner. It is saved with the canvas for everyone who can open it, and you can share it with anyone by a public link.

## Recording a Walkthrough

<Step n={1}>
  Open **Tools** in the footer, press **Walkthroughs**, then **Record Walkthrough**. **Record a
  Walkthrough** opens beside Tools.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick your **Microphone**. The **Camera** switch starts off, so a walkthrough records your voice
  alone and the browser asks only for the microphone; turn it on to pick a camera and show yourself
  in a round bubble, then pick its corner. The switch is remembered in this browser. If the browser
  hasn't been allowed to list your devices yet, press **Allow Microphone** (or **Allow Camera**) to
  ask it. **Mic Level** moves as you speak, so you know you are heard.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Press **Start Recording**. Your browser asks to share this tab: choose it. After a 3, 2, 1
  countdown, talk and move around the canvas as you would with someone beside you. Press **Finish**
  when you are done, or stop sharing from the browser's own bar.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  It saves straight away as "Walkthrough of" the tab you started on, and Tools opens at the
  Walkthroughs list with it at the top. **Rename** it from its ⋯ menu.
</Step>

A walkthrough can run for up to ten minutes; the time turns amber with "1 minute left" and the recording finishes on its own at ten. **Discard** in the recording bar throws it away, after asking. If your browser can't film its tab, or you don't share it, nothing is recorded and the set-up says so. If saving is refused (it is too large, the canvas already has 50 walkthroughs, or you have used all the walkthroughs you may keep), the card says why and offers **Try Again** and **Download a Copy**, so the recording isn't lost.

## Hiding Elements From the Recording

Before you start, press **Hide From Recording** in the set-up, then press each element viewers shouldn't see: a private note, an unfinished corner. Arrows attached to it go with it. **Hide Selection** hides what is already selected, and **Done** or `Esc` leaves the mode. While you set up you see hidden elements faded inside a dashed outline; while you record they are hidden from your screen too, so the video never shows them. They stay on the canvas itself for anyone who opens it. What is hidden can't be changed once recording starts.

## Watching a Walkthrough

Open **Tools** in the footer, press **Walkthroughs**, and press one to watch it. It plays in a video player over the editor, with its title, length, who made it and when, **Full Screen**, and the browser's own video controls.

Walkthroughs recorded before videos came in play over the canvas itself: the view follows the recording, with the presenter's pointer and bubble. Pan, zoom or press on the canvas and it pauses, leaving the view with you; **Resume** plays on. `Space` plays and pauses, `←` and `→` skip five seconds, and `Esc` closes. If the canvas has changed since, a line says so and it plays over the canvas as it is now.

## Sharing a Walkthrough by Link

Press **Share** in the player, or **Share** on a walkthrough's ⋯ menu, and turn on **Public Link**. **Copy Link** copies it. Anyone with the link can watch the video without signing in: they see its title and who made it, never the canvas. Turn **Public Link** off and the link stops working at once; turning it on again makes a new one. A row with a link on shows a link mark. Only video walkthroughs can be shared this way.

## Who Can Record and Manage

Editors record walkthroughs, and can rename, share and delete any of them. Everyone who can open the canvas can watch them, and anyone can rename, share or delete one they recorded. Walkthroughs aren't offered on a phone, in an embed, in a workbench, for a canvas kept in **This Browser**, or through a link to one tab. Every walkthrough you have recorded is also listed in the Explorer, under **Walkthroughs** in the Library.

## Privacy

Calls are never recorded. A walkthrough is only made when you press Start Recording and share the tab, and it is stored with the canvas for the people who can open it, or anyone you give its public link. Deleting a walkthrough, purging its canvas from the Trash or deleting your account deletes it. See the [privacy policy](https://www.liveworkspace.app/privacy).
