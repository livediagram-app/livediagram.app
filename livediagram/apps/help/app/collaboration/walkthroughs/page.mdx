A walkthrough is a recording of you talking someone through the canvas. It keeps your voice, your camera if you leave it on (shown as a round bubble in a corner), your pointer, and every pan, zoom, tab change and slide step you make. Whoever plays it watches it over the canvas itself, not in a video, so they can pause, look around and resume.

## Recording a walkthrough

<Step n={1}>
  Open **Tools** in the footer, press **Walkthroughs**, then **Record Walkthrough**. The recording
  dock opens at the bottom of the canvas.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick your **Microphone**. The **Camera** switch starts off, so a walkthrough records your voice
  alone and the browser asks only for the microphone; turn it on to pick a camera and show yourself
  in a round bubble. The dock remembers your choice in this browser. If the browser hasn't been
  allowed to list your devices yet, press **Allow Microphone** (or **Allow Camera**) to ask it. The
  level meter shows the microphone hears you.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Press **Start Recording**. After a 3, 2, 1 countdown, talk and move around the canvas as you would
  with someone beside you. Press **Finish** when you are done.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Give it a **Title** and press **Save**. It is listed under Walkthroughs in Tools for everyone who
  can open the canvas.
</Step>

A walkthrough can run for up to ten minutes; the bar turns amber with a minute left and finishes on its own at ten. If saving is refused (it is too large, or you have reached your limit), the dock says why and offers **Download a Copy** so the recording isn't lost.

## Hiding elements from the recording

Press **Hide From Recording** in the dock, then press each element viewers shouldn't see: a private note, an unfinished corner. **Hide Selection** hides what is already selected, and **Done** or `Esc` leaves the mode. You still see hidden elements, faded inside a dashed outline; people playing the walkthrough never do, and presses and pointer moves over them are left out of what is stored. They stay on the canvas itself for anyone who opens it.

## Playing a walkthrough

Open **Tools** in the footer, press **Walkthroughs**, and press one to play it. The view follows the recording, fitted to your screen, with the presenter's pointer and, in the corner, their camera bubble, or a small chip with their name when they recorded without the camera.

- **Pause, look around, resume.** Pan, zoom or press on the canvas and it pauses, leaving the view with you. **Resume** puts the view back where the recording is and plays on.
- **Keys.** `Space` plays and pauses, `←` and `→` skip five seconds, `Esc` closes. The bar also has a scrubber and a speed of 1x, 1.5x or 2x.
- **Edited since recording.** If the canvas has changed since, a line says so and it still plays over the canvas as it is now.

Recorded while you present the slide deck, a walkthrough replays the deck too: it starts the presentation at the slide you were on, moves through the slides as you did, and leaves it when you left it.

Closing puts you back where you were.

## Who can record and delete

Editors record walkthroughs and can delete any of them. Everyone who can open the canvas can play them, and anyone can delete one they recorded. Walkthroughs aren't offered on a phone, in an embed, for a canvas kept in **This Browser**, or through a link to one tab.

## Privacy

Calls are never recorded. A walkthrough is only made when you press Start Recording, and it is stored with the canvas for the people who can open it. Deleting a walkthrough, purging its canvas from the Trash or deleting your account deletes it. See the [privacy policy](https://www.liveworkspace.app/privacy).
