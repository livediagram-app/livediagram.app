The Explorer can show the same folders and items three ways: a grid of **cards**, each with a picture of the canvas, a compact **list** of rows, or a **details** table you can sort by any column. Recognising a canvas by its picture is often faster than reading near-identical names; the list packs more onto the screen when you know exactly what you're looking for.

## Switching views

Look for the three-button toggle at the top right of any page that lists items: My Work, a folder, a team's library, Recent, Favourites, Unsorted and search results. Click **List view** for rows, **Card view** for cards, or **Details view** for the table. The choice applies at once, to every one of those pages, and is remembered on this device.

Cards are what you get until you choose otherwise. On a phone the Explorer always shows cards and the toggle is hidden; the choice you made on a wider screen comes back when the window is wide enough again. [Shared With Me](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) and the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/) keep a fixed list of their own, so they have no toggle.

## What each view shows

- **Card** shows each item as a tile with a snapshot of its first tab, its name, a star if you favourited it, its visibility badge (**Shared** or **Team**) and when it was last updated, with its **⋯** menu. Folders appear as cards in the same grid, and a folder card **previews what is inside it**: a small mosaic of up to four tiles, the items it holds first, then its subfolders, with a **+N** tile when there is more. An empty folder keeps the plain folder glyph.
- **List** packs the same details into compact rows, each with the item's picture or icon, and its **⋯** menu at the end.
- **Details** lays everything out as a table, much like the details pane of a desktop file manager.

With **All Apps** chosen at the top of the page, each item also names the app it comes from.

## The details view

The table has a column for each of:

- **Name**: open an item or folder from it. Rest the pointer on a canvas's row for a moment and a **preview** of it appears beside it.
- **Type**: the kind of thing it is, such as **Canvas** or **Document**, or a folder.
- **Comments**: how many comments it holds.
- **Access**: what you may do with it.
- **Size**: how much it holds.
- **Created** and **Updated**: the date and time.

Press any column heading to sort by it; press it again to reverse. Folders always stay above items, and the sort is remembered on this device, like the view. A sort orders what has loaded so far. Narrow windows keep fewer columns.

You can drag any of your own or your team's canvases onto a folder, in any of the three views or in the sidebar, to move it there.

## Long lists

Lists load a page at a time as you scroll towards the end, so a large library opens quickly.
