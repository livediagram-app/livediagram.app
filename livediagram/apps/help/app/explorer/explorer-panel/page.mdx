The **Explorer panel** is the Explorer inside the editor. Press the **menu button** (☰) in the top-left corner of the canvas and it opens under the button; press the button again, or click anywhere outside, and it closes. It reads the same library as the [full-page Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), so you can browse your library, catch up on what happened and open another canvas without leaving the one you are on.

## What the panel shows

- **The current canvas** at the top: its name, its visibility badge (**Private**, **Shared**, **Team** or **This Browser**) and its **⋯** menu. **Click the name to rename it** in place: Enter saves, Escape keeps the old name.
- **The rail** down the left lists every section, grouped as on the Explorer page: **Overview** (Home, Inbox, Timeline, Shared With Me), **Spaces** (My Work and each of your teams) and **More** (Favourites, This Browser while this browser keeps any canvases, Library and Trash).
- **The pane** on the right shows the section you pick, a page at a time; **Show more** loads the next page. **Home** shows the canvases you were last in, and the **Inbox**, the **Timeline** (what happened, by day, with **Show more** for earlier days), the **Library** and the **Trash** open right there too. The Library holds your [Image Gallery](/help/explorer/image-gallery/) and your [Shape Libraries](/help/explorer/shape-libraries/), each on a tab of its own. **My Work** and each team list their folders and canvases; a folder opens right there in the pane, and picking a canvas opens it in the editor, while an item from another liveworkspace app opens on that app's site. The pane's header holds the section's title and **New Folder** (**New Subfolder** inside a folder), which makes a folder in My Work or the team you are looking at, with a team's **Members** beside it.
- The panel opens on **Home** and then remembers the section you last picked.
- **This Browser**, in More, shows how many canvases are saved only in this browser and opens their page ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)). It appears only while there is at least one.
- Along the foot are livediagram's other places: **Home**, **Explorer**, **Community** (when it is on) and **Help**.

It is the same data as the full page: a rename or a star shows in both at once. Each canvas has the same menu as on the Explorer page, from its **⋯** button or a right-click, so **Change Folder**, **Share**, **Add to Favourites**, **History** (the canvas's own Timeline) and the rest work here too. The panel's menu adds **Take Offline**, which moves a canvas into this browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)); **Sync Canvas** moves one back to your account. Drag one of your own canvases onto a folder in the pane, or onto **My Work** or a team in the rail, to file it there; rest it on a section in the rail for a moment and that section opens.

The **⋯** button in the panel's header, beside the help button, opens a menu with **New Canvas** and **Open Explorer** (the full-page library), then **Share** and **Export** for the canvas you are in, then **Search**, **GitHub** (the open-source code), **Licences** and **Settings**. With [power user mode](/help/user-interface/power-user-mode/)'s Minimal chrome on, the help button moves into this menu as **Help**.

## When you will see it

- **In the editor**, to jump to another canvas without going back to the library. [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) hides the menu button with the rest of the chrome.
- **On a phone**, the panel opens under the menu button across the screen: the sections become a row of chips over the pane.
- **When a canvas can't be opened**, it stays available beside the error screen, so you can pick another canvas.

Like everywhere else in the Explorer, the panel works whether or not you are signed in.
