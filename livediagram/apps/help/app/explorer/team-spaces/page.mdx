Each team you belong to is a space of its own in the Explorer sidebar: a row under **Spaces**, right after your own [My Work](/help/explorer/personal-space/). For how teams, roles and invites work, see the [Teams guide](/help/collaboration/teams/).

## Your teams

Each team you are a member of appears as a row with the team glyph and, once it has more than one member, how many it has. Click a team to open its library; expand it with the arrow to walk its folders without leaving the sidebar, and click a folder to open it.

A team's library holds the team's folders and items, from every liveworkspace app, and every member can manage them ([Team Shared Canvases](/help/collaboration/teams/team-shared-documents/)). At the top of the team's page sit how many have joined and its members, each with their role, and **Manage on liveworkspace.app**, where the team is run. On a team's library, **Create** offers **New Canvas** and **New Folder**, so what you make lands in the team.

The editor's Explorer panel (behind the menu button) lists your teams in its rail too, so you can reach team canvases without leaving the canvas you are in.

## Manage Teams

Teams belong to your liveworkspace account, not to livediagram, and are made, renamed, staffed and deleted on liveworkspace.app. **Manage Teams**, the last row of Spaces, takes you there. To make a team without leaving the Explorer page, use the **New Team** tile when you move a canvas with **Change Folder**: the canvas goes straight into it.

The teams the Explorer shows come from your sign-in: a team you make or join on liveworkspace.app appears here once livediagram next refreshes your sign-in, within the hour, when you load the Explorer again. Signing out and back in brings it at once.

## Invites

When someone invites you to a team, **Invites** appears in Spaces with the number waiting. Open it to see each invitation: the team, who invited you and when, and the role offered. You answer them on liveworkspace.app: each invitation links to your teams page there, where you accept or decline. Once you accept, the team shows up in Spaces.

## Signed-in only

Teams need an account, so team rows and Manage Teams appear only when you are signed in. Guests see a **Sign in to access Teams** card in their place ("Free, and your guest canvases come with you").
