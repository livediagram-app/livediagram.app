A **default folder** is where your new documents go when you create one without choosing a place. With none set, they land in My Work. Your default is your own: teammates choose theirs. The liveworkspace index keeps them for your account or guest identity, so they follow you to any browser, and it files each new document by them.

## Setting a default from a folder

Open a folder's menu (its **⋯** button, or right-click the folder) and choose **Use as Default for…**. Under **New canvases that open as**, tick **Canvases**, or a template family such as **Retrospective canvases**. The default points at one folder, so ticking it here moves it from wherever it was; unticking it sends new documents back to My Work.

**My Work** has the same menu: a ticked entry there means new documents land in My Work. Choosing it when unticked clears your default folder.

You can use a team's folders too, as long as you are a member of the team. A team's own top level cannot be a default: create inside the team to put a document there.

## Seeing your defaults

Your default folder shows a small mark after its name, in the sidebar, the Explorer's lists and cards, and the editor's Explorer panel. Hover it to read "Default folder for new canvases".

## When you create a document

**New Canvas** never asks where a document goes: it is filed in your default folder, or in My Work when you have none.

- Starting from inside a folder or a team keeps you there: that folder wins over your default.
- To file one document somewhere else, use **Change Folder** on it afterwards.
- A template picked on a new canvas's start screen counts too: pick a Retrospective or Kanban template on a canvas you just made, and the canvas goes to that family's default folder (or your Canvases default), with a note saying where. A canvas you made inside a folder stays there.
- As a guest it works the same way, and your folders and defaults move to your account when you sign in.

## In Settings

**Settings > Documents > Default Folders** shows the folder new documents go to, or My Work. **Change** picks a folder; **Clear** sends new documents back to My Work.

If your default folder has been deleted, or belongs to a team you have left, the row says so, for example "Drafts (deleted), using My Work", and new documents go to My Work. Deleting a folder that is one of your defaults warns you first.

<Tip>
  Defaults only decide where a new document starts. Moving a document later, or changing a default,
  never moves documents you already have.
</Tip>
