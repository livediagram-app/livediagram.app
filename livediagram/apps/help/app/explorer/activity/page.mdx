The **Inbox** sits under [Home](/help/explorer/home/) in the Explorer sidebar's Overview group. It lists what is still _open_ for you, in one place for every liveworkspace app: the [actions](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/) waiting on you, the ones you handed to someone else, and the [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) threads nobody has resolved yet.

## What lands here

The Inbox is one list, newest first, under **Today**, **Earlier This Week** and **Older**. Each row has a status that says why it is there:

- **To do:** an open action assigned to you, until it is done. An action you assigned to yourself lives here too, so the page doubles as your to-do list.
- **Waiting on** a teammate (for example **Waiting on Bo**): an open action you gave to someone else, so you can follow it up.
- An unresolved [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) thread you are part of: **Mentioned you** when someone [mentioned you](/help/collaboration/comments/#mentioning-a-teammate), **On your canvas** for a thread on your own canvas, **Replied to you** or **You replied** for a thread you wrote in, and **Your agent wrote** for a thread an AI tool working as you wrote in.

The filter menu at the top of the page, beside the app switch, narrows it to one kind, each with its count: **All**, **My Actions**, **Assigned by Me**, **Comments** and **Form Responses** (forms with new responses, from liveplanner). Your choice is remembered on this device. Done actions and resolved threads are not listed: the Inbox is a list of what is outstanding, not a history. When nothing is waiting, the page says **You’re all caught up**.

With **livediagram** chosen at the top of the page, the Inbox lists only what comes from your canvases; with **All Apps**, it lists every app's, and each row names its app.

## Reading and answering a row

Each row names the canvas it is from and the thing it is about, with the latest change, and on the right the person behind it with how long ago. Click a row and it opens in the **reading pane** beside the list: where it is (the canvas, the tab and the element), who wrote and why, a picture of the canvas, then the whole thread or the action's note.

From the pane you can act without leaving the Inbox:

- **Reply** to a thread, in the field under it, and **Resolve** it.
- **Mark Done** on an action.
- **Open in livediagram** takes you to the canvas at that spot, with the action or thread ready. A row from another app opens in that app.

On a phone, and in the editor's narrow [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/), the pane takes the list's place, with **Back** to return.

With the keyboard, **J** and **K** (or the arrow keys) move through the list and show each row in the pane, **Enter** opens it in its app, **R** jumps to the reply field and **E** resolves the thread or marks the action done.

## The badge

The Inbox row in the sidebar shows a count of what **waits on you**: the open actions assigned to you, and the comment threads that ask something of you (a mention, or a thread on your own item you haven't answered), so you can tell from any other page whether something needs you. Work you handed out and threads you replied in are listed but not counted.

## Canvases saved only in this browser

On livediagram's own Explorer page, and in the editor's [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/), the Inbox also lists the open actions and comment threads of canvases kept only Offline ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)), with everything else. Each of their rows carries an **Offline** pill, because the canvas is kept in this browser and nowhere else. Nobody else sees those rows, and another browser or device won't list them. Open those canvases to reply or mark them done: the reading pane can't change them. The Explorer on liveworkspace.app can't read this browser's storage, so it lists only what your account or guest identity holds.

## Guests and signing in

The Inbox works without an account, like the rest of livediagram. When you sign in, what you did as a guest moves to your account with your canvases.
