Watch It Built is the tour of [Diagram mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/). Instead of pointing at the controls one by one, a cursor called **Example** builds a small diagram in front of you: it drops two shapes from the palette, joins them with an arrow, names one, and gives it a colour from the element menu. Everything it uses is the editor's real chrome, so what you see is exactly what you will press. It takes about half a minute, and nothing it does is saved. (It was the stepped Diagram Tour until November 2026, and the Welcome Tour before October 2026.)

## Starting It

The tour never starts or pops up on its own. To watch it, press **Watch It Built** in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just left of **Search**. On a phone the button shows only its play icon.

The button appears on a **Diagram tab** you can edit, once any **Start Diagramming** panel is closed or a template picked. It doesn't appear on a view-only canvas, on a locked tab or in an embed. Draw and Workshop have tours of their own, on the same button: see [The Workshop Tour](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/workshop-tour/) and [The Draw Tour](/help/canvas/draw-mode/draw-tour/). When you have a complex element selected, such as a Sankey diagram or a flowchart step, the button offers that element's tour instead (Start Sankey Tour, for example).

Once you've watched it (to the end or not), it leaves the button, which then offers another tour you haven't taken. To watch it again, switch it back on in Settings (see below).

## What It Shows

Four short chapters, each with one line of explanation in the player at the bottom of the canvas:

1. **Add**: Example opens the palette's **Shapes** and drags a shape onto the canvas, then a second one.
2. **Connect**: it selects the first shape, so its handles and the **selection toolbar** show, then drags from the ring round it onto the second shape to join them with an arrow.
3. **Label**: it double-clicks the second shape and types its name, "Check stock".
4. **Style**: it right-clicks the shape to open the **element menu**, goes into **Style**, then **Presets**, and picks **Success**: the shape turns green.

On a touch screen the lines talk about taps, double-taps and long-presses instead of clicks.

## While It Plays

- **Your diagram is safe.** Your own elements fade back while Example works, and come back exactly as they were when it ends. Nothing Example draws is saved, sent to anyone you're working with, or added to undo.
- **The player** shows Example's line for the chapter, a **Pause** / **Play** button, the four chapters with a progress line each, and **Your Turn**. Press a chapter's name to watch it again from its start. At the end the player holds on the finished diagram, and its button reads **Replay**.
- **Keys**: **Space** pauses and plays, the **left** and **right arrow** keys step between chapters, and **Esc** ends it.
- **Your Turn** ends it and hands the canvas back to you. Switching to another tab or mode ends it too.
- **Reduced motion**: if your system or the editor's **Reduce motion** setting asks for less movement, nothing glides. Each chapter shows how it ends, and the play button reads **Next** to step to the next one (and **Done** on the last).

## Watching It Again

Once watched, it stays off the button until you switch it back on in Settings:

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Getting Started**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>Switch **Watch It Built** back on.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Close Settings. On a Diagram tab it starts straight away; on a tab in another mode, nothing happens,
  and the Watch It Built button is there when you get to a Diagram tab.
</Step>

Watching it switches **Watch It Built** off again, which takes it off the button until you switch it back on. The other modes have tours of their own, replayed the same way from **Draw** and **Workshop**: see [The Draw Tour](/help/canvas/draw-mode/draw-tour/) and [The Workshop Tour](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/workshop-tour/).
