Watch It Built is the tour of [Diagram mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/). Instead of pointing at the controls one by one, a cursor called **Example** shows you how something is done, on your own canvas: you pick a topic, and it builds, styles or tidies with the editor's real palette, toolbar, menus and dialogs, so what you see is exactly what you will press. Each topic takes 15 to 25 seconds, and nothing it does is saved. (Until November 2026 it was one replay, and before that a stepped Diagram Tour and the Welcome Tour.)

## Starting It

It never starts or pops up on its own. On a Diagram tab you can edit, press **Watch It Built** in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just left of **Search**. On a phone the button shows only its play icon. It doesn't appear on a view-only canvas, on a locked tab, in an embed, or for someone taking part rather than editing.

A press opens a small panel over the canvas: **What would you like to learn?** and the Diagram topics. Press one to watch it. A topic you've watched shows a **Done** tick, and you can press it to watch it again. **Not Now** (or **Esc**) closes the panel and leaves the button where it is.

The button stays until you've watched every Diagram topic. After that it offers another tour you haven't taken, and you can bring it back from Settings (see below).

## The Topics

| Topic             | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **The Basics**    | Drops two shapes from the palette, joins them with an arrow from the ring round the first, names one "Check stock", then right-clicks it and picks the **Success** colour from the element menu. |
| **Look and Feel** | Opens **Style** in the bottom bar, picks two looks for the whole tab in turn, then a new canvas background. Your tab keeps its own look when it ends.                                            |
| **Style a Shape** | Right-clicks a shape and goes through the element menu's **Style**: a thicker, dashed **Border**, a **Shadow**, then a handwritten **Font**.                                                     |
| **Find Anything** | Opens **Search**, types "database" and drags the result onto the canvas, then does the same for "decision".                                                                                      |
| **Tidy Up**       | Five joined steps land a little off, then **Tidy Up** in the bottom bar and **Auto Layout** glide them into a clean flow.                                                                        |

## While It Plays

- **Your work is safe.** Your own elements fade back while Example works, and come back exactly as they were when it ends. Nothing Example draws or changes is saved, sent to anyone you're working with, or added to undo. A look or layout it shows is never applied to your tab.
- **Panels it opens close again.** Example opens the editor's real menus and panels with their own buttons, and closes each one as it moves on.
- **The player** shows Example's line for the chapter, a **Pause** / **Play** button, the topic's chapters with a progress line each, and **Your Turn**. Press a chapter's name to watch it again from its start.
- **Keys**: **Space** pauses and plays, the **left** and **right arrow** keys step between chapters, and **Esc** ends it.
- **Your Turn** ends it and hands the canvas back to you. When there are more topics, **More Topics** beside it opens the list again. Switching to another tab or mode ends it too.
- **Reduced motion**: if your system or the editor's **Reduce motion** setting asks for less movement, nothing glides. Each chapter shows how it ends, and the play button reads **Next** to step to the next one (and **Done** on the last).
- **On a phone** Example is a soft touch dot instead of an arrow, and the lines talk about taps and long-presses instead of clicks.

## Watching Again

Every topic stays in the panel with its **Done** tick, so as long as the button is there you can watch any of them again. Once you've watched them all, bring the button back from Settings:

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Tours**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Open **Watch It Built in Diagram** and switch **Watch It Built** back on to offer every Diagram
  topic again, or switch on just the topics you want under it.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Close Settings. In Diagram mode the topic list opens straight away; otherwise the Watch It Built
  button is there the next time you work in Diagram.
</Step>

Draw and Workshop have topics of their own, on the same button: see [Watch It Built in Draw](/help/canvas/draw-mode/draw-tour/) and [Watch It Built in Workshop](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/workshop-tour/). When you have a complex element selected, such as a Sankey diagram or a flowchart step, the button offers that element's tour instead (Start Sankey Tour, for example).
