The Diagram tour is a guided, interactive introduction to [Diagram mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/): building a diagram from shapes, arrows and the palette. Rather than showing screenshots, it opens the real panels and menus one at a time, spotlighting each with a highlight ring and a short explanation. It takes about a minute, and you can leave at any point. (It was called the Welcome Tour until October 2026.)

## When It's Offered

When you create your first canvas from the [New Canvas wizard](/help/getting-started/your-first-diagram/), a **Getting started with Diagrams?** banner appears at the bottom of the screen once you have picked a template or closed **Start Diagramming**: **Yes** starts the tour, **No** dismisses it. (A canvas opened through **Start Blank**, a share link or an embed does not offer it, and nor does a view-only canvas.)

The tour only appears on a **Diagram tab**. If your first canvas opens in Draw or Workshop, the banner waits until you work on a tab in Diagram mode. Those modes have tours of their own: see [The Workshop Tour](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/workshop-tour/) and [The Draw Tour](/help/canvas/draw-mode/draw-tour/). Switching the tab to another mode partway through ends the tour.

If a dialog is open when the banner is due (Share, Settings, Search, any dialog), the banner waits and appears once you have closed it. The same goes for every other tour.

The offer appears **once ever**. However you answer (decline, skip midway, or finish), it won't come back on its own; the answer is saved with your other settings, so if you're signed in it follows you to every device. You can always [replay it from Settings](#replaying-the-tour).

## What It Covers

The tour walks the diagramming chrome in a natural order:

1. **The Palette**: the strip across the top that every shape, arrow, and icon comes from.
2. **Selection Modes**: the dropdown that changes what your pointer does (Select, Hand, Eraser, and friends). See [Selection Modes](/help/selection-modes/).
3. **Palette Categories**: the Palette's categories along the strip, with the first one, Shapes, open to show its elements in their groups.
4. **The Element Menu**: the right-click menu (long-press on touch) where styling, links, notes, comments, and actions live. On an empty tab the tour adds a square to show it on, and takes it away again when the tour ends.
5. **Tabs**: one canvas holds many tabs, each with its own work in its own mode, plus the **+** button and the tab menu (rename, duplicate, lock, export, side by side).
6. **Theme & Canvas**: the button in the bottom-right corner, drawn as a painter's palette, that restyles the whole tab.

A closing **You're ready to diagram** card then offers **Help** or **Start**.

On a phone the tour is shorter: it skips the Theme & Canvas step. On an [Event Storming board](/help/canvas/event-storming-boards/) it skips Selection Modes and Palette Categories, since the board offers neither.

## During the Tour

- Each step shows a **step counter** ("2 of 6") with progress dots, a title, a sentence or two of copy, and a **Next** button (with **Back** from the second step on), with **Skip tour** always available.
- The highlight ring **never blocks your pointer**: you're free to poke at whatever is highlighted while the step is up. The tour opens real menus, so what you see is exactly what you'll use afterwards.
- Opening a dialog partway through (Share, say) hides the tour while the dialog is up. Close it and the tour comes back on the same step. A dialog the tour opens itself to show you something stays part of the tour.
- Finishing (or skipping) closes anything the tour opened and returns you to a quiet editor, ready to work.

## Replaying the Tour

You can rerun the tour whenever you like:

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Tours**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>Switch **Diagram Tour** back on.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Close Settings. On a Diagram tab the tour relaunches from the welcome banner, exactly like the
  first run; on a tab in another mode, it waits until you're on a Diagram tab.
</Step>

Finishing (or skipping) the rerun switches **Diagram** off again. The other modes have tours of their own, replayed the same way from **Draw** and **Workshop**: see [The Draw Tour](/help/canvas/draw-mode/draw-tour/) and [The Workshop Tour](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/workshop-tour/).
