In [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/), Watch It Built shows you the dock by using it. A cursor called **Example** picks up a marker, draws, swaps colours and rubs things out on your own canvas, with the dock's real buttons, so what you see is exactly what you will press. Each topic takes 10 to 20 seconds, and nothing it does is saved. (It replaced the stepped Draw Tour in November 2026.)

## Starting It

It never starts or pops up on its own. On a Draw tab you can edit, press **Watch It Built** in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just left of **Search**. On a phone the button shows only its play icon. It doesn't appear on a view-only canvas, on a locked tab, in an embed, or for someone taking part rather than editing.

A press opens a small panel over the canvas: **What would you like to learn?** and the Draw topics. Press one to watch it. A topic you've watched shows a **Done** tick, and you can press it to watch it again. **Not Now** (or **Esc**) closes the panel and leaves the button where it is.

The button stays until you've watched every Draw topic. After that it offers another tour you haven't taken, and you can bring it back from Settings (see below).

## The Topics

| Topic                   | What it shows                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sketch**              | Picks up the first marker on the dock and draws a wavy line and a little house.                                                                             |
| **Shape Recognition**   | Turns on **Shape recognition** under the dock's settings cog, then draws a wobbly circle and a rough box and holds still, so each snaps into a clean shape. |
| **Pens and Colours**    | Presses a marker twice to open its colour and width, picks green and thick, and draws with it.                                                              |
| **Shapes and Stickies** | Opens the dock's **Shapes**, searches for a rectangle and drags one out, then adds a sticky note and types on it.                                           |
| **Erase and Board**     | Rubs out one of three strokes with the **Eraser**, then changes the board behind them to dots and then a grid.                                              |

## While It Plays

- **Your work is safe.** Your own elements fade back while Example works, and come back exactly as they were when it ends. Nothing Example draws or changes is saved, sent to anyone you're working with, or added to undo. The marker in your hand, its colour and width, Shape recognition and your board background are exactly as they were when it ends.
- **Panels it opens close again.** Example opens the editor's real menus and panels with their own buttons, and closes each one as it moves on.
- **The player** shows Example's line for the chapter, a **Pause** / **Play** button, the topic's chapters with a progress line each, and **Your Turn**. Press a chapter's name to watch it again from its start.
- **Keys**: **Space** pauses and plays, the **left** and **right arrow** keys step between chapters, and **Esc** ends it.
- **Your Turn** ends it and hands the canvas back to you. When there are more topics, **More Topics** beside it opens the list again. Switching to another tab or mode ends it too.
- **Reduced motion**: if your system or the editor's **Reduce motion** setting asks for less movement, nothing glides. Each chapter shows how it ends, and the play button reads **Next** to step to the next one (and **Done** on the last).
- **On a phone** Example is a soft touch dot instead of an arrow, and the lines talk about taps and long-presses instead of clicks.

## Watching Again

Every topic stays in the panel with its **Done** tick, so as long as the button is there you can watch any of them again. Once you've watched them all, bring the button back from Settings:

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Tours**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Under **Offer Again**, switch **Watch It Built in Draw** back on to offer every Draw topic again,
  or switch on just the topics you want under it.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Close Settings. In Draw mode the topic list opens straight away; otherwise the Watch It Built
  button is there the next time you work in Draw.
</Step>

Diagram and Workshop have topics of their own, on the same button: see [Watch It Built](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/) and [Watch It Built in Workshop](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/workshop-tour/).
