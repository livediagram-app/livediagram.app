The Draw tour shows you where everything for sketching lives. Like the [Diagram Tour](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/), it works on the real editor rather than screenshots: it spotlights each part of the dock with a highlight ring and a short explanation. It adds nothing to your canvas, takes under a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## Starting the Tour

The tour never starts or pops up on its own. When you're in [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/) on a tab you can edit, press Start Draw Tour in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just left of **Search**. On a phone the button shows only its play icon.

The button doesn't appear on a locked tab, in an embed, or for someone who can only view or take part. It stays there after you've taken the tour, so you can press it again whenever you like.

## What It Covers

1. **Pens and Tools**: the dock's first group, with **Select**, the three markers, **Text**, the **Path tool** and the **Eraser**. Press the marker in your hand again to change its colour and width.
2. **Quick Style**: the panel with colour and width for the marker in your hand, or for whatever you've selected, so you can restyle a stroke, shape or text after drawing it. If nothing is in hand, the tour picks up a marker to show it and puts it down again after. Not on a phone.
3. **Shapes**: the sticky note and shapes pinned to the dock, and **Shapes**, which opens every shape and a search. You can pin your favourites to the dock.
4. **Board Settings**: the cog, with the board's background, your cursor, and whether rough shapes you draw snap into clean ones.

Each step has **Next**, **Back** and **Skip tour**. The last card, **You're ready to draw**, links to the [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/) article and ends the tour with **Start**. If you leave Draw mode or switch tab mid-tour, it ends as skipped.
