In [Workshop mode](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/), Watch It Built shows you how to run a session by running one. A cursor called **Example** drags out votes, polls and stickies, and starts a timer and a dot vote, on your own canvas with the editor's real palette and Session strip, so what you see is exactly what you will press. Each topic takes 15 to 25 seconds, and nothing it does is saved. (It replaced the stepped Workshop Tour in November 2026.)

## Starting It

It never starts or pops up on its own. On a Workshop tab you can edit, press **Watch It Built** in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just left of **Search**. On a phone the button shows only its play icon. It doesn't appear on a view-only canvas, on a locked tab, in an embed, or for someone taking part rather than editing.

A press opens a small panel over the canvas: **What would you like to learn?** and the Workshop topics. Press one to watch it. A topic you've watched shows a **Done** tick, and you can press it to watch it again. **Not Now** (or **Esc**) closes the panel and leaves the button where it is.

The button stays until you've watched every Workshop topic. After that it offers another tour you haven't taken, and you can bring it back from Settings (see below).

## The Topics

| Topic                     | What it shows                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ask the Room**          | Opens the palette's **Ask** and drags out a **Dot Vote**, a **Poll** and a **Temperature Check**.                                                  |
| **Stickies and Stickers** | Drags out three stickies and types an idea on each, then adds a thumbs up and a heart from **Stickers**.                                           |
| **Reactions and Reveals** | Drags a **Confetti** reaction pad from **Session**, then draws a **Reveal Zone** over two answers so they hide under its cover.                    |
| **Run the Room**          | Starts a timer, which counts down on the board and the Session strip, runs a dot vote on the notes, then ends it so the most-voted notes light up. |

## While It Plays

- **Your work is safe.** Your own elements fade back while Example works, and come back exactly as they were when it ends. Nothing Example draws or changes is saved, sent to anyone you're working with, or added to undo. A timer or vote it shows is never started: nobody else in the room sees anything.
- **Panels it opens close again.** Example opens the editor's real menus and panels with their own buttons, and closes each one as it moves on.
- **The player** shows Example's line for the chapter, a **Pause** / **Play** button, the topic's chapters with a progress line each, and **Your Turn**. Press a chapter's name to watch it again from its start.
- **Keys**: **Space** pauses and plays, the **left** and **right arrow** keys step between chapters, and **Esc** ends it.
- **Your Turn** ends it and hands the canvas back to you. When there are more topics, **More Topics** beside it opens the list again. Switching to another tab or mode ends it too.
- **Reduced motion**: if your system or the editor's **Reduce motion** setting asks for less movement, nothing glides. Each chapter shows how it ends, and the play button reads **Next** to step to the next one (and **Done** on the last).
- **On a phone** Example is a soft touch dot instead of an arrow, and the lines talk about taps and long-presses instead of clicks.

## Watching Again

Every topic stays in the panel with its **Done** tick, so as long as the button is there you can watch any of them again. Once you've watched them all, bring the button back from Settings:

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Tours**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Open **Watch It Built in Workshop** and switch **Watch It Built in Workshop** back on to offer
  every Workshop topic again, or switch on just the topics you want under it.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Close Settings. In Workshop mode the topic list opens straight away; otherwise the Watch It Built
  button is there the next time you work in Workshop.
</Step>

Diagram and Draw have topics of their own, on the same button: see [Watch It Built](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/) and [Watch It Built in Draw](/help/canvas/draw-mode/draw-tour/).
