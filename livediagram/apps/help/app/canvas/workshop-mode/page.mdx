**Workshop mode** is a way of working on a tab, made for running a session with your team: a retrospective, a town hall, a lean coffee, a planning workshop or a quick icebreaker. It puts the tools that run a room in front of you (timers, dot votes, polls, reveals, quizzes, stickers) and keeps them out of the way when you are drawing a diagram.

Workshop is one of the three [editor modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/), beside Diagram and Draw, and it is on for everyone: there is nothing to turn on in Settings. It used to be called **Facilitate mode**.

## What Workshop Mode Changes

- **The palette** offers Shapes, **Write** (text, notes, tables and checklists), **Draw** (the pens and arrows) and **Layout** (frames, lanes, timelines, cards and badges), then the session kit: **Ask** (votes, polls, quizzes, estimates and the Q&A board) and **Session**, which holds **Tools**, **Record**, **React**, **Selection Mode** and **Navigate** down its side, from the timer and reveal to the agenda, the reactions and the places Avatars walk to. Session's search looks through all of them. After them come Icons, **Stickers**, Media and **Data**. Write and Draw keep what a session uses, leaving out Page, Annotation, Shape Pen, Polygon, Mind node, Flowchart and Sankey; search still adds any of them. See [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/).
- **The bottom-right corner** gains the **Timer**, **Vote** and **Poll** buttons, right after Undo and Redo. See [Session Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).
- **Everything else works as in Diagram mode:** selection, arrows, text, sticky notes, snapping, undo and shortcuts.

## What Is Only in Workshop

Diagram mode is for diagrams, so the session kit lives in Workshop:

- **The Collaborate elements** and **Stickers** are in Workshop's palette, not Diagram's. Diagram has the **Comment panel** and the **Action card** in its Write category, since comments and actions belong to every mode.
- **The Timer, Vote and Poll buttons** sit in Workshop's bottom-right corner only.
- **Session elements work in every mode.** On any tab, a session button starts its timer, a reveal zone reveals and a quiz takes answers.
- **The element search finds them everywhere.** Search for "timer" or a sticker from Diagram mode and it is there, under **Elements From Other Modes**, and it is placed without switching mode. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/).

## Switching Modes

- On an empty tab's start, press the mode switch at the top of its list and choose **Workshop**, or press **Shift+D** until you reach it. A tab with something on it keeps its mode, so add a new tab for the session. See [Editor Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/#choosing-a-mode).
- **One mode for everyone:** the mode belongs to the tab, so when someone who can edit switches it, everyone on the tab moves to Workshop together, and **Undo** switches it back.
- **Participants and viewers** have no mode switch: they see the tab in its mode. Participants take part in everything that is running (they vote, answer polls and add sticky notes); viewers watch.

## Starting a Session

- **A new canvas:** in the New Canvas wizard, choose **Workshop**. The empty tab opens on **Start a Workshop**: the session templates grouped as **Retrospectives**, **Meetings**, **Spaces**, **Quizzes & Games**, **Ideation**, **Planning**, **Strategy** and **Decisions**, with **Recommended** first. **Blank Workshop** (or the **×**) leaves one empty tab in Workshop mode.
- **Session templates open in Workshop:** the retrospectives (Retrospective, Start, Stop, Continue, Mad, Sad, Glad, 4Ls and Sailboat), Town Hall Q&A, Lean Coffee, Crazy Eights, Meeting Agenda, the Workshop Board and the Brainstorm Board, the boards that come with a dot vote (Affinity Map, Prioritization Matrix and Risk Matrix), so the vote is there to run from the Session strip, the boards a team fills in together in a session (Incident Postmortem, Objectives Planner, Roadmap, RACI Matrix, User Story Map, Business Model Canvas, Stakeholder Map, Value Proposition Canvas, Opportunity Solution Tree and User Persona), and the sticky-note boards a group fills in together, such as SWOT, Empathy Map and User Journey. So do the quizzes and games: a Pub Quiz of timed [quiz](/help/palette/collaborate/quizzes/) rounds with a scoreboard, Icebreaker Questions spun on [pickers](/help/palette/behaviour/pickers/), Two Truths and a Lie and Would You Rather on live polls, and Find Someone Who Bingo. So do the spaces to walk round together in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/): the Virtual Office, a Coworking Space, a Team Pod and an All-Hands Hall.
- **Any tab:** switch it to Workshop when the session starts, and back to Diagram when you are done. Everything on it stays where it is.

<Tip>
  Share a Participant pass with everyone in the session, so they can vote, answer polls and add
  sticky notes, and hand the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) baton to whoever is
  running it, so only they start and end the tools.
</Tip>

## Take the Tour

Press Start Workshop Tour in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just left of **Settings**, for a short [Workshop tour](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/workshop-tour/): the Collaborate categories, the Session strip and Share. It never starts on its own, and you can take it again whenever you like.
