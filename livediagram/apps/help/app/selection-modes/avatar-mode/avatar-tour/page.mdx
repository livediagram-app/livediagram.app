The Avatar tour shows you everything your character can do. It works on the real editor rather than screenshots: it spotlights each part with a highlight ring and a short explanation, opening the Avatar panel to show it and closing it again after. It changes nothing on your canvas or your character, takes under a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## Starting the Tour

The tour never starts or pops up on its own. While you're in [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/), press Start Avatar Tour in the bar along the bottom of the editor, just left of **Search**. On a phone the button shows only its play icon.

The button is there whatever your role, including a view-only link, since Avatar mode is yours too. It isn't there in an embed. It stays after you've taken the tour, so you can press it again whenever you like.

## What It Covers

1. **Your Avatar**: the **Avatar** button next to Undo and Redo, where your character sheet lives.
2. **Make It Yours**: gender, clothing, hair and size, the dice for a whole new look, and right-clicking your character to swap gender.
3. **Behaviour and Reactions**: jumping jacks, a wave, a spin, a cheer or a dance, and bursts like confetti and hearts.
4. **Say Something**: pressing **Enter** to type in a bubble above your head, or **Send a Message** in the panel. Not shown on a view-only link, where you can't speak.
5. **Give Someone a Push**: clicking someone else's character to walk over and push them, and walking with the arrow keys and hopping with Space.

Each step has **Next**, **Back** and **Skip tour**. The last card, **You're Ready to Roam**, links to the [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) article and ends the tour with **Start**. If you leave Avatar mode or switch tab mid-tour, it ends as skipped.

## Replaying the Tour

Press Start Avatar Tour again, or start it from Settings:
