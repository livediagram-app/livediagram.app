livepages is multiplayer from the first page. Sharing is just creating a link and handing it over: whoever opens it joins the same document, sees live cursors, and edits alongside you. The person you share with does not need an account. For the full details see [Sharing](/help/collaboration/sharing/).

## Create a share link

<Step n={1}>
  Open the document you want to share and click **Share** at the top right of the editor, or choose
  **Share** from the document's **⋯** menu in the Explorer. Only the document's owner can issue
  passes, so someone who joined through a link sees no **Share** button.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Under **Issue a pass**, click the role card to choose a role: **Editor** (draws with you in real
  time, the default), **Participant** (adds stickies, text and pictures, but can't reshape the
  pages) or **Viewer** (watches, pans and zooms; can't comment or change a thing). **Valid** sets
  how long it lasts: Forever, 1 week, 1 month or 6 months. In a document with several tabs,
  **Opens** can limit the link to one tab.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Click **Create Pass**. The link is copied for you straight away, ready to send to whoever you want
  in the document; each pass in the list also has its own copy button.
</Step>

If you are a guest, check the name under **Sharing as** at the foot of the dialog first (or press the
shuffle button beside it for another): that is the name peers see on your cursor and comments. A document
saved to **This Browser** has to be synced to your account before it can be shared.

## What happens when they open it

Someone opening your link for the first time enters the password if you set one, picks the name others will see (a signed-in visitor keeps their account name), and then lands in the document:

<Figure caption="Everyone in a shared document sees the others' live cursors and a presence stack of who is here.">
  <LiveCursors />
</Figure>

- **Live cursors and selections.** Everyone sees where everyone else is and what they have selected.
- **Simultaneous editing.** On an Editor pass, you both change the document at once. To avoid clashes, an element one person has selected is gently locked for the others while they hold it.
- **No sign-in required.** A guest can edit straight away, the editor never blocks them with a sign-in wall. See [Guest vs account](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## Managing and ending access

Back in the Share dialog you can manage links over time:

- **Multiple passes.** Issue several, with different roles, for example a Viewer pass for stakeholders and an Editor pass for collaborators. They are listed under **Passes**.
- **Revoke** a pass to cut off access through it. Expiring passes show a countdown, and expired ones move under **Expired**, where they can be extended or deleted. See [Share Link Expiry](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-link-expiry/).
- **Password Protection.** Protect every pass with one shared password; everyone opening a link must enter it first, embeds included. See [Share Passwords](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/).

<Tip>
  Want to put the document on a web page or in a doc? Each pass can also be copied as an embed
  (**Copy iframe**) or, while no password is set, as a live image that stays up to date. A Viewer or
  Participant pass embeds look-only; an Editor pass embeds an editable canvas. See
  [Embeds](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/).
</Tip>
