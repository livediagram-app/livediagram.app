A **shape library** is a named set of shapes you reuse: a UML preset, your team's icons, a notation of your own. Each library is yours, kept with your documents, and its shapes are ready to place on any tab from the palette's **My shapes** category.

## Making a library

Shape libraries are made with the [livepages API](/help/developers/working-with-documents/plan-sheets-images/).

You can have up to 100 shape libraries, each holding up to 1,000 shapes. A library must also fit in the same space as one tab, just under 2 MB. You can't make a library from shapes on the canvas yet.

## Placing a shape

Open **Library** on the palette strip, which sits right after Shapes, to see your libraries. It appears once you have a library with at least one shape. Each library has its own section, newest first, and each shape shows a small preview with its title (or "Shape 1", "Shape 2" when it has none). The **Search my shapes** box at the top finds shapes by their title or by the library's name.

- **Click a shape** to place it in the middle of the view, or **drag it** to where you want it. Placing a shape closes More; open it again to place the next, which lands beside the last instead of on top of it, so a run of placements lines up left to right.
- It arrives selected, ready to move, and one undo takes it back.
- What you place is ordinary shapes and connections: edit them like anything else.

With the keyboard, Tab to a shape and press Enter or Space to place it in the middle of the view.

My shapes isn't offered on a view-only tab, in Plan mode, or in [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/), whose dock replaces the palette. The palette loads your libraries when the editor opens, so after adding or changing a library, reopen the document to see the change.

## Managing your libraries

The Explorer's **Shape Libraries** page, under **Library** in the sidebar's More group, lists your libraries, newest first, with how many shapes each holds and a preview of the first eight.

  <ShapeLibraryCards />
</Figure>

- **Rename** a library in place: type the new name and press Enter, or Escape (or click away) to keep the old one. Names must be unique among your libraries, up to 120 characters.
- **Delete** a library after confirming. Its shapes leave My shapes; documents where you placed them keep them.
- **Show shapes** lists every shape in a library, each with its own **Delete**. Deleting a single shape takes effect straight away, without a confirmation; deleting the last shape leaves an empty library until you delete that too.

Deleting a library or a shape never changes a document: what you placed is ordinary elements.

<Tip>
  Shape libraries belong to you, signed in or not. If you start as a guest and later sign up, your
  libraries come with you.
</Tip>
