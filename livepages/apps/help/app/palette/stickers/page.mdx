The **Stickers** category, on the palette strip after Icons, holds around 190 colour emoji and 32 word badges you can drop straight onto the canvas. It is on the palette of every kind of page except a logo page, where the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) still finds and places them. They are the informal half of a canvas: a thumbs up on somebody's idea, a **BLOCKED** badge on the step that is stuck, an arrow pointing at the thing everyone should be looking at, or a few flowers in the corner because a page should not look like a spreadsheet.

Every sticker lands the way a real one would: on a **die-cut white plate** with a soft shadow, so it reads as something stuck on top of the canvas rather than drawn into it. It arrives straight; tilt it yourself from the **Rotation** section of its right-click menu if you want the scrapbook look.

## A sticker is not an icon

Stickers and [Icons](/help/palette/icons/) look like near neighbours in the palette and behave nothing alike:

- A sticker **keeps its own colours whatever the theme**. A thumbs up stays yellow, an APPROVED badge stays green, however you restyle the diagram. A line-art icon takes your theme's colour.
- A sticker **takes no text**. There is no caption under it and no editor when you double-click, because it says what it says in its own artwork. An icon can carry a caption.
- A sticker is **always its own element**. Dragging one onto a shape drops a sticker on top; it never folds into that shape's label the way an icon does.

## The eleven groups

Its panel lists the groups down the left, each with its count; click one to see everything it holds on the right. It reopens on the group you last had open.

- **Badges**: the word pills, coloured by what they mean: APPROVED, DONE, SHIPPED, BLOCKED, REJECTED, URGENT, AT RISK, WIP, ON HOLD, IN REVIEW, NEEDS REVIEW, P0, P1, OWNER?, DISCUSS, OPEN QUESTION, NEW, IDEA, MUST HAVE, MVP, NICE TO HAVE, QUICK WIN, TO DO, DRAFT, PARKED, LATER, OUT OF SCOPE, and more.
- **Reactions**: responding to somebody else's work: thumbs up and down, clapping, raised hands, a heart, fire, 💯, eyes.
- **Feelings**: the face set, from delighted to confused to completely done in.
- **Status**: check, cross, warning, red / amber / green dots, blocked, under construction, new, coming soon, trends up and down.
- **Direction**: arrows in every direction, pointing hands, map pins, a compass, play, and a chequered flag for the finish.
- **Celebrate**: party popper, confetti, trophy, medals, cake, gift, crown, rocket.
- **Decorate**: sparkles, rainbow, sun, snowflake, flowers, hearts in every colour, gems.
- **Meeting**: timers, calendar, speech and thought bubbles, megaphone, microphone, ballot box, brain, coffee.
- **Work**: bug, wrench, gear, key, folder, package, laptop, money bag, target.
- **People**: a person, a group, someone raising a hand, a worker, a detective.
- **Fun**: robot, alien, unicorn, cat, pizza, dice, a game controller.

## Finding one

Search by what you mean, not by the emoji's official name. The catalogue matches on synonyms, so "celebrate" finds the party popper, "wip" finds both the WIP badge and the roadworks, and "thanks" finds the folded hands. A badge also answers to the word printed on it. Search always runs across **every** group, so you never have to guess where something was filed.

Stickers turn up in the editor's other searches too: the [Search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) can add one to the canvas, and so can the palette strip's [Search](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/#search).

## Placing one

<Step n={1}>Rest on **Stickers** on the palette strip, or click it.</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick a group from the list down the side, or type in the search box to look across all eleven at
  once. Stickers reopens on the group you last had open.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Click a sticker to add it to the canvas, or drag it out and drop it where you want it.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Drop a **BLOCKED** or **AT RISK** badge over the step it applies to. A sticker always sits on top
  as its own element, so you can move it away again without touching what is underneath.
</Step>

A sticker behaves like any other element once it is on the canvas: move it, resize it, rotate it, and it exports with the rest of the canvas. What it will not do is take text or take your theme's colours, by design, so its right-click menu has no Colours, Border, Presets or Text controls. Delete it like anything else, or press **Undo** straight after placing it.

<Tip>
  Stickers work especially well for a quick reaction round on a shared document: drop a handful of
  thumbs and hearts near the ideas that landed, then move on. Because a sticker is just an element,
  everything you place is still there when you come back.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Emoji used to live as a category inside the Icons tab, and anything you placed back then stays
  exactly as it was: a plain tinted glyph, not a die-cut sticker. New ones come from here.
</Note>
