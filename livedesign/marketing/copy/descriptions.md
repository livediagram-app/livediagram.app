## Long description, ~250 words

For longer listing bodies and "about this project" sections.

> livedesign is a collaborative page editor that runs entirely in the browser,
> made for three kinds of page: logos, wireframes and photos. Its defining
> choice: the editor always works without signing in. A visitor can open a link
> and make a real design before being asked to create anything.
>
> Collaboration is real time and built in, not bolted on. Everyone on a design
> sees live cursors and selections, and comment threads with mentions; Follow
> keeps a group on the same view. Each design gets its own realtime room, so
> presence and edits stay snappy.
>
> Each kind of page has the tools it needs. A logo artboard has guides,
> mirroring and shape combining, and exports a Logo Kit. A wireframe page is a
> screen sized to its device, with ready-made layouts and web components that
> lay themselves out. A photo page is a layered pixel editor with adjustments
> and filters, exported as PNG, JPEG, WebP or PSD. On any page go text, shapes,
> images, charts, tables, icons and embeds.
>
> When it is time to show the work, point with a laser or a spotlight, or hide
> the toolbars in Zen mode. Export pages as PDF, PNG or SVG, share links as an
> Editor, Participant or Viewer and revoke them whenever you like, and keep everything in tabs
> and nested folders.
>
> livedesign is MIT-licensed, free for everyone with no paid tier and no plan
> for one. [236 words]

## Full description, ~500 words

For directories with a generous body field, a landing-page "about" block, or a
detailed GitHub / Product Hunt write-up.

> ### livedesign: design together, no sign-in required
>
> livedesign is a collaborative page editor that lives in the browser, for
> logos, wireframes and photos. It is built around one stubborn principle: the
> editor always works without signing in. A visitor can open a link and make a
> real design before being asked to create an account, because the easiest way
> to get a team designing together is to remove every step between them and the
> page.
>
> #### Real-time multiplayer, built in
>
> Collaboration is the product, not a feature. Everyone on a design sees live
> cursors, selections and comment threads as they happen, with mentions to pull
> someone in, and Follow to keep a group on one view. Each design runs in its
> own realtime room, and concurrent edits merge per element, so nobody clobbers
> anyone else.
>
> #### Three kinds of page
>
> A design holds tabs, and every tab is a run of pages of one kind. A logo
> artboard gives you guides, mirroring and shape combining, and exports a Logo
> Kit: the files a brand needs. A wireframe page is a screen of an app or a
> site, sized to its device, with ready-made layouts and web components that lay
> themselves out. A photo page is a layered pixel editor with adjustments and
> filters, for touching up a picture, a banner or a social image without a
> desktop tool. On any page you can place text, shapes, images, charts, tables,
> icons, stickers and embeds.
>
> #### Start fast, show it well
>
> Start from a template for each kind of page, or start blank, and restyle a
> whole tab with a theme. When the work is ready, walk people through it with a
> laser pointer and a spotlight, or clear the screen with Zen mode. Export pages
> as PDF, PNG or SVG, a photo as PNG, JPEG, WebP or PSD, and a tab as JSON.
>
> #### Nothing gets lost
>
> Every change saves on its own as you work, and undo and redo step back and
> forth through your own recent edits without touching anyone else's work. Share
> a design with Editor, Participant or Viewer links and revoke them whenever you want.
> Teams get a shared library of designs, and AI tools can read and write designs
> through the MCP server, the CLI or the REST API.
>
> #### Free, open, and yours
>
> livedesign is MIT-licensed and publicly viewable. The hosted version at
> livedesign.app is free for everyone, with no paid tier and no plan to
> introduce one. The open-source core never calls home and never gates features behind
> a license check.
>
> Open a link, design together, show it. That is the whole pitch. [443 words]
