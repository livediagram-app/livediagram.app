# Inbox

The Inbox is liveworkspace's: one view in the shared Explorer ([`@liveworkspace/explorer`](../../../../packages/explorer/README.md)), over liveworkspace's index, listing what is outstanding for the reader across every liveworkspace app. Its sections, each hidden when empty: **Assigned to You** (open actions assigned to the reader), **Assigned by You** (open actions they assigned to someone else) and **Open Comment Threads** (unresolved threads they are in, each saying why it is there: it mentions them, or it sits on their own design). The sidebar badge counts Assigned to You. The calls and types are in [one-explorer-contracts.md](../../../../liveworkspace/docs/one-explorer-contracts.md) "Inbox" and "What an item pushes"; the plan is [one-explorer.md](../../../../liveworkspace/docs/one-explorer.md).

What livedesign does is feed it and open its rows:

- **Threads and actions.** A design's Durable Object pushes each changed tab's threads and actions to the index (`PUT /app/items/:id/collab/:tabId`) with the tab's name, each thread's latest author colour, and each action's note and the names on it, on idle and at most once a minute, from its outbox (`apps/api/src/design-room/design-outbox.ts`, [API](../015-api/api.md) "Storage"). The comment text stays in the design.
- **This Browser.** Designs kept only in this browser never reach the index; the editor reads their actions and threads from the offline store (`apps/live/lib/offline/offline-activity.ts`) and hands them to the Explorer's `inbox` prop (`apps/live/lib/explorer-inbox-host.ts`), which lists them among the index's rows with the This Browser pill.
- **Opening a row.** The editor's deep link (`apps/live/lib/collab-deep-link.ts`): `#t=<tab>&el=<element>&open=action|comments`, which selects the element and opens its popover. `apps/live/lib/explorer-host.ts` `itemHref` builds it from the Explorer's `ItemPlace`.
- **Telemetry.** `Activity·Opened` (once a visit), `Activity·Selected` (`Action`, `Thread`) and `Activity·Loaded·Retry`, through the same host ([Telemetry](../017-telemetry/telemetry.md)).
