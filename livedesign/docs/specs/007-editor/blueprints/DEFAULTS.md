# Editor blueprint defaults

One row per default applied where a spec is silent or qualitative.

| #   | Blueprint       | Spec silence                                                                            | Default applied                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| D1  | appearance      | Lifetime of the OS watch                                                                | One `MediaQueryList`, attached on first subscribe or write, never detached                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| D2  | appearance      | Content of the `darkreader-lock` meta                                                   | `'true'`: Dark Reader keys on the name alone, and Next drops a meta with empty content                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| D3  | power-user-mode | What a "day" is for the offer's session count                                           | The local calendar day, `YYYY-MM-DD` from the device clock                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| D4  | power-user-mode | What counts as "a keyboard shortcut used"                                               | A keydown the editor's global shortcut listener newly claims (`defaultPrevented` false before, true after)                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| D5  | power-user-mode | Whether counting stops after the offer                                                  | It continues; the latch alone stops the offer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| D6  | power-user-mode | Shape of a palette tile without its caption                                             | The existing icon-only tile (`hideCaption`), named by a Tooltip                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| D7  | power-user-mode | Selection when switching to the view preview                                            | Cleared, so no edit toolbar lingers into viewing                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| D8  | power-user-mode | Look of the role status icon                                                            | The status bar control box, in the role's emerald or amber rather than the bar's slate                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| D9  | power-user-mode | Malformed offer counters                                                                | Reset to zero with a `[power-user-offer]` warning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

| D13 | ui-scale        | How a value between steps resolves                                                      | Snapped to the nearest 5% step, then rounded to 2 decimals                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

| D28 | pages           | The title bar's room for the cog and the layout invite                                  | Cog 32 px (`COG_ROOM`), the lock's room (`pageLockRoom`), and a logo page's title-bar controls (`logoTitleBarRoom`); invite 150 px with words, 30 px as an icon; words only with room for two 40 px labels beside                                                                                      |
| D29 | pages           | A layout tile's thumbnail size                                                          | 120 px wide outside the gallery (`THUMB_W`); in the gallery, its column less 12 px, the page's aspect                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| D30 | pages           | Where the layout preview stacks                                                         | `z-[1]` over the element layer, under the panel; the page's own content dropped from the clip meanwhile                                                                                                                                                                                                |

| D55 | pages           | The Add a page popover's size and place                                                 | 420 px wide, 10 px under the + (above it when there is no room), centred on it (retired 2026-10-24: a tab holds one kind, so there is no Add a Page popover; a wireframe tab's Add a Screen popover is 300 px wide, 10 px beside its button)                                                           |
| D56 | pages           | The first page's choice card's size                                                     | 420 px wide at one screen size, centred on the page                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| D57 | pages           | Where the page navigator sits                                                           | The bottom-right cluster, right of Undo / Redo                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| D58 | pages           | Which card the Add a page popover focuses                                               | The first, Logo (retired 2026-10-24 with the popover: the first page's choice focuses its first card)                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

| D66 | logo-pages      | Pixel size of the artboard                                                              | 1024 x 1024 (`LOGO_SIDE`): the common app-icon master                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

| D68 | logo-pages      | Whether Mirror persists                                                                 | Session only, per page (`useLogoTools`), released for every page when the tab changes (page ids repeat across tabs); the guide parts and strength are synced preferences (a lasting taste), Show Guides per page in local storage (page ids would fill the 4 KB preferences)                           |
| D70 | logo-pages      | Where arched text's baseline sits in its box                                            | Under 180 degrees the arc spans the box width, centred vertically; from 180 a circle fitted to the box less glyph room                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| D71 | logo-pages      | How smaller kit PNGs are made                                                           | Downscaled from one 1024 px raster by halving, smoothing high                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| D72 | logo-pages      | Weights offered for wordmark type                                                       | 400, 500, 700: what every catalogue font already loads, so no extra font bytes                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| D73 | logo-pages      | The size arched text is set at                                                          | `labelFontPx(textSize) x textScale`, the export's own size, so the canvas and every file agree                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| D75 | pages           | The Fit to Content tile's short name and the Split Into Pages icon                      | **Fit**, as the other tiles are one short word; Lucide `layout-grid` (`SplitPagesIcon`), one sheet as several                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| D76 | pages           | Where the view goes after Split Into Pages                                              | The first new page, as after Add page or Duplicate                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| D78 | pages           | The empty page's in-page layout card's size                                             | The page on screen less 16 px a side (`LAYOUT_CARD_MARGIN`), capped at 900 x 660 px; shown from 340 x 380 px, else the Choose a Layout button                                                                                                                                                          |
| D80 | pages           | How often a locked page repeats its notice                                              | Once per 2.5 s (`NOTICE_GAP_MS`) while presses keep landing on it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| D81 | pages           | "Blocks of a similar size" for even gaps                                                | Each side at most 1.5 times the line's first block's (`PAGE_TIDY_SIMILAR_RATIO`); a line is cut where one is not                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| D82 | pages           | How long a Tidy Up keeps settling                                                       | 6 passes per level, 10 runs over the page until one changes nothing (`PAGE_TIDY_PASSES`, `PAGE_TIDY_RUNS`)                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| D83 | pages           | When a card is too close to its page to read as a card                                  | Under 1.25:1 against the page (a gradient's mean under it), re-inked to 1.4:1 (`PAGE_CARD_FLOOR`, `PAGE_CARD_TARGET`); a border at 1.25:1 keeps it                                                                                                                                                     |
| D84 | pages           | How a gradient is measured under an element                                             | Its colours at 5 points across the element's span along the angle (`GRADIENT_SAMPLES`), the worst for text and lines, the mean for a card                                                                                                                                                              |
| D85 | pages           | How long re-inking remembers the author's colours                                       | The editor session, per tab, at most 20 tabs (`MAX_TABS`); never stored in the design                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| D86 | pages           | How carried words find their place in a new layout                                      | Roles by type size (title the most prominent at 32 px or more, large at 32 px or more, body the rest) and figures (12 chars at most, a digit, no three-letter word), paired in reading order with 4 px row tolerance; shrink to fit by a 0.55 / 0.6 bold glyph and 1.25 line estimate, never below 0.4 |
| D87 | pages           | When the view is on a page, and how many tabs remember theirs                           | The page under the view's centre, else the nearest by centre distance, once the view rests 400 ms (`LAST_PAGE_SETTLE_MS`: past a glide's 280 ms, short of a deliberate stop); the latest 200 tabs (`LAST_PAGE_MAX`), as Logo page guides keep 200 pages                                                |
| D88 | pages           | How far a pan or a flick goes before the page turns, and when the view counts as rested | A fifth of what shows, or 24 px at 0.5 px/ms as the hand lets go; within 2% of fit is the fit; 250 ms of rest with no pointer held (`SNAP_SWIPE_FRACTION`, `SNAP_FLICK_*`, `SNAP_FIT_TOLERANCE`, `VIEW_REST_MS`)                                                                                       |
| D89 | pages           | The layout gallery's columns and drawing                                                | Columns of at least 156 px (112 px on a tall page), never fewer than two, 8 px apart; a tile draws its preview within 320 px of view                                                                                                                                                                   |
| D90 | logo-pages      | Where the Fill tool sits in a logo page's Draw                                          | Its own Fill group after the Markers, inside an editor only; not in Search                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| D91 | logo-pages      | The Fill tool's key                                                                     | None: F is the Frame on every page                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| D92 | logo-pages      | The Fill tool's colour before one is picked                                             | The soft Blue standard colour, drawn for the canvas (`defaultFill`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
