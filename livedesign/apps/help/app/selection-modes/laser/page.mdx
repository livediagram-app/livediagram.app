The Laser Pointer mode gives you a temporary laser trail for drawing attention while you share your screen or walk a group through the canvas. Choose **Laser** from the tool picker at the start of the palette strip, or press **`K`**.

## How it works

Move the cursor to leave a glowing trail that fades on its own, after about a second at the default setting. It is purely for emphasis: it draws nothing permanent and changes none of your elements, so you can gesture freely without altering the canvas.

On a shared design everyone else sees your trail live, in your colour, and your cursor is hidden for the duration so they see only the laser dot.

- **With a mouse or trackpad**, just move: the trail follows the pointer. A click-drag pans the canvas, like the Hand tool, and the trail keeps drawing while you pan.
- **On a touchscreen**, drag a finger to draw the trail. To move around, switch to [Hand](/help/selection-modes/hand/) for a moment.

## Setting up the laser

While the mode is active a **Laser** panel sits at the bottom right, above the zoom controls, with four settings over a preview of the stroke. The preview holds still, and draws the stroke across once each time you change a setting:

- **Width**: **Fine**, **Medium** or **Bold**.
- **Colour**: **Your colour** (your participant colour, the default), one of the ten standard colours (each adjusted to read on a light or a dark canvas), or **Add a custom colour** (four coloured dots) for a colour of your own, for when your colour doesn't read against the canvas you are showing.
- **Trail**: how long the line lingers: **Quick**, **Normal** or **Long**. Long lets the end of the line linger for longer after you stop moving.
- **Effect**: **Beam** (a clean line), **Glow** (a soft halo that survives a projector), **Comet** (tapers away behind the tip), or **Spark** (a dotted trail).

Everyone in the room sees your laser as you set it, not their own default, and the settings are remembered on this device for next time.

## Who can use it

- **Participants** pick it from their palette like an Editor.
- **View-only visitors** can use the laser too, with `K`, since it changes nothing.
- **On an empty canvas** it is greyed out in the picker. Add an element and it becomes available again.
