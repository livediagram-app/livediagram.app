livedesign has no title bar: the canvas runs all the way to the top of the window. The design's main actions float in a small card at the **top right** of the canvas, level with the palette strip and the menu button at the top left. Reading from left to right, it holds **Prototype** on a wireframe tab, then **Share** if the design is yours or **Copy** if it is someone else's, and your account at the end.

On a computer each button shows its name beside its icon. On a phone, or when the top row is too crowded for names, the card shows its icons alone. The card hides in [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) and inside an [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/).

## Prototype

On a [wireframe tab](/help/pages/wireframe-pages/), **Prototype** comes first in the card, for every role, and plays the screens as a clickable prototype.

## Share or Copy

The card holds one main action, and which one you get depends on whose design it is.

**Share** is for the owner, and for a member of the team a [team design](/help/collaboration/teams/team-shared-designs/) belongs to, opening it from the team rather than through a share link. It opens the [Share dialog](/help/collaboration/sharing/), where you create and manage links. The button turns solid once the design has a live link, so you can tell at a glance that it is shared. On an [Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) design it asks you to save the design to your account first, since there is nothing on a server to share yet.

**Copy** is for everyone else who has the design open: someone on a share link of any role. It makes a copy of the design that belongs to you:

<Step n={1}>Click **Copy**. It reads **Copying** while the copy is made.</Step>
<Step n={2}>The editor opens your copy, named "Copy of" and the original name.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Find it again later at the top level of My Work in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
</Step>

Your copy starts **Private**: no share links come with it, and nothing you do to it touches the original. If you opened the design through a link to a single tab, the copy holds just that tab. You do not need an account to copy; as a guest the copy is saved under your [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/). On a design from the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/), Copy is in the Community bar along the top instead.

## Your account

At the end of the card is your account. Signed in, it shows your picture (or your initials) and first name; click it for a menu with your name and email, **Account** (which opens [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) on your account details, without leaving the design) and **Sign Out**. Not signed in, it shows **Sign In** instead (see [Signing In](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/)).

## The design's name and who can see it

The editor does not show the design's name: the menu card at the top left shows the open tab's name instead. To rename the design, use **Rename** on its **⋯** menu in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/). Names are capped at 60 characters.

Who can see a design (Private, Shared, Team, Public in the Community, or Offline) shows as a pill on the design in the Explorer, and the [Share dialog](/help/collaboration/sharing/) is where the owner changes it.
