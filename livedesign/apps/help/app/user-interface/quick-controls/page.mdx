The **quick controls** sit at the right-hand end of the bottom bar, after the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/)'s tabs, and gather the actions you want reachable at any moment, whatever you are doing on the canvas. They stay put while the rest of the interface comes and goes with your selection.

## What lives here

Left to right:

- **Get Started**: shown while a guided tour is on offer, such as the tour of the kind of page you are on, or of a complex element you have selected. Press it to pick a tour and start it. Once you have taken a tour it leaves the button; **Getting Started** in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/#getting-started) offers it again.
- **Tools**: one menu for the tools that work on the whole design, in groups:
  - **Search…** opens the [search panel](/help/search-panel/) to jump to any design, tab, or element, to drop something onto the canvas, or to run a command. `⌘K` (Ctrl+K) opens it from the keyboard too.
  - **Webhooks**, if you can manage the design's webhooks: tell [other services](/help/collaboration/webhooks/) when the design changes.
  - **Walkthroughs**: record a [walkthrough](/help/collaboration/walkthroughs/) of the design, or play one.
  - **History**, unless you opened the design through a share link: [who changed what and when](/help/collaboration/history/), with the design's [snapshots](/help/collaboration/snapshots/) pinned on its timeline.

  History, Webhooks and Walkthroughs need you signed in, and a design in [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) saved to your account first: until then their rows carry a **Sign In** or **Sync Design** badge, and pressing one asks you to do that.

- **Settings**: the [Settings dialog](/help/user-interface/settings/): the preferences that follow you between devices, plus the **Keyboard** category with the full [shortcut reference](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) and the switch that turns shortcuts off.
- **Appearance**: click to step the [interface between Light, Dark, and System](/help/tools/dark-mode/) (System follows your device). Hover it to see which is in force. This is the app's own chrome, not the tab's theme, which lives behind the **Theme & canvas** button in the bottom-right corner.

On a computer, Get Started, Tools and Settings show their names beside their icons (turn **Button Labels** off under **Appearance** in Settings for icons alone), and hovering a control shows what it does. On a phone the bar is tight, so the controls are icons only.

The full-page [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) has its own bar at the bottom, with **Search**, **Settings** and the appearance switch.

Because they are fixed in the corner rather than tied to a selection, the quick controls are the dependable starting point when you are not sure where an action lives: search finds both things and commands.
