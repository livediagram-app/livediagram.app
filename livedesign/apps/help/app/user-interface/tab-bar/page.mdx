A design can hold many tabs, each a row of pages, and the **tab bar** is how you move between them; the bar runs along the bottom of the editor and shows them in a row, so the whole design is one click wide. The [quick controls](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/) (Search, Settings and the appearance switch) sit at its right-hand end.

  <TabBar />
</Figure>

## What you can do here

- **Switch** between tabs by clicking one. A small stack of initials avatars on a tab shows who else is on it right now.
- **Add** a new tab with the **+** button straight after the last tab. It arrives numbered ("Tab 2", "Tab 3"), wearing the current tab's theme, background and font, on **Start Making Pages**, so you can begin from a [template](/help/canvas/templates/) or close it to leave the tab blank.
- **Rename** a tab by double-clicking the active one, or from its menu.
- **Reorder** tabs by dragging them along the bar; a caret shows where the tab will land.
- **Manage** a tab from its menu: right-click any tab (it becomes the active one), or click the **⋯** button on the active tab. The menu holds **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Paste**, **Lock Tab** and **Delete**, plus **Add to Folder**, **Add to Design** (link it into another design), import, export and more. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/#on-a-tab).
- **Organise** related tabs into one-level folders with **Add to Folder** when a design grows. See [Tab Folders](/help/tabs/tab-folders/).

On a view-only link the bar still switches tabs, but there is no **+** button and no tab menu. On a phone the "Tabs" label is dropped to save room.

For the depth, see [Tabs](/help/tabs/): this bar is just the control surface for them.
