The **zoom controls** sit at the end of the cluster in the bottom-right corner of the canvas, after Undo and Redo, the page navigator, **Contents** and **Collaborate**, and the **Theme & canvas** button, and govern how close you are to what is on it. The infinite canvas can hold far more than one screenful, so zoom is how you move between the detail and the big picture.

## The controls

- **Zoom out** / **Zoom in**: step 10% further or closer.
- **The percentage button**: shows the current level. **Click it to fit**: it frames the page nearest the middle of the screen, the quickest way to get your bearings after panning around. **Hover it** for a popover of preset levels (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, 125% and 150%, plus **Fit to screen**) and pick one to jump straight there (100% is true size). With the keyboard, `Enter` fits and the arrow keys open the presets.

Zoom runs from 10% to 500%. On a [photo page](/help/pages/photo-pages/) you can zoom in further, far enough to see its single pixels.

## From the keyboard and trackpad

| Keys                     | Does                              |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| `⌘+` / `⌘-`              | Zoom in / out                     |
| `⌘0`                     | Reset the zoom to 100%            |
| `⇧1`                     | Frame the page nearest the middle |
| `⌘` + scroll, or a pinch | Zoom in or out                    |

`⌘` is Ctrl on Windows and Linux. A plain two-finger scroll pans rather than zooms, and so do the [Hand tool](/help/selection-modes/hand/), `Space` + drag, and a middle-button drag. Zoom never changes the design, so it works on a view-only link too. See [Pan and Zoom](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/).

## On a phone and in zen mode

On a phone the zoom buttons and the percentage are dropped to save space: pinch to zoom, and a dedicated **Fit** button stands in for fit-to-screen. In [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) everything else hides but the zoom controls stay, joined by an **Exit zen mode** button. On a Participant or Viewer link, a **Zen mode** button also sits at the end of the zoom controls, so you can enter it from there.
