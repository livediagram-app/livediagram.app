<ArticleLayout title="Your First Design" description="From a new design to a first page you can share, in a couple of minutes, with no account required." categoryTitle="Getting Started" categorySlug="getting-started">

The quickest way to learn livedesign is to make something. This walkthrough takes you from a new design to a first page with text, shapes and a picture on it, ready to share. You do not need to sign in, the [editor always works as a guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## Create a new design

<Step n={1}>
  Open [a new design](/new). livedesign makes it straight away and opens it in the editor, with
  nothing to fill in first.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  It is saved where new designs go: to your account once you have signed in, or in this browser
  while you try things out as a guest (you can [move it to your
  account](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) any time). [Default
  Folders](/help/explorer/folders/default-folders/) decide which folder it lands in.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Its first tab opens on **Start Making Pages**: **Recommended** picks first, then every
  [template](/help/canvas/templates/) grouped by what it makes (**Logos**, **Photos** and
  **Wireframes**), with a search. Click one to start from its ready-made pages, or press the **×**
  to follow along from a blank page.
</Step>

You can rename the design any time: click its title in the header at the top of the editor and type. Renaming is instant and saved for you. Names cap at **60 characters**: typing simply stops there, which is plenty for a title and short enough to stay readable in a tab bar or a list row.

## Choose what the first page is for

Closing Start Making Pages leaves one empty page, which asks **What Is This Page For?** Choose now: a page keeps its kind once you start.

- **Logo**: a square artboard for a mark. Choose this to follow along. See [Logo Pages](/help/pages/logo-pages/).
- **Wireframe**: a screen, framed by the device you choose for it. See [Wireframe Pages](/help/pages/wireframe-pages/).
- **Photo**: a picture to edit in layers, from one you open or a blank canvas. See [Photo Pages](/help/pages/photo-pages/).

For a short tour of your kind of page, on your own page, press Start Pages Tour in the bar at the bottom of the editor, just left of **Settings**. It never starts on its own, and you can take it again whenever you like. See [The Pages Tour](/help/pages/pages-tour/).

## Add text and shapes

<Step n={1}>
  Find the **Palette**, the strip across the top of the editor. On a logo page it opens on **Draw**,
  the pens and outlines a mark is drawn with; its other categories (**Popular**, **Shapes**,
  **Write**, **Icons** and **Media**) sit along it by name.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick **Add Text** (in **Popular**, or **Write**, or press `T`), then **click the page** where you
  want it, and type a heading. Click away to finish.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Rest on **Shapes** and pick a shape tile, then click the page to drop it at its default size, or
  drag on the page to draw it at the size you want. **Double-click the shape** to give it a label.
</Step>

Anything you place on a page belongs to it: whatever hangs off its edge is cut off there, on screen and in exports. Move or resize an element and it snaps to the page's edges, centre lines and margins.

## Add a picture

<Step n={1}>
  Pick **Add Image** from **Media** (or press `9`), then click the page. The image lands as an empty
  placeholder reading "No image yet".
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Press its **Select Image** button (or double-click it), and on the **Upload** tab drop a file in,
  click to browse, or paste from your clipboard. You can also paste a picture straight onto the
  page. See [Images](/help/palette/tools/images/).
</Step>

Want a colour or a gradient behind the mark instead? Open the page's cog and its **Background** tab. See [Working with Pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/#backgrounds-and-patterns).

## Share it

<Step n={1}>
  Click **Share** at the top right of the editor, choose a role (**Editor**, **Participant** or
  **Viewer**), and press **Create Pass**. The link is copied for you.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Send it to whoever you like. They join the same design and see your cursor and every change live,
  even without an account. See [Sharing Your Design](/help/getting-started/sharing-your-design/).
</Step>

## Keep going

- **Add another page** with the **+** after the last page: it is the same kind as the tab's first page. For another kind, add a new tab.
- **Recolour everything** with a [theme](/help/canvas/themes/).
- **Present or export** your pages. See [Exporting and Presenting Pages](/help/pages/exporting-pages/).

<Tip>
  Made a mistake? [Undo](/help/canvas/undo/) and [Redo](/help/canvas/redo/) sit in the controls in
  the bottom-right corner (or press `Cmd/Ctrl+Z`), and the common keyboard shortcuts are in
  [Keyboard Essentials](/help/getting-started/keyboard-essentials/).
</Tip>
