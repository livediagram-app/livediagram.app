A **webhook** tells another service when something happens to a design, so your team hears about a new comment or a change in the channel they already watch. Each webhook belongs to one design: what you set up applies to that design only, and each design can have up to **10**.

## Adding a webhook

<Step n={1}>
  Open **Tools** at the end of the bottom bar and press **Webhooks** (under Automate). The design's
  webhooks open inside Tools. The same list is on the design's **⋯** menu in the
  [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Choose **Add Webhook** and pick where it posts: **Slack** (a message in a channel), **Microsoft
  Teams** (a card in a channel) or **Your Own Service** (signed JSON to an HTTPS address you run).
  The dialog shows, step by step, how to get the address to paste.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Give it a name, such as the channel it posts to, then tick what it should send (none are ticked at
  first) and **Who Did It**: **Anyone**, or **Only Me** for just your own changes. Press **Add
  Webhook**.
</Step>

## Looking after them

Each webhook in the list shows what it sends, and has **Send Test** to post a test message now, **Pause** (and **Resume**) to stop it for a while, **Edit Events** to change what it sends, **Delete**, and its **Recent Deliveries**, so you can see what arrived and what failed.

## Who can set them up

You need to be signed in and able to edit the whole design: its owner, someone in its team, or someone with an Editor link to every tab. Signed in without that access, there is no **Webhooks** row in Tools. Signed out, the row carries a **Sign In** badge, and for a design kept only in [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) a **Sync Design** badge, which saves the design to your account first.

On a team's design, adding or removing a webhook is recorded in the team's [Audit Log](/help/collaboration/teams/audit-log/).
