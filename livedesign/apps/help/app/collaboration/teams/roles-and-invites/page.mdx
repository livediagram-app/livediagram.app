Teams belong to your liveworkspace account, so a team is the same in every liveworkspace app, livedesign included. You make and run them in livedesign's Explorer: **New Team** at the bottom of the sidebar's **Spaces** group makes one, and a team's card, at the top of its view (or behind **Members** in the editor's Explorer panel), runs it. Everything here needs a signed-in account.

## Admin and Member

The person who creates a team is its first **Admin**. On the team's card, Admins can:

- rename the team (**Edit Team** on its ⋯ menu),
- invite people by email,
- change a member's role,
- remove a member or withdraw an invite,
- turn on a join link (**Invite by Link**),
- delete the team.

**Members** can see the team and its member list, work in the shared library, and leave the team.

A team always keeps an Admin: the last Admin can't step down or leave while others remain. Deleting a team gives its designs back to their owners (see [Team Shared Designs](/help/collaboration/teams/team-shared-designs/#when-a-team-is-deleted)).

**Invite by Link** gives a join link that works for 7 days: anyone signed in who opens it joins as a Member. **Copy** it, make a **New Link** (the old one stops working), or **Turn Off** the link.

## Inviting by email

<Step n={1}>
  Open your team in the Explorer, enter the person's email address at the foot of its card, choose
  Member or Admin, and press **Invite**.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  They get an email from liveworkspace with a link to the Explorer's **Invites**, where the invitation waits.
  They don't need an account yet: it waits until they sign up with that address. Signed in to
  livedesign, they also see it under **Invites** in the Explorer sidebar, with a count.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  They press **Accept** (which opens the team) or **Decline** on the invitation's card in Invites.
  Accepting takes a signed-in account whose verified email address is the one invited. The email's
  own decline link works without an account.
</Step>

Once they've joined, the team shows up in livedesign within a few minutes, usually at once.
