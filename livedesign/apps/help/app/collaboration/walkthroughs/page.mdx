A walkthrough is a video of you talking someone through the design. It films this browser tab as you see it, with your voice, and your camera as a round bubble in a corner if you turn it on. Anyone who can open the design can watch it, and you can share it with a public link too.

## Recording a walkthrough

<Step n={1}>
  Open **Tools** in the footer and press **Walkthroughs** (under Collaborate), then **Record
  Walkthrough** at the foot of the list. **Record a Walkthrough** opens beside Tools.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick your **Microphone**. The **Camera** switch starts off, so a walkthrough records your voice
  alone and the browser asks only for the microphone; turn it on to pick a camera, show yourself in
  a round bubble and choose its corner. The switch is remembered in this browser. If the browser
  hasn't been allowed to list your devices yet, press **Allow Microphone** (or **Allow Camera**).
  **Mic Level** moves as you speak, so you know you are heard.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Press **Start Recording**. Your browser asks you to share this tab: allow it. If you don't,
  nothing is recorded and the set-up says so. Safari and phones can't film a tab. After a 3, 2, 1
  countdown, talk and move around the design as you would with someone beside you.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Press **Finish** in the recording bar (or stop sharing from the browser's own bar). The
  walkthrough saves straight away as "Walkthrough of" the tab you started on, with a progress bar,
  then shows at the top of the Walkthroughs list. **Discard** throws the recording away, after
  asking.
</Step>

A walkthrough can run for up to ten minutes: the bar turns amber with "1 minute left" and finishes on its own at ten. If saving is refused (it is over 90 MB, or you already have 50 walkthroughs), the card says why and offers **Download a Copy** so the recording isn't lost, and **Try Again**.

## Hiding elements from the recording

Before you start, press **Hide From Recording** in the set-up, then press each element viewers shouldn't see: a private note, an unfinished corner. **Hide Selection** hides what is already selected, and **Done** or `Esc` leaves the mode; the button's count shows how many are hidden. While you record they are hidden from your own screen, so the video never shows them. They stay on the design itself for anyone who opens it. What is hidden is settled before the recording starts.

## Watching a walkthrough

Open **Tools**, press **Walkthroughs**, and press one in the list. It plays in a video player over the design, with its title, its length, who made it and when, the browser's own controls (play, seek, speed) and **Full Screen**.

Walkthroughs recorded before videos came in still play the old way, over the design as it is now: pan or zoom to pause and look around, then **Resume**.

Every walkthrough you have recorded in livedesign is also in the Explorer, under **Walkthroughs** in the Library, where you can play it, open the design it was made on, or delete it.

## Renaming, sharing and deleting

Each walkthrough's row has a **⋯** menu:

- **Rename** edits the title in place (up to 120 characters). `Enter` saves, `Esc` cancels.
- **Share**, on a video, turns its **Public Link** on or off. On, **Copy Link** copies a liveworkspace.app link: anyone with it can watch the video, its title and who made it, without signing in, but never the design. Off, the link stops working at once. A shared walkthrough shows a link mark in the list, and the player's **Share** reads **Shared**.
- **Delete** asks first, then deletes it for everyone who can open the design.

## Who can record and manage them

Editors record walkthroughs, and can rename, share and delete any of them. Everyone who can open the design can watch them; Participants and Viewers can rename, share and delete only one they recorded themselves. Walkthroughs aren't offered on a phone, in an embed or through a link to one tab. On a design kept in **This Browser**, the Walkthroughs row is marked **Sync Design** and asks you to sync it first.

## Privacy

Calls are never recorded. A walkthrough is only made when you press Start Recording and share the tab, and the browser shows that it is sharing the whole time. It is stored with the design for the people who can open it, and reaches anyone else only through a public link you turn on. Deleting a walkthrough, purging its design from the Trash or deleting your account deletes it. See the [privacy policy](https://www.liveworkspace.app/privacy).
