A snapshot keeps the whole design as it is right now, so you can try a big change (a reorganisation, an import, a clean-up with a teammate or an AI assistant) knowing you can put everything back in one step.

## Taking a snapshot

Open **Tools** in the footer and press [History](/help/collaboration/history/): snapshots live there, each one pinned on the design's timeline at the time it was taken. Press **Take Snapshot** at the foot of History, give the snapshot a name if you like (up to 80 characters), such as "Before the workshop", and press **Take Snapshot**. Without a name it is called by the time it was taken, like "Snapshot of 15 Nov, 14:32".

Each snapshot shows its name, who took it, how long ago and its size. The latest **five** are kept: when five are there, Take a Snapshot says which one taking another will remove, and the oldest goes.

If you opened the design through an Editor link, History shows the design's snapshots on their own, without the timeline.

## Reverting to a snapshot

Press **Revert** on a snapshot and confirm with **Revert**. The design goes back to exactly how it was: every tab, its folders and the component library. The design's name, its comments and who it is shared with stay as they are.

Reverting replaces everyone's work since that snapshot, so first it takes a snapshot of the design as it is now, marked **Before revert**. If you change your mind, revert to that one.

Everyone who has the design open sees it reload onto the reverted version straight away, so nobody keeps working on what was replaced.

## Deleting a snapshot

Press the bin (**Delete snapshot**) beside a snapshot. It goes straight away, without asking, and can't be brought back.

## Who can use snapshots

Editors of the whole design take, revert and delete snapshots. Participants, Viewers and people with a link to one tab don't see them. Snapshots are there on designs stored on the server: a design kept **Offline** has none, and AI assistants and scripts using an API token can't use them.
