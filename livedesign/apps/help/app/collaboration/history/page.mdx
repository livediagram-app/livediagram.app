**History** answers "what happened to this design?" without leaving the editor. It shows who worked on the design and when, and it is where the design's [snapshots](/help/collaboration/snapshots/) live, each one pinned on the timeline at the moment it was taken.

## Opening History

Open **Tools** at the end of the bottom bar and press **History**. It opens inside the Tools menu; the back bar at its top returns to the rest of Tools. The same History is on the design's **⋯** menu in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).

## What it shows

- **At a glance**: a card that says when the design last changed, with the faces of the people who worked on it, when it was made and how many events there are, and a bar for each of the last 14 days so you can see the shape of the work.
- **The timeline**: newest first, under a heading for each day. Each entry says what happened (made, edited, renamed, moved, shared, sent to the Trash and back), who did it ("You" for your own), and when.
- **Filters**: when the timeline holds more than one kind of event, chips over it narrow it to one kind, with **All** to show everything again.
- **Show older events** at the foot loads the next page.

## Snapshots on the timeline

If you can take snapshots, **Take Snapshot** sits at the foot of History and each snapshot appears on the timeline, with **Revert** and **Delete** in place. See [Snapshots](/help/collaboration/snapshots/) for how they work.

## Who sees it

The timeline is there when you are signed in and reach the design directly: one in your own space, or a team's design for a team you are in. A design opened through a share link has no timeline. An Editor on a link still gets History for the design's snapshots, shown on their own.

Signed out, or for a design kept only in [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/), the **History** row carries a **Sign In** or **Sync Design** badge, and pressing it asks you to do that first.
