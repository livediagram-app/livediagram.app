The full-page [Explorer](/explorer) is the complete library view, modelled on a desktop file browser: a sidebar on the left to navigate, and the page you picked on the right, with its folders and designs. Opening `/explorer` takes you to **Home**. It is where all your designs live, organised into folders by project, customer, or topic. (For the compact version inside the editor, see the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).)

The sidebar is one tree in three groups: **Overview**, **Spaces** and **More**. Every design lives in exactly one place, **My Work**, a team, or this browser, and everything else in the sidebar is a view that gathers designs from wherever they live. Search sits in the top bar: type there to search, or pick a filter, and the page you are on narrows to what matches ([Search and filter designs](/help/explorer/filters/)). Making things happens in the page header: **New Design** is on Home, Timeline, Recent, Favourites and search results, and on My Work and inside a folder it becomes a **Create** button offering **New Design** and **New Folder** (**New Subfolder** inside a folder). Pages that aren't a place a design lands (Inbox, Shared With Me, Tags, the Library pages, Trash, a team, This Browser) have no New Design.

## Overview

- **Home** is what the Explorer opens on ([Home](/help/explorer/timeline/)): the designs you use most and last, and what other people did while you were away. Its **See More** link opens [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/), which has no sidebar row of its own. The row carries a badge when something has happened since you last looked.
- **Inbox** lists the open [actions and comment threads](/help/explorer/activity/) waiting on you, with a badge counting the open actions assigned to you.
- **Timeline** is the [full feed](/help/explorer/timeline/#the-timeline) of everything that has happened across your designs, teams and account, your own doings included. Home's **See Timeline** link opens it too.
- **Shared With Me** gathers every design other people have [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) into one list, kept apart from your own library so collaborator work and your own never get mixed up. Its badge counts them.

## Spaces

Your spaces are the top-level folders of the tree.

- **My Work** is [your own library](/help/explorer/personal-space/). Beneath it sit the folders you create; opening it lists those folders, then the designs you have not filed. This is where you do the filing: moving designs into folders with **Change Folder**, renaming, and nesting folders inside folders. Its own menu (its **⋯** button or a right-click) holds **Use as Default for** ([Default folders](/help/explorer/folders/default-folders/)).
- **Each team** you belong to sits beside it, and expands to the team's shared folders ([Team spaces](/help/explorer/team-spaces/)). Its badge counts the members once there is more than one.
- **Manage Teams** opens your teams on liveworkspace.app, where teams are made and run. If you are not signed in, a **Sign in to access Teams** card sits here instead (a self-hosted copy without sign-in shows neither).

## More

- **This Browser** opens the page of designs saved only in this browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)), at [/offline](/offline). They are newest first, each opening in the editor, and when you are signed in each has **Sync Design** to move it to your account. A cloud design's menu in the editor's Explorer panel offers **Take Offline** to do the reverse.
- **Library** expands (it has no page of its own) to **Tags** first, which opens the [Tags page](/help/explorer/tags/) (your designs grouped by the coloured tags you put on them) and expands to a row for each tag, then the [Image gallery](/help/explorer/image-gallery/) (every image you have uploaded, with where each is used).
- **Trash** holds deleted designs for 30 days, ready to restore.

## Moving around with the keyboard

The sidebar is a single stop when you press Tab. Then the **arrow keys** move up and down, **Right** opens a row and **Left** closes it (or moves to its parent), **Home** and **End** jump to the first and last rows, **Enter** or **Space** opens the row, and typing a letter jumps to the next row starting with it. On a folder, or on My Work, **Shift+F10** (or the **Menu** key) opens its menu.

My Work starts expanded and Library starts closed (it opens by itself when you are on one of its pages). Teams and folders start closed, and what you expand lasts until you reload.

With [power user mode](/help/user-interface/power-user-mode/) and Minimal chrome on, the group titles give way to thin lines between the groups, with nothing above Home.

Each page is named after its row: **Home**, **Shared With Me**, **My Work**, **This Browser**, **Image Gallery** and the rest, in the page heading, the browser tab and the breadcrumb.

## List or card view

Every browse view (My Work, your folders, Recent, Favourites, Search results, This Browser, and a team's shared library) can be shown three ways, switched with the **List view** / **Card view** / **Details view** toggle at the top-right of the pane:

- **List** packs designs into compact rows, best for scanning a long library by name and date.
- **Card** shows each design as a tile with a thumbnail of its first tab, best for recognising a design by its shape at a glance.
- **Details** lays everything out as a table you can sort by any column: type, comments, access, size and dates.

Cards are the starting view. Your choice is remembered on this device, so the Explorer always opens the way you left it. [List, Card and Details Views](/help/explorer/list-and-card-views/) covers the three layouts and the snapshot previews in detail.

## Duplicating a Design

To make a copy of a design, open its menu (the **⋯** button on its row or card, or a right-click) and choose **Duplicate**. A notification naming the design ("“Roadmap” duplicated") confirms it, and the copy appears in the list.

- **The name** is the original's with "copy" on the end, so "Roadmap" becomes "Roadmap copy". Rename it from the same menu.
- **Where it goes:** beside the original, in the same team and folder. If you can't file a design there (a team you are not in, or a folder that isn't yours), the copy lands at the top of **My Work** instead.
- **Everything comes with it:** every tab and its tab folders.
- **Links between tabs** point at the copy's own tabs, so clicking one inside the copy stays inside the copy. Links to other designs and to websites are left as they are.
- **[Default folders](/help/explorer/folders/default-folders/) don't apply.** A copy always goes beside its original, never into the folder you set as a default.
- **A design in This Browser** duplicates as another design in This Browser, so nothing you chose to keep in this browser is uploaded. See [Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/).

If the copy can't be made, a message says so ("Could not duplicate “Roadmap”. Try again.") and nothing is created.

## Finding your way around

Whichever row you pick, the main pane on the right shows its contents. Inside a folder, a breadcrumb under the title traces the folder path so you can click any step to jump back up. On a phone, the menu button beside the page title opens the same sidebar as a drawer from the left; picking a page closes it. Everything works whether or not you are signed in: a guest sees the designs and folders their own browser owns, and can sign in later to carry that work into an account.
