**My Work** is your own library: the [designs](/explorer/all) you have created, and with **All** chosen, everything you made in the other liveworkspace apps too. It is the first row under **Spaces** in the Explorer sidebar. Each team you belong to sits beside it as a space of its own ([Team Spaces](/help/explorer/team-spaces/)).

An item lives in exactly one place: My Work or a team. Moving it changes where it lives. Everything else in the sidebar (Home, Inbox, Timeline, Shared With Me, and the Library's Recent, Favourites and Unsorted) is a view that gathers items from wherever they live.

## The root of My Work

Opening **My Work** lists its folders first, then every item of yours that is not in a folder. A freshly created design lands there unless you made it inside a folder or set a [default folder](/help/explorer/folders/default-folders/), so it always has an obvious home without you deciding anything first. Filing an item into a folder moves it out of the root; moving it back to **My Work** puts it there again.

Designs an AI tool made for you sit there too, like any other, and carry a **Made by AI** badge. To see only those, wherever they are filed, use the **Made by AI** filter ([Search and Filter Designs](/help/explorer/filters/)).

## This Browser

Designs saved only in this browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)) are yours too, but they live in this browser rather than in My Work: they collect under **This Browser**, in the sidebar's **More** group. Wherever one is listed, a **This Browser** pill marks it, because clearing this browser's site data deletes it. When you are signed in, **Sync Design** moves one into your account.

## Unsorted

**Unsorted**, under **Library** in the sidebar's More group, lists every item in no folder: yours and your teams'. It is the quickest way to find what still needs filing.

## Favourites

Choose **Add to Favourites** in any item's menu to star it. It marks the handful of items you care about with a star wherever they are listed, and the same menu reads **Remove from Favourites** to take it back off. Your starred items are gathered under **Favourites**, in the sidebar's Library, from your own folders and every team you're in, each showing where it actually lives.

**Starring is yours alone.** Favouriting an item in a team folder doesn't favourite it for your teammates.

## Folders

Folders give your library structure without forcing it. Create one with **Create**, then **New Folder**, at the top of My Work; move an item with **Change Folder** in its menu, or drag it onto a folder; and rename, move or delete a folder from its own menu. Deleting a folder never deletes what is inside: it moves up a level. See [Folders](/help/explorer/folders/) for the details.

## Two places to browse

You will meet My Work in two forms: the **[full-page Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/)**, and the compact **[Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/)** behind the editor's menu button. Both work whether or not you are signed in: a guest has a library of their own, and signing in carries it into the account.

<Tip>
  Do not over-organise on day one. Start at the root, and pull items into folders once a real
  grouping emerges.
</Tip>
