**Recent** lists the designs that changed most recently, newest first, so the work in progress is always one click away. It has no row in the [Explorer](/explorer) sidebar, where [Home](/help/explorer/timeline/) answers "what was I working on" and "what happened": open it from **See More** in Home's Jump Back In (the last tile of the strip on a phone), or at [/explorer/recent](/explorer/recent). Its breadcrumb reads **Home › Recent** and leads back to Home.

## What lands here

Recent draws on every design you can open: your own, your teams', and the ones [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/). It shows the **12** that were saved most recently, by you or by anyone you work with, so a teammate's edit to a team design brings it up the list too.

It is a view across your whole library, not a folder of its own: a design appearing in Recent has not been moved or copied, and you will still find it in its folder (or at the root of My Work).

<Note>
  Recent goes by when a design last changed, not when you last looked at it: simply opening a
  design does not move it. For the designs you open most and last, look at Jump Back In on
  [Home](/help/explorer/timeline/#jump-back-in).
</Note>

## Working from Recent

- **Open** a design by clicking it.
- Switch between [List, Card and Details views](/help/explorer/list-and-card-views/) with the toggle in the header; on a computer, List view adds an owner column, since Recent mixes your designs with other people's.
- Type in the Explorer's search box to [filter](/help/explorer/filters/) the list: Recent then shows the 12 newest designs that match.
- Each design's **⋯** menu (or a right-click) carries the same actions as anywhere else in the Explorer: Rename, Duplicate, Change Folder, Favourite, History, Hide from Recent, and Delete, plus **Open Team** on a team design.
- [Designs in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) appear here too, and their menu adds **Sync Design** (or **Take Offline** on a cloud design).
- A design shared with you has a shorter menu: **Open** and **Dismiss**.

## Seeing where a design lives

Recent spans every folder, so each design shows a small chip naming the folder it sits in, beside its name. Click the chip to jump straight to that folder instead of opening the design, which is the quickest way to see what else you filed alongside it. (In List and Details views on a phone the chip is hidden to save room.)

Designs at the root show **My Work**. Team designs name the team, and the chip opens the team's library. Designs shared with you don't show one at all: they sit in the other person's library rather than yours.

## Hiding a design from Recent

Some designs you open now and then but never want cluttering the list. Choose **Hide from Recent** in a design's menu and it stops appearing here, no matter how recently it changed; the next newest design takes its place. The same menu then reads **Show in Recent**, so it is easy to put back. The menu item is also in the editor's Explorer panel.

The design itself is untouched: it stays in its folder, in search, on its share links, and opens normally. Only this list changes, which is why there is no badge on it anywhere else: the menu item tells you the state where you would change it.

**The choice is yours alone.** Hiding a team design from your Recent doesn't hide it from your teammates'. (Designs shared with you can't be hidden; use **Dismiss** instead.) It travels with your account, so it applies on your other devices too, and works the same whether or not you're signed in. Up to 60 designs can be hidden at once; past that, the one you hid longest ago comes back.

<Tip>
  Recent is owner-scoped like the rest of the Explorer: a guest sees the designs their own browser
  can open, and signing in carries them into your account.
</Tip>
