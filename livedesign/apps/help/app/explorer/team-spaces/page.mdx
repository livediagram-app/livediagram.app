Each team you belong to is a space of its own in the Explorer sidebar: a row under **Spaces**, right after your own [My Work](/help/explorer/personal-space/). For how teams, roles and invites work, see the [Teams guide](/help/collaboration/teams/).

## Your teams

Each team you are a member of appears as a row with the team glyph and, once it has more than one member, how many it has. Click a team to open its library; expand it with the arrow to walk its folders without leaving the sidebar, and click a folder to open it.

A team's library holds the team's folders and items, from every liveworkspace app, and every member can manage them ([Team Shared Designs](/help/collaboration/teams/team-shared-designs/)). At the top of the team's page sits the team's card, where the team is run in place: who has joined and who is invited, each member with their role, **Invite by Link**, and the ⋯ menu with **Edit Team**, **Leave Team** and **Delete Team** (by role). On a team's library, **Create** offers **New Design** and **New Folder**, so what you make lands in the team.

The editor's Explorer panel (behind the menu button) lists your teams in its rail too, so you can reach team designs without leaving the design you are in.

## New Team

Teams belong to your liveworkspace account, so a team is the same in every liveworkspace app, and you make and run them here in the Explorer. **New Team**, the last row of Spaces, makes one: give it a name and, if you like, an organisation, and the new team opens. You can also make one while moving a design, with the **New Team** tile in **Change Folder**: the design goes straight into it. See [Roles and Invites](/help/collaboration/teams/roles-and-invites/) for who can do what on a team's card.

## Invites

When someone invites you to a team, **Invites** appears in Spaces with the number waiting. Open it to see each invitation: the team, who invited you and when, and the role offered. Answer each one right there: **Accept** opens the team, which then shows up in Spaces, and **Decline** takes the invitation away.

## Signed-in only

Teams need an account, so team rows and **New Team** appear only when you are signed in. Guests see a **Sign in to access Teams** card in their place ("Free, and your guest designs come with you").
