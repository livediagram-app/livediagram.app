Each team you belong to is a space of its own in the Explorer sidebar: a top-level row under **Spaces**, right after your own [My Work](/help/explorer/personal-space/). Expand a team to walk its shared folders. Teams are made and run on [liveworkspace.app](https://liveworkspace.app/account/teams); for how teams and roles work, see the [Teams guide](/help/collaboration/teams/).

## Your teams

Each team you are a member of appears as a row with the team glyph, and a badge showing the member count once the team has more than one person. Click a team to open its page; expand it with the arrow to walk its shared folder tree without leaving the sidebar, and click a folder to open the team page with that folder already showing. A team with no folders yet has no arrow.

Team-shared designs live in [a folder tree every member can manage](/help/collaboration/teams/team-shared-designs/), separate from My Work. The team page holds that library, the member list, and the team's own activity feed, which includes what happened before you joined. Admins see **Manage on liveworkspace.app** at the top, where they invite people, change roles, rename or delete the team.

The editor's Explorer panel (behind the menu button) lists your teams too, so you can reach team designs without leaving the design you are in.

## Manage Teams

**Manage Teams**, the last row of Spaces, opens your teams on liveworkspace.app. Create a team there, invite people by email, and answer invitations sent to you. A team you create or join shows up here within a few minutes, usually at once. To make a team without leaving livedesign, use the **New Team** tile in **Change Folder** on a design's menu: the design goes straight into it.

## Invites

When someone invites you to a team, **Invites** appears in Spaces with the number waiting. Open it to see each invitation: the team, who invited you and when, and the role offered. You answer them on liveworkspace.app: **Answer on liveworkspace.app** opens your teams page there, where you accept or decline. Once you accept, the team shows up in Spaces.

## Signed-in only

Teams need an account, so team rows and Manage Teams appear only when you are signed in. Guests see a **Sign in to access Teams** card in their place ("Free, and your guest designs come with you"). On deployments configured without authentication, neither appears, since teams cannot exist without it.
