The **Inbox** sits under [Home](/help/explorer/timeline/) in the Explorer sidebar's Overview group. It lists what is still _open_ for you, in one place for every liveworkspace app: the [actions](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/) waiting on you, the ones you handed to someone else, and the [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) threads nobody has resolved yet.

## What lands here

The page has up to three sections for your designs, each with a count beside its heading:

- **Assigned to You:** every open action assigned to you, until it is done. An action you assigned to yourself (the **Myself** row in the assign dialog) lives here too, so the page doubles as your to-do list.
- **Assigned by You:** open actions you gave to someone else, so you can follow them up.
- **Open Comment Threads:** unresolved threads you are part of: on your own designs, threads you wrote in, and threads where someone [mentioned you](/help/collaboration/comments/#mentioning-a-teammate). A thread you were mentioned in carries a **Mentioned You** hint, and one on your own design that you have not replied to yet carries a **Your design** hint.

A section with nothing in it is left out. Done actions and resolved threads are not listed: the Inbox is a list of what is outstanding, not a history. When nothing is waiting, the page says **Nothing waiting on you**.

With **Designs** chosen at the top of the page, the Inbox lists only what comes from your designs; with **All**, it lists every app's, and each row names its app. Other apps can add their own kinds of row there, such as a **Form Responses** section.

## Reading and opening a row

Each row names the item it is from and the thing it is about, with the latest change, and on the right the person behind it with how long ago. Click a row and the design opens at that spot, with the action or thread ready, so you can reply, resolve or finish it there. A row from another app opens in that app.

## The badge

The Inbox row in the sidebar shows a count of the open actions **assigned to you**, so you can tell from any other page whether something needs you. Work you handed out and comment threads are not counted.

## Designs saved only in this browser

Designs saved only in This Browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)) show up here too: their open actions and comment threads are listed with everything else, and counted in the badge. Each of their rows carries a **This Browser** pill, because the design is kept in this browser and nowhere else. Nobody else sees those rows, and another browser or device won't list them.

## Guests and signing in

The Inbox works without an account, like the rest of livedesign. When you sign in, what you did as a guest moves to your account with your designs.
