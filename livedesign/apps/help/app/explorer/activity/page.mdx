The **Inbox** sits under [Home](/help/explorer/timeline/) in the Explorer sidebar's Overview group. It lists what is still _open_ for you, in one place for every liveworkspace app: the [actions](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/) waiting on you, the ones you handed to someone else, and the [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) threads nobody has resolved yet.

## What lands here

The Inbox is one list, newest first, under the headings **Today**, **Earlier This Week** and **Older**. It holds:

- every open action assigned to you, until it is done. An action you assigned to yourself (the **Myself** row in the assign dialog) lands here too, so the list doubles as your to-do list;
- the open actions you gave to someone else, so you can follow them up;
- the unresolved comment threads you are part of: on your own designs, threads you wrote in, and threads where someone [mentioned you](/help/collaboration/comments/#mentioning-a-teammate).

Each row says why it is there: **To do** for an action of yours, **Waiting on** and the person's first name for one you handed out, **Mentioned you**, **On your design** for a thread on your design you have not replied to yet, and **Replied to you** or **You replied** for a thread you are in.

Done actions and resolved threads are not listed: the Inbox is a list of what is outstanding, not a history. When nothing is waiting, the page says **You’re all caught up**.

## Filtering

Chips over the list narrow it, each with its count: **All**, **My Actions** (assigned to you), **Assigned by Me**, **Comments** and **Form Responses** (forms with new responses, from apps that have forms). Your choice is kept on this device.

With **Designs** chosen at the top of the page, the Inbox lists only what comes from your designs; with **All Apps**, it lists every app's, and each row names its app.

## Reading and opening a row

Each row names the design it is from and the thing it is about, with the latest change, and on the right the person behind it with how long ago. Choose a row and it opens in the reading pane beside the list, with **Reply**, **Resolve** or **Mark Done**, and **Open in livedesign** to go to that spot in the design (a row from another app opens in that app). On a phone, and in the editor's Explorer panel, the pane takes the list's place, with **Back to Inbox** to return.

With the keyboard, `J` and `K` (or the arrow keys) move through the list, `Enter` opens the row, `R` replies and `E` resolves a thread or marks an action done.

## The badge

The Inbox row in the sidebar shows a count of the open actions **assigned to you**, so you can tell from any other page whether something needs you. Work you handed out and comment threads are not counted.

## Designs saved only in this browser

In the editor's [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/), the Inbox also lists the open actions and comment threads of designs saved only in This Browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)), with everything else. Each of their rows carries a **This Browser** pill, because the design is kept in this browser and nowhere else. Nobody else sees those rows, and another browser or device won't list them. The Explorer page lists only what your account or guest identity holds.

## Guests and signing in

The Inbox works without an account, like the rest of livedesign. When you sign in, what you did as a guest moves to your account with your designs.
