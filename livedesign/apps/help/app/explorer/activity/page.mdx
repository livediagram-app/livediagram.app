The **Inbox** sits under [Home](/help/explorer/timeline/) in the Explorer sidebar's Overview group. It lists what is still _open_ for you, in one place for every liveworkspace app: the [actions](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/) waiting on you, the ones you handed to someone else, and the [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) threads nobody has resolved yet.

## What lands here

The page has up to three sections for your designs, each with a count beside its heading:

- **Assigned to You:** every open action assigned to you, until it is done. An action you assigned to yourself (the **Myself** row in the assign dialog) lives here too, so the page doubles as your to-do list.
- **Assigned by You:** open actions you gave to someone else, so you can follow them up.
- **Open Comment Threads:** unresolved threads you are part of: on your own designs, threads you wrote in, and threads where someone [mentioned you](/help/collaboration/comments/#mentioning-a-teammate). A thread you were mentioned in says **Mentioned you**, one on your own design that you have not replied to yet says **On your design**, and one you replied in says **You replied**.

A section with nothing in it is left out. Done actions and resolved threads are not listed: the Inbox is a list of what is outstanding, not a history. When nothing is waiting, the page says **You’re all caught up**.

With **Designs** chosen at the top of the page, the Inbox lists only what comes from your designs; with **All Apps**, it lists every app's, and each row names its app. Other apps add their own kinds of row there, such as liveplanner's **Form Responses** section.

## Reading and opening a row

Each row names the design it is from and the thing it is about, with the latest change, and on the right the person behind it with how long ago. Click a row and the design opens at that spot, with the action or thread ready, so you can reply, resolve or finish it there. A row from another app opens in that app.

## The badge

The Inbox row in the sidebar shows a count of the open actions **assigned to you**, so you can tell from any other page whether something needs you. Work you handed out and comment threads are not counted.

## Designs saved only in this browser

In the editor's [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/), the Inbox also lists the open actions and comment threads of designs saved only in This Browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)), with everything else. Each of their rows carries a **This Browser** pill, because the design is kept in this browser and nowhere else. Nobody else sees those rows, and another browser or device won't list them. The Explorer page lists only what your account or guest identity holds.

## Guests and signing in

The Inbox works without an account, like the rest of livedesign. When you sign in, what you did as a guest moves to your account with your designs.
