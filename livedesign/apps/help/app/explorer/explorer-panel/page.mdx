The **Explorer panel** is the Explorer inside the editor. Press the **menu button** (☰) in the top-left corner of the canvas and it opens under the button; press the button again, or click anywhere outside, and it closes. It holds the same sections as the [full-page Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), so you can browse your library, catch up on what happened and open another design without leaving the canvas you are on.

## What the panel shows

- **Current Design** at the top is the design you are in: its thumbnail, its name, when it was last saved, its visibility badge (**Private**, **Shared**, **Team** or **This Browser**) at the right, and its **⋯** menu. **Click the name to rename it** in place: Enter saves, Escape keeps the old name. Hover the badge to see who can see the design.
- **The rail** down the left lists every section, grouped as on the Explorer page: **Overview** (Home, Inbox, Timeline, Shared With Me), **Spaces** (My Work and each team) and **More** (Favourites, Library and Trash).
- **The pane** on the right shows the section you pick. Nothing sends you away from the editor: **Home**, the **Inbox**, the **Timeline**, the **Library** (your [Image Gallery](/help/explorer/image-gallery/): upload an image, see which designs use each one, or delete one) and the **Trash** all open right there. **My Work**, each team and **Shared With Me** list their folders and designs, each design with its thumbnail and when it was last updated; a folder opens right there in the pane, with a path above it back to where you started, and picking a design opens it in the editor. **Open Page** at the top of the pane takes that section to the full Explorer, and **New Folder** makes a folder in My Work or the team you are looking at.
- The panel opens on **Home** (the designs you were last in, and what others did since) and then remembers the section you last picked.
- Along the foot are the product's other places: **Home**, **Explorer** and **Help**. Above them, **This Browser** shows how many designs are saved only in this browser and opens their page ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)); it appears only while there is at least one.

It is the same data as the full page: folders, ownership, and sharing all behave identically. Each design has the design menu from its **⋯** button, a right-click, or **Shift+F10**, so **Change Folder** (where **New Team** makes a team on the spot and files the design into it), **Favourite**, **History** (the design's own Timeline) and **Hide from Recent** work here as well. The panel's menu adds **Take Offline**, which moves a design into this browser; **Sync Design** moves one back to your account. A design saved only in this browser carries the **This Browser** pill ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)). Drag one of your own designs onto one of your folders to file it, or onto **My Work** in the rail to put it back at the root.

The keyboard works as on the Explorer page: in the rail, **Up** and **Down** move between sections and **Enter** picks one; in the pane, **Up** and **Down** move between rows and **Enter** opens one. Teams are made and run on liveworkspace.app; **Manage Teams** in the full Explorer takes you there.

The **⋯** button in the panel's header, beside the help button, opens a menu with **New Design** and **Open Explorer** (the full-page library), then **Share** and **Export** for the design you are in, then **Search**, **GitHub** (the open-source code), **Licences** and **Settings**. With [power user mode](/help/user-interface/power-user-mode/)'s Minimal chrome on, the help button moves into this menu as **Help**.

## When you will see it

- **In the editor**, to jump to another design without going back to the library. [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) hides the menu button with the rest of the chrome.
- **On a phone**, the menu button sits in its own card at the left of the palette strip, and the panel opens under it across the screen: the sections become a row of chips you swipe sideways, with the picked one's pane beneath. Held sideways, a phone has no menu button and no Explorer; turn it upright to open the Explorer.
- **When a design can't be opened**, it stays available beside the error screen, so you can pick another design.

Like everywhere else in the Explorer, the panel works whether or not you are signed in, showing the designs your browser or account owns.
