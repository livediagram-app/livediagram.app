A **Flowchart** starts as one **Start** step and grows from there. It lives in the palette's **Diagrams** category in Diagram mode. Every time you add, remove or change a step, the whole chart is laid out again, so it stays clean and tidy without you ever dragging a box into line.

## Take the tour

The first time you select a flowchart step, a small **Show Me** toolbar appears beside its toolbar. Press **Play** for a short walkthrough on your own chart: **Add Step** and **Add Branch** (and the Tab and Enter keys), **Step Type**, the **Lane** menu on a chart with swimlanes, and the **Flowchart** menu. Press the cross if you would rather not; either way it is not offered again. To be offered it again, open **Settings**, then **Tours**, open **Show Me** and switch **Flowchart** back on.

## Growing a chart

<Step n={2}>
  Press **Tab** to add the **next step**, connected from the one you are on, then start typing. The
  new step is already selected, so your first key starts its label.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Press **Enter** to add a **parallel step**: another step from the same step the one you are on
  came from, laid out beside it.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Press **Escape** when you are done. What you typed is kept; a new step you left empty is removed,
  so a Tab pressed one time too many costs nothing.
</Step>

The keys work from inside a step's label too, so you can type a whole process without stopping. Press **Shift+Enter** for a new line in a label.

With the mouse or on a touch screen, select a step and use **Add Step** and **Add Branch** on its toolbar, or the quick-add **+** on each side of it.

## Step types

New steps are **Process** steps. To change one, select it and pick from **Step Type** on its toolbar, or right-click it and open **Flowchart**, then **Step Type**. The types are the classic flowchart symbols:

- **Start / End**: where the flow begins or finishes.
- **Process**: a step someone or something does.
- **Decision**: a question. Its first exit is labelled **Yes** and its second **No**; edit the labels to say anything you like.
- **Input / Output**: data entering or leaving the flow.
- **Document**: a document the flow produces or reads.
- **Database**: stored data.
- **Preparation**: setting up before a step.
- **Connector**: an on-page jump, labelled with a letter.

## Joining steps and loops

To join two steps that already exist, draw an arrow from one to the other, as you would between any two shapes. The chart is laid out again with the new connector: two branches can **merge** into one step, and an arrow back to an earlier step makes a **loop**, which is drawn round the outside of the chart so it never crosses the steps in between.

## Always tidy

You can drag a step wherever you like, but the next change to the chart puts it back in its place: a flowchart is always laid out for you. To lay it out on demand, right-click a step, open **Flowchart**, then **Chart**, and choose **Tidy Flowchart**. The first step never moves, so the chart grows from where you started it.

## Direction

A flowchart flows **Down** unless it has swimlanes, when it flows **Right**. To change it, right-click a step, open **Flowchart**, then **Direction**, and pick one. The whole chart turns, lanes included.

## Swimlanes

Swimlanes are optional. Choose **Add Lane** under **Chart** in the **Flowchart** menu: a chart without lanes gets two, with every step in the first, and each further **Add Lane** adds one more. Rename a lane by editing its title.

A new step lands in the same lane as the step it grew from. To move a step to another lane, pick the lane from **Lane** on its toolbar, or drag it into the lane. To reorder lanes, drag one past another. Deleting a lane moves its steps into the first lane.

## Templates

The **Flowchart**, **Decision Tree**, **Swimlane** and **Approval Workflow** templates are flowcharts too, so you can select any of their steps and keep growing them with Tab and Enter.

## Who can do what

On a shared board, an **Editor** can do everything here. A **Participant** can grow a chart with Tab, Enter, Add Step and Add Branch, and write on its steps, but not change step types, the direction or the lanes. A **Viewer** can only look.
