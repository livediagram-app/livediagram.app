The **Library** keeps the things you reach for again and again: a logo lock-up, a hero header, a button style, a set of icons. Save it once and it is in your palette in every design, and in the other liveworkspace apps that can draw it. You have a **Personal** library, and every team you are in has one too, so a team shares one set of brand pieces.

## Saving to the Library

<Step n={1}>
  Select the elements you want to keep. One element, a group, or a box drawn around several all
  work.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>Press **Save to Library** on the selection toolbar, beside **Duplicate**.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Give the item a **Name**, and under **Save to** pick **Personal** (only you) or one of your teams
  (everyone in the team can place it). A **Folder** is optional: type a new one, or pick one the
  library already has. Press **Save**.
</Step>

<Figure caption="Save to Library: name the item, pick Personal or a team, and file it in a folder if you like.">
  <SaveToLibraryScene />
</Figure>

The item keeps its elements exactly as they were, pictures included. A design kept only in [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) has no Library to save to until it is saved to your account.

## Placing from the Library

Press the **Library** button on the palette strip, just before Search. It lists your Personal items first, then each team's, in their folders. **Search the library** narrows the list by name.

- **Click** a tile to place a copy in the middle of your view.
- **Drag** a tile onto the page to place it where you drop it.

<Figure caption="The Library on the palette strip: a search, a heading per library and folder, and a tile dragged onto the page.">
  <LibraryPanelScene />
</Figure>

What you place is a copy, with no live link back to the Library: changing or deleting the item later leaves every design that already uses it as it is. For a piece that should update everywhere it appears in one design, make it a [component](/help/palette/components/) instead.

An item saved in another app shows here only if livedesign can draw it. When some can't, a line under the list says how many.

## Tidying the Library

Hover a tile and press its **⋯** for **Rename** and **Delete**, and use **Rename Folder** on a folder's heading to rename it for everything in it. Deleting asks first, and anything already placed keeps what it has.

Your Personal library is yours to tidy. In a team's library every member can save and place, but only the team's Admins rename and delete, so members see no **⋯** on its tiles.

## Who has it

The Library is part of editing, so it is there for Editors: someone on a Participant link has the Participate tiles only, and a Viewer has no palette. A team's library is there only for the people in that team.
