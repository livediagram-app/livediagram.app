livedesign has an [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) server, so you can connect it to an AI tool you already use, like Claude, and then find, view, create, and edit your designs from inside that tool. Ask it for a set of app screens, a logo to start from, or a tweak to an existing page, and the result is saved to your account.

## You need an account

Connecting an AI tool is a **signed-in-only** feature, the same as [API tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) and [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/). The editor, sharing, and real-time collaboration stay account-free for guests, but driving your designs programmatically is an advanced, opt-in capability that needs an account.

## Connecting

In your AI tool, add a connector (an MCP server) pointing at **`https://mcp.livedesign.app/mcp`**. [Connecting AI Tools](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/ai-clients/) has the steps for Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT and others. The first time, your tool opens a livedesign page titled **Connect** followed by the tool's name (you'll sign in first if you aren't already). It says which site the access will be sent to: only continue if you recognise it. Choose **Connect** to approve, or **Cancel**.

Approving creates an [API token](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) behind the scenes and hands it to your tool. From then on the connection just works. The token is an ordinary API token: it appears with your other tokens on [liveworkspace.app](https://liveworkspace.app/explorer?settings=tokens), named after the tool, and you can revoke it there any time to disconnect the tool. Like all tokens, it lasts six months; reconnecting the tool then creates a fresh one.

## What the AI can do

The connection gives your AI tool these tools:

- **Find designs**: search your designs by name, across your personal library and the shared libraries of every [team](/help/collaboration/teams/) you belong to.
- **Read a design**: get a tab's contents as a text outline, and a picture of it when the AI asks for one.
- **List templates**: browse the same hand-tuned [template library](/help/canvas/templates/) the editor's template starts offer.
- **Create a design**: turn a request into a new design, with one tab or several. A tab can start from a template: ask for "a mobile app wireframe" and the AI can lay down the ready-made screens, then fill in your content.
- **Add a tab**: add another tab (its own row of pages) to an existing design, for example a detail view of one part. Tabs can start from a template too.
- **Update a design**: rework a whole tab or make a small adjustment.
- **Share a design**: turn a design into a [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) to hand to someone. Unless you ask for another level, the AI makes a **Participant** link: people can add stickies, text and pictures, but not reshape the pages.
- **Rename a design** and **Delete a design**: tidy your library from the AI tool, a whole design or a single tab. Deleting a design moves it to the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/); deleting a tab removes it for good.
- **List the Trash** and **Restore a design**: bring back something deleted by mistake, by you or by the AI.
- **Change pages**: make [Pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/) pages. Ask for a logo and the AI adds a logo artboard; ask for a screen and it adds a wireframe page; ask for a photo and it adds a photo page.
- **Search icons** and **List images**: find an [icon](/help/palette/icons/) by what it shows, and find a picture you uploaded to your [image gallery](/help/explorer/image-gallery/), so the AI can put them on a page.

Beyond plain boxes and arrows, the AI can fill in **sticky notes** (in the sticky colours), **tables**, **code blocks**, **bar, line, and pie charts**, and **Sankey diagrams** (from a list of flows: what moves from where to where, and how much). Your AI tool may also surface a ready-made **prompt** ("Show my design") as a quick action, so you don't have to know the tool names.

When the AI changes a design you have open, you see it happen: the change arrives on your canvas straight away, outlined for a moment in your colour, and a note at the bottom says what changed. **Show** takes you to it and **Undo** puts it back (your own Undo shortcut only ever undoes your own edits). Anything you change at the same time is kept. To review or undo a change after its note has gone, see [Reviewing and Undoing an Agent's Changes](/help/developers/agent-changes/). An element someone else in the design has selected is off limits: a change that touches it is refused, and the AI is told to leave it out or try again later.

The AI does the thinking; livedesign validates, lays out, saves, and renders what it produces. The MCP server runs no AI model of its own. New designs an AI creates land at the root of **My Work** (or your default folder, if you set one), each with a **Made by AI** badge; the **Made by AI** filter lists them all, wherever you file them.

## What a connection can and can't touch

By default the token behind a connection has full read and write access to your designs, including the designs in your teams' shared libraries, so only connect tools you trust, and revoke the token if you stop using one. If you'd rather a tool could look but not change anything, switch on **Read-only access** on the approval page when you connect it: the connection can then find and read your designs (and list your Trash) but can't create, edit, share, rename, delete or restore anything. The block is enforced by our server, not just the tool. Its token shows a **Read-only** badge in the [API Tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) list.

Either way, a connection can't manage your teams or your account: inviting members, changing roles, and deleting things other than designs stay with you, signed in. It can never delete a design for good either; that stays with you, in the Trash. (A tab it deletes is gone for good.)

Approving a connection creates a token, so it fails if you already have 20 tokens that haven't expired; revoke one on liveworkspace.app first.
