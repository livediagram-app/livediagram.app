Your documents are not locked inside livedesign. When you want a copy to print, attach, or drop into a chat, you can export a tab's pages as a PDF, as images or as an animated GIF, or keep the tab as a file you can import back. And when you want the document to stay live wherever you put it, you can embed it instead.

## Exporting a tab

Export works on one tab at a time, so switch to the tab you want first. Then either:

- open the tab's **⋯** menu and choose **Export**; or
- open the Explorer panel's menu and choose **Export**, which exports the tab you are on.

  <ExportPagePickerScene />
</Figure>

Pick a format, then **All pages** or **One page**:

- **PDF** to print or attach: every page in one PDF, in order, each at its own size, so an A4 page comes out a true A4 page. Or just the page you pick.
- **PNG** or **SVG** for a picture: PNG to paste into docs and chat, SVG to stay sharp at any size. One page gives you that page's image; all pages give you a **.zip** with one image per page.
- **GIF** for an animated image that plays anywhere a picture does, with everything that moves on a page moving in it.
- **JSON** for a `.livedesign-tab.json` file that [imports](/help/tabs/import-tabs/) back into livedesign with everything intact. This is the one to keep as a backup.

A tab with a [logo page](/help/pages/logo-pages/) also offers **Logo Kit**: the logo as an SVG, PNGs from 16 to 1024 pixels, and a favicon, in one .zip.

Each page exports exactly its sheet: its size, its background and what is on it, cut off at its edges just as the editor shows it. [Exporting and Presenting Pages](/help/pages/exporting-pages/) covers every format and option in detail.

An export is the right choice when you need a fixed snapshot: a handout, a report, an attachment, or a printout.

## Embedding a live view

When you want the document to keep updating wherever it lives, [embed it](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) instead. An embed is an iframe of a shared document that you can drop into Notion, a wiki, or any page that accepts an iframe.

An embed follows the role of the pass it was made from: a **Viewer** or **Participant** pass embeds a read-only view, an **Editor** pass an editable one. It hides the editor's controls, leaving your pages with pan and zoom, and it stays live: as the document is edited, the embed updates in place rather than showing a stale snapshot.

To grab one, open the **Share** dialog, find the pass you want, open its **Embed** menu and choose **Copy iframe** for a ready-made snippet you can paste straight into your page, or **Copy embed URL** for the address alone. A document in This Browser has no passes, so [sync it](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) first.

<Tip>
  Reach for an image when you need a fixed snapshot, and an embed when you want the document to keep
  updating in place wherever you have put it. For a picture that updates itself, see [Live
  Image](/help/collaboration/sharing/live-image/).
</Tip>
