A finished tab of pages shows everything at once, which is great for reference but harder to narrate. livedesign gives you a few ways to focus an audience as you walk through your work, whether you are on a call, a projector, or sharing your screen.

## Zen mode

When you want the full canvas with nothing else competing for attention, [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) hides every part of the interface, the header, tab bar, palette, and panels, leaving only the canvas and zoom controls (plus a Following banner if you are following someone). It is purely a view setting: it changes nothing on the canvas and is not shared with other people, so each viewer focuses independently (view-only visitors can use it too). Turn it on with `Z` or **Zen** in the Palette's selection-mode dropdown (view-only visitors, who have no palette, get a **Zen mode** button on the zoom controls), and leave with `Z`, `Escape`, or the **Exit zen mode** button on the zoom controls.

## The laser pointer

For pointing things out live, switch to the **Laser** tool (`K`). Moving the pointer leaves a trail, in your participant colour by default, that fades after about a second, and on a shared session other people see your laser in real time, so you can sweep across the canvas and direct the room's attention. Its panel sets the pen: width, colour, how long the trail lasts (Quick, Normal or Long) and its effect. See [Laser](/help/selection-modes/laser/).

## The spotlight

To focus on one part of the canvas at a time, switch to the [Spotlight](/help/selection-modes/spotlight/) tool (desktop only). It dims the rest of the canvas and casts a pool of light around your cursor, so the room's eye lands exactly where yours does. Unlike the laser, it is **only on your screen**: everyone else in the design still sees the whole canvas. It shines when you are sharing your screen or showing your work in the room; move the cursor from shape to shape to walk through the canvas one piece at a time. Click to grow the light and right-click to shrink it. Its panel sets the light's size, how far the rest dims, its edge and its shape.

## Follow along

When the people you are talking to are in the design with you, rather than watching your shared screen, have them follow you. [Follow Along](/help/canvas/follow-along/) keeps their view on yours as you pan and zoom from page to page, so nobody gets lost, and your laser trail shows on their screens as you point.

<Tip>
  Reveal a busy design gradually by building it up page by page, then switch to Zen mode and use the
