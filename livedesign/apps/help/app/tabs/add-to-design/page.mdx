Sometimes a tab you built in one design belongs in another too: a glossary, a shared timeline, a set of slides two projects both rely on. **Add to Design** copies the active tab into another design you own, without leaving the editor.

## Adding a tab to another design

<Step n={1}>Open the tab's menu (the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click the tab).</Step>
<Step n={2}>Choose **Add to Design**.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  In the **Add to Design** dialog, pick the destination. Each design shows as a tile with a
  preview of its canvas, and the filter box at the top narrows a long list. A message confirms when
  the tab has been added.
</Step>

The option is greyed out when there is nowhere to add the tab to: if this is your only design, until you create another, and in a [design in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/). It isn't offered to Viewers or Participants, or on a link shared for [one tab](/help/collaboration/sharing/one-tab/).

<Figure caption="Add to Design opens a dialog of your other designs, each with a preview of its canvas, and a filter box to narrow the list.">

</Figure>

## A copy, not a shared tab

Add to Design puts a copy of the tab in the other design, as it is at that moment. From then on the two tabs are separate: edit, rename or delete either one and the other stays as it was. In the other design the copy sits outside any [tab folder](/help/tabs/tab-folders/).

<Tip>
  Want a second copy in the same design instead? Use **Duplicate** in the same menu.
</Tip>
