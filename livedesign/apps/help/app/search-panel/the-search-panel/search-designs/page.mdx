Wherever you open the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/), it covers your own library, so you never have to remember which folder a design is in. Matching is on the name, and any part of it will do: `brief` finds "Project brief" and "Debriefing".

## In the editor

The **Designs** group matches your livedesign designs by name (a design you never named is listed as "Untitled design"), the ones in your teams' libraries included. Pick one and it opens, the quickest way back into a design without scrolling the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/). It searches your 100 most recently changed designs, read in the background once the open design has loaded.

Once you type, an **Every App** group lists the rest of what you can open that matches: a design [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) through a link, an older design, or work in another liveworkspace app, each marked with its app. Picking one opens it, a shared design on its share link with whatever access the owner granted.

Folders are not searched from the editor: open search on the Explorer page to find a folder.

## On the Explorer page

The Explorer's search asks liveworkspace's index, so it reaches everything you can open:

- **Items**: your designs and everything shared with you or in your teams, in every app (or just this app's, when the Explorer is showing only livedesign). An archived design says **Archived**, and picking it opens the Archived view on it.
- **Folders**: the folders in your [folder tree](/help/explorer/folders/) and your teams', a team's marked "in" its name. Picking one opens it.

## Limits

Each group lists up to 8 matches. If what you want is not among them, type a little more of its name. With an empty box the editor lists your designs (and, in a design, its tabs and elements), so you can open it and arrow straight down to one; the Explorer page lists nothing until you type.

<Tip>
  Search is owner-scoped: a guest finds what their own browser owns, and what it has opened through
  share links; signing in brings your account's designs into scope. Designs kept in [This
  Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) are not searched. Search only looks at names:
  it does not look inside other designs, so to find a word on a canvas, open that design and search
  there.
</Tip>
