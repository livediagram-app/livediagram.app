Wherever you open the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/), it always covers your own library, so you never have to remember which folder a design is in. Matching is on the name only, and any part of it will do: `brief` finds "Project brief" and "Debriefing".

## Designs

The **Designs** group matches your own designs by name (a design you never named is listed as "Untitled design"). Pick one and it opens, the quickest way back into a design without scrolling the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/). This group is in scope from anywhere: the editor and the Explorer page alike.

A design saved only in this browser (see [Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)) carries a **This Browser** label in the results, so you can tell it apart from one saved to your account.

Designs in a team's library are not in this group: they are listed under **Teams**, tagged with their team. See [Searching Teams](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-teams/).

## Shared with You

Once you have opened someone else's design through a share link, a **Shared with You** group matches it too, marked with a share icon. (It is the same list as [Shared With Me](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) in the Explorer, so a link you were sent but never opened is not in it yet.) Picking one opens it on its share link, with whatever access the owner granted. This keeps shared work findable alongside your own, without it cluttering your folders. If the owner turns sharing off, the link expires, or the design goes to the Trash, it drops out of the results.

## My Work (your folders)

The **My Work** group matches the folders in your personal [folder tree](/help/explorer/folders/) by name. Picking a folder opens it on the Explorer page (from the editor, search takes you there), so search doubles as a way to jump around your folder tree without expanding it by hand. Team folders are listed under **Teams** instead.

## Limits

Each group lists up to 8 matches. If what you want is not among them, type a little more of its name. With an empty box the panel lists your designs, shared designs, folders and teams (and, in a design, its tabs and elements), so you can open it and arrow straight down to one.

<Tip>
  Search is owner-scoped: a guest finds the designs their own browser owns, and the ones it has
  opened through share links; signing in brings your account's designs into scope. Search only
  looks at names: it does not look inside other designs, so to find a word on a canvas, open that
  design and search there.
</Tip>
