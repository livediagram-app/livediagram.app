The **search panel** is the fastest way to get anywhere in livedesign. It is one box that searches across everything you can reach and, when you are in the editor, can act on the canvas too. The scope adapts to where you are, so the same panel finds a design from the Explorer and an element on the canvas.

## Opening and closing search

- **Keyboard:** press `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `K`, or `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `.`. Both open the same panel. (`K` is the usual command-palette shortcut; `.` is the original binding, kept because browsers reserve `Cmd` + `T` for a new browser tab.) They work in the editor and on the Explorer page alike, including in [zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) and in view-only designs. While you are typing in a text box (such as the Explorer's filter box) or a dialog is open, they are left alone. In the editor they are off when you turn off **Keyboard Shortcuts** in Settings.
- **Button:** in the editor, open **Tools** near the right-hand end of the bottom bar and choose **Search…**, its first row. On the Explorer page it is the **Search** button in the bottom bar. On a phone each button is its icon alone.
- **Explorer panel menu:** inside the editor, the Explorer panel's **⋯** menu also has a **Search** row.

The panel pops up near the top of the screen and blurs what is behind it so you can focus on the query. Press `Esc`, click the **Esc** chip at the end of the search box, or click outside the panel to close it.

## What search covers

Type a few letters and matches appear, grouped by what they are. Matching is a simple, case-insensitive "contains" match on the name, so `flow` finds "Checkout flow" and "Workflow". What is in scope depends on where you open it:

</Figure>

**In the editor**, the groups come in this order:

- **[Designs](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-designs/)**: your livedesign designs, and your teams', by name.
- **[Teams](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-teams/)**: the teams you belong to, when you are signed in.
- **Every App**: once you type, what you can open anywhere in liveworkspace that the groups above miss, such as a design shared with you or work in another app. Each is marked with its app's logo and name in the app's colour, and picking one opens it in its own app.
- **[Tabs and Elements](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-tabs-and-elements/)** of the current design, including text inside table cells.
- Then **Actions**, **Add to canvas**, **Settings** and **Help** (see below).

With an empty box, the panel lists what it can browse (your designs, teams, and the tabs and labelled elements of this design), so you can open it and arrow straight to something. Most groups show at most 8 results (12 for Elements, 10 for Actions and Add to canvas, 6 for Settings and Help), so keep typing to narrow a long list.

**On the Explorer page**, the panel searches everything you can open: **Items** (your work in every liveworkspace app, including what is shared with you and your teams' work), **Folders** (yours and your teams', a team's marked "in" its name), **Teams**, **Help** (this help centre's Explorer articles) and, when you are signed in, **Settings**. Nothing is listed until you type, and each group shows at most 8 results.

Designs kept in [This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) are not searched in either place: find them under **This Browser** in the Explorer.

## Beyond navigation

In the editor the panel does more than jump around:

- **[Add to the canvas](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/):** search the palette and drop a shape or icon straight onto the canvas.
- **[Create a new tab](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-create-tab/):** an action result that spins up a fresh tab.
- **[Run any command](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/):** type an action's name (undo, auto layout, export, settings) and run it, or pick up a canvas tool. The panel doubles as a command palette on `⌘K`.

These three only appear once you have typed something, and only inside a design. Two more groups follow them:

- **Jump to a setting:** in the editor, a **Settings** group matches the rows of the [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) dialog, by name and by the words people use for them, so "dark mode" finds **Theme**. Each result says which category it lives in ("in Appearance"), and picking it opens Settings on that row with the row highlighted. On the Explorer page, signed in, it lists the sections of your account's Settings and each app's settings instead.
- **Find the help for it:** a **Help** group lists matching articles from this help centre, so the explanation of a feature is reachable from the same box as the feature. In the editor articles match on their search synonyms too, so "transparency" finds Opacity; on the Explorer page it offers the Explorer's own articles. Picking one opens the article in a new browser tab, so you do not lose your place.

The Help group always sits last, so a help article never steals the `Enter` from somewhere you were trying to go.

## Picking a result

Results are ordered by group, and the very first match is preselected, so for the common case you can just type and press `Enter`. Otherwise click a row, or press the `↑` and `↓` arrow keys to move the highlight and `Enter` to pick it. Picking a result takes you exactly where it points: opening a design or folder, switching to a tab, selecting an element, or jumping to a team. The panel closes as it goes.

## Read-only views

Search only navigates, so it works in a view-only design too: you can still find and jump to tabs and elements. **Add to canvas** results are not offered there, and the command palette keeps only the actions that do not change the design. See [The Command Palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/#in-read-only-views).
