livedesign is a web app where people design **pages together in real time**: logos, wireframes and photos. Anyone with access to a design can join, see other collaborators' cursors and edits live, and contribute at the same time. The result is something finished to share, a logo, a set of screens or an edited photo, that a team builds together rather than one person authoring and the rest reviewing.

## A design of pages

A design holds [tabs](/help/tabs/using-tabs/), and every tab is a row of **pages**. Each page is one of a few kinds, chosen when you make it. See [Working with Pages](/help/pages/working-with-pages/).

<Figure caption="Pages in a row on a tab, each labelled above its corner, with the buttons to add another after the last.">
  <PagesScene />
</Figure>

- **Logos** on a square artboard, with guides, mirror drawing and a logo kit export. See [Logo Pages](/help/pages/logo-pages/).
- **Wireframes**, each screen framed by the device it is for. See [Wireframe Pages](/help/pages/wireframe-pages/).
- **Photos**, edited in layers with brushes, selections, adjustments and filters. See [Photo Pages](/help/pages/photo-pages/).

You build a page from the [palette](/help/palette/): text, shapes, icons, pictures, charts, ready-made components and more. [Templates](/help/canvas/templates/) start a tab from ready-made pages, so you rarely begin from a blank one.

## Presenting, exporting and AI

- **Present** your pages full screen as a deck, with the [laser](/help/selection-modes/laser/) and the [spotlight](/help/selection-modes/spotlight/) to point things out. See [Presenting](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/).
- **Export** pages as a PDF, PNG or SVG, a logo as a logo kit, and a photo as a PNG, JPEG, WebP or layered PSD. See [Exporting and Presenting Pages](/help/pages/exporting-pages/).
- **AI**, where the people running livedesign have set it up, works alongside you on a tab, and AI tools can work on your designs over MCP. See [AI Assistance](/help/tools/ai/) and [Connect an AI Tool](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/).

## Built for working together

When more than one person is in the same design, everyone sees live cursors, who has what selected, and every change as it happens. An element someone else has selected is locked for everyone else, so collaborators rarely overwrite each other. [Comments](/help/collaboration/comments/), [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/) and [sharing](/help/collaboration/sharing/) are built in.

To make that frictionless, the editor **always works without signing in**. You can open a new design, build something real, and hand the link to a colleague who edits it straight away, no account required. See [Sharing your design](/help/getting-started/sharing-your-design/).

## Free and open source

livedesign is **MIT-licensed** and **free for everyone**. There is no paid tier and no plan to introduce one. A free hosted version runs at livedesign.app, and because the whole codebase is open, anyone can self-host it on their own infrastructure. Every feature is available to every user. See [Why use livedesign?](/help/about/why-livedesign/).
