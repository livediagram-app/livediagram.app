livedesign is for teams who design things and need to make them **together**. The product shines when several people are on the same design at once, building one set of pages in [real time](/help/collaboration/sharing/). That said, it works just as well for one person sketching a mark on their own, and the [editor always works without signing in](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## The teams it serves

A few groups feel right at home:

- **Brand and marketing teams** drawing marks, wordmarks and lockups on [logo pages](/help/pages/logo-pages/), then [exporting](/help/pages/exporting-pages/) them as PNG, SVG or PDF.
- **Product and design teams** sketching screens on [wireframe pages](/help/pages/wireframe-pages/), each framed by its device, with [comments](/help/collaboration/comments/) on the details.
- **People presenting** a design: every page is a slide, [presented](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/) full screen with the laser and spotlight.

## Whether you sign in or not

livedesign is friction-free on purpose. A first-time visitor can build a real design as a [guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/) and only later create an account to sync across devices and join [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/). A signed-in user can still hand a share link to a guest who edits without any account at all.
