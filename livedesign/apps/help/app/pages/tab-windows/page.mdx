A **Tab Window** shows another tab of the same design, live, inside a page. Build a logo, a screen or a whole set of pages on its own tab, put a window onto it on another page, and that page follows every change you (or a collaborator) make to that tab. There is no copy to paste again when it changes.

## Take the tour

Select a tab window and press Start Tab Window Tour in the bar at the bottom of the editor, just left of **Search**, for a short walkthrough on your own window: the tab it shows live, Open (or Choose a Tab on an empty window), its menu, and resizing it. The button is there whenever one is selected, so you can take the tour again any time (it isn't offered on a phone).

## Adding a Tab Window

<Step n={1}>
  Open **Media** in the palette and pick **Tab Window**. Tap the page to drop one, or drag to draw
  it at the size you want.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Press **Choose a Tab** in the window and pick the tab to show. The list holds every tab of the
  design but the one you are on.
</Step>

The window draws that tab's content **zoomed to fit**, centred on the tab's own background. Resize the window and the drawing follows.

The tab draws as its pages (logos, wireframes and photos, with their backgrounds).

Animations play in the window too: a box that bounces, a sticky note that flutters and an arrow whose flow runs all move inside the window as they do on their own tab. A few effects that need the canvas itself (a pulse ring, a trace light, words that type themselves in) show still in the window. With **Reduce Motion** on, the window holds still, like the canvas.

To show a different tab later, open the window's menu, then **Tab Window**, and pick another under **Shows**.

## Opening the Tab

Hover over a window (or select it) and press **Open** in its top-right corner to go to the tab it shows. The menu's **Tab Window** section has the same **Open** row.

## What a Window Shows While It Waits

- **Loading Flow…** while the tab's content is on its way (a tab's content arrives when it is first shown).
- **Couldn't load Flow.** with **Retry** if it didn't arrive.
- **Nothing on Flow yet.** for a tab with nothing on it.
- **This tab isn't available.** when the tab has been deleted, or isn't shared with you.

A window inside the tab it shows is drawn as a frame with its tab's name, never the tab again, so windows never repeat inside each other.

## Exporting

A PDF, PNG or SVG export draws each window's tab inside it, just as on screen (see [Exporting Pages](/help/pages/exporting-pages/)). Thumbnails, such as the Contents list, show the window as a frame named for its tab.

## Who Can Do What

An Editor places windows and chooses what they show. A Participant or Viewer sees them and can open the tab a window shows.
