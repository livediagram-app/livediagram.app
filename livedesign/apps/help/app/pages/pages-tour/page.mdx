The Pages tour shows you how each kind of page is made, on your own page. There is one tour for each kind: **wireframes**, **logos** and **photos**. It works on the real editor rather than screenshots, and it opens the real controls for you: the Layout and Background popovers and the page's settings. Anything you pick while a step is open is kept. It takes under a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## Starting the Tour

<Step n={1}>
  Open a tab in Pages that you can edit, with at least one page whose kind you've chosen.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Press **Get Started** in the bar at the bottom of the editor, just left of **Tools**, and pick
  **Pages Tour**. On a phone the button shows as a play button on its own.
</Step>

The tour starts straight away, on the page you can see most of. If several kinds of page are on screen, a kind you haven't taken the tour of yet goes first.

The Pages Tour waits while **Start Making Pages** is open and while a new tab's first page asks **What Is This Page For?**. It isn't offered on a locked tab, in an embed, or to someone who can only view or take part. Get Started lists every tour on offer, so while you have an element selected that has its own tour (a chart, a Sankey, a stack), that tour comes first and the Pages Tour sits under it.

The tour never pops up on its own. Once you've taken the tour of every kind of page on the tab, it leaves Get Started (and the button goes when no other tour is on offer); switch a kind back on in **Settings** to take it again (see below).

## What Each Tour Covers

**Wireframes**: the page's name, **Start From a Layout**, **Change the Background**, **Device** (the page's settings) and **Add a Page**. See [Wireframe Pages](/help/pages/wireframe-pages/).

**Logos**: the logo layouts, guides, mirror drawing and combining shapes, as the logo page's Show Me runs them. See [Logo Pages](/help/pages/logo-pages/).

**Photos**: the photo tools along the palette, the page's menus and the **Photo** panel with its layers. See [Photo Pages](/help/pages/photo-pages/).

A step whose control isn't on screen when the tour starts is left out, so the count stays honest. Each step has **Next**, **Back** and **Skip tour**; leaving a step closes whatever it opened. If you switch tab, or delete or lock the page mid-tour, it ends as skipped.

## Show Me on Any Page

The settings (the cog) of every logo, wireframe and photo page carry **Show Me** at the top, which runs that page's tour there and then, whenever you like, even after you've taken it. A locked page, or a first page that hasn't chosen its kind yet, has no Show Me.

## Taken Tours in Settings

**Settings**, under **Getting Started**, has a switch for each kind of page (**Wireframe Tour**, **Logo Tour** and **Photo Tour**) under **Pages Tours**, which switches them all. Each switch turns itself off as soon as you start that kind's tour, and if you're signed in that follows you to every device. The Pages Tour prefers a kind you haven't taken yet, and leaves Get Started once every kind of page on the tab has been taken, until you switch one back on.

Switch one back on and close Settings while you're in Pages, and the tour starts straight away.
