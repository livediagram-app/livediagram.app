The Pages tour shows you how each kind of page is made, on your own page. There is one tour for each kind: **wireframes**, **logos** and **photos**. It works on the real editor rather than screenshots, and it opens the real controls for you: the Layout and Background popovers and the page's settings. Anything you pick while a step is open is kept. It takes under a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## Starting the Tour

<Step n={1}>
  Open a tab in Pages that you can edit, with at least one page whose kind you've chosen.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Press Start Pages Tour in the bar at the bottom of the editor, just left of **Settings**. On a
  phone it shows as a play button on its own.
</Step>

The tour starts straight away, on the page you can see most of. If several kinds of page are on screen, a kind you haven't taken the tour of yet goes first.

The button waits while **Start Making Pages** is open and while a new tab's first page asks **What Is This Page For?**. It isn't there on a locked tab, in an embed, or for someone who can only view or take part. While you have an element selected that has its own tour (a chart, a Sankey, a stack), the button offers that tour instead; select nothing to get the Pages tour back.

The tour never pops up on its own, and the button stays after you've taken the tour, so you can run it again whenever you like.

## What Each Tour Covers

**Wireframes**: the page's name, **Start From a Layout**, **Change the Background**, **Device** (the page's settings) and **Add a Page**. See [Wireframe Pages](/help/pages/wireframe-pages/).

**Logos**: the logo layouts, guides, mirror drawing and combining shapes, as the logo page's Show Me runs them. See [Logo Pages](/help/pages/logo-pages/).

**Photos**: the photo tools along the palette, the page's menus and the **Photo** panel with its layers. See [Photo Pages](/help/pages/photo-pages/).

A step whose control isn't on screen when the tour starts is left out, so the count stays honest. Each step has **Next**, **Back** and **Skip tour**; leaving a step closes whatever it opened. If you switch tab, or delete or lock the page mid-tour, it ends as skipped.

## Show Me on Any Page

Every page's settings (its cog) carry **Show Me** at the top, which runs that page's tour there and then, whenever you like.

## Taken Tours in Settings

**Settings**, under **Tours**, has a switch for each kind of page (**Wireframe Tour**, **Logo Tour** and **Photo Tour**) under **Pages Tours**, which switches them all. Each switch turns itself off once you finish or skip that kind's tour, and if you're signed in that follows you to every device. Turning on **Power User Mode** marks them all as taken. The switches don't hide the Start Pages Tour button.

Switch one back on and close Settings while you're in Pages, and the tour starts straight away.
