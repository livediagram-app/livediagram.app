Most documents are saved to livediagram's servers so you can reach them from any device and share them. **Offline Mode** is the opposite choice: a document saved **only in the browser you made it in**, that never touches the server at all. It suits private, local-first work, or simply wanting a document that physically never leaves your machine.

## Creating an offline document

Offline Mode is off by default and chosen per document, so you can keep normal documents and offline ones side by side.

- On the [New Document](/new) screen, pick a template, then go on to the **Location** step and choose the **Local Browser** tile ("This device only") as the save location, then click **Create**. A warning under it reminds you that the document is not backed up or synced.
- The document is written to your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) and opens like any other. A **Local only** badge next to its name in the editor, and the matching **Local only** pill wherever it is listed (Explorer lists and cards, search results, the Explorer panel and the Trash), mark it so you always know which documents are local-only. Hover or focus the pill to read that the document lives only in this browser and that clearing this browser's site data deletes it; click it to come back to this guide. The Explorer sidebar's **This browser** row lists them all.

<Note>
  An offline document is **not synced and not backed up**. It lives only in this browser: clearing
  your site data, or the browser reclaiming storage, will delete it. Keep anything important on the
  cloud, or export a copy.
</Note>

## What's different

An offline document is a normal document: every editor feature works, including shapes, arrows, layers, tabs, themes, undo/redo, and export. Only the features that need the server are unavailable, because there's no server copy to power them:

- **Sharing** needs a document on the server, so opening **Share** on an offline document prompts you to sync it to your account first. Embeds are unavailable for the same reason.
- **Live collaboration** and **teams** don't apply; an offline document is yours alone. Comments still work, but only you see them.
- The AI assistant works while you're online (it reads your current canvas), but not with no connection.
- Images you add are stored **inside the document**, so it stays fully local.
- In the Explorer it shows a fixed offline picture instead of a thumbnail, because there is no server copy to draw one from.

## Moving to and from your account

You can change your mind at any time, from the document's **⋯** menu in the Explorer.

- **Sync Document** uploads an offline document to the cloud. It becomes a normal document, shareable and reachable from your other devices, and the local-only copy is removed.
- **Take Offline** does the reverse: it downloads a cloud document into this browser and then **removes it from your account and every other device**. Because that deletes the server copy for good, we ask you to confirm first.
- **A tab it shares with other documents** (see [Add a Tab to Another Document](/help/tabs/add-to-document/)) stays in those documents, and this browser keeps its own copy. From then on the two are separate: edits on one side no longer reach the other, and syncing the document back brings your copy in as a new tab beside the original.

<Note>
  **Take Offline** is deliberate and irreversible on the server side: once a document is offline it
  exists only in the browser you took it offline in. If it was shared, taking it offline also
  revokes the share links. It is a move, not a delete, so it never goes to the
  [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/); an offline document you delete goes to this browser's own
  Trash.
</Note>
