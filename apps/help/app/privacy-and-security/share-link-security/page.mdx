A share link (the Share dialog calls each one a **pass**) is a capability: anyone holding the URL has its role, **Editor** or **Viewer**. That is what makes sharing frictionless, but it also means a link left lying around keeps working. livediagram gives you two controls to tighten that up, a password on the document and an expiry on each link, plus revoking a link outright. All three live in the **Share** dialog, and none of them ever stops you, the owner.

## Share password

You can put a single password on a document: switch on **Password Protection** in the Share dialog, type it, and choose **Save**. Once it is set, anyone opening any share link for that document must enter it before they can view, and the password rides along on every later request so their edits stay gated too.

- **One password covers every link** on the document, [embeds](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) included, not one password per link.
- **You always bypass it.** The owner is identified directly, so you never get prompted on your own document. So are members of the [team](/help/collaboration/teams/) whose library holds it.
- **Nobody can read it back.** livediagram stores only a scrambled (hashed) form of the password, so even you see just **Password Set**. Note it down when you choose it; use **Replace** or **Remove** any time.
- **Changing it takes effect at once.** Setting or replacing the password sends everyone currently in the document through a link back to the password prompt.
- **Guessing is limited.** Share-code and password attempts are rate-limited per network (30 a minute on livediagram.app), wherever they are tried.

The goal is to stop people guessing or stumbling onto a share URL, not to provide cryptographic protection. See [Share Passwords](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/) for the full walkthrough.

## Link expiry

When you create a pass, the **Valid** control sets its lifetime, so access ends automatically without you having to remember to revoke it.

| Choice   | Lifetime                |
| -------- | ----------------------- |
| Forever  | no expiry (the default) |
| 1 week   | 7 days                  |
| 1 month  | 30 days                 |
| 6 months | 183 days                |

When a link expires it stops granting access everywhere at once: the share URL, its embed and live image, and the real-time room all behave as if the link were revoked. The expired link does not vanish, though. It moves to the **Expired** section of the Share dialog, where you can delete it or **Extend** it for the same lifetime again, counted from now. A link's lifetime can't be changed after it is created.

<Note>
  Existing live sessions are not force-disconnected at the exact expiry moment; a visitor loses
  access on their next load or save. To cut someone off instantly, revoke the link instead: revoking
  disconnects everyone using it straight away.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use the two together for sensitive work: a password stops casual access, and an expiry bounds how
  long even the right people keep it. Prefer a **Viewer** pass when people only need to look, and
  remember that anyone who can see an embed's page source can see its link.
</Tip>
