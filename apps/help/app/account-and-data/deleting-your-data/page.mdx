You own your documents, and removing one is straightforward. It helps to know how your data is scoped first, because that determines what "your data" means whether you are working as a guest or signed in.

## Deleting a document

You can delete a document from the [Explorer](/explorer/recent) or from the editor. Open the document's **⋯** menu (on its row or card, or in the Current Document section of the Explorer panel) and choose **Delete**. A short confirmation asks you to confirm by name and, when it applies, adds that its share links stop working or that it is deleted for the whole team. Its **Delete** button is amber rather than red, because nothing is lost yet.

Deleting a document moves it to the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), where it waits for 30 days before it is removed for good, along with the content that belongs only to it, such as its tabs and comments. While it waits, its share links stop working and it disappears from your lists, and you can restore it with everything it had. To remove it straight away, delete it permanently from the Trash.

A tab the document shares with another document is not deleted with it: it stays in the other document. [Offline documents](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) have their own Trash in this browser.

If you have organised documents into [folders](/help/explorer/folders/), you can also tidy up by moving documents between folders or removing folders, separately from deleting the documents themselves.

## Deleting your account

When you are signed in, open **Settings**, go to **Account**, and choose **Delete Account** under **Danger Zone**. You are asked to type your email address to confirm, because this cannot be undone.

Deleting your account removes, straight away and everywhere:

- every document you own, including anything already in the Trash, with its tabs, comments and share links;
- your folders, images, saved themes, shape libraries and preferences;
- your [API tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/), which stops any script or [connected AI tool](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/) at once;
- your [Google Drive](/help/account-and-data/google-drive/) connection (livediagram's access is revoked at Google, and the files already in your Drive stay yours);
- the account itself.

Documents in a [team's](/help/collaboration/teams/) shared library are not deleted with you: they pass to a remaining member, so the team keeps its work. A team you were the last member of is deleted. If email is set up, you get a short confirmation once it is done.

## How your data is scoped

Where your data lives depends on how you are identified:

- **As a guest**, your documents are owned by your [per-browser id](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/), stored in that one browser. Your data is scoped to that browser, so clearing the browser's storage removes the app's link to your guest content, and using a different browser or device shows a fresh, empty workspace.
- **When signed in**, your documents are owned by your account and synced across every device where you sign in. Deleting a document removes it everywhere, because the account is the single source of truth.

A guest has no account to delete. To remove what you created as a guest, delete the documents you no longer want and empty the Trash. Clearing the browser's storage only removes this browser's record of your guest identity: the documents stay on the server, out of reach from a fresh browser, so delete them first if you want them gone.

<Tip>
  If you plan to clear your browser, sign in first if you want to keep anything. Your guest
  documents migrate into the account on sign-up, so they survive the clear.
</Tip>
