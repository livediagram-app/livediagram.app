You own your documents, and removing one is straightforward. It helps to know how your data is scoped first, because that determines what "your data" means whether you are working as a guest or signed in.

## Deleting a document

You can delete a document from the [Explorer](/explorer/recent) or from the editor. Open the row's ellipsis menu, or the Current Document menu in the Explorer panel, and choose **Delete**.

Deleting a document moves it to the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), where it waits for 30 days before it is removed for good, along with the content that belongs only to it, such as its tabs, comments, and activity history. While it waits, its share links stop working and it disappears from your lists, and you can restore it with everything it had. To remove it straight away, delete it permanently from the Trash.

## How your data is scoped

Where your data lives depends on how you are identified:

- **As a guest**, your documents are owned by your [per-browser id](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/), stored in that one browser. Your data is scoped to that browser, so clearing the browser's storage removes the app's link to your guest content, and using a different browser or device shows a fresh, empty workspace.
- **When signed in**, your documents are owned by your account and synced across every device where you sign in. Deleting a document removes it everywhere, because the account is the single source of truth.

Deleting your account is the exception to the Trash: it removes every document at once, including anything already in the Trash.

Because guest content is per browser, the simplest way to clear everything you created as a guest is to delete the documents you no longer want, or to clear that browser's storage, which removes the app's record of your guest identity on that device.

<Tip>
  If you plan to clear your browser, sign in first if you want to keep anything. Your guest
  documents migrate into the account on sign-up, so they survive the clear.
</Tip>
