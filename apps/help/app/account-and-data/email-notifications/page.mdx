Email is an optional capability of livediagram. It only does anything when the deployment has been set up to send mail, and only signed-in users ever receive anything, because email needs an address and a [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/) has none. The canvas itself never needs an account or an email, so this is purely additive.

## What we might send

There are four groups:

- **Tips and check-ins.** A short welcome when you first sign in, a nudge a few days later if you have not made a document yet, a couple of getting-started notes over your first two weeks, and a friendly nudge if you have been away for about four weeks. The welcome goes straight away; everything after it can be turned off.
- **Milestones.** A small celebration when you hit a milestone, like sharing your first document or reaching your tenth.
- **Notifications about your work.** When someone opens a document you shared for the first time, when someone responds to a team invite you sent (for teams you are an admin of), when someone comments on a document you own, when a teammate assigns you an action on an element, and when a teammate @-mentions you in a comment. Each of these has its own switch.
- **Account messages.** A team invite when you are invited, a confirmation when you delete your account, and a one-time heads-up a week before an [API token](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) expires. These are tied to something you or a teammate did, so they always send.

## What an email contains

Notification emails keep your work out of your inbox where they can. A comment notification tells you who commented and on which document, with a link to open it, but not what the comment said.

Two carry a little more, because the words are addressed to you: a **mention** email quotes the comment you were mentioned in (cut to about 280 characters), and an **assigned action** email names the action and includes the start of its description (up to 200 characters). Both link to the document, or to the Plan card when the mention was on one. Nothing else from the document, and never a picture of it, goes into an email.

## Turning them off

Open **Settings**, go to **Account**, then **Notifications** beneath it (the account menu's **Account** gets you there too). The **Email** section has one switch per optional kind: **Someone Joins My Document**, **Someone Responds to a Team Invite**, **Someone Comments on My Document**, **Someone Assigns Me an Action**, **Someone Mentions Me in a Comment**, **Tips and Check-Ins**, and **Milestones**. They are all on until you turn them off. The whole row is the switch, so click anywhere on it.

  Every optional email also carries a "Manage your notifications" link in its footer that opens
  these switches, and mail apps that show a native unsubscribe button point at the same place.

The switches are saved to your account, so they apply wherever you are signed in, and the change takes effect before the next email would go out. Account messages (the invite, the deletion confirmation, and the token-expiry reminder) are not on this list, because they confirm something rather than nudge you.

As a guest, the same place shows a short note asking you to sign in instead of the switches. **In-Editor Notifications**, above them, is a different thing: it controls the brief confirmations inside the editor and never sends email.

## Self-hosting

If you run your own instance without email configured, none of this applies: no emails are sent, and the **Email** section of the Notifications settings does not appear. See [Signing In](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/) for how accounts work, and the project's self-hosting docs for enabling email.
