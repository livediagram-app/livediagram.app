The Explorer's browse views can render the same folders and documents two ways: a compact **list** of rows, or a grid of **cards**, each with a large live preview of the document's canvas. Recognising a document by its picture is often faster than reading near-identical names, so the card view shines in folders full of variations on a theme; the list packs more onto the screen when you know exactly what you're looking for.

## Switching views

Look for the two-button toggle at the right of the header on the [Explorer page](/help/explorer/explorer-page/)'s browse views (Recent, Favourites, Search results, My documents, folders, and This browser). Click **List view** (the rows icon) for the list, or **Card view** (the grid icon) for cards. The choice applies immediately and to all of those views at once.

The same toggle appears on a [team's shared library](/help/explorer/team-spaces/), and it shares the same preference, so a card-view person gets cards there too.

Cards are what you get until you choose otherwise: a document is easiest to recognise by its picture, and the grid is the view that shows you one. Your choice is then remembered **per device** (it's a layout preference, not account data), so your phone and your desktop can each keep the view that suits their screen.

## What a card shows

Each card carries everything its list row does, arranged around the preview:

- A **live snapshot** of the document's first tab, rendered from the actual canvas and refreshed when the document changes. The preview keeps the document's own background colour, so the card reads like a window onto the canvas.
- The **name**, the **last-updated** time, the owner where relevant, the **Made by AI** or **Local only** pill where one applies, and the same visibility badge and **⋯** menu as the list row: **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Change Folder**, **Favourite**, **Hide from Recent** and **Delete**, among others.
- A **star** on anything you have favourited: before the name in the list view, so a column of rows lines its stars up in one scannable vertical, and in the meta row on a card. It is amber rather than brand-coloured because it is a personal mark sitting beside badges that describe the document itself.
- Folders appear as folder cards in the same grid, so navigation doesn't change between views, and a folder card **previews what is inside it**: a small mosaic of up to four tiles, the document snapshots it holds first, then its subfolders. When there are more than four, the last tile becomes a muted **+N** counting the rest. An empty folder keeps the plain folder glyph. This is the card grid only; the list view keeps its count badge, and the editor's [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) is unchanged.

The grid is responsive: one column on a phone, two on a tablet, three on a wide screen.

## About the previews

Snapshot thumbnails also appear in list rows and other Explorer surfaces; the card view just shows them large. A few behaviours worth knowing:

- Previews load **lazily** as you scroll, so a long library doesn't fetch dozens of images up front.
- A document with an **empty first tab** shows a dashed sketch captioned "Nothing drawn yet" instead of a snapshot, and while a snapshot loads a placeholder fades into it, so the card never jumps in size.
- Previews are rendered fresh after the document is edited, so what you see stays current without you doing anything.
- A preview is **vector, not a photo of the canvas**, which is why it is crisp at any size. It is also the very same picture a [live image link](/help/collaboration/sharing/live-image/) serves: one snapshot per document, drawn once and used as a 36px row thumbnail, as this card, and in your README.
