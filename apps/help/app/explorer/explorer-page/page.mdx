The full-page [Explorer](/explorer) is the complete library view, modelled on a desktop file browser: a sidebar on the left to navigate, and a breadcrumb plus a list of folders and documents on the right. It is where all your documents live, organised into folders by project, customer, or topic. (For the compact version inside the editor, see the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).)

The sidebar is one tree in three groups: **Overview**, **Spaces** and **More**. Every document lives in exactly one place, **My documents**, a team, or this browser, and everything else in the sidebar is a view that gathers documents from wherever they live. Search sits in the top bar: type there to search, or pick a filter, and the page you are on narrows to what matches ([Search and filter documents](/help/explorer/filters/)). New folder and New document sit at the top of the page you are on.

## Overview

- **Home** is what the Explorer opens on: the [Timeline](/help/explorer/timeline/), a day-by-day feed of everything that has happened across your documents, teams and account, including the things other people did. It carries a badge when something has happened since you last looked.
- **Activity** lists the open [actions and comment threads](/help/explorer/activity/) waiting on you.
- **Shared with me** gathers every document other people have [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) into one list, kept apart from your own library so collaborator work and your own never get mixed up.

## Spaces

Your spaces are the top-level folders of the tree.

- **My documents** is [your own library](/help/explorer/personal-space/). Beneath it sit the folders you create; opening it lists those folders, then the documents you have not filed. This is where you do the actual filing: dragging documents into folders, renaming, and nesting folders inside folders.
- **Each team** you belong to sits beside it, and expands to the team's shared folders ([Team spaces](/help/explorer/team-spaces/)).
- **Invites** appears while a team invite is waiting for you.
- **New team** creates a team. If you are not signed in, a **Sign in to access Teams** card sits here instead.

## More

- **This browser** lists the documents saved only in this browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)). It shows only while there is at least one. They are yours, like everything in My documents, and every row and card of one carries a **Local only** pill: hover or focus it to read that it lives only in this browser, and click it for the Offline Mode guide. Their menus offer **Sync Document** to move one to your account, while a cloud document's menu offers **Take Offline** to do the reverse.
- **Library** opens to the [Image gallery](/help/explorer/image-gallery/) (every image you have uploaded, with where each is used), [Themes](/help/explorer/themes-library/) (your custom themes, ready to edit or reuse) and [Shape libraries](/help/explorer/shape-libraries/).
- **Trash** holds deleted documents for 30 days, ready to restore.

## Moving around with the keyboard

The sidebar is a single stop when you press Tab. Then the **arrow keys** move up and down, **Right** opens a row and **Left** closes it (or moves to its parent), **Home** and **End** jump to the first and last rows, **Enter** opens the row, and typing a letter jumps to the next row starting with it. On a folder, **Shift+F10** opens its menu.

With [power user mode](/help/user-interface/power-user-mode/) and Minimal chrome on, the group titles give way to thin lines between the groups, with nothing above Home.

Each page is named after its row: **Home**, **Shared with me**, **My documents**, **This browser**, **Image gallery** and the rest, in the page heading, the browser tab and the breadcrumb.

## List or card view

Every browse view, My documents, your folders, Recent, Search results, and a team's shared library, can be shown two ways, switched with the **List / Card** toggle at the top-right of the pane:

- **List** packs documents into compact rows, best for scanning a long library by name and date.
- **Card** shows each document as a tile with a thumbnail of its first tab, best for recognising a document by its shape at a glance.

The choice is remembered on your device, so the Explorer always opens the way you left it. [List and Card Views](/help/explorer/list-and-card-views/) covers the two layouts and the snapshot previews in detail.

## Finding your way around

Whichever row you pick, the main pane on the right shows its contents, and a breadcrumb across the top traces the folder path so you can click any step to jump back up. On a phone, the menu button at the top of the page opens the same sidebar as a drawer. Everything works whether or not you are signed in: a guest sees the documents and folders their own browser owns, and can sign in later to carry that work into an account.
