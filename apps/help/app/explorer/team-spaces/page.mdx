Each team you belong to is a space of its own in the Explorer sidebar: a top-level folder under **Spaces**, right after your own [My documents](/help/explorer/personal-space/). Expand a team to walk its shared folders, and keep your pending invites one click away. For the full picture of how teams, roles, and invites work, see the [Teams guide](/help/collaboration/teams/).

## Your teams

Each team you are a member of appears as a row, with a badge showing the member count once a team has more than one person. Click a team to open its page; expand it to walk its shared folder tree without leaving the sidebar, and click a folder to jump straight to it on the team page. Team-shared documents live in [a folder tree every member can manage](/help/collaboration/teams/team-shared-documents/), separate from My documents.

## Invites

While an invitation is waiting for you, an **Invites** row appears under your teams, with a badge counting them. Open it to accept or decline. Invites also reach you on **Home** and by email.

## New team

**New team**, the last row of Spaces, creates a team: give it a name (and an organisation, if you like), then invite people by email. They show up here as soon as they accept.

## Signed-in only

Teams need an account, so team rows and New team appear only when you are signed in. Guests see a **Sign in to access Teams** card in their place. On deployments configured without authentication, neither appears, since teams cannot exist without it.
