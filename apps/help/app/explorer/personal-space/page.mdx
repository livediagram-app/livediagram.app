**My documents** is your own library: the [documents](/explorer/all) you have created, organised however suits you. It is the first row under **Spaces** in the Explorer sidebar, a top-level folder with everything of yours beneath it. Each team you belong to sits beside it as a space of its own ([Team spaces](/help/explorer/team-spaces/)).

A document lives in exactly one place: My documents, a team, or this browser. Moving it changes where it lives. Everything else in the sidebar, Home, Activity, Shared with me, Unsorted and Generated, is a view that gathers documents from wherever they live.

## Unsorted and Generated

The first two rows beneath My documents fill themselves:

- **Unsorted** catches documents you have not filed anywhere, so a freshly created document always has a home without you deciding anything first.
- **Generated** collects AI-made documents you have not filed yet, keeping them apart from the ones you drew.

Neither can be renamed or deleted, and each shows a count badge only when it holds something. Filing a document into a folder of your own moves it out of Unsorted or Generated.

Documents saved only in this browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)) are not part of My documents: they collect under **This browser**, in the sidebar's **More** group, which shows while there is at least one.

### Favourites

Choose **Favourite** in any document's menu to star it. It's the answer to a busy team library: a shared folder fills up with documents that aren't all yours to care about, and starring the handful that are marks them wherever they are listed. The same menu reads **Unfavourite** to take it back off. Your starred documents are gathered at [/explorer/favourites](/explorer/favourites), from your own folders and every team you're in, each row showing where the document actually lives.

**Starring is yours alone.** Favouriting a document in a team folder doesn't favourite it for your teammates, and there's no limit on how many you keep.

## Folders

Folders give your library structure without forcing it. Every document is in exactly one folder, or none, and folders can nest, so you can build something like "Projects / Customer / Engagement" when you want that depth, or keep everything flat under Unsorted when you do not.

Deleting a folder is safe for its contents: the documents inside drop to Unsorted and any subfolders move up to the top level, so nothing is ever lost when you tidy up.

## Two places to browse

You will meet My documents in two forms: the **full-page Explorer**, modelled on a desktop file browser, and the compact **floating Explorer panel** in the editor for finding a document mid-task, where it is the **My documents** tab. Both work whether or not you are signed in: a guest sees the documents and folders their own browser owns, and can sign in later to carry that work into an account.

<Tip>
  Do not over-organise on day one. Start in Unsorted, and pull documents into folders once a real
  grouping emerges. Folders are easy to add and rearrange whenever you need them.
</Tip>
