**Personal Space** is your own library: the [documents](/explorer/all) you have created, organised however suits you. It is the tree in the middle of the Explorer sidebar, holding a collapsible **Dynamic** parent for the automatic buckets and whatever folders you have made.

## The Dynamic buckets

At the top of Personal Space, a collapsible **Dynamic** parent groups three live views that file themselves, so your own folders lead the tree:

- **Unsorted** catches documents you have not filed anywhere, so a freshly created document always has a home without you deciding anything first.
- **Generated** collects AI-made documents you have not filed yet, keeping them apart from the ones you drew.
- **Offline** collects documents saved only in this browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)).

**Favourites** is the fourth of that kind and deliberately sits higher, in **Quick find** beside Timeline, Recent and Shared with you: it is a shortlist you curated, not a bucket that fills itself with whatever you left unfiled.

### Favourites

Choose **Favourite** in any document's menu to star it, and it collects under **Favourites**. It's the answer to a busy team library: a shared folder fills up with documents that aren't all yours to care about, and starring the handful that are gives you one short list to work from. The same menu reads **Unfavourite** to take it back off.

Favourites gathers from everywhere at once, your own folders and every team you're in, so each row shows where the document actually lives, its folder for a personal document or the team name for a team one. Click that to jump to the real location.

**Starring is yours alone.** Favouriting a document in a team folder doesn't favourite it for your teammates, and there's no limit on how many you keep.

None of the buckets can be renamed or deleted, and each shows a count badge only when it holds something. Filing a document into a folder of your own moves it out of Unsorted or Generated; Offline is different, listing every browser-only document whatever folder it sits in, so there is always one place to find them.

## Folders

Folders give your library structure without forcing it. Every document is in exactly one folder, or none, and folders can nest, so you can build something like "Projects / Customer / Engagement" when you want that depth, or keep everything flat under Unsorted when you do not.

Deleting a folder is safe for its contents: the documents inside drop to Unsorted and any subfolders move up to the top level, so nothing is ever lost when you tidy up.

## Two places to browse

You will meet Personal Space in two forms: the **full-page Explorer**, modelled on a desktop file browser, and the compact **floating Explorer panel** in the editor for finding a document mid-task. Both work whether or not you are signed in: a guest sees the documents and folders their own browser owns, and can sign in later to carry that work into an account.

<Tip>
  Do not over-organise on day one. Start in Unsorted, and pull documents into folders once a real
  grouping emerges. Folders are easy to add and rearrange whenever you need them.
</Tip>
