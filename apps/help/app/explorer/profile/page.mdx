Your account lives in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), under **Account**. Open it by clicking the **Hi** greeting with your name at the top of the Explorer sidebar, or pick **Account** from the account menu (the initial bubble in the top-right corner of the editor). Either way Settings opens on the right place.

It used to be a separate Profile page in the Explorer. Everything it held now sits in Settings alongside the rest of your preferences, so there is one place to look rather than two.

## What it shows

**Account** opens on your identity card: the same initial-letter avatar as the header account button, your name, your email address, and the date you joined. It only displays this identity, which is read from your account rather than edited here.

## Email notifications

Under **Notifications**, the **Email** section lists every optional email with its own switch: someone joining your diagram, responses to team invites you sent, comments on your diagrams, actions assigned to you, tips and check-ins, and milestones. The whole row is the switch, so click anywhere on it to flip it. The toggles are saved to your account and apply wherever you sign in. See [Email Notifications](/help/account-and-data/email-notifications/) for what each kind of email contains and when it sends.

This section only appears when the deployment can actually send mail and you are signed in; without an address to send to, the switches could never apply, so a card explains that instead.

## Danger zone

**Account** ends with a **Danger Zone** holding the **Delete Account** button. It opens a confirmation dialog that asks you to type your email address, a deliberate speed bump before anything irreversible happens. Confirming permanently removes your diagrams, folders, and participant record from the server, then deletes the account itself and signs you out. This cannot be undone. For deleting a single diagram, or how guest data is scoped, see [Deleting Your Data](/help/account-and-data/deleting-your-data/).

## Signed-in feature

Deleting removes the data held against a verified account, so it needs one: the row is still shown to guests, saying why it is unavailable rather than hiding. A guest's diagrams belong to their browser, and [Deleting Your Data](/help/account-and-data/deleting-your-data/) covers clearing those.

<Tip>
  You do not have to go looking for the notification switches: every optional email carries a
  "Manage your notifications" link in its footer that opens Settings on this same section.
</Tip>
