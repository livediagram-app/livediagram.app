**Recent documents** lists the documents you have opened or edited most recently, newest first, so the work you are in the middle of is always one click away. It has no row in the [Explorer](/explorer) sidebar, where **Home** (the [Timeline](/help/explorer/timeline/)) answers "what happened": open it at [/explorer/recent](/explorer/recent). It answers the narrower "what did I touch last".

## What lands here

Every document you open or edit moves to the top of Recent, whichever folder it actually lives in. It is a view across your whole library, not a folder of its own, so a document appearing in Recent has not been moved or copied: it still belongs to its folder (or to Unsorted), and you will find it there too.

## Working from Recent

- **Open** a document by clicking its row.
- Each row carries the same actions as anywhere else in the Explorer, including rename, move to a folder, and share.
- [Offline documents](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) appear here too, and their rows add **Sync Document** (or **Take Offline** on a cloud document) alongside those actions.
- Because Recent spans every folder, it is the quickest place to reopen something without remembering where you filed it.

## Seeing where a document lives

Recent spans every folder, so each row shows the folder it belongs to beside its name. Click that folder name to jump straight there instead of opening the document, which is the quickest way to see what else you filed alongside it.

Documents you haven't filed anywhere show **Unsorted**. Team documents name the team, since that's where they live. Documents shared with you don't show one at all: they sit in the other person's library rather than yours.

## Hiding a document from Recent

Some documents you open now and then but never want cluttering the list. Choose **Hide from Recent** in a document's menu and it stops appearing here, no matter how recently you opened or edited it. The same menu then reads **Show in Recent**, so it is easy to put back.

The document itself is untouched: it stays in its folder, in search, on its share links, and opens normally. Only this list changes, which is why there is no badge on it anywhere else, the menu item tells you the state where you would change it.

**The choice is yours alone.** Hiding a shared or team document from your Recent doesn't hide it from anyone else's. It travels with your account, so it applies on your other devices too, and works the same whether or not you're signed in.

<Tip>
  Recent is owner-scoped like the rest of the Explorer: a guest sees the documents their own browser
  has opened, and signing in carries that history into your account.
</Tip>
