Every list of documents in the [Explorer](/explorer) can be narrowed. Type a few words of a document's name into the **Search or filter documents** field in the top bar, or pick a **filter chip** under the page title. Both edit the same search, so a chip you pick shows up in the field, and a filter you type lights its chip. On a phone the field sits on its own row under the logo.

Words match the document's name, ignoring capitals and accents ("cafe" finds "Café"). With several words, a document has to contain all of them.

## Where it works

- **My documents, a folder, a team, and This browser.** The search stays inside the place you are in, and reaches into every folder below it: the list shows each matching document once, with the folder it lives in beside its name. Folders step aside while a search is set and come back when you clear it.
- **Recent, Favourites, Shared with me and Search results** gather documents from everywhere, so they add a **Space** chip: My documents, Shared with me, or one of your teams.
- **Home, Activity, the Library pages and Trash** have no list of documents to narrow. Typing in the field there opens **Search results**, every document you can open, narrowed by what you typed.

## The chips

| Chip       | Keeps documents that                                                                     |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Opens in   | Were created to open in Diagram, Draw, Illustrate or Plan mode                           |
| Kind       | Were created as an Event Storming board                                                  |
| Template   | Were made from a Retrospective or Kanban template                                        |
| Made by AI | An AI tool made for you; they also carry a **Made by AI** badge wherever they are listed |
| Edited     | Were last edited today, in the last 7, 30 days or 12 months, or this year                |
| People     | You own (Me), or someone else owns (Others)                                              |
| Space      | Live in My documents, are shared with you, or are in a team (gathered lists only)        |

Click a chip to open its list and tick values; **Any** at the top unsets it. **Made by AI** has only one value, so it is a simple on/off toggle. A chip can hold several values: picking Retrospective and Kanban keeps both. Different chips narrow together: Template and Edited keeps the retrospectives you edited in the last 7 days. **Clear** at the end of the row empties the search.

A document made before livediagram recorded how it was created has no Opens in, Kind or Template, so setting one of those chips hides it; it shows again once they are back on Any. Documents in **Shared with me** don't carry that information either, so the Opens in, Kind, Template and Made by AI chips leave them out of a gathered list.

## Typing filters

The field takes filters as words too, the way the chips write them:

- `made-by:ai` keeps what an AI made.
- `template:retrospective,kanban` keeps both template families.
- `edited:7d` keeps what you edited in the last 7 days; `today`, `30d`, `12m` and `this-year` work too.
- `opens-in:draw` (or `diagram`, `illustrate`, `plan`), `kind:event-storming`, `people:me` or `people:others`, and `space:mine` or `space:shared` work the same way. A team is offered by name as you type `space:`.

As you type, suggestions appear under the field: use **Down** and **Up** to move through them, **Enter** or **Tab** to take one, and **Esc** to close the list. A suggestion marked **No matches** would leave nothing in the list on its own. A finished filter becomes a pill in the field, with a **×** to remove it; **Backspace** at the start of the words selects the last pill, and a second **Backspace** removes it.

A word that looks like a filter but isn't one, such as `colour:red` or `edited:yesterday`, is searched as plain text, and the Explorer says so under the chips, so a typo never hides documents silently. A **Space** filter typed on My documents, a folder or a team does nothing there, because the place you are in already sets the space; the note under the chips says so. The search reads at most 512 characters.

## Share a search

The search lives in the page's address, so copying the link shares exactly what you are looking at. Moving between Recent, Favourites, Shared with me and Search results keeps your search; opening a folder or a team starts afresh. A search is not remembered for next time.
