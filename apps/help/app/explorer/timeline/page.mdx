**Timeline** is the view the [Explorer](/explorer/timeline) opens on. Where [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/) answers "what did I touch last", the Timeline answers a different question: **what has happened since I was last here**, including the things you did not do yourself.

## What lands here

Every event on the feed comes from something that actually happened in your account. Nothing is guessed, and nothing is written by hand.

- **Your diagrams** — created, renamed, duplicated, filed into a folder, published to a team, deleted, and a daily summary of the days you worked on one.
- **Collaboration** — comments added to diagrams you own or share, threads resolved, [actions](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/) assigned to you or completed by someone else, and share links created.
- **Teams** — invites you receive, members joining or leaving, people being removed, and role changes, for every [team](/help/explorer/team-spaces/) you belong to.
- **Your account** — [API tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) created, custom themes saved, and images uploaded.

If a diagram lives in a team's shared library, everyone in that team sees its events. A personal diagram's events are yours alone.

## Reading a day

Events are grouped by the day they happened, newest day first, with a dot and a line running down the left. Today's group is marked with a **Today** pill.

Each row names **what it happened to first** — "Payments architecture deleted", not "Diagram deleted" with the name underneath — so you can scan the left edge and stop where something matters. Diagrams show a small preview of themselves on the right.

Click a row to go to whatever it is about: the diagram, the team, your invites. Rows you cannot open are dimmed, so you can tell at a glance. A deleted diagram is the usual case: there is nothing left to open.

### Colour tells you what kind of thing happened

The colour of a row is about **what happened**, not which part of the product it happened in:

- **Red** — something was destroyed or someone lost access: a diagram deleted, a member removed from a team.
- **Amber** — the shape of things changed: renames, moves, team membership, roles, sharing, and anything about to expire.
- **Green** — the ordinary business of building: created, edited, commented, completed.

Three colours rather than one per feature, because the question you are asking as you scan is "is any of this alarming?", and only that question has a useful colour answer.

### Busy days collapse

A day where you renamed six diagrams should not be six lines. When several events of the same kind land on one day, they fold into a single bubble that says how many there were.

<Figure caption="Several events of one kind become one bubble; clicking expands the run in place.">
  <TimelineStacking />
</Figure>

Click it to open the run, and **Collapse** at the bottom of it to fold it back up.

Two kinds never collapse, because collapsing them would hide the thing you came to read:

- **Comments** stay separate, so you can always read what was actually said.
- **Deleted diagrams** stay separate, so a deletion can never hide inside a stack.

## What is coming up

Not everything on the feed is in the past. Anything with an expiry — an [API token](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) approaching its six-month limit, a [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-link-expiry/) about to lapse — appears **above** today in an Upcoming band.

<Tip>
  This is the reason to open the Timeline before something breaks rather than after: a script that
  stops working next Tuesday says so this week.
</Tip>

## Calendar view

Switch **List** to **Calendar** in the header to see the same events as a month grid. Each day carries one dot per colour present, with a number when there was more than one — so a red dot tells you something was deleted that day without opening anything. Click a dot to read that day's events of that kind.

The arrows skip straight to the next month that actually has something in it, so a quiet stretch does not cost you four clicks to page through.

## Filtering

The **Filter** button opens a chip per area — Diagrams, Teams, Account — along with a small calendar for jumping to a particular day. Turn a chip off and that area drops out of the feed; a dot on the button tells you a filter is on.

The Timeline is not live. It loads when you open it, so it never shifts under you while you are reading; reopening the page brings anything new.

Filters and the calendar view are per visit. Come back tomorrow and the Timeline opens as a plain list again.

## Guests and signing in

The Timeline works without an account, like the rest of the canvas. A guest sees their own diagram activity and comments on their diagrams; team, invite, and token events need an account, so they simply do not appear.

**Your history follows you.** Signing up moves your Timeline to your new account alongside your diagrams, so a week of work as a guest is still there afterwards.

<Tip>
  The Timeline keeps a year of history. The per-diagram [Activity
  panel](/help/activity-panel/) is the other half of this: it records every individual
  element change on one diagram, and lets you undo them.
</Tip>
