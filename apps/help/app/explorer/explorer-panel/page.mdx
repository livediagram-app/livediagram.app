The **Explorer panel** is the Explorer inside the editor. Press the **menu button** (☰) in the top-left corner of the canvas and it opens under the button; press the button again, or click anywhere outside, and it closes. It holds the same sections as the [full-page Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), so you can browse your library, catch up on what happened and open another document without leaving the canvas you are on.

## What the panel shows

- **Current Document** at the top is the document you are in: its thumbnail, its name, when it was last saved, its visibility badge (**Private**, **Shared**, **Team** or **This Browser**) at the right, and its **⋯** menu. **Click the name to rename it** in place: Enter saves, Escape keeps the old name. Hover the badge to see who can see the document.
- **The rail** down the left lists every section, grouped as on the Explorer page: **Overview** (Home, Inbox, Timeline, Shared With Me), **Spaces** (My Documents and each team) and **More** (This Browser, when documents are saved only here, then [Tags](/help/explorer/tags/), Library and Trash).
- **The pane** on the right shows the section you pick. Nothing sends you away from the editor: **Home**, the **Inbox**, the **Timeline**, the **Library** (Image gallery, Themes and Shape libraries) and the **Trash** all open right there. **My Documents**, each team, **This Browser** and **Shared With Me** list their folders and documents, each document with its thumbnail and when it was last updated; a folder opens right there in the pane, with a path above it back to where you started, and picking a document opens it in the editor. **Open Page** at the top of the pane takes that section to the full Explorer, and **New Folder** makes a folder in My Documents or the team you are looking at.
- The panel opens on **Home** (the documents you were last in, and what others did since) and then remembers the section you last picked.
- Along the foot are the product's other places: **Home**, **Explorer** and **Help**.

It is the same data as the full page: folders, ownership, and sharing all behave identically. Each document has the document menu from its **⋯** button, a right-click, or **Shift+F10**, so **Change Folder**, **Favourite** and **Hide from Recent** work here as well. A document saved only in this browser carries the **This Browser** pill ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)). Drag one of your own documents onto one of your folders to file it, or onto **My Documents** in the rail to put it back at the root.

The keyboard works as on the Explorer page: in the rail, **Up** and **Down** move between sections and **Enter** picks one; in the pane, **Up** and **Down** move between rows and **Enter** opens one. Answering team invites and creating a team happen in the full Explorer.

The **⋯** button in the panel's header, beside the help button, opens a menu with **New Document** and **Open Explorer** (the full-page library), then **Share** and **Export** for the document you are in, then **Search**, **GitHub** (the open-source code), **Licences** and **Settings**. With [power user mode](/help/user-interface/power-user-mode/)'s Minimal chrome on, the help button moves into this menu as **Help**.

## When you will see it

- **In the editor**, to jump to another document without going back to the library. [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) hides the menu button with the rest of the chrome.
- **On a phone**, the menu button sits in its own card at the left of the palette strip, and the panel opens under it across the screen: the sections become a row of chips you swipe sideways, with the picked one's pane beneath. Held sideways, a phone has no menu button and no Explorer; turn it upright to open the Explorer.
- **When a document can't be opened**, it stays available beside the error screen, so you can pick another document.

Like everywhere else in the Explorer, the panel works whether or not you are signed in, showing the documents your browser or account owns.
