The floating **Explorer panel** is the compact side panel you meet inside the editor and the new-document flow. Where the [full-page Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) is the complete library, the panel is built for one job: finding a document fast while you are mid-task, without leaving the canvas you are on.

## What the panel shows

The panel names your spaces the way the full page does, trimmed to fit a side rail: tabs for your **Recent** documents, for the common case, then **My documents** and **Teams** when you need to reach further. Pick one and it opens straight away; the document you were in stays put behind it.

</Figure>

It is the same data as the full page, just a quicker door into it: folders, ownership, and sharing all behave identically, so anything you can open from the [Explorer page](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) you can open here too. **Hide from Recent** works here as well, and a document you hide from either surface stays hidden in both.

Three things are deliberately page-only, because a side rail has no room for them: the [Timeline](/help/explorer/timeline/) feed, the folder or team chip on each Recent row, and the content previews on folder cards. The panel shows the plain name.

The **⋯** button in the panel's header, beside the help button, opens a menu with **New Document** and **Open Explorer** (the full-page library's Recent list), then **Share** and **Export** for the document you are in, then **Search**, **GitHub** (the open-source code) and **Settings**.

## When you will see it

- **In the editor**, to jump to another document without going back to the library.
- **In the new-document flow**, to pick an existing document instead of starting blank.

Like everywhere else in the Explorer, the panel works whether or not you are signed in, showing the documents your browser or account owns.
