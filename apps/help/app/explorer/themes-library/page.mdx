The **Themes** view sits under **Library** in the Explorer sidebar's More group, beside the [Image gallery](/help/explorer/image-gallery/) and [Shape libraries](/help/explorer/shape-libraries/), and holds the [custom themes](/help/canvas/themes/custom-themes/) you have built. It is where your themes live between documents, so a palette you crafted once is ready to reuse everywhere.

## Your theme library

Each saved theme shows as a card with a **swatch preview** and its name, so you can recognise it at a glance, plus a dashed **New theme** card to start another. If you have not built any yet, the view says "No custom themes yet" and offers a **New theme** button instead.

</Figure>

Each theme card carries three icon buttons (hover one to see its name):

- **Edit** opens it in the theme builder to tweak its colours.
- **Duplicate** creates a copy named "&lt;name&gt; copy", a starting point for a variation.
- **Delete** removes it after you confirm. Any document still using a deleted theme falls back to the Default theme, and the removal cannot be undone.

**New theme** opens the same builder with a fresh theme. The builder here is the one the editor uses, so a theme made in either place looks and works the same.

## Where themes get used

Saved themes appear alongside the built-in ones wherever you pick a theme, in the theme browser's **Custom** category, so applying your own palette to a diagram is no different from choosing a built-in. See [Themes](/help/canvas/themes/) for applying them, and [Custom Themes](/help/canvas/themes/custom-themes/) for building one in the first place.

Themes are yours whether or not you are signed in: a guest's themes belong to their browser, and come with them when they sign up. They are not shared with your teams.

<Tip>
  Duplicate a theme before a big change rather than editing in place, so you keep the original as a
  fallback while you experiment with the copy.
</Tip>
