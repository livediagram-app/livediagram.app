The canvas is where you actually build diagrams in livediagram. It is an open, scrollable surface you can pan and zoom freely, with a floating palette sitting on top of it for adding elements and switching tools. Everything works straight away, no sign-in required, so you can [open a fresh canvas](/new) and start placing shapes in seconds.

## The Palette

A floating panel titled **Palette** sits over the canvas, holding everything you add to it. On desktop it starts in the top-right corner, but its header doubles as a drag handle: press it and drag to move the whole panel. Drop it near any corner to dock it there, or anywhere else to leave it floating. Your arrangement is remembered in this browser, even after a reload, and a reset button appears in the header once the panel has left its home corner. A collapse button in the header folds the panel down to its title bar, giving you back canvas space while keeping it one click away. The header also carries the mode switch (**Diagram**, **Draw**, **Illustrate** and **Plan**).

Under the header sit two dropdowns: the canvas tool on the left (**Select**, **Hand**, **Laser** and the other [selection modes](/help/selection-modes/)) and the palette category on the right. The palette opens on **Popular**, the tiles most reached for in the current mode.

If you prefer a single strip along the top of the canvas, the [Toolbar layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) in Settings swaps the floating panel for one. A phone always uses the Toolbar layout.

## Adding Elements

Most elements are added straight from the palette, which is organised into categories you pick from its category dropdown. In Diagram mode that is **Popular**, then **Shapes**, **Write** (text, notes and pages) and **Draw** (pens and arrows), then **Build** (mind nodes, lanes, frames, tables), then **Icons**, **Stickers**, **Tech** and **Media**, then **Collaborate**. Each mode offers its own set: **Components**, **Devices** and **Data** (charts) belong to [Illustrate mode](/help/canvas/illustrate/), for example. Clicking a tile arms a combined gesture: a tap drops the element at your pointer, and a drag sizes it as you go. Single-key shortcuts skip the palette altogether, such as `R` for a rectangle, `T` for text and `N` for a sticky note. See [Adding Elements](/help/canvas/the-canvas/adding-elements/) for the full walkthrough.

## Moving Around

The canvas is effectively infinite, so you are never boxed in. Use the Hand tool, a middle-click drag, two fingers on a trackpad, or hold Space and drag to pan, and `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + scroll, a pinch, or the zoom controls in the bottom-right corner to scale the view. The [Panning and Zooming](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/) article covers every way to navigate.

Each tab has its own backdrop and theme. The paintbrush button beside the zoom controls opens the **Tab Look & Feel** dialog, where you can [change the canvas background](/help/canvas/the-canvas/changing-the-background/) or [pick a theme](/help/canvas/themes/).
