The canvas is where you actually build diagrams in livediagram. It is an open, scrollable surface you can pan and zoom freely, with the palette pinned across the top of it for adding elements and switching tools. Everything works straight away, no sign-in required, so you can [open a fresh canvas](/new) and start placing shapes in seconds.

## The Palette

The **Palette** is a strip pinned to the top centre of the canvas, holding everything you add to it. It starts with two dropdowns: the canvas tool (**Select**, **Hand**, **Laser** and the other [selection modes](/help/selection-modes/)) and the palette category. Then come the category's first tiles, **More** (⋯) for the rest of the category, and **Search** for finding any element by name. The palette opens on **Popular**, the tiles most reached for in the current mode. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) for the details.

In the top-left corner, beside the strip, the **menu button** opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/), and on a computer the mode switch (**Diagram**, **Draw**, **Illustrate** and **Plan**) sits beside it.

## Adding Elements

Most elements are added straight from the palette, which is organised into categories you pick from its category dropdown. In Diagram mode that is **Popular**, then **Shapes**, **Write** (text, notes and pages) and **Draw** (pens and arrows), then **Build** (mind nodes, lanes, frames, tables), then **Icons**, **Stickers**, **Tech** and **Media**, then **Collaborate**. Each mode offers its own set: **Components**, **Devices** and **Data** (charts) belong to [Illustrate mode](/help/canvas/illustrate/), for example. Clicking a tile arms a combined gesture: a tap drops the element at your pointer, and a drag sizes it as you go. Single-key shortcuts skip the palette altogether, such as `R` for a rectangle, `T` for text and `N` for a sticky note. See [Adding Elements](/help/canvas/the-canvas/adding-elements/) for the full walkthrough.

## Moving Around

The canvas is effectively infinite, so you are never boxed in. Use the Hand tool, a middle-click drag, two fingers on a trackpad, or hold Space and drag to pan, and `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + scroll, a pinch, or the zoom controls in the bottom-right corner to scale the view. The [Panning and Zooming](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/) article covers every way to navigate.

Each tab has its own backdrop and theme. The paintbrush button beside the zoom controls opens the **Tab Look & Feel** dialog, where you can [change the canvas background](/help/canvas/the-canvas/changing-the-background/) or [pick a theme](/help/canvas/themes/).
