A **layout** is a ready-made arrangement put onto one page: a starting point you then make your own. It is made of ordinary elements (text, stat rows, charts, icons, image placeholders), so everything in it can be edited, restyled, moved or deleted like anything else on the canvas.

## Choosing a Layout

- **On an empty page**, press **Start from a layout** in the page's title bar, beside the cog. On a phone, or when the page is small on screen, it shows as just its icon.
- **On any page**, open the cog and choose the **Layouts** tab.

Each tile shows the layout as it would be built for **this** page, at its size and orientation, so what you see is what you get.

**Hover a tile to preview it on the page.** The page shows that layout in place of what is there now, in the page's own background; move away and your page comes back. A preview is only a picture: nothing is placed and nothing is added to your undo history until you press a tile.

## Placing It

- **On an empty page** a press places the layout straight away.
- **On a page with content**, the panel asks first: "Replace this page's content?", saying how many elements would go. Press **Replace** to clear the page and place the layout, or **Cancel** to keep what you have. Either way it is a single step: **Undo** brings the old content back.

After placing, nothing is selected and the panel closes, so the page reads clean.

## The Layouts

An empty infographic page also shows them inside itself, on a card: press a layout to place it, or **Hide** the card; it goes on its own once anything lands on the page. ## How Layouts Fit Your Page

- **Any size, any orientation.** A layout is measured against the page it lands on and keeps a margin all round. On a tall page it stacks; on a wide page (landscape, a slide) it sets things side by side, so Process and Timeline turn from a column of numbered discs into a strip across the page.
- **Type sized for a page.** Headlines are scaled to fill their space, body text uses the larger text sizes, so a page reads from across the room and exports exactly as it looks.
- **Your theme, your background.** A layout carries no colours of its own: the tab's theme paints it, and on a dark background its text is drawn light.
- **Change your mind later.** Turn the page or change its size and the layout re-fits, scaled down where it must be, never cut off.
