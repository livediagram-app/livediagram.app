A **logo page** is a kind of page in [Illustrate mode](/help/canvas/illustrate/): a square **artboard**, 1024 x 1024 pixels, to design a logo on. You build it from ordinary elements (shapes, icons, text, paths and strokes), with a few tools made for logos on top.

## Adding a Logo Page

- Press the **+** after the last page and choose **Logo**, or choose **Logo** when a new tab's first page asks **What Is This Page For?**
- A logo page is always the artboard: it has no other size and no portrait or landscape. It takes a **Background** like any page, but no pattern.
- Its margin is its **safe area**, 10% in from each edge: keep the mark inside it so it never touches the edge of an icon or an avatar.

## Drawing Your Mark

While your tab has a logo page, the palette has a **Logo** category, and it opens there when you add a logo page or move to one; moving to any other page turns it back to **Popular**. It holds the tools a mark is drawn with: the **Pen** (click for corners, drag for curves), the **Freehand pencil**, **Text**, a **Square**, **Circle** and **Diamond**, and your three markers from Draw mode. Pick a tool and drag on the artboard to draw; it puts itself down afterwards. Press a marker again while you hold it to change its colour and width.

Beside the page's cog sit **Mirror** and **Guides**, plus **Tidy Up** (the wand) when you have a drawing selected. Each works on that page only.

To start from nothing rather than a layout, press **Start From Scratch** at the foot of the layout card. The card won't come back on that page; its layouts are still in the page's cog panel, on **Layouts**.

Drew something a bit wobbly? Select it and press **Tidy Up**: hand-drawn lines become straight segments, nearly level or upright lines become exactly so, corners and straight runs near a guide or grid line line up with it, and a loop whose ends meet closes into a shape. One **Undo** brings the drawing back.

A good way to start: draw a few overlapping shapes, select them, and **Combine** them into one mark. Then add the name with **Text** and set its **Wordmark** type.

## Starting From a Layout

An empty logo page offers its layouts inside itself (and **Start from a layout** in its title bar), and the cog's **Layouts** tab offers the same: **twenty-five logo layouts** in four categories, each a complete mark to make your own.

- **Lockups**: an icon with the name, above, beside, below or inside a pill.
- **Wordmarks**: the name is the mark: large, in two lines, in spaced capitals, a monogram, or with an accent.
- **Emblems**: the name inside or round a shape: a badge, a seal, a stamp, a hexagon, a diamond.
- **Marks**: a symbol alone, several of them made of shapes ready to combine.

Each placeholder reads **Brand** and **Your tagline here**: double-click to type your own.

## Construction Guides

Guides sit over the artwork to build a balanced mark on: centre lines, diagonals, the safe area, a keyline circle and an inner circle, a keyline square, and an 8 x 8 grid. Moving or resizing an element snaps to the inner circle and the keyline square as well as the page's edges, centre and safe area. While guides show, a **Pen** click and the start and end of a pencil or marker stroke land on a guide near them, preferring where guides cross.

- Press **Guides** beside the page's cog: **Show Guides** at the top turns them on or off for that page (each page remembers its own), the tiles under it choose which guides show, and **Strength** sets them **Faint**, **Medium** or **Strong**.
- **Logo Guides** in **Settings** sets whether a page you haven't chosen for shows them.
- The parts and strength are also in **Settings**, under **Editor**, then **Illustrate**. They are yours: collaborators see their own.
- Guides never export, and they hide in Zen mode and while presenting.

## Mirror

- Press **Mirror** beside the page's cog and switch on **Mirror While Drawing**. While it is on, what you draw on that page (a stroke, a path, a shape) is repeated as you draw. One **Undo** removes the lot.
- Pick an **Axis**: **Vertical** reflects left to right, **Horizontal** top to bottom, **Both** four ways, and **Radial** turns your drawing round the page centre, with **Copies** from 3 to 8 (handy for emblems, stars and petals).
- With **Merge Into One** on, a drawing and its copies become one element when you let go; switch it off to keep each copy separate.
- The axes show as solid lines over the guides. Each page keeps its own settings while the tab is open.
- **Mirror Copy** (in the element menu or the command palette) reflects what you have selected, text and images included.
- Twins are ordinary elements: editing one leaves the other as it is.

## Wordmark Type

Select a text on a logo page, right-click, and open **Text**, then **Wordmark**:

- **Tracking**: the space between letters, tighter or looser.
- **Weight**: **Regular**, **Medium** or **Bold**.
- **Case**: **As Typed**, **Capitals** or **Lower Case**; what you typed is kept.
- **Arc**: bends the text along a circle, up or down, all the way round at 360. **Flat** straightens it. Arched text is one line.

Hovering a choice previews it; sliders preview as they move and are one **Undo** when you let go.

## Combining Shapes

Select two or more shapes, paths or strokes on the same logo page and press **Combine** in the selection toolbar (also in the selection menu and the command palette):

- **Unite**: everything any of them covers.
- **Subtract**: the bottom shape, less the ones above it.
- **Intersect**: only where they all overlap.
- **Exclude**: everything except where they overlap.

The result is one path in the bottom shape's place and colours, holes and all. If nothing is left (shapes that don't overlap, say), nothing changes and a message says so. A combined shape moves, resizes and restyles as one; its points are not edited one by one.

## Exporting

- A logo page on plain paper exports **see-through** as a PNG or SVG, so it sits over anything. Its PDF page stays white, and a background you chose is kept.
- **Logo Kit** downloads everything in one .zip: `logo.svg`, PNGs from 16 to 1024 pixels, and `favicon.ico`. Find it in **Export**: the **Logo Kit** card shows while the tab has a logo page, and asks which one when there are several.
