The Illustrate tour shows you where everything for making pages lives. Like the [Diagram Tour](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/), it works on the real editor rather than screenshots: it spotlights each part with a highlight ring and a short explanation. It adds nothing to your canvas, takes under a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## When It's Offered

The first time you work in [Illustrate mode](/help/canvas/illustrate/) on a tab you can edit (switching a tab to Illustrate, or opening a document whose tab opens in Illustrate), a centred **Getting started with Illustrate?** card asks whether you'd like a look: **Yes** starts it, **No** dismisses it.

It's offered on its own, whether or not you've taken the [Diagram Tour](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/), which belongs to Diagram mode and never shows in Illustrate. On an empty tab it waits until you've picked a template on **Start Illustrating** or closed it. The tour isn't offered on a locked tab, in an embed, or to someone who can only view or take part.

The offer appears **once ever**. However you answer (decline, skip midway, or finish), it won't come back on its own, and if you're signed in the answer follows you to every device. Turning on **Power User Mode** in Settings marks it as seen too.

## What It Covers

1. **Your Pages**: a page's name above its top-left corner. Press it to fit that page to the screen, or drag it to put your pages in another order.
2. **Page Settings**: the cog above a page's top-right corner, which opens its settings: its size, its background and a layout to start from. See [Infographic Layouts](/help/canvas/illustrate/infographic-layouts/).
3. **Add a Page**: the **+** after the last page, to add an infographic, article, slide or logo page.
4. **Slides**: the **Slides** button in the bottom-right corner, where you build a slide deck from your pages and present it.

A step whose control isn't on screen when the tour starts (a page too small on screen to show its name and cog, say) is left out, so the count stays honest.

Each step has **Next**, **Back** and **Skip tour**. The last card, **You're ready to illustrate**, links to the [Illustrate mode](/help/canvas/illustrate/) article and ends the tour with **Start illustrating**. If you leave Illustrate mode or switch tab mid-tour, it ends as skipped.

## Replaying the Tour

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Accessibility**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>Under **Onboarding Tutorials**, switch **Illustrate** back on.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Close Settings. If you're in Illustrate mode the tour's welcome card shows straight away;
  otherwise it's offered the next time you work in Illustrate.
</Step>
