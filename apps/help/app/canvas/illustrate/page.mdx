**Illustrate mode** is a way of working on a tab, made for building finished visuals: a poster, a one-pager, a set of social posts or a short deck. Instead of an endless canvas you work on **pages**, sheets laid out in a row, each one a picture you build up and then export or present. It is one of four modes, beside **Diagram**, **Draw** and **Plan**, and like them it never changes what is on the tab: the same elements are there in every mode. It is on for everyone, with nothing to turn on first.

## Two Kinds of Page

- **Infographic**: a sheet to lay out freely, starting from a layout if you like. Everything on this page is about infographic pages unless it says otherwise.
- **Article**: a page to write on, like a document, its writing flowing onto new pages as it grows. See [Writing Articles](/help/canvas/illustrate/articles/).

A tab's first page asks **What Is This Page For?** while it is the only page and still empty; after that, every page keeps the kind it was made with.

## Switching to Illustrate Mode

- **The switch** shows the current mode: press it and choose **Illustrate**. It sits in the **Palette** panel's title row (or, with the Toolbar layout, beside the menu button at the top left). **Shift+D** cycles through the modes too. See [Editor Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/) for how the switch works.
- **On a phone** there is no switch: open the tab menu (the **…** on the tab), choose **Opens in**, then **Illustrate**. That switches you as well.
- **Opens in:** to have a tab open in Illustrate mode for everyone, open the tab menu, choose **Opens in**, then **Illustrate**. Switching with the switch does the same for a tab you can edit: the tab then opens in the mode its editors last worked in, while anyone already on it stays in their own mode.
- People who can only view a tab see it in the mode it opens in. Event storming boards have no switch.

## Starting From a Template

In the New Document wizard, or Quick Start on a new tab, **Blank Illustration** (on the **Popular** shelf) starts a tab in Illustrate mode on one empty page, ready to ask what it is for. **Article** starts on one article page. To see every template that opens in Illustrate mode, open the **Show templates for** menu beside the search box and choose **Illustrate**: Event Poster, Year in Review, Résumé, Recipe Card, Data Story, How It Works, Versus and Social Carousel among them ([Templates](/help/canvas/templates/)).

## Your Content, Laid Out Into Pages

Switch a tab that already has a diagram, a mind map or a sketch on it into Illustrate mode and its content is laid out onto pages for you. Things joined by arrows, or sitting close together, stay together; each group gets a page of its own, turned portrait or landscape to suit its shape, in reading order (top to bottom, then left to right). Anything too big for its page is scaled down to fit, text included. At most 20 pages are made in one go; anything left over shares the last of them.

It is one step, and a message says so: **Undo** puts the tab back exactly as it was. Content you later leave off the pages (in the space around them) is gathered onto new pages the next time the tab enters the mode. Because every mode shows the same elements, Diagram mode sees the new arrangement too.

## Pages

- **Add a page** with the **+** after the last page, or the **+** at the end of the toolbar strip in the Toolbar layout, and choose **Infographic** or **Article** in **Add a Page**. A new infographic page takes the last one's size and orientation, on plain paper. A tab holds up to 100 pages.
- **Each page's label** sits above its top-left corner: its name, or "Page 2", then its size ("A4 · Portrait", "Square", "Story (9:16)") and its kind ("Infographic" or "Article"). **Press the label** to fit that page to the screen.
- **Drag a label sideways to reorder the pages.** A blue bar in the gap shows where the page will land; let go and it moves there with everything on it. **Escape** cancels.
- **Content belongs to its page.** Anything whose centre is on a page moves with it when the pages around it change, and whatever hangs off a page's edge is cut off at the edge, on screen and in exports, as if the pages were the only paper.
- **The page navigator** under each page (once there are two or more) shows "2 of 5" between **Previous page** and **Next page**, each fitting that page to the screen.
- **Snapping:** move or resize an element and it snaps to its page's edges, centre lines and margins, with the same guides as lining up with other elements.
- **An empty page** shows **Start from a layout** in its title bar, beside the cog. See [Infographic Layouts](/help/canvas/illustrate/infographic-layouts/).
- The Layers panel and its button are not offered in Illustrate mode; in their place is the **Slides** button (see [Exporting and Presenting Pages](/help/canvas/illustrate/exporting-pages/)).
- In [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) and while presenting, the pages show on their own, without labels, cogs or buttons.

## The Page Panel

Press the cog above a page's top-right corner to open its panel, which opens beside the page so the page stays in view (on a phone it slides up from the bottom of the screen). At the top is the page's **name** (leave it empty to go back to "Page 2"). Below are its tabs: **Page** and **Layouts** on an infographic page; **Page**, **Style** and **Text** on an article page (see [Writing Articles](/help/canvas/illustrate/articles/)). Along the bottom are the page's actions.

### Size and Orientation

The **Page** tab starts with the **Size** tiles, each drawn to scale:

| Size   | For                                                    |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| A4     | Print, the default                                     |
| Letter | US Letter, for print in North America                  |
| A3     | Posters                                                |
| Square | Square social posts                                    |
| Post   | Portrait posts for Instagram and LinkedIn (4:5)        |
| Story  | Stories (9:16), or a widescreen slide (16:9) landscape |

Below them, **Portrait** and **Landscape** turn the page (a square page has no orientation). Changing a page's size or orientation keeps everything on it: content that still fits stays as it is, and content that no longer fits is scaled down as one to fit inside the margins, its text with it. Nothing is cut off.

### Backgrounds and Patterns

- **From your theme**, in the first row: pale tints of the tab's theme colour, the theme's own fill, a deep shade, and a light and a dark gradient. Change the tab's theme and the row follows it.
- **Solid colours**, from Paper (the plain page) through pale Cream, Mist, Sky, Mint, Lavender, Blush and Sunshine to dark Ink, Midnight, Forest and Plum, plus the rainbow swatch for a **custom colour** of your own.
- **Gradients**: Sunrise, Ocean, Meadow, Peach, Dusk and Night.
- **Pattern**, over any of them: **None**, **Dots**, **Grid** or **Lines**, drawn faintly so they never fight the content.

**Hover a swatch to preview it** on the page; press it to keep it. A dark background keeps the page readable: elements without colours of their own are drawn in light ink on it, and text, arrows and icons you coloured yourself are lightened if they would no longer stand out (or darkened again on a light page).

### Page Actions

Along the bottom of the panel: **Duplicate page** (a copy right after it, with everything on it), **Move page left**, **Move page right** and **Delete page** (the page and everything on it; the pages after it close the gap). On an article page the same four act on the whole article. Each one is a single step that **Undo** reverses.

## Leaving Illustrate Mode

Diagram, Draw and Plan modes do not show pages, so switching away from a tab you can edit asks first:

- **A tab with pages** asks **Switch to Diagram?** (or Draw, or Plan) beside the switch: what you change there may not fit back onto your pages when you return. Press **Switch** to go, or **Cancel** to stay.
- **A tab with articles** asks **Turn Articles Into Pages?**: **Turn Into Pages**, **Keep as Articles** or **Cancel** (see [Writing Articles](/help/canvas/illustrate/articles/#exporting-presenting-and-leaving)).

## Good to Know

- Everything else works as always: sharing, live collaboration, comments, undo and redo. Collaborators see your pages and their changes live.
- Your mode is yours: someone else on the same tab can work in Diagram mode while you build pages.
- People who can only view a tab see the pages but not their cogs, buttons or the **+**.
