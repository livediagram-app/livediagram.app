In Illustrate mode, export and present work in **pages**, not the content of the whole tab.

## Exporting

Open **Export** as usual (see [Exporting a Tab](/help/tabs/export-tabs/)). Illustrate mode offers the formats that suit pages: **PDF**, **PNG** and **SVG**, plus **JSON** to keep a copy of the tab. The diagram-tool formats (Mermaid, Markdown, Excalidraw) are not offered, since a page is not a diagram.

Pick a format and choose **All pages** or **One page** above the preview:

- **PDF** starts on **All pages**: one PDF with every page in order, each at its own size and orientation. Paper sizes come out at print size: an A4 page is a true A4 page. With **One page** you get a one-page PDF of the page you pick.
- **PNG** and **SVG** start on **One page**, the first by default, and you get that page's image. With **All pages** you get a **.zip** holding one image per page, numbered in page order (`01 Page 1 - A4 - Portrait.png`, ...).

The file is named after the document and tab, plus the page's label when you export one page. With All pages, the page list picks which page the preview shows.

Each page exports **exactly its sheet**: its size, its background and pattern, and what is on it, cut off at its edges just as the canvas shows it. The space around the pages is never included. The plain paper exports white, and the preview shows the page you picked.

The **Isometric view** and **Background pattern** switches are not offered for pages: a page is its own background, and it is never tilted.

## Importing

In Illustrate mode, **Import** takes **JSON** only: a livediagram tab, pages and all. Mermaid, Markdown, Excalidraw and draw.io files are diagrams; switch to Diagram mode to import those.

## Turning Pages Into Slides

Pages are often presented, so its deck is one press away: in the bottom-right corner, where the Layers button sits in the other modes, the **Slides** button opens the Slide Deck panel just above it.

In Illustrate mode the panel adds slides **a page at a time**: pick a page from the list and press **Add as slide**. A page slide is the page itself:

- It shows whatever is on the page **now**, so it follows your edits, a new layout, a resize or a reorder without you touching the deck.
- It is framed to **exactly** the page.
- Its row reads the tab and the page ("Tab 1 · Page 2 · A4 · Portrait · Article"), and its thumbnail is the page on its background.
- Delete the page and the slide stays, empty, so you can see it and fix it.

Page slides sit in the same deck as any other slides, so a deck can mix infographic pages with slides built from other tabs. Name them, add notes, hide or reorder them as in [Building a Deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/building-a-deck/).

## Presenting

Press **Present** and each page fills the screen on its own: no labels, cogs or buttons around it, and none of its neighbouring pages beside it. Everything else about presenting is as for any deck; see [Presenting](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/).
