A blank canvas is freeing, but sometimes you would rather start with structure already in place. Templates give you a ready-made document as a starting point, filled with believable example content, so you can begin from something shaped roughly like what you need and adapt it, instead of building every diagram from nothing.

## Starting from a Template

When you [create a new document](/new), the **New Document** wizard asks what you are making: pick a mode (Diagram, Draw, Facilitate, Illustrate or Plan), name the document, choose where it lives, and press **Create**. The empty tab then opens on that mode's start, **Start Diagramming**, **Start Facilitating**, **Start Illustrating** or **Start Planning**: its **Recommended** templates first, then every template of that mode in groups beside them, with a search. Click a template and the tab fills with it; from there it is an ordinary document, to move, edit, add to and delete from as on any canvas.

Every new tab, and every tab you empty, offers the same start. Close it with its **×** to begin from a blank canvas; while the tab is still empty, **Show Templates** in the strip under the palette brings it back. Picked the wrong mode? The mode switch at the top of the start's list changes it, Plan included, while the tab is empty. In Draw the start is **Start Drawing**, with the pen already in hand, and since Draw has a dock rather than the palette, **Show Templates** sits in a pill at the top of the canvas.

**Something Else**, at the foot of a start's list, opens **Quick Start**: the whole catalogue, every mode at once. It opens on the **Popular** shelf. Below it, **Explore More Categories** opens any other category as a shelf of its own, and **Search templates...** finds a template across the whole catalogue. Click a template to fill the tab with it, or **Cancel** to keep it blank.

**Search templates...** finds a template by its name, its description, its category, or the everyday words people use for it: try "sprint", "1:1", "retro", "invoice", "mortgage", "crm", "erd" or "persona". Every word you type has to match.

Five blanks lead the **Popular** shelf, one for each way of working, and sit apart from the categories since starting from nothing is not a kind of diagram: **Blank Diagram** (an empty canvas), **Blank Whiteboard** (a board in [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/) for free drawing without distractions), **Blank Session** (an empty tab in [Facilitate mode](/help/canvas/facilitate-mode/), to run a retro, town hall or workshop with timers, votes and polls to hand), **Blank Illustration** (an empty page in [Illustrate mode](/help/canvas/illustrate/), for an infographic, an article, a slide or a logo) and **Blank Plan** (an empty tab in [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/) that opens on **Start Planning**, to pick the board (or Sheet) that fits the work). A sixth blank, **Blank Spreadsheet**, makes a tab holding one empty Sheet, ready to type into; search for it, or pick it from **Blank Slate** on the livediagram home page. After the blanks, Popular shows a few of the templates most people start from; choose a mode and Popular changes to that mode's favourites, its blank first.

## Finding Templates for a Mode

Every template opens in one mode: **Diagram**, **Draw**, **Facilitate**, **Illustrate** or **Plan**. The small icon before a template's name says which. To see only one mode's templates, choose it in the dropdown to the left of the search box (hover it, or click it, to see the choices, each with how many templates it holds). Popular, the categories, their counts and your search results all narrow to that mode, and if the template you had selected is not of that mode, the selection moves to the mode's blank. **Everything** shows every template again. The filter starts on Everything each time the wizard opens, unless you came from a link that starts it on a mode.

- **Draw** templates are drawn by hand, ready to draw over: **Sketchnote**, **Rich Picture**, **Comic Strip**, **Doodle Warm-Up**, **Paper Prototype**, **Journey Doodle**, **Pre-Mortem** and **Idea Garden**.
- **Facilitate** templates are sessions to run with your team: **Blank Session**, **Retrospective**, **Start, Stop, Continue**, **Mad, Sad, Glad**, **4Ls retrospective**, **Sailboat retrospective**, **Town Hall Q&A**, **Lean Coffee**, **Crazy 8s**, **Meeting agenda**, and the boards that come with a dot vote, **Affinity map**, **Prioritization matrix** and **Risk matrix**. See [Facilitate Mode](/help/canvas/facilitate-mode/).
- **Illustrate** templates open on pages of their own: **Article**, **Slide deck**, **Logo design**, **Group card**, **Event Poster**, **Year in Review**, **Résumé**, **Recipe Card**, **Data Story**, **How It Works**, **Versus** and **Social Carousel**.
- **Plan** templates set up a way of working across several tabs, with no cards yet: **Kanban Board**, **Project Planner**, **Bug Tracker**, **Team Retro**, **Weekly Planner**, **Content Calendar**, **Hiring Pipeline**, **OKRs**, **Product Launch** and **Feedback Board**; or a spreadsheet ready to use: **Budget Planner**, **Timesheet**, **Contact List**, **Task Tracker**, **Invoice**, **Expense Report**, **Loan Calculator**, **Sales Pipeline**, **Inventory**, **Guest List**, **Grade Book** and **Habit Tracker**.
- Every other template is a **Diagram** template.

Each one arrives as real elements rather than a picture: a Gantt chart's bars are shapes you drag, a wireframe's frames are [device frames](/help/palette/devices/) you can rearrange. Nothing in a template is locked or special.

## Changing the Theme

A new document starts on the Default theme, so the wizard only asks for a template and where to save it (Quick Start keeps the tab's current theme). Once you are on the canvas, click the paintbrush button in the bottom-right corner to open the **Tab Look & Feel** dialog and try a different [theme](/help/canvas/themes/), including any of your [custom themes](/help/canvas/themes/custom-themes/): it recolours the whole template at once.

## Making It Your Own

A template is just a head start, not a constraint. Everything in it is fully editable: rename and recolour the elements, restyle the whole thing with a different theme, or strip out the parts you do not need. Because the document works the same as any other, you can also add [tabs](/help/tabs/using-tabs/), invite others, and share it once you have shaped it into what you want.

<Tip>
  Templates pair naturally with themes: pick a layout that fits your content, then try a few themes
  from the Tab Look & Feel dialog to find the look you want. Double-click a theme to apply it and
  close the dialog in one go.
</Tip>
