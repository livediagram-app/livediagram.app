The built-in catalogue covers a lot of ground, but sometimes you want your own palette: brand colours, a house diagram style, or a notation of your own. Custom colour schemes let you author exactly that, save it, and reuse it. A saved custom colour scheme behaves just like a built-in one: pick it on a tab and new elements adopt its colours. Custom colour schemes work for everyone, signed in or not, so the canvas keeps working without a sign-in.

## Building a colour scheme

You build a colour scheme from the **Custom** category in the Tab Look & Feel Colour scheme tab. Choose **+ New colour scheme** to open the colour scheme builder. It is fast by default and deep on demand:

<Step n={1}>
  Give the colour scheme a name, then set the **Base colours**: **Background** for the canvas, **Fill** for
  element fill, and **Stroke** for outlines. From just these three, livediagram derives sensible
  defaults for everything else, so a usable colour scheme exists after three clicks.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  **Text** sits beside them in the same row. It follows the colours above until you set it, so touch
  it only when the derived one is not what you want.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Expand **Pattern** to pick the backdrop pattern, choose an animated one, and set its opacity.
  There is no pattern colour to pick: it tints itself from your background.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Expand **Per-shape colours**, an advanced option, to give each shape kind its own fill, border,
  and text, which is how you reproduce a notation like "diamonds are amber".
</Step>

A live preview shows a few sample shapes in your in-progress colour scheme, rendered the same way the canvas would, so you see the result before saving. **Save** stores the colour scheme and applies it to the active tab.

## Saving and reusing

Custom colour schemes are tied to your account and listed under the **Custom** category wherever you pick a colour scheme: the Tab Look & Feel dialog and the new-diagram template picker both show them, so you can apply or author one the moment you start a diagram. If you sign up after creating colour schemes as a guest, they carry over to your account.

## Managing your colour schemes

The [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) has a **Colour schemes** entry under its Library section where you can manage them all in one place: each is shown as a preview card you can **Edit**, **Duplicate**, or **Delete**.

## Editing and deleting

Editing a colour scheme opens the same builder again. Deleting one is safe for diagrams that still reference it: they fall back to the default look and keep rendering rather than breaking. If you delete a colour scheme that the diagram you have open is using, that tab reverts to the default colour scheme straight away so the change is visible.

<Tip>
  To tweak a built-in colour scheme, start a new custom colour scheme from the same colours and edit from there. The
  shipped catalogue stays read-only, so your edits never touch the originals.
</Tip>
