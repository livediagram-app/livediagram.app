The built-in catalogue covers a lot of ground, but sometimes you want your own palette: brand colours, a house diagram style, or a notation of your own. Custom themes let you author exactly that, save it, and reuse it. A saved custom theme behaves just like a built-in one: pick it on a tab and the tab takes its colours. Custom themes work for everyone, signed in or not.

## Building a Theme

Open the **Tab Look & Feel** dialog from the paintbrush button in the bottom-right corner of the editor. On its **Theme** tab, open the **Custom** category and choose the **New theme** card to open the theme builder in place of the theme grid. (To start from a built-in theme instead, hover its card and press **Copy**: the builder opens with that theme's colours filled in.) The builder is fast by default and deep on demand:

<Step n={1}>
  Give the theme a name, then set the **Base colours**: **Background** for the canvas, **Fill** for
  element fill, and **Stroke** for outlines. From just these three, livediagram derives sensible
  defaults for everything else, so a usable theme exists after three clicks.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  **Text** sits beside them in the same row. It follows the **Fill** colour, staying readable
  against it, until you set it yourself, so touch it only when the derived one is not what you want.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  The **Pattern** section (open to start with) picks the backdrop pattern, static or animated, and
  sets its opacity. There is no pattern colour to pick: it tints itself from your background.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Expand **Per-shape colours**, an advanced option, to give each shape kind its own fill, outline,
  and text, which is how you reproduce a notation like "diamonds are amber". A shape kind you leave
  alone uses the base colours, and its reset button puts it back.
</Step>

A live preview across the top shows a small diagram in your in-progress theme, with the real pattern behind it, so you see the result before saving. Every colour box can also copy its colour and paste it into another, which saves retyping a brand colour. **Save theme** stores the theme and applies it to the active tab; **Cancel** (or **Back**) leaves without saving.

## Saving and Reusing

Custom themes belong to you, not to a document, so they are listed under the **Custom** category in the Tab Look & Feel dialog on every tab and every document you open. As a guest they are kept with your browser's guest identity, and if you sign up after creating themes as a guest, they carry over to your account.

Applying a custom theme saves its colours on the tab and its elements, so collaborators see the same look; only you see the theme itself in your Custom category.

## Managing Your Themes

The [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) has a **Themes** entry under **Library** in its sidebar where you can manage them all in one place (see [Themes Library](/help/explorer/themes-library/)). Each is shown as a preview card you can **Edit**, **Duplicate**, or **Delete**, beside a **New theme** card. In the dialog's Custom category, hovering a theme's card offers Edit and Delete too.

## Editing and Deleting

Editing a theme opens the same builder again, and **Save changes** updates it. Deleting one asks you to confirm first, and is safe for documents that still reference it: they fall back to the default look and keep rendering rather than breaking. If you delete a theme that the document you have open is using, the tabs using it revert to the default theme straight away so the change is visible.

<Tip>
  To tweak a built-in theme, press **Copy** on its card and edit from there. The shipped catalogue
  stays read-only, so your edits never touch the originals.
</Tip>
