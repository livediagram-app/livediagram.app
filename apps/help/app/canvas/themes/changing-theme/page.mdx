Changing a diagram's look is a matter of picking a new theme and watching the canvas update. livediagram brings the theme picker front and centre in a dedicated dialog, so there is plenty of room to compare looks.

</Figure>

## Opening the Dialog

Click the **paintbrush button** in the bottom-right corner of the editor, beside the zoom controls (it is there on every screen size, phones included). It opens the **Tab Look & Feel** dialog on its **Theme** tab. You can also run **Open theme** from the [command palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/). The dialog also has a **Canvas** tab for the [backdrop pattern and colours](/help/canvas/the-canvas/changing-the-background/) and a **Font** tab for the [tab's default font](/help/canvas/text-and-fonts/choosing-fonts/), and you can switch between them from the tab strip below the header.

The paintbrush only appears for people who can edit the tab, so it is missing on a view-only link, in zen mode, and in [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/).

The dialog is an editor, not a wizard: there is no Apply or Cancel. Every change applies live to the active tab, and closing it (with the X, the Escape key, or a click outside it) simply dismisses it without rolling anything back. Undo takes a theme change back like any other edit.

## Browsing by Category

The Theme tab opens on an overview: a **Default** quick-pick plus a card for each category (**Formal**, **Cool**, **Warm**, **Dark** and **Multi-colour**), alongside a **Custom** category for your own [saved themes](/help/canvas/themes/custom-themes/). If the tab already uses a theme from a category, the dialog opens straight into that category.

<Step n={1}>
  Click a category to drill into its themes. **All themes** at the top steps back out.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Click a theme to apply it live. Double-click a theme to apply it and close the dialog in one move.
</Step>

The themes inside a category are shuffled each time you open the dialog, so different ones greet you first. While you browse the Dark themes in light chrome (or a light category in dark chrome), a **Turn on Dark mode** (or **Turn on Light mode**) switch below the grid matches the editor's own appearance to what you are looking at.

To build on a built-in theme, hover its card and press **Copy**: the theme builder opens with its colours filled in, ready to save as a [custom theme](/help/canvas/themes/custom-themes/).

## Recolouring Elements

Applying a theme sets the tab's backdrop and recolours the elements on it, but keeps colours you have hand-set on individual shapes and text, so deliberate customisations survive the switch. Arrows are the one exception: every arrow's stroke always snaps to the new theme's colour, because connectors left on an old hue read as broken. Sticky notes keep their own note colours.

If you want a clean slate, **Reset elements to theme** at the bottom of the Theme tab repaints every element on the tab to the theme's defaults, overriding your customisations. A shape carrying a [style preset](/help/palette/shapes/style-presets/) keeps its preset, redrawn in the theme's colours.

<Tip>
  Use double-click to apply and close once you have found the theme you want, and single clicks
  while you are still comparing options.
</Tip>
