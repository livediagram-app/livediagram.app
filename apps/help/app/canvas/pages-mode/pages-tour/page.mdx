The Pages tour shows you how each kind of page is made, on your own page. There is one tour for each kind: **slides**, **infographics**, **articles** and **logos**. Like the [Diagram Tour](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/), it works on the real editor rather than screenshots, and it opens the real controls for you: the Layout and Background popovers, the page's settings, the Slide Deck. Anything you pick while a step is open is kept. It takes about a minute, and you can leave at any point.

The tour never covers your page. It speaks from a strip just above the tab bar, and the canvas makes room for it: the tour's words and buttons sit there, and on the page a ring with the step's number marks the control it's about. Nothing is dimmed, and everything stays usable.

## When It's Offered

The first time you work on a page of a kind in [Pages mode](/help/canvas/pages-mode/) on a tab you can edit, the strip above the tab bar asks whether you'd like a look (**New to slides?**, for a slide), and says how long it is. **Take the Tour** starts it; **No Thanks** dismisses it. If several kinds are on screen, it runs on the page you can see most of. Add a page of another kind later and that kind's tour is offered then.

You can also just carry on. Left alone for a few seconds, the offer folds into a small **Slides Tour** chip in the tab bar, beside Search: press it to start the tour, or its **×** to dismiss it. Leaving it isn't an answer, so the offer comes back on your next visit until you take or dismiss it.

It waits while **Start Making Pages** is open and while a new tab's first page asks **What Is This Page For?**. It isn't offered on a locked tab or page, in an embed, or to someone who can only view or take part.

Each kind's tour is answered **once**. However you answer (dismiss, end it midway, or finish), it won't come back on its own for that kind, and if you're signed in the answer follows you to every device. Turning on **Power User Mode** in Settings marks them all as seen. If you answered the single Pages tour before it had one tour per kind, each kind's tour is offered to you once more. Until you answer, it keeps coming back: leaving it alone, or moving on before you answer, isn't an answer.

## What Each Tour Covers

**Slides**

1. **Choose a Layout**: points at the slide's own **Start From a Layout** card on a new slide, or opens its **Layout** popover. Hover a layout to preview it, press one to place it.
2. **Change the Background**: opens the **Background** popover: colours, gradients, animated backgrounds and pictures.
3. **Add It to the Deck**: the slide's **Add to Slides** button, which becomes the eye that hides or shows it once it's in.
4. **Add Another Slide**: the **+** after the last page.
5. **Present**: opens the Slide Deck from the **Slides** button, where you order the slides and press **Present**.

**Infographics**: the page's name, **Start From a Layout**, **Change the Background**, **Page Size** (the page's settings), **Add a Page** and **Present It** (any page can be a slide). See [Infographic Layouts](/help/canvas/pages-mode/infographic-layouts/).

**Articles**: writing on the page, the page toolbar (held up for you while its step is showing), and the article's style and its fonts and spacing (the page's **Style** and **Text** settings). See [Articles](/help/canvas/pages-mode/articles/).

**Logos**: the logo layouts, guides, mirror drawing and combining shapes, as the logo page's Show Me runs them. See [Logo Pages](/help/canvas/pages-mode/logo-pages/).

Some steps have one thing to try, such as **Try it: Pick any layout**. Do it on your page and the step ticks itself off, then moves on by itself a moment later, so you see your pick land. **Next** still works if you'd rather just look.

A step whose control isn't on screen when the tour starts (the deck button and **Slides** on a phone, say) is left out, so the count stays honest. Each step has **Next**, **Back** and **End Tour**; leaving a step closes whatever it opened. If you leave Pages mode, switch tab, or delete or lock the page mid-tour, it ends as skipped.

## Show Me on Any Page

Every page's settings (its cog) carry **Show Me** at the top, which runs that page's tour there and then, whenever you like, in the same strip, without asking first.
