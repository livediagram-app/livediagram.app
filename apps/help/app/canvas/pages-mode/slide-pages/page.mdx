A **slide page** is a kind of page in [Pages mode](/help/canvas/pages-mode/) made for a presentation: one slide of a deck, always landscape, laid out to be read across a room. You build it like any page, from shapes, text, icons, images and charts.

## Adding a Slide Page

- New to slides? Open the page's cog and press **Show Me** at the top of its panel: it opens the Layout and Background popovers on your own slide, points at its deck button, and opens the Slide Deck where you press **Present**. The [Pages Tour](/help/canvas/pages-mode/pages-tour/) offers the same walkthrough the first time you work on a slide.

- Press the **+** after the last page and choose **Slide** in **Add a Page**, or choose **Slide** when a new tab's first page asks **What Is This Page For?**
- To start a whole deck at once, the Slide deck template opens a six-slide pitch (title, problem, solution, traction, team and the ask) on slide pages. Find it on **Start Making Pages**, an empty Pages tab's start, or by searching any start ([Templates](/help/canvas/templates/)).
- A slide page is always **landscape**. Its page panel's **Page** tab offers two sizes and no orientation: **Slide** (widescreen 16:9) and **Classic** (the older 4:3). Its label reads, for example, "Page 3 · Slide (16:9)".
- It takes a **Background** and a pattern like any page, from the **Background** button in its title bar or its panel's **Background** tab ([Pages Mode](/help/canvas/pages-mode/#backgrounds-and-patterns)). Its panel holds the **Page** and **Background** tabs.
- A slide page's Background also has **Animated**: twenty-four moving backgrounds such as **Aurora**, **Starfield**, **Retro Grid**, **Waves**, **Warp**, **Orbits**, **Confetti** and **Threads**, each in your theme's colours, with **Colours** below to choose how deep or bright. They move on the slide, while you present and in a GIF export; the other exports show them still, and with **Reduce Motion** on they stay still on screen too.

## Slide Layouts

A slide page's **Layout** button, in its title bar beside **Background** and the cog, opens its layouts on any slide, empty or not, and an empty slide page also shows a card of layouts over it. They offer the **slide layouts** instead of the infographic ones, built for a landscape slide in your tab's theme, with type sized for a room. They are grouped into six categories:

| Category       | Layouts                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Openers        | Title Slide, Section Header, Agenda, Title and Image, Keynote Title, Opening Question, About the Speaker, Objectives, Agenda Grid, Agenda and Image               |
| Content        | Title and Bullets, Two Columns, Image and Text, Statement, Quote, Three Points, Full Image, Key Takeaway, Four Ideas, Title Only, Sidebar Title, Compare Two      |
| Pictures       | Text and Picture, Picture and Text, Hero Picture, Picture Band, Picture and Card, Quote and Picture, Two Pictures, Three Pictures, Picture Mosaic                 |
| Data           | Big Number, Number and Picture, Key Stats, Chart Story, Comparison, Three Big Numbers, Big Chart, Chart and Insights, Goals Progress, KPI Dashboard, Market Share |
| Steps and Time | Process, Timeline, Roadmap, Process Flow, Milestones, Now, Next, Later, Funnel, Cycle, Project Plan                                                               |
| Closers        | Thank You, Team, Team of Three, Summary, Call to Action, Next Steps, Contact, Questions, Thank You and Image, Resources                                           |

That is 61 in all. A few are worth knowing by name:

- **Title Slide**: a large title, a subtitle, an accent rule, and the presenter and date at the foot.
- **Section Header**: a big section number, an accent rule, the section's title and a line.
- **Title and Bullets**: a title over five bullet points.
- **Two Columns**: a title over two columns, each a heading and three bullet points.
- **Image and Text**: an image filling the left half, with a title, a line and three bullet points to its right.
- **Text and Picture**: a title, a line and three points on the left, a picture filling the right half right to the slide's edges. **Picture and Text** is the same the other way round.
- **Hero Picture**: a picture filling the whole slide, darkened, with a large title over it in white.
- **Statement**: one sentence set very large and centred, with a line under it.
- **Thank You**: a large "Thank you", "Questions?" and a contact line.
- **Opening Question**: one question set large to open the talk, and a line under it.
- **Three Big Numbers**: three figures across, all the same size, a caption and a note under each.
- **Now, Next, Later**: a roadmap in three columns of cards.
- **Call to Action**: one bold ask, centred, with a button-like pill and where to start.

The rest are the same layouts as the infographic ones of the same name. Hover a layout to preview it on the page, and click to place it: it is placed at once, your titles, words and pictures carried into it, and one **Undo** brings the old layout back. Every layout is ordinary elements holding sample text, so change anything you like. See [Infographic Layouts](/help/canvas/pages-mode/infographic-layouts/) for how the browser works.

<Tip>
  Arrows on a slide stay on that slide: in Pages mode an arrow's end only pins to an element on the
  same page, so nothing on one slide is tied to another.
</Tip>
