The Facilitate tour shows you where everything for running a session lives. Like the [Welcome Tour](/help/getting-started/welcome-tour/), it works on the real editor rather than screenshots: it spotlights each part with a highlight ring and a short explanation. It adds nothing to your canvas, takes under a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## When It's Offered

The first time you work in [Facilitate mode](/help/canvas/facilitate-mode/) on a tab you can edit (switching a tab to Facilitate, opening a document whose tab opens in Facilitate, or making a retro or a Blank Session), a centred **Welcome to Facilitate** card asks whether you'd like a look: **Show me around** starts it, **No thanks** dismisses it.

If you haven't taken the Welcome Tour yet, that comes first, and its last card, **You're ready to go**, offers this tour: **Show me Facilitate** goes straight into it, and **Start creating** finishes without it. Either way you won't be asked again with a separate card. The tour isn't offered on a locked tab, in an embed, or to someone who can only view or take part.

The offer appears **once ever**. However you answer (decline, skip midway, or finish), it won't come back on its own, and if you're signed in the answer follows you to every device. Turning on **Power User Mode** in Settings marks it as seen too.

## What It Covers

1. **Collaborate**: the category picker's **Collaborate** heading, with **Ask**, **Tools**, **Record**, **React**, **Selection Mode** and **Navigate**. See [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/). The rest of the palette is the [Welcome Tour](/help/getting-started/welcome-tour/)'s.
2. **Run the room**: the **Timer**, **Vote** and **Poll** buttons in the bottom-right corner, which run for everyone without placing anything. See [Session Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).
3. **Bring people in**: the **Share** button, where a Participant link lets people add notes, vote and answer without changing the rest. Left out where there is no Share button.

Each step has **Next**, **Back** and **Skip tour**. The last card, **You're ready to facilitate**, links to the [Facilitate mode](/help/canvas/facilitate-mode/) article and ends the tour with **Start facilitating**. If you leave Facilitate mode or switch tab mid-tour, it ends as skipped.

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Accessibility**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>Switch **Show Facilitate Tour** back on.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Close Settings. If you're in Facilitate mode the tour's welcome card shows straight away;
  otherwise it's offered the next time you work in Facilitate.
