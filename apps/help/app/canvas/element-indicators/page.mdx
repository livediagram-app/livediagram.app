<ArticleLayout title="Element Indicators" description="The quiet marks that tell you an element carries something you can't otherwise see, and how to change or hide them." categoryTitle="Canvas" categorySlug="canvas">

Plenty of what an element carries is invisible on the canvas: the [link](/help/canvas/links/) it jumps to, the [note](/help/canvas/notes/) behind it, an [action](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/) waiting on someone, a [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) thread. **Element indicators** are the small icons that give these away. They read like metadata printed on a card: quiet until you look for them, and one click opens whatever they point to.

## What They Show

An element shows an indicator for each of these it has, always in this order:

- **Link**: the element links to another tab, another document or a web address. Hovering it shows a card naming the destination.
- **Note**: the element has a note.
- **Action**: the element has an open assigned action. Hovering it reads **Assigned to** and the person's name. A completed action stops showing.
- **Comments**: the element has unresolved comments, with the count beside the icon. Resolving the last thread clears it.

Comments come last because they are the most frequent and the most urgent, and the far end is the first place your eye lands.

A few elements show these things on their own face instead, so they carry no indicator for them: an [annotation](/help/canvas/annotations/)'s marker is its note, a link card is its link, a comment pin or comment panel shows its own comments, and an action panel its own action.

A **mind map root** can also carry two commands, **Edit Outline** and **Tidy Map**. They work a little differently; see [Mind Map Commands](#mind-map-commands) below.

## Choosing a Style

Open [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), go to **Editor** and pick an option for **Element Indicators**. It is your own preference, so it changes what you see and nobody else's view.

- **Top** (the default) prints the icons inside the element along its top, in the element's own text colour at half strength, so they suit any fill, theme or style.
- **Footer** puts a row along the inside of the element's bottom edge, the way a task card shows its details, with a word beside each icon: **Link**, **Note**, the assignee's initials on a small coloured disc with **Action**, and the comment count. When the words don't fit, the row drops them and keeps the icons, disc and count.
- **Off** draws no indicators at all. Everything they open is still in the element's menu (see [With Indicators Off](#with-indicators-off)).

## Where They Sit

Indicators always sit **inside** the element's outline, never across it:

- On a box-like element (a rectangle, rounded rectangle, pill, mind node, card or panel), Top sits at the **top-right**.
- On a round or pointed shape (a circle, diamond, hexagon, cloud, triangle, trapezoid, star or actor), Top is **centred** along the top, since there is no corner for it.
- Footer starts at the bottom-left of a box, and is centred on the same round or pointed shapes.

They look for a spot clear of the element's label and icon first. When there isn't one, the label shifts just far enough to make room (down under Top, up over Footer), and a label set to scale to fit shrinks a little instead.

On an element too small, thin or spiky to hold them (a small pill, a progress bar, a star, an actor, a small diamond), the icons gather in a small **chip on the element's edge** instead, half in and half out, drawn in the element's own fill.

## Using Them

Each indicator is a button. Click it to:

- **Link**: follow the link, to the other tab, the other document, or the web page in a new tab.
- **Note**: open the note.
- **Action**: open the action, showing who it is assigned to.
- **Comments**: open the comment thread.

Hovering the element brings its indicators up a little, and the one under the pointer to full strength. From the keyboard, each indicator is reachable with **Tab** and shows a focus ring.

## While an Element Is Selected

Selecting an element brings its own handles, **+** buttons and toolbar onto the same edges, so its indicators **shrink and fade away** while it is selected and come back when you deselect it. Nothing else moves: the label stays exactly where it was. To reach a link, note, action or comments on a selected element, deselect it or use its menu.

## Mind Map Commands

A mind map root with at least one child carries **Edit Outline** (edit the whole map as a list) and **Tidy Map** (lay the map out neatly again). These are things you **do**, not things the root **has**, so they stay hidden until you **hover** the root. Their space is kept free so nothing shifts when they appear. In Top they lead the cluster, and in Footer they end the row.

They aren't shown while the root is selected, on a root with no children, on a [locked tab](/help/tabs/locking-tabs/), or to someone who can only view the document. Both are always in the root's **Mind Map** menu section too, which is the way in from the keyboard or on a touch screen. See [Edit Outline](/help/palette/mind-maps/edit-outline/).

## With Indicators Off

Turning indicators **Off** hides them on every element, and a mind map root's commands no longer appear on hover. Nothing becomes unreachable:

- Links and notes are in the element's menu under **Resources**.
- Actions and comments are in the element's menu under **Collaborate**, and every open thread and action is listed in the **Collaborate** panel.
- **Edit Outline** and **Tidy Map** are in the root's **Mind Map** section.

## When They Don't Show

- **Zoomed far out**: below 40% zoom, indicators hide, along with the padlock on locked elements and the avatars of collaborators who have an element selected. An overview is for shape, not detail. See [Pan and Zoom](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/).
- **Exports**: indicators are editor chrome, so PNG and SVG exports and document thumbnails never include them.
- **View-only**: someone who can only view the document still sees links, notes, actions and comments, and can open each one to read it. They don't get the mind map commands.
- **Embeds**: a document [embedded](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) in another page shows no indicators at all, whether the embed is for viewing or editing and whatever style you chose. Open it in livediagram to see them.
- **Community**: a visitor viewing a document in the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/) sees no comment counts, since there is no comments panel for them to open. The other indicators still show.

<Tip>
  [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) hides the editor's panels and bars, but indicators are part of
  the elements, so they stay. Set **Element Indicators** to **Off** too for the cleanest possible
  canvas.
</Tip>

## On a Phone or Tablet

Indicators work the same on a touch screen: tap one to open what it points to. There is no pointer to hover with, so a mind map root's commands don't appear; long-press the root and use its **Mind Map** section instead. See [Mobile](/help/supported-devices/mobile/).

<Note>
  Element indicators are separate from the other marks an element can wear. The padlock on a [locked
  element](/help/canvas/locking/), the avatars of collaborators who have it selected, and the tally
  on an element during a [vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/) are not affected by this
  setting.
</Note>
