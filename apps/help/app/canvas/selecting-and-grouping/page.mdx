Diagrams rarely come together one shape at a time. Once a few elements are on the canvas you will want to move several at once, keep related pieces together, and make a row of shapes share the same look. livediagram gives you the tools for exactly that: selecting many elements, frames, and the format painter. None of them need a sign-in, so you can [open a canvas](/new) and try them straight away.

## Selecting More Than One

The quickest way to grab several elements is the marquee: drag across an empty patch of canvas and a selection rectangle picks up every element that sits entirely inside it. You can also build a selection click by click with Shift, or press `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `A` to select everything on the tab. Either way, the selection becomes a single thing you can move, copy, lock, or restyle in one go. See [Marquee and Multi-Select](/help/canvas/selecting-and-grouping/multi-select/) for every way to gather a selection.

## Keeping Elements Together

A selection scatters as soon as you click away. When some elements should keep travelling together, draw a [frame](/help/palette/build/frames/) around them: moving the frame carries everything inside it. And when the thing you are building is really one object, such as a banner, a KPI row or a website header, reach for a [component](/help/palette/components/), which is a single element rather than pieces you have to hold together.

## Copying a Look

Once you have styled one element just right, the [format painter](/help/selection-modes/format-painter/) copies that look onto others in a couple of clicks, so a whole diagram can share one consistent style without re-setting each colour and border by hand.
