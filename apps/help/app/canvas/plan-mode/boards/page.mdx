A **board** is where [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/) work happens: columns your cards move through, a header of widgets that tell you how it is going, and the [cards](/help/canvas/plan-mode/cards/) themselves.

## Pick a Board

An empty Plan tab shows **Start with a Board** in the middle. Each choice is a small picture of the board over its name and what it is for; pick one and it is placed, empty, in the middle of your view. Every board, the Archive board included, is also in the palette's **Boards** category: press one and click the canvas, or drag it out to the size you want. People who can only view the document don't see the picker.

</Figure>

| Board                                        | For                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Blank** (**Board** in the palette)         | A board with no columns: name the first one and build from there.   |
| **Kanban**                                   | Flow from Backlog to Done, with WIP limits on the busy columns.     |
| **Sprint**                                   | A sprint's tasks, a row per person, with estimates.                 |
| **Retro**                                    | Notes hidden until you reveal them, then everyone votes.            |
| **Roadmap**                                  | Projects on Now, Next and Later.                                    |
| **Bug Triage**                               | Bugs from New to Fixed, a row per priority.                         |
| **Weekly Planner** (**Week** in the palette) | A column a day, Monday to Friday.                                   |
| **All Cards**                                | Every card in the document, a row per status, so nothing gets lost. |
| **Archive** (palette only)                   | Every archived card, out of the way, ready to restore.              |

A board placed from the palette or the picker starts **empty**: its columns are its own, so cards from your other boards stay where they are. Boards from a [Plan template](/help/canvas/plan-mode/#plan-templates) are set up for their job, share columns between tabs where the work hands over, and come with no cards either.

## Columns

<Figure caption="A column's cog opens its settings, from its name and colour down to Remove Column.">
  <PlanColumnSettingsScene />
</Figure>

Hover a column's head and press its cog (always there on a touch screen) to:

- **Rename** it and give it a **colour** (none, or one of eight).
- Set a **WIP Limit**: the count turns amber when the column holds more. It warns; it never refuses a card.
- Make it **Counts as Done**, so its cards fill the board's Completion and count as done in charts.
- Set its **Width**: one, two or three slots across the board.
- **Move Left** or **Move Right**, **Add Column After**, or **Remove Column** (it asks where its cards go: **Move and Remove**, or **Keep It**). A board's last column can't be removed.

A board holds 1 to 12 columns. A board with no columns (a new Blank board) shows **No columns yet** and asks you to name the first.

## The Board's Own Settings

Right-click the board (or select it and press **⋯**):

- **Board:** its **Title**, and its **Swimlanes**: **No Swimlanes**, **By Assignee**, **By Card Type**, **By Priority**, **By Project** or **By Status**. Under **By a Field**, pick any field your card types have, your own included (say a **Customer** you added to Projects), or Labels, Estimate, Start Date or Due Date. A choice field gets a row per option, a checkbox a **Yes** and a **No** row, numbers and dates run lowest first, and text runs A to Z; cards without a value sit in the last row. Dropping a card into a row sets that field (into a label's row, that label moves to the front of the card's labels). If the field is later taken off its type, the board simply shows no rows until it comes back. **Add to Slides** puts the whole board in the [Slide Deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/).
- **Cards:** which card types **New Cards Can Be** (a retro takes Notes and Ideas; a sprint takes Tasks and Actions; at least one stays on), the **Card Size** (**Minimal**, **Compact** or **Detailed**), and which fields each card shows. A field the chosen size can't show keeps its setting, with its tile dimmed.

Each swimlane folds away on its own from its label, just for you. Several people can set up one board at once: each change travels on its own, so two people editing different columns both keep their work. Board settings are undone with **Undo** like any other edit.

While a board is selected, the grip beside its title is where to take hold of it to move it. In Plan mode the header moves the board; the columns and cards don't.

## What the Board Shows

<Figure caption="A board's header holds its widgets; each column shows its name, its count and, past its WIP limit, a warning.">
  <PlanBoardScene />
</Figure>

- **Every board shows every card whose status is one of its columns.** There is no per-board filter by type or label.
- **Not on this board:** cards whose status no column holds are counted in the header ("3 not on this board"); open the count to move each to a column.
- **Columns fill the board:** make a board taller and its columns run to the bottom edge. A column with more cards than fit scrolls inside itself; the board never grows on its own.
- **Card sizes:** **Minimal** shows the title alone; **Compact** the title over a line of number, priority, due date, votes and assignee; **Detailed** (the default) everything, down to a description excerpt, custom fields shown on cards and checklist progress.

## Widgets

The header holds **widgets**: Item Count, Completion, Filter, Only Mine, People, Not on Board, Card Types, WIP Alerts, Due Soon, Votes Left, Points, Priorities, Unassigned, Top Voted and Stale Cards. Each board type starts with the ones that suit it.

- **Add one:** drag it from the palette's **Widgets** category into the header (a bar shows where it lands), or press the **+** at the end of the header to open that category. Tapped rather than dragged, a widget goes to the end of the selected board's header.
- **Arrange them:** drag left or right, or focus one and press **Alt+←** or **Alt+→**. Take one off with its **×** (shown while the board is selected) or **Delete**.
- **Narrow the board:** press a person in People, a type in Card Types, a priority, Unassigned or the overdue count; press again to see everything. Item Count reads "3 of 14 · Show all" while the board is narrowed. **Filter** and **Only Mine** are yours alone and aren't saved.
- **Stale Cards** counts cards not done that nobody has changed in 14 days. **Due Soon** counts overdue cards and those due in the next 7 days.

A board holds each widget once. Only people who can edit the document add, move or remove widgets; everyone can use them.

## Voting and Hidden Writing

A **Retro** board (and boards from templates that vote, such as Team Retro, Content Calendar and Feedback Board) has a vote control on each card. You see your own votes and the total; with a vote budget, **Votes Left** shows what you have left (a Retro gives everyone 5). On a board that hides writing, everyone else's cards show face down until someone who can edit presses **Reveal** in the header. Hiding is a facilitation aid, not privacy: it hides the card's face, not its data. Neither setting has a menu control: they come with the board.

## Archive and All Cards

**Archive** a card (right-click it) to take it off every board without deleting it; the **Archive** board shows them all, and **Restore** brings one back (or drag it onto another board). The **All Cards** board shows every other card with a row per status: the place to find a card no board shows, and give it a status a board has. To search every card instead, use the **Cards** button in the bottom-right corner (see [Cards](/help/canvas/plan-mode/cards/#find-a-card)).
