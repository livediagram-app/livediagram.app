**Plan mode** is a way of working on a tab, made for running work on boards: a Kanban board, a sprint, a bug triage, a retro or a roadmap. A **Plan board** shows your work as columns of cards. Each card is an **item**, a task, a bug, a note or an idea, stored with the document rather than drawn on the tab, so moving a card changes the item for everyone at once, and the same item can sit on two boards (a sprint board and a roadmap) and move on both. See [Items](/help/canvas/plan-mode/items/).

Plan mode gives a tab over to boards. Its palette holds **Cards**, **Boards**, **Widgets**, **Metrics** and **Visualisations**, then **Content** (sticky notes, text, images and pages) and **Tools** (Temperature, Estimate, Idea Box, Picker, Timer and Stopwatch), it starts on the **Hand** tool (cards take the pointer themselves; pick **Select** to move or resize a board), and Layers, the Eraser and Format stay with the other modes.

## Switching modes

- Press the mode switch and choose **Plan**, or press **Shift+D** until you reach it. The switch sits in the **Palette** panel's title row, or beside the menu button with the Toolbar layout.
- Your mode is yours: someone else on the tab can be in Diagram mode while you are in Plan mode. They see the cards move as you move them.
- To have a tab open in Plan mode for everyone, open the tab menu, choose **Opens in**, then **Plan**.
- Plan mode is new, so it has a switch in **Settings**, **Experimental**, **Plan Mode**. It is on unless you turn it off.
- **Switch modes whenever you like.** Your boards and cards stay where they are. In Diagram or Draw mode a board is an element like any other: you can move it, resize it and draw around it, and it still updates as cards change. Its cards only take clicks and drags in Plan mode, so switch back to work them.

## Starting a board

An empty Plan tab shows **Start with a Board** in the middle: pick a board type and it is placed, empty, ready to build.

- **A new document:** in the New Document wizard, pick **Blank Plan** on the **Popular** shelf, or choose **Plan** beside the search box to see the Plan templates. Each sets up a way of working across several tabs, with no cards, so you start straight on your own work (see [Plan templates](#plan-templates) below).
- **On any tab:** in Plan mode, open the palette's **Boards** category (under **Plan**) and press a board (Kanban, Sprint, Retro, Roadmap, Bug triage, Week or a blank board), then click the canvas, or drag it out to the size you want.

## Plan templates

Every Plan template but **Blank Plan** makes several tabs, each with its board set up for the job (columns, rows, card fields and widgets), charts of your cards and, where a session is run, a how-to note, a timer and other tools beside it.

| Template             | Tabs                                                                                                      |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Project Planner**  | **Roadmap** (projects on Now, Next, Later with a Gantt chart), **Backlog**, **Sprint**, **Daily Standup** |
| **Kanban Board**     | **Board** (with WIP limits), **Requests**, **Flow** (charts of where the work stands)                     |
| **Bug Tracker**      | **Triage** (a row per priority), **Fixing**, **Health**                                                   |
| **Team Retro**       | **Retro** (notes hidden until you reveal them, then a vote), **Actions**, **Archive**                     |
| **Weekly Planner**   | **This Week**, **Inbox**, **Calendar**                                                                    |
| **Content Calendar** | **Ideas** (voted on), **Production**, **Calendar**                                                        |
| **Hiring Pipeline**  | **Roles** (with a Gantt chart), **Pipeline** (a row per role), **Onboarding**                             |
| **OKRs**             | **Objectives** (with a Gantt chart), **Key Results** (on track, at risk or off track)                     |
| **Product Launch**   | **Timeline**, **Checklist** (a row per workstream), **Launch Day** (go or no-go)                          |
| **Feedback Board**   | **Feedback** (voted on), **Delivery**                                                                     |

- **Cards move between tabs.** Boards on different tabs share some columns: move a task to **This Sprint** on the Project Planner's **Backlog** and it is on the **Sprint** board too. A card's **Status** in its panel lists the columns of every tab, so you can send it anywhere.
- **Charts read every card**, whichever tab its board is on.
- Picking a template from **Quick Start** on an empty tab fills that tab and adds the rest straight after it.

## Working on a board

- **Add a card:** every column ends in **+ Add card**. It opens **Add a Card**: press a type (**Project**, **Task**, **Note**, **Idea**, **Action**, or any your document adds) to add a card of it, then give it a title in its panel.
- **From the palette:** drag a card from the **Cards** category into a column. While you drag, the column opens a gap where it will land. Cards from the palette always go onto a board, never loose on the canvas.
- **Right-click a card** to **Open**, **Duplicate** or **Delete** it, **Move to** another column, **Archive** it, or **Add to Slides** to present it full screen from the [Slide Deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/).
- **Trash a card:** drag it onto the **Trash** button beside Undo (it grows as you drag). Open the Trash to restore a card or delete it for good; **Empty Trash** deletes them all.
- **Archive a card** to take it off your boards without deleting it. An **Archive** board (in the palette's Boards) shows every archived card, and an **All Cards** board shows every other card, a row per status, so nothing gets lost between boards; drag one back onto another board, or choose **Restore**, to bring it back.
- **Move a card:** drag it to another column, or up and down its column. On a board with rows (by assignee, priority, type or parent), dropping it in a row also sets that field: drop it in Sam's row to give it to Sam.
- **Open an item:** click a card. Its panel opens over the canvas: the title and the card's tabs on the left, and **Details** (status, assignee, priority and the rest) on the right, with who made it and who changed it last. The **description** is rich text: bold, italic, underline, headings, lists and links, from the toolbar over it or the usual shortcuts. Every change saves as you make it. On a phone the panel rises from the bottom, with Details as its first tab. A card type's tabs are set in [Card Types](/help/canvas/plan-mode/card-types/).
- **Take a card off the board:** drag it out onto the canvas to leave a **Plan card** there, for example beside the part of a diagram it is about. The item stays on the board as well. Drag a Plan card onto a board to file it in the column you drop it in. Drag a card from one board onto another to move it there.
- **Undo:** **Ctrl+Z** (**Cmd+Z** on a Mac) undoes your item changes in the same order as your canvas edits: a moved card goes back, a deleted item comes back with its number.

In Diagram and Draw modes a board is an element like any other: pressing it selects and moves the whole board. Double-click a card to open its item.

### With the keyboard

Tab to a card, then:

- **Arrow keys** move between cards.
- **Enter** opens the item.
- **Shift+Left** and **Shift+Right** move the card to the previous or next column; **Shift+Up** and **Shift+Down** move it within its column.
- **N** opens **Add a Card** for the card's column.
- **Delete** deletes the item (you can undo it).

Screen readers hear every move ("#12 moved to In progress, position 2 of 4").

## Setting up a board

**A column** is set up from the column itself: hover its head and press the cog at the far right (on a touch screen it is always there). You can rename it, give it a colour, set a **WIP limit** (its count turns amber when it holds more than that), mark it **Counts as Done** (its cards show a progress bar in the header), move it left or right, add a column after it, or remove it. Removing a column with cards asks where they should go.

**The board** is set up from its own menu: right-click the board (or select it and press **⋯**):

- **Board:** its **Title**, and its **Swimlanes**: none, or one per assignee, card type, priority or project.
- **Cards:** the **Card Size** (**Minimal** shows the title alone, **Compact** the title over a line of number, priority, due date, votes and assignee, **Detailed** everything, down to a description excerpt and checklist progress), and which fields each card face shows, a tile each to press on or off. Tiles the chosen size can't show are dimmed.

Every board shows every card whose status is one of its columns. A **Team Retro** board comes with voting and hidden writing on: everyone else's cards show face down until someone presses **Reveal** in the header, so people write without reading each other.

Items whose status matches none of the board's columns are counted in the header ("3 not on this board"); open the count to move them to a column.

## Widgets in the header

A board's header holds **widgets**: small read-outs and controls such as **Completion**, **Filter**, **People**, **Card Types**, **WIP Alerts**, **Due Soon**, **Points**, **Priorities**, **Unassigned**, **Top Voted** and **Stale Cards**. Each board type starts with the ones that suit it (Points on a sprint, Priorities on a bug triage, Top Voted on a retro). Find them all in the palette's **Widgets** category:

- **Drag a widget** into a board's header. A bar shows where it will go.
- **Tap a widget** to add it to the end of the selected board's header.
- **Reorder** widgets by dragging them left or right along the header, or focus one and press **Alt+←** or **Alt+→**.
- **Remove** a widget with its **×**, or focus it and press **Delete**.

A board holds each widget once; adding one it already has makes it flash.

**Widgets narrow the board**: press a person in People, a type in Card Types, a priority, Unassigned, or the overdue count in Due Soon to see only those cards, and press it again to see everything. While the board is narrowed, Item Count reads "3 of 14 · Show all". Top Voted opens the leading card, and a Completion widget on a board without a done column offers **Set Done Column**.

## Metrics and Visualisations

**Metrics** are the read-out widgets, free on the canvas: place **Completion**, **Due Soon**, **People** or any other beside your boards, and each counts every card in the document, whatever board it is on. Archived cards and the Trash are left out.

**Visualisations** chart every card:

- **Project Gantt Chart**: a row per Project card, a bar from its **Start** to its **Due** date, filled to the share of its cards done, with a line for today. A project with only a due date is a diamond; press **Add a start date** to open it and set one.
- **Due Calendar**: this month, each day listing the cards due then. Step through months with the arrows.
- **Workload by Person**: a bar per person, split Not Started, In Progress and Done.
- **Status Breakdown**: a donut of the cards by status.
- **Priority by Status**: how many cards of each priority are not started, in progress and done.

A card counts as done when its status is a board's done column. In Plan mode, press a row or entry to open its card; in other modes, double-click it. Resize a visualisation like any element.

## Together

Everyone with the document open sees cards move, appear and change as they happen. A card someone is dragging or has open shows their colour and name. Voting and hiding writing are in the board itself, so a retro needs no other tools: write, reveal, vote, then turn the top votes into actions.
