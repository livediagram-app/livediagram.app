**Plan mode** is a way of working on a tab, made for running work on boards: a Kanban board, a sprint, a bug triage, a retro or a roadmap. A **Plan board** shows your work as columns of cards. Each card is an **item**, a task, a bug, a note or an idea, stored with the document rather than drawn on the tab, so moving a card changes the item for everyone at once, and the same item can sit on two boards (a sprint board and a roadmap) and move on both. See [Items](/help/canvas/plan-mode/items/).

Like the other modes, Plan mode never changes what the tab is. Boards and cards are on the tab in every mode; Plan mode is how you work them. Notes, arrows, frames and pictures around a board work as they always do.

## Switching modes

- Press the mode switch and choose **Plan**, or press **Shift+D** until you reach it. The switch sits in the **Palette** panel's title row, or beside the menu button with the Toolbar layout.
- Your mode is yours: someone else on the tab can be in Diagram mode while you are in Plan mode. They see the cards move as you move them.
- To have a tab open in Plan mode for everyone, open the tab menu, choose **Opens in**, then **Plan**.
- Plan mode is new, so it has a switch in **Settings**, **Experimental**, **Plan Mode**. It is on unless you turn it off.

## Starting a board

- **A new document:** in the New Document wizard, pick **Blank Plan** on the **Popular** shelf, or choose **Plan** beside the search box to see the Plan templates: **Kanban Board**, **Sprint Board**, **Bug Triage**, **Team Retro**, **Roadmap Board** and **Weekly Planner**. Each starts with real cards, so you can see how it works and change them, or delete them and start your own.
- **On any tab:** in Plan mode, open the palette's **Plan** category and press a board (Kanban, Sprint, Retro, Roadmap, Bug triage, Week or a blank board), then click the canvas, or drag it out to the size you want.

## Working on a board

- **Add an item:** every column ends in **Add item**. Type a title and press **Enter**; the field stays open for the next one. A few words in the title fill in the rest as you type:
  - `@sam` assigns it to Sam,
  - `#design` adds the label design,
  - `!high` sets the priority (`!urgent`, `!high`, `!medium` or `!low`),
  - `~3` sets the estimate,
  - `bug:` at the start makes it a bug (any item type works: `story:`, `idea:`, `risk:`...).
- **Move a card:** drag it to another column, or up and down its column. On a board with rows (by assignee, priority, type or parent), dropping it in a row also sets that field: drop it in Sam's row to give it to Sam.
- **Open an item:** click a card. The item panel opens beside the canvas with every field of its type: title, description, status, assignee, priority, labels, estimate, due date, checklist and parent. Changes save as you make them.
- **Take a card off the board:** drag it out onto the canvas to leave a **Plan card** there, for example beside the part of a diagram it is about. The item stays on the board as well. Drag a Plan card onto a board to file it in the column you drop it in.
- **Undo:** **Ctrl+Z** (**Cmd+Z** on a Mac) undoes your item changes in the same order as your canvas edits: a moved card goes back, a deleted item comes back with its number.

In Diagram and Draw modes a board is an element like any other: pressing it selects and moves the whole board. Double-click a card to open its item.

### With the keyboard

Tab to a card, then:

- **Arrow keys** move between cards.
- **Enter** opens the item.
- **Shift+Left** and **Shift+Right** move the card to the previous or next column; **Shift+Up** and **Shift+Down** move it within its column.
- **N** adds an item to the card's column.
- **Delete** deletes the item (you can undo it).

Screen readers hear every move ("#12 moved to In progress, position 2 of 4").

## Setting up a board

Press the cog in a board's header to open its set-up:

- **Columns:** add, rename, recolour and reorder columns. Give a column a **WIP limit** and its count turns amber when it holds more than that; mark one column as **Done** to show a progress bar in the header. Removing a column with cards asks where they should go.
- **Rows:** none, or one per assignee, item type, priority or parent (an epic).
- **Shows:** all items, only some item types, or only items with a label.
- **Card shows:** which fields each card face shows.
- **Voting:** on or off, with an optional number of votes each person may spend on the board.
- **Hide writing:** while it is on, everyone else's cards on this board show face down, so people write without reading each other. Press **Reveal** in the header to turn every card face up for everyone. It hides cards on the board, not the items themselves.

Items whose status matches none of the board's columns are counted in the header ("3 not on this board"); open the count to move them to a column.

## Together

Everyone with the document open sees cards move, appear and change as they happen. A card someone is dragging or has open shows their colour and name. Voting and hiding writing are in the board itself, so a retro needs no other tools: write, reveal, vote, then turn the top votes into actions.
