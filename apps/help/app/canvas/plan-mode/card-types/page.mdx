Every [item](/help/canvas/plan-mode/items/) has a **type**: Project, Task, Note, Idea or Action. A document starts with those five, and you can change them or add your own, such as a **Customer call** with an **Outcome** and a **Followed up** box. Card types belong to the document, so everyone working on it sees the same ones, at once.

## Open Card Types

In [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/), press the **Card Types** button in the bottom-right corner, where Layers sits in the other modes, or **Edit Cards** at the foot of the palette's **Cards** category (at the end of the strip with the Toolbar layout). The panel lists the types in two groups, **Built-In Types** and **Your Types**, each with its colour, its glyph and how many fields it has; a type no card has yet is drawn a little grey.

- **Add Type** makes a new one; press a type to edit it.
- **Duplicate** (the copy button on a type, or **Duplicate Type** in its editor) starts a new type from that one: same colour, glyph, fields and tabs, named "… copy" for you to rename. Nothing is added until you press **Save**.
- The types keep their order: the built-ins first, then yours in the order you added them. The palette's **Cards** list follows the same order.
- **Restore built-in types** puts back the five the document started with, after asking. It shows once you have changed the types. Items of a type you made keep it, and show as a plain **Item** card.

Someone who can only view the document sees the list but can't change it.

## Edit a Type

The type editor (**Edit Card Type**, or **New Card Type**) opens over the canvas (on a phone, it slides up from the bottom) in three tabs: **General**, **Fields** and **Statuses**. A small red dot on a tab shows where something needs fixing before you can save. The help link at its top right brings you here, and the cross closes it without saving.

- **General**: the **Name**, **Colour** (one of twelve) and **Glyph**, how its cards look. Two types can't share a name.
- **Fields**: what its card panel offers, laid out the way the panel shows them: **On the Card** (Title, and Votes), **Details** (the column beside the tabs) and each tab. **Title** and **Status** are always there, and a **Project** always keeps **Start** and **Due**. Move a field within its group with the arrows, to another group with **Move To**, or take it off with the cross.
- **Add Field** offers the **Built-In Fields** the type doesn't have yet (Assignee, Priority, Due date, Checklist, Comments...) and a **New Custom Field**: give it a name and a kind, which is **Text**, **Long text**, **Number**, **Date**, **Checkbox**, **Link**, **Choice** (one of the options you list, one a line) or **Card**, a link to one card of a type you pick under **Links To**, then press **Add Custom Field**.
- **Edit** on a custom field changes its name, its options and **Show on card**, which draws its name and value on every card of the type.
- **Link cards together:** add a **Card** field to say how one card relates to another. Make a **Person** type and an **Objective** type, then give Objective a Card field named **Owner** that links to Person. On an Objective, **Owner** is a field you click to pick a Person from a list (type to filter a long one); the arrow at its end opens that Person. On the Person, a **Linked as Owner** section lists every Objective it owns, with **New Objective** to make one already linked. A board can lay its rows by **Owner** too, one row per Person. The built-in **Parent** works the same way, for cards under a Project.
- **Tabs**: each tab is a group in **Fields**. Rename it in its heading, move it with the arrows, or take it off with the cross (its fields go to **Details**). **Add Tab** makes a new one (up to six); **Add Field** at the foot of a group adds the field straight into it. **Details** and **Overview** can be renamed but always stay, and a tab left empty is dropped when you save. A type starts with one **Overview** tab holding its description, checklist and comments.
- **Statuses** lists every status your boards use. Turn one off and cards of that type can never be moved into it: the column turns red with "Task cards can't be Done" when you drag one over it, and the card's **Status** list leaves it out. New statuses are on for every type until you turn them off. You can turn them all off: cards of that type then stay in whichever column they're made in.
- Press **Save** to apply the whole edit, or **Cancel** to leave it as it was. If something is missing (a name, a Choice's options), it says so beside **Save**. **Undo** takes a saved change back.

Taking a field off a type never deletes anything: items keep what they hold in it, and it shows again if you add the field back.

### Limits

A document holds up to 32 card types. Each type holds up to 24 fields, 12 of them custom, and up to 6 tabs. A Choice field has up to 20 options.

## Delete a Type

Open the type and press **Delete Type** (the last type can't be deleted). If items have the type, you choose where they go: another type, or **Keep as Item**, which keeps their fields and shows them as a plain Item card. If a board showed that type alone, it shows and takes every type again; pick the ones it should show in its menu's **Card Types**.

## Where Your Types Show

- The palette's **Cards** category has a card for each type ("Customer call Card"), ready to drag into a board's column.
- **+ Add card** on a board offers the types that board shows, set in its menu's **Cards** › **Card Types**.
- A card's panel lets you change its type from the picker at the top.
- A board's rows can be one per type (**Type** under **Swimlanes**), in this panel's order.

When someone saves a change, everyone with the document open sees their cards, panels and palette update at once. If two people save the same type at the same moment, the later save wins.
