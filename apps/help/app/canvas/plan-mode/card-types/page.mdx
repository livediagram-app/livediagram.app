Every [item](/help/canvas/plan-mode/items/) has a **type**: Task, Bug, Note and the rest. A document starts with the built-in eight, and you can change them or add your own, such as a **Customer call** with an **Outcome** and a **Followed up** box. Card types belong to the document, so everyone working on it sees the same ones, at once.

## Open Card Types

In [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/), press the **Card Types** button in the bottom-right corner, where Layers sits in the other modes. The panel lists each type with its colour, its glyph and how many items have it.

- **Add Type** makes a new one; **Edit** opens a type.
- Drag a row to reorder the types, or focus it and hold Alt while you press the up or down arrow. The palette's **Cards** list follows the same order.
- **Restore Built-In Types** puts back the eight the document started with.

## Edit a type

The type editor opens over the canvas (on a phone, it slides up from the bottom).

- **Name**, **Colour** and **Glyph**: how its cards look.
- **Fields**: what its item panel offers, in order. **Title** and **Status** are always there. Move a field with the arrows, or take it off with the cross.
- **Add Field** offers the built-in fields the type doesn't have yet (Assignee, Priority, Due date, Checklist...) and **New Custom Field**: give it a name and a kind, which is **Text**, **Long text**, **Number**, **Date**, **Checkbox**, **Link** or **Choice** (one of the options you list).
- **Edit** on a custom field changes its name, its options and **Show on card**, which draws its value on every card of the type.
- Press **Save** to apply the whole edit, or **Cancel** to leave it as it was. Undo takes a saved change back.

Taking a field off a type never deletes anything: items keep what they hold in it, and it shows again if you add the field back.

## Delete a type

Open the type and press **Delete Type**. If items have the type, you choose where they go: another type, or **Keep as Item**, which keeps their fields and shows them as a plain Item card. A board that showed only that type then shows every type.

## Where your types show

- The palette's **Cards** category has a card for each type, ready to place or drag onto a board.
- Quick add on a board understands a type's name before a colon: `customer call: Acme renewal`.
- A board's set-up lists them under **Shows**, and its rows can be one per type.
