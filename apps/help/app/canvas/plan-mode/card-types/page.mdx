Every [item](/help/canvas/plan-mode/items/) has a **type**: Project, Task, Note, Idea or Action. A document starts with those five, and you can change them or add your own, such as a **Customer call** with an **Outcome** and a **Followed up** box. Card types belong to the document, so everyone working on it sees the same ones, at once.

## Open Card Types

In [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/), press the **Card Types** button in the bottom-right corner, where Layers sits in the other modes, or **Edit Cards** at the foot of the palette's **Cards** category (at the end of the strip with the Toolbar layout). The panel lists the types in two groups, **Built-In Types** and **Your Types**, each with its colour, its glyph and how many fields it has; a type no card has yet is drawn a little grey.

  <PlanCardTypesScene />
</Figure>

- **Add Type** makes a new one; press a type to edit it.
- **Duplicate** (the copy button on a type, or **Duplicate Type** in its editor) starts a new type from that one: same colour, glyph, fields and tabs, named "… copy" for you to rename. Nothing is added until you press **Save**.
- The types keep their order: the built-ins first, then yours in the order you added them. The palette's **Cards** list follows the same order.
- **Restore built-in types** puts back the five the document started with, as they started, after asking. It shows once you have changed the types. Types you made yourself stay as they are.

Someone who can only view the document sees the list but can't change it.

## Edit a Type

The type editor (titled with the type's name, **Edit Task Card Type** say, or **New Card Type**) opens over the canvas (on a phone, it slides up from the bottom) in four tabs: **General**, **Fields**, **States** and **Display**. A small red dot on a tab shows where something needs fixing before you can save. The help link at its top right brings you here, and the cross closes it without saving.

- **General**: the **Name**, **Colour** (one of twelve) and **Glyph**, how its cards look. Two types can't share a name.
- **Fields**: what its card panel offers, laid out the way the panel shows them: **On the Card** (Title, and Votes), **Details** (the column beside the tabs) and each tab. **Title** and **Status** are always there, and a **Project** always keeps **Start** and **Due**. Drag a field by the handle at its left to reorder it within its group (it works with a finger too), or open its **⋯** menu to move it up or down, to another group, or take it off.
- **Add Field** offers the **Built-In Fields** the type doesn't have yet (Assignee, Priority, Due Date, Checklist, Comments...) and a **New Custom Field**: give it a name and a kind, which is **Text**, **Long Text**, **Number**, **Date**, **Checkbox**, **Link**, **Choice** (one of the options you list, one a line) or **Link to Card**, a link to one card of a type you pick under **Links To**; a preview shows how it will look on a card. Then press **Add Custom Field**.
- **Edit** on a custom field changes its name, its options and **Show on card**, which draws its name and value on every card of the type.
- **Link cards together:** add a **Link to Card** field to say how one card relates to another. Make a **Person** type and an **Objective** type, then give Objective a Link to Card field named **Owner** that links to Person. On an Objective, **Owner** is a field you click to pick a Person from a list (type to filter a long one); the arrow at its end opens that Person. On the Person, a **Linked as Owner** section lists every Objective it owns, with **New Objective** to make one already linked. When that list holds more than one card type or state, menus above it narrow it to one type or state. A board can lay its rows by **Owner** too, one row per Person. The built-in **Parent** works the same way, for cards under a Project.
- **Tabs**: each tab is a group in **Fields**. Rename it in its heading, move it with the arrows, or take it off with the cross (its fields go to **Details**). **Add Tab** makes a new one (up to six); **Add Field** at the foot of a group adds the field straight into it. **Details** and **Overview** can be renamed but always stay, and a tab left empty is dropped when you save. A type starts with one **Overview** tab holding its description, checklist and comments.
- **States** lists every status your boards use, each with a tick box; **Select All** and **Deselect All** do them all at once. Untick one and cards of that type can never be moved into it: the column turns red with "Task cards can't be Done" when you drag one over it, and the card's **Status** list leaves it out. New statuses are on for every type until you turn them off. You can turn them all off: cards of that type then stay in whichever column they're made in. Pick a **Default State** under them to say where a card of that type starts when it's made outside a board (from a linked card's **New** button, or by an AI tool); a card added to a board always takes that column's state.
- **Display** arranges this type's cards at each card size, right on the card: pick **Minimal**, **Compact** or **Detailed**, then drag any field to any part of the card (the header, under the title, the footer...), or take it off with its cross. **Available Fields** lists the fields not on the card yet: drag one onto the card, or press it to choose where it goes. Each built-in type starts with its own sensible layout (Notes and Ideas lead with their votes, Actions with their due date and checklist); its Reset to Default button puts a size back.
- Press **Save** to apply the whole edit, or **Cancel** to leave it as it was. If something is missing (a name, a Choice's options), it says so beside **Save**. **Undo** takes a saved change back.

Taking a field off a type never deletes anything: items keep what they hold in it, and it shows again if you add the field back.

### Limits

A document holds up to 32 card types. Each type holds up to 24 fields, 12 of them custom, and up to 6 tabs. A Choice field has up to 20 options.

## Delete a Type

Open the type and press **Delete Type** (the last type can't be deleted), then confirm. Any cards of that type move to the **Trash** with it, so you can restore them there; a restored card shows as a plain Item card, keeping its fields. If a board showed that type alone, it shows and takes every type again; pick the ones it should show in its menu's **Card Types**.

## Where Your Types Show

- The palette's **Cards** category has a card for each type ("Customer call Card"), ready to drag into a board's column.
- **+ Add card** on a board offers the types that board shows, set in its menu's **Cards** › **Card Types**.
- A card's panel lets you change its type from the picker at the top.
- A board's rows can be one per type (**Type** under **Swimlanes**), in this panel's order.

When someone saves a change, everyone with the document open sees their cards, panels and palette update at once. If two people save the same type at the same moment, the later save wins.
