The Plan tour shows you how to run work on a board. Like the [Welcome Tour](/help/getting-started/welcome-tour/), it works on the real editor rather than screenshots: it puts an example board on your canvas, adds a few cards, moves one along, opens it, and spotlights each part with a highlight ring and a short explanation. It takes about a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## When it's offered

The first time you work in [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/) (switching a tab to Plan, or opening a Plan board) a centred card asks whether you'd like a look around: **Show me around** starts the tour, **No thanks** dismisses it. If the Welcome Tour is still running, the Plan tour waits until it has finished.

The offer appears **once ever**. However you answer (decline, skip midway, or finish), it won't come back on its own, and if you're signed in the answer follows you to every device.

## What it covers

1. **Your board**: the example board, a Kanban board, and where the other boards come from. See [Boards](/help/canvas/plan-mode/boards/).
2. **Add cards**: the **Add card** button at the foot of each column, and dropping a card from the palette.
3. **Move work along**: dragging a card to the next column, or **Shift** and an arrow key.
4. **The card panel**: everything about one piece of work, saved as you type. See [Cards](/help/canvas/plan-mode/cards/).
5. **The board header**: the widgets that count and filter the board, and a column's cog.
6. **Card types**: the colours and fields of each kind of card. See [Card types](/help/canvas/plan-mode/card-types/).
7. **The Plan palette**: cards, boards, widgets, metrics and visualisations.

## Your work is left alone

- The tour's example board and cards are its own. It never adds cards to your boards or changes them.
- Everything it made is **taken away** when the tour ends, however it ends. If you close the window mid-tour, it's tidied up the next time you open the document.
- None of it goes into your undo history, so **Undo** afterwards never brings it back.
- Anyone else in the document at the time sees the example board come and go.

<Step n={2}>Switch **Show Plan Tour** back on.</Step>

  Close Settings. If you're in Plan mode the tour starts straight away; otherwise it's offered the
