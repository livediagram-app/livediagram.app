A **Sheet** is one spreadsheet tab, the kind you know from Google Sheets or Excel, placed on a [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/) tab beside your boards. Its formulas can read the other sheets on the tab and count or add up your [cards](/help/canvas/plan-mode/cards/).

## Place a Sheet

On an empty Plan tab, choose **Spreadsheets** in **Start Planning**'s rail, then a start: **Empty Sheet** gives you a blank grid at once, and the others set it up from a layout or your cards. A new sheet has 100 rows by 26 columns, and is called **Sheet 1** (or the next free number on the tab).

You can also drop a `.csv` or `.tsv` file on the canvas in Plan mode: it becomes a Sheet named after the file, filled with its rows.

A Sheet moves by its header and resizes by its handles, like a board. Its colours follow the tab's theme. Outside Plan mode it is drawn like any other element, live as its cells change; double-click it to **Switch to Plan** and edit it.

## Setup Sheet

A new Sheet opens on **Setup Sheet**, a few quick steps:

1. **Start From**: a **Blank** grid, your **Plan Cards** as rows, **Import CSV**, or one of twelve templates (below) (its **Choose File…** goes straight to the file picker, and the Sheet fills from the file). For Plan Cards, the next steps narrow the cards with the same search and filters as the Cards panel, then choose the columns from the fields those cards have (Number, Title, Type and State are always there). The rows stay linked to the cards, both ways:

- Change a card anywhere and its row updates.
- Edit a row and it waits as a draft, tinted, with **Save Card** (✓, or **Add Card** on a new row) and **Cancel Changes** (✕) in its **Controls** cell at the end of the table. **Save Card** writes the row to its card; **Cancel Changes** puts it back.
- Clearing a **Title**, **State** or **Type** cell doesn't clear it on the card: a card always has them, so it keeps its value and the row shows it again.
- Every card row has **Open Card** at the right of its title.
- **Type**, **State**, **Priority**, **Assignee** and choice fields have a dropdown arrow in the selected cell to pick from; **Due** and **Start** pick a date.
- Type in the row under the table (or a row you insert inside it) and press **Add Card** to make it a new card.
- Delete a row and its card moves to the Trash, where you can restore it.

2. **Style**: **Header**, **Banded**, **Boxed**, **Minimal** or **Plain**, whether to **Freeze Header Row** (not for a Blank start), and a **Cell Size**. A template starts on **Banded**.

Press **Create Sheet**, or **Start Blank** to skip it. Everything can change later from the cog.

## Cells and Formulas

- Click a cell and type. **Enter** saves and moves down, **Tab** moves right, **Escape** throws the edit away. After a run of Tabs, Enter goes back to the column you started in, one row down.
- What you type is read the way a spreadsheet reads it: `12` is a number, `12%` a percent, `£12` currency, `8/10/2026` a date, `true` a true or false, and a leading `'` keeps it as text.
- Start with `=` for a formula: `=SUM(A1:A10)`, `=IF(B2>100,"Over","OK")`. While you write one, click a cell (or drag across a range) to put its reference in; each reference is coloured, with its cells outlined to match. **F4** cycles `A1`, `$A$1`, `A$1` and `$A1`.
- Typing a function name lists the matching functions; inside its brackets a hint shows what it takes. The toolbar's **Functions** category also lists every function, a menu per family (Maths, Statistics, Lookup and the rest), each with what it takes and what it does: pick one to start it in the selected cell, or, with several cells selected, to total them in the cell below. See [Sheet Functions](/help/canvas/plan-mode/sheet-functions/) for every function.
- Read another sheet on the tab by clicking its cells while you write, or by typing its name: `='Budget'!B4`.
- A formula that can't be read isn't saved: the part that can't be read is underlined, with a line saying why.

## Move Around a Sheet

Scroll as usual, or drag the cells around: with the middle mouse button, the right button, or the left button while you hold Space. A right-click that doesn't move still opens the menu. On a touch screen, drag with one finger; a tap still selects a cell, and two fingers zoom the canvas.

## Select, Copy and Fill

- Drag (or Shift and click) to select a range; Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) and click adds another range. Click a column letter or a row number to select it, or the corner for everything.
- For a selection of numbers, the bar at the bottom shows its **Sum**, **Average** and **Count**. Press it to choose what it shows, **Min** and **Max** included.
- **Copy** and **Paste** work with Google Sheets, Excel, documents and emails: rows and columns, values and simple formats come across. A formula pasted elsewhere in the sheet keeps pointing at the same relative cells. Paste values only with Ctrl+Shift+V (⌘⇧V), or from **Paste Special** in a cell's right-click menu. **Paste** from the right-click menu asks your browser to let the page read the clipboard the first time; Ctrl+V (⌘V) never needs to ask.
- Drag the small square at the selection's corner (the **fill handle**) to fill: one value is copied, `1, 2` continues as `3, 4`, `Item 1` as `Item 2`, `Jan` as `Feb`. Double-click it to fill down as far as the column beside it goes.

## Named Ranges

Give a cell or a range a name, so a formula reads it by name and keeps reading the same cells however far you fill it: select the cells, type a name such as `TaxRate` in the name box (left of **fx**), and press Enter. Then `=B2*TaxRate` works in any row. Typing a name there later selects its cells. Names start with a letter or `_`, can't look like a cell reference (`AB12`), and are listed in **Sheet Settings** › **Named Ranges**, where you can go to one or remove it. A formula can also use a name from another sheet on the same tab, as long as only one sheet has it.

## Comment on a Cell

A cell can carry its own comment thread, about that cell rather than the whole Sheet. Right-click the cell and choose **Insert Comment** (or press Ctrl+Alt+M, ⌘⌥M on a Mac), type your note and press **Comment**. The cell shows a small amber triangle in its top-right corner while the thread is open; rest the pointer on the cell to read the first comment, and press the triangle (or **Show Comments** in the cell menu) to open the whole thread, reply, or **Resolve** it. A comment stays with its cell when rows and columns are inserted, moved or sorted.

Each cell's thread is listed in the [Collaborate panel](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/#the-collaborate-panel) by the Sheet and the cell, such as `Budget!B4`. Editors and Participants can comment on cells; Viewers can read the threads. A copy of the Sheet starts without them. See [Comments](/help/collaboration/comments/).

## Rows and Columns

Right-click a column letter or row number to insert, delete, clear or hide, fit it to its contents, set its exact size with **Resize…**, or sort by a column. Drag a header's edge to resize it, or drag selected headers to move them. Right-click a cell (or a selection) for the cell menu. Its top names the selection and how many cells it covers, with **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**, **Paste Values**, **Clear Contents** and **Clear Formatting** as buttons along it. Below, each row says exactly what it will do: **Insert 3 Rows Above** (or Below), **Insert 2 Columns Left** (or Right), **Delete Rows 2 to 4**, **Delete Column B**, and **Merge Cells** or **Unmerge Cells**. The rarer actions open to the side: **Sort** (A to Z, Z to A, Custom Sort…), **Shift Cells** (insert or delete cells, moving their neighbours), **Hide**, **Paste Special** and **Insert Chart**. Formulas follow their cells: inserting, deleting, moving or sorting rows never breaks them.

Below the last row, **Add** puts more rows at the bottom (100 at a time, or the number you type).

## Formatting

The toolbar (shown in Plan mode to Editors) starts with a category switcher: **Text**, **Cells**, **Numbers**, **Data** and **Functions**, each showing just its own buttons. Together they set bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, text and fill colour, **Font** (each font listed in its own lettering; **Default** follows the tab), **Font Size** (step it with − and +, or type a size), borders, alignment (a button each for left, centre and right, and for top, middle and bottom), merging and wrapping, number formats (Automatic, Number, Percent, Currency, Accounting, Scientific, Date, Time, Date Time, Duration, Plain Text) and decimals. When the Sheet is narrow, the toolbar folds the buttons that don't fit into **More**. To find a function, press **Search** at the toolbar's far right and type its name or what it does: pick one and the toolbar opens its family's menu with the function highlighted.

## Charts

Select some cells, then pick **Data** in the Sheet's toolbar and choose **Bar Chart**, **Line Chart** or **Pie Chart** (or right-click the cells and open **Insert Chart**). Select a single cell to chart the whole table it sits in. To chart columns that are not side by side, hold Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) and drag each one: pick the labels in A and the amounts in C, and the chart leaves out B.

The first row names the series and the first column labels the bars, slices or points. The chart appears straight away inside the Sheet, beside the cells you charted, and sits over the cells as a spreadsheet's chart does: it scrolls and zooms with them, and shows wherever the Sheet does (on the canvas, maximised, on a Sheet tab or in a Dashboard). It stays live, so changing the cells changes the chart.

Click a chart to select it, drag it to move it, or drag its corner handle to resize it. Press Delete (or use its **Delete Chart** button) to remove it, and Escape to let it go. With a chart selected, the arrow keys move it (hold Shift for bigger steps) and Alt with the arrow keys resizes it. Each of these is one step for Undo. Copying, duplicating or deleting a Sheet takes its charts with it. Only an Editor can move, resize or delete charts; Participants and Viewers see them. Right-click a chart, or select it and press its **Chart Options** button (⋯), for its menu: change the chart type, its style, legend and palette there. To edit a chart's data by hand instead, open its **Data** menu and choose **Unlink From Sheet**.

## Sheet Settings

The cog in the Sheet's header opens **Sheet Settings** (the same sections are under **Sheet** when you right-click the Sheet):

- **Sheet Setup**: rename the sheet, import or download a CSV, set up an empty sheet again with **Setup Sheet**, or start it over with **Clear Sheet** (every cell, chart, format and setting goes, and Setup Sheet shows again).
- **Named Ranges**: the names given to ranges of cells, to use in formulas.
- **Sheet Options**: turn the gridlines, and the row numbers and column letters, on or off for everyone, and, on a Plan tab, choose whether the Sheet sits **On Canvas** or fills the tab ([Fill Tab](#fill-tab)).
- **Cells**: a **Cell Size** (**Compact**, **Default** or **Roomy**, as Setup Sheet offers; none is chosen once you set your own), and the exact width of new columns and height of new rows; ones you sized yourself keep their size.
- **Freeze**: how many rows and columns stay put.
- **Calculations**: which totals (Sum, Average, Count, Min, Max) show under a selection of numbers. The totals are your own choice.

## Freeze, Sort, Filter and Find

- **Freeze** keeps the first rows or columns in place while the rest scrolls. You can also drag the thick line on the row or column headers to freeze more or less.
- **Sort Sheet A to Z** or **Sort Sheet Z to A** in the toolbar's Sort menu (**Sort A to Z** and **Sort Z to A** on a column's header menu) sorts the sheet's rows by a column, keeping frozen header rows in place. **Custom Sort…** sorts just the selection, by several columns, with **Data has a header row**.
- **Filter** puts a button on each header cell: filter by values (a searchable list with counts) or by a condition (contains, greater than, between, date is before, and more). Filtered rows are hidden for everyone.
- **Find** (Ctrl+F, ⌘F) searches values, and formulas too if you ask, and highlights every match. **Find and Replace** (Ctrl+H, or ⌘⇧H on a Mac) replaces in what was typed; **Replace All** is one step to undo.

## Focus

Press **Focus Sheet** (left of Maximise in the Sheet's header, and gone while the Sheet is maximised) to glide the view so the Sheet fits the screen. Press it again to zoom back out to the whole tab.

## Maximise

Press **Maximise Sheet** in the header, before the cog, to fill the canvas with the sheet, toolbar and formula bar included. **Restore Sheet** or **Escape** puts it back. Escape first closes Find or ends an edit, so a second Escape restores. On a wide enough screen the header grows to hold the menu, so the sheet starts at the top of the canvas, and its name moves into the menu box at the top left. While it is maximised, or fills its tab, the zoom buttons in the bottom-right corner zoom the sheet's cells instead of the canvas, from 50% to 200%; **Fit** puts it back to 100%. Maximising is just for you: nobody else's view changes.

## Fill Tab

To make a Sheet the whole tab, open **Sheet Settings**, then **Sheet Options**, and choose **Fill Tab**. The Sheet then fills the tab for everyone, in every mode. Anything else on the tab is deleted (you're asked first), as one step you can undo. Choose **On Canvas** to put it back on the canvas.

## Delete a Sheet

Select a Sheet and press Delete (or use the trash button or the right-click menu). If nothing else in the document uses its sheet, you're asked first: **Delete** removes the Sheet and its cells together. Undo brings both back while the page stays open.

Nothing is asked, and the cells are kept, when:

- another Sheet shows the same sheet, or a copy of it hasn't been opened yet (a duplicated Sheet or tab);
- you **Cut** the Sheet, so you can paste it back;
- someone else, or an agent, removes it.

Cells nothing uses any more are deleted for good after 30 days.

## CSV

From the Sheet's **Sheet Settings** (the cog in its header), **Download CSV** saves `<title>.csv` with each value as shown. On a Sheet tab they are in the tab's menu instead (the **⋯** on its tab): **Import CSV…**, then **Export** for a picture of the sheet (PNG, SVG or PDF, its charts where they sit; see [Exporting tabs](/help/tabs/export-tabs/#board-sheet-and-dashboard-tabs)), then **Download CSV**. **Import CSV…** reads a `.csv` or `.tsv` file in: on a sheet that already has cells, choose **Replace Sheet** or **Insert at Selection**.

## Together

Everyone on the document sees each change as it is made, and each person's selection is outlined in their colour with their name. Two people typing in different cells never overwrite each other. Sheet changes join the canvas's **Undo** and **Redo**.

- **Editors** do everything on this page.
- **Participants** can type into cells, but get no toolbar, no row, column or merge controls and no Sheet Settings; a row or column header menu offers them only **Copy**.
- **Viewers** read the Sheet and can't change it.

## Limits

- A Sheet holds up to 10,000 rows, 200 columns and 50,000 cells with something typed or formatted in them, and freezes up to 100 rows and 26 columns.
- A Sheet's title is up to 60 characters.
