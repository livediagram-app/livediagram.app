The Drawing tour shows you where everything for sketching lives. Like the [Diagram Tour](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/), it works on the real editor rather than screenshots: it spotlights each part of the dock with a highlight ring and a short explanation. It adds nothing to your canvas, takes under a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## When It's Offered

The first time you work in [Drawing mode](/help/canvas/drawing-mode/) on a tab you can edit (switching a tab to Drawing, or opening a document whose tab opens in Drawing), a centred **Getting started with Drawing?** card asks whether you'd like a look: **Yes** starts it, **No** dismisses it.

It's offered on its own, whether or not you've taken the [Diagram Tour](/help/getting-started/diagram-tour/), which belongs to Diagram mode and never shows in Drawing. The tour isn't offered on a locked tab, in an embed, or to someone who can only view or take part.

The offer appears **once ever**. However you answer (decline, skip midway, or finish), it won't come back on its own, and if you're signed in the answer follows you to every device. Turning on **Power User Mode** in Settings marks it as seen too.

## What It Covers

1. **Pens and Tools**: the dock's first group, with **Select**, the three markers, **Text**, the **Path tool** and the **Eraser**. Press the marker in your hand again to change its colour and width.
2. **Quick Style**: the panel with colour and width for the marker in your hand, or for whatever you've selected, so you can restyle a stroke, shape or text after drawing it. If nothing is in hand, the tour picks up a marker to show it and puts it down again after. Not on a phone.
3. **Shapes**: the sticky note and shapes pinned to the dock, and **Shapes**, which opens every shape and a search. You can pin your favourites to the dock.
4. **Board Settings**: the cog, with the board's background, your cursor, and whether rough shapes you draw snap into clean ones.

Each step has **Next**, **Back** and **Skip tour**. The last card, **You're ready to draw**, links to the [Drawing mode](/help/canvas/drawing-mode/) article and ends the tour with **Start drawing**. If you leave Drawing mode or switch tab mid-tour, it ends as skipped.
