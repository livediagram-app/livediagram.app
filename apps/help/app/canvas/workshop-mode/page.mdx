**Workshop mode** is a way of working on a tab, made for running a session with your team: a retrospective, a brainstorm or a planning workshop. It puts the tools that run a room in front of you (timers, dot votes, temperature checks, pickers, stickers) and keeps them out of the way when you are drawing a diagram.

It used to be called **Facilitate mode**.

## What Workshop Mode Changes

- **The palette** offers Shapes, **Write** (text, notes, tables and checklists), **Draw** (arrows and lines) and **Layout** (frames, lanes, timelines, cards and badges), then the session kit: **Ask** (dot votes and the temperature check) and **Session**, which holds **Tools**, **Record** and **Navigate** down its side, from the timer and picker to comments, actions, decisions and portals. Session's search looks through all of them. After them come Icons, **Stickers**, Media and **Data**. Write and Draw keep what a session uses, leaving out Page, Annotation, Polygon, Mind node, Flowchart and Sankey; search still adds any of them. See [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/).
- **The bottom-right corner** gains the **Timer** and **Vote** buttons, right after Undo and Redo. See [Session Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).
- **Everything else works as in Diagram mode:** selection, arrows, text, sticky notes, snapping, undo and shortcuts.

## What Moved Out of Diagram

Diagram mode is for diagrams, so the session kit now lives here:

- **The Collaborate elements** and **Stickers** are no longer in Diagram's palette. Diagram keeps the **Comment panel** and the **Action card** in its Write category, since comments and actions belong to every mode.
- **The Timer and Vote buttons** are no longer in Diagram's bottom-right corner.
- **Nothing on your tabs changes.** Session elements already on a tab keep working in every mode: a session button still starts its timer or vote.
- **The element search still finds them.** Search for "timer" or a sticker from Diagram mode and it is still there, under **Elements From Other Modes**, and it is placed without switching mode. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/).

## Switching Modes

- **One mode for everyone:** the mode belongs to the tab, so when someone who can edit switches it, everyone on the tab moves to Workshop together, and **Undo** switches it back.
- **Participants and viewers** have no mode switch: they see the tab in its mode. Participants take part in everything that is running (they vote, respond and add sticky notes); viewers watch.

## Starting a Session

- **A new document:** in the New Document wizard, choose **Workshop**. The empty tab opens on **Start a Workshop**: the session templates grouped as **Retrospectives**, **Meetings**, **Ideation**, **Planning**, **Strategy** and **Decisions**, with **Recommended** first. **Blank Session** (or the **×**) leaves one empty tab in Workshop mode.
- **Session templates open in Workshop:** the retrospectives (Retrospective, Start, Stop, Continue, Mad, Sad, Glad, 4Ls and Sailboat), Crazy Eights, the Workshop Board and the Brainstorm Board, the boards that come with a dot vote (Affinity map, Prioritization matrix and Risk matrix), so the vote is there to run from the Session strip, and the sticky-note boards a group fills in together, such as SWOT, Empathy Map and User Journey. Team Retro stays in [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/), since it is a board of cards.
- **Any tab:** switch it to Workshop when the session starts, and back to Diagram when you are done. Everything on it stays where it is.

<Tip>
  Share a Participant pass with everyone in the session, so they can vote and add sticky notes
  without reshaping the board.
</Tip>
