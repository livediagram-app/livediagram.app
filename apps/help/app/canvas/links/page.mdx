Links connect your diagram to the rest of your work. Any element can become a clickable jump point, and a dedicated link card turns a URL into a tidy bookmark with a title, favicon, and preview image. Together they let a diagram point at the things it describes instead of just naming them.

## Linking an Element

Give any element a link, and it gains a small "Follow link" badge whose hover card names the target. A link can point at:

- **A tab** in the same document, or **a specific element** on another tab.
- **Another document** entirely.
- **An external URL**, which opens in a new browser tab.

To add one, right-click the element, open the **Resources** category and choose **Add Link** (it reads **Edit Link** once there is one). The **Link element** dialog opens on three choices:

- **External URL**: type a **Web address** and press **Save link** (or Enter). Leave off the `https://` and it is added for you.
- **Tab**: pick one of the document's tabs.
- **Document**: pick one of your other documents.

To change a link, open the same dialog again and pick a new target; the Remove link button at its foot takes the link off. The link follows the element if you move it, and like any edit it can be undone. For more on tab and element targets, see [Linking Across Tabs](/help/tabs/linking-tabs/).

<Note>
  For safety, only `http`, `https`, and `mailto` addresses are allowed as external links. Other URL
  schemes are blocked.
</Note>

## Link Cards

A **link card** is a rectangular element that turns a URL into a rich preview: the page's favicon, its title, the site name, and, when available, its preview image. It is the right choice when the link is the content, such as a reference doc, a ticket, or a related page you want visible on the canvas.

<Step n={1}>
  Open the palette's **Collaborate** category and pick **Link card**, under **Navigate**. Click the
  canvas to drop it at its default size, or drag to draw it the size you want.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  The empty card prompts you to add a link. Double-click it to open the link dialog and paste in a
  URL.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  The card fills in with the page's title, favicon, and preview image automatically. Clicking the
  row along the bottom of the card, with its title, follows the link.
</Step>

A link card recolours, moves, locks, layers, and resizes like any other element. To learn how the preview is fetched and what gets shown, see [Link Cards](/help/canvas/links/link-cards/).

## Choosing Between Them

Both rely on the same kind of URL, so the choice is about emphasis:

- Use an **element link** when an existing shape or node should also be clickable.
- Use a **link card** when the bookmark itself is what you want people to see on the canvas.

<Tip>
  Drop a link card next to the part of the diagram it supports, so a reader can jump straight from
  the picture to the source without hunting for it.
</Tip>
