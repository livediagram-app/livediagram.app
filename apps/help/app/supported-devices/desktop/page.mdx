A computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux) with a mouse or trackpad and a keyboard gives you the complete livediagram experience. Everything in the help centre works here, so this is the best place to do detailed work.

## Everything is available

All of the tools in the palette work on desktop, including the presenting tools that need room and a pointer: [Spotlight](/help/selection-modes/spotlight/), which dims the canvas and follows your cursor to highlight one thing at a time, and the [Slide Deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/). Neither appears on a phone-sized screen.

You also get the floating panels (the palette, the Explorer, the [Map](/help/user-interface/minimap/), and the Layers and Collaborate panels), which you can drag to any corner and collapse when you want more room (see [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/)), and **UI Scale** in Settings to make the panels and buttons bigger or smaller.

## Mouse and keyboard

A mouse and keyboard unlock the fastest ways to work:

- **Keyboard shortcuts** for picking tools and running common actions, like undo, copy, duplicate, and zoom. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) for the full list.
- **Hold Shift** while resizing to keep an element's proportions, and **Shift-click** to add or remove elements from a selection.
- **Drag a box** across empty canvas to select several elements at once.
- **Hold Space and drag**, drag with the middle mouse button, or scroll (two fingers on a trackpad) to move around; hold Ctrl (or Cmd) and scroll, or pinch on a trackpad, to zoom.

## Right-click and hover

Right-click any element, tab, or empty canvas to open its menu of formatting and actions (see [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/)). In those menus, resting the pointer on a colour, border or preset previews it on the selection before you commit, which touch screens can't do. Rest the pointer on a button for a second and a tooltip names it; some controls explain themselves at once with a hover card. Keyboard focus shows either straight away, and Escape hides it.
