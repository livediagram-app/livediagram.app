livediagram works on a phone right in your browser, with no app to install. The editor automatically switches to a touch-friendly layout on a phone-sized screen (narrower than 640 pixels, or a touch screen shorter than 500 pixels, so a phone held sideways counts too) so the canvas stays the star and everything is reachable with a thumb. It is great for viewing a diagram, making quick edits, and joining a live session on the go.

## A layout built for touch

On a phone the editor keeps the same [layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) as on a computer, fitted to the width. Along the top, a small card holds the menu button (for the Explorer; a phone has no mode switch, see [Editor Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/#on-a-phone)), and beside it the palette's strip: the selection mode, the category, its tiles, **More** for everything that doesn't fit, and **Search** at the end. **Layers** and **Collaborate** open as pop-overs above their buttons in the bottom-right corner, and the tabs and quick controls run along the bottom.

A few panels are simplified to fit: they stay where they are rather than being dragged around (tap a panel's header to collapse or expand it), the [Map](/help/user-interface/minimap/) is hidden, and the zoom controls give way to a single **Fit** button, since you pinch to zoom.

The editor opens zoomed out a little (to 60%) so you land on a useful overview of the canvas, and when you add a new element it slides into view automatically.

## Touch gestures

Your fingers do the work a mouse would on a computer:

- **Pinch** with two fingers to zoom in and out.
- **Drag** empty canvas to move around: a phone starts on the **Hand** tool.
- **Tap** to select, and **drag** an element to move it.
- **Press and hold** an element for half a second (a long press) to open its menu, the touch version of a right-click. On a phone menus open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen; drag it down to close it.
- **Press and hold** a button that shows only an icon to see its name. Letting go does not press it.

To select several elements, switch the selection mode to **Select**: then dragging across empty canvas draws a selection box.

Drag handles (an arrow's ends, for instance) have a little extra invisible padding on touch, so they are easier to hit accurately with a fingertip.

## What works differently on a phone

A phone has a small screen, no mouse pointer and usually no keyboard, so a few things are not available or work differently:

- **Spotlight** and the **Slide Deck** (the presenting tools) are reserved for larger screens.
- **The Map** and **UI Scale** are desktop only.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** need a keyboard: with none, use the palette and the on-screen controls, which do everything the shortcuts do. Pair a keyboard and they work as on a computer, as do held-key moves like Shift to keep proportions.
- **Hover previews** (styles previewing as you rest on them) need a hovering pointer, so on touch a tap simply applies the change.

<Tip>
  For a long or detailed editing session, a [tablet](/help/supported-devices/tablet/) with more room
  (or a [computer](/help/supported-devices/desktop/)) is more comfortable. A phone is perfect for
  quick changes and following along live.
</Tip>
