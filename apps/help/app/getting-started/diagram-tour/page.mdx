The Diagram tour is a guided, interactive introduction to [Diagram mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/): building a diagram from shapes, arrows and the palette. Rather than showing screenshots, it opens the real panels and menus one at a time, spotlighting each with a highlight ring and a short explanation. It takes about a minute, and you can leave at any point. (It was called the Welcome Tour until October 2026.)

## When It's Offered

When you create your first document from the [New Document wizard](/help/getting-started/your-first-diagram/), it opens with a centred **Welcome to Diagram** card: **Show me around** starts the tour, **No thanks** dismisses it. (A document opened through **Start Blank**, a share link or an embed does not offer it, and nor does a view-only document.)

The tour only appears on a **Diagram tab**. If your first document opens in Draw, Facilitate, Illustrate or Plan, the card waits until you work on a tab in Diagram mode. Those modes have tours of their own: see [The Plan Tour](/help/canvas/plan-mode/plan-tour/) and [The Facilitate Tour](/help/canvas/facilitate-mode/facilitate-tour/). Switching the tab to another mode partway through ends the tour.

The offer appears **once ever**. However you answer (decline, skip midway, or finish), it won't come back on its own; the answer is saved with your other settings, so if you're signed in it follows you to every device. You can always [replay it from Settings](#replaying-the-tour).

## What It Covers

The tour walks the diagramming chrome in a natural order:

1. **The Palette**: the strip across the top that every shape, arrow, and icon comes from.
2. **Selection Modes**: the dropdown that changes what your pointer does (Select, Hand, Eraser, and friends). See [Selection Modes](/help/selection-modes/).
3. **Palette Categories**: the Palette's category dropdown, with Popular first and the rest grouped into bands such as Common, Structure and Decorate.
4. **Tab Mode**: the editor mode switch. The tab you're on is a diagram; the switch (or **Shift+D**) moves it to another mode. See [Editor Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/).
5. **The Element Menu**: the right-click menu (long-press on touch) where styling, links, notes, comments, and actions live. On an empty tab the tour adds a square to show it on, and takes it away again when the tour ends.
6. **Tabs**: one document holds many diagrams, one per tab, plus the **+** button and the tab menu.
7. **Theme & Canvas**: the paintbrush button in the bottom-right corner that restyles the whole tab.

A closing **You're ready to diagram** card then offers **Visit Help Centre** or **Start diagramming**.

On a phone the tour is shorter: it skips the Tab Mode and Theme & Canvas steps. On an [Event Storming board](/help/canvas/event-storming-boards/) it skips Selection Modes, Palette Categories and Tab Mode, since the board offers none of them.

## During the Tour

- Each step shows a **step counter** ("2 of 7") with progress dots, a title, a sentence or two of copy, and a **Next** button (with **Back** from the second step on), with **Skip tour** always available.
- The highlight ring **never blocks your pointer**: you're free to poke at whatever is highlighted while the step is up. The tour opens real menus, so what you see is exactly what you'll use afterwards.
- Finishing (or skipping) closes anything the tour opened and returns you to a quiet editor, ready to work.

## Replaying the Tour

You can rerun the tour whenever you like:

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Accessibility**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>Under **Show Tours**, switch **Diagram** back on.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Close Settings. On a Diagram tab the tour relaunches from the welcome card, exactly like the first
  run; on a tab in another mode, it waits until you're on a Diagram tab.
</Step>

Finishing (or skipping) the rerun switches **Diagram** off again. Plan and Facilitate modes have tours of their own, replayed the same way from **Plan** and **Facilitate**: see [The Plan Tour](/help/canvas/plan-mode/plan-tour/) and [The Facilitate Tour](/help/canvas/facilitate-mode/facilitate-tour/).
