The Diagram tour is a guided, interactive introduction to [Diagram mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/): building a diagram from shapes, arrows and the palette. Rather than showing screenshots, it opens the real panels and menus one at a time, ringing each one and explaining it in a strip above the tab bar, the coach dock, so nothing you're working on is ever covered. It takes about a minute, and you can leave at any point. (It was called the Welcome Tour until October 2026.)

## When It's Offered

Whenever you're on a Diagram tab you can edit and haven't answered the tour yet, **New to diagrams?** appears in the coach dock above the tab bar once you have picked a template or closed **Start Diagramming**: **Take the Tour** starts it, **No Thanks** dismisses it. Leave it alone and after a few seconds it folds into a small **Diagram Tour** chip in the tab bar: press it to start, or its **×** to say no thanks. (A view-only document or an embed does not offer it.)

The tour only appears on a **Diagram tab**. On a tab in Draw, Workshop, Pages or Plan, the offer waits until you work on a tab in Diagram mode. Those modes have tours of their own: see [The Plan Tour](/help/canvas/plan-mode/plan-tour/), [The Workshop Tour](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/workshop-tour/), [The Draw Tour](/help/canvas/draw-mode/draw-tour/) and [The Pages Tour](/help/canvas/pages-mode/pages-tour/). Switching the tab to another mode partway through ends the tour.

If a dialog is open when the offer is due (Share, Settings, Search, any dialog), the offer waits and appears once you have closed it. The same goes for every other tour.

You only need to answer **once**. However you answer (decline, skip midway, or finish), it won't come back on its own; the answer is saved with your other settings, so if you're signed in it follows you to every device. You can always [replay it from Settings](#replaying-the-tour). Until you answer, it keeps coming back: leaving it alone, or moving on before you answer, isn't an answer.

## What It Covers

The tour walks the diagramming chrome in a natural order:

1. **The Palette**: the strip across the top that every shape, arrow, and icon comes from.
2. **Selection Modes**: the dropdown that changes what your pointer does (Select, Hand, Eraser, and friends). See [Selection Modes](/help/selection-modes/).
3. **Palette Categories**: the Palette's categories along the strip, with the first one, Shapes, open to show its elements in their groups.
4. **The Element Menu**: the right-click menu (long-press on touch) where styling, links, notes, comments, and actions live. On an empty tab the tour adds a square to show it on, and takes it away again when the tour ends.
5. **Tabs**: one document holds many diagrams, one per tab, plus the **+** button and the tab menu.
6. **Theme & Canvas**: the button in the bottom-right corner, drawn as a painter's palette, that restyles the whole tab.

The tour closes with **You're ready to diagram**, a **Help Centre** link and **Start**.

On a phone the tour is shorter: it skips the Theme & Canvas step. On an [Event Storming board](/help/canvas/event-storming-boards/) it skips Selection Modes and Palette Categories, since the board offers neither.

## During the Tour

- Each step shows in the coach dock: a **step counter** ("2 of 6") with a progress bar, a title, a sentence or two, and **Next** (with **Back** from the second step on), with **End Tour** always there. The canvas gives the dock its room, so nothing is covered.
- A ring with the step's number marks what the step is about, with nothing dimmed. It **never blocks your pointer**: you're free to poke at whatever is ringed while the step is up. The tour opens real menus, so what you see is exactly what you'll use afterwards.
- Opening a dialog partway through (Share, say) hides the ring while the dialog is up. Close it and the tour comes back on the same step. A dialog the tour opens itself to show you something stays part of the tour.
- Finishing (or skipping) closes anything the tour opened and returns you to a quiet editor, ready to work.

## Replaying the Tour

You can rerun the tour whenever you like:

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Tours**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>Switch **Diagram Tour** back on.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Close Settings. On a Diagram tab the tour's offer shows again in the coach dock, exactly like the
  first run; on a tab in another mode, it waits until you're on a Diagram tab.
</Step>

Finishing (or skipping) the rerun switches **Diagram** off again. The other modes have tours of their own, replayed the same way from **Draw**, **Workshop**, **Pages** and **Plan**: see [The Draw Tour](/help/canvas/draw-mode/draw-tour/), [The Workshop Tour](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/workshop-tour/), [The Pages Tour](/help/canvas/pages-mode/pages-tour/) and [The Plan Tour](/help/canvas/plan-mode/plan-tour/).
