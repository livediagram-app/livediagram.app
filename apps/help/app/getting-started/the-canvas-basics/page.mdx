The [canvas](/help/canvas/the-canvas/) is the surface where you build diagrams. It fills most of the editor, with floating panels on top for adding elements and navigating your library. This article covers moving around it so the rest of livediagram feels natural.

## Moving around

You will pan and zoom constantly. There are a few ways to do each:

- **Pan** by holding the **Space** bar and dragging, by **middle-click dragging** from any tool, or by switching to the **Hand** tool (shortcut `H`) and dragging on empty canvas. On a trackpad, a two-finger drag pans too.
- **Zoom** with the zoom buttons in the bottom-right of the canvas, `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + scroll, `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `+` and `-`, or pinch-zoom on a trackpad or touch screen. `Shift+1` zooms to fit everything on the tab. The pattern background tiles endlessly as you move, so you never run out of canvas.

On a phone-sized screen the Hand tool is the default, so a drag moves the view; on a larger screen the default is the Select tool. See [Pan and Zoom](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/) and [Zoom Controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/).

## Select versus Hand

The selection-mode dropdown at the top of the Palette holds the canvas tools (see [Selection Modes](/help/selection-modes/)). The two you will use most:

- **Select** (shortcut `V`) is for working with elements: click to select (click again to deselect), shift-click to add to the selection, and drag on empty canvas to draw a marquee that selects everything inside it.
- **Hand** (shortcut `H`) is for moving the view: drag on empty canvas to scroll around without touching any elements.

Middle-click panning works from any tool, so you rarely need to leave Select just to move the view a little.

## The floating panels

A few panels float over the canvas. On a computer each can be dragged by its header to another corner and collapsed to a banner (see [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/)):

- **Palette** (top-right by default) adds shapes, arrows, icons, and tools. See the [Palette](/help/palette/) guides.
- **Explorer** (top-left by default) is your library of documents and folders. See [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).

Around them sit the fixed controls: the **tab bar** along the bottom, with Search, Settings and the light/dark switch at its right-hand end, and a cluster in the bottom-right corner with Undo and Redo, Layers, the **Theme & canvas** paintbrush and the zoom controls. **Right-click menus** carry per-element formatting, and the tab menu carries tab-wide tools.

On a phone, the palette becomes a strip across the top with a menu button for the Explorer (the [Toolbar layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/)), panels stay where they are, and tapping a panel's header collapses it, so the canvas stays readable.

## Light and dark

The editor has its own appearance switch at the right-hand end of the bottom bar: click it to step through **Light**, **Dark** and **System** (which follows your device). This recolours the editor's interface around your canvas. It is separate from a diagram's [theme](/help/canvas/themes/), which recolours the canvas content itself and is set from the **Theme & canvas** paintbrush. See [Dark Mode](/help/tools/dark-mode/).

<Tip>
  Lost on a big canvas? Zoom out with the bottom-right controls to get your bearings, then zoom back
  in on the part you need. For total focus, try [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) to hide all the
  toolbars and panels.
</Tip>
