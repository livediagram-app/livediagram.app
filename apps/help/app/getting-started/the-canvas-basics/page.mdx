The [canvas](/help/canvas/the-canvas/) is the surface where you build diagrams. It fills most of the editor, with the palette and a few panels on top for adding elements and navigating your library. This article covers moving around it so the rest of livediagram feels natural.

## Moving around

You will pan and zoom constantly. There are a few ways to do each:

- **Pan** by holding the **Space** bar and dragging, by **middle-click dragging** from any tool, or by switching to the **Hand** tool (shortcut `H`) and dragging on empty canvas. On a trackpad, a two-finger drag pans too.
- **Zoom** with the zoom buttons in the bottom-right of the canvas, `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + scroll, `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `+` and `-`, or pinch-zoom on a trackpad or touch screen. `Shift+1` zooms to fit everything on the tab. The pattern background tiles endlessly as you move, so you never run out of canvas.

On a phone-sized screen the Hand tool is the default, so a drag moves the view; on a larger screen the default is the Select tool. See [Pan and Zoom](/help/canvas/the-canvas/pan-and-zoom/) and [Zoom Controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/).

## Select versus Hand

The selection-mode dropdown at the top of the Palette holds the canvas tools (see [Selection Modes](/help/selection-modes/)). The two you will use most:

- **Select** (shortcut `V`) is for working with elements: click to select (click again to deselect), shift-click to add to the selection, and drag on empty canvas to draw a marquee that selects everything inside it.
- **Hand** (shortcut `H`) is for moving the view: drag on empty canvas to scroll around without touching any elements.

Middle-click panning works from any tool, so you rarely need to leave Select just to move the view a little.

## Around the canvas

A few things sit over the canvas (see [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/)):

- **Palette**, the strip across the top, adds shapes, arrows, icons, and tools. See the [Palette](/help/palette/) guides.
- **Explorer**, behind the menu button in the top-left corner, is your library of documents and folders. See [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
- **Mode switch**, beside the menu button, moves the tab between Diagram, Draw, Illustrate, Plan and Facilitate. On a phone, use **Mode** in the tab menu instead. See [Editor Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/).
- **Corner panels**, such as the Map and the AI Assistant, can be dragged by their header to another corner and collapsed to a banner.

Around them sit the fixed controls: the **tab bar** along the bottom, with Search, Settings and the light/dark switch at its right-hand end, and a cluster in the bottom-right corner with Undo and Redo, the [session tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/) (timer, vote and poll), Layers beside the **Theme & canvas** paintbrush, and the zoom controls. **Right-click menus** carry per-element formatting, and the tab menu carries tab-wide tools.

On a phone the layout is the same: the menu button sits in its own card left of the strip, the strip's tiles swipe sideways, and tapping a panel's header collapses it, so the canvas stays readable.

## Light and dark

The editor has its own appearance switch at the right-hand end of the bottom bar: click it to step through **Light**, **Dark** and **System** (which follows your device). This recolours the editor's interface around your canvas, and the canvas too while the tab uses the **Default** theme, which has a light and a dark side. Any other [theme](/help/canvas/themes/) keeps its own colours whatever the appearance: a theme recolours the canvas content itself and is set from the **Theme & canvas** paintbrush. See [Dark Mode](/help/tools/dark-mode/).

<Tip>
  Lost on a big canvas? Zoom out with the bottom-right controls to get your bearings, then zoom back
  in on the part you need. For total focus, try [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) to hide all the
  toolbars and panels.
</Tip>
