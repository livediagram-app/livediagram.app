The welcome tour is a guided, interactive introduction to the editor. Rather than showing screenshots, it opens the real panels and menus one at a time, spotlighting each with a highlight ring and a short explanation. It takes about a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## When it's offered

The first document you ever create opens with a centred welcome card: **Show me around** starts the tour, **No thanks** dismisses it. The same card lets you **choose your panel layout**: Floating or Toolbar, drawn side by side with the one in force ringed. Click a drawing and the editor switches straight away, so you can try both before deciding, and the tour that follows points at the layout you picked. Your choice sticks whether you take the tour or not, and you can change it later in Settings under **Panel Layout**. (A phone always uses Toolbar, so there the card has no layout choice.) The offer appears **once ever**. However you answer (decline, skip midway, or finish), it won't come back on its own, and if you're signed in the answer follows you to every device. You can always [replay it from Settings](#replaying-the-tour).

## What it covers

The tour walks the editor's chrome in a natural order:

1. **The Palette**: the floating panel every shape, arrow, and icon comes from.
2. **Selection modes**: the dropdown that changes what your pointer does (Select, Hand, Eraser, and friends). See [Selection modes](/help/selection-modes/).
3. **Shape categories**: the Palette's category picker, with Favourites first and the rest grouped into the Common, Structure, Decorate and Dynamic bands.
4. **Diagram & Draw**: the switch between the two editor modes, Diagram for shapes and arrows, Draw for sketching by hand (**Shift+D** flips between them). See [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/).
5. **The Explorer**: browsing and organising your documents without leaving the editor.
6. **The element menu**: the right-click menu where styling, links, notes, comments, and actions live.
7. **Tabs**: pages within one document, plus the **+** button and the tab menu.
8. **Theme & Canvas**: the paintbrush button that restyles the whole tab. (The button lives on every screen size; the tour step itself only runs on desktop.)

On a phone the tour is slightly shorter: it skips the Theme & Canvas step.

## During the tour

- Each step shows a **step counter**, a title, a sentence or two of copy, and **Back** / **Next** buttons, with **Skip** always available.
- The highlight ring **never blocks your pointer**: you're free to poke at whatever is highlighted while the step is up. The tour opens real menus, so what you see is exactly what you'll use afterwards.
- Finishing (or skipping) closes anything the tour opened and returns you to a quiet editor, ready to work.

## Replaying the tour

You can rerun the tour whenever you like:

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Accessibility**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>Switch **Show Welcome Tour** back on.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Close Settings. The tour relaunches from the welcome card, exactly like the first run.
</Step>

Finishing the rerun marks it completed again.
