The welcome tour is a guided, interactive introduction to the editor. Rather than showing screenshots, it opens the real panels and menus one at a time, spotlighting each with a highlight ring and a short explanation. It takes about a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## When it's offered

When you create your first document from the [New Document wizard](/help/getting-started/your-first-diagram/), it opens with a centred **Welcome to livediagram** card: **Show me around** starts the tour, **No thanks** dismisses it. (A document opened through **Start Blank**, a share link or an embed does not offer it, and nor does a view-only document.)

The offer appears **once ever**. However you answer (decline, skip midway, or finish), it won't come back on its own; the answer is saved with your other settings, so if you're signed in it follows you to every device. You can always [replay it from Settings](#replaying-the-tour).

## What it covers

The tour walks the editor's chrome in a natural order:

1. **The Palette**: the strip across the top that every shape, arrow, and icon comes from.
2. **Selection modes**: the dropdown that changes what your pointer does (Select, Hand, Eraser, and friends). See [Selection Modes](/help/selection-modes/).
3. **Shape categories**: the Palette's category dropdown, with Popular first and the rest grouped into the Common, Structure, Decorate and Dynamic bands.
4. **Diagram & Draw**: the editor mode switch. Diagram is for shapes, arrows and the palette, Draw for pens, the eraser and sketching by hand, and the same switch also offers Illustrate and Plan. **Shift+D** moves to the next mode. See [Editor Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/).
5. **The Explorer**: browsing and organising your documents without leaving the editor. The step opens it from the menu button.
6. **The element menu**: the right-click menu (long-press on touch) where styling, links, notes, comments, and actions live.
7. **Tabs**: pages within one document, plus the **+** button and the tab menu.
8. **Theme & canvas**: the paintbrush button in the bottom-right corner that restyles the whole tab.

A closing **You're ready to go** card then offers **Visit Help Centre** or **Start creating**.

On a phone the tour is shorter: it skips the Diagram & Draw and Theme & canvas steps. On an [Event Storming board](/help/canvas/event-storming-boards/) it skips Selection modes, Shape categories and Diagram & Draw, since the board offers none of them.

## During the tour

- Each step shows a **step counter** ("2 of 8") with progress dots, a title, a sentence or two of copy, and **Back** / **Next** buttons, with **Skip tour** always available.
- The highlight ring **never blocks your pointer**: you're free to poke at whatever is highlighted while the step is up. The tour opens real menus, so what you see is exactly what you'll use afterwards.
- Finishing (or skipping) closes anything the tour opened and returns you to a quiet editor, ready to work.

## Replaying the tour

You can rerun the tour whenever you like:

<Step n={1}>Open **Settings** from the editor and go to **Accessibility**.</Step>

<Step n={2}>Switch **Show Welcome Tour** back on.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Close Settings. The tour relaunches from the welcome card, exactly like the first run.
</Step>

Finishing (or skipping) the rerun switches **Show Welcome Tour** off again. Plan mode has a tour of its own, replayed the same way from **Show Plan Tour**: see [The Plan Tour](/help/canvas/plan-mode/plan-tour/).
