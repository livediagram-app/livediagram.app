The Avatar tour shows you everything your character can do. It works on the real editor rather than screenshots: it rings each part and explains it in the coach dock, a strip above the tab bar that never covers your canvas, opening the Avatar panel to show it and closing it again after. It changes nothing on your canvas or your character, takes under a minute, and you can leave at any point.

## When It's Offered

The first time you use [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/), **New to your avatar?** appears in the coach dock above the tab bar: **Take the Tour** starts it, **No Thanks** dismisses it. Leave it alone and after a few seconds it folds into a small **Avatar Tour** chip in the tab bar: press it to start, or its **×** to say no thanks. It comes back on your next visit until you answer.

It's offered whatever your role, including a view-only link, since Avatar mode is yours too. It isn't offered in an embed.

The offer appears **once ever**. However you answer (decline, skip midway, or finish), it won't come back on its own, and if you're signed in the answer follows you to every device. Turning on **Power User Mode** in Settings marks it as seen too.

## What It Covers

1. **Your Avatar**: the **Avatar** button next to Undo and Redo, where your character sheet lives.
2. **Make It Yours**: gender, clothing, hair and size, the dice for a whole new look, and right-clicking your character to swap gender.
3. **Behaviour and Reactions**: jumping jacks, a wave, a spin, a cheer or a dance, and bursts like confetti and hearts.
4. **Say Something**: pressing **Enter** to type in a bubble above your head, or **Send a Message** in the panel. Not shown on a view-only link, where you can't speak.
5. **Give Someone a Push**: clicking someone else's character to walk over and push them, and walking with the arrow keys and hopping with Space.

Each step shows in the coach dock with **End Tour**, **Back** and **Next**, and a ring with the step's number marks what it's about. It closes with **You're Ready to Roam**, which links to the [Avatar mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) article and ends the tour with **Start**. If you leave Avatar mode or switch tab mid-tour, it ends as skipped.
