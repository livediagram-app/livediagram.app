The Laser Pointer mode gives you a temporary laser trail for drawing attention while presenting or walking a group through the canvas. Choose **Laser** from the tool picker at the top of the floating palette, or press **`K`**.

## How it works

Move the cursor to leave a glowing trail that fades on its own, after about a second at the default setting. It is purely for emphasis: it draws nothing permanent and changes none of your elements, so you can gesture freely without altering the canvas.

On a shared document everyone else sees your trail live, in your colour, and your cursor is hidden for the duration so they see only the laser dot.

- **With a mouse or trackpad**, just move: the trail follows the pointer. A click-drag pans the canvas, like the Hand tool, and the trail keeps drawing while you pan.
- **On a touchscreen**, drag a finger to draw the trail. To move around, switch to [Hand](/help/selection-modes/hand/) for a moment.

## Setting up your pen

While the mode is active a **Laser** panel sits under the palette, with four settings over a live preview of the stroke:

- **Width**: **Fine**, **Medium** or **Bold**.
- **Colour**: **Your colour** (your participant colour, the default) or one of eight presets, for when your colour doesn't read against the canvas you are presenting.
- **Trail**: how long the line lingers: **Quick**, **Normal** or **Long**. Long turns the pointer into something you can draw a shape with.
- **Effect**: **Beam** (a clean line), **Glow** (a soft halo that survives a projector), **Comet** (tapers away behind the tip), or **Spark** (a dotted trail).

Everyone in the room sees your pen, not their own default, and the settings are remembered on this device for next time.

## The laser inside a presentation

The laser is not only an editor tool. While you are running a [slide deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/), press **`L`** to arm it over the slide and `L` again to put it away. It is one of only two tools reachable from a presentation (the [spotlight](/help/selection-modes/spotlight/) is the other), because pointing at the canvas is the one thing a presenter needs to do to it and neither tool can change it.

While the laser is armed, clicking draws rather than advancing the slide. The keys and the buttons in the presenter's controls still move the deck. Leaving the presentation gives your tool back the way you found it.

## Who can use it

- **View-only visitors** can use the laser too, with `K`, since it changes nothing.
- **On an empty canvas** it is greyed out in the picker. Add an element and it becomes available again.
- **In [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/)** the palette shows Draw's own tools and `K` is not a shortcut there.
- During a vote with [**Hide cursors**](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-privacy/) on, other people's laser trails are hidden along with their cursors.
