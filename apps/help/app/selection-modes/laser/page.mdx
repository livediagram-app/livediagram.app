The Laser Pointer mode gives you a temporary laser trail for drawing attention while presenting or walking a group through a diagram. Choose it from the mode picker at the top of the floating palette, or press **`K`**.

## How it works

Move the cursor to leave a glowing trail that fades on its own. It is purely for emphasis: it draws nothing permanent and changes none of your elements, so you can gesture freely without altering the diagram.

## Setting up your pen

While the mode is active a **Laser** panel sits under the palette, with four settings over a live preview of the stroke:

- **Width** — Fine, Medium, or Bold.
- **Colour** — your own participant colour (the default) or one of eight presets, for when your colour doesn't read against the diagram you are presenting.
- **Trail** — how long the line lingers: Quick, Normal, or Long. Long turns the pointer into something you can draw a shape with.
- **Effect** — **Beam** (a clean line), **Glow** (a soft halo that survives a projector), **Comet** (tapers away behind the tip), or **Spark** (a dotted trail).

Everyone in the room sees your pen, not their own default, and the settings are remembered on this device for next time.

## When it is unavailable

The Laser Pointer is disabled on an empty canvas. Add an element and the mode becomes available again.
