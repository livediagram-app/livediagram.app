The Eraser mode is the fastest way to remove elements from the canvas. Choose it from the tool picker at the top of the floating palette, or press **`E`**.

## Erasing elements

- **Click** an element to delete it.
- **Drag** across several elements to remove them all in one sweep.

This is quicker than selecting elements and then deleting them, especially when you want to clear out a cluster of shapes or arrows at once. Erasing a shape also removes the arrows attached to it, just as deleting it would.

## Setting up the brush

While the mode is active an **Eraser** panel sits under the palette, over a preview of the brush. Each row shows its current setting; press it to change it.

- **Mode**: **Sweep** (drag across things to erase them) or **Tap** (one press, one thing), which is the safer choice on a crowded canvas.
- **Size**: **Point** for the exact pixel, or **Small**, **Medium** and **Large** brushes. Above Point, a ring follows the cursor at the brush's real size, so you can see what a sweep will take before you press. The brush stays the same size on screen however far you zoom.
- **Erases**: **Anything**, **Drawings** (pencil and [highlighter](/help/palette/tools/highlighter/) strokes only), or **Arrows** (connectors only). Drawings is what makes sketching over the canvas safe: sweep the whole thing at Large and everything underneath is untouched. Arrows lets you rewire without disturbing the boxes.

<Figure caption="The Eraser panel set to a Large brush that erases Drawings only: the ring turns amber, and the shape under the scribble is safe.">
  <EraserPanelScene />
</Figure>

The ring turns amber whenever a filter is on, so a restricted eraser never looks like a broken one. Your settings are remembered on this device, so check the **Erases** row if the eraser seems to be ignoring something.

Locked elements, and anything on a locked or hidden layer, are never erased whatever the panel says. A whole sweep is still a single undo.

## When it is unavailable

- **On an empty canvas** the Eraser is greyed out, since there is nothing to erase.
- **On a view-only link** there is no Eraser: erasing changes the document.
- **In [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/)** the Eraser is left out of the picker.
- **In [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/)** the drawing dock has its own Eraser (still `E`), with its settings in the dock rather than this panel.

<Note>
  Erasing removes elements straight away. If you take off something you meant to keep, press
  `Ctrl+Z` (`Cmd+Z` on a Mac) to [undo](/help/canvas/undo/) the sweep and bring it all back.
</Note>
