The Spotlight mode dims the canvas except a soft circle of light that follows your cursor, so a viewer's attention lands on one spot at a time. Choose it from the tool picker at the start of the palette strip. It has no keyboard shortcut.

## How it works

As you move the cursor, the circle of light moves with it while everything outside stays dimmed. **Left-click grows the circle and right-click shrinks it**, so you can match the beam to a single shape or open it up over a whole cluster. This is useful when you are talking through the canvas and want to focus the room on one area before moving on to the next.

The canvas cannot be edited while the light is on: clicks size the light rather than selecting anything, and picking Spotlight clears whatever you had selected. You can still pan by dragging with the middle mouse button, holding `Space` and dragging, scrolling with two fingers on a trackpad, or with the [zoom controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/). Pick **Select** (or press `V`) to go back to editing.

## Setting up the light

While the mode is active a **Spotlight** panel sits at the top right, over a live preview of the shroud:

- **Size**: **Small**, **Medium** or **Large**. Clicking the canvas still grows and shrinks the light freely; the row reads **Custom** once you have nudged it off a preset.
- **Dim**: how dark everything outside the light goes: **Soft**, **Normal**, **Dark**, or **Blackout**, which is the one to reach for on a projector.
- **Edge**: **Soft** for a feathered rim that reads as lighting, or **Crisp** for a defined pool of light.
- **Shape**: a **Circle**, or **Wide**: a broad ellipse for lighting a swimlane or a table row without dragging the rows around it into the dark.

<Figure caption="The Spotlight panel with a Wide shape on Blackout, lighting one row of a board.">

</Figure>

Dim, Edge and Shape are remembered on this device; the light always opens at Medium size. The shroud is yours alone: everyone else keeps seeing the whole canvas, so on a call, share your screen for others to see the light.

## The spotlight inside a presentation

While you are running a [slide deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/), press **`S`** to light the spotlight over the slide and `S` again to put it out. It and the [laser](/help/selection-modes/laser/) are the only two tools a presentation lets you reach, because neither can change what is on the slide.

With the spotlight lit, a click sizes the beam instead of advancing the slide, so use the keys (arrows, Space, Page Up and Page Down) or the presenter's buttons to move the deck while you are lighting something up.

## Availability

- **Desktop only.** Spotlight relies on a hovering pointer and on left and right clicks, so it is not offered on a phone-sized screen. If the window shrinks to phone width while it is on, you are put back on Select.
- **Not on an empty canvas.** Add an element and it becomes available.
- **Participants** pick it from their palette like an Editor.
- **View-only visitors** can use it too: with no palette, they type "Spotlight" into the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/).
