The Spotlight mode dims the canvas except a soft circle of light that follows your cursor, so a viewer's attention lands on one spot at a time. Choose it from the mode picker at the top of the floating palette.

## How it works

As you move the cursor, the circle of light moves with it while everything outside stays dimmed. **Left-click grows the circle and right-click shrinks it**, so you can match the beam to a single shape or open it up over a whole cluster. This is useful when you are talking through a diagram and want to focus the room on one area before moving on to the next.

## Setting up the light

While the mode is active a **Spotlight** panel sits under the palette, over a live preview of the shroud:

- **Size** — Small, Medium, or Large. Clicking the canvas still grows and shrinks the light freely; the row reads **Custom** once you have nudged it off a preset.
- **Dim** — how dark everything outside the light goes: Soft, Normal, Dark, or **Blackout**, which is the one to reach for on a projector.
- **Edge** — **Soft** for a feathered rim that reads as lighting, or **Crisp** for a defined pool of light.
- **Shape** — a **Circle**, or **Wide**: a broad ellipse for lighting a swimlane or a table row without dragging the rows around it into the dark.

Your settings are remembered on this device. Remember that the shroud is yours alone — everyone else keeps seeing the whole diagram.

## The spotlight inside a presentation

While you are running a [slide deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/presenting/), press **`S`** to light the spotlight over the slide and `S` again to put it out. It and the [laser](/help/selection-modes/laser/) are the only two tools a presentation lets you reach, because neither can change what is on the slide.

With the spotlight lit, a click sizes the beam instead of advancing the slide, so use the arrow keys or the presenter's buttons to move the deck while you are lighting something up.

## Availability

Spotlight is **desktop only**, and is disabled on an empty canvas. Add an element on a desktop browser and the mode becomes available.
