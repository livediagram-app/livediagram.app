**Appearance** is how livediagram itself looks, just for you. It has three settings, **Light**, **Dark** and **System**, and one button that steps through them in that order.

**One choice covers everything.** Pick Dark in the help centre and the editor opens dark too, and the other way round.

## Where to change it

- **In the editor:** the Appearance button sits in the right-hand cluster of the tab bar, beside **Search** and **Settings**. It shows the setting you are on (a sun for Light, a moon for Dark, a monitor for System) and, on a wider screen, its name. Each click moves to the next setting; hover it to see which one is current. On a phone it is icon-only.
- **In Settings:** the **Appearance** category has a **Theme** row with all three settings side by side, if you would rather pick one directly than cycle.
- **On the website, this help centre and the telemetry dashboard:** a small sun / moon / monitor button on a rail at the right-hand edge of the page. It only shows on wide screens, where there is room beside the content; on a narrower screen the page follows your saved choice, or your device.

With [Power User Mode](/help/user-interface/power-user-mode/) on, the editor's button becomes a quick switch: a click flips straight between Light and Dark, and a right-click sets System.

## The three settings

- **Light.** The editor's chrome stays light, whatever your device is set to.
- **Dark.** The editor's chrome stays dark, whatever your device is set to.
- **System.** The editor follows your operating system, so a machine that turns dark at sunset takes the editor with it, live, without a reload.

**System is where everyone starts.** Open livediagram on a machine set to dark and the editor is dark from the first paint, canvas included. Pick Light or Dark yourself and that choice outranks your device from then on, in both directions: a light editor on a dark machine is perfectly allowed.

## Appearance is yours; a theme is the tab's

The distinction worth holding on to is _who_ each setting belongs to.

Appearance belongs to **you and this browser**. It recolours the editor's interface (the tab bar, header, panels, and the backdrop around your work) and it is never saved into the document, so nobody you share with is affected by it.

A [theme](/help/canvas/themes/) belongs to **the tab**. It sets the canvas backdrop and the colours of your elements, it is stored on the tab, and everyone who opens that tab sees it.

So a light, pastel diagram can sit in a dark editor late at night, and a dark architecture diagram can sit in a light one, whichever is comfortable.

## The one that follows you: Default

The **Default** theme is the exception, deliberately. It is the scheme every new tab starts on, and it has a light half and a dark half: your appearance decides which one you see. Work in Dark and its canvas is charcoal with grey elements; switch to Light and the same tab is white with the familiar blue ones.

Nothing is written to the document when you switch: the tab is not edited, and there is nothing to save. A colleague reading the same tab in light chrome sees the light half at the same moment you are looking at the dark one.

Every other theme is a decision somebody made and stored, so it looks the same for everyone, in either appearance.

## The match nudge

Because the two are independent, they can end up mismatched: a dark-backdrop diagram in a light editor, or the reverse. When that happens, a small card appears at the bottom of the screen, just above the tab bar, saying **This tab uses a dark theme** (or a light one) with a button that switches the editor to match in one click. It works out "light" or "dark" from the theme's background brightness, so it is right for built-in and custom themes alike. Dismiss it with its close button and it stays away for that pairing until you reload, though it can still offer again on a differently styled tab. The theme picker offers the same one-click switch inline. The card never appears in zen mode or in an embedded diagram.

Tabs on the Default theme never trigger it. They already match, which is the point of them.

## Your choice sticks

- **Remembered.** Your pick is saved in this browser, so a refresh, or your next visit, keeps the appearance you chose, on every livediagram page. It is not tied to your account, so another browser or device starts on System until you pick there too.
- **No flash.** It is worked out before the page first paints (your saved pick, or your device setting if you have not made one) on every screen including the website, this help centre and the welcome and new-document flows, so you never see a flash of the wrong one.
