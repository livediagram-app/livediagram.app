Everything around the canvas is a **panel** or a fixed control. The canvas itself fills the screen, and the panels float on top of it at the edges rather than taking fixed slots in a rigid layout, so what is on the canvas always has room to breathe.

## What sits where

- **Top-left**: the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/), your documents and folders.
- **Top-right**: the [Palette](/help/palette/), for adding things and picking a selection mode. The AI, Poll and Vote panels open here too when they are in use.
- **Bottom-left**: the [Map](/help/user-interface/minimap/), once a tab has a few elements.
- **Bottom-right**: a fixed cluster of buttons: Undo and Redo, **Layers**, **Collaborate** (while the tab has a comment or an action), the **Theme & canvas** paintbrush, and the [zoom controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/). The Layers and Collaborate panels open from their buttons here.
- **Along the bottom**: the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/), with the [quick controls](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/) (Search, Settings, appearance) at its right-hand end.
- **Next to a selection**: the [toolbar](/help/user-interface/toolbar/), and the [Quick Style panel](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/) for a shape or arrow.

The tool panels (Laser, Spotlight, Eraser, Format, Avatar and Slide Deck) appear while their [selection mode](/help/selection-modes/) is in use.

## Put panels in the corner you want

On a computer, the floating panels can be dragged by their header to any corner of the screen: the Palette, Explorer, Layers, Collaborate, AI Assistant and Map panels, the tool panels, and the Poll and Vote panels that appear while a [session tool](/help/collaboration/session-tools/) is running. Snap guides appear in the four corners while you drag, and the nearest one highlights to show where the panel will land; drop it away from every corner and it stays free-floating right where you left it. Panels sharing a corner stack neatly and reflow when one of them is hidden.

Your arrangement is remembered on this device, so a reload restores it. Once a panel has moved from its default spot, a **Reset position** button in its header sends it back. Each panel can also be collapsed from its header, to a slim banner or back into its button.

The bottom-right buttons and the bottom bar stay where they are.

## The Toolbar layout and phones

Prefer less floating? Set **Panel Layout** to **Toolbar** in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) (under **Panels**): the Palette becomes one strip across the top of the canvas, and the Explorer opens from a menu button at its left. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/).

A phone always uses the Toolbar layout. There, panels can't be dragged: tap a panel's header to collapse or expand it, and Layers and Collaborate open as pop-overs above their buttons.

[Panel Opacity](/help/palette/panel-opacity/) fades every panel so the canvas shows through, and **UI Scale** under **Appearance** makes them bigger or smaller.

<Tip>
  Want even less on screen? [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) (`Z`) hides every panel and leaves
  just the canvas and the zoom controls.
</Tip>
