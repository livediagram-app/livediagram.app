Everything around the canvas is a toolbar, a **panel** or a fixed control. The canvas itself fills the screen, and the rest sits on top of it at the edges rather than taking fixed slots in a rigid layout, so what is on the canvas always has room to breathe. The layout is the same on a computer, a tablet and a phone.

## What sits where

- **Top-left**: the **menu button**, which opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) (your documents and folders) under it. On a computer the [editor mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/) switch sits beside it.
- **Top centre**: the [palette strip](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/), for picking a selection mode and adding things to the canvas. In [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/) its dock takes the strip's place.
- **Top-right**: the AI, Poll and Vote panels and the tool panels, when they are in use, below the strip when it reaches that far.
- **Bottom-left**: the [Map](/help/user-interface/minimap/), once a tab has a few elements.
- **Bottom-right**: a fixed cluster of buttons: Undo and Redo, **Layers**, **Collaborate** (while the tab has a comment or an action), the **Theme & canvas** paintbrush, and the [zoom controls](/help/user-interface/zoom-controls/). Layers and Collaborate open as popovers over their buttons, and close when you press the button again.
- **Along the bottom**: the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/), with the [quick controls](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/) (Search, Settings, appearance) at its right-hand end.
- **Next to a selection**: the [toolbar](/help/user-interface/toolbar/), and the [Quick Style panel](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/) for a shape or arrow.

The tool panels (Laser, Spotlight, Eraser, Format, Avatar and Slide Deck) appear while their [selection mode](/help/selection-modes/) is in use.

## Put panels in the corner you want

The corner panels can be dragged by their header to any corner of the screen: the AI Assistant and Map panels, the tool panels, and the Poll and Vote panels that appear while a [session tool](/help/collaboration/session-tools/) is running. Snap guides appear in the four corners while you drag, and the nearest one highlights to show where the panel will land; drop it away from every corner and it stays right where you left it. Panels sharing a corner stack neatly and reflow when one of them is hidden.

Your arrangement is remembered on this device, so a reload restores it. Once a panel has moved from its default spot, a **Reset position** button in its header sends it back. Each panel can also be collapsed from its header to a slim banner.

The palette strip, the menu button, the bottom-right buttons and the bottom bar stay where they are.

## On a phone

On a phone the menu button sits in a card of its own at the left of the strip, without the mode switch, and the strip's tiles swipe sideways. Tap a panel's header to collapse or expand it. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/#on-a-phone).

[Panel Opacity](/help/palette/panel-opacity/) fades every panel so the canvas shows through, and **UI Scale** under **Appearance** makes them bigger or smaller.

<Tip>
  Want even less on screen? [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) (`Z`) hides every panel and leaves
  just the canvas and the zoom controls.
</Tip>
