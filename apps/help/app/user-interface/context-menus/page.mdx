A **context menu** is the menu you get by right-clicking, or by pressing and holding for half a second on touch. Each one is scoped to whatever you clicked, so it only ever offers what makes sense there. On a phone the menus open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen.

## On an element

The element menu is where an element is **styled and described**. It is built from collapsible sections, and which ones appear depends on what you clicked. For a shape:

- **Layer**: **Bring to Front**, **Send to Back**, moving it to another [layer](/help/canvas/layers/), and its opacity.
- **Size**, **Shape** (change the kind of shape) and **Rotation**.
- **Style**: one-click [presets](/help/palette/shapes/style-presets/), **Colours** (text and background, with **Reset to theme**) and **Border**.
- **Text**: font, size, alignment and markers.
- **Collaborate**: **Add Comment** and **Assign Action**. **Resources**: a link and a note.

An arrow's menu has **Line** (straight, curved or angled, pattern and thickness) and **Pointer** (its arrowheads) instead of the shape sections, and images, tables, icons and data elements each add a section of their own.

The everyday verbs live elsewhere: **Duplicate**, layer order, **Lock** and **Delete** are on the [selection toolbar](/help/user-interface/toolbar/), and cut, copy and paste are on the keyboard (see [Copy and Paste](/help/tips-and-tricks/copy-and-paste/)). Right-click a multi-selection and you get the same kind of menu for the whole group.

On a desktop pointer, the style controls **preview on hover**: rest the pointer on a colour, border, preset, shape, rotation, marker, alignment, or size tile and the selection shows the change live; it only sticks when you click, and moving away reverts it. Comparing looks costs nothing.

## On empty canvas

Right-click empty canvas and you get the **tab menu** (below) with **Paste** leading it, since a right-click on empty space is usually "paste what I copied". Pasted elements land just offset from where they were copied, selected and ready to drag into place.

## On a tab

Right-click a tab in the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/), or use its **⋯** button, for the tab menu:

- A row of quick actions: **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Paste**, **Lock tab** (or **Unlock tab**), and **Delete**, which asks you to confirm and is unavailable while the tab is locked.
- **Organise**: **Add to Folder** (a [tab folder](/help/tabs/tab-folders/)) and **Add to Document** (copy it into another document).
- **Content**: **Import**, **Export** and **Clear**.
- **Mode**: the tab's [editor mode](/help/canvas/editor-modes/#one-mode-for-everyone), the same for everyone; choosing one switches the tab.
- **Cleanup**: auto layout and **Auto-align**. See [Layout Cleanup](/help/tools/layout-cleanup/).
- **Collaborate**: start a timer, a vote or a poll. See [Session Tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).

A tab's theme and canvas background are not in this menu: they are behind the **Theme & canvas** paintbrush in the bottom-right corner. See [Themes](/help/canvas/themes/).

View-only visitors get no element or tab menu, since everything in them changes the document.

Context menus close as soon as you pick an action or click elsewhere, and `Escape` closes them too.
