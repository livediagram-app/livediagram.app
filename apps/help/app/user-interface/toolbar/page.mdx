Select something on the canvas and a **toolbar** appears next to it, holding the quick actions for what you have selected. It is contextual: it shows up only when there is a selection, and its contents change to match. A small caption above it names the selection, for example "Selected Square".

<Figure caption="With one shape selected, the toolbar floats beside it with More, Add text, Duplicate, layer order, Lock and Delete.">
  <ContextualToolbar />
</Figure>

## One element selected

From left to right:

- **More** (**⋯**): opens the element's full menu, the same one a right-click opens, with its colours, borders, text styling and the rest. See [Context Menus](/help/user-interface/context-menus/).
- **Add Text** (or **Edit Text** once the element has some), and **Edit Points** on a path (drawn with the Path tool), to adjust its points and curves.
- On a [mind map](/help/palette/mind-maps/) node, **Add Child** and **Add Sibling**.
- **Duplicate**.
- **Bring to Front** and **Send to Back**.
- **Lock** (or **Unlock**) and **Delete**.

Which buttons show depends on the element: an image has no text to add, and a locked element hides **Edit Points** and the mind-map buttons, and its **Delete** is greyed out until you **Unlock** it. To comment on an element, open **More** and choose **Add Comment** under **Collaborate**. On a view-only link, where there is no element menu, the toolbar holds just an **Add Comment** button instead, so visitors can still join a discussion (except in embeds and Community posts, which show no comments).

## Several elements selected

Drag a marquee or Shift-click to select several, and the toolbar switches to the actions that make sense across a group:

- **More** (**⋯**): the selection's menu, for styling everything at once.
- **Filter Selection** (only when the selection mixes kinds): narrows it by type, so you can marquee over a busy area and keep, say, just the arrows. See [Multi-Select](/help/canvas/selecting-and-grouping/multi-select/).
- **Duplicate**, with the arrows between the selected elements included.
- **Combine** (on a [logo page](/help/canvas/illustrate/logo-pages/), when the shapes can be combined): unite, subtract, intersect or exclude them into one shape.
- **Export**: export just these elements.
- **Lock** (or **Unlock**) and **Delete**.

The caption reads "Selected Elements" with a count.

<Figure caption="Select a mix of elements and the toolbar switches to More, Filter Selection, Duplicate, Export, Lock and Delete.">
  <MultiSelectToolbar />
</Figure>

The toolbar follows your selection so the controls are always close to what they affect, and disappears the moment you deselect, handing the canvas back. Beside a selected shape or arrow, the [Quick Style panel](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/) offers the most-used colours and widths too.

In [power user mode](/help/user-interface/power-user-mode/) on a computer, **More** leaves the toolbar (right-click instead), and with Minimal Chrome on so do the caption and the bin (press `Delete` instead). Touch screens keep **More** and the bin, since they can't right-click or press `Delete`; the caption is hidden there too.

<Tip>
  To copy one element's look onto others, use the **Format** tool in the Palette's selection-mode
  dropdown (see [Format Painter](/help/selection-modes/format-painter/)).
</Tip>
