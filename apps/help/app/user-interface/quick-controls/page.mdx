The **quick controls** sit at the right-hand end of the bottom bar and gather the actions you want reachable at any moment, whatever you are doing on the canvas. They stay put while the rest of the interface comes and goes with your selection, and a matching cluster appears at the bottom of the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), so the two screens don't hide their controls in different places.

## What lives here

Three controls, left to right:

- **Search** — open the [search panel](/help/search-panel/) to jump to any diagram, tab, or element, or to drop something onto the canvas.
- **Settings** — the [Settings dialog](/help/user-interface/settings/): the preference toggles that follow you between devices, plus the **Keyboard** category with the full [shortcut reference](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) and the switch that turns single-key shortcuts off.
- **Appearance** — cycle the [interface between light, dark, and your device setting](/help/tools/dark-mode/). This is the app's own chrome, not the diagram's theme.

On a computer each control in the editor's bar shows its name beside the icon (the appearance toggle names the mode in force: Light, Dark, or System). On a phone the bar is tight, so the controls are icons only.

Because they are fixed in the corner rather than tied to a selection, the quick controls are the dependable starting point when you are not sure where an action lives.
