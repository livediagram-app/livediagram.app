**Settings** opens from the gear in the [quick controls](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/) at the end of the bottom bar, in the editor and in the Explorer. It holds the preferences that are about _you_ rather than about any particular document, which is why there is one set of them and not one per document.

The dialog lists its categories down the left, with a **Search settings** field above them, and shows one category at a time on the right. Type into the search to find a setting by name or by a word it is about ("transparency" finds **Panel Opacity**). On a phone, the list and the category take turns: tap a category to open it, and go back for the list.

Some categories hold **sub-categories** (Editor holds Draw and Illustrate; Panels holds one per panel; Account holds Notifications and API Tokens). On a computer, click the parent (or its arrow) to show them beneath it, and again to fold them away. On a phone, tap the parent and its sub-categories are listed at the bottom of its page.

## Editor

- **Quick-Add on Hover**: opens an element's quick-add **+** menu when you hover it, instead of waiting for a click; the **+** buttons still only appear on the selected element. Off by default. See [Quick-Add on Hover](/help/palette/quick-add-on-hover/).
- **Element Indicators**: how an element shows that it has a link, a note, an action or comments. **Top** (the default) puts small icons near its top, **Footer** puts a labelled row along its bottom edge, and **Off** hides them. See [Element Indicators](/help/canvas/element-indicators/).
- **Alignment Guides**: snap lines while you move or resize, so elements line up with their neighbours. On by default. See [Alignment Guides](/help/palette/alignment-guides/).
- **Auto-Attach Arrows**: when a move leaves an arrow running through a shape it connects, its end moves to the side facing the other end. On by default. See [Auto-Attach Arrows](/help/palette/auto-attach-arrows/).
- **Middle-Mouse Pan**: hold the middle mouse button and drag to pan from anywhere, whatever tool is active. On by default; turn it off to leave the middle button to your browser.
- **Power User Mode**, under **Power User**: applies a set of recommended settings in one go and unlocks **Minimal Chrome**, which hides labels and hints while keeping every control. Switching it off puts back the settings you did not change. See [Power User Mode](/help/user-interface/power-user-mode/).

### Draw

- **Dock Position**: where [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/)'s dock of pens, shapes and tools sits, **Top** or **Bottom** (closer to hand when drawing on a tablet). Only Draw mode has a dock, so Diagram mode is unchanged.

### Illustrate

- **Logo Guides**: shows a [logo page](/help/canvas/illustrate/logo-pages/)'s construction guides, which the artwork snaps to while they show. On by default; a page you switch them on or off for keeps its own choice.
- **Logo Guide Strength**: how strongly the guides show, **Faint**, **Medium** (the default) or **Strong**.
- **Logo Centre Lines**, **Logo Diagonals**, **Logo Safe Area**, **Logo Circles**, **Logo Square** and **Logo Grid**: one switch per guide, all on by default.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**, which follows your device. This is the editor's own chrome, not a diagram's [theme](/help/canvas/themes/), although a tab on the Default theme follows it. The same control sits in the bottom bar. Saved on this device only (see below). See [Dark Mode](/help/tools/dark-mode/).
- **UI Scale**: makes the panels, the toolbar and the buttons in the bottom-right corner (zoom, undo and redo, layers) bigger or smaller, from 80% to 120%, with 100% in the middle of the slider. The canvas, dialogs and menus stay as they are, so it is not the same as your browser's zoom. Beneath it, **Panel Scale**, **Toolbar Scale** and **Corner Buttons Scale** size each of those on its own (**Toolbar Scale** goes up to 140%); moving **UI Scale** again sets all three back to the same size. The chrome resizes as you drag. Desktop only: a phone always uses 100%.

## Keyboard

- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: the switch that turns the editor's keyboard shortcuts on and off. Off means all of them, chords like undo included, not just the single-letter keys. Saved on this device only (see below).
- **All Shortcuts**: the full shortcut reference, in collapsible groups. The **Keyboard Shortcuts** command in [search](/help/search-panel/) opens Settings straight on this category. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Panels

- **Panel Opacity**: fades every panel and the toolbar, from 30% to 100%, so the canvas shows through (buttons stay opaque); a panel snaps back to fully opaque while you hover or focus it. See [Panel Opacity](/help/palette/panel-opacity/).

**Panels** holds four sub-categories, one per panel. Each starts with a switch that turns its panel on or off; the panel's other settings show beneath it only while it is on.

- **Layers**: **Enable Layers Panel**, then **Layer Thumbnails**, **Layer Element Counts** and **Preview Layer on Hover**. Turning the panel off also hides its button and the **Move to layer** choices in the element menu; your layers keep working as before. See [Layers](/help/canvas/layers/).
- **Map**: **Enable Map** (the small overview of the whole tab in the bottom-left corner, once a tab has a few elements), **Dim Outside the View**, and **Map Size** (**Short**, **Medium** or **Tall**). Desktop only. See [Map](/help/user-interface/minimap/).
- **Collaborate**: **Enable Collaborate Panel**, the list of a tab's comment threads and actions. It only appears while the tab has a comment or an action. With it off, comments and actions still work from the elements. See [Comments](/help/collaboration/comments/).
- **Quick Style**: **Enable Quick Style Panel**, the panel of common styles that opens beside a selected shape or arrow. With it off, every style is still in the element's right-click menu. See [Quick Style Panel](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/).

## Accessibility

- **Reduce Motion**: turns off the decorative animations and transitions (panels, popovers, the snap guides) so the interface appears instantly instead of sliding. Your device's own reduce-motion setting is always respected; this lets you force it on even where the device doesn't ask for it. See [Accessibility](/help/getting-started/accessibility/).
- **Show Tours**: one switch for each guided tour, **Welcome Tour**, **Plan Tour** and **Facilitate Tour**. Each switches itself off once you have taken or dismissed its tour; switch it back on and close Settings to run that tour again. See [The Welcome Tour](/help/getting-started/welcome-tour/), [The Plan Tour](/help/canvas/plan-mode/plan-tour/) and [The Facilitate Tour](/help/canvas/facilitate-mode/facilitate-tour/).

## AI Tools

Only present on a deployment with AI configured.

- **AI Assistant**: shows the AI panel, with its **Ask** and **Clean** modes. Off by default. See [AI Tools](/help/tools/ai/ai-tools/).
- **Suggested Prompts**: a row of one-click starter prompts under the AI panel's mode buttons. On by default.
- **API Tokens**: **Manage API Tokens** jumps to **Account › API Tokens**, since the AI tools you connect over MCP use a token.

## Documents

- **Where New Documents Go**: the default folder for each kind of new document (diagrams, whiteboards, plans and so on), used when you create one without choosing a place. **Skip the Location Step** saves every new document in one place without asking; you turn it on from the **Location** step of the New Document wizard. See [Default Folders](/help/explorer/folders/default-folders/).

## Account

**Account** shows who you are (a guest, or your name and email when signed in), **Show My Profile Picture** (signed in only), your [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/) (deleted documents wait there for 30 days), **Cloud Sync** to [Google Drive](/help/account-and-data/google-drive/) where the deployment offers it, and **Delete Account**. It holds two sub-categories:

### Notifications

- **In-Editor Notifications**: the brief confirmation shown when you do something whose result isn't on screen, like moving a document to a folder or linking a tab. **Errors are always shown**, whatever this is set to, so a failure is never silently swallowed.
- **Email**: once signed in, one switch per email (someone joins, comments on or mentions you in your document, assigns you an action, responds to a team invite, plus tips and milestones). A guest sees a sign-in prompt instead. See [Email Notifications](/help/account-and-data/email-notifications/).

### API Tokens

- Create, view and revoke the tokens your own scripts and connected AI tools use to call livediagram as you. Each expires six months after it is created. A new token's secret is shown once, so copy it before you choose **Done**. Signed-in only, and absent on a deployment without accounts. See [API Tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/).

Anywhere Settings says something needs an account, it ends with a **Sign In** link that brings you back to where you were.

## Privacy

- **Send Anonymous Usage Events**: the small first-party events listed on the public [telemetry dashboard](/telemetry): no user content, no third-party trackers. Turn it off to keep everything you do on your device. See [What We Collect](/help/privacy-and-security/what-we-collect/).

## Settings follow you, most of them

Settings are **stored against your identity, not your browser**, and apply to every document you open on every device you sign in from. For a guest, "your identity" is the per-browser id, so the preferences last as long as that browser does and [migrate with your documents](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/) if you later sign up.

A few things deliberately stay on the device you are using:

- **Theme** (light, dark or system) in **Appearance**.
- The **Keyboard Shortcuts** switch, so you can keep shortcuts on where you have a full keyboard and off where you don't.
- **Panel positions**: which corner each panel docks into.
- **Explorer view**: List, Card or Details.
- **Tool settings**: the eraser's brush, the laser's pen, the spotlight, the avatar, Draw mode's pens and the presenter's settings.

The canvas-behaviour toggles ([auto-attach arrows](/help/palette/auto-attach-arrows/), [alignment guides](/help/palette/alignment-guides/), [quick-add on hover](/help/palette/quick-add-on-hover/) and [panel opacity](/help/palette/panel-opacity/)) used to sit in the palette's own gear menu. They now live here, under **Editor** and **Panels**, so every setting has one home.
