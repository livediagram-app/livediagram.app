A **Card** is a single diagram node with slots: a title, a muted meta line, a body, an icon and a row of tags. Use it wherever a node has more to say than its name: a service with what it runs on, a persona with their role, a feature with its status.

In Diagram and Workshop mode it sits in the palette's **Layout** category ([Build Elements](/help/palette/build/)); in Pages mode it is in **Components**. A new card shows every slot filled in, so you can see what each one is and switch off the ones you don't need.

## The slots

From the top:

- **Header Band**: a coloured band across the top that carries the title and meta line, for a card that reads as a titled panel.
- **Media**: at the top left, an icon on a **Badge** (a filled disc), on a **Tile** (a soft rounded square), or the title's **Initial**. Drop an icon from the palette onto a card to set it; a card with no media turns Tile on.
- **Title** and **Meta Line**: the name, and a quieter line beside it for the product, runtime, team or date.
- **Body**: the card's ordinary label. Double-click to edit it with the full text toolbar.
- **Tags**: up to six small chips along the bottom. A tag that doesn't fit the width is hidden until the card is wide enough again.

Select a card, then click its title, meta line or a tag to type in place. Enter keeps the change and Escape puts it back.

Right-click and open the **Card** section to switch **Header Band**, **Title** and **Meta Line** on or off, pick the **Media**, and add, reorder or remove tags. **Add Tag** is also on the ring round a selected card.

Everything else is an ordinary element: resize it and the text re-wraps, recolour it from **Colours**, connect arrows to it, and animate it like any shape.

## Callouts are cards

The **Callout** tile places a card with a badge, a heading and a body: a soft note box for a tip or a warning. It is a card underneath, so you can add tags or a meta line to it like any other.

## Accent edge

Any card, rectangle, stadium or mind-map node can carry an **accent edge**: a coloured strip down its left side, or across its top or bottom. Use it to colour-code nodes by team or status without filling the whole box.

<Figure caption="An accent edge on the left, the top and the bottom.">
  <AccentEdges />
</Figure>

Right-click, open **Border** and pick the side under **Accent Edge**. **Edge Colour** sets its colour, and **Match Border** makes it the border's own colour again. The format painter copies it along with the border.

<Tip>
  For a node with a name and one quiet line under it, you don't need a card: type both lines in a
  rectangle, then pick **Title and Caption** from the text toolbar's paragraph menu. The first line
  turns bold and the rest turns into a smaller, muted caption.
</Tip>
