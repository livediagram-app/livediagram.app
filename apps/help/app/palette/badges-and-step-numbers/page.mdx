A **Badge** is a small pill of text ("New", "v2.1", "PII", "3×"). A **Step Number** is a small numbered disc that walks a reader through a diagram in order, the way annotated architecture diagrams mark a request's path.

Both sit in the palette's **Layout** category in Diagram and Facilitate mode ([Build Elements](/help/palette/build/)), and in **Components** in Pages mode.

On the Toolbar strip, the Badge tile opens a few ready-made badges: **New**, **Beta**, **Version**, **Count**, **Draft** and **Warning** (a rose "PII"). Each is an ordinary badge you can then retype or restyle.

## Attaching to a node

Drop a badge or step near a corner or the middle of an edge of another element and it **attaches** there, centred on the point so it straddles the edge.

<Figure caption="The eight points a badge or a step attaches to.">
  <AttachPoints />
</Figure>

Once attached, it follows the node everywhere:

- Moving, nudging or laying out the node takes it along, and resizing or rotating the node keeps it on its point.
- Copying or duplicating the node copies it too, and deleting the node deletes it.
- It always draws above the node, whatever the layer order.

Drag it more than a short way off its point to detach it, or drop it on another point (on any element) to move it there. **Detach** in its right-click menu does the same without dragging. Frames, lanes and boundaries don't take badges: their corners are too far from what they hold.

A badge or step that isn't dropped on a point stands on its own, which suits a step sitting on an arrow.

## Badges

Double-click a badge to edit its text, up to 24 characters. Its width follows the text, so a single character makes a circle.

## Step numbers

A new step takes the **next number** on the tab: one more than the highest so far. Moving or deleting a step never renumbers the others, so numbers don't change while you work. When you've rearranged a walkthrough, pick **Renumber Steps** from a step's right-click menu to number every step on the tab 1, 2, 3 in reading order (top to bottom, then left to right).

A step's number is its label, up to three characters, so "12" and "3a" both fit. Resizing a step grows the disc and its number together.

## Looks

Right-click and open the **Badge** or **Step Number** section to pick one of five looks: **Solid**, **Ink**, **Outline**, **Tinted** and **Quiet**. They all come from the element's colour, so a theme switch or a new colour in **Colours** carries them. A new badge is Solid and a new step is Ink, so the two read apart on the same node.

## Animating steps in order

Give a step an animation (Pulse suits it) and **Play in Order** appears in its Step Number section. Turn it on and every step on the tab plays its animation one after another, 1, 2, 3, then round again: a walkthrough that walks itself.
