The **Stickers** category holds around 190 colour emoji you can drop straight onto the canvas. They are the informal half of a board: a thumbs up on somebody's idea, a red flag on the risky step, an arrow pointing at the thing everyone should be looking at, or a few flowers in the corner because a workshop board should not look like a spreadsheet.

Unlike the line-art [Icons](/help/palette/icons/), stickers are real colour glyphs, so they **keep their own colours whatever the theme**: a thumbs up stays yellow and a fire stays orange however you restyle the diagram.

## The ten groups

The category opens on a grid of group tiles, each showing one of the stickers inside it. Click a group to see everything it holds, and use the breadcrumb at the top to come back.

- **Reactions** — responding to somebody else's work: thumbs up and down, clapping, raised hands, a heart, fire, 💯, eyes.
- **Feelings** — the face set, from delighted to confused to completely done in.
- **Status** — check, cross, warning, red / amber / green dots, blocked, under construction, new, coming soon, trends up and down.
- **Direction** — arrows in every direction, pointing hands, map pins, a compass, play, and a chequered flag for the finish.
- **Celebrate** — party popper, confetti, trophy, medals, cake, gift, crown, rocket.
- **Decorate** — sparkles, rainbow, sun, snowflake, flowers, hearts in every colour, gems.
- **Meeting** — timers, calendar, speech and thought bubbles, megaphone, microphone, ballot box, brain, coffee.
- **Work** — bug, wrench, gear, key, folder, package, laptop, money bag, target.
- **People** — a person, a group, someone raising a hand, a worker, a detective.
- **Fun** — robot, alien, unicorn, cat, pizza, dice, a game controller.

## Finding one

Search by what you mean, not by the emoji's official name. The catalogue matches on synonyms, so "celebrate" finds the party popper, "blocked" finds the no-entry sign, "wip" finds the roadworks, and "thanks" finds the folded hands. Search always runs across **every** group, so you never have to guess where something was filed.

## Placing one

<Step n={1}>Open the palette and choose **Stickers** from the category dropdown.</Step>

<Step n={2}>Pick a group, or type in the search box to look across all ten at once.</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Click a sticker to add it to the canvas, or drag it out and drop it where you want it.
</Step>

<Step n={4}>
  Or **drag a sticker onto a shape** to make it that shape's icon, sitting beside the label. This is
  the quickest way to mark a box as done, blocked, or at risk.
</Step>

A sticker behaves like any other element once it is on the canvas: move it, resize it, put it in a group or a layer, and it exports with the diagram.

<Tip>
  Stickers work especially well with a [Reaction](/help/collaboration/) round in a workshop: drop a
  handful of thumbs and hearts near the ideas that landed, then move on. Because a sticker is just an
  element, everything you place is still there when you come back to the board.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Stickers used to live as an Emoji category inside the Icons tab. They have their own category now,
  so the Icons tab is purely line art and Stickers is the one place to browse or search the colour
  glyphs.
</Note>
