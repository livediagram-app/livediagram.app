Reset Palette Position is a button, not a setting. It lives in the palette's own header, between the help button and the collapse button, and only appears once you have moved the palette away from its home.

## What it does

The palette is movable: drag it by its header to any corner of the screen, where it docks, or drop it anywhere else and it floats right where you left it. The **Reset position** button snaps it back to its default spot, the top-right corner. Use it whenever the palette has drifted somewhere awkward and you want it back in its familiar place.

## When it shows

While the palette is in its home corner, the button isn't there at all, which keeps the header short. It appears as soon as the palette is somewhere else, whether floating free or docked in another corner, and hovering it shows "Snap back to the default corner." Once the palette is home again, the button goes away.

Every other floating panel has the same button in its header, so the Explorer, Layers, Collaborate and the Map can each be sent home the same way. See [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/) for docking panels into corners.

## Good to know

- **Per device.** Where your panels sit is remembered for this browser and device, not synced to your account, since a laptop and a large monitor want different arrangements.
- **Not on a phone.** Panels can't be dragged on a phone, so there is nothing to reset and the button never shows there.
- **Not in the Toolbar layout.** In the [Toolbar layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) the palette is a fixed strip across the top of the canvas, so it has no position to reset.
