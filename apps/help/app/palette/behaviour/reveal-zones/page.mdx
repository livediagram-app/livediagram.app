A **Reveal** zone is a cover you put over part of the canvas: answers, estimates, a retro column nobody should read early. It lives in the **Run the room** group of the [Behaviour](/help/palette/behaviour/) category.

## Uncovering it

**Double-click** (or double-tap) uncovers it **on your screen only**, with a small Hide pill to put it back. Two presses, so a stray click on a busy board doesn't give the game away.

<Figure caption="Covered, and uncovered for one person: the Hide pill puts the cover straight back.">
  <RevealZone />
</Figure>

**Tools › Reveal › Reveal for all** takes it off for everyone at once, and **Hide for all** in the same place puts it back over the room's screens.

## It hides, it does not protect

A Reveal zone hides content from a reader's eye, not from a determined reader: everything underneath is still in the diagram, so don't put anything genuinely private under one.
