A **Reveal** zone is a cover you put over part of the canvas: answers, estimates, a retro column nobody should read early. It lives in the palette's **Tools** category, one of the six [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/) categories.

## Uncovering it

The cover shows its label ("Hidden" until you give it one, such as "Estimates") and says how to open it: "Double-click to reveal", or "Double-tap" on a touch screen.

**Double-click** (or double-tap) uncovers it **on your screen only**, with a small **Hide** pill in its corner to put it back. Two presses, so a stray click on a busy canvas doesn't give the game away; the Hide pill needs only one. Nobody else's canvas changes, and nothing is saved, so it works on a view-only link too.

<Figure caption="Covered, and uncovered for one person: the Hide pill puts the cover straight back.">
  <RevealZone />
</Figure>

To take it off for everyone at once, right-click it and choose **Reveal for All** in its **Reveal** category; **Hide for All** in the same place puts it back over the room's screens. This one is saved with the document and can be undone, and it needs edit access.

While somebody holds the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) role, lifting the cover is theirs to do, and their double-click uncovers it for everyone rather than just for them.

## It hides, it does not protect

A Reveal zone hides content from a reader's eye, not from a determined reader: everything underneath is still in the document, its exports and its API, so don't put anything genuinely private under one.
