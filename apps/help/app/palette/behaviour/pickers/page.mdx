A **Picker** chooses at random when you press it. It lives in the **Run the room** group of the [Behaviour](/help/palette/behaviour/) category.

## What it picks from

Either one of the people currently in the room, or one of the options you write. Both are set in **Tools › Picker**: choose **People here** or **A list**, and a list gets an **Options** box, one entry per line.

## Rolling it

It spins the candidates past and slows to a stop on the answer. People appear with their own avatar as they go by, and everyone sees the same result.
