The **Collaborate** elements are every element whose content arrives at _runtime_. Everywhere else in the palette you are placing a picture; here you are placing something that does a job when somebody presses it, or collects an answer from everybody in the room.

This page is the map. Each element has its own short article, so you can link somebody straight to the one they need.

## Where to find them

Diagram mode keeps the **Comment panel** and the **Action card** in its **Write** category. [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/) has no palette. From any mode, the [element search](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) still finds and places every one.

## What the categories contain

In each category, every element is a row with its name and a one-line description of what it does, because a glyph cannot say what a Picker picks or where a Portal goes.

The groups, room-first:

- **Ask**: the [temperature check](/help/palette/collaborate/temperature-checks/) and the **Dot Vote** [session button](/help/palette/behaviour/session-buttons/).
- **Tools**: the **Timer** and **Stopwatch** [session buttons](/help/palette/behaviour/session-buttons/) and the [Picker](/help/palette/behaviour/pickers/).
- **Record**: [comment panels](/help/palette/collaborate/comment-panels/), [action panels](/help/palette/collaborate/action-panels/) and the [decision record](/help/palette/collaborate/decision-records/).
- **Navigate**: the [Portal](/help/palette/tools/portals/) and the [Link card](/help/canvas/links/link-cards/).

They are grouped by what the element does with the room rather than by what it looks like. That is why the Portal sits beside the Link card (each takes somebody somewhere: another tab, or the page the card points at), and why the dot vote sits with the temperature check rather than in a group of its own: putting a question to everybody is one job however it is asked.

## Changing one

Most elements here carry a **…** in their top corner, which opens that element's own menu beside it. It is the same menu a right-click gives you, in the place you are already looking. The Comment and Action panels have no **…**: their faces already say what they do, so right-click them for their settings instead.

A Session button draws a richer **…** of its own, with the settings you change mid-session right in it: a Timer's length or a dot vote's dots. It ends with **All Settings…**, which takes you to the full menu.

## Working with them

Each behaves like any other element once placed: select, restyle, and move it freely. A click presses it and a drag moves it, so you can arrange a row of them without setting one off. They keep working in [Isometric mode](/help/selection-modes/isometric-mode/), which makes the rest of the canvas read-only.

On a Participant or Viewer link, the elements that run the session (Session buttons, Pickers and the presses that run a round) are inactive. A Participant can still answer the cards and cast dots; a Viewer only watches.

<Tip>
  Put a row of these along the top of a canvas and it carries its own running order: a Timer for the
  writing block, a Dot Vote for choosing, and a Picker for who goes next.
</Tip>
