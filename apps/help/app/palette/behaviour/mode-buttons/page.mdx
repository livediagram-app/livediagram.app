A Selection Mode button is an element that hands out a [selection mode](/help/selection-modes/). Put one on the canvas and anybody can press it, without ever having opened the mode picker.

## Adding one

The **Selection Mode** row of the [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/) category opens into eight buttons, one per mode: **Avatar**, **Select**, **Hand**, **Laser**, **Spotlight**, **Eraser**, **Format** and **Isometric**.

Pick the mode you want and it drops ready to press, reading "Switch to Avatar" and switching whoever presses it into that mode. You can still repoint an existing button from **Button**, or type your own label over the derived one.

## What pressing it does

Pressing it changes the mode for **the person who pressed it** and nobody else. Everyone else carries on in whatever tool they were already using.

Pressing it again while you are in that mode hands you back your previous one, so a button is never a one-way door: a button for the mode you are already in reads "Leave Avatar" rather than offering it again.

A click presses the button; a drag still moves it, so you can arrange a bar of them without setting one off.

## Why a bar of them works

A Selection Mode button keeps working while you are in a mode that makes the rest of the canvas read-only, so a row of them makes a usable little control bar. That is what makes it useful on a walkthrough: the viewer has the controls they need in front of them, on the canvas, rather than in a panel they were never told about.

<Tip>
  Pair an **Eraser** button with a **Select** button so a reviewer can tidy up what they see and put
  the eraser down again without leaving the canvas.
</Tip>
