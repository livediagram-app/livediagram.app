A **Reaction pad** throws a burst over itself when somebody presses it, or walks an [Avatar-mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) character onto it, which is the same act.

## Adding one

The **React** group of the [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/) category opens into five pads, one per reaction, so you pick the one you want and it drops ready, already captioned with what pressing it means: "Celebrate", "Nice one", "Show some love", "Thanks" or "It shipped". Type over the caption to say something else.

To switch an existing pad to another reaction, right-click it and pick one from **Reaction**.

## The five reactions

- **Confetti** for a result worth celebrating.
- **Sparkles** for a good idea nicely done.
- **Hearts** for warmth towards a person rather than a result.
- **Applause** to thank somebody for the talk or the demo.
- **Fireworks** for when it shipped.

## Nothing is saved

Everyone in the room sees the burst, and nothing is saved: a reaction is a moment, not a mark on the canvas, so there is nothing to undo and nothing for a latecomer to find.

Pressing a pad again while it is still bursting starts the burst over rather than piling another on top. Standing on a pad in Avatar mode throws one burst; step off and back on for another.

On a view-only link the pad shows but does not fire. With reduced motion on, the pad stops bobbing, but the burst still plays so the room sees the moment.

<Tip>Drop one at the end of a retro column or under a demo and let the room use it.</Tip>
