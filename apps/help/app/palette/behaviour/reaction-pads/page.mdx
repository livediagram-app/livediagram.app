A **Reaction pad** throws a burst over itself when somebody presses it, or walks an [Avatar-mode](/help/selection-modes/avatar-mode/) character onto it, which is the same act.

## Adding one

The **Reactions** row of the [Behaviour](/help/palette/behaviour/) category opens into five pads, one per reaction, so you pick the one you want and it drops ready. You can still change an existing pad from **Tools › Reaction**.

<Figure caption="The five pads, and the burst one of them has just thrown over the board.">
  <ReactionPads />
</Figure>

## The five reactions

- **Confetti** for a result worth celebrating.
- **Sparkles** for a good idea nicely done.
- **Hearts** for warmth towards a person rather than a result.
- **Applause** to thank somebody for the talk or the demo.
- **Fireworks** for when it shipped.

## Nothing is saved

Everyone in the room sees the burst, and nothing is saved: a reaction is a moment, not a mark on the board, so there is nothing to undo and nothing for a latecomer to find.

<Tip>Drop one at the end of a retro column or under a demo and let the room use it.</Tip>
