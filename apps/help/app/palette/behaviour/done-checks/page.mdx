A **Done check** asks the room to say when they have finished. It lives in the **Run the room** group of the [Behaviour](/help/palette/behaviour/) category.

## Marking yourself done

Everyone presses **I'm done** on the card, and it shows two lists: who has, and who it is still waiting on. When the last person marks themselves done the card flashes, so you do not have to sit watching it.

<Figure caption="The card keeps two lists: who has finished, and who it is still waiting on.">
  <DoneCheckCard />
</Figure>

Press **I'm not done** to take your mark off again.

## The waiting list is who is here now

The waiting list is built from whoever is actually **in the room** at that moment, not from everyone who has ever opened the diagram. That is deliberate: it means somebody who closed their tab stops holding the card open, and the check can actually finish. If they come back their mark is still there.

## Running the next round

The card's **…** menu has **Clear my mark** and **Reset everyone**, which clears the round so you can run the next one.

<Tip>
  Good over a silent-writing block, a review column, or any "tell me when you're ready" moment.
