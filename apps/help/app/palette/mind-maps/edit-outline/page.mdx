Dragging nodes around one at a time is slow when you want to rethink a whole map. **Edit Outline** opens the map as a list instead, one row per node, so you can reshuffle it the way you would reorganise notes in a document.

## Open it

<Step n={1}>Find the map's root, the node the map starts from.</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Press **Edit Outline** on the badge at its top-right corner, or open the root's menu and find it
  under **Mind Map**, just below **Tidy Map**.
</Step>

Beside it, the **Tidy Map** badge lays the map out neatly again and tells you whether anything moved.

The first row is the root, a size larger. Every other row has a bullet and sits one step in from its parent, and each level has its own colour, so the nesting reads at a glance.

## Edit the rows

You never type spaces to nest a row: its level is part of the row, so nothing can end up misaligned.

- **Enter** starts a new node after the one you are on, at the same level. On an empty row, it steps the row out a level instead.
- **Tab** and **Shift+Tab** move a row a level in or out, with its children.
- **Backspace** at the start of an empty row removes it; on a row with text, it joins the row onto the one above.
- **Shift+Enter** starts a second line inside the same node.
- **Alt+↑** and **Alt+↓** move a row, children and all, past its neighbour.

The toolbar above the rows does the same things with a tap: **Outdent**, **Indent**, **Move Up**, **Move Down** and **Line Break**. On a phone, where there is no Tab key, that is how you nest rows and break a line.

## Bold, italic and underline

Select some text in a row and press **Bold**, **Italic** or **Underline** on the toolbar, or use **⌘/Ctrl+B**, **I** and **U**. The rows show a node's formatting as it looks on the canvas. Other styling (colour, size, links) is not shown here and is left exactly as it was.

## Paste a list in

Paste several lines into a row and they arrive as rows, nested from the row you pasted into. Pasting is forgiving, so a list from a document or another app works: bullets, numbered items, indented lines and `#` headings all become levels, `**bold**`, `_italic_` and `<u>underline</u>` become formatting, and links or task boxes are reduced to their text.

## What Save does

**Save** (or **⌘/Ctrl+Enter**) applies the whole outline to the map at once. A short line beside the buttons tells you what it will do, such as "3 added, 1 renamed, 2 removed".

- **Nodes you kept stay as they were.** Each line is matched to its existing node, so colours, sizes, icons, comments and links stay put, even when you move a line under a different parent.
- **New lines become new nodes**, styled like the other nodes at their level and joined by matching connectors.
- **Removed lines ask first.** If any nodes would be removed, you are shown which ones and can choose **Remove** or **Keep Editing**.
- **The map is laid out again** when its structure changed (nodes added, removed or moved), in the outline's order, with the root staying where it was, just as **Tidy Map** does. Changing only words or formatting leaves every node where it is.

**Cancel** (or **Escape**) closes the dialog without changing anything.

<Tip>Saving is a single step, so one **Undo** puts the whole map back exactly as it was.</Tip>
