Arrows are how a diagram turns a pile of shapes into a flow, a hierarchy, or a relationship. Add one with the **Arrow** tool in the palette's **Draw** category (or press `A`), then connect, style, and reshape it however the diagram needs. livediagram gives arrows real flexibility: every line style, draggable curve and elbow handles, and endpoints that snap onto shapes or even onto other arrows.

## Drawing and connecting

An arrow links two points on the canvas. An arrow's endpoint can sit freely, pin to one of a shape's connection points (so it tracks the shape as it moves), or attach along another arrow. On a selected arrow, a pinned end's handle is filled and a free end's is hollow.

There are two ways to draw one:

- **Quick-connect**: select a shape, click one of the **+** buttons on its sides, and press-drag the ring's **Arrow** option onto the target shape. On a touch screen, tap **Arrow** and then tap the target.
- **From the palette**: add an **Arrow** (or press `A`) and drag its ends onto the shapes you want to join. The palette's **Arrow** has a pointer at its end; its **Line** has none.

Drag an end near a shape and the shape's connection points light up; let go near one and the end pins there. Hold **Cmd** or **Ctrl** while dragging an end to place it freely, with no snapping.

Two Shift tricks make fanning out fast:

- **Shift-chain**: hold **Shift** while a quick-connect arrow's endpoint lands and a fresh arrow immediately starts from the same spot on the source, so a hub can point at several targets in one flow. A plain click places the last one.
- **Shift-fork**: hold **Shift** and drag a selected arrow's **head** to peel a branch off its line, one trunk that splits toward a second target instead of a parallel near-duplicate arrow. The branch takes the trunk's colour, weight and pattern.

When a new arrow's straight line would cut through another shape, it lands with a gentle bow around it instead; see [Arrows That Curve Around Things](/help/palette/arrows/avoiding-elements/). As you move shapes later, [Auto-Attach Arrows](/help/palette/auto-attach-arrows/) keeps an arrow from running through the shapes it connects.

## Styling the line

From the arrow's right-click menu you can set its line style, thickness, dash pattern, colour and arrowheads, and choose whether it **passes behind boxes** it crosses (on by default). Double-click an arrow to give it a label. When several arrows connect to the same point on a box, they spread out a little so their ends don't pile up; turn on **Start at exact anchor** for an arrow to keep its start exactly where you connected it. The controls are independent, so you can mix any line with any head. See [Arrow styles](/help/palette/arrows/arrow-styles/) for the full set.

## Shaping the path

Drag an arrow's line to bend it where you grabbed it, or use the handle on a curved or angled arrow to bow a curve or position an elbow exactly, without breaking the arrow's connections. An arrow attached to nothing at either end shows a selection frame instead: drag its edge to move the whole arrow, or a handle to scale it.
