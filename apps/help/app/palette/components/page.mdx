The **Components** category holds ready-made web components. Instead of building a banner or a KPI row shape by shape, you drop one element that already knows how to lay itself out, then fill in your own words.

## Finding them

Components are part of the [Illustrate mode](/help/canvas/illustrate/) palette: open the category dropdown, pick **Components** under **Structure**, and open the **Web Elements** row to see all six. Tap one to add it, or drag it to where you want it.

<Figure caption="The Components category in Illustrate mode, with the Web Elements group opened in place.">
  <ComponentsCategory />
</Figure>

The Diagram mode palette doesn't offer the category, but every component still works there: search for it by name in the editor's [Search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) and add it from **Add to canvas**.

## What the category contains

- **Banner** is a themed title block: an accent bar with a title and a subtitle.
- **Callout** is a soft note box with an icon badge, a heading, and a body.
- **Stat row** is a row of KPI cards, each a big number over a caption.
- **Process** is a row of numbered circles joined by arrows, with a caption under each.
- **Hero** is a large image with a caption card over it.
- **Header** is a website-style bar with a logo, a brand name, and navigation links.

Hero builds on an image, so it only appears when image upload is available.

The [Entity](/help/palette/entities/) also belongs to this category, but Illustrate mode leaves it out of its palette; in Diagram mode it is in **Popular**.

## Editing the text

Every piece of text on a component is editable where it sits:

<Step n={1}>Click the component once to select it.</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Click the line you want to change, such as a stat's number, a step's caption or a nav link, and
  type.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>Press **Enter** to keep the change, or **Escape** to put the old text back.</Step>

Before the component is selected its text ignores clicks, so the first click always selects (and lets you drag) the whole component.

The main text (a banner's title, a callout's body, a header's brand name) is the element's ordinary label, so double-clicking edits it like any shape's label, with the usual bold, size and alignment controls.

## Adding and removing rows

A Stat row, a Process and a Header carry rows: stats, steps and links. To add one, select the component and press the **+** at the end of the row (**Add stat**, **Add step** or **Add link**). To remove, reorder or retype rows in one place, right-click the component and open **Tools**, then the **Stats**, **Steps** or **Links** section.

Each has limits, so the layout stays readable: a stat row holds 1 to 6 stats, a process 2 to 8 steps, and a header up to 6 links.

## Resizing

Each component is one element that lays itself out, so resizing changes the room it has rather than zooming it:

- Banner and callout text re-wraps to the new width.
- Stat cards share the width, and their numbers grow as you make the row taller.
- Process steps spread across the width, and the circles shrink rather than overlap when you add more.
- A header keeps its logo and brand on the left and its links on the right. Links that no longer fit a narrowed header are hidden from the end until you widen it again.

## Colours and images

Components follow the tab theme when you drop them. A banner and a header paint their bar in the theme's accent and keep white text through a theme change; pick a **Background** colour in the right-click menu's **Colours** section to use your own. A callout, a stat row and a process are ordinary cards that use the accent for the badge, the numbers and the step circles.

Drop an icon on a header to make it the logo, or on a callout to replace its badge. To set a hero's picture, double-click the image; its caption card can be turned off (or added to any image) with **Caption Card** in the image's right-click menu, under **Image**.
