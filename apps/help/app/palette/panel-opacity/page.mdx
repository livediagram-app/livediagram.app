Panel Opacity lets you see the canvas through the editor’s panels. It is a slider in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), under **Panels**.

<Figure caption="Settings, Panels: the Panel Opacity slider, here at 70%.">
  <SettingsPanelsPane highlight="panel-opacity" />
</Figure>

## What it does

Drag the slider down and every panel becomes translucent, so the canvas behind them stays visible while you work near an edge. The percentage next to the slider shows the current setting, from 100% (fully solid, the default) down to 30%, in steps of 5%. The change shows live as you drag.

<Figure caption="At a lower opacity the panels turn translucent, so the canvas behind them stays visible.">
  <PanelOpacity />
</Figure>

So a translucent panel never gets in your way, each panel snaps back to fully opaque the moment you hover over it or focus something inside it, then fades back once you move away. You always get a clear, readable panel exactly when you are using it.

## Where it applies

Panel Opacity affects every panel: the corner panels (the AI assistant, the Map and the Poll and Vote panels), the panels that open as popovers from a button (the Explorer, Layers and Collaborate), the [Quick Style panel](/help/canvas/quick-style-panel/) and the [palette strip](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/). Buttons are not panels, so the menu button and the buttons in the bottom-right corner stay fully opaque, and so do menus and dialogs.

## Resetting it

Drag the slider back to 100% to make the panels solid again. The setting is saved with your other [settings](/help/user-interface/settings/). When you are signed in it follows you to your other devices; as a guest it stays with this browser.
