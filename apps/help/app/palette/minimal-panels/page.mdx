Minimal Panels is a toggle, and it is off by default. You will find it in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), under **Appearance**. It is only shown when the layout supports it.

<Figure caption="The Appearance settings, with Minimal panels highlighted and switched off.">
  <PaletteSettings highlight="minimal" />
</Figure>

## What it does

When the setting is on, the floating panels (Explorer, Palette, Collaborate, AI, and Layers) are swapped for a compact button bar, a dock. Each panel then opens as a popover from its button instead of floating on the canvas. Under the switch the description reads: "Swap floating panels for a compact button bar."

<Figure caption="Floating panels on the left versus the compact dock on the right, where each panel opens as a popover.">
  <MinimalPanels />
</Figure>

## When to use it

Minimal Panels is good for small screens, where floating panels can crowd the canvas, and for anyone who simply prefers a tidier workspace. Tap a button in the dock to open that panel as a popover, then dismiss it to give the canvas back its full width.
