The **Concepts** category holds four graphics for the shapes ideas come in. Each one is a single element that lays out its own rows, so adding a tier or resizing it redraws the whole thing, with nothing to re-cut or realign.

- **Layer Stack**: bands stacked top to bottom, each with an optional side note. A tech stack, the OSI model, architecture tiers.
- **Pyramid**: tiers cut from one triangle. A hierarchy of needs, a strategy stack.
- **Funnel**: bands narrowing downward, each with an optional value. A conversion funnel.
- **Cycle**: steps round a ring joined by arrows, with a label in the middle. Plan, Do, Check, Act; a flywheel; a release loop.

Concepts is in the palette in Diagram, Illustrate and Facilitate modes.

## Editing the rows

Select the graphic, then click a row's words to type in place. Enter keeps the change and Escape puts it back. A layer's side note and a funnel band's value edit the same way.

To add, remove or reorder rows, right-click and open the **Rows** section, or use **Add Layer**, **Add Tier** or **Add Step** on the ring round the selected graphic. A stack takes 2 to 8 layers, a pyramid 2 to 6 tiers and a cycle 3 to 8 steps.

The first row is the top layer, the top tier (a pyramid's apex, a funnel's widest band) or the cycle's first step at twelve o'clock, with the rest following clockwise. Double-click a cycle to edit the label in its middle.

## Pyramid, inverted or funnel

A Pyramid, an Inverted pyramid and a Funnel are one element in three shapes. Right-click and pick one under **Shape** in its section to switch, keeping its rows.

## Colours

The rows are a ramp from the element's colour: the first row in full, each later one lighter. Each row's words take whichever ink reads best on it. Pick a new colour in **Colours** and the whole ramp follows, as does a theme switch. A cycle draws its first step solid, to show where the loop starts.

## Animation

Concept graphics animate their rows rather than the whole box. Right-click, open the graphic's section and pick under **Animation**:

- **Build**: the rows appear one after another in reading order (a stack top down, a pyramid from its base up, a cycle clockwise). It plays once unless you turn on **Repeat**.
- **Pulse**: each row lights in turn, then the loop starts again. On a cycle it reads as "we are here" travelling round.
- **Flow** (cycles only): dashes travel round the arrows in the loop's direction.

**Speed** sets the pace. Exports, and anyone who has turned on reduced motion, see every row at rest.
