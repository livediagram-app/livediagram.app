A sticky note is a small coloured card for a short piece of text: an idea, a question, a label, a brainstorm item. It is the digital version of the paper sticky, and it behaves like any other canvas element once placed.

</Figure>

## Adding one

<Step n={1}>
  Open the floating palette and switch to the **Write** category, or press `N` to skip the palette.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Pick **Note**, then tap the canvas to drop a 200 &times; 200 note, or drag to draw it at the size
  you want.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>Double-click the note to type.</Step>

<Figure caption="The Write category with Note armed: the sticky note's row, with its N key on the right.">
  <PaletteRows category="Write" active="Note" />
</Figure>

The note is also in **Popular** in Diagram mode, and in Plan mode's **Content** category.

## Writing on a note

A note holds several lines of text and wraps it as you type. While you are editing, **Enter** starts a new line, **Escape** cancels the edit, and clicking away commits it. Text that outgrows the note is clipped rather than spilling out, so resize the note to show the rest.

## Colours

A sticky note keeps its own colour whatever the [theme](/help/canvas/themes/): it lands in the classic amber of a paper note, and changing the tab's theme never recolours it, because a note's colour is usually your own shorthand (ideas in yellow, risks in pink) rather than part of the diagram's palette.

To recolour one, right-click it and open **Style**. Its **Presets** are a pad of twelve note colours, each with a readable ink for its paper: **Classic**, **Lemon**, **Peach**, **Rose**, **Lilac**, **Sky**, **Mint**, **Teal**, **Slate**, **Paper**, **Charcoal** and **Ink**. **Colours** sets the paper and text by hand if none of them fits.

## Working with them

Stickies move, resize, lock and layer like everything else. Scatter a wall of them for a brainstorm, select several at once to restyle or move them together, or pull them into a [frame](/help/palette/build/frames/) so the cluster travels as one.

<Tip>
  Sticky notes pair naturally with [dot voting](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/) in a live
  session: capture ideas on stickies, then vote on them together.
</Tip>
