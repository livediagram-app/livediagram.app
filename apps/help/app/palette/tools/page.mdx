There is no longer a **Tools** tab. Every group it held is now a palette **category** of its own, one click away in the category picker rather than two, so this page is a map of where each tool went.

The categories are grouped into four bands: **Common** (Shapes, Write, Draw), **Structure** (Build, Components, Devices), **Decorate** (Icons, Stickers, Technology, Media), and **Dynamic** (Data, Behaviour, Collaborate), with your **Favourites** across the top.

## Where each tool lives

**Build** holds the elements you lay a diagram out with, the ones that hold other work: **Mind Node** (Tab adds a child, Enter a sibling), **Lane** (a titled band that carries its steps), **Frame** (a labelled box that groups a section), **Timeline** (a track for sequencing events), and **Table** (an editable grid of cells).

**Write** holds the wordy elements: **Text** drops a free-standing label, **Page** is a paper-sized sheet you write into, **Sticky Note** is a coloured note card, and **Annotation** is a small note marker for callouts.

**Draw** holds the gesture tools: **Freehand** sketches a stroke and keeps it exactly as drawn, the **Shape Pen** turns a rough sketch into a real shape, **Highlighter** lays a wide translucent marker stroke, **Polygon** places straight-edged outlines point by point, and **Arrow** drops a plain connector you position by hand. See [Drawing and Sketch](/help/palette/tools/drawing/). (**Frame** moved to Build.)

**Components** holds the ready-made pieces: **Code Block**, **Checklist**, **Entity**, **Link Card**, and the themed composites (Banner, Callout, Stat row, Process, Hero, Header). (**Timeline** moved to Build.)

**Data** holds the charts and meters. See [Data and Chart Elements](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/). (**Table** moved to Build.)

**Behaviour** holds the elements that _do_ something when somebody interacts with them: Selection Mode buttons, Portals, Session buttons, Reveal zones, and Pickers. See [Behaviour Elements](/help/palette/behaviour/).

**Media** covers pictures and figures: **Image** places an uploaded picture and **Avatar** places a circular one.

Each behaves like any other element once placed: select, restyle, group, and move it freely.

## Guides for these tools

Several tools have their own walkthroughs, listed below and linked here:

- [Drawing and Sketch](/help/palette/tools/drawing/) covers Freehand, the Shape Pen, the Highlighter, and the Polygon tool.
- [Images](/help/palette/tools/images/) covers uploading and placing pictures, avatars, and user figures.
- [Tables](/help/palette/tools/tables/) covers the editable grid: adding one, filling cells, and rows/columns.
- [Sticky Notes](/help/palette/tools/sticky-notes/) covers the coloured note card for short annotations.
- [Code Blocks](/help/palette/tools/code-blocks/) covers the dark monospace card for syntax-highlighted snippets.
- [Checklists](/help/palette/tools/checklists/) covers the card of checkable to-do rows you tick on the canvas.
- [Portals](/help/palette/tools/portals/) covers linking two portals and travelling between them, including across tabs.
- [Data and Chart Elements](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/) covers the **Data** category: Pie Chart, Bar Chart, Line Chart, Progress Bar, Progress Ring (a donut meter), and Rating (stars).
- [Behaviour Elements](/help/palette/behaviour/) covers the **Behaviour** category: Selection Mode buttons, Portals, Session buttons, Reveal zones, and Pickers.

Arrows are deep enough to deserve their own guide: see [Arrows](/help/palette/arrows/).
