The palette's **Tools** tab gathers the elements that are not plain shapes. It opens on a grid of **categories** — one tile each for **Write &amp; Draw**, **Structure**, **Blocks**, **People &amp; Media**, **Behaviour**, and **Data**. Click a category to see its tools; a **breadcrumb** at the top ("‹ Tools › Write &amp; Draw") tells you where you are and takes you back in one click. Hovering a category tile describes what is inside it.

Not sure which category holds the tool you're after? A **search box** sits above the navigation, just like the one on the Icons tab: type a name ("table", "chart", "pencil") and the categories give way to a single grid of every matching tool. Clear the search to return to where you were.

## The categories

**Write &amp; Draw** holds everything you put down yourself. The wordy elements: **Text** drops a free-standing label, **Sticky Note** is a coloured note card, and **Annotation** is a small note marker for callouts. And the gesture tools: **Pencil** lets you sketch freehand, with an optional shape-recognition mode; **Highlighter** lays a wide translucent marker stroke for calling attention to things; **Polygon** places straight-edged outlines point by point; and **Arrow** drops a plain connector you can position by hand.

**Structure** is for organising: **Table** is an editable grid of cells, **Frame** is a labelled container you group work inside, and **Timeline Rail** is a horizontal track for sequencing events.

**Blocks** holds the rich content cards: **Code Block** is a dark monospace card for syntax-highlighted snippets, **Checklist** is a card of tickable to-do rows, and **Link Card** is a clickable preview of a URL.

**Behaviour** holds the elements that *do* something when somebody interacts with them. A **Selection Mode** button reads "Switch to Avatar" and switches whoever presses it into that mode; right-click it and open **Tools › Button** to point it at any other mode — Select, Hand, Laser, Spotlight, Avatar, Eraser, Format, or Isometric — or type your own label over the derived one. Pressing it changes the mode for **the person who pressed it** and nobody else, pressing it again while you are in that mode hands you back your previous one, and it keeps working while you are in a mode that makes the rest of the canvas read-only, so a row of them makes a usable little control bar. A **Portal** is a ring of energy you link to another portal: click it — or walk an Avatar-mode character into it — and you come out the far side, even on another tab. See [Portals](/help/palette/tools/portals/).

A **Session** button starts a session tool for everyone in the room: a countdown **timer**, a **dot vote**, or a **poll** you write in advance. Right-click it and open **Tools › Session** to pick which, and its one setting — the number of minutes, the dots per person, or the question and its answers. It is how a board carries its own running order: put "5 minutes" over the writing column and "Vote — 3 dots each" over the results, and anyone can run the session. Starting a tool needs edit access, so on a view-only link the button shows as unavailable, though you can still take part once somebody starts it.

A **Reveal** zone is a cover you put over part of the canvas — answers, estimates, a retro column nobody should read early. Clicking it uncovers it **on your screen only**, with a small Hide pill to put it back; **Tools › Reveal › Reveal for all** takes it off for everyone at once. It hides content from a reader's eye, not from a determined reader: everything underneath is still in the diagram, so don't put anything genuinely private under one.

A **Picker** chooses at random when you press it — one of the people currently in the room, or one of the options you write in **Tools › Picker**. It spins for a moment and lands on the answer, and everyone sees the same result: handy for "who demos next" without anyone having to volunteer.

**People &amp; Media** covers pictures and figures: **Image** places an uploaded picture, **Avatar** places a circular image, and **User** places an actor figure, both handy for people in a flow.

Each behaves like any other element once placed: select, restyle, group, and move it freely.

## Guides in this tab

Several tools have their own walkthroughs, listed below and linked here:

- [Drawing and Sketch](/help/palette/tools/drawing/) covers the Pencil, Highlighter, and Polygon tools, plus shape recognition.
- [Images](/help/palette/tools/images/) covers uploading and placing pictures, avatars, and user figures.
- [Tables](/help/palette/tools/tables/) covers the editable grid: adding one, filling cells, and rows/columns.
- [Sticky Notes](/help/palette/tools/sticky-notes/) covers the coloured note card for short annotations.
- [Code Blocks](/help/palette/tools/code-blocks/) covers the dark monospace card for syntax-highlighted snippets.
- [Checklists](/help/palette/tools/checklists/) covers the card of checkable to-do rows you tick on the canvas.
- [Portals](/help/palette/tools/portals/) covers linking two portals and travelling between them, including across tabs.
- [Data and Chart Elements](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/) covers the Data sub-section: Pie Chart, Bar Chart, Line Chart, Progress Bar, Progress Ring (a donut meter), and Rating (stars).

The **Arrow** tool lives here too, but arrows are deep enough to deserve their own guide: see [Arrows](/help/palette/arrows/).
