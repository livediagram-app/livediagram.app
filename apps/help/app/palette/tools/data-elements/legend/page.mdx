A legend is a small card of rows, each one a coloured dot and a label. It's the piece that turns a colour-coded diagram into one somebody else can read: which colour is which team, which status, which environment.

<Figure caption="A legend card with a coloured dot and a label on each row.">

</Figure>

## Adding one

<Step n={1}>
  In [Illustrate](/help/canvas/illustrate/), open the **Data** category on the palette strip. In
  Diagram mode, type "legend" into the [search
  panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) instead.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick **Legend**, then tap the canvas to drop it, or drag to draw it at a size. It starts with
  three rows, "First", "Second" and "Third".
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Move, resize, recolour, or lock it like any other element. Its background and text colours come
  from **Colours** under **Style**, the same as every other card.
</Step>

## Editing the colours and labels

Right-click the legend, open **Tools**, then **Legend**. Each row gives you:

- A round **colour chip**: click it to pick that row's colour.
- A **label** field: type what the colour means.
- A **×** to remove the row.

**Add Row** puts another row on the end (up to 40). A new row takes the next colour from the built-in palette automatically, so you only need to set a colour when you want a specific one. Nothing on the legend itself is clickable: all its editing happens here.

Under the rows, **Text Size** (**Small**, **Medium**, or **Large**) sets how big the labels are. It's the same scale a chart's own key uses, so a legend beside a chart can match it.

## Matching a chart

A row you never colour takes the next colour from the built-in **Vivid** palette, in order. A chart uses your theme's colours by default, so to line a legend up with a chart, set the chart's palette to **Vivid** or colour the legend rows by hand. A legend's **Presets** under **Style** restyle its card, not its dots.

<Tip>
  A legend is a key, not a chart: it holds colours and words, and no values. If you want the numbers
  as well, hovering a slice or bar on the chart itself shows its value.
</Tip>
