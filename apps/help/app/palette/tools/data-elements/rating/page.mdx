A rating element is a row of five stars showing a score from one to five. It's a compact, instantly readable way to mark how something measures up, so it sits well next to options you're comparing or criteria you're scoring.

## Adding one

<Step n={1}>
  In [Illustrate](/help/canvas/illustrate/), open the floating palette and switch to the **Data**
  category. In Diagram mode, type "rating" into the [search
  panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) instead.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick **Rating**, then tap the canvas to drop it, or drag to draw it at a size. It lands as five
  stars with three filled, in an amber accent.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Move, resize, recolour, or lock it like any other element. Its stroke (accent) colour tints the
  filled stars.
</Step>

## Setting the score and animation

Right-click the rating, open **Tools**, then **Rating**. There you'll find:

- A **Stars** picker: click a star to set the score from one to five.
- An **Animation** row with five options: **None**, **Pop**, **Twinkle**, **Pulse**, and **Rock**.

The animations split into two kinds. **Pop** and **Rock** play once as an entrance, so they're a nice touch when the rating first appears. **Twinkle** and **Pulse** loop by default, drawing a little ongoing attention. Once you pick one, a speed row (**Slowest**, **Slow**, **Normal**, **Fast**) and a **Repeat** toggle appear so you can adjust the timing or override whether it loops.

The wrapper draws no box border, so the stars sit cleanly on the canvas without a frame around them.

<Tip>
  Animations respect your system's reduce-motion setting and freeze when you export, so an exported
  image always shows the stars at rest with the correct score.
</Tip>
