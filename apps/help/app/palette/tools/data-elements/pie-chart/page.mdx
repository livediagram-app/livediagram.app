A pie chart shows how a total divides into parts. Each slice is sized by its value, coloured from your active theme, and named in a key, so a reader sees the breakdown at a glance. You build the chart from a short list of label and value rows, with no spreadsheet required.

## Adding one

<Step n={1}>
  In [Illustrate](/help/canvas/illustrate/), open the **Data** category on the palette strip
  (**Pie** is also in Illustrate's **Popular**). In Diagram mode, type "pie" into the [search
  panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) instead.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Pick **Pie**, then tap the canvas to drop it, or drag to draw it at a size. It lands with sample
  slices so it looks finished straight away.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>Move, resize, or lock it like any other element.</Step>

## Editing the data

Right-click the chart, open **Tools**, then **Data**. It edits inline, one row per slice, and each row has:

- A recolourable **swatch** to set that slice's colour.
- A **label** for the slice name.
- A **value** that sizes the slice.

Remove a slice with its **×**, or add one with **+ Add Slice**; the chart redraws live as you type. New slices default to variants of the active theme, so the chart reads as part of your diagram out of the box, but you can override any slice's colour with its swatch.

## Legend, palette and animation

- **Chart**, also in **Tools**, has a **Legend** placement picker: **Off**, **Top**, **Left**, **Right**, or **Below** (the default). Picking a side shows the key there, and **Off** hides it. While the key is on, **Text Size** (**Small**, **Medium**, or **Large**) sets how big its labels are. The key names each slice; hovering a slice always shows a readout with its label and value, whether or not the key is on.
- **Style** holds the chart's **Presets**: eight palettes (**Vivid**, **Ocean**, **Forest**, **Sunset**, **Berry**, **Earth**, **Grey** and **Contrast**) that recolour every slice you haven't coloured by hand, including slices you add later. Resetting the presets puts the chart back on the theme's colours. A chart has no Colours or Border controls: its colour lives in its slices.
- **Animation** carries chart-specific animations: **Grow**, **Pop**, **Spin** and **Pulse**. Grow and Pop play once as an entrance, while Spin and Pulse loop. Once you pick one, a speed row (**Slowest**, **Slow**, **Normal**, **Fast**) and a **Repeat** toggle appear.

<Tip>
  Because slice colours default to your theme, changing the tab's theme restyles the chart to match
  without any per-slice work.
</Tip>
