Most diagrams are shapes and arrows, but sometimes you want to show a value: how far along a project is, how good something scored, how a total splits up, or where milestones fall on a line. livediagram has a small family of data elements for exactly that. They behave like any other element once they land, so you can move, resize, recolour, lock, and theme them as usual.

## Where to find them

The charts and meters live in the palette's **Data** category, which [Illustrate](/help/canvas/illustrate/) offers (under **Dynamic** in the category picker). Diagram mode has no Data category: open the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) (`⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`), type the element's name, such as "pie" or "rating", and pick it under **Add to canvas**. The **Timeline** rail is the exception: it sits in the **Build** category, in both modes.

## What you can add

- **Pie chart** splits a total into slices you edit as label and value rows, drawn in your theme's colours. See [Pie Chart](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/pie-chart/).
- **Bar and line charts**: a bar chart compares one value per category, and a line chart plots one or more series across categories, from an editable grid or a CSV import. See [Bar and Line Charts](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/bar-and-line-charts/).
- **Legend** is a key of coloured rows that says what each colour means. See [Legend](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/legend/).
- **Progress** and **Donut** show a single 0 to 100 percentage as a filling bar or a ring, with optional fill, pulse, or stripe animations. See [Progress Bars and Rings](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/progress-elements/).
- **Rating** shows a one to five score as a row of stars, in an amber accent, with star-specific animations. See [Rating](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/rating/).
- **Timeline** is a horizontal rail of evenly spaced, labelled points, handy for roadmaps and process steps. See [Timeline Rail](/help/palette/tools/data-elements/timeline-rail/).

## How they work

Pick a tile, then tap the canvas to drop the element with sensible sample values, or drag to draw it at a size, so you always start from something that looks finished. From there you tune it in the element's context menu: right-click (or long-press on a touch screen), open **Tools**, and you'll find a section for that element's data, whether that's a percentage slider, a star picker, editable value rows, or a points stepper.

Because they are ordinary canvas elements underneath, everything you already know carries over. Charts and meters take their colours from the active theme (a rating keeps its amber stars), so they read as part of the diagram out of the box, and animations respect your system's reduce-motion setting and freeze when you export, so a still image looks right.
