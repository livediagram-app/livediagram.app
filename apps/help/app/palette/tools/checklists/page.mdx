A checklist is a card of checkable to-do rows, a visual home for the task lists that end up on agile boards, retros, and project plans. It is a real canvas element, so you can place, resize, recolour, lock and move it like anything else, and its colours follow the tab's [theme](/help/canvas/themes/) like any shape.

## Adding one

<Step n={1}>
  In [Illustrate](/help/canvas/illustrate/), open the **Components** category on the palette strip.
  In Diagram mode there is no Components category: open the [search
  panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/) (`⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`), type
  "checklist" and pick it under **Add to canvas**.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Pick **Checklist**, then tap the canvas to drop one, or drag to draw it at a size. It starts with
  three unticked rows, "First task", "Second task" and "Third task", ready to overwrite.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>**Click a row's checkbox** to tick it off.</Step>

## Ticking rows

Clicking a checkbox on the canvas toggles that row, no edit mode needed. Done rows tick the box, **strike through** the text, and mute it, and a **done count** (for example "2/5") appears in the card's bottom-right corner once at least one row is done.

Ticks **sync live**, so in a shared session everyone sees a box tick the moment it is clicked, and two people ticking different rows at once both land. A tick is the room's answer rather than an edit, so it is **not undoable**: click the box again to untick it. Only people who can edit the document can tick; with view-only access, or on a locked checklist, tab or layer, the boxes don't respond.

## Editing the rows

Right-click the checklist, open **Tools**, and expand **Checklist** to edit the rows: retitle any row, toggle its done state, remove it with **×** (a checklist keeps at least one row), or **Add Row** (up to 30 rows per checklist, each up to 200 characters). Text, add and remove edits commit as normal undoable changes; the done toggle here is a tick like the canvas box, so it isn't undoable. Double-clicking the card does nothing on purpose: the boxes are the only thing to click on the canvas.

<Figure caption="Right-click, Tools, then Checklist: a done toggle, the text and a remove button per row, with Add Row underneath.">
  <ChecklistMenu />
</Figure>

The card's colours follow the tab's theme like any shape, and you can restyle it from **Style** in the same menu.

<Tip>
  Pair a checklist with [assigned actions](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/): the checklist
  shows the plan at a glance on the canvas, while assigned actions put a specific person's name on a
  piece of work.
</Tip>
