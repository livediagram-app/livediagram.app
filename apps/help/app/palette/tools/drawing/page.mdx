The palette's **Draw** category holds the tools you place by hand: the **Polygon** tool for clean point-by-point outlines, then **Arrow** and **Line**, each covered in its own guide. Everything they draw is a regular element you can move, resize, and style.

<Figure caption="The Draw category: Polygon, then Arrow and Line, with their keys on the right.">
  <PaletteRows category="Draw" active="Polygon" />
</Figure>

Draw is in the Diagram and [Workshop](/help/canvas/workshop-mode/) palettes (Workshop's leaves out the Polygon).

## The Polygon tool

The Polygon tool fills the gap the fixed shapes leave: clean, straight-edged outlines with exactly the corners you choose, for zones on an architecture diagram, floor plans, or any irregular region.

<Step n={1}>Select **Polygon** from the Draw category. It has no keyboard shortcut.</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Click to place each point. A preview line follows your pointer from the last point you placed.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Click the **start point** (a snap ring appears when you are close) to close the outline into a
  **filled polygon**, or **double-click** (or press `Enter`) to finish an **open line** at the last
  point.
</Step>

<Figure caption="Placing polygon points click by click, with the snap ring on the start point ready to close the outline.">
  <PolygonDraw />
</Figure>

While placing points, `Backspace` removes the last point so you can walk a mistake back without starting over, and `Escape` clears the in-progress outline (press it again to put the tool down). An open line needs at least two points and a closed polygon three: until then, double-click and `Enter` do nothing, and clicking the start point just places another point. On a touch screen, taps place points just like clicks. A closed polygon fills with the theme's fill colour like a shape; an open line stays stroke-only.

## Styling an outline

A committed polygon is a regular boxed element. Its stroke colour follows the tab's theme so it reads as part of the diagram, and a closed polygon fills with the theme's fill colour. From there you can:

- Move it, resize it (the outline scales proportionally), rotate it, and lock it.
- Recolour its stroke and fill from the element's Colours controls.
- Apply the format painter, themes, and comments, exactly as with any shape.
