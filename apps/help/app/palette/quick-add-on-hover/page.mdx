When you select an element, a **+** button appears on each of its sides. Clicking one opens the quick-add menu, a strip of actions for that side: **Duplicate** (copy the element to that side, connected), **Arrow** (drag out an arrow), **Pencil** (sketch freehand) and **Text** (add a label beside it, with no connector). Quick-add on Hover changes how that menu opens, so you can reach it without a click.

Some elements put their own actions on the **+**: a mind node leads with **Add Child** and **Add Sibling**, a table offers **Add Row** and **Add Column**, and a timeline offers **Add Point**.

## What it does

With the setting on, **hovering** a **+** opens its menu straight away, and the menu stays open while your pointer is over the + or the menu itself. Move away and it closes on its own after a moment, long enough to cross the gap between the two. When you are adding several connected elements in a row, it saves a click each time.

<Figure caption="With Quick-add on Hover on, resting the pointer on a + opens its menu, no click needed.">
  <QuickAddHoverMenu />
</Figure>

The **+** buttons still only appear on the element you have selected, and clicking a + still works exactly as before, so turning this on only adds the hover shortcut; it never takes the click away. Dragging a **+** still skips the menu and starts an arrow from that side, either way.

Hovering needs a mouse or trackpad. On a touch screen there is no hover, so you tap the **+** as usual.

## Turning it on

It is **off by default**, since menus that open on hover can feel twitchy if you didn’t ask for them. Switch on **Quick-Add on Hover** in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), under **Editor**.

<Figure caption="Settings, Editor: Quick-Add on Hover is the first row, off until you switch it on.">
  <SettingsEditorPane highlight="quick-add" />
</Figure>

The setting is saved with your other [settings](/help/user-interface/settings/). When you are signed in it follows you to your other devices; as a guest it stays with this browser.
