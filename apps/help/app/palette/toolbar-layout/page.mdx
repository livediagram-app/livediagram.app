The editor lays its tools out the same way on every device, a computer, a tablet or a phone: the palette is one strip across the top of the canvas, and the Explorer opens from a menu button in the top-left corner.

## What it does

The palette is a single strip pinned to the top centre of the canvas. From left to right it holds:

- **Selection mode**: Select, Hand, Eraser, Laser and the rest.
- **Category**: which set of tiles the strip shows, such as Popular, Shapes or Icons.
- **Up to twelve tiles** from that category (fewer on a narrow window). Click one to add it, or drag it onto the canvas. Each tile shows its keyboard shortcut in the corner.
- **More** (the **⋯** button), when the category has more than the strip can hold.
- **Search** (the magnifier, or press **S**), last on the strip, to find any element, icon or sticker by name.

In the top-left corner, in a card of their own, sit the **menu button** and the **editor mode switch** (Diagram, Draw, Facilitate, Illustrate or Plan; **Shift+D** moves to the next mode). The menu button opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) as a popover under it, and clicking it again closes it.

**Layers**, **Collaborate** and the Session buttons' **Poll** and **Vote** open just above their buttons in the bottom row, and close when you click the button again. Every other panel (the AI assistant, the Map and the tool panels) docks in a corner of the canvas, and you can drag it to another (see [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/)). Undo and Redo stay in the bottom-right corner.

Only one of the strip's menus is open at a time: opening the selection mode, category, More or Search closes whichever other was open, and a click anywhere outside closes it.

## More

**More** opens the whole category under the button: its search box, its groups and every tile. Adding a tile from it closes it again, so the canvas is clear to draw on, and switching category closes it too. Icons, Stickers, Tech, Collaborate and My shapes always have a More, since their search and groups only live there.

## Search

**Search** finds an element without knowing which category holds it. Before you type, it shows the kinds of element already on your tab, so the ones you are using are one click away. Type a name, or a word you would use for it ("database" finds the cylinder, a database icon and the database brands), and matching tiles, icons, stickers and technology marks appear in one list, the best name matches first; where two match equally well, the tiles your current editor mode offers lead. The × at the end of the box clears it. Click one to add it, or press Enter to add the best match.

Each editor mode keeps its palette to the tools that suit it, so a Diagram has no charts and an Illustrate page has no pens. Search still finds those: they wait below, under **Elements From Other Modes**, closed until you open it, and every one of them works in any mode. That includes Draw mode's **Marker 1, 2 and 3**, in the colours and widths you gave them there: pick one and draw, and press Escape to put it down.

Plan's own elements (its cards, boards, metrics, charts of cards and sheets) are made for Plan's boards, so only Plan mode adds them. Elsewhere, Search lists them last, under **Not Available in Diagram Mode** (or whichever mode you are in), greyed out so you can see where they live. Switch the tab to Plan mode to add one.

Search is not on an Event Storming board, where the notation is the palette, nor for a Participant, whose strip holds only the Participate tiles, and Draw mode shows its own dock instead of the strip. Wherever the strip's Search isn't shown, **S** keeps its old meaning and picks the Select tool.

## The order of the tiles

Each category keeps its own order on the strip: adding something never moves it. Thin dividers split a category into groups of related tiles, such as the desktop and the mobile devices, or the charts and the meters. Each time a document loads the strip opens on the mode's [Popular](/help/palette/popular/) in Diagram and Illustrate, on **Cards** in Plan (or **Boards** while the tab has none), on the notation on an Event Storming board, and on **Participate** for a Participant.

## What else the strip shows

- **Plan mode** adds an **Edit Cards** button to the strip on the Cards category, for someone who can edit, and **Illustrate** an **Add Page** button (a **+**). Neither shows on a phone.
- **On an Event Storming board** the strip shows the notation's tiles instead of the two pickers, led by **Add from Photo** where it is available.
- **Viewers** who can't edit have no strip, since there is nothing to add; the menu button still shows.
- **Zen mode** hides the strip and the menu button with the rest of the editor's chrome.

## On a phone

On a phone the strip is narrower:

- The **menu button** sits in a card of its own at the left of the strip, without the mode switch: to change editor mode on a phone, open the tab menu (the **…** on the tab) and choose a mode from its mode rows.
- The **category** shows its icon only, which leaves room for more tiles. Swipe the tiles sideways to see the rest of the category; **More** opens across the width of the screen.
- **Layers** and **Collaborate** open above their buttons in the bottom row, as on a computer. Pinch to zoom; the **Fit** button is there too.

A phone held sideways counts as a phone too.
