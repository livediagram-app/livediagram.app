Toolbar is one of the two choices in the **Panel Layout** setting (Floating and Toolbar). You will find it in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), under **Panels**, where a small picture of each layout shows how they differ before you switch. Floating is the default on a computer.

## What it does

The palette becomes a single strip across the top of the canvas. From left to right it holds:

- **Selection mode**: Select, Hand, Eraser, Laser and the rest, the same choices as the palette's own picker.
- **Category**: which set of tiles the strip shows, such as Favourites, Shapes or Icons.
- **Twelve tiles** from that category. Click one to add it, or drag it onto the canvas, exactly as from the palette. Each tile shows its keyboard shortcut in the corner.
- **More** (the **⋯** button), when the category has more than the strip can hold.

There is no floating Explorer panel. The **menu button** in the top-left corner opens the Explorer under it, and clicking it again closes it. **Layers**, **Tab Activity** and **Collaborate** open just above their buttons in the bottom row, and close when you click the button again. Every other panel (the AI assistant, the Map, polls, votes and the tool panels) floats and docks in its corner exactly as it does in the Floating layout.

## More

**More** opens the whole category under the button: its search box and every tile. Adding a tile from it closes it again, so the canvas is clear to draw on.

## The order of the tiles

Each category keeps its own order on the strip: adding something never moves it. Thin dividers split a category into groups of related tiles, such as the desktop and the mobile devices, or the charts and the meters. Your **Favourites** stay in the order you set: use **Reorder** at the bottom of **More** to change it, and **Edit** to choose them.

## On a phone

Toolbar works on a phone too. The strip is narrower there:

- The **menu button** sits at the left end of the strip rather than in the corner.
- The **category** shows its icon only, which leaves room for more tiles. How many tiles fit depends on how wide your screen is; **More** has the rest, and opens across the width of the screen.
- **Layers**, **Tab Activity** and **Collaborate** open above their buttons in the bottom row, as on a computer. Pinch to zoom; the **Fit** button is there too.

A phone always uses Toolbar. Floating is for computers, so if you pick it, a phone still shows Toolbar (and your computer still shows Floating).
