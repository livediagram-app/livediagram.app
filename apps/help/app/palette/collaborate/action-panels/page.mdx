An action panel is a card on the canvas that carries a list of [assigned actions](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/): what needs doing, who each one is for, and whether it is done. It is the natural home for the follow-ups a meeting agrees on. You read it where it sits, without opening anything.

## Adding one

Add one from the **Record** group of the palette's [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/) category. A fresh panel has no actions yet, so it invites you to give someone a clear next step, with an **Add Action** button.

Press it and the usual **Assign Action** dialog opens: pick who it is for, give it a name and an optional description, and choose whether to email them. Confirm, and the action appears on the card. Add as many as you need from the dashed **Add Action** bar at the bottom of the card.

A panel can be _about_ an element: drop one beside it and join the two with an ordinary arrow, the same way a [comment panel](/help/palette/collaborate/comment-panels/) is attached.

## Working with it

Each action is a row: a round check, its name, and who it is for. The header counts how many are still open, and says **All Done** once every one is ticked.

  <ActionPanelCard />
</Figure>

- **Tick the check** to mark an action done. It fills green, the name is struck through, and the row stays on the card as a record of what was agreed. Tick it again to reopen it. Finishing the last open action throws a small burst of confetti.
- **Click the action's name** to open the dialog with everything filled in, so you can rename it, change the description or hand it to someone else.
- To remove one action, open it and use **Delete Action** at the bottom of the dialog (you confirm it with a second click). Deleting the card removes all of its actions.

Each one is the same kind of action as any other: it appears in the [Collaborate panel](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/#the-collaborate-panel) and on your Activity page, and anyone who can view the document can read it, while only editors can change it.

## Panel or action badge

You can already put an action on any element with **Assign Action** in its menu. That shows as a small badge that opens a popover for one reader at a time. A panel holds the same kind of action, as many as you like, left out on the canvas, so they are in the export and in everyone else's view.
