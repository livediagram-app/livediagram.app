An action panel is a card on the board that carries one [assigned action](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/): what needs doing, who it is assigned to, who assigned it, and whether it is done. You read it where it sits, without opening anything.

## Adding one

Add one from the [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) group of the palette's **Behaviours** category. A fresh panel has no action yet, so it shows a **Set Up Action** button.

Press it and the usual **Assign Action** dialog opens: pick who it is for, give it a name and an optional description, and choose whether to email them. Confirm, and the card shows the action.

A panel can be _about_ an element: drop one beside it and join the two with an ordinary arrow, the same way a [comment panel](/help/palette/collaborate/comment-panels/) is attached.

## Working with it

- **Complete** marks the action done. The card dims and shows a **Done** chip, but stays on the board as a record of what was agreed. **Reopen** brings it back.
- **Edit** reopens the dialog with everything filled in, so you can rename it, change the description or hand it to someone else.
- To remove the action, delete the card. To keep the card and clear its action, use **Collaborate → View Action** in the card's menu and delete it from there.

It is the same action as any other: it appears in the [Collaborate panel](/help/collaboration/assigned-actions/#the-collaborate-panel) and on your Activity page, and anyone who can view the diagram can read it, while only editors can change it.

## Panel or action badge

You can already put an action on any element with **Assign Action** in its menu. That shows as a small badge that opens a popover for one reader at a time. A panel is the same action, left out on the board, so it is in the export and in everyone else's view.
