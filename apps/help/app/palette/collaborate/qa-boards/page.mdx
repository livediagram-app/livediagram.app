A Q&A board is a live question queue. Anyone adds a note, everyone upvotes the ones they want to talk about, and the list keeps itself sorted so the most wanted note is always on top. It sits in the **Ask** group of the [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) category.

## Adding a note

Type into **Add a note…** at the foot of the board and press Enter. Your name goes on the note unless you switch on **Anonymous** under the field, in which case no name is stored with it at all. The switch stays on for every note you add until you reload the page. A note can be up to 280 characters, and a board holds up to 200 notes.

People on a **Participant** share link can add notes and vote too: the audience asking questions rarely has edit rights, and doesn't need them here. A **Viewer** link only watches the board.

## Voting

Press the arrow beside a note to upvote it. Press it again to take your vote back. You get **one vote per note**, and the board enforces that itself, so it holds however many tabs you have open.

When votes change the ranking, notes slide to their new place. The top note is marked **Most wanted**, and the thin bar under each note shows its votes against the top note's. Discussed notes take no more votes.

## Running the discussion

The [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) runs the board. If nobody has taken that role, anyone with edit rights can.

- **Discuss the Top Note**, or **Discuss Now** on any note you hover, lifts it into the spotlight at the top.
- **Done** on the spotlight folds the note into **Discussed** below the queue, with its votes frozen. **Done, next** closes it and spotlights the current top note in one press. Hovering a note in the queue offers **Mark Done** for the same thing without discussing it first.
- **Remove** takes a note off the board, for duplicates and anything off-topic.
- Open **Discussed** to see what the room has covered, and **Reopen** a note to put it back in the queue.
- The board's **…** menu offers **Clear the Spotlight** and **Empty the Board** for the next session.

## Templates

Two [templates](/help/canvas/templates/) in the **Agile** category start from a Q&A board:

- **Lean Coffee**: the room brings the topics, upvotes them, and talks through the top ones in timeboxes. The four steps of the loop are drawn down the left, and the board sits beside the tools that run it: an 8-minute timer, a "Keep going on this topic?" poll, a 4-minute extension timer and a takeaways checklist.
- **Town Hall Q&A**: an audience Q&A for an all-hands or a panel. The board sits between the panel and a run-of-show agenda on one side, and a 30-minute timer, an applause pad, a closing rating poll and a follow-ups checklist for anything the panel can't answer live on the other.
