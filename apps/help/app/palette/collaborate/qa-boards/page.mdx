A Q&A board is a live question queue. Anyone adds a note, everyone upvotes the ones they want to talk about, and the list keeps itself sorted so the most wanted note is always on top. It sits in the **Ask** group of the [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) category.

  <QaBoardCard />
</Figure>

## Adding a note

Type into **Add a note** at the foot of the board and press Enter. Your name goes on the note unless you switch on **Anonymous**, in which case no name is stored with it at all. The switch stays on for the rest of your session.

People on a **view-only** share link can add notes and vote too: the audience asking questions rarely has edit rights, and doesn't need them here.

## Voting

Press the arrow beside a note to upvote it. Press it again to take your vote back. You get **one vote per note**, and the board enforces that itself, so it holds however many tabs you have open.

When votes change the ranking, notes slide to their new place. The top note is marked **Most wanted**, and the thin bar under each note shows its votes against the top note's.

## Running the discussion

The [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) runs the board. If nobody has taken that role, anyone with edit rights can.

- **Discuss the top note**, or **Discuss now** on any note, lifts it into the spotlight at the top.
- **Done** folds it into **Discussed** below the queue, with its votes frozen. **Done, next** closes it and spotlights the current top note in one press.
- **Remove** takes a note off the board, for duplicates and anything off-topic.
- Open **Discussed** to see what the room has covered, and **Reopen** a note to put it back in the queue.
- The board's `…` menu clears the spotlight or empties the board for the next session.

## Templates

Two [templates](/help/canvas/templates/) in the **Agile** category start from a Q&A board:

- **Lean Coffee**: the room brings the topics, upvotes them, and talks through the top ones in timeboxes. The board sits beside an 8-minute timer, a "Keep going on this topic?" poll and a takeaways checklist.
- **Town Hall Q&A**: an audience Q&A for an all-hands or a panel, with an agenda, a timer for the Q&A block and a follow-ups checklist for anything the panel can't answer live.
