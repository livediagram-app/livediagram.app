Most elements show what one person drew. These are the ones that collect what **everybody** thinks, and keep it on the canvas afterwards.

This page is the map. Each element has its own short article, so you can link somebody straight to the one they need.

## Finding them

They are [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/) elements, in [Facilitate mode](/help/canvas/facilitate-mode/)'s palette as six categories under **Collaborate** in the category dropdown. These elements sit in two of them: **Ask** for the ones that put a question to everybody, and **Record** for the ones you leave behind on the canvas, the comment and action panels among them.

<Figure caption="The six categories under Collaborate. The elements on this page sit in Ask and Record.">
  <CollaborateCategories />
</Figure>

The other four hold the elements that _do_ something when pressed (timers, reactions, Selection Mode buttons, portals): you reach for both kinds while running a session, so they sit side by side. Ask also holds the [dot vote and poll buttons](/help/palette/behaviour/session-buttons/).

- **Ask**: [estimate cards](/help/palette/collaborate/estimate-cards/), the [quiz](/help/palette/collaborate/quizzes/), the [temperature check](/help/palette/collaborate/temperature-checks/), the [idea box](/help/palette/collaborate/idea-boxes/) and the [Q&A board](/help/palette/collaborate/qa-boards/).
- **Record**: [comment panels](/help/palette/collaborate/comment-panels/), [action panels](/help/palette/collaborate/action-panels/), the [agenda](/help/palette/collaborate/agendas/), the [decision record](/help/palette/collaborate/decision-records/) and the [roll call](/help/palette/collaborate/roll-calls/).

These categories are part of the **Facilitate** mode palette; **Diagram** keeps the comment and action panels in its **Write** category. In [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/) the palette's **Tools** category carries the temperature check, the estimate card and the idea box. In any mode you can add any of them by name from the editor's [Search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/).

## Who can use them

Anyone with an **edit** link can answer. Someone on a view-only link sees every card and its answers, but the controls that would change them stay inactive. The [Q&A board](/help/palette/collaborate/qa-boards/) is the one exception: view-only visitors can add notes and vote on it.

The presses that _run_ a round (Reveal, New round, opening an idea box, taking the roll, starting an agenda segment, starting or revealing a quiz) belong to the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) while somebody holds that role. When nobody does, any editor can press them. Answering is always everybody's.

Answers **aren't undoable**: your Ctrl+Z should never retract somebody else's answer. Setting a card up (its label, an estimate's scale, an agenda's segments, a decision's status) is an ordinary edit, and undoes like one.
