Most elements show what one person drew. These are the ones that collect what **everybody** thinks, and keep it on the board afterwards.

This page is the map. Each element has its own short article, so you can link somebody straight to the one they need.

## Finding them

They live in the palette's **[Behaviours](/help/palette/behaviour/)** category, under **Dynamic** in the category dropdown, across two of the groups you click into: **Ask** for the ones that put a question to everybody, and **Record** for the ones you leave behind on the board — the comment and action panels among them. A search box above the groups cuts across all of them at once.

</Figure>

These had their own **Collaborate** category once. It was merged into Behaviours: you reach for both sets while running a session, and a category boundary between "pressing this does something" and "the board is collecting an answer" was a line to memorise rather than one to navigate by.

- **Ask** — [estimate cards](/help/palette/collaborate/estimate-cards/), the [temperature check](/help/palette/collaborate/temperature-checks/) and the [idea box](/help/palette/collaborate/idea-boxes/).
- **Record** — [comment panels](/help/palette/collaborate/comment-panels/), [action panels](/help/palette/collaborate/action-panels/), the [agenda](/help/palette/collaborate/agendas/), the [decision record](/help/palette/collaborate/decision-records/) and the [roll call](/help/palette/collaborate/roll-calls/).

## Who can use them

Anyone with an **edit** link. Someone on a view-only link sees every card and its answers but can't cast, reveal or clear — the controls render but stay inactive rather than pretending to work.

Answers **aren't undoable**: your Ctrl+Z should never retract somebody else's answer.
