Most elements show what one person drew. These are the ones that collect what **everybody** thinks, and keep it on the canvas afterwards.

This page is the map. Each element has its own short article, so you can link somebody straight to the one they need.

## Finding them

They are [Collaborate](/help/palette/behaviour/) elements: **Ask** for the ones that put a question to everybody, and **Record** for the ones you leave behind on the canvas, the comment and action panels among them. Ask also holds the [dot vote button](/help/palette/behaviour/session-buttons/).

- **Ask**: the [temperature check](/help/palette/collaborate/temperature-checks/).
- **Record**: [comment panels](/help/palette/collaborate/comment-panels/), [action panels](/help/palette/collaborate/action-panels/) and the [decision record](/help/palette/collaborate/decision-records/).

**Diagram** keeps the comment and action panels in its **Write** category. In any mode you can add any of them by name from the editor's [Search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-add-to-canvas/).

## Who can use them

Editors and anyone on a Participant pass can answer. Someone on a view-only link sees every card and its answers, but the controls that would change them stay inactive.

The presses that _run_ a round, such as a temperature check's **Reset Answers**, belong to editors. Answering stays open to everyone who can take part.

Answers **aren't undoable**: your Ctrl+Z should never retract somebody else's answer. Setting a card up (its label, a decision's status) is an ordinary edit, and undoes like one.
