A roll call records who was in the room, frozen into the diagram. It sits in the **Keep a record** group of the [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) category.

## Taking the roll

Press **Take roll** and it records everyone present at that moment, and keeps them after they leave.

<Figure caption="The roll is a snapshot with its own timestamp, not a live list of who is here.">
  <RollCallCard />
</Figure>

It is minutes, not a live list, which is why it still means something tomorrow. Taking the roll again (the button then reads **Take again**) replaces the list, which is what you want when latecomers arrive.
