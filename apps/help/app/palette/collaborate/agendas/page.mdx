An agenda is the run of the session, with minutes against each segment. It sits in the **Record** group of the [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) category. A new one arrives as a one-hour-shaped session (Set the scene, Gather input, Discuss, Agree actions) for you to rename and re-time.

## Running it

Press a segment and it starts the [tab timer](/help/collaboration/session-tools/timer/) counting down for that long, and marks itself as where the room is. Hover an upcoming segment and it shows **Start** in place of its minutes, so you can see what the press will do.

<Figure caption="Done segments are ticked and struck through, the current one counts down, and the header holds the total.">
  <AgendaCard />
</Figure>

- Done segments show a tick and strike through; upcoming ones show their minutes; the current one is lit, with the time remaining and a bar draining as it runs.
- The slim bar under the header shows how far through the whole session you are.
- The header shows the **total**, which is the number that tells you the plan doesn't fit before you start.
- Pressing another segment while one is running replaces the timer rather than queueing behind it, so an overrun never stops you moving on.

Starting a segment is a facilitator's press: while somebody is [facilitating](/help/collaboration/facilitator/), only they can, and otherwise any editor can. View-only visitors see where the room is but can't start a segment.

## Editing the segments

Right-click the agenda and open **Tools**, then **Segments**. Each row has a name, its minutes, a **↑** to move it up and a **×** to remove it, with **Add Segment** at the foot.

- A segment runs from 1 to 240 minutes; anything outside that is pulled back to the nearest limit when you leave the field.
- An agenda holds up to 60 segments.
- The agenda's own label is the session's name in the header. Double-click it to rename it.

## Nothing advances by itself

Nothing advances when a timer ends. A segment running out is a prompt for a person, not an instruction: press the next one when the room is ready.
