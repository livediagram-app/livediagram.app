Planning poker. Everyone picks a number privately; one **Reveal** shows every answer at once.

## Choosing a scale

Add an **Estimate** card from the **Ask** row of the [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) category. A new card asks which scale to use right on the canvas: **Fibonacci** (1 2 3 5 8 13 21 ?), **T-shirt** (XS to XL) or **Powers of two**. Tap one and the card is ready. You can change it later from the card's menu, under Scale.

Every scale ends in **?**, which is a real answer: "I can't size this."

## Before the reveal

Tap a card to pick it. The card shows **who** has answered, as a face-down card with their avatar and a count of how many are in, not what they said. That is the point: seeing a number first is what anchors a room.

<Figure caption="Before the reveal the card names who has answered, never what they picked.">
  <EstimateCard />
</Figure>

Press your own pick again to withdraw it. Picking a different one replaces it.

## After the reveal

Every card turns face up at once, sorted low to high with its author's avatar, and the **spread** is called out, or "Unanimous" when everyone agreed. The lowest and highest cards are ringed: they are the two people who should talk first. **New round** empties the card for the next story.
