Planning poker. Everyone picks a number privately; one **Reveal** shows every answer at once.

## Choosing a scale

The **Ask the room** row of the [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) category opens into one tile per scale: **Fibonacci** (1 2 3 5 8 13 21 ?), **T-shirt** (XS to XL) and **Powers of two**. Pick the scale you want and the card drops on it; you can still change an existing card from its menu, under Scale.

Every scale ends in **?**, which is a real answer: "I can't size this."

## Before the reveal

The card shows **who** has answered, not what they said. That is the point: seeing a number first is what anchors a room.

<Figure caption="Before the reveal the card names who has answered, never what they picked.">
  <EstimateCard />
</Figure>

Press your own pick again to withdraw it. Picking a different one replaces it.

## After the reveal

Every answer appears under its author with the **spread** called out, or "Unanimous" when everyone agreed. **Clear** empties the card for the next story.
