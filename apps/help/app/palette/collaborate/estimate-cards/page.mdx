Planning poker. Everyone picks a value privately; one **Reveal** shows every answer at once.

## Choosing a scale

Add an **Estimate** card from the **Ask** group of the [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) category. A new card asks **Choose a scale** right on the canvas: **Fibonacci** (1 2 3 5 8 13 21 ?), **T-shirt** (XS S M L XL ?) or **Powers of two** (1 2 4 8 16 ?). Tap one and the card is ready. Anyone who can edit may choose; a view-only visitor sees "Waiting for a scale".

To change it later, right-click the card and open **Tools**, then **Scale**. Changing the scale keeps answers already cast, so start a new round first if people have answered.

Every scale ends in **?**, which is a real answer: "I can't size this."

The card's label is the thing being estimated. Double-click it to name the story.

## Before the reveal

Tap a value to pick it; your pick lifts in the accent colour. The card shows **who** has answered, as a face-down card with their avatar, and the header counts how many are in ("3/5 answered"), never what they said. That is the point: seeing a number first is what anchors a room.

<Figure caption="Before the reveal the card shows who has answered, never what they picked, with Reveal at the foot.">
  <EstimateCard />
</Figure>

Press your own pick again to withdraw it. Picking a different one replaces it.

**Reveal** sits at the foot of the card once anybody has answered. It doesn't wait for everyone: the count already tells you who is missing.

## After the reveal

Every card turns face up at once, sorted low to high with its author's avatar, and the **spread** is called out ("Spread 3 → 13"), or "Unanimous" when everyone agreed. The lowest and highest cards are ringed: they are the two people who should talk first. **New round** takes Reveal's place and empties the card for the next story.

Reveal and New round belong to the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) while somebody holds that role, and to any editor otherwise. Only people who can edit the document can pick a value, and picks are never undone by somebody else's Ctrl+Z.
