A fist-of-five check-in. Everyone taps the face that fits, from a frown (1, blocked) to a beam (5, enthusiastic), and the bars and the mood meter move as the answers land. Tap another face to change your reading. It sits in the palette's **Ask** category, one of the six [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) categories.

The label is the question; a new card asks "How are we feeling?". Double-click it to ask your own.

<Figure caption="Five faces, the bars they fill, and the room's average on the mood meter.">
  <TemperatureCheckCard />
</Figure>

- **The bars** show how many people chose each value, with the count over each one.
- **The mood meter** under them runs cool to warm, with a marker at the average and the average itself, to one decimal, beside the number of people who answered.
- **Empty**, the card says "No readings yet" rather than drawing an average of zero.

The five values and their colours are fixed: fist of five is a ritual with a shared meaning, so the card doesn't offer a different range, and the colours don't change with the tab theme.

Editors and anyone on a Participant pass can answer. Viewers see the readings but can't add one, and nobody else's Ctrl+Z can take your reading back.

## It is never hidden

This one is **never hidden**, deliberately. Watching the shape of the room appear is the reading: a flat 3 across the board and a split between 1s and 5s have the same average and mean opposite things. There is no reveal and no reset button; for a fresh reading, drop a new card.
