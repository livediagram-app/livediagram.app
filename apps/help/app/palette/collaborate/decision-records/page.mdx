A decision record keeps what was decided, why, when, and whether it still stands, beside the thing it decided. It sits in the **Keep a record** group of the [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) category.

## What is on the card

- The element's **label** is the decision itself ("Use D1 for durable storage").
- A **status** chip: Proposed, Accepted, Rejected or Superseded.
- A **date**, and the **drivers** that led to it.

<Figure caption="The statement is the label, with the status chip, the drivers and the date around it.">
  <DecisionRecordCard />
</Figure>

All three are set from the element's menu, under Decision.

## Superseding one

To show that one decision replaced another, draw an [arrow](/help/palette/arrows/) between the two cards.
