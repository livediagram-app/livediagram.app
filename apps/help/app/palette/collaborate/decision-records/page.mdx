A decision record keeps what was decided, why, when, and whether it still stands, beside the thing it decided. It sits in the palette's **Record** category, one of the six [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) categories.

## What is on the card

- The element's **label** is the decision itself ("Use D1 for durable storage"). A new card reads "We will …"; double-click to write yours. A long statement is clamped to three lines.
- A **status** badge in the top-right corner: Proposed, Accepted, Rejected or Superseded, each with its own colour and glyph. A new card starts as Proposed.
- The **drivers** that led to it, listed under "Because".
- A **date**, as a pill at the foot. An undated card simply shows no date.

</Figure>

The status colours the badge, a soft glow and the driver markers, never the card's own fill, so the card keeps the tab theme like everything else.

## Setting it

Right-click the card and open **Tools**, then **Decision**. There you pick the **Status**, set the **Date** (clear it to remove it), and add drivers with **Add Driver** (up to 20, each up to 200 characters). Every change is an ordinary edit, so it undoes like one.

## Superseding one

To show that one decision replaced another, mark the old one **Superseded** and draw an [arrow](/help/palette/arrows/) from the new card to it. The arrow is the link: there is no separate "replaced by" field.
