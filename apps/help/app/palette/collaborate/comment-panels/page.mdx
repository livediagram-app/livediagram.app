A comment panel is a card on the board that shows a whole comment thread: the count in its header, the comments themselves, a composer, and resolve or reopen. You read it where it sits, without opening anything.

## Adding one

Add one from the [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) category to put a remark on a **place**: an empty patch of canvas, the gap between two clusters, the spot where something should go.

Or select an element and choose **Collaborate › Comment Panel**, which drops a panel to its right and joins the two with an ordinary arrow. Nothing bespoke: the panel is _about_ that element, and an arrow is what "about" already means on this canvas, so the pair moves, exports and behaves like anything else you have drawn.

## Panel or comment badge

You can already [comment on any element](/help/collaboration/comments/) by selecting it and using its comment badge. That popover is **one reader's transient view**: it opens for you and closes again.

A panel is the same thread, composer and resolve button, left out on the board, so it is in the export and in everyone else's session. That is what makes a remark part of the diagram rather than a note somebody left.

## It does not fold away

A collapse-to-summary was built and then dropped, because a panel you deliberately put on the board is there to be read, and folding it hides the whole point of it behind another click. If you do not want a thread on display, the element's own comment badge is still there.
