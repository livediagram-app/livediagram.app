An idea box collects anonymous submissions and holds them until you open it. It sits in the **Ask the room** group of the [Collaborate](/help/palette/collaborate/) category.

## While it is closed

Anyone types into **Add an idea**. No name is recorded against it: the element has nowhere to store one.

While it is closed the box shows a **count only**, not the text, and not even to the person who wrote it.

<Figure caption="Closed, the box shows how many ideas are in it and nothing else.">
  <IdeaBoxCard />
</Figure>

## Opening it

**Open the box** shows everything to the room. There is no closing it again: the flag protects the writing round, it isn't a toggle.

**Scatter to stickies** turns the ideas into ordinary [sticky notes](/help/palette/tools/sticky-notes/) beside the box, so you can group, move and [dot-vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/) them.

<Tip>
  Use it for retro round one, pre-mortems, and anything where the honest answer needs the safety of
  not being attributed.
</Tip>
