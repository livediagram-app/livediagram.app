A **Sequence Diagram** shows a conversation between parts of a system: participants side by side over dashed lifelines, and a row for each message, top to bottom in the order they happen. It is one element: you type the messages and it draws the arrows, bars and loops.

It sits in the palette's [Build](/help/palette/build/) category, after Flowchart.

## Editing it

Right-click the diagram and open its **Sequence** section.

**Participants** are listed left to right. Rename one, mark it as an **Actor** to draw a stick figure instead of a box, reorder with the arrows, or remove it (its messages go with it). A diagram has 2 to 12 participants.

**Messages** are listed top to bottom. Each has a **From**, a **To**, its words and a kind:

- **Call**: a solid line with a filled head.
- **Async**: a solid line with an open head, for a message nobody waits for.
- **Reply**: a dashed line.

Press **Enter** in a message's words to add the next message under it, from that message's receiver to the next participant along, so you can type a whole conversation straight down. A message from a participant to itself loops out and back.

A call that is later answered by a reply draws an activation bar on the participant while it works.

## Options

- **Numbered** puts each message's number in front of it ("1: Place order"), so a review can say "step 4".
- **Play** reveals the diagram top to bottom, so the messages appear in the order they happen. Set its speed, and turn on **Repeat** to loop it.

Resizing the diagram scales the drawing, keeping its shape.
