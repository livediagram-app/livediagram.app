An **Org Chart** is a [mind map](/help/palette/mind-maps/) of people. Each node shows an initials disc, the person's name and their role, and the chart grows downward with each report hung off an elbow line under their manager. Use it for teams, service ownership or any tree of things with a name and a role.

It sits in the palette's **Diagrams** category ([Build Elements](/help/palette/build/)), after Mind Node.

## Take the tour

The first time you select a person in an org chart, a small **Tour** toolbar appears beside its toolbar. Press **Play** for a short tour on your own chart: the name, role and initials, adding a report (Tab) or a peer (Enter), moving a whole team, and the **Mind Map** menu. Press the cross if you would rather not; either way it is not offered again. To be offered it again, open **Settings**, then **Tours**, open **Element Tours** and switch **Org Chart Tour** back on.

## Building one

Place the **Org Chart** tile and it drops one person, ready to rename. With a person selected:

- **Tab** adds a report below them.
- **Enter** adds a peer beside them.

Type the name straight away. The chart lays itself out after every change.

To write a role, select the person and click the line under their name. The initials come from the name: "Ana Kim" shows "AK".

## Everything a mind map does

An org chart is a mind map with a person look, so everything in [Mind Maps](/help/palette/mind-maps/) works here: drag a manager and their whole team comes too, **Tidy Map** straightens it, and **Edit Outline** edits it as indented text.
