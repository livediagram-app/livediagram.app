A marker is a small status symbol shown inside a shape: a traffic-light dot or a checkbox. It lets you flag status at a glance, such as a red, amber or green status, or a to-do that is done or not, without adding a separate element to the canvas.

<Figure caption="A traffic-light dot and a checkbox marker sit just left of each shape's label.">
  <ShapeMarkers />
</Figure>

## The markers

There are five markers, plus **None**, which clears it. Each tile in the menu is labelled:

- **Green**, **Orange** and **Red**: filled status dots. They keep their colours whatever the theme.
- **To do**: an empty checkbox.
- **Done**: a ticked checkbox. The checkbox takes the shape's text colour, so it stays readable on any fill.

## Where the marker sits

The marker sits just to the left of the shape's text. If you later clear the label, the marker stays and is centred in the shape instead. It composes neatly with an inline icon: the marker hugs the label while the icon keeps its chosen side.

You can also size the marker. The **Size** row mirrors the text-size control:

- **Scale** (the default, the first tile) tracks the shape's text size, so the marker grows and shrinks with the label.
- **Small**, **Medium** and **Large** (one, two and three dots) give fixed sizes.

## Adding a marker

Markers decorate the label, so the controls only appear once the shape has text.

<Step n={1}>Give the shape a label if it doesn't have one yet.</Step>
<Step n={2}>Right-click the shape to open its menu.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Open the **Text** row and expand **Markers**, below Font, Size, Padding and Alignment.
</Step>
<Step n={4}>
  Hover a tile to preview it on the canvas, then click to keep it: None, or one of the five markers.
</Step>
<Step n={5}>
  Use the **Size** row below to pick Scale, Small, Medium or Large. It is always on screen, greyed
  out until you pick a marker, so choosing one never changes the menu's height under your pointer.
</Step>

<Figure caption="The Markers section: None and the five markers, then the Size row.">
  <MarkersMenu />
</Figure>

To mark several shapes at once, select them all and right-click: the same **Markers** section appears in the selection's **Text** row once at least one selected shape has a label, and your choice applies to every selected shape (on one without a label, the marker sits centred). Each change is one undo step, so **Undo** takes a marker straight back off.

Markers are for shapes only. They are not offered for arrows, text, images or tables, nor for elements that draw their own face, such as charts, progress bars and rings, ratings, code blocks, and the Behaviour and Collaborate elements (a temperature check or an idea box, for example).

<Tip>
  Use a row of shapes with green, orange and red dots as a quick status board, then flip a dot's
  colour from the Markers section as work progresses.
</Tip>
