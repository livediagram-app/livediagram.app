Sometimes you want a shape to stand out, or a set of arrows to look consistent, without fiddling with fill, border, weight and pattern one control at a time. Style presets are ready-made looks you apply in a single click. Right-click an element and open its **Style** row: **Presets** is the first section in it, above Colours and Border.

## Shape presets

Right-click a shape, open **Style** and expand **Presets** to find a grid of ready-made looks, plus **Reset to default**. Each preset is a complete look: it sets the shape's fill, border and text colour together with a matching border weight and pattern, so one click restyles the whole shape. Presets deliberately leave the corner radius alone, so a rounded card stays rounded and a sharp box stays sharp whichever look you pick.

The grid offers twenty looks on a single-colour theme, four across, in four runs from quiet to loud:

- **Your theme's own colours:** the theme's default look, then Soft, Tinted, Solid and Bold versions of its accent. A multicolour theme adds a look for each of its branch colours.
- **Greys:** Ghost, Paper, Muted, Slate and Inked, the same under every theme.
- **Line treatments:** Hairline, Outline, Dotted, Dash-Dot and a heavy Frame.
- **Status:** Info, Success, Warning, Danger and a violet Highlight, the same under every theme.

Text colour is chosen for contrast on the filled looks, so labels stay readable. **Reset to default** clears the shape's colour and border changes, returning it to its theme default in one click.

## Arrow presets

Right-click an arrow and its **Presets** offer twelve one-click styles, ordered from plain lines to patterns to animated flows: Fine, Plain and Bold; Fine Dash, Dashed, Bold Dash and Dotted; then Flow, Dash Flow, Dot Flow, Signal and Pulse. Each combines a line pattern, a thickness and an optional flow animation. **Reset to default** clears the arrow's pattern, thickness and animation changes. See [Arrow Styles](/help/palette/arrows/arrow-styles/) for the controls behind them.

## Other elements

A few other elements have a **Presets** grid of their own in the same place:

- **Sticky notes:** twelve paper colours, each paired with a readable ink. These don't follow the theme, because a note's colour is your own shorthand.
- **Tables:** eight complete looks that set the cells, grid lines, header band and header text together.
- **Charts:** a set of colour palettes for the slices, bars and lines.
- **Code blocks:** colour schemes for the code.

## Good to know

- **Hover to preview.** With a mouse, hovering a preset shows it live on the canvas; it only sticks when you click. Moving away puts the element back. On a touch screen, a tap applies it.
- **Works on a selection.** Select several shapes, or several arrows, and right-click: the preset applies to all of them at once. When the selection holds both shapes and arrows, you get a **Shape Presets** and an **Arrow Presets** section side by side.
- **One undo step.** Each preset applies as a single step, so one **Undo** reverts it, however many elements it changed.
- **Presets follow the theme.** A shape on a theme-derived look (Bold, say) stays bound to it: switch the tab's [theme](/help/canvas/themes/) and it becomes the new theme's Bold. Editing any of its colours by hand ends the binding.
