liveplanner is a web app where people build **diagrams, whiteboards, mind maps, infographics and documents together in real time**. It is a multiplayer canvas in the browser: anyone with access to a document can join, see other collaborators' cursors and edits live, and contribute at the same time. The result is a shared visual artifact, a flowchart, an architecture diagram, a mind map, or a brainstorm board, that a team builds together rather than one person authoring and the rest reviewing.

## A multiplayer canvas

The heart of liveplanner is the [canvas](/help/canvas/the-canvas/). You drop down shapes, connect them with arrows, drag things around, and the diagram takes shape as you work. When more than one person is in the same document, everyone sees live cursors, who has what selected, and every change as it happens.

- **Shapes and arrows** for flowcharts, system diagrams, and process maps. See [Shapes](/help/palette/shapes/) and [Arrows](/help/palette/arrows/).
- **Mind maps and brainstorms** built from quick, keyboard-driven nodes: Tab adds a child, Enter a sibling. See [Mind Maps](/help/palette/mind-maps/).
- **Real-time presence** so collaborators rarely overwrite each other: an element someone else has selected is locked for everyone else.
- **Tabs** to keep related views of one document in a single place. See [Tabs](/help/tabs/using-tabs/).
- **[Plan](/help/canvas/plan-mode/)**: boards of cards you drag through columns, sheets, dashboards and forms, every tab worked on the same way, for everyone on it.
- **Templates** for retrospectives, Kanban boards, flowcharts and more, so you rarely start from a blank page. See [Templates](/help/canvas/templates/).

## Built for teams who think visually

liveplanner is aimed at people who need to think together, visually: engineering teams sketching architecture and sequence flows, product and design teams mapping journeys and information architecture, and cross-functional groups in workshops, planning sessions, and retrospectives. The unit of value is the team, not the single author.

To make that frictionless, the canvas **always works without signing in**. You can land on a blank diagram, build something real, and hand the link to a colleague who edits it straight away, no account required. See [Sharing your document](/help/getting-started/sharing-your-document/).

## Free, with no trackers

liveplanner is **free for everyone**. There is no paid tier and no plan to introduce one, and every feature is available to every user. There are no ads and no third-party trackers: the only analytics are anonymous, first-party and easy to turn off. See [What we collect](/help/privacy-and-security/what-we-collect/). See [Why use liveplanner?](/help/about/why-liveplanner/).
