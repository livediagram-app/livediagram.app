The palette is the strip across the top of the canvas. It holds the canvas tools and every category of thing you can add to a diagram, so it is your main launchpad while you build.

## The two dropdowns

The **editor mode** switch (Diagram, Draw, Illustrate or Plan; see [Editor Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/)) sits just left of the strip, beside the menu button (on a phone, switch mode from the tab menu's **Mode** instead). The strip itself starts with two dropdowns:

- On the left, the **selection mode** (what your pointer does), shown as a grid in three groups. **Edit**: **Select** (`V`), **Hand** (`H`), **Eraser** (`E`) and the [Format](/help/tips-and-tricks/format-painter/) painter. **Present**: **Laser** (`K`), **Spotlight**, **Avatar** (`W`) and [Slide Deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/). **Preview**: **Isometric** (`I`) and **Zen** (`Z`). Spotlight and Slide Deck are desktop only, Plan mode leaves Eraser and Format out, and most of these stay greyed out until the canvas has something on it. See [Selection Modes](/help/selection-modes/).
- On the right, the **category**. In Diagram and Illustrate mode it opens on **Popular**, the twelve tiles a diagram is most often built from (Square, Circle, Diamond, text, an arrow, a frame, a sticky note, an image, the shape pen, a table, a code block and an entity). The rest are grouped into bands:
  - **Common**: Shapes, Write and Draw (plus My shapes once you have [imported a shape library](/help/explorer/shape-libraries/)).
  - **Structure**: Build (frames, lanes, mind maps and more).
  - **Decorate**: Icons, Stickers, Tech (full-colour technology marks) and Media.
  - **Dynamic**: Collaborate, the interactive elements for workshops.

Pick a category from the dropdown and its first tiles fill the strip; when a category holds more than fits, **More** (⋯) opens the whole of it, and **Search** (the magnifier, or `S`) finds any element by name. Each mode has its own set: Illustrate mode adds the wireframing **Components** and **Devices** and the **Data** charts, and Plan mode swaps the list for its own categories, opening on **Cards**. An [Event Storming board](/help/canvas/event-storming-boards/) opens on its notation and has no Search. See [where each one lives](/help/palette/tools/).

## Adding elements

Pick a tile and it arms a **tap-or-drag** gesture: click the canvas to drop the element at its natural size, or drag on the canvas to size it as you place it. You can also drag a tile straight onto the canvas. An annotation is the exception: it drops at the centre of the visible canvas, ready to move.

If a boxed element is already selected when you click to drop another, the new one inherits its width and height, so you can chain similarly-sized nodes quickly. (A circle or diamond takes a square the size of the larger side, and a selected frame or lane never passes its size on.)

A tile with a keyboard shortcut shows its key in the corner on the strip; in the More and Search grids, hold `⌘` (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) to show them. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## More about the strip

The strip stays pinned to the top of the canvas on every device; on a phone its tiles swipe sideways. It steps aside in [Draw mode](/help/canvas/draw-mode/), where a dock takes its place, in Zen mode, and on a view-only link. See [Toolbar Layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/) for More, Search and the order of the tiles, and [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/) for where everything else sits.

Looking for commands rather than elements? `⌘K` opens search with its actions; see [the Command Palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/).
