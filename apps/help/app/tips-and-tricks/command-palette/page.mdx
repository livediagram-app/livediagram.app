The palette is the floating panel that sits over the canvas, top-right by default, labelled `PALETTE`. It holds the canvas tools and every category of thing you can add to a diagram, so it is your main launchpad while you build.

## The title row and the two dropdowns

The palette's title row carries the **editor mode** switch (Diagram, Draw, Illustrate or Plan; see [Editor Modes](/help/canvas/editor-modes/)). Beneath it, a header band holds two dropdowns:

- On the left, the **selection mode** (what your pointer does), shown as a grid in three groups. **Edit**: **Select** (`V`), **Hand** (`H`), **Eraser** (`E`) and the [Format](/help/tips-and-tricks/format-painter/) painter. **Present**: **Laser** (`K`), **Spotlight**, **Avatar** (`W`) and [Slide Deck](/help/selection-modes/slide-deck/). **Preview**: **Isometric** (`I`) and **Zen** (`Z`). Spotlight and Slide Deck are desktop only, and most of these stay greyed out until the canvas has something on it. See [Selection Modes](/help/selection-modes/).
- On the right, the **category**. It opens on **Popular**, the twelve tiles a diagram is most often built from (Square, Circle, Diamond, text, an arrow, a frame, a sticky note, an image, the shape pen, a table, a code block and an entity). The rest are grouped into bands:
  - **Common**: Shapes, Write and Draw (plus My shapes once you have [imported a shape library](/help/explorer/shape-libraries/)).
  - **Structure**: Build (frames, lanes, mind maps and more).
  - **Decorate**: Icons, Stickers, Tech (full-colour technology marks) and Media.
  - **Dynamic**: Collaborate, the interactive elements for workshops.

Pick a category from the dropdown and its tiles fill the panel. Each mode has its own set: Illustrate mode adds the wireframing **Components** and **Devices** and the **Data** charts, and Plan mode leads with its cards and boards. See [where each one lives](/help/palette/tools/).

## Adding elements

Pick a tile and it arms a **tap-or-drag** gesture: click the canvas to drop the element at its natural size, or drag on the canvas to size it as you place it. You can also drag a tile straight onto the canvas. An annotation is the exception: it drops at the centre of the visible canvas, ready to move.

If a boxed element is already selected when you click to drop another, the new one inherits its width and height, so you can chain similarly-sized nodes quickly. (Circles and diamonds stay round and square, and a selected frame or lane never passes its size on.)

Hold `⌘` (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) and each tile shows its keyboard shortcut. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Moving and folding it away

On a computer the palette is movable: drag its header to another corner, and use the collapse button to shrink it to a banner and reclaim canvas space. See [Panel Layout](/help/user-interface/panel-layout/). On a phone it becomes a strip across the top of the canvas instead (the [Toolbar layout](/help/palette/toolbar-layout/)).

<Tip>
  Prefer a tidier workspace? Set **Panel Layout** to Toolbar in Settings to swap the floating

</Tip>
