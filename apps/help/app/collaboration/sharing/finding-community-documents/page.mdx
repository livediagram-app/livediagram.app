The [Community](/community/) is a gallery of documents people have shared: architecture diagrams, retrospectives, roadmaps, wireframes, sketches and more. You don't need an account to browse it, like a document or make your own copy.

## Searching

One search box holds everything you are looking for, written as words:

- **Plain words** search the title, description and tags, for example `checkout flow`.
- **`#tag`** finds documents with that tag. Add several, like `#aws #event-driven`, and a document has to have all of them.
- **`category:planning`** keeps to one subject, such as Planning, Workshops or Design & Wireframes.
- **`sort:loved`** puts the most liked documents first, and **`sort:copied`** the most copied. Without one, the newest come first.

You can type these yourself, or use the three buttons inside the right of the search box, which write the same words for you:

<Step n={1}>
  Choose **Category** to keep to one subject, or **All Categories** to see everything again.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  Choose **Tags** to see the most used tags. Pick one to add its `#tag` to your search; pick it
  again to take it out.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Choose the sort button (it reads **Newest** to start) to order by **Most Loved** or **Most
  Copied**.
</Step>

On a phone the three buttons show just their icons. Your search stays in the page address, so you can bookmark it or send it to someone.

<Tip>On a document's own page, choose any of its tags to search the gallery for that tag.</Tip>

## Looking at a document

Open a card to see the document's page: a live preview you can pan, zoom and page through, the author's description and its tags. **Open Document** shows it full screen. Documents in the Community are always read-only, and their comments are private.

## Liking a document

Choose the heart on a card, or **Like** on a document's page, to show the author you liked it. Choose it again to take your like back. Likes are kept per browser, and **Most Loved** sorts by them.

## Making your own copy

Choose **Make a Copy** on a document's page, or in the bar across the top of an open document. A copy of the whole document opens in the editor as your own document, with every tab. Change anything you like; the original never changes. Each person who copies a document counts once towards its copy count.

## Reporting a document

If a document is spam, offensive, shows someone's personal information or isn't the author's to share, choose **Report** on its page, pick a reason and add a note if it helps. Documents reported by several people are hidden automatically while they are reviewed.
