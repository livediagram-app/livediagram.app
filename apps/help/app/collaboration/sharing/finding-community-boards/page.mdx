The [Community](/community/) is a gallery of boards people have shared: architecture diagrams, retrospectives, roadmaps, wireframes, sketches and more. You don't need an account to browse it, like a board or make your own copy.

## Searching

One search box holds everything you are looking for, written as words:

- **Plain words** search the title, description and tags, for example `checkout flow`.
- **`#tag`** finds boards with that tag. Add several, like `#aws #event-driven`, and a board has to have all of them.
- **`sort:loved`** puts the most liked boards first, and **`sort:copied`** the most copied. Without one, the newest come first.

You can type these yourself, or use the two buttons inside the right of the search box:

<Step n={1}>Choose **Tags** to see the most used tags and how many boards have each.</Step>
<Step n={2}>Pick a tag to add its `#tag` to your search. Pick it again to take it out.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Choose the sort button (it reads **Newest** to start) to order by **Most Loved** or **Most
  Copied**.
</Step>

Your search stays in the page address, so you can bookmark it or send it to someone. The **category** chips under the search box narrow the gallery to one subject, such as Planning or Design & Wireframes.

<Tip>On a board's own page, choose any of its tags to search the gallery for that tag.</Tip>

## Looking at a board

Open a card to see the board's page: a live preview you can pan, zoom and page through, the author's description and its tags. **Open Board** shows it full screen. Boards in the Community are always read-only, and their comments are private.

## Liking a board

Choose the heart on a card, or **Like** on a board's page, to show the author you liked it. Choose it again to take your like back. Likes are kept per browser, and **Most Loved** sorts by them.

## Making your own copy

Choose **Make a Copy** on a board's page, or in the bar across the top of an open board. A copy of the whole board opens in the editor as your own document, with every tab. Change anything you like; the original never changes. Each person who copies a board counts once towards its copy count.

## Reporting a board

If a board is spam, offensive, shows someone's personal information or isn't the author's to share, choose **Report** on its page, pick a reason and add a note if it helps. Boards reported by several people are hidden automatically while they are reviewed.
