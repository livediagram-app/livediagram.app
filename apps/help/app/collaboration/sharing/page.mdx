Sharing turns a document into something other people can open. A share link lets anyone with the URL join your document in real time, with live cursors and edits flowing between everyone. You can protect a link with a password, give it an expiry date, or embed the document in another page. None of this needs the other person to sign in: a share link can be handed to a guest who edits without an account.

## Share links

Open the **Share** dialog on a document to create a link. The dialog draws every link as a **pass**, a ticket that lets whoever holds it in, and the line under its title always tells you who can get in right now: only you, or anyone holding one of your passes.

Pick what the pass lets people do, then press **Create Pass**. The link is created and copied in one go, ready to paste. Each pass carries a role, printed on its coloured stub:

- An **Editor** pass lets the visitor change the document with you, live.
- A **Viewer** pass lets them watch and pan and zoom, but not edit.

A document can have several links at once, so you can hand out an edit link to your team and a view link to everyone else. Whoever opens a link joins the same realtime room, so cursors and changes appear as they happen.

A link opens every tab unless you [scope it to one tab](/help/collaboration/sharing/one-tab/). The person you send a one-tab link to sees that tab, and the rest of the document never reaches their browser.

## Controlling access

Two self-serve controls let you bound who gets in and for how long:

- A [share password](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/) makes every link prompt for a password before it opens. The document's owner always bypasses it.
- A [share link expiry](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-link-expiry/) gives a link a lifetime (a week, a month, or six months). When it runs out the link stops working, but stays in your dialog so you can extend or delete it.

These are independent: you can use either, both, or neither. They are layered on top of the link role, not a replacement for it.

## Embedding a document

An [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) puts a live copy of a document inside another page, like a wiki, a doc, or Notion. The embed honours the link's role: a **view** link embeds a read-only canvas with pan and zoom, while an **edit** link embeds a fully editable one. Either way it hides the editor chrome and updates as the document is edited. Each pass in the Share dialog has an **Embed** menu offering **Copy embed URL** for the bare URL and **Copy iframe** for a ready-made snippet to paste.

## Sharing with everyone

Proud of a board? [Share it to the Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/), the public gallery where anyone can find it, view it read-only and make their own copy. The **Community** section at the bottom of the Share dialog publishes it, edits its listing and takes it down again.

<Tip>
  Need to stop sharing entirely? Use the bin (**Revoke link**) on each pass in the Share dialog.
  Anyone currently viewing through it loses access, and the URL stops resolving.
</Tip>

<Note>
  An [offline document](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) lives only in your browser, so
  there is nothing on the server to share yet. Opening **Share** on one shows a gate instead of the
  link controls, with a **Sync Document** button that uploads it to your account first.
</Note>
