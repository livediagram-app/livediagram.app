Sharing turns a document into something other people can open. A share link lets anyone with the URL join your document in real time, with live cursors and edits flowing between everyone. You can protect a link with a password, give it an expiry date, or embed the document in another page. None of this needs the other person to sign in: a share link can be handed to a guest who edits without an account, and you can create links as a guest too.

## Opening the Share dialog

Press **Share** at the top right of the editor. It turns solid blue once the document has a live link, so you can tell at a glance that it is shared. You can also reach it from the [command palette](/help/tips-and-tricks/command-palette/) (search "Share document") or from a document's menu in the Explorer.

Only the document's owner manages its links. Someone who opened your document through a link doesn't see **Share**; they get a **Copy** button instead, which makes their own copy.

## Share links

The dialog draws every link as a **pass**, a ticket that lets whoever holds it in. The line under the title always tells you who can get in right now: **Private** while only you can open it, **Shared** once any pass is live, and **Public** while it is in the [Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/).

Under **Issue a pass**, pick what the pass lets people do, then press **Create Pass**. The link is created and copied in one go, ready to paste. Each pass carries a role, printed on its coloured stub:

- An **Editor** pass lets the visitor change the document with you, live.
- A **Participant** pass lets them take part without reshaping the board. They add stickies and text, write on anything, move and recolour stickies, cast dots and answer [session tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/), and work with Plan cards and Sheet cells. They can delete only the stickies and text they added, and they cannot change the tab's mode, add or remove tabs, move or restyle shapes and frames, or hold the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/) baton. Their palette has one category, **Participate**, holding just what they can add, and the selection modes they need: Select, Hand, Laser, Spotlight, Avatar, Isometric and Zen. It is the pass to hand a team for a retrospective or a workshop.
- A **Viewer** pass lets them watch, pan and zoom, follow along and point with the laser, and nothing else: no [comments](/help/collaboration/comments/), votes, [polls](/help/collaboration/session-tools/polls/) or changes. Hand out a Participant pass to anyone who should chime in.

A document can have several links at once, so you can hand out an edit link to a co-facilitator, a Participant link to your team and a view link to everyone else. Newest passes sit at the top. Whoever opens a link joins the same realtime room, so cursors and changes appear as they happen, and the document lands in their [Shared with me](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) list for next time.

Each pass shows its link with a copy button inside it, how long it is **Valid**, and which tabs it **Opens** (on a document with more than one tab). Below that sit its **Embed** and **Live image** menus, and the bin to revoke it.

A link opens every tab unless you [scope it to one tab](/help/collaboration/sharing/one-tab/). The person you send a one-tab link to sees that tab, and the rest of the document never reaches their browser.

## Your name when sharing

As a guest, the **Sharing as** field at the bottom of the dialog is the name other people see on your cursor and your comments. Type a new one, or press the shuffle button for another random name. It is saved when you create a pass or close the dialog. Signed in, your account name is used and the field is hidden. See [Guest Identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/).

## Controlling access

Two self-serve controls let you bound who gets in and for how long:

- A [share password](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/) makes every link prompt for a password before it opens. The document's owner always bypasses it.
- A [share link expiry](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-link-expiry/) gives a link a lifetime (a week, a month, or six months). When it runs out the link stops working, but stays in your dialog so you can extend or delete it.

These are independent: you can use either, both, or neither. They are layered on top of the link role, not a replacement for it. [Share Link Security](/help/privacy-and-security/share-link-security/) explains what a link does and does not protect.

## Embedding a document

An [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) puts a live copy of a document inside another page, like a wiki, a doc, or Notion. The embed honours the link's role: an Editor pass embeds an editable canvas, while a Participant or Viewer pass embeds a read-only one with pan and zoom. Either way it hides the editor chrome and updates as the document is edited. Each pass's **Embed** menu offers **Copy embed URL** for the bare URL and **Copy iframe** for a ready-made snippet to paste.

Where an `<iframe>` isn't allowed, like a GitHub README, use a [live image](/help/collaboration/sharing/live-image/) from the pass's **Live image** menu instead.

## Sharing with everyone

Proud of a document? [Share it to the Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/community/), the public gallery where anyone can find it, view it read-only and make their own copy. Looking for a starting point instead? [Find documents in the Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/finding-community-documents/) by searching their words and tags. The **Community** section at the bottom of the Share dialog publishes it, edits its listing and takes it down again.

## Stopping sharing

To stop a link working, press the bin (**Revoke link**) on its pass and confirm with **Revoke**. Anyone viewing through it is sent out of the document straight away, and the URL stops resolving. A revoked link can't be brought back: create a new pass if you change your mind, which gives a new URL.

<Note>
  An [offline document](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) lives only in your browser, so
  there is nothing on the server to share yet. Opening **Share** on one shows a gate instead of the
  link controls, with a **Sync Document** button that uploads it to your account first.
</Note>
