The [Community](/community/) is a public gallery of documents people are proud of. Anyone can browse it, filter it by category and tag, open a document read-only, like it, and make their own copy to start from. Sharing a document there is a way to show your work and to help someone else get going.

## Sharing a document

You need to be signed in to share a document to the Community, so every document there has a real author. Browsing, liking and copying need no account.

<Step n={1}>Open the **Share** dialog on your document.</Step>
<Step n={2}>In the **Community** section at the bottom, choose **Share to Community**.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Give it a title, a description of what it shows and how someone could reuse it, a **category** and
  up to five **tags**, then choose **Share to Community**.
</Step>

Good tags are the words someone would search for: the tools, the method, the subject. They help people find your document through the filters on the Community page.

## What people see

A Community document always shows your **latest saved version**, so your edits appear there too. Visitors see it read-only:

- They can pan, zoom and switch tabs, and make their own copy with **Make a Copy**. Their copy is theirs; it never changes yours.
- They do not join your document live, so you won't see strangers' cursors, and you won't get an email for each visitor.
- **Comments stay private.** They are not shown to Community visitors.

Each card shows how many people liked the document and how many made a copy.

Opened from the Community, a document is read-only for you too, so you can see it exactly as visitors do and never change it by accident from the gallery. The bar across the top offers **Edit Your Document**, which opens your own document.

## Editing or removing it

Open the **Share** dialog again. The **Community** section shows your post with **Edit Listing**, to change the title, description, category or tags, and **Remove From Community**, which takes it out of the gallery straight away. Copies people already made stay theirs.

<Tip>
  A document in the Community can't also have a [share
  password](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/), because a public document can't ask its
  visitors for one. Remove it from the Community first if you want to set a password.
</Tip>

## Things that can't be shared

- Documents in a **team library** belong to the team, so they can't be shared to the Community.
- A document with nothing on it can't be shared until you add something.
- A document in the **Trash** disappears from the Community until you restore it.

## Finding documents

Browsing the Community, searching it by tag and making your own copy are covered in [Finding Documents in the Community](/help/collaboration/sharing/finding-community-documents/), along with reporting a document that shouldn't be there.
