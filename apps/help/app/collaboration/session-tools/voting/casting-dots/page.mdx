While a [vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/) is open, every votable element carries a small **− 0 +** counter in its bottom-right corner.

## Spending and taking back

Press **+** to add one of your dots and **−** to take one back. Clicking the element itself also adds a dot, which is quicker when you are working through a wall of stickies. Stacking several dots on one element is allowed, each counting separately, unless the vote was started with **One each** under **Dots per item**: then each of your dots must go on a different element. The banner at the top of the canvas keeps count, like "2 of 3 dots left".

The counter starts at zero, so you can always see which elements are in play and how many dots each has. **−** greys out at zero and **+** greys out once your budget is spent, rather than disappearing, so the buttons stay where your pointer expects them.

Once the vote ends the buttons go and the counter becomes a plain tally, since at that point it is a result to read rather than something to change.

## What can take a dot

**Votable** elements are shapes, sticky notes, and images. Section backdrops (frames), text, freehand drawings, tables, arrows, and annotations are not votable, so you vote on the ideas, not the scaffolding around them. Nor are elements that do something when pressed (mode buttons, portals, session buttons, reveal zones, pickers and reaction pads), or comment and action panels. If the vote is [limited to one layer](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-layers/), only that layer's elements count.

On a tab with Plan boards, the **cards** take the dots: each card the boards show carries the counter in its corner (a card shown on two boards is one card, with one count), while the boards themselves, and Plan views, take none, so you can still work the board during the vote. A card face down on a board that hides writing takes no dots until it is revealed. When the host ends the vote, each card keeps the dots it gathered, for **Top Voted** and the card's ▲ count.

Casting needs edit access: someone on a Viewer share link sees the tallies but has no **+** to press.

Non-votable elements show no counter and behave normally, so the canvas stays fully editable during a vote.
