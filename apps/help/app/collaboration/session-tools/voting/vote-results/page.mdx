**Show results** starts a guided walkthrough of everything that received dots in a [vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/), most votes first.

## Stepping through the picks

The current pick pulses an amber highlight and the canvas centres on it, while the top of the [Vote panel](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-panel/) shows where you are and how many dots it got ("Top result 1 of 4 · 5 votes"), with **Previous** and **Next** buttons to step through in order. The panel opens above the **Vote** button for whoever runs the walkthrough, and the button's dot turns amber while it runs.

<Figure caption="The Vote panel drives the walkthrough above the ranked list of every element that got a dot.">
  <VoteResultsWalkthrough />
</Figure>

The walkthrough is shared: the person who started the vote (or whoever is facilitating) drives it, and everyone else's canvas follows to the same element. Their Vote panel shows the same readout without the buttons, and says "The host is walking through the results." That is the point of reviewing together, and it means "everyone look at this one" actually works.

## The ranked list

The [Vote panel](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-panel/) switches to the **ranked list** at the same moment: every element that got a dot, most votes first, with the winners flagged.

If you started the vote or are facilitating, click any row to move the room straight to that element instead of stepping through in order. Everyone else sees the same list and follows along.

## Finishing

On the last pick, Next becomes **Done**. Pressing it ends the walkthrough and clears the whole vote session: the banner, the tally pills, and the highlights all go together, leaving the canvas clean for whatever comes next.
