While a [vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/) is running, press the **Vote** button in the bottom bar (a green dot on it means a vote is on) to open the **Vote** panel above it. Press the button again, or anywhere on the canvas, to put it away.

## While voting is open

It shows how far the room has got: how many people have finished ("2 of 5 finished"), dots cast against dots available with a progress bar, and a row per voter with their spent dots as filled pips and the rest hollow, ending in how many they have left.

<Figure caption="Filled pips are dots spent; a part-filled row is somebody still deciding.">
  <VotePanelProgress />
</Figure>

The tally pills on the canvas tell you what is winning; this tells you whether anyone is still deciding, which is the thing that decides when to press **End vote**.

The buttons at the bottom follow the vote along: **End vote** while it is open, then **Show results** or **Clear** once it has ended, then **Clear vote** after the reveal. They are only there for whoever started the vote (or the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/)); everyone else sees "Only the person who started this vote, or the facilitator, can end it." in their place.

## Why the rows have no names

Voter rows aren't labelled with names. Dots aren't recorded against people in a way the app can turn back into a name, so the panel shows the shape of the room rather than a register: all-filled rows are done, part-filled rows are who you are waiting on.

## After the reveal

The panel switches to the [ranked list](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-results/) the moment you choose **Show results**.
