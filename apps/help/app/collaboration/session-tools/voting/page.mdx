Dot-voting is a quick way for a group to prioritise. Each participant gets a budget of dots to spend on the elements they care about most, the dots tally up live, and you reveal the winners at the end. It is ideal for narrowing a wall of retro stickies or brainstorm ideas down to the few worth acting on.

## Running a vote

Press the **Vote** button in the bottom bar (in Diagram and Plan mode) to open the vote set-up above it.

<Step n={1}>
  Set the **Dots per person** by tapping the row of dots (tap the fifth for five each; anywhere from
  1 to 10, starting at 3). With more than one dot, choose whether people can stack dots under **Dots
  per item** (**Any number**, or **One each** so every dot goes on a different item). Decide the two
  [privacy switches](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-privacy/) and, on a tab with
  [layers](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-layers/), which layer takes dots. Then
  choose **Start vote**. The popover becomes the [Vote
  panel](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-panel/), and each participant's **Vote**
  button counts the dots they have left.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  While the vote is open, participants [click the elements they want to
  back](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/casting-dots/). The Vote panel shows your own dots
  left at the top and how far through the room you are underneath. On a desktop it stays open while
  the vote runs, until you press the **Vote** button again.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Choose **End vote** to close casting (the tallies stay put), then **Show results** to start the
  [guided results walkthrough](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-results/), or **Clear**
  to skip it. After the walkthrough, **Clear vote** puts the canvas back. All of these are on the
  Vote panel, so you never have to leave it mid-session.
</Step>

## Who drives a vote

**A vote belongs to whoever started it.** Only they (or the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/), when there is one) can end it, reveal the results, clear it, or move the walkthrough; everyone else's Vote panel says so in place of the buttons. You can always run another vote, but you can't get the dots back, so an accidental **End vote** from a participant would cost the room the whole round.

Starting a vote needs edit access. Visitors on a Participant pass cast dots like editors; on a Viewer share link they can watch the counts, the reveal, and the walkthrough but cannot cast a dot, so observers see the outcome without skewing it. A tab runs one vote at a time, and each tab can run its own.

<Tip>
  A common rule of thumb is roughly one dot per three or four options. Set the stepper before you
  start the vote, then leave it: keeping budgets small forces real choices.
</Tip>
