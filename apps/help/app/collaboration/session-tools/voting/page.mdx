Dot-voting is a quick way for a group to prioritise. Each participant gets a budget of dots to spend on the elements they care about most, the dots tally up live, and you reveal the winners at the end. It is ideal for narrowing a wall of retro stickies or brainstorm ideas down to the few worth acting on.

## Running a vote

Dot-voting is controlled from the tab's menu, under **Collaborate → Vote**.

<Step n={1}>
  Set the **dots per person** with the stepper, decide the two [privacy
  switches](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-privacy/) and, on a tab with
  [layers](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-layers/), which layer takes dots. Then
  choose **Start vote**. A banner tells each participant how many dots they have left.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  While the vote is open, participants [click the elements they want to
  back](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/casting-dots/). A live "N cast" readout tracks the
  total, and the [Vote panel](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-panel/) shows how far
  through the room you are.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Choose **End vote** to close casting (the tallies stay put), then **Show results** to start the
  [guided results walkthrough](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-results/), then **Clear
  vote** to put the board back. All three are on the Vote panel, so you never have to go back into
  the menu mid-session.
</Step>

## Who drives a vote

**A vote belongs to whoever started it.** Only they can end it, reveal the results, clear it, or move the walkthrough. You can always run another vote, but you can't get the dots back, so an accidental **End vote** from a participant would cost the room the whole round.

View-only visitors can watch the counts, the reveal, and the walkthrough but cannot cast a dot, so observers see the outcome without skewing it.

<Tip>
  A common rule of thumb is roughly one dot per three or four options. Set the stepper before you
  start the vote, then leave it: keeping budgets small forces real choices.
</Tip>
