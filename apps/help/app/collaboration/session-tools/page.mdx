Session tools make a shared document interactive for live workshops. There are three: a **timer** for time-boxing an activity, **dot-voting** for prioritising as a group, and **polls** for asking the room a question. Anyone who can edit the document can run them (or only the [facilitator](/help/collaboration/facilitator/), once someone takes that role), and they sync to everyone in real time, so they pair naturally with collaborative boards like retros, brainstorms, and planning sessions.

## Built for facilitating live

The tools live in the bottom bar: **Timer**, **Vote**, and **Poll** sit together in one group, right after Undo and Redo. Diagram mode has all three; [Plan mode](/help/canvas/plan-mode/) has **Timer** and **Vote** (its cards take the dots); Draw and Illustrate mode have none.

Each button opens its tool in a popover above it. While a tool is idle its button is just an icon, and pressing it opens the set-up. Once it is going the button shows what is happening: the **Timer** shows its clock, the **Vote** button shows how many dots you have left, and a green dot marks a running poll. View-only visitors see a button only while its tool is running.

On a desktop, a running vote's or poll's popover stays open while you work on the canvas: only pressing its button again puts it away, and it closes by itself when the activity ends. The timer's popover, and any set-up popover, closes as soon as you click elsewhere: the clock stays on the **Timer** button.

You can also put a session tool **on the canvas** as a [session button](/help/palette/behaviour/session-buttons/), so a template can carry its own facilitation.

While someone else is facilitating, the set-up says who at the top and its controls are greyed out.

The timer and the vote are scoped to a single tab, so different tabs can run different activities, and they ride the same live sync as everything else on the canvas. That means people who join late or reload simply see what is happening now: a timer mid-countdown, or the votes cast so far, with nothing to set up again. **Polls are the exception**: they are never saved to the document, and vanish completely when you end one. While a poll runs, anyone who joins late or reloads is still asked.

- **Timer** time-boxes an activity, as a countdown or a stopwatch counting up from zero (one runs at a time), shown on its button in the bottom bar. See [The Timer](/help/collaboration/session-tools/timer/).
- **Dot-voting** lets each participant spend a budget of dots on the elements they care about, with live tallies and a results reveal. See [Dot Voting](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/).
- **Polls** ask everyone a question (yes/no, a rating, a choice, a person in the room, or free text) and tally the answers live, saving nothing unless you keep the results as a chart. See [Polls](/help/collaboration/session-tools/polls/).

You can also put a timer, a vote or a poll **on the canvas** as a [session button](/help/palette/behaviour/session-buttons/), so a template can carry its own running order.

## Who can do what

Session tools follow the document's roles automatically. Anyone with an **Editor** pass (and the owner, and team members) can run the controls and take part: starting the timer, casting votes. Visitors on a **Participant** pass take part but never run anything: they cast dots and answer estimates, temperature checks, the Done check, quizzes and the idea box, while starting, revealing and clearing stay with editors. Visitors on a **Viewer** pass can still watch, they see the timer ticking and the tallies, but they cannot control the session or cast a dot, and they only get a tool's button in the bottom bar while it is running. Typically the facilitator holds an Editor pass, the team shares a Participant pass, and observers follow a Viewer pass.

**A vote is narrower still.** Edit access lets you cast dots, but only the person who _started_ the vote (or the facilitator) can end it, reveal the results, clear it, or drive the results walkthrough. Ending a vote can't be undone, so it isn't something a participant should be able to do by accident. See [Dot Voting](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/).

What a viewer actually sees during a vote also depends on its privacy settings: with **Hide running counts** on, nobody sees anyone else's dots until the results are revealed.

**Polls follow the same rule**: Participants and editors are asked and can answer; a Viewer only watches. To hear back from an audience, share a Participant pass. Starting and ending a poll still needs edit access.

## What does and does not get recorded

None of the tools adds to [Undo](/help/canvas/undo/): starting a timer, placing a dot or answering a poll is not the kind of thing you would want to Ctrl+Z. An [offline document](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) has no live room to share them with.

<Tip>
  Share a Participant pass with everyone who should vote, answer polls and add stickies, and a
  Viewer pass with anyone who only needs to watch. The roles do the gating for you, with no extra
  setup.
</Tip>
