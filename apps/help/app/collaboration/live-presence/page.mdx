Open a document with other people and it comes alive. Everyone sees each other move, select, and edit in real time, so it feels like working at the same whiteboard. Nothing needs setting up: as soon as two or more people are in a shared document, presence just appears.

## Live cursors

You see everyone else's pointer move around the canvas, each labelled with their name in their own colour. Cursors are tied to a tab, so you only see the cursors of the people looking at the same tab as you. When someone moves to a different tab, their cursor follows them there.

## Moving things together

When someone drags, resizes or reshapes something, you see it move as they do, with any arrows attached to it following along, and it settles where they let go. If they press Escape halfway, it simply goes back. Only people who can edit the document move things; someone on a view-only link can't.

## Who is here, and where

Each person is given a colour and a name. Signed-in people show their account name; everyone else gets a friendly auto-generated name like "Curious Otter". You can see who is around in two places:

- **On each tab**, small initials show who is currently looking at that tab, so you can tell at a glance where everyone is working.
- A coloured ring around someone shows whether they are **active**, **away** (after a few quiet minutes), or have gone **offline**.

## Collaborators

Click any avatar on a tab, or the **+N** badge when a tab is busy, to open **Collaborators**. It lists everyone in the document grouped by the tab they are on, so you can see where the whole room is at once rather than hovering tab by tab. The person you clicked is highlighted.

- Your own tab is marked **You're Here**. Every other tab has a **Go to Tab** button that takes you straight there.
- Each person shows their name, their role when it is known, whether they are online, away or offline, and how long ago they were last active.
- **Follow** beside someone makes your view follow theirs. See [Following someone](/help/canvas/follow-along/) for how it works.

## Seeing what others have selected

When someone selects an element, a small badge with their initials appears on it, so you always know what a teammate is working on. While they have it selected, that element is gently locked for everyone else, with a "Locked to (name)" note, so two people can not fight over the same shape at once. It frees up again the moment they click away.

## Shared laser pointer

The [Laser Pointer](/help/selection-modes/laser/) is built for presenting together: when you draw with it, everyone in the document sees your glowing trail in real time, then it fades away. It is perfect for "look over here" without changing anything on the canvas.

## Who can see presence

Live presence works for everyone in a shared document, including guests and people opening a [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) to view, not just signed-in editors. A private document you have not shared has no presence, since it is just you.

<Tip>
  Names and colours are chosen automatically, and a colour is nudged if two people happen to get the
  same one, so everyone stays easy to tell apart.
</Tip>
