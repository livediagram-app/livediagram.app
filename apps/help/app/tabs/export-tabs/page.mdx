**Export** downloads the active tab in the format you need, whether that is an image for a doc, a PDF to print, or a file to move the tab into another document.

## Opening Export

There are a few ways in, and they all open the same **Export Tab** dialog:

- The tab's menu (the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click the tab): choose **Export**.
- **Export…** in the [command palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/) (`Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `K`). This works on a view-only share link too, so viewers can download a copy.
- **Export** in the ⋯ menu at the top of the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).

To export only part of a tab, select several elements and press the **Export** button on the toolbar that appears above the selection. The dialog is then headed **Export Selection** and covers just those elements.

## Export formats

The dialog shows a card per format:

- **JSON**: a portable `.liveplanner-tab.json` snapshot that [imports](/help/tabs/import-tabs/) back with full fidelity. A tab with Plan boards or Plan cards carries their cards and any custom card types too, without votes or comments.
- **Markdown**: the tab's labelled elements and every connection between them as a Markdown outline (`.md`), a readable summary rather than a full copy: a flowchart reads as its flow, step to step. Each Plan board lists its columns and every card in them.
- **PNG**: a high-resolution image, good for slides, docs and chat.
- **SVG**: a crisp vector image that scales to any size and opens in design tools.
- **PDF**: a single-page PDF of the tab, ready to print or share.

The **text** formats (JSON, Markdown, and CSV on a Board or Sheet tab) each open an editor pre-filled with the tab in that format: tweak the text and press **Copy**, or download the file (**Download .json**, **Download .md** and so on). Your edits in that box never change the tab. The three **image** formats (PNG, SVG, PDF) open an options screen with a preview and a download button (see below). **All formats** at the top of either screen takes you back to the cards.

Files are named after the document and the tab, for example `Roadmap - Overview.png`.

## Board, Sheet and Dashboard Tabs

A [Plan](/help/canvas/plan-mode/) tab offers only the formats that suit it, and its images show it as it is laid out, not the canvas hidden underneath:

| Tab           | Formats                      |
| ------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Board tab     | PNG, SVG, PDF, Markdown, CSV |
| Sheet tab     | PNG, SVG, PDF, CSV           |
| Dashboard tab | PNG, SVG, PDF, JSON          |
| Form tab      | JSON                         |

**CSV** shows the board's cards or the Sheet's cells as comma-separated text you can copy, or save as a `.csv` with **Download .csv**.

- A **Board tab**'s image is its board: the header, each column with its count, and the cards in it. Its Markdown lists every column and its cards.
- A **Sheet tab**'s image is the sheet's cells as they show now, with its charts where they sit in the grid.
- A **Dashboard tab**'s image is the whole page, 1440 px wide: each slot drawn as a card holding a picture of what it shows (a board, a sheet, a diagram tab, a chart, a metric, a note, a checklist and so on). A Visualisation tab gives its one chart. Its JSON is the file a Dashboard's [Import](/help/tabs/import-tabs/) reads back.
- A **Form tab** exports only its JSON, which carries the whole form: the form isn't drawn on the canvas, so a picture of it would be blank.
- A plan view (a Gantt chart, a calendar, Cards by Field, Priority by Status, Card Search or a metric) on any tab's image is drawn as its chart, as it looks in the editor.

These images have no **Isometric view**, **Background pattern** or **Hidden layers** options: they are pages, not canvases.

## Image options

The PNG, SVG, and PDF exports show the tab as it looks. After you pick one, the options screen offers:

- **Isometric view** (off by default) tilts the export into the [isometric projection](/help/selection-modes/isometric-mode/).
- **Background pattern** (on by default) paints the tab's grid, dots or texture behind the diagram. Switch it off for a plain background.
- **Hidden layers** appears only when the tab has layers hidden and the [Layers panel](/help/canvas/layers/) is switched on in Settings, and chooses whether those layers are included. It is off by default, so what you hid stays out.

Set them and press the download button (**Download PNG**, **Download SVG** or **Download PDF**), and what you get matches what you see. The JSON and Markdown exports capture the underlying content rather than the picture.

## Exporting Pages

In [Pages mode](/help/canvas/pages-mode/) the dialog offers **PDF**, **PNG**, **SVG**, **GIF** and **JSON**, and the image formats export **pages** rather than the whole tab. Choose **All pages** or **One page**: a PDF of all pages has one PDF page each at its own size, and PNG or SVG of all pages comes as a **.zip** with one image per page. Each page comes out as exactly its sheet, background included, and page exports skip the **Isometric view** and **Background pattern** options. **GIF** makes one animated image of the pages, moving as they move on the canvas. When the tab has a logo page, a **Logo Kit** card also offers an SVG, PNGs from 16 to 1024 px and a favicon in one .zip. See [Exporting and Presenting Pages](/help/canvas/pages-mode/exporting-pages/).

<Tip>
  Reach for **SVG** when the image might be resized or printed large: it stays sharp at any scale,
  unlike a fixed-resolution PNG. For sharing a live, always-current view instead of a static file,
  see [Sharing and Embeds](/help/collaboration/sharing/).
</Tip>
