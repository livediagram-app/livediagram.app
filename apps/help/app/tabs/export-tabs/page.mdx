**Export** downloads the active tab in the format you need, whether that is an image for a doc, a PDF to print, or a file to move the tab into another document.

## Opening Export

There are a few ways in, and they all open the same **Export tab** dialog:

- The tab's menu (the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click the tab): expand **Content** and choose **Export**.
- **Export…** in the [command palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/) (`Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `K`). This works on a view-only share link too, so viewers can download a copy.
- **Export** in the ⋯ menu at the top of the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).

To export only part of a tab, select several elements and press the **Export** button on the toolbar that appears above the selection. The dialog is then headed **Export selection** and covers just those elements.

## Export formats

The dialog shows a card per format:

- **JSON**: a portable `.livediagram-tab.json` snapshot that [imports](/help/tabs/import-tabs/) back with full fidelity. A tab with Plan boards or Plan cards carries their cards and any custom card types too, without votes or comments.
- **Mermaid**: the tab as [Mermaid](https://mermaid.js.org/) flowchart text (`.mmd`), which keeps every connection and its style (unlike Markdown); frames export as `subgraph` blocks.
- **Markdown**: the tab's elements and connections as a Markdown outline (`.md`), a readable summary rather than a full copy. Each Plan board lists its columns and every card in them.
- **Excalidraw**: a `.excalidraw` scene you can open at [excalidraw.com](https://excalidraw.com/). Boxes, text, arrows (with their connections), and drawings carry over; element kinds Excalidraw doesn't have (charts, tables, device frames and so on) simplify to labelled boxes. A Plan board comes across as its columns and card faces.
- **PNG**: a high-resolution image, good for slides, docs and chat.
- **SVG**: a crisp vector image that scales to any size and opens in design tools.
- **PDF**: a single-page PDF of the tab, ready to print or share.

The four **text** formats (JSON, Mermaid, Markdown, Excalidraw) each open an editor pre-filled with the tab in that format: tweak the text and press **Copy**, or download the file (**Download .json**, **Download .mmd** and so on). Your edits in that box never change the tab. The three **image** formats (PNG, SVG, PDF) open an options screen with a preview and a download button (see below). **All formats** at the top of either screen takes you back to the cards.

Files are named after the document and the tab, for example `Roadmap - Overview.png`.

## Image options

The PNG, SVG, and PDF exports show the tab as it looks. After you pick one, the options screen offers:

- **Isometric view** (off by default) tilts the export into the [isometric projection](/help/selection-modes/isometric-mode/).
- **Background pattern** (on by default) paints the tab's grid, dots or texture behind the diagram. Switch it off for a plain background.
- **Hidden layers** appears only when the tab has layers hidden in the [Layers panel](/help/canvas/layers/), and chooses whether those layers are included. It is off by default, so what you hid stays out.

Set them and press the download button (**Download PNG**, **Download SVG** or **Download PDF**), and what you get matches what you see. The JSON, Mermaid, Markdown, and Excalidraw exports capture the underlying content rather than the picture.

## Exporting Illustrate Pages

In [Illustrate mode](/help/canvas/illustrate/) the dialog offers **PDF**, **PNG**, **SVG** and **JSON**, and the image formats export **pages** rather than the whole tab. Choose **All pages** or **One page**: a PDF of all pages has one PDF page each at its own size, and PNG or SVG of all pages comes as a **.zip** with one image per page. Each page comes out as exactly its sheet, background included. See [Exporting and Presenting Pages](/help/canvas/illustrate/exporting-pages/).

<Tip>
  Reach for **SVG** when the image might be resized or printed large: it stays sharp at any scale,
  unlike a fixed-resolution PNG. For sharing a live, always-current view instead of a static file,
  see [Sharing and Embeds](/help/collaboration/sharing/).
</Tip>
