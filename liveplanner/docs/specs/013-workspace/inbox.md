# Inbox

The Inbox is liveworkspace's: one view in the shared Explorer ([`@liveworkspace/explorer`](../../../../packages/explorer/README.md)), over liveworkspace's index, listing what is outstanding for the reader across every liveworkspace app. Its sections, each hidden when empty: **Assigned to You** (open actions, and open Plan cards whose Assignee is the reader), **Assigned by You**, **Form Responses** (forms with responses the reader hasn't seen, [Forms](../030-forms/forms.md#hearing-about-responses)) and **Open Comment Threads** (on elements and on Plan cards, marked **Mentioned you**, **On your plan** on the reader's own plan before they have commented, or **You replied**). The sidebar badge counts Assigned to You. The calls and types are in [one-explorer-contracts.md](../../../../liveworkspace/docs/one-explorer-contracts.md) "Inbox" and "What an item pushes"; the plan is [one-explorer.md](../../../../liveworkspace/docs/one-explorer.md).

What liveplanner does is feed it and open its rows:

- **Threads and actions.** A plan's Durable Object pushes each changed tab's threads and actions to the index (`PUT /app/items/:id/collab/:tabId`) with the tab's name, each thread's latest author colour, and each action's note and the names on it, on idle and at most once a minute, from its outbox (`apps/api/src/plan-room/plan-outbox.ts`, [API](../015-api/api.md) "Storage"). The comment text stays in the plan.
- **Forms.** The api pushes a form's heading, board, response count and latest response time (`PUT /app/items/:id/forms/:tabId`) when a response lands, the form is published, deleted or renamed, and each reader's seen count (`.../seen`) when they open the Form tab or publish (`apps/api/src/forms/form-inbox.ts`).
- **Cards.** Plan cards and their threads go under the tab id `~cards` with the card's number, type, title and board, pushed by the plan's Durable Object (`plan-room/plan-cards-push.ts`) whenever a card, a board's tab or the type catalogue changes, however the card was changed. A card names its assignee by a one-way hash; the plan names them to the index by matching it against the people it knows (its owner, the editors it has seen, everyone who commented on or was mentioned in a card; `@liveworkspace/index-app` `cardsCollabPush`). A card assigned to someone the plan has never seen write or comment is listed once they do.
- **This Browser.** Plans kept only in this browser never reach the index; the editor reads their actions, cards and threads from the offline store (`lib/offline/offline-activity.ts`) and hands them to the Explorer's `inbox` prop (`lib/explorer-inbox-host.tsx`), which lists them among the index's rows with the This Browser pill.
- **Opening a row.** The editor's deep link (`lib/collab-deep-link.ts`): `#t=<tab>&el=<element>&open=action|comments` for an action or a thread, `#t=<tab>&el=<board>&item=<card>` for a card on a board (`#item=<card>` when no board shows it), and `#t=<board tab>` for a form's responses. `lib/explorer-host.ts` `itemHref` builds them from the Explorer's `ItemPlace`.
- **Telemetry.** `Activity·Opened` (once a visit), `Activity·Selected` (`Action`, `Card`, `Thread`, `CardThread`, `FormResponses`) and `Activity·Loaded·Retry`, through the same host ([Telemetry](../017-telemetry/telemetry.md)).

## How a row reads

Every Inbox row, on the Explorer page and in the editor's Explorer panel, is one link drawn by one shared component (`packages/explorer/src/explorer/inbox/InboxRows.tsx`) at one of two densities: **comfortable** on the page, **compact** in the panel (tighter padding, a smaller avatar, shorter times). Top to bottom it reads:

- **Where**, as a quiet breadcrumb: the item's app mark (in the All filter), the team glyph for a team's item, the item's title, then the tab and the element or board. On the right, when it last changed, in a `<time>` element with the full date on hover.
- **What needs you**, in bold: the action's name, the card's number and title, the element a thread is on, or the form's heading. One line, the full text on hover.
- **The detail**, up to two lines: the latest comment quoted under its author's name, or the action's note.
- **Why it is here**, a chip that pairs an icon with its words, never colour alone: **Assigned to you** (followed by who assigned it), **Waiting on Bo** (work you assigned), **Mentioned you**, **On your {kind}** (the item is yours and you haven't replied), **You replied**, or **3 new responses**. Beside it the card's type glyph and label, the thread's comment count, and **This Browser** for an item only this browser holds.
- **Who**, as an avatar at the start: who assigned it to you, who you assigned it to, or who wrote the latest comment, in their presence colour when the app sends one (darkened until the initials read at AA, a quiet disc otherwise), with a small badge for the row's kind. A form's row shows the form glyph instead.

The row's accessible name is its title, why it is here and its item; the detail and the time describe it. Hover and keyboard focus tint the row at `duration-micro` (no motion under reduced motion), and on the page an Open arrow shows. A row only opens its place: the Inbox offers no Mark Done, and the index sends no read state for actions and threads, so rows carry no unread mark (a form's row counts the responses you haven't seen). Loading shows skeleton rows of the same shape. With nothing open, the Inbox says **You're all caught up**.
