# Timeline

**Moved to liveworkspace's index (October 2026).** The Timeline, the record of what happened across your projects, teams and account (`timeline_events`, `timeline_event_scopes`), is removed rather than moved, along with everything that read it: Explorer Home's What Happened, a project's History and a team's Team Activity, and the Home row's unread badge. Nothing in liveplanner or liveworkspace records it any more.

The plan is [one-explorer.md](../../../../liveworkspace/docs/one-explorer.md); the calls and types are in [one-explorer-contracts.md](../../../../liveworkspace/docs/one-explorer-contracts.md) "Inbox". The Explorer that shows it is the shared package, [`@liveworkspace/explorer`](../../../../packages/explorer/README.md).
