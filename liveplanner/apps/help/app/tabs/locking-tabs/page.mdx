When a tab is set up the way you want it, locking it guards the tab itself: nobody can delete it, clear it or import something over it by accident, and a Dashboard's layout stays exactly as you left it.

## Locking and unlocking a tab

Open the tab's menu (the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click the tab) and press the padlock button, **Lock Tab**, in the row of quick actions along the top. The same button reads **Unlock Tab** afterwards (and shows as pressed), so it toggles the tab back. A locked tab shows a small **padlock** before its name in the bar, so you can tell at a glance which tabs are locked.

The lock is part of the plan, so it applies to everyone who opens it, not just you. Anyone with edit access can unlock it again.

## What a locked tab protects

- The tab **can't be deleted** until you unlock it.
- In the tab menu, **Import** (or **Import CSV…**) and **Clear** are greyed out, and an import started from the command palette is refused.
- A **Dashboard** can't be changed: its rows, slots and what they show stay put, and you see it as a Viewer does, without its settings or editing.
- The **Undo** and **Redo** buttons are greyed out on the tab.

You can still **rename**, **duplicate** or **export** the tab from the same menu, and move it into a folder. Duplicating gives you an unlocked copy if you want to keep building from a locked original.

## What a lock leaves alone

The cards on a board and the cells of a sheet belong to the plan, not to one tab, so a lock doesn't freeze them: people can still add, edit and move cards on a locked Board tab, and type into a locked Sheet. A lock protects the tab, not the work flowing through it.

To stop someone changing anything at all, share a [view-only link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) instead.

## When to use it

- Freeze a Dashboard you've set up for a team or a review, so its layout can't be knocked out of place.
- Protect a tab you rely on from being deleted or cleared by accident.
- Hand a plan to collaborators with its set-up tabs locked, leaving the work on them free to move.

<Tip>
  Need a fresh start from a locked tab? **Duplicate** it from the tab menu: the copy is unlocked and
  ready to change.
</Tip>
