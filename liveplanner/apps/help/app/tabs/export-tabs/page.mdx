**Export** downloads the tab you are on in the format you need, whether that is a CSV for a spreadsheet, an image for a slide, a PDF to print, or a file to move a dashboard into another plan.

## Opening Export

There are a few ways in, and they all open the same **Export Tab** dialog:

- The tab's menu (the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click the tab): choose **Export**.
- **Export…** in the [command palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/) (`Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `K`). This works on a view-only share link too, so viewers can download a copy.
- **Export** in the ⋯ menu at the top of the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).

## Formats for each kind of tab

The dialog shows a card for each format that suits the tab:

| Tab           | Formats                      |
| ------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Board tab     | Markdown, CSV, PNG, SVG, PDF |
| Sheet tab     | CSV, PNG, SVG, PDF           |
| Dashboard tab | JSON, PNG, SVG, PDF          |
| Form tab      | JSON                         |

- **CSV**: a Board's cards or a Sheet's cells as comma-separated text, for a spreadsheet. A board's CSV reads back in with [Import CSV…](/help/tabs/import-tabs/). A value a spreadsheet would run as a formula is made safe with a leading `'`.
- **Markdown**: a Board's columns and every card in them, as a readable outline (`.md`).
- **JSON**: a `.liveplanner-tab.json` file that [imports](/help/tabs/import-tabs/) back with full fidelity. A Dashboard's JSON carries its rows, slots and what they hold; a Form's carries the whole form.
- **PNG**: a high-resolution image, good for slides, reports and chat.
- **SVG**: a crisp vector image that scales to any size and opens in design tools.
- **PDF**: a single-page PDF of the tab, ready to print or share.

The **text** formats (CSV, Markdown, JSON) each open an editor filled with the tab in that format: tweak the text and press **Copy**, or download the file (**Download .csv**, **Download .md** and so on). Your edits in that box never change the tab. The **image** formats (PNG, SVG, PDF) open a preview with a download button (**Download PNG**, **Download SVG** or **Download PDF**). **All formats** at the top of either screen takes you back to the cards.

Files are named after the plan and the tab, for example `Roadmap - Overview.png`.

## What the images show

Each image shows the tab as it is laid out:

- A **Board tab**'s image is its board: the header, each column with its count, and the cards in it.
- A **Sheet tab**'s image is the sheet's cells as they show now, with its charts where they sit in the grid.
- A **Dashboard tab**'s image is the whole page, 1440 px wide: each slot drawn as a card holding a picture of what it shows (a board, a sheet, a chart, a metric, a note, a checklist and so on).
- A Gantt chart, a calendar or another [visualisation](/help/plan/visualisations/) is drawn as its chart, as it looks in the editor.

A **Form tab** exports only its JSON: a picture of a form wouldn't tell you much, and the JSON carries the whole thing.
