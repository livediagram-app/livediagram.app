Sometimes a tab you built in one plan belongs in another too: a dashboard layout two teams both use, or an intake form two plans share. **Add to Plan** adds the active tab to another plan you own, without leaving the editor.

## Adding a tab to another plan

<Step n={1}>Open the tab's menu (the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click the tab).</Step>
<Step n={2}>Choose **Add to Plan**.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  In the **Add to Plan** dialog, pick the destination. Each plan shows as a tile with a
  preview, and the filter box at the top narrows a long list. A message confirms when the tab has
  been added.
</Step>

The option is greyed out on a **Board** or **Sheet** tab, which work with this plan's cards and stay in it. It is also greyed out when there is nowhere to add the tab to: if this is your only plan, until you create another, and in a [plan in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/). It isn't offered to Viewers or Participants, or on a link shared for [one tab](/help/collaboration/sharing/one-tab/).

## One tab, shared between plans

Add to Plan doesn't make a copy: it is the same tab in both places. Change its layout, restyle it, or rename it from either plan and the change lands in the other as well. If the other plan is already open in another window, reload it to see the latest edits.

A Dashboard slot that shows another of this plan's tabs reads as deleted in the other plan, since that tab isn't there; clear the slot or fill it again.

Two things stay per-plan, so each plan can organise the shared tab its own way: the tab's position in the tab bar, and which [tab folder](/help/tabs/tab-folders/) (if any) it sits in.

Removing the tab from one plan doesn't touch the others; the tab and its contents are only deleted for good when you remove it from the last plan containing it. Deleting a whole plan works the same way: a tab it shares stays, with its history, in every other plan it is in, and the delete confirmation tells you how many tabs that is before you go ahead.

<Tip>
  Prefer an independent copy you can tailor per plan? Use **Duplicate** in the same menu first,
  then add the duplicate to the other plan.
</Tip>
