Sometimes a tab you built in one plan belongs in another too: a dashboard layout two teams both use, or an intake form two plans share. **Add to Plan** copies the active tab into another plan you own, without leaving the editor.

## Adding a tab to another plan

<Step n={1}>Open the tab's menu (the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click the tab).</Step>
<Step n={2}>Choose **Add to Plan**.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  In the **Add to Plan** dialog, pick the destination. Each plan shows as a tile with a preview, and
  the filter box at the top narrows a long list. A message confirms when the tab has been added.
</Step>

The option is greyed out on a **Board** or **Sheet** tab, which work with this plan's cards and stay in it. It is also greyed out when there is nowhere to add the tab to: if this is your only plan, until you create another, and in a [plan in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/). It isn't offered to Viewers or Participants, or on a link shared for [one tab](/help/collaboration/sharing/one-tab/).

## A copy, not a shared tab

Add to Plan puts a copy of the tab in the other plan, as it is at that moment. From then on the two tabs are separate: change, rename or delete either one and the other stays as it was. In the other plan the copy sits outside any [tab folder](/help/tabs/tab-folders/).

A Dashboard slot that shows another of this plan's tabs reads as deleted in the other plan, since that tab isn't there; clear the slot or fill it again.

<Tip>Want a second copy in the same plan instead? Use **Duplicate** in the same menu.</Tip>
