A plan is a collection of **tabs**. Each tab holds exactly one thing: a **Board** of cards in columns, a **Sheet** of cells and formulas, a **Dashboard** of metrics and charts, or a **Form** whose answers become cards. A new plan starts with one tab, and you add more whenever a piece of work deserves its own space. The tab bar runs along the bottom of the editor, and the tab you are on fills the space above it.

## What a tab is

Common ways people split a plan across tabs:

- A **Board** for the day-to-day work, and a **Dashboard** beside it that sums it up.
- A **Sheet** for the budget or the numbers behind the plan.
- A **Form** for requests, whose answers land as cards on a board.

The cards on a plan's boards belong to the plan rather than to one tab, so two boards can show the same cards, and a Dashboard can chart them. See [Planning Basics](/help/plan/planning-basics/).

The small icon at the start of a tab's name shows what it holds: a Board, Sheet, Dashboard or Form.

## Adding a tab

Press the **+** button at the end of the tab bar, or run **Create New Tab** from the [command palette](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/command-palette/) (`Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `K`). The new tab:

- is named `Tab 2`, `Tab 3` and so on, after the number of tabs in the plan;
- takes the look of the tab you were on, so it matches the work around it;
- opens on **Start Planning**, where you pick what it holds: a board, sheet, form, visualisation or dashboard, from a template or blank. Picking one usually names the tab too, such as "Kanban" for a Kanban board.

**Duplicate** in the tab menu makes a copy instead: it is named after the original with "copy" on the end, sits right after it, and is a separate tab from then on.

## Naming and reordering tabs

Rename any tab whenever you like:

- **Double-click** the active tab's name to edit it inline. Enter or clicking away saves, Escape cancels, and an empty name keeps the old one.
- **Rename** in the tab menu (and **Rename Tab** in the command palette) opens the same editor.

Tab names are capped at **60 characters**. Runs of spaces and line breaks collapse to one, and anything longer is cut at the last whole word with an ellipsis.

Drag a tab left or right to reorder it. A blue bar shows where it will land: drop on the left half of a tab to land before it, the right half to land after it. Reordering only moves the tab in the list, it never touches what is on any tab.

Drag a tab up off the bar to the **right edge** of the screen instead, and it opens beside the tab you're on: see [Side by Side Tabs](/help/tabs/side-by-side/).

## The tab menu

Click the **⋯** button on the active tab, or right-click any tab (which switches to it first), to open its menu. On a phone it opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen.

Along the top sits a row of quick actions: **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Paste** (greyed out until you have copied something), **Side by Side** (on a large enough window, with another tab to sit beside; **Close Side by Side** while two tabs are open), **Lock Tab** and **Delete**. Below them, everything else is a full-width row, in groups split by a line:

- **Add to Folder** and **Add to Plan**.
- **Import** (**Import CSV…** on a Board or Sheet tab), **Export**, and **Clear**, which empties the tab after a confirmation (Undo brings it back).

## Deleting a tab

Choose **Delete** in the tab menu. The tab goes straight away, and **Undo** brings it back. Once you reload or leave the plan it is gone (a copy you made with [Add to Plan](/help/tabs/add-to-project/) is a separate tab and stays), so export or duplicate anything you might want later. A few things to know:

- Delete asks first only when it does something Undo can't take back: a [form](/help/plan/forms/)'s tab deletes the form's link with it, and a board or sheet that forms send their responses to stops them taking responses.
- A plan always keeps at least one tab, so Delete is greyed out on the last one.
- A [locked tab](/help/tabs/locking-tabs/) can't be deleted until you unlock it.
- If the tab is also in [another plan](/help/tabs/add-to-project/), it is only removed from this one.

## Share links

On a share link for a Viewer or a Participant, and on any link scoped to one tab, you can still click between tabs, but the **+** button, the tab menu, renaming and drag-to-reorder are all hidden. Only an Editor with a link to the whole plan changes its tabs.

## More with tabs

- **[Tab Folders](/help/tabs/tab-folders/)** group related tabs under one named chip so a long tab bar stays readable.
- **[Side by Side Tabs](/help/tabs/side-by-side/)** puts two tabs on screen at once, each live.
- **[Add a Tab to Another Plan](/help/tabs/add-to-project/)** copies a Dashboard or Form tab into a different plan, where it is a separate tab.
- **[Importing](/help/tabs/import-tabs/)** and **[Exporting](/help/tabs/export-tabs/)** move a tab's contents in and out as a file.
- **[Locking a Tab](/help/tabs/locking-tabs/)** stops a tab being deleted, cleared or imported over, and freezes a Dashboard's layout.
