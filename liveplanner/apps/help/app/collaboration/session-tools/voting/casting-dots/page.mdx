While a [vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/) is open, every card the tab's board shows carries a small **− 0 +** counter in its corner.

## Spending and taking back

Press **+** to add one of your dots and **−** to take one back. Stacking several dots on one card is allowed, each counting separately, unless the vote was started with **One each** under **Dots per item**: then each of your dots must go on a different card. For an Editor, the **Vote** button in the bottom-right cluster keeps count: its badge shows how many dots you have left, and the Vote panel above it says so in words, like "2 of 3 dots left".

The counter starts at zero, so you can always see which cards are in play and how many dots each has. **−** greys out when none of the dots on it are yours, and **+** once your budget is spent (or, under **One each**, once you have a dot on that card), rather than disappearing, so the buttons stay where your pointer expects them.

The rest of the card keeps working during a vote: click it to open its panel, or drag it to another column. Once the vote ends the buttons go and the counter becomes a plain tally, since at that point it is a result to read rather than something to change.

## What can take a dot

The **cards** take the dots. The board itself takes none, so you can still work the board during the vote. A card shown on two boards is one card, with one count. A card face down on a board that hides writing takes no dots until it is revealed.

When the host ends the vote, each card keeps the dots it gathered as its **Votes**, which the card's ▲ count and a board's **Top Voted** widget read afterwards.

Casting needs an Editor or Participant role: someone on a Viewer share link sees the tallies but can't add a dot.
