**Show results** starts a guided walkthrough of every card that received dots in a [vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/), most votes first.

## Stepping through the picks

The current card pulses an amber highlight, while the top of the [Vote panel](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-panel/) shows where you are and how many dots it got ("Top result 1 of 4 · 5 votes"), with **Previous** and **Next** buttons to step through in order. The panel opens above the **Vote** button for whoever runs the walkthrough, and the button's dot turns amber while it runs.

<Figure caption="The Vote panel drives the walkthrough above the ranked list of every card that got a dot.">
  <VoteResultsWalkthrough />
</Figure>

The walkthrough is shared: the person who started the vote drives it, and everyone else sees the same card highlighted. Their Vote panel shows the same readout without the buttons, and says "The host is walking through the results." That is the point of reviewing together, and it means "everyone look at this one" actually works.

## The ranked list

The [Vote panel](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/vote-panel/) switches to the **ranked list** at the same moment: every card that got a dot, by its title and number, most votes first, with the winners flagged.

If you started the vote, click any row to move the room straight to that card instead of stepping through in order. Everyone else sees the same list and follows along.

## Finishing

On the last pick, Next becomes **Done**. Pressing it ends the walkthrough and clears the whole vote session: the tally pills, the highlights and the Vote panel all go together, leaving the board clean for whatever comes next. Each card keeps the dots it gathered as its **Votes**.
