A team is a named group of signed-in people. Teams belong to your liveworkspace account, so a team is the same in every liveworkspace app, and you make and run them on [liveworkspace.app](https://liveworkspace.app/account/teams). Each member has a role, either Admin or Member, and every team gets a shared library that all members can open and edit. Teams are not a paid tier: liveplanner is free, so the roles are about who may manage the team, not about unlocking features.

## What a team is

A team has a name and a list of members. To create one, choose **Manage Teams** at the bottom of the Explorer sidebar's **Spaces** group, which opens your teams on liveworkspace.app. The person who creates a team becomes its first Admin.

Teams need a signed-in account because they are keyed to your account identity and invites are matched by email. liveplanner itself always works without an account, and a share link can still be handed to a guest who edits without signing in.

In the Explorer sidebar, every team you have joined is a space under **Spaces**, beside My Work, with **Manage Teams** as the last row. liveplanner learns your teams from your sign-in, so a team you join on liveworkspace.app shows up once your sign-in next refreshes, within the hour.

## Roles and invites

There are two roles. **Admins** can invite people, change roles, rename the team, remove members, and delete the team, all on liveworkspace.app. **Members** can see the team and its members, work in the shared library, and leave. A team always keeps an Admin. See [Roles and Invites](/help/collaboration/teams/roles-and-invites/) for the details.

## The shared library

Every team has its own folder tree, holding items from every liveworkspace app. Every joined member can open and edit everything in it, and create, rename, move, share and delete what is there. See [Team Shared Plans](/help/collaboration/teams/team-shared-projects/).

liveplanner does not yet list a team's members inside a plan, so [mentions](/help/collaboration/comments/) and card assignees offer no teammates to pick from for now.

## Leaving or deleting a team

Leaving a team, or deleting it, happens on liveworkspace.app. Once you have left, you lose access to the team's library within a few minutes, including any of its plans you have open.
