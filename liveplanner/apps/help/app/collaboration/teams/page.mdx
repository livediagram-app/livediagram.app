A team is a named group of signed-in people. Teams belong to your liveworkspace account, so a team is the same in every liveworkspace app, and you make and run them on [liveworkspace.app](https://liveworkspace.app/account/teams). Each member has a role, either Admin or Member, and every team gets a shared library of projects that all members can open and edit. Teams are not a paid tier: liveplanner is free, so the roles are about who may manage the team, not about unlocking features.

## What a team is

A team has a name (up to 80 characters) and a list of members. To create one, choose **Manage Teams** at the bottom of the Explorer sidebar's **Spaces** group, which opens your teams on liveworkspace.app. The person who creates a team becomes its first Admin. A team is not tied to a single owner, so it carries on even if its creator later leaves.

Teams need a signed-in account because they are keyed to your account identity and invites are matched by email. liveplanner itself always works without an account, and a share link can still be handed to a guest who edits without signing in.

In the Explorer sidebar, every team you have joined is a space under **Spaces**, beside My Work, with **Manage Teams** as the last row. Open a team to see its page: the member list and the shared library, with **Manage on liveworkspace.app** at the top for Admins. A team you join on liveworkspace.app shows up here within a few minutes, usually at once.

## Roles and invites

There are two roles. **Admins** can invite people, change roles, rename the team, remove members, and delete the team, all on liveworkspace.app. **Members** can see the team and its members, work in the shared library, and leave. The last remaining Admin cannot leave or be demoted, so a team always has someone in charge. Promote another member first, or delete the team.

You add people by email address on liveworkspace.app. See [Roles and Invites](/help/collaboration/teams/roles-and-invites/) for the full handshake.

## The shared library

Every team has its own folder tree of projects, shown as a **Team projects** card on the team page. Any joined member can create, rename, duplicate, move, and delete projects there, so the work belongs to the whole team rather than to one person. Team projects also turn up in your Recent list, marked **Team**. See [Team Shared Projects](/help/collaboration/teams/team-shared-projects/) for how placement and ownership work.

Inside a team project, members can [mention](/help/collaboration/comments/#mentioning-a-teammate) each other in comments and [assign cards](/help/plan/cards/) to each other.

## Leaving or deleting a team

- Leaving a team (on liveworkspace.app) takes you out. Projects you created in the team library stay with the team (another member, the longest-standing Admin, takes them over), and if you have one of its projects open, you lose access straight away.
- Deleting a team (Admins only, on liveworkspace.app) removes the team and its member list for everyone. Its projects are not lost: each one moves back to the root of its owner's My Work.
