Teams belong to your liveworkspace account, so a team is the same in every liveworkspace app, liveplanner included. You make and run them in liveplanner's Explorer: **New Team** at the bottom of the sidebar's **Spaces** group makes one, and a team's card, at the top of its view (or behind **Members** in the editor's Explorer panel), runs it. Everything here needs a signed-in account.

## Admin and Member

The person who creates a team is its first **Admin**. On the team's card, Admins can:

- rename the team (**Edit Team** on its ⋯ menu),
- invite people by email,
- change a member's role,
- remove a member or withdraw an invite,
- turn on a join link (**Invite by Link**),
- delete the team.

**Members** can see the team and its member list, work in the shared library, and leave the team.

A team always keeps an Admin: the last Admin can't step down or leave while others remain.

## Inviting by email

<Step n={1}>
  Open your team in the Explorer, enter the person's email address at the foot of its card, choose
  Member or Admin, and press **Invite**.
</Step>
<Step n={2}>
  They get an email from liveworkspace with a link to the Explorer's **Invites**, where the invitation waits.
  They don't need an account yet: it waits until they sign up with that address.
</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  They press **Accept** (which opens the team) or **Decline** there. Accepting takes a signed-in
  account whose verified email address is the one invited. The email's own decline link works
  without an account.
</Step>

Once they've joined, the team shows up in their Explorer when their sign-in next refreshes, within the hour; signing out and back in brings it at once.
