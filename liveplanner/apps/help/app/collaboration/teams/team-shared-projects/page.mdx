Every team has a shared library: a folder tree plus projects that all joined members can see and manage. It works just like your personal folder tree, only shared. It appears as a **Team projects** card on the team page in the Explorer.

## What everyone can do

A team project belongs to the whole team, not just to the person who made it. Any **joined** member can:

- create a new project or folder in the library,
- open and edit every project,
- rename, duplicate, and delete a project,
- re-folder a project within the team, or move it to another team they belong to.

Deleting a team project moves it to the team's Trash, which every joined member can see in the Trash (the **More** group of the Explorer sidebar, or **Open Trash** in Settings) and restore from. See [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/).

Joined membership grants edit access to every project in the library. There are no per-folder or per-member permissions inside a team: everyone who has joined can manage everything. People with a pending, un-accepted invite get nothing until they accept.

A few things stay with the project's **owner** (the person who created it, or who moved it in): creating [share links](/help/collaboration/sharing/) and setting a share password. Other members open the project directly from the library and don't need a link.

## How projects are placed

A project lives in exactly one place: your personal tree, or one team's library. Within a team, a project sits either in a folder or at the team's root, listed beside its folders.

- **Moving a project into a team** files it in that team's library and removes it from your personal lists. Only the project's owner can move a personal project into a team.
- **Moving a project out of a team** into your own personal library transfers ownership to you, the mover, since personal folders are tied to their owner. (If the owner is the one moving it out, ownership stays with them.)
- **Moving a project to another team** whose members don't include its owner also transfers ownership to you, so the project never stays owned by someone outside the team that holds it.
- **Re-foldering within the team** can be done by any joined member.

**Change Folder** opens a browser of every place you can file a project: pick a space first (**My Work**, or one of your teams), then browse into the folder you want, so you can file a project anywhere you have access from one place. A project already in a folder opens the picker where it is, that folder selected. On a team's own page the picker opens on that team, with My Work and your other teams one step back, under **All spaces**, so a team project can always be moved back to your personal tree.

## When someone leaves

When a member leaves the team or is removed, the projects and folders they created in the library stay with the team: ownership passes to a remaining member (the longest-standing Admin). If they have one of the team's projects open at the time, they lose access straight away.

## When a team is deleted

Deleting a team never destroys anyone's work. Its projects are re-homed to the root of their owners' My Work, and the team's folders are dropped. Each member keeps the projects they own.

A project in a team library shows a **Team** badge in the editor instead of "Private", since every joined member can open it. If you also share that project with a link, it reads **Shared** as normal: share links and team membership are separate, independent grants.

</Tip>
