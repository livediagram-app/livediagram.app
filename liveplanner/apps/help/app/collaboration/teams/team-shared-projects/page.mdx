Every team has a shared library: a folder tree plus plans that all joined members can see and manage. It works just like your personal folder tree, only shared. It appears as a **Team plans** card on the team page in the Explorer.

## What everyone can do

A team plan belongs to the whole team, not just to the person who made it. Any **joined** member can:

- create a new plan or folder in the library,
- open and edit every plan,
- rename, duplicate, and delete a plan,
- re-folder a plan within the team, or move it to another team they belong to.

Deleting a team plan moves it to the team's Trash, which every joined member can see in the Trash (the **More** group of the Explorer sidebar, or **Open Trash** in Settings) and restore from. See [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/).

Joined membership grants edit access to every plan in the library. There are no per-folder or per-member permissions inside a team: everyone who has joined can manage everything. People with a pending, un-accepted invite get nothing until they accept.

A few things stay with the plan's **owner** (the person who created it, or who moved it in): creating [share links](/help/collaboration/sharing/) and setting a share password. Other members open the plan directly from the library and don't need a link.

## How plans are placed

A plan lives in exactly one place: your personal tree, or one team's library. Within a team, a plan sits either in a folder or at the team's root, listed beside its folders.

- **Moving a plan into a team** files it in that team's library and removes it from your personal lists. Only the plan's owner can move a personal plan into a team.
- **Moving a plan out of a team** into your own personal library transfers ownership to you, the mover, since personal folders are tied to their owner. (If the owner is the one moving it out, ownership stays with them.)
- **Moving a plan to another team** whose members don't include its owner also transfers ownership to you, so the plan never stays owned by someone outside the team that holds it.
- **Re-foldering within the team** can be done by any joined member.

**Change Folder** opens a browser of every place you can file a plan: pick a space first (**My Work**, or one of your teams), then browse into the folder you want, so you can file a plan anywhere you have access from one place. A plan already in a folder opens the picker where it is, that folder selected. On a team's own page the picker opens on that team, with My Work and your other teams one step back, under **All spaces**, so a team plan can always be moved back to your personal tree.

## When someone leaves

When a member leaves the team or is removed, the plans and folders they created in the library stay with the team: ownership passes to a remaining member (the longest-standing Admin). If they have one of the team's plans open at the time, they lose access straight away.

## When a team is deleted

Deleting a team never destroys anyone's work. Its plans are re-homed to the root of their owners' My Work, and the team's folders are dropped. Each member keeps the plans they own.

A plan in a team library shows a **Team** badge in the editor instead of "Private", since every joined member can open it. If you also share that plan with a link, it reads **Shared** as normal: share links and team membership are separate, independent grants.
