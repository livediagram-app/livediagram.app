A share link is a standing invitation: anyone holding the URL keeps the link's level for as long as it lives. A lifetime bounds that, so a link stops working on its own without you having to remember to revoke it. It complements the [share password](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/).

## Choosing a lifetime

When you create a link in the **Share** dialog, **Valid** sets its lifetime, beside **Create Pass**. You can pick:

- **Forever**, the default: the link lasts until you revoke it.
- **1 week**
- **1 month**
- **6 months**

The countdown starts when you create the link. Pick the shortest span that fits, like a week for a workshop group or a month for a plan review.

Each pass in the dialog's **Passes** list shows how long it has left, like "6d left", or **Forever**.

## When a link runs out

When a link's lifetime runs out, it stops opening the plan everywhere: the share URL, any [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) and [live image](/help/collaboration/sharing/live-image/) made from it, and the realtime room. People who already have the plan open lose access within a few minutes. If you need someone out straight away, revoke the link instead.

## Extending a link

A link that has run out moves to the dialog's **Expired** list. Press **Extend** on it (**Extend 1 week**, say) to open it again for another round of the lifetime it was issued with, counted from now. The link keeps its address, so anyone who already has it can use it again.

To get rid of an expired link for good, press its bin. A live pass is stopped with its bin (**Revoke link**).
