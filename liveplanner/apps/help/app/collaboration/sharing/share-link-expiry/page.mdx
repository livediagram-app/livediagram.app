A share link is a standing invitation: anyone holding the URL keeps the link's level for as long as it lives. A lifetime bounds that, so a link stops working on its own without you having to remember to turn it off. It complements the [share password](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/).

## Choosing a lifetime

When you create a link in the **Share** dialog, **Lasts** sets its lifetime, beside **Create and Copy Link**. You can pick:

- **Forever**, the default: the link lasts until you turn it off.
- **1 week**
- **1 month**
- **6 months**

The countdown starts when you create the link. Pick the shortest span that fits, like a week for a workshop group or a month for a plan review.

Each link in the dialog's **Links** list shows how long it has left, like "6d left", or **Forever**.

## When a link runs out

When a link's lifetime runs out, it stops opening the plan everywhere: the share URL, any [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) and [live image](/help/collaboration/sharing/live-image/) made from it, and the realtime room. People who already have the plan open lose access within a few minutes. If you need someone out straight away, turn the link off instead.

## Extending a link

A link that has run out stays in the dialog's **Links** list, marked **Expired**. Press **Extend** on it, or on a link that is still counting down, to give it more time. The link keeps its address, so anyone who already has it can use it again.

To get rid of a link for good, press the bin (**Turn off link**).
