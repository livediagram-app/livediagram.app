Sharing turns a plan into something other people can open. A share link lets anyone with the URL join your plan in real time, with live cursors and edits flowing between everyone. You can protect your links with a password, give a link a lifetime, or embed the plan in another page. None of this needs the other person to sign in: a share link can be handed to a guest who edits without an account, and you can create links as a guest too.

## Opening the Share dialog

Press **Share** at the top right of the editor. You can also reach it from the [command palette](/help/tips-and-tricks/command-palette/) or from **Share** in a plan's menu in the Explorer.

The plan's owner, and every member of the [team](/help/collaboration/teams/) whose library holds it, can manage its links. Someone who opened your plan through a link can't.

## Creating a link

The dialog opens on a new link. Choose what it lets people do from the three levels across the top:

- **Viewer**: watches along. Can't comment or change a thing. They can open cards to read them.
- **Participant**: comments, votes and works items, but can't reshape the plan. They add cards and edit their fields, move cards between columns, write in Sheet cells, [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) and cast dots in a [vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/). They cannot change a board's columns, card types or settings, format a Sheet, or add, rename, reorder or delete tabs. It is the level to hand a team for a retrospective or a planning session.
- **Editor**: works with you in real time, changing anything you can.

Below the levels, **Opens** chooses whether the link opens **Every tab** or only one ([Sharing One Tab](/help/collaboration/sharing/one-tab/)); it shows when the plan has tabs to choose from. **Lasts** chooses how long the link works: **Forever**, **1 week**, **1 month** or **6 months** ([Share Link Expiry](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-link-expiry/)).

Press **Create and Copy Link**. The link is made and copied in one go, ready to paste.

A plan can have several links at once, so you can hand an Editor link to a co-facilitator, a Participant link to your team and a Viewer link to everyone else. Whoever opens a link joins the same realtime room, and the plan lands in their [Shared With Me](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) list for next time.

## Your links

Under **Links**, each link shows its level, its address and how long it has left. On each one you can:

- change its level with the level menu, and which tabs it opens with the tab menu, at any time; the address stays the same;
- press **Extend** on a link with a lifetime to give it more time;
- press **Copy** to copy its address again;
- press the bin (**Turn off link**) to stop it working.

Turning a link off takes effect at once: anyone in the plan through it is sent out, and the address stops opening it. It can't be turned back on; make a new link if you change your mind, which gives a new address.

Changes to a link's level, tabs or password reach the people already using it within a few minutes.

## A password for every link

At the foot of the dialog, **Set Password** puts one password on every link to the plan ([Share Passwords](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/)). [Share Link Security](/help/privacy-and-security/share-link-security/) explains what a link does and does not protect.

## Embedding a plan

An [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) puts a live copy of a plan inside another page, like a wiki, a doc or Notion. Where an `<iframe>` isn't allowed, like a GitHub README, a [live image](/help/collaboration/sharing/live-image/) shows a picture of the plan instead. Both are made from a share link's address.

<Note>
  A [plan in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) lives only in your browser, so
  there is nothing on the server to share yet. Opening **Share** on one shows a gate instead of the
  link controls, with a button that uploads it first.
</Note>
