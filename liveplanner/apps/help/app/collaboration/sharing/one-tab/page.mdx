A share link normally opens the whole plan, every tab of it. Sometimes you only want to show one: the roadmap to a client, but not the pricing tab next to it. A link scoped to one tab does exactly that, without copying the tab into a new plan.

## Scoping a new link

When a plan has more than one tab, the **Share** dialog gains an **Opens** dropdown just above **Valid**. Leave it on **All tabs** for an ordinary link, or pick the tab you want to share (tabs are listed in tab bar order), then **Create Pass**. The role still applies: a one-tab link can be an **Editor**, **Participant** or **Viewer** pass.

<Figure caption="On a plan with several tabs, Opens picks which tab a new pass opens, or All tabs.">
  <ShareOpensRow />
</Figure>

You can make as many links as you like, each scoped differently: one tab to one person, another tab to someone else, and an All-tabs link for your team. A link opens one tab or all of them; it can't open a chosen handful.

## What the visitor sees

Someone opening a one-tab link lands straight on that tab, whichever tab the plan was last left on. The other tabs stay in their tab bar as **Not shared** pills, so they know the plan has more, but without names or folders, and nothing happens when they press one (hovering says "This tab isn't shared with you"). A search result or keyboard shortcut that points at another tab only shows the message "That tab isn't shared with you".

<Figure caption="A visitor on a one-tab link: the shared Roadmap tab, with the rest of the plan shown as Not shared pills.">
  <OneTabShare />
</Figure>

The other tabs are private, not just hidden. Their content never reaches the visitor's browser: not their boards, Sheets or forms, not their names, not their comments, not their live edits. Cards are kept to the tab too: the visitor gets only the cards their tab shows, never the rest of the plan's cards. With an **Editor** pass the visitor can change their tab, but not rename, add, reorder, lock or delete tabs, use tab folders, or rename the plan.

The scope follows the link everywhere it goes:

- The **Copy** button in the header makes the visitor a copy of their tab only.
- An [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) of the link shows its tab only.
- A [live image](/help/collaboration/sharing/live-image/) of the link always shows its tab, so its menu has no **Tab** picker.

## Changing a link's tabs

On a plan with more than one tab, every pass in the Share dialog shows which tabs it opens, beside **Opens**. Change it there at any time: widen a one-tab link to **All tabs**, narrow an All-tabs link to one tab, or move it to another tab. The link's URL stays the same, and anyone using it right now reloads into the new choice.

<Tip>
  Deleting the tab a link is scoped to deletes that link too. Anyone using it leaves the plan,
  just as if you had revoked it.
</Tip>
