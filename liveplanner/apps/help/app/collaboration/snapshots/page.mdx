A snapshot keeps the whole plan as it is right now, so you can try a big change (a reorganisation, an import, a clean-up with a teammate or an AI assistant) knowing you can put everything back in one step.

## Taking a snapshot

Snapshots live in the plan's **History**. Open **Tools** in the footer and choose **History**, then press **Take Snapshot** (the camera) at its foot. In **Take a Snapshot**, give it a name if you like, such as "Before the workshop", and press **Take Snapshot**. Without a name it is called by the time it was taken.

Each snapshot sits on the History timeline at the time it was taken, marked **Snapshot**, among the plan's other changes. It shows its name, who took it, how long ago and its size. The latest **five** are kept: when five are there, **Take a Snapshot** says which one taking another will remove, and the oldest goes.

If you came in through an Editor share link, History has no timeline for you, so it shows the snapshots on their own.

## Reverting to a snapshot

Press **Revert** on a snapshot's row and confirm. The plan goes back to exactly how it was: every tab, its card types, every card (each with its number) and every Sheet. Comment threads and Automations rules stay as they are, and so do the plan's name and who it is shared with. A card that was deleted since comes back with its old number; a card made since goes.

Reverting replaces everyone's work since that snapshot, so first it takes a snapshot of the plan as it is now, badged **Before revert**. If you change your mind, revert to that one.

Everyone who has the plan open sees it reload onto the reverted version straight away, so nobody keeps working on what was replaced.

## Deleting a snapshot

Press the bin on a snapshot's row. It goes at once, with no question, and a deleted snapshot can't be brought back.

## Who can use snapshots

Editors of the whole plan take, revert and delete snapshots. Participants, Viewers and people with a link to one tab don't see them. Snapshots are there on plans stored on the server: a plan kept in **This Browser** has none, and AI assistants and scripts using an API token can't use them.
