liveplanner is for people who plan work **together**. It shines when a team shares one project and everyone sees the same boards and sheets change in [real time](/help/collaboration/live-presence/). It works just as well for one person keeping track of their own work, and it [always works without signing in](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## The teams it serves

A few groups feel right at home:

- **Software and product teams** running sprints, backlogs, bug triage and roadmaps on [boards](/help/plan/boards/), with [card types](/help/plan/card-types/) for stories, tasks and bugs, and a [Gantt chart](/help/plan/visualisations/) of what's coming.
- **Project and operations teams** keeping a launch, a content calendar or a weekly plan moving, with [dashboards](/help/plan/dashboards/) that show where the work stands at a glance.
- **People running sessions** such as retros and planning meetings, where everyone adds cards at once, then votes on what matters with [session tools](/help/collaboration/session-tools/).
- **Anyone who lives in spreadsheets**: [Sheets](/help/plan/sheets/) handle budgets, timesheets and contact lists, with formulas, beside the boards they relate to.
- **Anyone collecting requests or feedback**: a [Form](/help/plan/forms/) turns each answer into a card, ready to sort.

## Whether you sign in or not

liveplanner is friction-free on purpose. A first-time visitor can plan something real as a [guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/) and only later create an account to sync across devices and join [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/). A signed-in user can still hand a share link to a guest, who joins without any account at all.
