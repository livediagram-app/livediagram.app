liveplanner is a web app for **planning work together, live**. You make a **plan**, and fill its tabs with boards of cards, spreadsheets, forms and dashboards. Share one link and everyone on it sees the same plan change as it happens: a card moved to Done, a cell filled in, a new answer to a form.

## One plan, four kinds of tab

A plan holds **tabs**, and each tab is one of four things:

- **[Boards](/help/plan/boards/)**: columns of cards you drag from status to status, for a Kanban board, a sprint, a bug triage, a retro or a roadmap.
- **[Sheets](/help/plan/sheets/)**: spreadsheets with formulas, formatting and charts, for budgets, timesheets, contact lists and anything else that suits a grid.
- **[Forms](/help/plan/forms/)**: questions you send out, whose answers arrive as cards, ready to sort.
- **[Dashboards](/help/plan/dashboards/)**: metrics, charts, Gantt charts and calendars drawn from the plan's cards, side by side with its boards and sheets.

Every card is an **item** stored with the plan, so the same card can sit on a sprint board and a roadmap, and every dashboard counts it wherever it is. [Card types](/help/plan/card-types/) decide what a card holds (a task, a bug, a candidate), with fields of your own. Each new tab opens on **Start Planning**, a picker of ready-made boards, sheets, forms and dashboards, plus whole ways of working set up across several tabs, so you rarely start from a blank page. See [Planning Basics](/help/plan/planning-basics/).

## Built for working together

- **Real time:** everyone on a plan sees changes as they happen, and a card someone is dragging or has open shows their name. See [Live Presence](/help/collaboration/live-presence/).
- **Share with a link:** an Editor, Participant or Viewer link, with a password or an expiry date if you want one. The person you share with needs no account. See [Sharing Your Plan](/help/getting-started/sharing-your-project/).
- **Comments** on cards and on sheet cells, so the conversation stays beside the work. See [Comments](/help/collaboration/comments/).
- **Teams** for a shared space with the people you work with. See [Teams](/help/collaboration/teams/).

## No sign-up to start

liveplanner **always works without signing in**. Open a new plan, plan something real, and hand the link to a colleague who joins straight away. An account is there when you want your plans on every device; see [Guest vs Account](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## Free, with no trackers

liveplanner is **free for everyone**. There is no paid tier, no trial and no plan to introduce one: every feature is available to every user. There are no ads and no third-party trackers; the only analytics are anonymous, first-party counts that never include your content, and you can turn them off. See [What We Collect](/help/privacy-and-security/what-we-collect/) and [Why Use liveplanner?](/help/about/why-liveplanner/).
