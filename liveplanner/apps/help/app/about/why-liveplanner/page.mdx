<ArticleLayout title="Why Use liveplanner?" description="Free with no trackers, no sign-in wall, and real-time collaboration from the very first card." categoryTitle="About liveplanner" categorySlug="about">

There are plenty of ways to plan work. liveplanner is built around a few ideas that make it a comfortable home for teams: it is **free**, it carries **no trackers**, it never puts a **sign-in wall** in front of your work, it keeps **your work yours**, and it is **live from the start**. Here is what each of those means for you.

## Free, with no paid tier

liveplanner is **free for everyone**. There is no paid tier, no "Pro" upgrade, no trial that expires, and no plan to introduce any of those. Every feature, from boards and sheets to forms, dashboards and sharing, is available to every user.

## No trackers, no ads

liveplanner carries **no ads and no third-party trackers**. The only analytics are anonymous, first-party feature counts that never include your content, and you can switch them off in Settings. See [What We Collect](/help/privacy-and-security/what-we-collect/) and the [privacy policy](/help/policies/privacy-policy/).

## Your work stays yours

Export boards and sheets as CSV, images, PDF or Markdown whenever you like, so you are never trapped. See [Export Tabs](/help/tabs/export-tabs/). Nothing in the editor is held back behind a plan or a trial.

## No sign-in wall

liveplanner [always works without signing in](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/). A first-time visitor can open a [new project](/new), plan something real, and only later be asked to create an account, never blocked before they have even started. An account adds cross-device sync and [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/), but it is never a gate in front of the editor.

## Live from the first card

Collaboration is not a bolt-on. Hand someone a [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) and they join the same project, where everyone sees cards move, cells change and new cards arrive as it happens. A card someone is dragging or has open shows their colour and name, so you can see who is working on what.
