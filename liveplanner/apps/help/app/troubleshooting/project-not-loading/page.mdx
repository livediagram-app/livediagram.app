When a plan does not open, the cause is usually access-related rather than data loss. liveplanner deliberately returns the same "not found" surface for a plan you cannot reach as for one that truly does not exist, so a blank or "not found" screen does not mean your work is gone. Work through the checks below in order.

## Quick checks

1. **Reload the page.** A transient network blip is the single most common cause. A refresh re-runs the load from scratch.
2. **Check your connection.** The editor needs to reach the API to load a plan. Confirm you are online and not behind a network that blocks the site.
3. **Open it from your own list.** If you own the plan, open it from [your plans](/explorer/recent) rather than an old URL, to rule out a stale link.

## Stuck on "Opening your plan"

The opening screen tries to fix a slow load on its own:

1. **After 10 seconds** it says "This is taking longer than usual." and offers **Refresh**.
2. **After 30 seconds** it reloads the page once by itself, with a fresh start.
3. **If that does not help**, it shows "Something went wrong" ("We couldn't load this plan: it didn't finish loading.") with **Try again**, and under "Still not loading?" two more ways out: **Copy Diagnostics**, a short report to send to [support](/help/contact/), and **Repair This Browser**, which clears stuck settings and caches while keeping your plans. You can also run the repair from [Repair This Browser](/help/troubleshooting/repair-this-browser/), even if the editor will not start.

If your browser is offline, the screen says "You're offline" instead, and the plan opens by itself as soon as you reconnect. While you are offline in an open plan, a banner says so, and your changes save when the connection is back. Changes to cards and Sheet cells are kept in this browser, so they wait even if you close the tab or reload, and the banner counts them; open the plan again once you are online and they go through. Other changes stay in that tab, so keep it open until the connection returns.

## A Single Tab Won't Load

Each tab's content loads on its own when you open it, so a plan can open fine while one tab doesn't. While a tab is loading, it shows "Loading tab…" and can't be clicked. If the load fails, a card reads "Couldn't load this tab" ("We couldn't reach the server to load this tab's contents. Your work is safe. Check your connection and try again.") with a **Retry** button.

1. **Press Retry.** It asks for the tab again, and a brief connection blip usually clears with one try.
2. **Carry on in another tab.** Only the tab's content is covered: the tab bar and the header above it keep working, so you can switch to another tab or leave the plan.
3. **Check your connection,** then reload the page if the card keeps coming back.

The card covers the tab on purpose. Until the tab's real content has arrived, what is underneath would be an empty tab, and working on it could save over your work. Nothing you made is lost while the card is up.

## It opens for a colleague but not for you

When the same link works for someone else, the plan is fine and the difference is on your side:

1. **Check who you are signed in as.** A plan belongs to one account or one guest browser. If your colleague shared it with a different account, or you made it as a guest in another browser, it will not be in your list. Compare the account in the top right with the one the plan was shared with.
2. **Open the share link itself**, not a copy of the address bar from your colleague's browser. The owner's address only works for the owner.
3. **[Repair this browser](/help/troubleshooting/repair-this-browser/)**, which clears anything saved in this browser that could be in the way.
4. **Try another browser or network.** A browser extension, an out-of-date browser, or a work network that blocks parts of the site can stop a load that works elsewhere.

## If you opened it from a share link

A share link is the most common reason a plan refuses to load, and the fixes are different from a plan you own.

1. **You may need a password.** If the plan has a [share password](/help/privacy-and-security/share-link-security/), you will see a lock screen. Enter the password the owner gave you. A wrong password shows an error and lets you retry.
2. **The link may have expired.** Share links can be created with a lifetime. An expired link stops working everywhere and looks just like a revoked one. Ask the owner to **Extend** it or send a fresh link.
3. **The link may have been revoked.** If the owner stopped sharing or deleted that specific link, it no longer grants access. Ask for a new one.
4. **Check you copied the whole URL.** A truncated link, missing its share code, will not resolve.

## Still not loading

1. **Try a different browser or an incognito window** to rule out an extension or cached data. See [browser compatibility](/help/troubleshooting/browser-compatibility/).
2. **Try another device** on a different network to separate a local problem from a plan problem.
3. **Confirm you are signed in as the right identity.** A guest plan is tied to the browser that made it, so a different browser has a different guest identity. Signing in moves this browser's guest plans into your account. See [working as a guest](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

If none of this helps, the plan itself may be reachable but stuck on real-time connection; see [real-time collaboration problems](/help/troubleshooting/collaboration-issues/). If you are still stuck, [contact us](/help/contact/) with the URL you used, what you see, and the report from **Copy Diagnostics**.
