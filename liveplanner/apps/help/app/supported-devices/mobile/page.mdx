liveplanner works on a phone right in your browser, with no app to install. The editor switches to a compact, touch-friendly layout on a phone-sized screen (narrower than 640 pixels, or a touch screen shorter than 500 pixels, so a phone held sideways counts too). It is great for checking where the work stands, moving a card or two, filling in a form, and following along live on the go.

## The layout on a phone

The editor keeps the same parts as on a computer, fitted to the width: the plan's name and **Share** along the top, the work in the middle, the bottom-right controls, and the tabs along the bottom with **Search** and **Settings** as icons at the end.

- **Boards** show one column a screen, centred, with a sliver of its neighbours on either side: swipe sideways and the board settles on the next column, one at a time. Scrolling down a column stays where you leave it. Tap a column's empty space (not a card or a button) and it glides to the centre. Cards are drawn at their smallest size so a column shows more of them, and the board's header leaves out its widgets to make room for the title. A card opens in a panel that rises from the bottom of the screen.
- **Dashboards** stack their slots one under another, in reading order, and you scroll down through them. To reorder slots, use **Move Up** and **Move Down** in a slot's settings.
- **Sheets** keep their grid, with a toolbar that packs its buttons tighter and folds the rest into **More**.
- **Forms** fill the width of the screen, and you build and answer them just as on a computer.

Menus open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen; drag it down to close it.

## Held sideways

Turn your phone sideways and the screen gets short, so the header along the top and the tabs along the bottom fold away and the work fills the screen. To reach Share, the plan's name or your tabs, tap the small arrow button in the top-right corner, **Show Header and Tabs**: both bars come back, and the same button (**Hide Header and Tabs**) folds them away again. The [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) needs the phone upright. Turn the phone upright and everything is back as usual.

## Touch gestures

Your fingers do the work a mouse would on a computer:

- **Tap** a card to open it, or a button to press it.
- **Drag** a card to move it to another column or up and down its column: hold it still for a moment, then move. A finger that moves straight away scrolls the board, whether it starts on a card or on empty space.
- **Press and hold** a card for half a second to open its menu (when you can edit), the touch version of a right-click.
- **On a sheet**, tap a cell to select it and drag to scroll. Hold a finger still on a cell until it grabs, then drag to select a range; hold and let go without moving to open the cell's menu.
- **Press and hold** a button that shows only an icon to see its name. Letting go does not press it.

## What works differently on a phone

A phone has a small screen, no mouse pointer and usually no keyboard, so a few things work differently:

- **UI Scale** and the **Trash** button in the bottom-right corner are left out on a phone. (While the session tools are showing, the card tools fold into a **More card tools** menu, and Trash is in it.)
- **Board widgets** aren't shown, and a quick filter set with one on a wider screen stands down, so no cards are hidden without you seeing why.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** need a keyboard: without one, the on-screen controls do everything the shortcuts do. Pair a keyboard and they work as on a computer.
- **Hover previews and tooltips** need a hovering pointer, so on touch a tap simply applies a change.
