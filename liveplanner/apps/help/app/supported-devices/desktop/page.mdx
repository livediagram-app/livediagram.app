A computer (Windows, Mac or Linux) with a mouse or trackpad and a keyboard gives you the complete liveplanner experience. liveplanner runs in your browser, with nothing to install. Everything in the help centre works here, so this is the best place for detailed planning and for setting up boards, sheets and dashboards.

## The full layout

On a computer the whole editor is on screen at once: the project's name and **Share** along the top, the board, sheet, form or dashboard in the middle, the bottom-right controls (**Undo** and **Redo**, then **Trash**, **New**, **Find** and **Types**, and **Style**), and the tab bar along the bottom with **Search** and **Settings** at its right end.

Columns show side by side, cards show at the size the board is set to, and a dashboard lays its slots out in rows. **UI Scale** in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) makes the panels and buttons bigger or smaller, and on a Sheet tab the zoom controls make the cells bigger or smaller.

## Mouse and keyboard

A mouse and keyboard unlock the fastest ways to work:

- **Drag** cards between columns, drag the edges of a sheet's columns and rows to resize them, and drag across cells to select a range.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** for undo and redo, search, Zen mode, moving cards and working in a sheet. See [Keyboard Essentials](/help/getting-started/keyboard-essentials/) and the full [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/).
- **Scroll** with the mouse wheel or two fingers on a trackpad to move through a long column, a big sheet or a dashboard.

## Right-click and hover

Right-click a card, a cell, a sheet's row or column header, or a board's empty space to open its menu, and use a tab's **⋯** for the tab's own. Rest the pointer on a button for half a second and a tooltip names it; some controls explain themselves at once with a hover card. Keyboard focus shows either straight away, and **Escape** hides it.
