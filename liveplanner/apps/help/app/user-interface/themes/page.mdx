A theme is the fastest way to make a tab look intentional. Instead of colouring things by hand, pick a theme and liveplanner recolours the whole tab at once. The catalogue ships twenty-four themes, and you can save your own too.

## What a Theme Covers

A theme sets the colours a tab is drawn in, so everything on it reads as one coherent style:

- On a [board](/help/plan/boards/), the board's surface, its columns, its cards and their borders and text.
- On a [Sheet](/help/plan/sheets/), its surface, header and grid.
- On a [Dashboard](/help/plan/dashboards/), every slot, whose border takes the theme's colour. A board or Sheet shown in a slot is drawn in the Dashboard's theme there, while its own tab keeps its own.
- On a [Form](/help/plan/forms/), the page the questions sit on.

Text that would not read on a theme's colours is swapped for a readable one, so every theme stays easy to read. Switching themes leaves the tab's font alone.

A theme belongs to one tab, so the tabs of one plan can each wear a different one. A new plan starts on Default, and a tab you add starts on the theme of the tab you added it from.

## Light and Dark, for Everyone

Every theme has a light half and a dark half. Which half you see is your own [appearance](/help/tools/dark-mode/): on **Default**, light chrome shows the familiar light board and dark chrome a dark one, and every other theme turns the same way, keeping its own hues.

Nothing is saved when the half changes: a colleague in light chrome sees the light half of the very same tab while you work in the dark one. A colour someone picked by hand is drawn as it is for everyone.

## Browsing and Applying

You change a tab's theme from the **Tab Style** dialog: click **Style** in the bottom-right corner of the editor, and it opens on its home. At the top are **Looks**, each setting the theme, the finish, the tab font and the background pattern together in one click, led by a **Mashup** card that rolls a random mix each time you press it. To pick a theme on its own, press **Colours** under **Fine-tune**: every theme sits in one list, nine to a page. Click one to apply it. See [Changing the Theme](/help/user-interface/themes/changing-theme/) for the full walkthrough.
