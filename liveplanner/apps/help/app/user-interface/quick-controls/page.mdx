The **quick controls** sit at the right-hand end of the bottom bar, after the [tab bar](/help/user-interface/tab-bar/)'s tabs, and gather the actions you want reachable at any moment, whatever you are doing in the plan. They stay put whichever tab you are on, and a matching cluster appears at the bottom of the full-page [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), so the two screens don't hide their controls in different places.

## What lives here

Three controls, left to right:

- **Tools**: one button (a toolbox) that opens a popover of the tools that work on the whole plan. See [Tools](#tools) below.
- **Settings**: the [Settings dialog](/help/user-interface/settings/): the preferences that follow you between devices, plus the **Keyboard** category with the full [shortcut reference](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/) and the switch that turns shortcuts off.
- **Appearance**: click to step the [interface between Light, Dark, and System](/help/tools/dark-mode/) (System follows your device). This is the app's own chrome, not the tab's [theme](/help/user-interface/themes/), which lives behind the **Style** button in the bottom-right corner.

While a guided tour is on offer, a **Get Started** button sits just before Tools. It starts the tour, or lists every tour on offer with its length when there is more than one. See [The Plan Tour](/help/plan/plan-tour/).

On a computer each control in the editor's bar shows its name beside the icon, unless **Button Labels** is off in Settings, and hovering one shows what it does. On a phone the bar is tight, so the controls are icons only.

## Tools

**Tools** lists, under small headings, every tool you may use on this plan. Which rows you see depends on your role and on whether the plan is saved to an account:

- **Find**: **Search…** opens the [search panel](/help/search-panel/) to jump to any plan, tab or card, or to run a [command](/help/tips-and-tricks/command-palette/). `⌘K` or `⌘.` (Ctrl+K or Ctrl+. on Windows and Linux) opens it straight from the keyboard.
- **Automate**: **Automations…** ([Automations](/help/plan/automations/)), **Email to Board** ([Email to Board](/help/plan/email-to-board/)) and **Webhooks**.
- **Collaborate**: **Walkthroughs** ([Walkthroughs](/help/collaboration/walkthroughs/)), **Subscribe to Calendar** ([Calendar Feed](/help/plan/calendar-feed/)) and **Publish as Roadmap** ([Public Roadmap](/help/plan/public-roadmap/)).
- **Versioning**: **History**, who changed what and when, with [Snapshots](/help/collaboration/snapshots/) in it.

Most tools open inside the popover, with a back bar at the top to return to the list, so you can hop from one to the next. **Search…** and **Automations…** close Tools and open their own window, which is what the ellipsis on their names says. Escape steps back a level, then closes Tools. A small amber dot on the button means an automation rule has paused itself.

On a phone, Tools (and Get Started) opens as a sheet across the foot of the screen instead of a popover.

### Tools That Need an Account or a Synced Plan

A tool that needs you signed in, or needs the plan saved to liveplanner, still shows in Tools so you can find it. Small badges beside its name say what it needs first: **Sign In**, then **Sync Plan** for a plan kept in Browser Storage. Pressing it opens a short step in its place: **Sign In** takes you to sign in and brings you back with the tool open, and **Sync Plan** saves the plan to liveplanner and carries straight on to the tool. Automations, Email to Board, Subscribe to Calendar, History and Webhooks work this way. A tool your role can't use stays out of the list.
