liveplanner has no bar across the top of the editor, so your board, sheet, dashboard or form gets the whole height of the window. What used to sit in a title bar now floats in two small cards over the top corners of the tab:

- **Top left**: the liveplanner logo, then the menu button that opens the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).
- **Top right**: **Copy** if the plan is someone else's, **Share** if it is yours, and your account.

Both cards take the tab's style, so they read as part of the tab rather than a frame around it, and **Panel Opacity** in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) fades them like the other panels.

## The top left

The **logo** opens the Explorer on the place that holds this plan (its folder, its team's library, Offline or Shared With Me), so you land beside it. On a narrower screen it shows the mark without the wordmark, and on a phone the logo is left off to make room.

Beside it, the **menu button** (three lines) opens the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) under it: your plans and folders, with links to the full Explorer, Help and more along its foot. Press it again to close it.

When a board or a Sheet fills its tab, or the tab is a Dashboard, its name rides in the same card, right after the menu button.

## Renaming the plan

With no title bar, the plan's name is not shown above your work. To rename the plan, open the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/) from the menu button and choose **Rename** on the plan's ⋯ menu. Names are capped at 60 characters. Only people who can change the plan's structure can rename it: its owner, a member of its team, and anyone on an Editor link.

## Share or Copy

The card at the top right holds one main action, and which one you get depends on whose plan it is.

**Share** is for the owner (and, on a team plan, the team's members). It opens the [Share dialog](/help/collaboration/sharing/), where you create and manage links. The button turns solid once the plan has a live link, so you can tell at a glance that it is shared, and hovering it then reads **Shared**. On an [Offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) plan it asks you to save the plan to your account first, since there is nothing on a server to share yet. While a [Form](/help/plan/forms/) tab is open, **Share Form** takes Share's place: it publishes the form and copies its link.

**Copy** is for everyone else who has the plan open, on a share link of any role. It makes a copy of the plan that belongs to you:

<Step n={1}>Click **Copy**. It reads **Copying** while the copy is made.</Step>
<Step n={2}>The editor opens your copy, named "Copy of" and the original name.</Step>
<Step n={3}>
  Find it again later at the top level of My Work in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/).
</Step>

Your copy starts **Private**: no share links come with it, and nothing you do to it touches the original. If you opened the plan through a link to a single tab, the copy holds just that tab. You do not need an account to copy; as a guest the copy is saved under your [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/).

On a phone, and whenever the card would crowd the tab's own toolbar, its buttons show their icons without their names.

## Your account

At the far right is your account. Signed in, it shows your initial; click it for a menu with your name and email, **Account** (which opens [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/) on your account details, without leaving the plan) and **Sign Out**. Not signed in, it shows **Sign In** instead (see [Signing In](/help/account-and-data/signing-in/)).

## When the corners are hidden

[Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) hides both cards, and an [embed](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) never shows them.
