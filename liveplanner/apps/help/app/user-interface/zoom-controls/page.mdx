The **zoom controls** appear in the bottom-right corner on a [Sheet](/help/plan/sheets/) tab, at the end of the cluster after Undo and Redo, the card buttons and **Style**. They make the sheet's cells larger or smaller. Boards, Dashboards and Forms fit their tab, so those tabs have no zoom controls.

  <ZoomControls />

## The controls

- **Zoom out** / **Zoom in**: step 10% smaller or larger.
- **The percentage button**: shows the current level. **Click it to go back to 100%**. **Hover it** for a popover of preset levels (50%, 75%, 100%, 125% and 150%) and pick one to jump straight there.

A sheet zooms from 50% to 200%. The column letters, row numbers and cells grow and shrink together, while the toolbar and formula bar keep their size, and everything works as it does at 100%. Zoom is your own view: it is never saved or shared, and the sheet opens at 100% next time.

## From the trackpad and mouse

Hold `⌘` (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) and scroll, or pinch on a trackpad, to zoom smoothly within the same range.

## On a phone and in zen mode

On a phone the zoom buttons and the percentage are dropped to save space: pinch to zoom, and a **Fit** button puts the sheet back to 100%. In [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) the zoom controls stay, joined by an **Exit zen mode** button.
