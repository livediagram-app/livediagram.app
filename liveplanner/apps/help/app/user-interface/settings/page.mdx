**Settings** opens from the **Settings** button (the sliders icon) in the [quick controls](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/) at the right-hand end of the tab bar, in the editor and in the Explorer. It holds the preferences that are about _you_ rather than about any particular plan, which is why there is one set of them and not one per plan.

The dialog lists its categories down the left, with a **Search settings** field above them, and shows one category at a time on the right. Type into the search to find a setting by name or by a word it is about ("transparency" finds **Panel Opacity**). On a phone, the list and the category take turns: tap a category to open it, and go back for the list.

Only a guest sees **sub-categories**: Account holds Notifications and API Tokens, which are categories of their own once you sign in. Everything else in a category is in its sections, so nothing is hidden behind another level. On a computer, click Account (or its arrow) to show them beneath it, and again to fold them away. On a phone, tap Account and they are listed at the bottom of its page.

Each category groups its settings into cards, one card per section, with a short line under each setting saying what it does. A setting that only matters while another is on sits indented beneath it, and shows only while that switch is on.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**, which follows your device. This is the editor's own chrome, not a tab's [theme](/help/user-interface/themes/), although every tab theme has a light and a dark half that follows it. The same control sits at the end of the tab bar. Saved on this device only (see below). See [Appearance](/help/tools/dark-mode/).
- **Button Labels**: names the buttons along the bottom (Search, Settings, the appearance switch, and **Trash**, **Find**, **Types** and **Style** in the bottom-right corner) beside their icons, on a screen wide enough for them. On by default; turn it off for icons alone. A phone always shows icons alone.
- **UI Scale**: makes the panels, the menu button and the buttons in the bottom-right corner bigger or smaller, from 80% to 120%, with 100% in the middle of the slider. Your boards, sheets, dialogs and menus stay as they are, so it is not the same as your browser's zoom. Beneath it, **Panel Scale**, **Toolbar Scale** (which goes up to 140%) and **Corner Buttons Scale** size each of those on its own; moving **UI Scale** again sets all three back to the same size. The chrome resizes as you drag. Desktop only: a phone always uses 100%.

## Keyboard

- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: the switch that turns the editor's keyboard shortcuts on and off. Off means all of them, chords like undo included, not just the single-letter keys. Saved on this device only (see below).
- **All Shortcuts**: the full shortcut reference, in collapsible groups. The **Keyboard Shortcuts** command in [search](/help/search-panel/) opens Settings straight on this category. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Panels

- **Panel Opacity**: fades the panels, from 30% to 100%, so what is behind them shows through (buttons and the Explorer stay opaque); a panel snaps back to fully opaque while you hover or focus it.

Below it, the **Collaborate** section holds **Enable Collaborate Panel**: the list of a tab's comment threads and actions, behind a button in the bottom-right corner that only appears while the tab has a comment. With it off, comments still work where they were left. See [Comments](/help/collaboration/comments/).

## Accessibility

- **Reduce Motion**: turns off the decorative animations and transitions (panels, popovers, dialogs) so the interface appears instantly instead of sliding. Your device's own reduce-motion setting is always respected; this lets you force it on even where the device doesn't ask for it. See [Accessibility](/help/getting-started/accessibility/).

## Getting Started

One switch per guided tour. **Plan Tours** switches the planning tours all at once, with **Boards Tour**, **Sheets Tour** and **Dashboards Tour** beneath it, and **Forms Tour** sits on its own. Every tour starts from its **Start Tour** button in the bottom bar, just left of **Search** (Start Boards Tour, Start Forms Tour and so on), which is there whenever the tour can run, until you've taken it. A tour's switch turns itself off once you have finished or skipped it. Switch one back on and close Settings to take that tour again: it starts straight away if you are where it belongs; otherwise use the button when you get there. See [The Plan Tour](/help/plan/plan-tour/).

## Explorer

Once called **Plans**.

- **Automatically Open Explorer**: off by default. While it's on and you're signed in, opening liveplanner.app takes you straight to your Explorer instead of the homepage. A link to the homepage from inside liveplanner still shows it, and so does `liveplanner.app/?home`. It's one setting for every app: each other app picks up a change the next time you open its Explorer or an item in it.
- **Show Home Banner**: the greeting, status pills and large search at the top of the Explorer's [Home](/help/explorer/home/). On by default. When it is off, Home's search sits in the header, as on every other Explorer page; closing the banner on Home turns this off too.
- **Where New Plans Go**, under **Default Folders**: the folders new plans are saved in when you make one without choosing a place, with a separate entry for Kanban and Retrospective plans. Each entry has **Change** and **Clear**. See [Default folders](/help/explorer/folders/#default-folders).

## Account

**Account** shows who you are (a guest, or your name and email when signed in), **Reset All Settings** and **Delete Account**. **Reset All Settings** puts every setting back to its default once you confirm. Signed in, that is every setting in every liveworkspace app; your profile, API tokens, webhooks and sign-ins stay as they are, and so does your choice about anonymous usage events. As a guest, it is this app's settings in this browser. Tours offer themselves again afterwards. It holds two sub-categories:

### Notifications

- **In-Editor Notifications**: the brief confirmation shown when you do something whose result isn't on screen, like moving a plan to a folder. **Errors and warnings are always shown**, whatever this is set to, so a failure is never silently swallowed. A notification stays put while you point at it or tab into it, and Escape closes the one you are on.
- **Email**: once signed in, one switch per email (in this order: someone comments on your work, mentions you or assigns you an action, someone responds to a team invite, someone joins your plan, then someone fills in your form, then tips and check-ins and milestones last). A guest sees a sign-in prompt instead. See [Email Notifications](/help/account-and-data/email-notifications/).

### API Tokens

- **API Tokens** is where you make, see and revoke the tokens your own scripts and connected AI tools use to call liveplanner as you, right here in Settings: name a token (switch on **Read-only** if it only needs to look), copy its secret while it is shown (only once), and revoke one with **Revoke**. Tokens belong to your liveworkspace account, so one works in every liveworkspace app, and the same list shows in each one's Settings and on liveworkspace.app. Each expires six months after it is created. Below them, **Paired workbenches** lists the developer tools your CLI sign-in has paired, each with **Unpair** ([Workbenches](/help/developers/workbenches/)). Signed-in only, and absent on a deployment without accounts. See [API Tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/).

Signed in, the list has an **Account** heading over **Profile** (in Account's place), Notifications, Privacy, Accessibility and **Your Data**, where you download everything you own, and each team's you run, as one zip, and a **Developers** heading over API Tokens alone. Webhooks, which post what happens to your work to Slack, Microsoft Teams or a service you run, are set per document: open the document's ⋯ menu in the Explorer and choose **Webhooks**. With your profile are your **Time Zone** (when the emails that can wait arrive; **Automatic** follows your browser), your **Sign-in Methods** (**Email Code**, always on, and **Google**, which you can **Connect**) and **Where You’re Signed In**, each browser you are signed in on, with **Sign Out** for one and **Sign Out Others** for the rest. These are the same as in liveworkspace.app's Settings, right here.

Anywhere Settings says something needs an account, it ends with a **Sign In** link that brings you back to where you were.

## Privacy

- **Send Anonymous Usage Events**: the small first-party events listed on the public [telemetry dashboard](/telemetry): no user content, no third-party trackers. Turn it off to keep everything you do on your device. See [What We Collect](/help/privacy-and-security/what-we-collect/).

## Settings follow you, most of them

Settings are **stored against your identity, not your browser**, and apply to every plan you open on every device you sign in from. For a guest, "your identity" is the [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/) liveworkspace gives this browser, so the preferences last as long as that does.

A few things deliberately stay on the device you are using:

- **Theme** (light, dark or system) in **Appearance**.
- The **Keyboard Shortcuts** switch, so you can keep shortcuts on where you have a full keyboard and off where you don't.
- **Explorer view**: List, Card or Details.
