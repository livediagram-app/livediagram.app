A plan can hold many tabs, each filled by a Board, a Sheet, a Dashboard or a Form, and the **tab bar** is how you move between them. The bar runs along the bottom of the editor and shows them in a row, so the whole plan is one click wide. The [quick controls](/help/user-interface/quick-controls/) (Search, Settings and the appearance switch) sit at its right-hand end.

## What you can do here

- **Switch** between tabs by clicking one. Each tab shows a small icon for what fills it, and a small stack of initials avatars on a tab shows who else is on it right now.
- **Add** a new tab with the **+** button straight after the last tab. It arrives numbered ("Tab 2", "Tab 3"), wearing the current tab's theme, and opens on **Start Planning**, where you pick a board, sheet, form or dashboard for it. See [Planning Basics](/help/plan/planning-basics/).
- **Rename** a tab by double-clicking the active one, or from its menu.
- **Reorder** tabs by dragging them along the bar; a caret shows where the tab will land.
- **Manage** a tab from its menu: right-click any tab (it becomes the active one), or click the **⋯** button on the active tab. Along its top are **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Paste**, **Side by Side**, **Lock Tab** and **Delete**; below them **Add to Folder**, **Add to Plan** (copy it into another plan), **Import**, **Export** and **Clear**. See [Using Tabs](/help/tabs/using-tabs/).
- **Organise** related tabs into one-level folders with **Add to Folder** when a plan grows. See [Tab Folders](/help/tabs/tab-folders/).

On a view-only link the bar still switches tabs, but there is no **+** button and no tab menu. On a phone the "Tabs" label is dropped to save room.

For the depth, see [Tabs](/help/tabs/): this bar is just the control surface for them.
