_Last updated: 22 October 2026._

liveplanner is built to need as little of your data as possible. This policy explains what the hosted service at **liveplanner.app** does with your data. It sits alongside our [Terms of Service](/help/policies/terms/).

## What we store

- **Your projects.** The content you create is saved so it is there when you come back, and so the people you share it with can see it. It is stored in our database on Cloudflare. A project you create in [Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) is the exception: it stays in your browser and never reaches our servers.
- **A browser id.** If you use liveplanner without an account, we keep a random identifier in your browser's local storage to link projects to your browser. It is not tied to your name or email.
- **Your display name and colour.** The name and cursor colour collaborators see are stored on our server against your browser id or account.
- **Account details, if you sign in.** Sign-in is handled by liveworkspace accounts (liveworkspace.app), the one sign-in shared by our apps, which emails you a code to confirm your address. If you create an account we receive your email address and the name on your account. We use these to identify your account and to send you the account and onboarding emails described under **Emails we send** below.
- **API tokens, if you create them.** If you create an [API token](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) to call the API from your own scripts, we store a one-way hash of it (never the token itself), its name, and a last-used timestamp, so the token can be verified and you can see when it was last active. Tokens are a signed-in-only feature, expire after six months, and are deleted when you delete your account.
- **Connecting an AI tool, if you do.** If you connect an AI tool via our [MCP server](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/), we store the app's registration (its name and redirect address, kept for 30 days), short-lived authorization sessions and codes that expire within minutes, and, for an hour, a count of registrations from your IP address to stop abuse. The connection itself is an API token, covered above, and you can revoke it any time.
- **Team invitations.** If a teammate invites you by email, we store that address with the team, send you the invitation (and up to three reminders while nobody has answered it, each with a link to decline it), and may send you action and mention emails at that address before you create an account.
- **Form responses.** If you publish a [form](/help/plan/forms/), each response someone sends is stored in your project as a card or a sheet row, holding only what they typed into the form. People filling in a form need no account, and we store no name, account or browser id for them. A report about a form stores its reason, the optional note, a random key kept in the reporter's browser and a one-way hash of their network.
- **Votes from guests.** To stop one person voting many times, a guest's vote stores a one-way hash of their network address combined with the project's id. It is deleted with the project.
- **Project activity.** liveplanner records what happened to your projects (created, renamed, shared, commented on) and which ones you opened, so [Home](/help/explorer/home/) and each project's History can show you. These records are kept for a year.
- **Basic operational logs.** Our host, Cloudflare, keeps standard request logs that help us run the service securely.

## Connected AI tools

If you [connect an AI tool](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/) such as Claude, that tool reads and changes your projects through our API using the token you approved. What the tool does with what it reads is governed by that tool's own terms and privacy policy, not this one.

We do not use your projects to train AI models.

## What we do not do

We do not use tracking pixels, advertising, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell your data, and we do not build advertising profiles.

## Anonymous product analytics

To understand which features actually help, we record anonymous, first-party product events, for example "a project was created" or "a dashboard tab was added". Each event is just three fixed labels: a category, an action, and an optional type like "DashboardTab" or "Edit". They never include anything that identifies you or your work: no names, no project or element content, no account id, no IP address. Page views are counted the same way: which page was opened, with ids and anything after a `?` or `#` removed in your browser before it is sent. When one of our own buttons opens the New Project page, we count which button it was (from a fixed list, like the landing page's hero) and whether a project followed; the label is removed from the address straight away. Error health is counted the same way too: a failed request or a browser-side exception is recorded only as a generic status or kind, never a message or stack trace. How long key moments take (a project opening, a save, a page loading) is counted the same way: only the range it fell in, like "under one second", never the exact time. Events are deleted after 60 days.

To count sign-ups and sign-ins once each, our server also remembers which sign-in sessions it has already counted, for 90 days, and that an account has been seen before (deleted when you delete your account). These records are never published; only the totals are.

We collect this ourselves, through liveworkspace's own telemetry service (liveworkspace is the family of apps liveplanner belongs to), which stores it in its own database beside the same anonymous events from our other apps, each labelled with the app it came from. We use no third-party analytics vendors. The data is never sold or shared, and the only place it goes beyond our own use is our public [telemetry dashboard](/telemetry) at liveworkspace.app, where you can see exactly the same anonymous totals we do, for liveplanner or for all our apps together. You can turn off the events your browser sends at any time from Settings (see [What We Collect](/help/privacy-and-security/what-we-collect/)). A few anonymous totals are counted by our server and can't be turned off: sign-ups and sign-ins, first visits to a share link, multiplayer sessions, emails sent, and server errors.

## Cookies and local storage

We use local storage to keep your browser id, and your preferences (also saved to our database so they follow you). If you sign in, we set cookies to keep you signed in and to remember that you are; no sign-in script loads for visitors who don't sign in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Emails we send

If you have an account, we use your email address to send you a small number of emails:

- **Account and service emails**: an invitation when someone invites you to a team (with up to three reminders while it is unanswered), a confirmation when you delete your account, a one-time reminder a week before an API token expires, and a note if one of your forms is suspended after reports.
- **Notifications about your work**: when someone opens a project you shared for the first time, comments on a project you own, fills in one of your forms, responds to a team invite you sent, assigns you an action, or mentions you in a comment. A mention email quotes the comment (up to about 280 characters) and an assigned-action email includes the action's name and the start of its description; the others name the project but not its contents.
- **A short onboarding series**: a welcome message, a few tips during your first two weeks, a nudge if you have not made a project after a few days or have been away for about four weeks, and the occasional milestone.

You can turn off every notification, tip and milestone email in **Settings**, under **Account**, then **Notifications**, or from the link at the bottom of each one (see [Email Notifications](/help/account-and-data/email-notifications/)). We do not send advertising or marketing beyond these, and we never share your address other than with our email provider for the sole purpose of delivering them. Apart from team invitations and the emails that follow from them, people who use liveplanner without an account are never emailed.

## Service providers

We rely on a small number of providers to run the service:

- **Cloudflare**, for hosting, database storage, image storage, and the sign-in codes we email you.
- **Resend**, for delivering the emails described above (to account holders, and to addresses a teammate invites).

These providers process data on our behalf so that we can run the service.

Your browser also loads the editor's fonts from **Google Fonts**.

## Sharing and visibility

Anything you put in a project you share becomes visible to people who have the share link. You can protect a link with a password or give it an expiry, and revoking a link stops further access through it. See [Share Link Security](/help/privacy-and-security/share-link-security/).

## Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete any project you own at any time. A deleted project waits in the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/) for 30 days, so you can restore it, and is then removed for good; you can also delete it permanently from the Trash sooner. Comments, answers, ideas and votes that passed through a live collaboration session are also kept in that session's own storage on Cloudflare, and that copy is removed with the project. A project left empty and untouched for 30 days moves to the Trash on its own.

You can delete your account, and the data associated with it, yourself: open **Settings**, then **Account**, then **Delete Account**. This removes your projects (the Trash and their collaboration-session copies included), folders, themes, preferences and API tokens straight away, closes your liveworkspace account (the one sign-in for all our apps, so it closes for them too), and signs you out. Projects in a team's shared library pass to another member of that team rather than being deleted. See [Deleting Your Data](/help/account-and-data/deleting-your-data/).

Other things are kept only as long as they are useful: anonymous product events for 60 days, project activity for a year, and changes made by connected AI tools for 30 days (so they can be undone). A guest's projects have no account to delete: remove them from the Explorer, or ask us.

## Security

We protect your data with authentication and access controls on every request, and the code is public so the controls can be checked. If you find a security vulnerability, please report it privately as described in [Reporting a Vulnerability](/help/policies/report-a-vulnerability/).

## Children

liveplanner is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
