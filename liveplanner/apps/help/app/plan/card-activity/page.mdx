Every [card](/help/plan/cards/) keeps a **history** of its own: who made it, who moved it from column to column, who set or cleared its fields, and when. Open a card and pick its **Activity** tab to read it. It answers "who moved this?" for one card; it isn't a log of the whole plan, and it doesn't undo anything (use **Undo** for that).

## Open a Card's Activity

Open a card and pick **Activity**, the last of its tabs. Every card has it, whatever its [card type](/help/plan/card-types/) holds, so a type with one tab now shows two. On a phone, **Activity** sits just before **Details**. A card always opens on its first tab, so the history is only read when you pick Activity.

## What It Shows

The newest changes come first, in groups by day: **Today**, **Yesterday**, then the date ("Mon 27 Oct"). Down the left runs a thin line; each person's run of changes hangs from their picture, with their name (**You** for yourself) and the time.

Each change is one short line with a small glyph for its kind:

- **Created** this card, and the column it started in ("Created this card in **To Do**").
- **Moved** from one column to another ("Moved **In Progress** → **Review**").
- **Set**, **changed** or **cleared** a field ("Set **Priority** to **High**", "Changed **Due Date** **3 Oct** → **10 Oct**", "Cleared **Estimate**"). A long value, such as the description or a checklist, is named rather than copied ("Edited the **Description**").
- **Renamed**, **changed type**, **flagged** and **unflagged**, **archived** and **restored**, **trashed** and **restored from the Trash**.

Statuses show as chips with a dot in their stage's colour, priorities in the priority's own colour. The old value shows before the arrow when the card's history still holds it. Changes one person makes within a minute read as one entry, and a change made and taken back inside that minute (a move there and back, a flag on and off) drops out.

- **Times**: changes from today say how long ago ("2h ago"), older ones the time of day ("14:32"). Hover a time for the exact date and time.
- **Long histories**: the first 20 entries show, then **Show N More** brings the rest. An entry with more than three changes shows three and **Show N More Changes**.
- **Undo and redo**: a change someone took back with Undo ends with **(Undo)**, so a card moving back reads as a person taking their change back.
- **AI agents**: a change made by an AI tool through the [MCP server](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/), the [CLI](/help/developers/cli/) or the API carries the AI sparkle beside the name, worded by what it did: **Transitioned by AI** for a move, **Created by AI**, **Edited by AI**, **Renamed by AI**, **Trashed by AI**, **Archived by AI**, **Restored by AI**, **Flagged by AI** or **Unflagged by AI**, and **Changed by AI** when it did several kinds of thing.
- **Forms**: a card made from a [form](/help/plan/forms/) response says so.
- **Deleted accounts** read **Deleted account**.

## What It Leaves Out

Activity is about changes someone would ask about, so it leaves out a card's place within its column, the dots of a vote, and its [comment threads](/help/plan/cards/#comment-threads), which have their own place on the card. A card's history never travels with it: a duplicate, a copied plan or an imported card starts with none.

## How Long It Keeps

A card keeps its newest 200 entries, none older than 180 days. Trashing or archiving a card keeps its history; deleting it for good from the Trash deletes the history with it. A card made before history was kept, or with nothing left in that window, says **No Activity Yet**.

Plans in **This Browser** keep no card history: their Activity tab says **No Activity in This Browser**. Save the plan to your account to start keeping it.

If the history can't be read (you've lost your connection, say), the tab says **Couldn't Load Activity**, with **Try Again**.

## Who Can See It

Everyone who can open the card reads its Activity, Viewers included. Nobody writes it or edits it: the plan records each change as it is made. Someone on a link to one tab sees the history only of the cards that tab shows.
