**Subscribe to Calendar** gives you a private link to a plan's card dates. Add it to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar once, and each card with a date shows there as an all-day event, beside your meetings. Your calendar fetches it again every so often, so a date changed on the board moves in your calendar too.

It is read-only: nothing you do in your calendar changes a card.

There are two kinds of link: one for a plan (below), and one for [My Work](#my-work), the cards assigned to you across all your plans.

## Who Can Subscribe

The plan's owner and the members of its team, signed in, on a plan saved to an account. Someone who opened the plan from a share link (as an Editor, Participant or Viewer) doesn't see Subscribe to Calendar, and neither does a guest or a plan saved only in this browser.

Each person makes their own link, and it shows only what they can still open: if you leave the team, or the plan moves out of it, your link stops answering on its next fetch.

## Make Your Link

1. Press **Tools** in the bottom bar.
2. Choose **Subscribe to Calendar**, under Collaborate.
3. Pick what it shows under **What It Shows** (below).
4. Press **Make Calendar Link**.

The link appears with **Copy Link**. This is the only time it is shown, so copy it now: the plan keeps only a fingerprint of it, never the link itself. If you lose it, **Reset Link** makes a new one.

## Add It to Your Calendar

- **Google Calendar**: press **Google Calendar** beside the link. Google opens its page to add a calendar from a link, with yours filled in.
- **Apple Calendar**: press **Apple Calendar**. Your Mac or iPhone asks to subscribe.
- **Outlook**: in Outlook, choose **Add Calendar**, then **Subscribe from Web**, and paste the link.

Any other calendar that subscribes to an internet calendar (an `.ics` address) takes the link too.

Calendars fetch a subscribed link on their own schedule: Outlook every few hours, Google up to a day apart. A change on the board shows on the calendar's next fetch, not straight away.

## What It Shows

Each card with a **Start Date** or a **Due Date** is one all-day event:

- With both, it runs from its Start to its Due.
- With only a Due, it sits on its Due day.
- With only a Start, it sits on its Start day, titled "Starts: …".

Each event is titled with the card's number and title, and opening it in your calendar links back to the card. Archived cards and cards in the Trash never show. A plan with a great many dated cards shows the 500 nearest today.

Three options choose which cards:

- **Board**: **All Boards**, or just one of the plan's boards.
- **Only My Cards**: just the cards that name you in a person field, as **Assigned to You** does.
- **Include Done Cards**: cards in a done column too. Off, finished work drops out of your calendar.

Change them whenever you like, from the same dialog: a change saves at once and **keeps your link**, so there's nothing to add again. Your calendar shows the new set on its next fetch.

## My Work

The **Subscribe to Calendar** button at the top of the **Inbox**, in the Explorer panel, makes a link to the cards assigned to you, the same cards as **Assigned to You**, across every plan you own or share with a team. One calendar for all your work, without a link per plan.

1. Open the Explorer panel and find the **Inbox**.
2. Press the calendar button in the Inbox's header.
3. Press **Make Calendar Link**, then copy it or hand it to your calendar as above.

It shows each open card assigned to you that has a Start or Due date, the same way a plan's link does, each event naming its plan. It has no options: finished cards drop out, and a card you're no longer assigned to leaves on the next fetch. Like a plan's link, it shows the 500 cards nearest today.

It is yours alone, so it needs an account: a guest doesn't see the button. Each fetch checks again that you can still open each plan, so a plan you leave, or one taken out of your team, drops out. Cards in a plan someone shared with you only by a share link never show.

**Reset Link**, **Turn Off** and the privacy advice below work the same for it. Closing your account ends it.

## Keep It Private

Anyone with your link can see the titles and dates of the cards in your feed, without signing in. Treat it like a password:

- Don't share it, post it in a chat or paste it anywhere others can read.
- If it leaks, open **Subscribe to Calendar** (the tab menu for a plan's link, the Inbox for My Work) and press **Reset Link**. The old link stops working at once; add the new one to your calendar.

The link opens only your feed: it can't sign in, change anything or reach anything else.

## Reset Link and Turn Off

Open **Subscribe to Calendar** again and it says when your link was made:

- **Reset Link** makes a new link with the same options. The old one stops at once, so calendars using it stop updating until you add the new one.
- **Turn Off** ends your link. Calendars using it stop updating. You can make a new one any time.

Leaving the plan's team, or closing your account, ends your link too.
