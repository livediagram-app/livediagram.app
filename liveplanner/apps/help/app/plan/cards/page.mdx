A **card** shows one [item](/help/plan/items/) on a [board](/help/plan/boards/). The same item can sit on more than one board, and a change to it shows everywhere at once.

## Add a Card

- **+ Add card** at the foot of a column (or of a row, on a board with swimlanes) opens **Add a Card**: press a type (Project, Task, Note, Idea, Action or [your own](/help/plan/card-types/)) and a card of it lands at the end of the column and opens straight away with its title selected, ready to name.
- **With the keyboard:** focus a card and press **N** to open **Add a Card** for its column.
- **Off any board:** the **New** (+) button in the bottom-right strip makes a card of any type without placing it on a board.

On an empty board, the first column's button reads **Add your first card**.

A board shows and offers only the card types set in its menu's **Supported Cards**; cards of other types are hidden on it, not deleted. A card dropped into a row takes that row's assignee, priority or field.

## Open a Card

Click a card to open its panel. It closes with **Escape** or the close button, and every change saves as you make it.

<Figure caption="The card panel: a breadcrumb back to the plan, the card's tabs and Linked as Parent on the left, Details on the right.">
  <PlanCardPanelScene />
</Figure>

- **The header:** the card's type (change it from the picker), its number, the **⋯** menu (Duplicate, Flag, Archive, Trash, **Edit Card Type**, which opens the card's type in the type editor, and **Help**, which opens this page) and the close button.
- **On the left:** the title, then the card type's **tabs** (Overview by default) and their fields. The **description** is rich text: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, size, colour, headings, links, and bullet or numbered lists, from the toolbar over it or the usual shortcuts. It reads as text until you click it.
- **On the right, in Details:** status, assignee and parent, then priority, labels, estimate, due date and, on a plan, start date (a start after the due date says **Starts after it is due**). Labels are coloured chips, with the plan's labels offered as you type. **Open** beside a parent takes you to it. At the foot: who made the card and who last changed it.
- **A checklist** shows its progress ("2 of 5"); tick steps, edit them in place, and **Add a step**.
- **Linked as Parent:** a plan lists the cards under it (the cards whose **Parent** is that plan) on its first tab, each with its number, title and status. Click one to open it in the same panel, or **New Task** to make one already under it. Trashed cards are left out; an archived one shows an **Archived** chip.
- **Find your way back:** as you move from a card to its parent or a child, the top of the panel keeps a breadcrumb of where you have been. Click a card in it to go straight back. It shows the nearest three (one on a phone), with earlier ones folded into **…**. Opening a card from a board starts a new trail.
- **The ⋯ menu** holds **Duplicate**, **Flag** (or **Remove Flag**), **Archive** (or **Restore**) and **Trash**.

On a phone the panel rises from the bottom, with **Details** as its first tab and the breadcrumb on a row of its own. You can assign a card to yourself, to the members of the plan's team (when it lives in a team's library) or to a custom assignee; a plan in My Work or This Browser offers only you and custom assignees, and so does a guest's. In the open Assignee list, people with a liveworkspace account have the small liveworkspace mark to the left of their name; custom assignees have none. A Participant's **⋯** menu offers **Duplicate** and **Flag** but not Archive, Trash or Edit Card Type; a Viewer opens cards to read them, with only **Help** in the **⋯** menu.

## Flag a Card

**Flag** (in a card's right-click menu or its panel's **⋯** menu) marks a card for attention: it shows a red flag at the end of its title on every board it is on, and **Flagged** beside its number in the panel. Flagging changes nothing else about the card; **Remove Flag** takes it off.

## Comment Threads

A card holds **comment threads**: as many separate conversations as it needs, each with its own title, replies and resolved state. They sit at the end of the card's **Overview** tab, under **Comments**, and use the same [comments](/help/collaboration/comments/) as the rest of the plan.

<Figure caption="A card's comment threads: two open, the first on three cards, and one resolved at the bottom.">
  <PlanCardThreadsScene />
</Figure>

- **Start a thread** with **New Thread**. Type the first comment and press **Start Thread** (or **Cmd+Enter**, **Ctrl+Enter** on Windows; **Escape** cancels). The thread is titled for you from your name and the start of the comment ("Gentle Falcon: Basket empties after switching restaurants"), shown under the box as you type.
- **Open threads** come first, the most recently active at the top and open. Each shows its title, the people in it, how many comments it has and when it was last active; click its header to open or close it.
- **Reply** in the box at the foot of an open thread. Type **@** to mention someone.
- **Resolve** a thread once it is settled. It moves, with every comment kept, under **Resolved** at the bottom of the list, drawn quieter with who resolved it. **Reopen** brings it back.
- **The ⋯ menu** on a thread holds **Rename** (or press **F2** on its header; clear the title to go back to the automatic one), **Add to Card…**, **Resolve** or **Reopen**, and **Delete Thread…**.
- **Edit** and **Delete** your own comments from the buttons beside them. Someone who can edit the plan can delete any comment.
- A comment an AI tool wrote through the [MCP server](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/) or the [CLI](/help/developers/cli/) says **Generated by AI** above it.

### One Thread on Several Cards

**Add to Card…** puts a thread on another card too: search for it by number, title or type, in this plan or in any other plan you can edit cards in. It stays **one thread**: a reply, a resolve or a rename on either card shows on both, and a link chip on the thread ("3" with a link icon) lists every card it is on, opening each one.

Before a thread goes into another plan, liveplanner asks first: everyone who can see that card there will be able to read the thread, and its Participants and Editors can reply and resolve it, while its Viewers only read. A thread is never taken off just one card; **Delete Thread…** removes it from every card it is on, after saying how many that is. An Editor can delete any thread, and whoever started a thread can delete their own.

### Who Can Do What

- Editors and Participants start, reply to, resolve, reopen, rename and share threads; a Viewer reads them.
- On a thread shared in from another plan, what you can do follows your role on the card you are looking at.
- A card shows how many comments its open threads hold, beside its votes. Whether the count shows is part of the card type's **Display** ([Card Types](/help/plan/card-types/)).
- Each open thread you are in, or that mentions you, is a row of its own in the [Inbox](/help/explorer/activity/), with its title.

Trashing or archiving a card keeps its threads; deleting it for good from the Trash takes it off them (a thread on no other card goes with it). Comments are not undone with **Undo**. A card holds up to 50 threads, a thread up to 200 comments, and a thread can be on up to 20 cards.

## Right-Click a Card

<Figure caption="A card's right-click menu: Open, Duplicate, Edit Card Type, a Move to row per column, Flag, Archive and Trash.">
  <PlanCardMenuScene />
</Figure>

**Open**, **Duplicate** (a copy right after it, without its votes), **Edit Card Type**, **Move to** each of the board's other columns that the card's type allows, **Flag**, **Archive**, and **Trash**. A Participant gets **Open**, **Duplicate**, **Move to** and **Flag**; a Viewer gets **Open** alone. A face-down card on a board that hides writing has no menu.

## Find a Card

The **Find** button in the bottom-right corner (beside **Types**) lists every card in the plan that isn't archived or in the Trash, of every card type, your own included, newest change first:

- **Search** by number (`12` or `#12`), title, description or a type's name (**Person** finds every Person card).
- **Add Filter**, at the right of the search box, narrows the list by any field, **Card Type** included: pick a field, then one of its values (each says how many cards have it), such as **State: Done** or **Assignee: No assignee**. Each filter shows inside the search box; add up to eight. A filter's cross takes it off (so does Backspace in an empty box), and the box's clear takes off the words and every filter.
- **All Cards** and **Not on a Board** switch between every card and the ones no board shows, on any tab: the strays a renamed or removed column left behind, or cards of a type every board with their status leaves out.
- Click a card to open it. Someone who can edit can move a card to the Trash from its bin without leaving the list.

## The Trash

Every way of deleting a card moves it to the Trash: **Trash** in its menus, **Delete** on a focused card, or dragging it onto the **Trash** button at the start of the bottom-right strip (it opens out into a drop target as you drag). The button's badge counts the cards in it. On a phone there is no Trash button, so use **Trash** in a card's menu (hold your finger on the card).

Open the Trash to **Restore** a card to the column it came from, **Delete** one for good (it asks first), or **Empty Trash** (which empties it at once, without asking). Deleting for good can't be undone. The Trash button is only shown to Editors.

## Archive a Card

**Archive** takes a card off every board without deleting it, keeping its status for when it comes back. An **Archive** board shows every archived card; **Restore**, or dragging it onto another board, brings it back.
