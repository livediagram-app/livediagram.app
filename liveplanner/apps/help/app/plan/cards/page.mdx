A **card** shows one [item](/help/plan/items/) on a [board](/help/plan/boards/). The same item can sit on more than one board, and a change to it shows everywhere at once.

## Add a Card

- **From the board's header:** beside the board's name sits a little card for each card type the board takes, in its colour with its icon. Click one to add a card of that type to the column it starts in, opened and ready to name, or drag it onto the board and let go in the column and row you want it in: the cards there make room, and a column that type can't go in turns red and says why. They aren't shown on a phone; use **+ Add card** there.
- **+ Add card** at the foot of a column (or of a row, on a board with swimlanes) opens **Add a Card**: press a type (Project, Task, Note, Idea, Action or [your own](/help/plan/card-types/)) and a card of it lands at the end of the column and opens straight away with its title selected, ready to name.
- **With the keyboard:** focus a card and press **N** to open **Add a Card** for its column.

On an empty board, the first column's button reads **Add your first card**.

A board shows and offers only the card types set in its menu's **Supported Cards**; cards of other types are hidden on it, not deleted. A card dropped into a row takes that row's assignee, priority or field.

## Open a Card

Click a card to open its panel. It closes with **Escape** or the close button, and every change saves as you make it.

<Figure caption="The card panel: a bar back to the card before, the card's tabs and Linked as Parent on the left, Details on the right.">
  <PlanCardPanelScene />
</Figure>

- **The header:** the card's type (change it from the picker; a plain label when there is no other type it could be), its number, the **⋯** menu (Duplicate, Flag, Archive, **Repeat**, Trash, **Edit Card Type**, which opens the card's type in the type editor, **Copy Card Link**, and **Help**, which opens this page), **Maximise Card** (below) and the close button.
- **On the left:** the title, then the card type's **tabs** (Overview by default) and their fields, then **Activity**, the card's own history of who changed what ([Card Activity](/help/plan/card-activity/)). The **description** is rich text: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, size, colour, headings, links, and bullet or numbered lists, from the toolbar over it or the usual shortcuts. It reads as text until you click it. Every **Long Text** field the card type has works the same way.
- **On the right, in Details:** status, assignee and parent, then priority, labels, estimate, due date and, on a plan, start date (a start after the due date says **Starts after it is due**). Labels are coloured chips, and so are the options of a **Choice** or **Multiple Choice** field: click the field to pick from a list (with a search box when it is long, ticks for a Multiple Choice, and **Clear**). Click into the field and the plan's labels open under it, most used first, each with how many cards carry it; type to narrow them, then press Enter or click one to add it, or pick **Create** for a new one. A label typed in another case takes the plan's spelling, so near-duplicates don't pile up, and a label no card uses any more is forgotten. **Open** beside a parent takes you to it. At the foot: who made the card and who last changed it.
- **A checklist** shows its progress ("2 of 5"); tick steps, edit them in place, and **Add a step**.
- **Linked as Parent:** a plan lists the cards under it (the cards whose **Parent** is that plan) on its first tab, each with its number, title and status. Click one to open it in the same panel, or **New Task** to make one already under it. **Link Existing** puts a card you already have under it: search by number, title or type and pick one. A card already under another plan says so, and you're asked before it moves. Trashed cards are left out; an archived one shows an **Archived** chip.
- **Find your way back:** as you move from a card to its parent or a child, a bar above the card names the one you came from, with this card's number beside it. Click it to go back; keep clicking to retrace your steps one card at a time. Opening a card from a board starts a new trail.
- **The ⋯ menu** holds **Duplicate**, **Flag** (or **Remove Flag**), **Archive** (or **Restore**), **Repeat** (or **Edit Repeat** and **Stop Repeating**), **Copy Card Link** and **Trash**.
- **Maximise Card:** the button just left of the close button grows the card to fill the window, with roomier Details, and turns into **Minimise Card** to put it back. Your choice is remembered: every card you open afterwards, in any plan and on any device you're signed in on, opens the same way until you press it again. It isn't on a phone, where the card already fills the screen.
- **Link to a card:** while a card is open, the page's address points at it, so refreshing or sharing that address opens the plan with the card open. **Copy Card Link** in the **⋯** menu copies it for you. The link opens only for people who can already see the plan, and each sees the card as their role allows. Press your browser's **Back** to close a card you opened.

On a phone the panel rises from the bottom, with **Details** as its first tab and the bar back above it. You can assign a card to yourself, to the members of the plan's team (when it lives in a team's library) or to a custom assignee; a plan in My Work or This Browser offers only you and custom assignees, and so does a guest's. In the open Assignee list, people with a liveworkspace account have the small liveworkspace mark to the left of their name; custom assignees have none. A Participant's **⋯** menu offers **Duplicate**, **Flag** and **Copy Card Link** but not Archive, Repeat, Trash or Edit Card Type; a Viewer opens cards to read them, with **Copy Card Link** and **Help** in the **⋯** menu. Every role can maximise a card.

## Attachments and a Cover Image

Keep screenshots, mock-ups and photos on the card itself. Give its card type the **Attachments** field first: in the [type editor](/help/plan/card-types/), **Add Field**, then **Image**, and it's under **Ready to use** ("Pictures on the card, and its cover").

- **Add pictures:** press **Add Images** in the card's Attachments section to pick files (several at once), drop files on it, or paste an image while the card is open. You can also drop a file straight onto a card on the board: it rings and reads **Drop to Attach**, then **Uploading…**. Each can be a PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF of up to 10 MB, and a card holds up to 20.
- **Each picture** is a row with its thumbnail, name and size. Click it to open it full size in a new tab; its **⋯** menu holds **Open**, **Download**, **Set as Cover** (or **Remove Cover**) and **Remove**.
- **A cover image:** **Set as Cover** makes that picture the card's cover. On a **Detailed** card whose type shows **Cover Image** (placed from the type's **Display** tab), it fills the top of the card on the board.
- **On the board**, the **Attachments** card field shows a picture glyph and how many the card holds.
- **Who can:** Editors and Participants add, remove and pick the cover; a Viewer opens and downloads them. Removing a picture takes it off the card (Undo puts it back); trashing the card keeps its pictures for when it's restored. A duplicate of the card shows the same pictures without copying them.
- **This Browser** plans can't hold attachments: save the plan to your account first.

## Flag a Card

**Flag** (in a card's right-click menu or its panel's **⋯** menu) marks a card for attention: it shows a red flag at the end of its title on every board it is on, and **Flagged** beside its number in the panel. Flagging changes nothing else about the card; **Remove Flag** takes it off.

## Repeat a Card

**Repeat** (in a card's panel **⋯** menu) makes a fresh copy of the card on a rule, for routine work like a weekly report. Pick **Every** **Day**, **Weekday** (Monday to Friday), **Week** on the days you tick, or **Month** on a date (a shorter month uses its last day); whether it **Ends** **Never** or **On a date**; and which column a copy **Lands In** (its type's starting state, or one you pick).

- **Each copy** keeps the title, description, checklist (every row unticked), labels, assignee and fields, with its start and due dates moved on to its own day. It is made by **Repeat**, and its Activity says it came from a repeating card. Only the original repeats.
- **Days are yours**: the rule's days are the days where you set it, made from midnight there, so "Monday" is your Monday wherever the rest of the team is. One copy comes per day, even if the plan wakes late: missed days are skipped, never made in a burst.
- **On the card**: a repeating card shows a small repeat glyph at the end of its title, and the panel's Details names the rule and the next day, with **Edit** and **Stop** beside it.
- **Stopping**: **Stop Repeating** ends it; a card in the Trash makes no copies until it is restored, and deleting it ends it. Copies already made stay.
- **Who can**: only an Editor sets, changes or stops a repeat; a Participant and a Viewer see the glyph and the line. A plan in This Browser makes no copies, so its menu offers no **Repeat**.

## Comment Threads

A card holds **comment threads**: as many separate conversations as it needs, each with its own title, replies and resolved state. They sit at the end of the card's **Overview** tab, under **Comments**, and use the same [comments](/help/collaboration/comments/) as the rest of the plan.

A card type can hold more Comments fields of its own, say a **Design Review** beside **Comments** ([Card Types](/help/plan/card-types/)). Each shows the same way under its own name, listing only its threads; **New Thread** in a field starts the thread there.

<Figure caption="A card's comment threads: two open, the first on three cards, and one resolved at the bottom.">
  <PlanCardThreadsScene />
</Figure>

- **Start a thread** with **New Thread**. Type the first comment and press **Start Thread** (or **Cmd+Enter**, **Ctrl+Enter** on Windows; **Escape** cancels). The thread is titled for you from your name and the start of the comment ("Gentle Falcon: Basket empties after switching restaurants"), shown under the box as you type.
- **Open threads** come first, the most recently active at the top, each closed when you open the card (one you have just started, or one a link from your Inbox or an email takes you to, opens). Each shows its title, the people in it, how many comments it has and when it was last active; click its header to open or close it.
- **Reply** in the box at the foot of an open thread and press **Reply**. Type **@** to mention someone.
- **React** to a comment with one of six emoji (👍 👎 😄 🎉 ❤️ 👀): **Add Reaction** (the smile beside a comment) opens them under its text. Each emoji in use is a chip with a count; yours are highlighted, pressing a chip adds or takes back yours, and hovering it (or a long press) names who reacted. A resolved thread keeps its reactions but takes no new ones.
- **Resolve** a thread once it is settled, from **Resolve** beside **Reply**. With a reply typed, it reads **Comment and Resolve**: your reply is posted, then the thread is resolved, in one press. It moves, with every comment kept, under **Resolved** at the bottom of the list, drawn quieter with who resolved it. **Reopen** brings it back.
- **The ⋯ menu** on a thread holds **Rename** (or press **F2** on its header; clear the title to go back to the automatic one), **Add to Card…**, **Resolve** or **Reopen**, and **Delete Thread…**.
- **Edit** and **Delete** your own comments from the buttons beside them. Someone who can edit the plan can delete any comment.
- A comment an AI tool wrote through the [MCP server](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/) or the [CLI](/help/developers/cli/) says **Generated by AI** above it.

### One Thread on Several Cards

**Add to Card…** puts a thread on another card in the same plan too: search for it by number, title or type. Threads can't be added to cards in other plans. It stays **one thread**: a reply, a resolve or a rename on either card shows on both, and a link chip on the thread ("3" with a link icon) lists every card it is on, opening each one.

A thread is never taken off just one card; **Delete Thread…** removes it from every card it is on, after saying how many that is. You can delete a thread only when every comment in it is yours; once anyone else has commented, it can't be deleted (by Editors either), only resolved, so the ⋯ menu greys **Delete Thread…** and says to resolve it instead.

### Who Can Do What

- Editors and Participants start, reply to, react to, resolve, reopen, rename and add threads to other cards; a Viewer reads them and sees the reactions.
- A card shows how many comments its open threads hold, beside its votes. Whether the count shows is part of the card type's **Display** ([Card Types](/help/plan/card-types/)).
- Each open thread you are in, or that mentions you, is a row of its own in the [Inbox](/help/explorer/activity/), with its title.

Trashing or archiving a card keeps its threads; deleting it for good from the Trash takes it off them (a thread on no other card goes with it). Comments are not undone with **Undo**. A card holds up to 50 threads, a thread up to 200 comments, and a thread can be on up to 20 cards.

## Right-Click a Card

  <PlanCardMenuScene />
</Figure>

**Open**, **Duplicate** (a copy right after it, without its votes), **Edit Card Type**, **Move to** each of the board's other columns that the card's type allows, **Select** (to [pick several cards](/help/plan/select-cards/) and change them together), **Flag**, **Archive**, and **Trash**. A Participant gets **Open**, **Duplicate**, **Move to**, **Select** and **Flag**; a Viewer gets **Open** alone. A board in a Dashboard slot has no **Select**. A face-down card on a board that hides writing has no menu.

## Find a Card

The **Find** button in the bottom-right corner (beside **Types**) lists every card in the plan that isn't archived or in the Trash, of every card type, your own included, newest change first:

- **Search** by number (`12` or `#12`), title, description or a type's name (**Person** finds every Person card).
- **Add Filter**, at the right of the search box, narrows the list by any field, **Card Type** included: pick a field, then one of its values (each says how many cards have it), such as **State: Done** or **Assignee: No assignee**. Each filter shows inside the search box; add up to eight. A filter's cross takes it off (so does Backspace in an empty box), and the box's clear takes off the words and every filter.
- **All Cards** and **Not on a Board** switch between every card and the ones no board shows, on any tab: the strays a renamed or removed column left behind, or cards of a type every board with their status leaves out.
- Click a card to open it. Someone who can edit can move a card to the Trash from its bin without leaving the list.

## The Trash

Every way of deleting a card moves it to the Trash: **Trash** in its menus, **Delete** on a focused card, or dragging it onto the **Trash** button at the start of the bottom-right strip (it opens out into a drop target as you drag). The button's badge counts the cards in it. On a phone there is no Trash button, so use **Trash** in a card's menu (hold your finger on the card).

Open the Trash to **Restore** a card to the column it came from, **Delete** one for good (it asks first), or **Empty Trash** (which empties it at once, without asking). Deleting for good can't be undone. The Trash button is only shown to Editors.

## Archive a Card

**Archive** takes a card off every board without deleting it, keeping its status for when it comes back. An **Archive** board shows every archived card; **Restore**, or dragging it onto another board, brings it back.
