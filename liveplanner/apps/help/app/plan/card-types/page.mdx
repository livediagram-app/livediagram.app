Every [item](/help/plan/items/) has a **type**, such as a Project, a Task, a Bug or an Idea. Your boards and templates bring the types they need, and you can change them or add your own, such as a **Customer call** with an **Outcome** and a **Followed up** box. Card types belong to the plan, so everyone working on it sees the same ones, at once.

## Open Card Types

Press the **Types** button in the bottom-right corner (the strip shows once the tab has a board, a chart or a Sheet that shows cards). The panel lists every card type in one list, each with its colour, its glyph and how many fields it has; a type no card has yet is drawn a little grey. Whether a template or a board brought a type or you made it yourself, it works the same way: you can rename, change or delete any of them.

</Figure>

- **Add New Card Type** makes a new one; press a type to edit it.
- **Duplicate** (the copy button on a type, or **Duplicate** in its editor) starts a new type from that one: same colour, glyph, fields and tabs, named "… copy" for you to rename. Nothing is added until you press **Save**.
- **A new plan has no card types.** A template, or a board picked from **Start Planning**, gives the plan just the card types it needs: a Hiring Pipeline has **Role**, **Candidate** and **Onboarding Task**, a Kanban board **Task** and **Action**. A **Blank** board brings none, so make your own or use **Add Default Types**. Boards you add later bring any of their types the plan doesn't have yet.
- The types keep their order: the order the boards brought them in, then yours in the order you added them. A board's **Add a Card** follows the same order.
- **Add Default Types** adds any of Project, Task, Note, Idea and Action the plan doesn't have, without changing the types it has. It asks first, naming the types it will add: press **Add Types** to go ahead, or **Cancel**. It shows only while one of them is missing. The five never appear unless you ask for them.

Only an Editor can change the types: a Participant or a Viewer sees the list but can't change it.

## Edit a Type

The type editor (titled with the type's name, **Edit Task Card Type** say, or **New Card Type**) opens over the tab (on a phone, it slides up from the bottom) in three tabs: **Configuration**, **States** and **Display**. It opens on **Configuration**, one scrolling page: the type's name and colour at the top, then **Fields** under them. A small red dot on a tab shows where something needs fixing before you can save. At its top right, **Show Me** runs a short tour of making a card type, the help link brings you here, and the cross closes it without saving.

- **Name and colour** (at the top of **Configuration**): one **Name** field holds all three. Press the **Glyph** at its start to choose one, type the name after it, and press the **Colour** swatch at its end to open the colour picker (any colour). Together they're how its cards look. Two types can't share a name.
- **Fields** (in **Configuration**, under the name and colour): what its card panel offers, laid out the way the panel shows them: **On the Card** (Title, and Votes), **Details** (the column beside the tabs) and each tab. **Title** and **Status** are always there, and a **Plan** always keeps **Start** and **Due**. Drag a field by the handle at its left to reorder it within its group (it works with a finger too), or open its **⋯** menu to move it up or down, to another group, or take it off.
- **Add Field** works in two steps. First, **pick** a kind: every kind a field can be is in one list, whether liveplanner's own fields use it or not. Type in **Search fields** at the top to find one by name or by an everyday word ("money" finds Currency, "progress" Percent, "hours" or "timer" Duration, "stars" Rating, "parent" Link to Card, "yes/no" Checkbox, "due" Date, "photo" Image), or browse the six groups, opened one at a time: **Text & links** (**Text**, **Long Text**, **Link**, **Email**, an address that opens in your mail app, **Phone**, a number that calls on a phone), **Numbers & dates** (**Number**, **Currency**, an amount in the currency you pick, shown as "£12,500", **Percent**, how far along something is, below, **Duration**, hours and minutes or time tracked with a timer, below, **Rating**, stars out of a scale you pick from 3 to 10, **Date**, **Date and Time** and **Date Range**, below), **Choices** (**Choice**, one of the options you list, **Multiple Choice**, several of them, **Checkbox** and **Labels**), **People & teamwork** (**Person**, **Checklist**, **Comments** and **Votes**), **Colour & images** (**Colour** and **Image**, a picture you upload) and **Linked cards** (**Link to Card**, a link to one card of the types you tick under **Links To**, say a Project or an Epic, and **Calculated**, below). Then, **shape** it. When the type doesn't have liveplanner's own field of that kind yet, **Ready to use** comes first: the **Assignee** under Person, **Start Date** and **Due Date** under Date, **Priority** under Choice, and so on, each saying what uses it (the Gantt chart, Workload, notifications). One press adds it. Or make your own: name it, and fill in only what that kind needs (for a Choice, its options, one a row: press Enter for the next, or paste a list to add one a line); switch on **Required** if you like, watch the preview of a card change as you go, and press **Add** and its name. A type can hold more than one field of a kind, say a **Reviewer** as well as its Assignee, or two Colours, each with its own name and value; the Assignee notifies its person, and any other Person field can too (**Notifies**, below). **Comments** and **Votes** work the same way: **Ready to use** offers liveplanner's own while the type lacks it, and a field of your own needs only its name. **All kinds** (or Escape) takes you back to pick another. Use the arrow keys to move through the list, and Enter to pick.
- **Image fields** hold one picture. In the card, press **Add an Image** (or drop a PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF of up to 10 MB onto it) to upload one; **Replace** and **Remove** change it. The picture lives in your image gallery, and everyone the plan is shared with sees it, Viewers included. Place it on cards from the **Display** tab to show a small thumbnail.
- **Attachments** hold up to 20 pictures on a card, one of them its cover: pick **Image** in **Add Field** and it's under **Ready to use**. Place **Cover Image** on the **Detailed** card in the **Display** tab and the cover fills the top of each card that has one. See [Attachments and a Cover Image](/help/plan/cards/#attachments-and-a-cover-image).
- **Edit** on a custom field changes its name and its options (and, for a Link to Card field, the types it **Links To**, or for a Currency field its **Currency**). To show it on cards, place it from **Available Fields** on the **Display** tab, like any field.
- **Percent fields** say how far along something is, from 0 to 100. In the card panel, drag the bar (or press anywhere on it), use the arrow keys, or type a number in the box beside it; a number over 100 is kept as 100, and emptying the box clears it. On cards it shows as a short bar with the number, say "40%", and exports draw the same bar. Boards sort, group swimlanes and search by it like a number. In a CSV, write the number ("40" or "40%"); agents and the command line do the same, and a value outside 0 to 100 is turned away.
- **Date and Time fields** hold a day and a time, say a meeting at "Fri 31 Oct, 17:00". Everyone sees it in their own time zone, so 17:00 in London reads 13:00 in New York; hover it to see your zone and, when it was set somewhere else, the time there. Once it has passed it turns red on the card. A plain **Date** field stays a day and never moves with time zones.
- **Date Range fields** hold a start and an end in one field, say an event's days or a trip: "31 Oct to 2 Nov". Fill in **From** and **To** in the card panel; the end can never come before the start, so moving one past the other moves both. Switch on **Include Time** when you add the field to give each end a time too (it can't be changed later). Pick a Date and Time or a Date Range as a Gantt chart's or calendar's dates in its settings: a range alone draws a bar from start to end, and a calendar shows a timed card with its time.
- **Phone fields** hold a phone number as you type it, say "+44 20 7946 0958": digits, spaces, brackets, dashes and a leading +. The phone button beside it calls the number on a phone. Export CSV writes it exactly as typed, the + and the spacing kept.
- In a **CSV** and from **AI agents**, a Date and Time is written as an ISO 8601 time with its offset, say `2026-10-31T17:00:00Z`, and a Date Range as two days or times joined by `/`, say `2026-10-31/2026-11-02`. Import CSV also reads a time without an offset ("2026-10-31 17:00") in your own time zone, and a range written "31/10/2026 to 2/11/2026".
- **Duration fields** hold how long something takes or took, say an **Estimate** of "2h 30m". Type it as "2h 30m", "90m", "1.5h" or "2:30" (a number on its own is minutes) and it reads back as "2h 30m". Boards sort by it, rollups add it up (a Sum of Durations stays a Duration), and formulas read it as minutes, so `Estimate / 60` gives hours. In a CSV and from agents, write it the same way.
- **Time tracking**: switch on **Track Time** when you add a Duration field (call it **Time Spent**) and the card panel gets a **Start Timer** button. The timer keeps counting when you reload or switch devices, and everyone sees a green pill with your picture on the card while it runs. **Stop** records an entry with your name and the time (a timer stopped in under a minute records nothing), **Add Time** records time you worked without the timer, and the field shows the total of everyone's entries. Two people can run timers on the same card at once; both count. A Participant tracks and removes only their own time, an Editor can stop or remove anyone's, and a Viewer sees it all but can't change it. Undo takes back an Add Time or a removed entry. Duplicating a card leaves its time behind. The **Time** board widget adds up the hours on a board's cards and shows how many timers are running.
- **Calculated fields** work their value out for you. Pick **Calculated** in **Add Field**, then a **Calculation**: **Child Count** counts the cards that link to the card through a Link to Card field (a Project's tasks, through Parent), and **Children Done (%)** shows how many of them are done, meaning in a column that counts as done, as a percentage. Switch on **Show as Percent Bar** for a Children Done and it draws as a Percent field does, a bar on the card, in the panel and in exports. The card panel shows the value with a calculator icon and can't be typed into; place it on cards from the **Display** tab. It updates as soon as a linked card moves, is added or goes to the Trash. Forms, Import CSV and sorting leave it out (but not a Formula, Rollup or Lookup, below).
- **Formula fields** do sums with the card's own fields. Pick **Calculated**, then **Formula**, and write it with your field names: `Budget - Spent`, `Estimate * Rate`, `days(Due - Start)` or `if(Score >= 8, "Strong", "Weak")`. Press a field or a function under the box to write it where the cursor is; a name with other characters goes in braces, like `{Children Done (%)}`. Use `+ - * /`, brackets, comparisons (`=`, `!=`, `<`, `>=` and so on) and the functions `round`, `min`, `max`, `abs`, `if`, `and`, `or`, `not`, `days`, `today`, `concat` and `len`. Pick a **Result** (Number, Currency, Percent, Text, Date or Checkbox) to say how it reads: `Spent / Budget * 100` as a Percent reads "40%", and **Show as Percent Bar** draws it as a bar. The line under the box checks it as you type, naming a field it doesn't know or a bracket it's missing, and **Preview** works it out on one of your cards. A field that holds several people or cards counts with `len()` and joins with `concat()`. Renaming a field keeps formulas working; deleting one shows the problem on the field instead of a value. Boards can sort by a Number, Currency, Percent or Date formula, lay swimlanes by one that gives a number, a percent, a date, text or Yes and No, and Export CSV includes it.
- **Rollup and Lookup fields** read other cards for you, so nobody copies values by hand. Pick **Calculated**, then **Rollup** to total the cards that link here: choose **Through** (**All Linked Cards**, or one Link to Card field such as Parent), what to **Calculate** (**Sum**, **Average**, **Min**, **Max**, **Count**, **Count Done**, **Earliest** or **Latest**) and the **Field** to read, say a Project's **Total Points** (Sum of Points), **Total Spend** (Sum of Cost) or **Last Due** (Latest Due Date). Archived cards count; cards in the Trash don't. Pick **Lookup** to show a field of the card a Link to Card field links to, say a Task's **Project Due** or **Client Email**. Each reads as the field it reads: a Sum of a Currency field stays in that currency, a Lookup of a date is a date, and an Average of a Percent field can show as a Percent bar. **Preview** works it out on one of your cards. A formula can use a rollup's or lookup's value by its name, and a rollup can total a formula. Boards sort and lay swimlanes by them, and Export CSV, sheets, image exports and AI agents read them too.
- **Required fields:** switch on **Required** in a custom field's settings (any kind but Checkbox, Calculated, Image, Comments and Votes) and every new card of the type needs a value for it. Making one, from a board or a linked card's **New** button, first opens a **New Task** dialog (named for the type) asking for the title and each required field; **Create** waits until they're all filled. A card can't have the field emptied afterwards, though you can change it. Cards made before the field was required keep working until someone fills it in. A required field shows a red asterisk after its name, in the card and in the type editor. **Import CSV** skips rows without one and says how many, and a form for the type can't be published until a required question goes to each one.
- **Comments fields** hold comment threads of their own. Give a type a **Design Review** and a **QA Notes** beside its **Comments**, and the card shows each one at the end of its **Overview** tab, under its name, with its own **New Thread** and its own threads. Place one on cards from the **Display** tab and its chip counts the comments in its own open threads. A thread stays in the field it was started in; if the card's type stops offering that field, the thread shows under **Comments** instead (or the type's first Comments field), so none is ever hidden.
- **Votes fields** hold votes of their own, say a **Priority Vote** beside the type's **Votes**. A new one sits **On the Card**, and placed on cards from the **Display** tab it shows its own ▲ count, as Votes does. Votes still come only from a [dot vote](/help/collaboration/session-tools/voting/): when the tab's card types offer more than one Votes field, the vote's set-up asks which one to **Count In** (Votes first). A card whose type lacks the field picked keeps its dots in Votes. Top Voted and sorting by votes read Votes.
- **Notifies:** every Person field has a **Notifies** switch, which tells its person when a card is theirs: it lists under **Assigned to You** in their Inbox, may email them, and shows on the board's **Only Mine**. The **Assignee**'s is in its **⋯** menu in the type editor and starts on; a Person field you add has it in its settings and starts off. Switch it on for a **Reviewer**, say, and the card is theirs too. Someone two fields name is told once, and a custom person (someone without an account) is never told.
- **Holds Several:** a **Person** field or a **Link to Card** field can hold more than one value. Switch on **Holds Several** in its settings (or when you add it) for **Reviewers**, a pair, or **Related** cards. The card panel then shows a chip for each person or card, each with an **×** to take it off, and **+** to add more from a list that stays open while you pick (pick one again to take it off). Cards show the first three people as overlapping discs, or the first three linked numbers, then **+N** for the rest. A board laid out by the field puts the card in **each** person's or card's row; drag it from one row to another to swap that one for the new one. A Person field that notifies tells everyone it holds. Turning the switch off keeps every card's values; the field then shows the first. Agents write several as a comma list, "Sam, Ana", Import CSV takes one for a Person field, and Export CSV joins them with commas.
- **Link cards together:** add a **Link to Card** field to say how one card relates to another. Make a **Person** type and an **Objective** type, then give Objective a Link to Card field named **Owner** that links to Person. On an Objective, **Owner** is a field you click to pick a Person from a list (type to filter a long one); the arrow at its end opens that Person. On the Person, a **Linked as Owner** section lists every Objective it owns, with **New Objective** to make one already linked and **Link Existing** to link one you already have: search for it by number, title or type and pick it. Only cards of the types the field is on are offered (each named by its type when there are several), never the card itself, one already linked here, or an archived or trashed card. A card already linked to another Person says so, and picking it asks first, since it moves here. Undo puts it back. When that list holds more than one card type or state, menus above it narrow it to one type or state. A board can lay its rows by **Owner** too, one row per Person. **Parent** is one of these too: a Task's Link to Card field to Plans. Rename it, point it at other types, or remove it, like any field you add.
- **Blocked By:** switch on **Blocks** in a **Link to Card** field's settings (it turns on **Holds Several** too) and the cards it holds block the card, say a Task's **Blocked By** listing the Tasks it waits on. While any of them is outside a done column, the card shows a small red link with how many are still open, on every board, at every card size and in image exports. The card panel lists its blockers with their state, a tick on the done ones, and the other cards list it under **Blocks**. A Gantt Chart draws an arrow from each blocker to the card it blocks. Trashed and archived blockers are ignored. A card can't wait on itself through other cards: picking one that already waits on it is refused, and the panel says which cards make the loop. Only an Editor sets the links; Participants and Viewers see them.
- **Tabs**: each tab is a group in **Fields**. Rename it in its heading, move it with the arrows, or take it off with the cross (its fields go to **Details**). **Add Tab** makes a new one (up to six); **Add Field** at the foot of a group adds the field straight into it. **Details** and **Overview** can be renamed but always stay, and a tab left empty is dropped when you save. A type starts with one **Overview** tab holding its description, checklist and comments.
- **States** starts with **Order**: the statuses this type uses, numbered in the order the card's **Status** list shows them, on every tab. Drag a status by the handle at its left to move it (it works with a finger too), or focus the handle and press **Alt+Up** or **Alt+Down**; its cross turns it off. Under Order, every status your boards use is listed under its board, each with a tick box; **Select All** and **Deselect All** do them all at once. Tick one and it joins the end of the order. Untick one and cards of that type can never be moved into it: the column turns red with "Task cards can't be Done" when you drag one over it, and the card's **Status** list leaves it out. When you add a board, or a column to a board, its new statuses are added only to the types that board takes: a new Bug Triage board's statuses don't appear on your Stories. A board that takes every type adds them to every type. You can turn them all off: cards of that type then stay in whichever column they're made in. Pick a **Default State** under them to say where a card of that type starts when it's made outside a board (from a linked card's **New** button, or by an AI tool); a card added to a board always takes that column's state. Types a template or board brings start with one already: Plans in **Backlog**, Tasks, Actions and Notes in **To Do**, Ideas in **Ideas**, Candidates in **Applied** and so on, whenever your boards have a state of that name (the menu shows it picked until you choose another).
- **Display** arranges this type's cards at each card size, right on the card: pick **Minimal** or **Detailed**, then drag any field to any part of the card (the header, under the title, the footer...): the field follows your pointer and a bar shows where it will land. To take a field off, drag it onto **Available Fields** or press its cross (shown when you hover it, and always on a touch screen); Escape cancels a drag. **Available Fields** lists the fields not on the card yet: drag one onto the card, or press it to choose where it goes. Each type a template or board brings starts with its own sensible layout (Notes and Ideas lead with their votes, Actions with their due date and checklist); its Reset to Default button puts a size back. When the type has a **Colour** field of its own, **Card Colour** under the card picks which Colour field tints its cards and Gantt bars: the built-in **Colour**, or yours (say a **Team Colour**).
- **Age** is a field you can add from **Available Fields** to show how long each card has sat in its column, as a small "4d" with an hourglass. It stays quiet for the first week, turns amber at 7 days and red at 14 (the same line the Stale Cards widget draws), and moves on by itself at midnight. Cards in a done column never show it. No type shows Age until you place it.
- **Created At**, **Created By**, **Last Edited At** and **Last Edited By** sit with Age under **Filled In Automatically** in **Available Fields**, on every type. Place one to show when a card was made or last changed (a small "Created 3 Oct" or "Edited 31 Oct", the full date and time when you hover it) or who did it (their picture). They fill themselves in and nobody can type into them. A vote's dots, a card's place within its column and the Actions board keeping a card in step with its action don't count as an edit. A card from before these were kept shows nothing rather than a guess. The card's own panel shows the same at the foot of its details: who created it and when, and who last edited it.
- Press **Save** to apply the whole edit, or **Cancel** to leave it as it was. If something is missing (a name, a Choice's options), it says so beside **Save**. **Undo** takes a saved change back.

Taking a field off a type never deletes anything: items keep what they hold in it, and it shows again if you add the field back.

### Limits

A plan holds up to 32 card types. Each type holds up to 24 fields, 12 of them custom, and up to 6 tabs. A Choice field has up to 20 options.

## Build a Type on Another

A card type can **inherit** from another, its **base type**, so several types share one set of fields kept in one place: a **Bug** and a **Story** built on one **Work** type, each adding fields of its own.

- **Set it up:** in the type editor's **Configuration**, under the name, pick the base type in **Inherits From**. The type takes its fields (in the base type's order, with every setting: options, Required, Notifies, Holds Several and the rest), its tabs, its statuses and their order, its Default State, and its **Display** at each card size, at once.
- **What stays its own:** its name, colour, glyph and new-card title. It can **add fields** of its own, which go under **Details**, after the base type's.
- **Inherited fields are locked:** each shows a lock ("Inherited from Work") and has no Edit, Remove or Move. The **States** and **Display** tabs show what it takes, read-only, with "Bug takes its states from Work. Change them on Work, or Detach to give Bug its own."
- **Change the base, change them all:** a field added to, removed from or renamed on Work changes on Bug and Story in the same save. Because the fields are the same, a card keeps its values when its type changes between Work and Bug.
- **Detach** (the scissors beside **Inherits From**, or picking **None**) makes it an ordinary type holding a copy of the base type's settings as they are, no longer following it. Deleting a base type detaches every type built on it the same way, so none loses a field.
- **One level:** a base type can't itself inherit, so a type others build on shows **Inherits From** greyed out, naming them ("Bug and Story inherit from this type."). A base type is still an ordinary type, with cards of its own; a board that takes Work doesn't take Bug unless you tick it too.
- In the **Card Types** panel, a type built on another says "Inherits Work" under its name. The usual limits count its base type's fields and its own together.

Only an Editor sets or changes inheritance. AI agents can set it too, through the [MCP server](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/).

## Delete a Type

Open the type and press **Delete**, then confirm (the last type can go too). Any cards of that type move to the **Trash** with it, so you can restore them there; a restored card shows as a plain Item card, keeping its fields. If a board showed that type alone, it shows and takes every type again; pick the ones it should show in its menu's **Supported Cards**.

## Where Your Types Show

- **+ Add card** on a board offers the types that board shows, set in its menu's **Supported Cards**.
- **Add New Card Type** at the bottom of **Add a Card** makes a new type right from the board: only that board's states start switched on, and once you save, the board offers it.
- A card's panel lets you change its type from the picker at the top.
- A board's rows can be one per type (**Type** under **Swimlanes**), in this panel's order.

When someone saves a change, everyone with the plan open sees their cards and panels update at once. If two people save the same type at the same moment, the later save wins.
