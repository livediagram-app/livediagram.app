A **Form** is a tab of your plan that holds one form (see [Planning Basics](/help/plan/planning-basics/)). You build it in place, share its link, and anyone can fill it in without an account. Each response becomes a card on a [board](/help/plan/boards/) in the same plan, already in the column you chose, so collecting answers leads straight into working on them.

## Start a Form

On a new tab, choose **Forms** in **Start Planning**'s rail, then a starter:

- **Blank Form**: one question to start from.
- **Bug Report**: a summary, what happened, severity and an email, landing on a triage board as Bugs.
- **Feedback**: a star rating and comments.
- **Feature Request**: an idea and how important it is, as Ideas your team can vote on.
- **Event RSVP**: who is coming and what they need.
- **Job Application**: two sections, about the person and their experience, as Candidates.
- **Contact**: a message you can assign and close, as a Request.

Or choose **Describe a Form** and say what it should ask, for example "an event RSVP with dietary needs and plus-ones". AI drafts the questions and where responses go; you check them, pick a board or a sheet, and press **Create Form**. An empty Form tab offers it too. It needs you to be signed in, and it shares Describe a Plan's 10 drafts a day.

A starter picks where responses go: your first board that takes its kind of card, or a new **Responses** board made after the form with **New**, **In Progress** and **Done** columns. The fields its questions fill are added to the card type for you.

Two plan templates come with a form already set up: **Help Desk**'s **Contact Form** sends each message to its **Tickets** board as a Ticket, and **Event Planner**'s **RSVP** adds each reply as a row on its **RSVP Responses** sheet. Like any new form, each waits for you to publish it.

New to forms? With a Form tab open, press Start Forms Tour in the bottom bar, just left of **Search**, for a short tour: building the form in place, adding questions, its responses and Preview, where responses go and **Share Form**. Once you've taken it, the tour leaves the button; **Show Me Around**, at the foot of the panel the cog opens, starts it any time, and switching **Forms Tour** back on under **Settings**, **Getting Started** puts it back on the button.

## Build the form

The tab shows the form as people will see it. Click a question (or Tab to it and press Enter) to edit it: type its question, a description under it, and its options, and choose its type. Its footer has **Answer goes to**, **Move Up**, **Move Down**, **Duplicate**, **Delete** and **Required**. Escape lets go of it.

- **Add a question** with the bar under the selected question: it adds one of the same type. Its arrow opens **Add a Question**, a gallery of every type in five groups (**Text**, **Choices**, **Ratings and Numbers**, **Dates** and **Contact Details**), each with a picture of it being answered and a line on what it collects. Search it, or use the arrow keys and Enter, and the type you pick is added straight away.
- **Add Section** splits the form after the selected question, so people answer it one page at a time with **Next** and **Back** and a progress bar.
- **Drag** a question by the handle at its top to move it, into another section too.
- **Options**: press Enter for the next one, Backspace in an empty one to remove it, Alt with the arrow keys to move it, and paste several lines to add one option per line. **Add “Other”** lets people type their own answer.
- **The cover**: hover the top of the form and choose **Add Cover** for a colour band, plain, as a gradient or dotted.
- **Show a section only sometimes**: under a later section's description, the badge reading **Always shown** opens its rule. Choose **Only when an earlier answer is…**, then the question and the answers that show the section, such as showing "Dietary Needs" only to people who answered **Yes** to "Are you coming?". People who answer otherwise skip it.
- **The look**: a form wears its tab's style, in the editor and on its link alike. Change it with **Style**: the theme gives the form its colour and backdrop, the tab font its lettering, and the finish its boxes (square under Professional, drawn by hand under Sketch, in bold marker under Marker).

### Question types

**Short Answer**, **Paragraph**, **Number** (with a minimum, a maximum and whole numbers only), **Email**, **Phone**, **Link**, **Date**, **Date and Time** (answered in the respondent's own time zone), **Date Range** (a From and a To, with times when you switch on **Include Time**), **Single Choice** (shown as a dropdown past seven options, or always), **Multiple Choice** (with the fewest and most picks), **Rating** (stars, or numbers from 1 to between 3 and 10 with a label at each end) and **Checkbox** (one box to tick, such as agreeing to terms).

## Where answers go

Everything is edited in the form itself. Select a question to change its type, its own settings (shown inside it) and **Required**; the **Answer goes to** badge at its foot opens **Answer Goes To**.

The cog at the end of the bar above the form opens two more:

- **Responses Go To**: the board, the card type and the column every response is made in. **Column** offers only the columns the card type uses, so responses show on that type's boards, and **+ New Board** makes its columns from the type's own states. Picking a card type, or a board (which picks its card type), on a form with no questions yet adds a question for each field people can fill in, already mapped (Undo takes them away and keeps your pick); on a form that has questions, **Add Questions for** the type's fields adds the ones no question asks. It works the other way too: **+ New Card Type** makes a type with a field for each question, named after it, and maps each answer there, and on a card type you already have, **Add Fields for Questions** adds a field for each question whose answer would otherwise land in the card's Description (a question going to the title keeps it, and the first paragraph question stays the card's Description). **Set Automatically** gives every card a field without asking: a choice that is always the same (such as Type: Bug), labels, text, a number, a priority, or a date from the day the response was sent, plus some days for a due date. For a sheet, **Extra Columns** does the same for columns. Removing an extra column (or a question) never deletes anything from the sheet: its column stays with the values earlier responses wrote, and new rows leave it empty.
- **Card Type by Answer**: when the board takes more than one card type and the form has a choice or checkbox question, responses can make a different card type by an answer, such as a **Bug** card when "What type of request is this?" is Bug and a **Feature** card when it is Feature. Each rule picks the question, one or more answers, the card type and its column (only columns that type uses), and **Answers on** that type's cards maps answers to its own fields. Every other answer makes the card type chosen above. A rule whose question, answer, card type or column is removed is listed under **Problems with this Form**, and its responses use the card type chosen above until you fix it, so none is lost. The card type is always picked by liveplanner when a response arrives, never by the person filling in the form.
- **Settings**: **Accepting Responses**, **Close On**, **Response Limit**, and the message people see after sending and once the form has closed.

You can also start a form from a board: open the board's cog and choose **New Form**. Its responses go to that board.

Responses can be **Cards on a Board** or **Rows in a Sheet**. A sheet needs no mapping: each response becomes a row, with a column for every question, headed by its wording. The columns appear as you build the form, before anyone answers, and stay linked to their questions, so you can rename a heading in the sheet and answers still land under it. A response either lands whole in the sheet or the person filling in the form is told it didn't go through: it is never counted without its row. Choose **+ New Sheet** to make one.

**Answer Goes To** decides where each answer is kept on the card:

- **Description**, the default: written into the card's description under its question.
- **Title**: the card's name on the board. One question at most. With none, cards are named after the form and numbered, such as **Bug Report #14**.
- **A field** of the card type that fits the answer, or **New Field**, which adds one named after the question. A choice question mapped to a Choice or Multiple Choice field keeps its options in step with the field.

**Preview** at the top right shows the form exactly as people will, working. Submit in Preview makes a real card on your board, titled **Preview:** and then its title, so you can check every answer lands where you expect. Once the form is published, a preview counts as a response; delete its card when you're done.

## Share it

Press **Share Form** (in the header, where Share is on other tabs) and then **Publish Form**. You get a link to copy or open; anyone with it can fill in the form, and they never see your plan. The same dialog has **Accepting Responses**, to close the form for a while without losing its link, and **Delete Link**. Publishing again makes a new link.

Once it is published, **More Ways to Share** in the same dialog has a **QR Code** to download (PNG or SVG), an **Embed** code to put the form on your own site, and a **Pre-filled Link** that opens the form with some answers already chosen.

While a form is taking responses it is locked, so nobody fills in a form that changes under them. Press **Edit Form** on the strip above it to stop taking responses while you edit; the link stays the same, and turning **Accepting Responses** back on opens it again once Share Form lists no problems. Deleting the board or sheet a form sends to pauses it the same way.

Forms are published from plans saved to liveplanner. On a plan in This Browser, Share Form offers **Sync Plan** first; once it is synced, the same dialog carries on to **Publish Form**.

## Responses

Each response becomes one card in the column you chose, made by the form (its **Created by** names it) and showing **New** until you open it. The card's panel shows its response number and when it came. A count of responses, with how many are new to you, sits at the top of the Form tab with **View on** its board, and the Explorer's **Inbox** lists forms with new responses. The plan's owner also gets an email, at most one every 15 minutes per form; turn it off under **Someone Fills In My Form** in Settings.

If its board, column or card type is removed, the form pauses and asks you to choose where responses go.

Whenever a published form isn't taking responses, a red **Not Taking Responses** sits beside the count, so you never miss it: hover it to see why (its link is switched off, it reached its Close On or Response Limit, its responses have nowhere to go, or its link was suspended) and press it to open where you fix that.

## Who can do what

Editors build, publish, open and close forms. Participants and Viewers see the form as people filling it in do, with how many responses it has, but not its link.

## Safety

People can report a form they were sent from **Report This Form** under it. Reports from three different places suspend its link for good, and the owner is told by email. Forms never ask for passwords: don't use them to collect any.
