A share link is a capability: anyone holding the URL has its level: **Viewer**, **Participant** or **Editor**. That is what makes sharing frictionless, but it also means a link left lying around keeps working. liveplanner gives you two controls to tighten that up, a password on the plan and a lifetime on each link, plus turning a link off outright. All three live in the **Share** dialog, and none of them ever stops you, the owner.

## Share password

You can put a single password on a plan: turn on **Password Protection** in the Share dialog, type it, and press **Save**. Once it is set, anyone opening any share link for that plan must enter it before they can view.

- **One password covers every link** on the plan, [embeds](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/) included, not one password per link. While a password is set, a live image of the plan shows nothing, because an image can't ask for a password.
- **You never need it.** The owner is identified directly, so you never get prompted on your own plan. Neither are members of the [team](/help/collaboration/teams/) whose library holds it.
- **Nobody can read it back.** Only a scrambled (hashed) form of the password is stored, so even you see just "Links need a password." Note it down when you choose it; use **Change** or **Remove** any time.
- **Changing it reaches everyone.** People already in the plan through a link are asked for the new password within a few minutes.

The goal is to stop people guessing or stumbling onto a share URL, not to provide cryptographic protection. See [Share Passwords](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/) for the full walkthrough.

## Link lifetime

When you create a link, **Lasts** sets its lifetime, so access ends automatically without you having to remember to turn it off: **Forever** (the default), **1 week**, **1 month** or **6 months**.

When a link runs out it stops granting access everywhere: the share URL, its embed and live image, and the real-time room. It stays in the Share dialog marked **Expired**, where **Extend** gives it more time. See [Share Link Expiry](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-link-expiry/).

<Note>
  Access is checked with short-lived passes that are renewed every few minutes, so a change to a
  link, its password or a team reaches people already in the plan within a few minutes. Turning a
  link off is faster: everyone using it is sent out straight away.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use the two together for sensitive work: a password stops casual access, and a lifetime bounds how
  long even the right people keep it. Prefer a **Viewer** link when people only need to look, or a
  **Participant** link when they need to add and move cards, comment and vote without changing a
  board's columns or settings, and remember that anyone who can see an embed's page source can see
  its link.
</Tip>
