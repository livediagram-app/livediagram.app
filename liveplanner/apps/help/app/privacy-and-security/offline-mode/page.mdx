Plans are saved to liveplanner so you can reach them from any device and share them. **Offline Mode** is the other place a plan can live: **only in the browser it was made in**, never stored on the server. It suits private, local-first work, or simply trying liveplanner out.

## Where new plans go

- **If you haven't signed in**, your new plans start Offline. Nothing you try out is sent to our servers until you choose to share it, sync it, or sign in and move it to your account. Everything you make from [New Plan](/new), and from the links that create a plan straight away (a template from the home page, say), starts Offline. Use **Sync Plan** on one if you would rather it went to liveplanner now.
- **Once you've signed in**, new plans go to your account.
- **A plan made inside a folder or a team** goes to liveplanner, whoever makes it, because that is where the folder or team lives.

While a new plan is still fresh (yours, one empty tab, not shared, not in a team and nobody else in it), its **Start Planning** screen has a **Sync Plan to Server** switch at the bottom. It says where the plan is saved now, and flipping it moves the plan to liveplanner or Offline, in place.

A plan kept Offline is written to your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) and opens like any other. In the Explorer each one is marked **Offline**, so you always know which plans live only in this browser, and that clearing this browser's site data deletes them. The [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) lists them under **Offline**, a space of its own in its sidebar's **Spaces** group, with how many there are, and the [Offline](/offline) page opens that view.

<Note>
  A plan kept Offline is **not synced and not backed up**. It lives only in this browser: clearing
  your site data, or the browser reclaiming storage, will delete it. Keep anything important on
  liveplanner, or export a copy.
</Note>

## What's different

A plan kept Offline is a normal plan: every editor feature works, including boards and cards, sheets, dashboards, forms, tabs, themes, undo and redo, and export. Only the features that need the server are unavailable, because there's no server copy to power them:

- **Sharing** needs a plan on the server. Opening **Share** on a plan kept Offline tells you so and offers **Sync Plan**: press it and the plan uploads, and the Share options appear in the same dialog, with no reload. Embeds are unavailable until it is synced, for the same reason.
- **Live collaboration** and **teams** don't apply; a plan kept Offline is yours alone. Comments still work, but only you see them.

## Offline Folders

**New Folder** on the Offline page, or the **⋯** menu on its row in the sidebar, makes a folder that lives in this browser too, and folders can hold folders, as in My Work. **Move to Folder** on a plan's menu, or a drag onto a folder, files it. A folder's menu has **Rename**, **New Subfolder**, **Move Folder** and **Delete Folder**; deleting a folder never deletes what is in it, which moves up a level. The folders are kept in this browser alone, like the plans in them.

## Archiving an offline plan

**Move to Archived** works on a plan kept Offline too. It never leaves this browser: it moves out of the Offline list into **Archived**, and it won't open until you move it out. Opening it shows a **Plan Archived** card with **Change Folder**, which puts it back Offline (or, when you are signed in, moves it into your account). Clearing this browser's data removes it like any other plan kept here.

## Moving to your account

- **When you sign in** with plans kept Offline, we offer to move them to your account in a **Move Offline Plans to Your Account?** dialog. Every one is ticked; untick any you want to keep in this browser, then **Move**. **Not Now** leaves them where they are, and we only ask again when there are more.
- **Sync** on a plan's row under Offline, in the Explorer, moves it into your account when you are signed in.
- **Sync Plan**, from the Share dialog, uploads a plan kept Offline to liveplanner, signed in or not. It becomes a normal plan, shareable and listed in your space in the Explorer, and the copy in this browser is removed. A guest's synced plans move to the account when they sign in, like the rest of their work.
- **A plan in a folder keeps its place.** Sync Plan offers the My Work folder with the same name (or My Work itself when there is none) and says where it will go. The folder in this browser stays, empty, until you delete it.

## Taking a plan offline

To go the other way, choose **Take Offline** on one of your own plans in the Explorer (one you can edit, not one reached through a share link). After you confirm, the plan is removed from your account and every other device and lives only in this browser, with no backup.
