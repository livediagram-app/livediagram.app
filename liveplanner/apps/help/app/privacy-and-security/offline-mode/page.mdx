Plans are saved to liveplanner so you can reach them from any device and share them. **Offline Mode** is the other place a plan can live: **only in the browser it was made in**, never stored on the server.

## Where new plans go

Every new plan starts on liveplanner, whether or not you are signed in. A guest's plans belong to their [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/), are listed in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/) like anyone's, and move to your account when you sign in.

A plan kept in This Browser is one you chose to keep there, for private, local-first work. It is written to your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) and opens like any other. A **This Browser** badge next to its name in the editor marks it, so you always know which plans live only in this browser. Hover or focus the badge to read that clearing this browser's site data deletes the plan.

<Note>
  A plan in This Browser is **not synced and not backed up**. It lives only in this browser:
  clearing your site data, or the browser reclaiming storage, will delete it. Keep anything
  important on liveplanner, or export a copy.
</Note>

## What's different

A plan in This Browser is a normal plan: every editor feature works, including boards and cards, sheets, dashboards, forms, tabs, themes, undo and redo, and export. Only the features that need the server are unavailable, because there's no server copy to power them:

- **The Explorer** lists only plans on liveplanner, so a plan in This Browser does not appear there. Keep its address, or move it to liveplanner, to find it again.
- **Sharing** needs a plan on the server. Opening **Share** on a plan in This Browser tells you so and offers **Sync Plan**: press it and the plan uploads, and the Share options appear in the same dialog, with no reload. Embeds are unavailable until it is synced, for the same reason.
- **Live collaboration** and **teams** don't apply; a plan in This Browser is yours alone. Comments still work, but only you see them.
- **Add a Tab to Another Plan** is unavailable for a plan in This Browser in either direction.

## Moving to your account

- **When you sign in** with plans in This Browser, we offer to move them to your account in a **Move Plans in This Browser to Your Account?** dialog. Every one is ticked; untick any you want to keep in this browser, then **Move**. **Not Now** leaves them where they are, and we only ask again when there are more.
- **Sync Plan**, from the Share dialog, uploads a plan in This Browser to liveplanner. It becomes a normal plan, shareable and listed in the Explorer, and the copy in this browser is removed.
