The **Inbox** sits under [Home](/help/explorer/home/) in the Explorer sidebar's Overview group. It lists what is still _open_ for you, in one place for every liveworkspace app.

## What lands here

The page has three sections:

- **Assigned to You:** every open action assigned to you, until it is done.
- **Assigned by You:** open actions you gave to someone else, so you can follow them up.
- **Threads:** unresolved [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) threads you are part of: on your own items, threads you wrote in, and threads where someone mentioned you.

Done actions and resolved threads are not listed. The Inbox is a list of what is outstanding, not a history. When nothing is waiting, the page says **You’re all caught up**.

With **Plans** chosen at the top of the page, the Inbox lists only what comes from your plans; with **All**, it lists every app's, and each row names its app.

## Reading and opening a row

Each row names the item it is from and the thing it is about, with the latest change. Click a row and the item opens at that spot, with the action or thread ready, so you can reply, resolve or finish it there. A row from another app opens in that app.

## The badge

The Inbox row in the sidebar shows a count of the open actions **assigned to you**, so you can tell from any other page whether something needs you.

## Guests and signing in

The Inbox works without an account, like the rest of liveplanner. When you sign in, what you did as a guest moves to your account with your items.
