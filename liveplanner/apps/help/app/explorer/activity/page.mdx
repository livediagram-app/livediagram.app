The **Inbox** sits under [Home](/help/explorer/home/) in the Explorer sidebar's Overview group. Where Home's What Happened tells you what _happened_, the Inbox tells you what is still _open_: the [cards](/help/plan/cards/) assigned to you, responses to your [forms](/help/plan/forms/) you have not seen yet, and the [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) threads nobody has resolved yet.

## What lands here

Everything on the page comes from one of your plans, so the row always knows where it is from.

- **Assigned to You:** every card whose **Assignee** is you, on any plan you can open, until the card is finished: moved to a board's done column or past it, archived, or put in the Trash. A card you assigned to yourself lives here too, so the page doubles as your to-do list across plans.
- **Form Responses:** forms with responses you have not seen yet, on plans you own or reach through a team, such as "3 new responses to **Bug Report**".
- **Open Comment Threads:** unresolved threads you are in. That means threads you wrote a comment in, threads where a teammate [mentioned you](/help/collaboration/comments/#mentioning-a-teammate), and threads on plans you own. Card threads and Sheet cell threads are both listed. A thread you were mentioned in carries a **Mentioned You** hint, and a row on your own plan that you have not replied to yet carries a small **Your plan** hint, so you can tell them apart.

The sections appear in that order, each with a count beside its heading, and a section with nothing in it is left out. When all of them are empty the page says **Nothing waiting on you**.

Finished cards and resolved threads are not listed. The Inbox is a list of what is outstanding, not a history; each plan's [History](/help/explorer/home/#team-and-plan-histories) keeps the past.

## Reading a row

A card's row shows its card type's glyph, its number and title, and the type. A card thread's row shows the card the same way, with the latest comment under it. The last line says where the row is from: the board the card is on and that board's tab, or **Not on a board** when no board in the plan shows it, beside a chip with the plan's name (a team plan's chip carries the team glyph).

On the right is the person: you as the assignee for a card (brand-tinted and labelled **You**), or the latest commenter for a thread, with how long ago the most recent change was beneath. Hovering the avatar names them and, for a thread, says how many comments it holds.

Rows are ordered by their most recent change, newest first, within each section. Each kind of row is capped at 100, far more than anyone scrolls through.

## Opening a row

Click a card's row, or a card thread's, and the plan opens on the tab with the card's board and opens the card, the same as clicking it on the board. From there you update the card, resolve the thread, or reply, with the editor's usual rules about who can edit what. A card that is not on any board opens on the plan's usual tab.

A form's row opens the board its responses land on. A Sheet cell thread's row opens the Sheet's tab.

## The badge

The Inbox row in the sidebar shows a count of the open cards **assigned to you**. It counts only work waiting on you, not form responses or comment threads, and it shows **0** when nothing is waiting, so you can tell from any other Explorer section whether something needs you, or that you are all done.

## Guests and signing in

The Inbox works without an account, like the rest of liveplanner. Signed out, you can still assign cards to yourself and comment on your own plans, and both show up here.

**Your Inbox follows you when you sign up.** Cards you gave yourself and threads you wrote in as a guest still read as yours after you create an account, alongside the plans that move with you.

## Plans saved only in this browser

Plans saved only in This Browser (Offline Mode) show up here too: their open cards and comment threads are listed with everything else, and counted in the badge. Each of their rows carries a **This Browser** pill, because the plan is kept in this browser and nowhere else. Nobody else sees those rows, and another browser or device won't list them.

<Tip>
  Clearing this browser's site data deletes a plan in This Browser, and its rows go with it. Sync
  the plan to keep it on liveplanner and see its cards from anywhere.
</Tip>
