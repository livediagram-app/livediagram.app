The **Inbox** sits under [Home](/help/explorer/home/) in the Explorer sidebar's Overview group. It lists what is still _open_ for you, in one place for every liveworkspace app.

## What lands here

The Inbox lists four kinds of row, newest first, under **Today**, **Earlier This Week** and **Older**:

- **Assigned to you:** every open action assigned to you, and every open Plan card whose Assignee is you, until it is done. A card shows its number, title and type, and the board it sits on.
- **Assigned by you:** open actions you gave to someone else, so you can follow them up.
- **Form responses:** forms in plans you own or reach through a team that have responses you haven't seen yet, such as **3 new responses to Bug Report**. Opening the form marks them seen.
- **Open comment threads:** unresolved [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) threads you are part of, on elements and on Plan cards: on your own plans, threads you wrote in, and threads where someone mentioned you. A thread where someone mentioned you says **Mentioned you**; one on your plan that you haven't joined yet says **On your plan**; one where someone answered you says **Replied to you**, and one where you wrote last says **You replied**. A Plan card can hold several threads, and each is a row of its own, titled with the thread's title and placed on its card; a thread shared onto cards in two of your plans is listed once.

Above the list, the filter chips narrow it, each with its count: **All**, **My Actions**, **Assigned by Me**, **Comments** and **Form Responses**. The chip you pick is remembered on this device.

Done actions and cards, and resolved threads, are not listed. The Inbox is a list of what is outstanding, not a history. When nothing is waiting, the page says **You’re all caught up**, with a link to how actions work.

With **liveplanner** chosen at the top of the page, the Inbox lists only what comes from your plans; with **All Apps**, it lists every app's, and each row names its app.

## Reading and opening a row

Each row names the plan it is from and where in it, with the latest change and when: the action's note, or the latest comment with its writer's initials. Choose a row and it opens in the reading pane beside the list. There, **Reply** (or `R`) answers a thread, **Resolve** or **Mark Done** (or `E`) settles it, and **Open in liveplanner** (or `Enter`) opens the plan at that spot: an action with its popover, a thread with its comments, a card on its board with the card open, and a form's responses on the board they land on. A row from another app opens in that app. `J` and `K`, or the arrow keys, move between rows.

On a phone, and in the editor's Explorer panel, the reading pane takes the list's place, with the **Inbox** bar at its top (or the browser's Back, or `Escape`) to return.

In the editor's [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/), the Inbox also lists the rows of plans saved only in this browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)), each marked **This Browser**. Nobody else sees those rows, and the Explorer page lists only what your account or guest identity holds.

Come back to a page that has been open a while and the Inbox reads itself again, so it is never a day out of date.

## The badge

The Inbox row in the sidebar shows a count of the open actions and Plan cards **assigned to you**, so you can tell from any other page whether something needs you. It shows **0** when nothing does, and nothing while the Inbox is still loading.

## Guests and signing in

The Inbox works without an account, like the rest of liveplanner. When you sign in, what you did as a guest moves to your account with your items.
