The **Inbox** sits under [Home](/help/explorer/home/) in the Explorer sidebar's Overview group. It lists what is still _open_ for you, in one place for every liveworkspace app.

## What lands here

The page has four sections, each left out when it is empty:

- **Assigned to You:** every open action assigned to you, and every open Plan card whose Assignee is you, until it is done. A card shows its number, title and type, and the board it sits on.
- **Assigned by You:** open actions you gave to someone else, so you can follow them up.
- **Form Responses:** forms in plans you own or reach through a team that have responses you haven't seen yet, such as **3 new responses to Bug Report**. Opening the form marks them seen.
- **Open Comment Threads:** unresolved [comment](/help/collaboration/comments/) threads you are part of, on elements and on Plan cards: on your own plans, threads you wrote in, and threads where someone mentioned you. A thread where someone mentioned you says **Mentioned You**; one on your plan that you haven't joined yet says **Your plan**.

Done actions and cards, and resolved threads, are not listed. The Inbox is a list of what is outstanding, not a history. When nothing is waiting, the page says **Nothing waiting on you**, with a link to how actions work.

With **Plans** chosen at the top of the page, the Inbox lists only what comes from your plans; with **All**, it lists every app's, and each row names its app.

## Reading and opening a row

Each row names the plan it is from and where in it, with the latest change and when: the action's note, or the latest comment with its writer's initials. Click a row and the plan opens at that spot: an action opens with its popover, a thread with its comments, a card on its board with the card open, and a form's responses on the board they land on. A row from another app opens in that app.

Plans kept only in this browser have their rows here too, marked **This Browser**.

Come back to a page that has been open a while and the Inbox reads itself again, so it is never a day out of date.

## The badge

The Inbox row in the sidebar shows a count of the open actions and Plan cards **assigned to you**, so you can tell from any other page whether something needs you. It shows **0** when nothing does, and nothing while the Inbox is still loading.

## Guests and signing in

The Inbox works without an account, like the rest of liveplanner. When you sign in, what you did as a guest moves to your account with your items.
