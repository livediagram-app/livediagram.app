A **default folder** is where your new plans go when you create one without choosing a place. You can keep one for each of these, listed under **New plans that open as**:

- **Plans**: every new plan.
- **Retrospectives**: plans made from any retrospective template.
- **Kanban boards**: plans made from the Kanban template.

A plan goes to the most specific default that fits it: a retrospective's or a Kanban board's first, then the one for every plan. So a retrospective goes to your Retrospectives folder even if you also have one for Plans. With none set, it lands in My Work. Your defaults are your own: teammates choose theirs. Default folders are stored with your account or guest identity, not in the browser.

## Setting a default from a folder

Open a folder's menu (its **⋯** button, or right-click the folder) and choose **Use as Default for**. Under **New plans that open as**, tick the kinds of plan that should land there; the list stays open so you can tick several in one go. Each kind points at one folder, so ticking it here moves it from wherever it was; unticking it sends those plans back to My Work.

**My Work** has the same menu: a ticked entry there means those plans land in My Work. Choosing an unticked one clears its default folder.

You can use a team's folders too, as long as you are a member of the team. A team's own top level cannot be a default: create inside the team to put a plan there.

## Seeing your defaults

A folder that is one of your defaults shows a small mark after its name, in the sidebar, the Explorer's lists and cards, and the editor's Explorer panel: the icon of each kind it receives (two at most, then **+N**). Hover it to read, for example, "Default folder for new retrospectives".

## When you create a plan

The New Plan wizard never asks where a plan goes: choose what you are making, and the plan is filed in your default folder for it, or in My Work when you have none. Pick a template on its start afterwards, and a plan made a moment ago moves to that template's default folder, if it has one (a toast says so).

- Starting from inside a folder or a team keeps you there: that folder wins over your default.
- To file one plan somewhere else, use **Change Folder** on it afterwards.
- While you try things out as a guest, new plans are saved in this browser instead, so they have no folder until you move them to your account.

## In Settings

**Settings > Plans > Default Folders** lists every kind with the folder it goes to, or My Work. **Change** picks a folder; **Clear** sends that kind back to My Work.

If a default folder has been deleted, or belongs to a team you have left, the row says so, for example "Retros (deleted), using My Work", and new plans go to the next default that fits, or My Work. Deleting a folder that is one of your defaults warns you first.

<Tip>
  Defaults only decide where a new plan starts. Moving a plan later, or changing a default,
  never moves plans you already have.
</Tip>
