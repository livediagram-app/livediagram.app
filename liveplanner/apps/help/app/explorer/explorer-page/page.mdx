The full-page [Explorer](/explorer) is your library: a sidebar on the left to move around, and the page you picked on the right, with its folders and items. Opening `/explorer` takes you to **Home**. (For the compact version inside the editor, see the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).)

The Explorer is shared by every liveworkspace app. It lives once, on liveworkspace.app, and opens at liveplanner's own `/explorer` too, so a folder can hold a Plan beside a Canvas from livediagram and a Design from livedesign. Its header shows the liveworkspace logo and then liveplanner's; click liveplanner's to go back to liveplanner. Your folders, favourites, sharing and Trash are the same whichever app you open it from.

## liveplanner or All Apps

At the top right of most pages, a two-way switch chooses what the lists show:

- **liveplanner** shows only liveplanner's plans.
- **All Apps** shows every item from every app. Each row then carries its app's name, and the lists say "items" rather than "plans".

Coming from liveplanner, the Explorer opens on **liveplanner**, and your choice holds as you move from page to page. Opening an item from another app takes you to that app's site to open it.

## Making things

**New Plan** sits in the page header wherever a new plan has an obvious home: Recent, Favourites and search results put it in My Work, outside any folder, and on Home it sits inside the search field. On My Work, inside a folder and in a team's library, the button becomes **Create**, offering **New Plan** and **New Folder** (**New Subfolder** inside a folder). New always makes a plan here, whichever filter is on; to make something in another app, open the Explorer from that app.

Pages that are not a place an item lives (the Inbox, the Timeline, Shared With Me and the Trash) have no New button.

## Overview

- **Home** is what the Explorer opens on ([Home](/help/explorer/home/)): the plans you use most, what was shared with you recently, and what other people did to your items.
- **Inbox** lists the [actions, cards, form responses and comment threads](/help/explorer/activity/) waiting on you, with a badge counting the open actions and cards assigned to you.
- **Timeline** is the record of what happened to your items, your teams and your account, by day ([Timeline](/help/explorer/timeline/)), with a count of what is new since you last looked.
- **Shared With Me** gathers every item other people have [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) through a link, kept apart from your own library, with how many there are.

## Spaces

- **My Work** is [your own library](/help/explorer/personal-space/). Beneath it sit the folders you create; opening it lists those folders, then the items you have not filed. Its own menu (its **⋯** button or a right-click) holds **New Folder** and **Use as Default for…** ([Default folders](/help/explorer/folders/#default-folders)).
- **Each team** you belong to sits beside it, with its member count, and expands to the team's folders ([Team Spaces](/help/explorer/team-spaces/)).
- **Invites** appears while a team invitation waits for you.
- **New Team**, when you are signed in, makes a team in place. If you are not signed in, a **Sign in to access Teams** card sits here instead.

## More

- **Library** expands to **Recent** (everything by when you last opened it, [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/)) and **Favourites** (what you starred).
- **This Browser** lists the plans saved only in this browser ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)), with how many there are, and shows only while there is at least one. They are newest first, each opening in the editor, and when you are signed in each has **Sync Plan** to move it to your account. A plan's menu offers **Take Offline** to do the reverse.
- **Trash** holds deleted items for 30 days, ready to restore ([Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/)). While you drag an item, the Trash row wakes up as a place to drop it.

Which rows you expand is remembered in this browser, and opening a Library page opens the Library for you.

## Moving around with the keyboard

The sidebar is a single stop when you press Tab. Then the **arrow keys** move up and down, **Right** opens a row and **Left** closes it (or moves to its parent), **Home** and **End** jump to the first and last rows, **Enter** or **Space** opens the row, and typing a letter jumps to the next row starting with it. On a folder, or on My Work, **Shift+F10** (or the **Menu** key) opens its menu.

## Searching

The search field at the top of the page narrows the list you are on by the titles of your items and the names of their folders. On a page with no list of its own, such as Home or the Inbox, typing opens a **Search results** page across the whole library. The chips under each list's title, and typed filters like `made-by:ai` or `edited:7d`, narrow it further: [Search and Filter Plans](/help/explorer/filters/) covers them. Search does not look inside an item's content.

## List, Card and Details

Every list of items (My Work, a folder, a team, Recent, Favourites and search results) can be shown three ways, switched with the **List view**, **Card view** and **Details view** buttons at the top right of the page. Cards are what you get until you choose otherwise. Shared With Me and the Trash keep a fixed list of their own. [List, Card and Details Views](/help/explorer/list-and-card-views/) covers them.

## An item's menu

Each row and card has a **⋯** button (or right-click it) with what you can do to that item: **Open**, **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Change Folder**, **Share**, **Add to Favourites**, **History**, **Hide from Recent**, **Open Team** for a team's item, **Take Offline**, and **Delete Plan** to move it to the Trash. What appears depends on your access: someone who reaches an item through a link cannot rename, move, share or delete it, and its menu offers **Remove from Shared With Me** instead.

**Duplicate** makes a copy named after the original with "(copy)" on the end, every tab included, and puts it in your library.

## On a phone

The sidebar folds away on a small screen. Press the menu button beside the page title (**Browse sections**) to open it, and pick a page to close it again.
