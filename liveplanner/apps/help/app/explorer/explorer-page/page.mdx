The full-page [Explorer](/explorer) is your library: a sidebar on the left to move around, and the page you picked on the right, with its folders and items. Opening `/explorer` takes you to **Home**. (For the compact version inside the editor, see the [Explorer panel](/help/explorer/explorer-panel/).)

The Explorer is shared by every liveworkspace app. The same library opens on liveplanner, livediagram, livedesign and the others, so a folder can hold a Plan beside a Canvas from livediagram and a Design from livedesign. Your folders, favourites, sharing and Trash are the same wherever you open it.

## Plans or All

At the top right of most pages, a two-way switch chooses what the lists show:

- **Plans** shows only liveplanner's plans.
- **All** shows every item from every app. Each row then carries its app's name, and the lists say "items" rather than "plans".

Your choice is remembered in this browser. Opening an item from another app takes you to that app's site to open it.

## Making things

**New Plan** sits in the page header wherever a new plan has an obvious home: Home, Recent, Favourites and Unsorted put it in My Work, outside any folder. On My Work, inside a folder and in a team's library, the button becomes **Create**, offering **New Plan** and **New Folder** (**New Subfolder** inside a folder). New always makes a plan here, whichever filter is on; to make something in another app, open the Explorer on that app's site.

Pages that are not a place an item lives (the Inbox, Shared With Me, Tags, Trash and search results) have no New button.

## Overview

- **Home** is what the Explorer opens on ([Home](/help/explorer/home/)): the items you opened last, and what was shared with you recently.
- **Inbox** lists the [actions and comment threads](/help/explorer/activity/) waiting on you, with a badge counting the open actions assigned to you.
- **Shared With Me** gathers every item other people have [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) through a link, kept apart from your own library.

## Spaces

- **My Work** is [your own library](/help/explorer/personal-space/). Beneath it sit the folders you create; opening it lists those folders, then the items you have not filed.
- **Each team** you belong to sits beside it, and expands to the team's folders ([Team Spaces](/help/explorer/team-spaces/)).
- **Manage Teams**, when you are signed in, takes you to liveworkspace.app, where teams are made and run.

## More

- **Library** expands to **Recent** (everything by when you last opened it, [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/)), **Favourites** (what you starred), and **Unsorted** (every item in no folder, yours and your teams').
- **Trash** holds deleted items for 30 days, ready to restore ([Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/)).

Which rows you expand is remembered in this browser.

## Searching

The search field at the top of the page looks through the titles of your items, their tags and their folders, across the whole library. Results appear as you pause typing, on a **Search results** page. Search does not look inside an item's content.

## List, Card and Details

Every list of items (My Work, a folder, a team, Recent, Favourites, Unsorted, a tag, Shared With Me, search results and the Trash) can be shown three ways, switched with the **List view**, **Card view** and **Details view** buttons at the top right of the page. [List, Card and Details Views](/help/explorer/list-and-card-views/) covers them.

## An item's menu

Each row and card has a **⋯** button (or right-click it) with what you can do to that item: **Open**, **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Change Folder**, **Share**, **Add to Favourites**, **Tags**, **Open Team** for a team's item, and **Delete Plan** to move it to the Trash. What appears depends on your access: someone who reaches an item through a link cannot rename, move, share or delete it, and its menu offers **Remove from Shared With Me** instead.

**Duplicate** makes a copy named after the original with "(copy)" on the end, every tab included, and puts it in your library.

## On a phone

The sidebar folds away on a small screen. Press the menu button beside the page title (**Browse sections**) to open it, and pick a page to close it again.
