Each team you belong to is a space of its own in the Explorer sidebar: a row under **Spaces**, right after your own [My Work](/help/explorer/personal-space/). For how teams, roles and invites work, see the [Teams guide](/help/collaboration/teams/).

## Your teams

Each team you are a member of appears as a row with the team glyph and, once it has more than one member, how many it has. Click a team to open its library; expand it with the arrow to walk its folders without leaving the sidebar, and click a folder to open it.

A team's library holds the team's folders and items, from every liveworkspace app, and every member can manage them ([Team Shared Plans](/help/collaboration/teams/team-shared-projects/)). At the top of the team's page sits the team's card, where the team is run: how many have joined and its members, each with their role, invitations, and a menu with **Leave Team** and, for an Admin, **Edit Team** and **Delete Team**. On a team's library, **Create** offers **New Plan** and **New Folder**, so what you make lands in the team.

The editor's Explorer panel (behind the menu button) lists your teams in its rail too, so you can reach team plans without leaving the plan you are in.

## New Team

Teams belong to your liveworkspace account, not to liveplanner alone, so a team is the same in every liveworkspace app. You make and run them right here in the Explorer: **New Team**, the last row of Spaces, makes one in place, and the team's card runs it. You can also make a team while moving a plan: the **New Team** tile in **Change Folder** makes it and puts the plan straight in.

A team you join in another liveworkspace app appears here once liveplanner next refreshes your sign-in, within the hour, when you load the Explorer again. Signing out and back in brings it at once.

## Invites

When someone invites you to a team, **Invites** appears in Spaces with the number waiting. Open it to see each invitation: the team, who invited you and when, and the role offered. Answer each one right there with **Accept** or **Decline**. Accepting opens the team, and it shows up in Spaces.

## Signed-in only

Teams need an account, so team rows and **New Team** appear only when you are signed in. Guests see a **Sign in to access Teams** card in their place ("Free, and your guest plans come with you").
