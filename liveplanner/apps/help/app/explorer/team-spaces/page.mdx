Each team you belong to is a space of its own in the Explorer sidebar: a row under **Spaces**, right after your own [My Work](/help/explorer/personal-space/). For how teams, roles and invites work, see the [Teams guide](/help/collaboration/teams/).

## Your teams

Each team you are a member of appears as a row with the team glyph. Click a team to open its library; expand it with the arrow to walk its folders without leaving the sidebar, and click a folder to open it.

A team's library holds the team's folders and items, from every liveworkspace app, and every member can manage them ([Team Shared Plans](/help/collaboration/teams/team-shared-projects/)). On a team's library, **Create** offers **New Plan** and **New Folder**, so what you make lands in the team.

The editor's Explorer panel (behind the menu button) lists your teams in its rail too, so you can reach team plans without leaving a board.

## Manage Teams

Teams belong to your liveworkspace account, not to liveplanner, and are made, renamed, staffed and deleted on liveworkspace.app. **Manage Teams**, the last row of Spaces, takes you there. Invitations wait there too, and are answered there.

The teams the Explorer shows come from your sign-in: a team you make or join on liveworkspace.app appears here once liveplanner next refreshes your sign-in, within the hour, when you load the Explorer again. Signing out and back in brings it at once.

## Signed-in only

Teams need an account, so team rows and Manage Teams appear only when you are signed in.
