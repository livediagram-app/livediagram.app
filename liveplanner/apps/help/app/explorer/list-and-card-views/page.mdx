The Explorer can show the same folders and items three ways: a compact **list** of rows, a grid of **cards**, or a **details** table you can sort by any column.

## Switching views

Look for the three-button toggle at the top right of any page that lists items: My Work, a folder, a team's library, Recent, Favourites, Unsorted, a tag, Shared With Me, search results and the Trash. Click **List view** for rows, **Card view** for cards, or **Details view** for the table. The choice applies at once, to every one of those pages, and is remembered in this browser.

The list is what you get until you choose otherwise.

## What each view shows

- **List** packs items into compact rows: each item's icon, its name, its tags, a star if you favourited it, its visibility badge (**Shared** or **Team**) and when it was last updated, with its **⋯** menu at the end. With **All** chosen at the top of the page, each row also names the app it comes from.
- **Card** shows each item as a tile with the same details arranged around it. Folders appear as cards in the same grid, with a count of the subfolders they hold.
- **Details** lays everything out as a table, much like the details pane of a desktop file manager.

## The details view

The table has a column for each of:

- **Name**: open an item or folder from it.
- **Type**: the kind of thing it is, such as **Plan** or **Canvas**.
- **Comments**: how many comments it holds.
- **Access**: what you may do with it.
- **Size**: how much it holds.
- **Created** and **Updated**: the date and time.

Press any column heading to sort by it; press it again to reverse. Narrow screens keep fewer columns, so on a phone the table shows the name and the last update.

## Long lists

Lists load a page at a time as you scroll towards the end, so a large library opens quickly.
