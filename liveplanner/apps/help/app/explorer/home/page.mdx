**Home** is the page the [Explorer](/explorer) opens on, and the first row of its sidebar, above the [Inbox](/help/explorer/activity/) and the [Timeline](/help/explorer/timeline/). It answers the two questions you bring back with you, on one screen: **what was I working on?** and **what happened while I was away?**

Home opens with a greeting across the top of the page. Under it, two pills say how many plans you have across My Work and your teams and whether anything waits in your **Inbox**; click one to go there. The search field sits right there in the greeting, with **New Plan** inside it, so the first screen always offers a way to find something or start something. A pill shows once its count has arrived, so it never says "0 plans" while your plans are still loading. On a wide screen a Board, a Sheet, a Dashboard and a Form fan out beside it: a glimpse of what a plan can hold.

Prefer a plainer Home? Press the **×** beside **Help** in the greeting's corner to hide it. Home then opens with its title and the search field in the header, like every other page, and stays that way on your other devices. Turn it back on in **Settings > Plans** with **Show Home Banner**.

Your first visit looks different. With nothing made yet, Home welcomes you and offers ways to start instead: the search field looks through the templates, **Start Blank** offers a Blank Plan, a Blank Spreadsheet and a Blank Form, and below it sit the most popular templates, each one click from a new plan. If you have used liveplanner before and see nothing, **Sign In** brings your plans back. Once you have a plan, Home shows your work instead.

Below the greeting, Home is one column: **Jump Back In**, then **Shared With You Recently**, then **What Happened**, each with its link at the right of its heading.

## Jump Back In

**Jump Back In** holds up to sixteen plans within reach: the **eight you opened most** lately, then the **eight you used last**.

- **Most Used** counts how many times you opened a plan over the last 14 days, so a plan you open a lot over a few days rises to the top. A day you only edited or made it counts as one open. A plan you have not opened in 14 days drops off, so the list stays about what you are working on now.
- **The last ones** are the plans you opened, edited or made most recently, newest first.
- **What you make is in at once**, opened or not: a new plan, a duplicate, a single imported plan, or one an AI tool made for you. An import of several plans at once is the exception, so a big import never pushes your work aside; those join when you first open one.
- A plan is never shown twice: if one of the last ones is also among the most used, it stays with the most used, and the next most recent takes its place.

On a computer or a tablet they sit as cards in two groups: **Most Used** first, with a line under each saying how many times you opened it, then **Recent**, saying when you last used each. Each group is up to two rows of four on a computer. On a phone they share one strip of up to eight you scroll sideways, taking turns, and the strip ends in a **See More** tile.

Each plan shows a picture of its first tab, with its name underneath; click one to open it. A plan saved only in this browser wears the **This Browser** pill and takes its place like any other, counted in this browser. Plans in the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), and ones whose share link has lapsed or whose team you have left, never appear. With nothing yet to show, the section says "The plans you use most and last will gather here."

Opening a plan is private: it counts towards your own Jump Back In, and nobody else ever sees that you opened it.

**See More** opens [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/): the 12 plans you opened most recently, newest first, with the same filters as the rest of the Explorer. Its breadcrumb leads back to Home.

## Shared With You Recently

Under Jump Back In sit the four items most recently [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) through a link. **See All** opens Shared With Me.

## What Happened

Below Jump Back In, **What Happened** lists what **other people** recently did to plans you can open (read from your latest 30 Timeline events), under day headings (**Today**, **Yesterday**, then the date): comments and replies, threads resolved, a day of edits, actions assigned or completed, and plans shared into one of your teams. Each entry says who did it, what they did, which plan, and where it lives (its team or **My Work**, then its folder, or **Shared by** and the owner), and opens the plan when you click it. When none of those events are someone else's, Home leaves the section out, and **See More** and **See Timeline** only show once there is something to follow.

"Plans you can open" means your own, your teams', and those shared with you through a link that still works. A link that only reaches [one tab](/help/collaboration/sharing/one-tab/) adds nothing here.

When several people worked on the same plan on the same day, they share **one entry**: their avatars overlap and one sentence says what they did, such as "Priya, Sam and Lee commented, edited and assigned you an action in Payments architecture", with where it lives, how many updates there were and the time of the latest underneath. Click it to see every action underneath, newest first. A sentence names at most three people, then "and 2 others".

Anything that arrived since you last looked carries a **New** pill, as on the Timeline. The very first time you visit, nothing is marked: a whole history marked new would tell you nothing.

What Happened has no filters, on purpose. Whether someone simply looked at a plan is theirs to know, so opens never appear here.

**See Timeline**, at the end of the heading, opens the [Timeline](/help/explorer/timeline/): everything that has happened, your own doings included, with filters and a calendar. Comments and actions waiting on you are in the [Inbox](/help/explorer/activity/).

Visiting Home does not count as having caught up: what is marked New here is still new on the Timeline, and the count on the sidebar's Timeline row clears when you read the Timeline.
