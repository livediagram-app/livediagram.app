**Home** is the page the [Explorer](/explorer) opens on, and the first row of its sidebar, above the [Inbox](/help/explorer/activity/). It answers the two questions you bring back with you, on one screen: **what was I working on?** and **what happened while I was away?**

Home opens with a greeting across the top of the page. Under it, two pills say how many projects you have and whether anything waits in your **Inbox**; click one to go there. The search field sits right there in the greeting, with **New Project** inside it, so the first screen always offers a way to find something or start something. On a wide screen a Board, a Sheet, a Dashboard and a Form fan out beside it: a glimpse of what a project can hold.

Prefer a plainer Home? Press the **×** beside **Help** in the greeting's corner to hide it. Home then opens with its title and the search field in the header, like every other page, and stays that way on your other devices. Turn it back on in **Settings > Projects** with **Show Home Banner**.

Your first visit looks different. With nothing made yet, Home welcomes you and offers ways to start instead: the search field looks through the templates, **Start Blank** makes an empty project, and below it sit a blank board, Sheet and form and the most popular templates, each one click from a new project. If you have used liveplanner before and see nothing, **Sign In** brings your projects back. Once you have a project, Home shows your work instead.

Below the greeting, Home is one column: **Jump Back In**, then **What Happened**, each with its link at the right of its heading.

## Jump Back In

**Jump Back In** holds eight projects within reach: the **four you used most**, then the **four you used last**.

- **Most Used** counts the days you opened, edited or made a project over the last 90 days. A project you open most days stays put; one glance does not push it aside.
- **The last ones** are the projects you opened, edited or made most recently, newest first.
- **What you make is in at once**, opened or not: a new project, a duplicate, a single imported board, or one an AI tool made for you. An import of several projects at once is the exception, so a big import never pushes your work aside; those join when you first open one.
- A project is never shown twice: if one of the last ones is also among the most used, it stays with the most used, and the next most recent takes its place.

On a computer or a tablet they sit as cards in two groups: **Most Used** first, with a line under each saying on how many days you used it, then **Recent**, saying when you last used each. On a phone they share one strip you scroll sideways, taking turns, and the strip ends in a **See More** tile.

Each project shows a picture of its first tab, with its name underneath; click one to open it. A project saved only in this browser wears the **This Browser** pill and takes its place like any other, counted in this browser. Projects in the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), and ones whose share link has lapsed or whose team you have left, never appear. With nothing yet to show, the section says "The projects you use most and last will gather here."

Opening a project is private: it counts towards your own Jump Back In, and nobody else ever sees that you opened it.

**See More** opens [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/): the 12 most recently changed projects you can open, newest first, with the same filters as the rest of the Explorer. Its breadcrumb leads back to Home.

## What Happened

Below Jump Back In, **What Happened** lists what **other people** did to projects you can open in the last 14 days, under day headings (**Today**, **Yesterday**, then the date): comments and replies, threads resolved, a day of edits, and projects shared into one of your teams. Each entry says who did it, what they did, which project, and where it lives (its team or **My Projects**, then its folder, or **Shared by** and the owner), and opens the project when you click it. When nobody else has done anything in that time, Home leaves the section out, and **See More** only shows once there is something to follow.

"Projects you can open" means your own, your teams', and those shared with you through a link that still works. A link that only reaches [one tab](/help/collaboration/sharing/one-tab/) adds nothing here.

When several people worked on the same project on the same day, they share **one entry**: their avatars overlap and one sentence says what they did, such as "Priya, Sam and Lee commented and edited in Q3 roadmap", with where it lives, how many updates there were and the time of the latest underneath. Click it to see every action underneath, newest first. A sentence names at most three people, then "and 2 others".

Anything that arrived since you last looked carries a **New** pill. The very first time you visit, nothing is marked: a whole history marked new would tell you nothing.

What Happened has no filters, on purpose. Whether someone simply looked at a project is theirs to know, so opens never appear here.

Visiting Home counts as having caught up: the count on the sidebar's Home row, which counts what other people did since you last looked, clears.

## Guests and signing in

Home works without an account, like the rest of liveplanner. A guest sees what happened to their own projects; team events need an account, so they simply do not appear.

**Your history follows you.** Signing up moves your history, and what you open, to your new account alongside your projects, so a week of work as a guest is still there afterwards.

## Team and project histories

Two places keep the whole record of what happened, your own doings included:

- **A team's activity**, on the team page. It includes what happened before you joined, so if you joined last month you can still read what the team did before you arrived.
- **One project's history**, from the **History** item in a project's menu: everything that has happened to that project, from created to commented on, shared and filed.

  <TimelineFeed />
</Figure>

Events are grouped by the day they happened, newest first, in your local time. A card leads with a preview of the project, then its name, then **what happened** ("Project Created", "Comment Added") with the time and, where it matters, who did it. Click a card to go to whatever it is about.

- **Colour tells you what kind of thing happened:** red when something was destroyed or someone lost access, amber when the shape of things changed (renames, moves, roles, sharing, anything about to expire), green for the ordinary business of building.
- **Busy days collapse:** several events of one kind on one day fold into a single stacked card; click it to open the run. New comments and team invites always keep a card of their own.
- Switch **Cards** to **Calendar** to see the same events as a month grid, one dot per colour on each day.
- **Filter:** show **Everyone** or **Other people**, and turn kinds of event on or off. **Show More** loads the next 50 events.

<Figure caption="Several events of one kind become one stacked card; a comment always keeps a card of its own.">

</Figure>

Deleting a project hides its history while it waits in the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/); deleting it for good removes it.

<Tip>
  liveplanner keeps a year (365 days) of history. To step back your own recent edits on a project,
  use [Undo](/help/user-interface/undo/) in the editor.
</Tip>
