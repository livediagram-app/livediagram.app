**Home** is the page the [Explorer](/explorer) opens on, and the first row of its sidebar, above the [Inbox](/help/explorer/activity/). It answers the question you bring back with you: **what was I working on?**

## The greeting

Home opens with a greeting across the top of the page. Under it, two pills say how many plans you have in **My Work** and whether anything waits in your **Inbox**; click one to go there. A pill shows once its count has arrived, so it never says "0 plans" while your plans are still loading. The search field sits right there in the greeting, with **New Plan** inside it, so the first screen always offers a way to find something or start something. On a wide screen a Board, a Sheet, a Dashboard and a Form fan out beside it: a glimpse of what a plan can hold.

Prefer a plainer Home? Press the **×** beside **Help** in the greeting's corner to hide it. Home then opens with its title and the search field in the header, like every other page, and stays that way on your other devices. Turn it back on in **Settings > Plans** with **Show Home Banner**.

## Jump Back In

**Jump Back In** shows the items you opened most recently as cards, newest first: the last four as large cards under **Recent**, then the four before them under **Earlier**. Each has a preview and its name; click one to open it. On a phone they share one strip you scroll sideways, ending in a **See More** tile. **See More** at the right of the heading opens [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/), the full list.

With **Plans** chosen at the top of the page, Jump Back In shows only your plans; with **All**, it shows whatever you opened last in any liveworkspace app, and opening a Canvas or a Design takes you to its app.

Before you have opened anything, the section offers **New Plan** instead.

## Shared With You Recently

Below it, **Shared With You Recently** shows the latest items other people shared with you through a link. **See All** opens [Shared With Me](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/). The section is left out until something has been shared with you.

## What Home no longer shows

Home used to carry a feed of what other people did while you were away, and each plan and team had a history. The Explorer is now shared by every liveworkspace app, and that feed was not carried over. To see what is waiting on you, open the [Inbox](/help/explorer/activity/).

<Tip>
  Opening an item is private: it counts towards your own Jump Back In, and nobody else sees that you
  opened it.
</Tip>
