**Home** is the page the [Explorer](/explorer) opens on, and the first row of its sidebar, above the [Inbox](/help/explorer/activity/). It answers the question you bring back with you, on one screen: **what was I working on?**

Home opens with a greeting across the top of the page. Under it, two pills say how many plans you have across My Work and your teams and whether anything waits in your **Inbox**; click one to go there. The search field sits right there in the greeting, with **New Plan** inside it, so the first screen always offers a way to find something or start something. A pill shows once its count has arrived, so it never says "0 plans" while your plans are still loading. On a wide screen a Board, a Sheet, a Dashboard and a Form fan out beside it: a glimpse of what a plan can hold.

Prefer a plainer Home? Press the **×** beside **Help** in the greeting's corner to hide it. Home then opens with its title and the search field in the header, like every other page, and stays that way on your other devices. Turn it back on in **Settings > Explorer** with **Show Home Banner**.

Rather skip the homepage? Turn on **Automatically Open Explorer** in **Settings > Explorer**, and opening liveplanner.app while you're signed in comes straight here.

Your first visit looks different. With nothing made yet, Home welcomes you and offers ways to start instead: the search field looks through the templates, **Start Blank** offers a Blank Plan, a Blank Spreadsheet and a Blank Form, and below it sit the most popular templates, each one click from a new plan. If you have used liveplanner before and see nothing, **Sign In** brings your plans back. Once you have a plan, Home shows your work instead.

Below the greeting, Home is one column: **Jump Back In**, then **Shared With You Recently**, each with its link at the right of its heading.

## Jump Back In

**Jump Back In** holds up to sixteen plans within reach: the **eight you opened most** lately, then the **eight you used last**.

- **Most Used** counts how many times you opened a plan over the last 14 days, so a plan you open a lot over a few days rises to the top. A day you only edited or made it counts as one open. A plan you have not opened in 14 days drops off, so the list stays about what you are working on now.
- **The last ones** are the plans you opened, edited or made most recently, newest first.
- **What you make is in at once**, opened or not: a new plan, a duplicate, a single imported plan, or one an AI tool made for you. An import of several plans at once is the exception, so a big import never pushes your work aside; those join when you first open one.
- A plan is never shown twice: if one of the last ones is also among the most used, it stays with the most used, and the next most recent takes its place.

On a computer or a tablet they sit as cards in two groups: **Most Used** first, with a line under each saying how many times you opened it, then **Recent**, saying when you last used each. Each group is up to two rows of four on a computer. On a phone they share one strip of up to eight you scroll sideways, taking turns, and the strip ends in a **See More** tile.

Each plan shows a picture of its first tab, with its name underneath; click one to open it. A plan saved only in this browser wears the **Browser Storage** pill and takes its place like any other, counted in this browser. Plans in the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), and ones whose share link has lapsed or whose team you have left, never appear. With nothing yet to show, the section says "The plans you use most and last will gather here."

Opening a plan is private: it counts towards your own Jump Back In, and nobody else ever sees that you opened it.

**See More** opens [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/): the 12 plans you opened most recently, newest first, with the same filters as the rest of the Explorer. Its breadcrumb leads back to Home.

## Shared With You Recently

Under Jump Back In sit the four items most recently [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/) through a link. **See All** opens Shared With Me.

## A Plan's History

The **History** item in a plan's menu opens everything that has happened to that plan: created, commented on, shared, filed. History covers the **last 30 days**; anything older is no longer listed.
