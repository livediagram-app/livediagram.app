Every list of plans in the [Explorer](/explorer) can be narrowed. Type a few words into the search field in the top bar, the one that reads “Search or filter plans”, or pick a **filter chip** under the page title. Both edit the same search, so a chip you pick shows up in the field as a pill, and a filter you type lights its chip.

Words match a plan's name or the name of the folder it is in, ignoring capitals. With several words, a plan has to match all of them. Accents count, so "cafe" does not find "Café".

## Where it works

- **My Work, a folder and a team.** The search stays inside the place you are in, and reaches into every folder below it: the list shows each matching plan once, with the folder it lives in beside its name. Folders step aside while a search is set and come back when you clear it.
- **Recent, Favourites, Shared With Me, Unsorted and Search results** gather plans from everywhere, so they add a **Space** chip: My Work, Shared With Me, or one of your teams.
- **Home, Inbox, Timeline and Trash** have no list of plans to narrow. Typing in the field there opens **Search results**, every plan you can open, narrowed by what you typed.

## The chips

| Chip       | Keeps plans that                                                                         |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Template   | Were made from a Retrospective or Kanban template                                        |
| Made by AI | An AI tool made for you; they also carry a **Made by AI** badge wherever they are listed |
| Edited     | Were last edited today, in the last 7, 30 days or 12 months, or this year                |
| People     | You own (Me), or someone else owns (Others)                                              |
| Space      | Live in My Work, are shared with you, or are in a team (gathered lists only)             |

Click a chip to open its list and tick values; **Any** at the top unsets it. **Made by AI** has only one value, so it is a simple on/off toggle. A chip can hold several values: picking Retrospective and Kanban keeps both. Different chips narrow together: Template and Edited keeps the retrospectives you edited in the last 7 days. **Clear** at the end of the row empties the search.

A plan made before liveplanner recorded how it was created has no Template, so setting that chip hides it; it shows again once the chip is back on Any. Which plans show at all, plans only or every app's items, is the **Plans / All** switch's job, not a chip's.

## Typing filters

The field takes filters as words too, the way the chips write them:

- `made-by:ai` keeps what an AI made.
- `template:retrospective,kanban` keeps both template families.
- `edited:7d` keeps what you edited in the last 7 days; `today`, `30d`, `12m` and `this-year` work too.
- `people:me` or `people:others`, and `space:mine` or `space:shared`, work the same way. A team is offered by name as you type `space:`.

As you type, suggestions appear under the field: use **Down** and **Up** to move through them, **Enter** or **Tab** to take one, and **Esc** to close the list. When the whole list is loaded, a suggestion marked **No matches** would leave nothing in it on its own. A finished filter becomes a pill in the field, with a **×** to remove it; **Backspace** at the start of the words selects the last pill, and a second **Backspace** removes it.

A word that looks like a filter but isn't one, such as `colour:red` or `edited:yesterday`, is searched as plain text, and the Explorer says so under the chips, so a typo never hides plans silently. A **Space** filter typed on My Work, a folder or a team does nothing there, because the place you are in already sets the space; the note under the chips says so. An old link's `opens-in:` filter is read as no filter at all, and the note says that too. The search reads at most 512 characters.

When the filters leave nothing, the list says so and offers **Clear Filters**. A screen reader hears how many plans are left once the list has loaded; while more pages are still to come, it says "or more".

## Share a search

The search lives in the page's address, so copying the link shares exactly what you are looking at. Typing changes the address in place rather than adding a step to Back for every letter. Moving between Recent, Favourites, Shared With Me, Unsorted and Search results keeps your search; opening a folder or a team starts afresh. A search is not remembered for next time.
