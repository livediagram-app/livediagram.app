The **Explorer panel** is the Explorer inside the editor. Press the **menu button** (☰) at the top left of the tab, beside its title, and it opens under the button; press the button again, or click anywhere outside, and it closes. It reads the same library as the [full-page Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), so you can open another plan without leaving the one you are on.

## What the panel shows

- **The current plan** at the top: its name, its visibility badge and its **⋯** menu. Click the name to rename it in place: Enter saves, Escape keeps the old name.
- **The rail** down the left: **New Plan** at the top, then **Home**, **Inbox**, **Shared With Me**, **My Work**, each of your teams, **Favourites** and **Trash**.
- **The list** beside it shows the section you pick, a page at a time; **Show more** loads the next page. Picking a plan opens it in the editor, and an item from another app opens on that app's site.
- **Every app**, a tick box at the foot of the rail, switches the lists from just your plans to every liveworkspace app's items, the same choice as **Plans** and **All** on the Explorer page.
- **Open Explorer** takes the section you are looking at to the full page, where folders, tags, Recent and Unsorted live.

Each item has the same menu as on the Explorer page, from its **⋯** button or a right-click, so **Change Folder**, **Share**, **Add to Favourites** and the rest work here too.

The **⋯** button in the panel's header opens a menu with **New Plan** and **Open Explorer**, then **Share** and **Export** for the plan you are in, then **Search**, **Licences** and **Settings**. With [power user mode](/help/user-interface/power-user-mode/)'s Minimal chrome on, the help button moves into this menu as **Help**.

## When you will see it

- **In the editor**, to jump to another plan without going back to the library. [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) hides the menu button with the rest of the chrome.
- **On a phone**, the panel opens under the menu button across the screen, down to the tab bar.
- **When a plan can't be opened**, it stays available beside the error screen, so you can pick another plan.

Like everywhere else in the Explorer, the panel works whether or not you are signed in.
