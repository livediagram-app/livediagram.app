The **Explorer panel** is the Explorer inside the editor. Press the **menu button** (☰) at the top left of the tab, beside its title, and it opens under the button; press the button again, or click anywhere outside, and it closes. It holds the same sections as the [full-page Explorer](/help/explorer/explorer-page/), so you can browse your library, catch up on what happened and open another plan without leaving the plan you are on.

## What the panel shows

- **Current Plan** at the top is the plan you are in: its thumbnail, its name, when it was last saved, its visibility badge (**Private**, **Shared**, **Team** or **This Browser**) at the right, and its **⋯** menu. **Click the name to rename it** in place: Enter saves, Escape keeps the old name. Hover the badge to see who can see the plan.
- **The rail** down the left lists every section, grouped as on the Explorer page: **Overview** (Home, Inbox, Shared With Me), **Spaces** (My Work and each team) and **More** (This Browser, when plans are saved only here, then [Tags](/help/explorer/tags/), Library and Trash).
- **The pane** on the right shows the section you pick. Nothing sends you away from the editor: **Home**, the **Inbox**, the **Library** (Image gallery and Themes) and the **Trash** all open right there. **My Work**, each team, **This Browser** and **Shared With Me** list their folders and plans, each plan with its thumbnail and when it was last updated; a folder opens right there in the pane, with a path above it back to where you started, and picking a plan opens it in the editor. **Open Page** at the top of the pane takes that section to the full Explorer, and **New Folder** makes a folder in My Work or the team you are looking at.
- The panel opens on **Home** (the plans you were last in, and what others did since) and then remembers the section you last picked.
- Along the foot are the product's other places: **Home**, **Explorer** and **Help**.

It is the same data as the full page: folders, ownership, and sharing all behave identically. Each plan has the plan menu from its **⋯** button, a right-click, or **Shift+F10**, so **Change Folder**, **Favourite** and **Hide from Recent** work here as well. A plan saved only in this browser carries the **This Browser** pill ([Offline Mode](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/)). Drag one of your own plans onto one of your folders to file it, or onto **My Work** in the rail to put it back at the root.

The keyboard works as on the Explorer page: in the rail, **Up** and **Down** move between sections and **Enter** picks one; in the pane, **Up** and **Down** move between rows and **Enter** opens one. Answering team invites and creating a team happen in the full Explorer.

The **⋯** button in the panel's header, beside the help button, opens a menu with **New Plan** and **Open Explorer** (the full-page library), then **Share** and **Export** for the plan you are in, then **Search**, **Licences** and **Settings**. With [power user mode](/help/user-interface/power-user-mode/)'s Minimal chrome on, the help button moves into this menu as **Help**.

## When you will see it

- **In the editor**, to jump to another plan without going back to the library. [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) hides the menu button with the rest of the chrome.
- **On a phone**, the panel opens under the menu button across the screen: the sections become a row of chips you swipe sideways, with the picked one's pane beneath. Held sideways, a phone has no menu button and no Explorer; turn it upright to open the Explorer.
- **When a plan can't be opened**, it stays available beside the error screen, so you can pick another plan.

Like everywhere else in the Explorer, the panel works whether or not you are signed in, showing the plans your browser or account owns.
