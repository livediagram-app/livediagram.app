**Shared With Me** collects the items other people have handed you, so a link a colleague sent last week is still easy to find this week. It sits in the Explorer sidebar's **Overview** group, just below the Timeline.

## How an item gets here

When you open a [share link](/help/collaboration/sharing/) someone sent you, that item is remembered and added to **Shared With Me**. Opening the link once is enough. Until you have opened one, the page says **Nothing shared with you yet**.

Opening a link to one of your own items doesn't add it, and items in your [teams](/help/explorer/team-spaces/) live in the team's library.

Shared With Me covers every liveworkspace app: choose **All** at the top of the page to see a Canvas or a Design someone shared with you beside your plans.

## Working with the list

- Click an item to open it on its share link, without digging the original link out of your messages.
- Each item shows a **Shared** badge: you reach it through a link, at the level that link gives you.
- Switch between [List, Card and Details views](/help/explorer/list-and-card-views/) with the buttons at the top right.

## Removing an item

Choose **Remove from Shared With Me** in an item's **⋯** menu when you are done with it. That only removes it from your list; it does not affect the item or anyone else's access. Open the share link again and it comes back.

The list only offers items you can still open: if the owner turns the link off, it [runs out](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-link-expiry/), or the item goes to the Trash, it drops off.

## Guests and signing in

Shared With Me works without an account: the list is kept for your guest identity, which is the same in every liveworkspace app. Signing in carries it into your account.

<Tip>
  Shared items stay separate from **My Work**: they are other people's items you have access to, not
  your own library, so they never clutter your folders. They do show up in
  [Recent](/help/explorer/recent/) and in search.
</Tip>
