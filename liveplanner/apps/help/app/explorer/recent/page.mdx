**Recent** lists the items you opened most recently, newest first, so the work in progress is always one click away. Find it under **Library** in the **More** group of the [Explorer](/explorer) sidebar, or press **See more** beside Jump back in on [Home](/help/explorer/home/).

## What lands here

Recent goes by when **you** last opened something: your own items, your teams' and the ones [shared with you](/help/explorer/shared-with-you/). Opening an item is private, so your Recent is yours alone, and nobody else sees what you opened.

It is a view across your whole library, not a folder of its own: an item appearing in Recent has not been moved or copied, and you will still find it in its folder.

With **Plans** chosen at the top of the page, Recent shows only plans; with **All**, it shows what you opened in every liveworkspace app.

## Working from Recent

- **Open** an item by clicking it.
- Switch between [List, Card and Details views](/help/explorer/list-and-card-views/) with the buttons at the top right.
- Each item's **⋯** menu (or a right-click) carries the same actions as anywhere else in the Explorer.
- **New Plan** makes a plan in My Work.

## Seeing where an item lives

Recent spans every folder, so each item shows a small chip naming the folder it sits in. Click the chip to jump straight to that folder instead of opening the item.

<Tip>
  A guest's Recent is kept for their guest identity, and signing in carries it into the account with
  everything else.
</Tip>
