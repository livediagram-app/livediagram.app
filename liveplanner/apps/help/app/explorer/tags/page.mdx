A **tag** is a coloured label you put on your items: a client, a project, a quarter, a status. A folder says where an item lives, and an item is in exactly one; tags say what it is about, so one item can carry several, and a tag can gather items from different folders, teams and apps. Tagging never moves anything.

Your tags are **yours alone**, and the same in every liveworkspace app. Tagging a team plan marks it for you, not for the rest of the team.

## Tagging an item

Open an item's menu (its **⋯** button, or a right-click) anywhere in the Explorer or the editor's Explorer panel, and choose **Tags**. The tag picker opens:

- **Tick a tag** to put it on the item, untick it to take it off. Each tick saves at once.
- **Type a name** in the **New tag** field and press **Add** to make a tag and put it on the item in one go. Each new tag gets the next colour in turn.
- Press **Done** to close the picker.

## Seeing your tags

A tagged item shows its tags as small coloured chips beside its name in the lists. **Click a chip** to see every item with that tag.

## The Tags page

**Tags** sits inside **Library**, in the sidebar's **More** group, and expands to a row for each of your tags. The Tags page lists every tag; click one, or its row in the sidebar, to see the items that carry it.

## Renaming and deleting tags

A tag's **⋯** menu on the Tags page offers **Rename** and **Delete Tag**. Deleting a tag takes it off every item; the items themselves stay where they are.

Names are up to 32 characters, and you can have up to 100 tags. Tags made while you were a guest come with you when you sign in.
