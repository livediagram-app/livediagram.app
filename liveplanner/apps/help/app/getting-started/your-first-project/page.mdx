This walk-through takes a few minutes and needs no account. By the end you will have a plan with a board of cards, a second tab beside it, and a link you can send to someone so you can plan together.

## Open liveplanner

Open a [new plan](/new). There is no sign-up and nothing to install: you start straight away as a guest, and your work is saved in this browser as you go. You can sign in later to keep it on every device; see [Guest vs Account](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

A plan holds **tabs**, listed along the bottom of the screen. Each tab is one **Board**, **Sheet**, **Form** or **Dashboard**. Your new plan has one empty tab, so it opens on **Start Planning**.

## Pick how to run the work

**Start Planning** lists ready-made starts down its left side: **Recommended**, **Boards**, **Spreadsheets**, **Forms**, **Visualisations**, **Dashboards** and **Inspiration** (whole ways of working set up across several tabs, such as a Hiring Pipeline or a Content Calendar). Or type in the search box to find a board, sheet, form or layout by name.

<Step n={1}>
  Under **Recommended**, pick **Kanban**. The tab becomes a board with the columns **Backlog**, **To
  Do**, **In Progress**, **Review** and **Done**, and limits on how many cards the busy columns
  should hold at once.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  For a quick look round your board, press Start Boards Tour in the bottom bar, just left of
  **Settings**. It adds an example card to show you, and takes it away after.
</Step>

The plan takes the board's name ("Untitled Kanban"). To call it something else, click the name at the top of the screen, type, and press **Enter**.

## Add and move cards

<Figure caption="A Kanban board: columns of cards, with a card being dragged into another column.">
  <PlanBoardScene />
</Figure>

- **Add a card:** press **Add your first card** (or **Add card**) at the top of a column, pick the kind of card, and type its title. On a computer you can also click a card type beside the board's name, or drag it straight into a column.
- **Open a card:** click it to see its details: who it's assigned to, its priority, dates, a description and comments. Every change saves as you make it.
- **Move a card:** drag it to another column as the work moves on, or up and down its column to put it in order. With the keyboard, focus a card and press **Shift** with the arrow keys.
- **Undo a slip:** the **Undo** and **Redo** buttons sit in the bottom-right corner, or press **Ctrl+Z** (**Cmd+Z** on a Mac).

The buttons beside them help as the board grows: **Find** finds any card in the plan, **Types** sets up the kinds of card you use and their fields, and **Trash** keeps what you delete until you restore it. More in [Boards](/help/plan/boards/) and [Cards](/help/plan/cards/).

## Add another tab

Press **+** at the end of the tab bar. The new tab opens on **Start Planning** too, so you can put something different beside your board:

- **A Sheet:** under **Spreadsheets**, pick **Empty Sheet** for a blank grid with formulas, or a ready-made one such as a budget. See [Sheets](/help/plan/sheets/).
- **A Dashboard:** under **Dashboards**, pick one such as **Overview** to see metrics and charts drawn from the plan's cards. Because every card lives in the plan, the dashboard keeps up as cards move on your board. See [Dashboards](/help/plan/dashboards/).
- **A Form:** under **Forms**, pick one such as **Feedback**. Each answer someone sends becomes a card. See [Forms](/help/plan/forms/).

Click a tab to switch to it. Its **⋯** menu renames, duplicates, locks or deletes it; see [Using Tabs](/help/tabs/using-tabs/).

## Share it

<Step n={1}>Press **Share** at the top right.</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Choose what the person can do: **Editor** (changes anything, with you), **Participant** (adds and
  moves cards and fills in cells, without changing how the plan is set up) or **Viewer** (looks
  only).
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Press **Create Pass**. The link is copied for you: paste it into a message. Whoever opens it joins
  the same plan, with no account needed, and you see each other's changes as they happen.
</Step>

More in [Sharing Your Plan](/help/getting-started/sharing-your-project/), including passwords and links that expire.
