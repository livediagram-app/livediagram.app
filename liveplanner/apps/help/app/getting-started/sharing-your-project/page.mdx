liveplanner is shared and live from the first card. Sharing is just creating a link and handing it over: whoever opens it joins the same plan and sees every change as it happens, alongside you. The person you share with does not need an account. For the full details see [Sharing](/help/collaboration/sharing/).

## Create a share link

<Step n={1}>
  Open the plan you want to share and click **Share** at the top right of the editor, or choose
  **Share** from the plan's **⋯** menu in the Explorer. Only the plan's owner can issue
  passes, so someone who joined through a link sees no **Share** button.
</Step>

<Step n={2}>
  Under **Issue a pass**, click the role card to choose a role: **Editor** (changes anything with
  you in real time, the default), **Participant** (adds cards, edits and moves them, fills in sheet
  cells, comments and votes, but can't change columns, card types, tabs or settings) or **Viewer**
  (looks and opens cards; can't comment, vote or change a thing). **Valid** sets how long it lasts:
  Forever, 1 week, 1 month or 6 months. In a plan with several tabs, **Opens** can limit the link
  to one tab.
</Step>

<Step n={3}>
  Click **Create Pass**. The link is copied for you straight away, ready to send to whoever you want
  in the plan; each pass in the list also has its own copy button.
</Step>

If you are a guest, check the name under **Sharing as** at the foot of the dialog first (or press the
shuffle button beside it for another): that is the name others see on your comments and on the cards you are working on. A plan
saved to **This Browser** has to be synced to your account before it can be shared.

## What happens when they open it

Someone opening your link for the first time enters the password if you set one, picks the name others will see (a signed-in visitor keeps their account name), and then lands in the plan:

- **Live presence.** Everyone sees who else is in the plan and which tab they are on. A card someone is dragging or has open shows their colour and name. See [Live Presence](/help/collaboration/live-presence/).
- **Simultaneous editing.** On an Editor pass, you both change the plan at once: cards move, cells fill in and new cards arrive for everyone as it happens.
- **No sign-in required.** A guest can join straight away; liveplanner never blocks them with a sign-in wall. See [Guest vs account](/help/getting-started/guest-vs-account/).

## Managing and ending access

Back in the Share dialog you can manage links over time:

- **Multiple passes.** Issue several, with different roles, for example a Viewer pass for stakeholders and an Editor pass for collaborators. They are listed under **Passes**.
- **Revoke** a pass to cut off access through it. Expiring passes show a countdown, and expired ones move under **Expired**, where they can be extended or deleted. See [Share Link Expiry](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-link-expiry/).
- **Password Protection.** Protect every pass with one shared password; everyone opening a link must enter it first, embeds included. See [Share Passwords](/help/collaboration/sharing/share-passwords/).

<Tip>
  Want to put the plan on a web page or in a doc? Each pass can also be copied as an embed
  (**Copy iframe**) or, while no password is set, as a live image that stays up to date. A Viewer or
  Participant pass embeds look-only; an Editor pass embeds an editable plan. See
  [Embeds](/help/collaboration/sharing/embeds/).
</Tip>
