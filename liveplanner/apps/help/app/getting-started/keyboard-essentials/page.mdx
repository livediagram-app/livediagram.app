You can do everything in liveplanner with the mouse or a finger, but a few keyboard shortcuts make planning much faster. This is the short list worth committing to memory. For the complete reference, see [Keyboard Shortcuts](/help/tips-and-tricks/keyboard-shortcuts/). On Windows and Linux, read **Cmd** as **Ctrl**.

## Everywhere in the editor

- **`Cmd+Z`** undoes, and **`Cmd+Shift+Z`** (or **`Ctrl+Y`**) redoes. A moved card goes back, a trashed card comes back. The same controls sit in the bottom-right corner. See [Undo](/help/user-interface/undo/).
- **`Cmd+K`** (or **`Cmd+.`**) opens [search](/help/search-panel/), which finds plans, tabs, settings and help articles and also runs commands (to find a card, use **Find** in the bottom-right corner). See [Command Palette](/help/tips-and-tricks/command-palette/).
- **`Z`** turns [Zen mode](/help/tools/zen-mode/) on and off, hiding the toolbars and panels so you can focus on the work.
- **`Escape`** closes an open dialog, menu or card.
- **`F6`** moves focus between the parts of the screen (the header, the bottom-right controls and the tab bar), and **`Shift+F6`** moves back.

## On a board

Press **Tab** until a card is focused, then:

- **Arrow keys** move between cards.
- **Enter** (or **Space**) opens the card.
- **Shift+Left** and **Shift+Right** move the card to the previous or next column; **Shift+Up** and **Shift+Down** move it within its column.
- **N** adds a card to the focused card's column.
- **Delete** moves the card to the Trash, where you can restore it.

More in [Boards](/help/plan/boards/).

## On a sheet

Sheets use the keys you know from other spreadsheets:

- **Arrow keys** move between cells; hold **Shift** to select a range, and **Cmd** to jump to the edge of the data.
- **Tab** and **Enter** move right and down (with **Shift**, left and up). Start typing to replace a cell, or press **F2** to edit what's in it.
- **Delete** or **Backspace** clears the selected cells.
- **`Cmd+C`**, **`Cmd+X`** and **`Cmd+V`** copy, cut and paste, and **`Cmd+Shift+V`** pastes values only.
- **`Cmd+B`**, **`Cmd+I`** and **`Cmd+U`** set bold, italic and underline.
- **`Cmd+D`** and **`Cmd+R`** fill down and right, and **`Cmd+F`** finds in the sheet.

More in [Sheets](/help/plan/sheets/).

<Tip>
  If you dictate into your browser, or something else needs the keys, you can turn the editor's
  shortcuts off: open **Settings** at the bottom-right of the tab bar, pick **Keyboard**, and switch
  off **Keyboard Shortcuts**. It is saved on this device only.
</Tip>
