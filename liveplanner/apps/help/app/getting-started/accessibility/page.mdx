You can plan in liveplanner without a mouse. This page covers the keyboard and screen-reader basics: getting round the editor, working a board and a sheet, and hearing what changes. For the everyday shortcuts, see [Keyboard Essentials](/help/getting-started/keyboard-essentials/).

## Getting round the editor

Every button, tab and field is in the normal **Tab** order, with a focus ring when you navigate by keyboard (mouse users see nothing new). To skip a long run of controls, press **`F6`** to jump to the next part of the screen (the header, the work in the middle, the bottom-right controls and the tab bar) and **`Shift+F6`** to go back. Open dialogs keep focus inside them until you close them with **Escape**.

**`Cmd+K`** (**`Ctrl+K`** on Windows and Linux) opens [search](/help/search-panel/), which reaches plans, tabs, settings and commands from the keyboard.

## Working a board

Tab to a card on a board, then use the **arrow keys** to move between cards. **Enter** opens the card's panel, **Shift** with the arrow keys moves the card to another column or up and down its column, **N** adds a card to its column, and **Delete** moves it to the Trash.

Screen readers hear every move, for example "#12 Fix login moved to In Progress, position 4 of 4", and when a card goes to the Trash. Viewers can move between and open cards, but not change them. See [Boards](/help/plan/boards/).

## Working a sheet

A sheet takes the keys of any spreadsheet: the arrow keys move between cells, **Shift** with them selects, **Enter** and **Tab** move down and across, typing replaces a cell, and **F2** edits it. **Alt+Down** opens a cell's dropdown, and the **menu key** (or **Shift+F10**) opens the cell's menu. See [Sheets](/help/plan/sheets/).

## Undo is announced

**Undo** and **Redo** (**`Cmd+Z`** and **`Cmd+Shift+Z`**) work from anywhere in the editor, and each is announced to screen readers ("Undid the last change", "Redid the last change"). These announcements are separate from the on-screen notifications, so you'll hear them even if you've turned notifications off.

## Reducing motion

If animation is uncomfortable, turn on **Reduce Motion** in [Settings](/help/user-interface/settings/), under **Accessibility**. It turns off the editor's animations and transitions, so panels and cards appear instantly instead of sliding or fading. Your device's own reduce-motion preference is always respected as well, and the setting follows you to your other devices.

## Larger controls

If the panels and buttons are too small to read or hit comfortably, **UI Scale** in Settings, under **Appearance**, makes the panels, the toolbar and the corner buttons smaller or bigger (80% to 120%), with a separate scale for each underneath. Desktop only. On a Sheet tab, the zoom controls in the bottom-right corner make the cells themselves bigger, from 50% to 200%.

## Turning shortcuts off

If you dictate into your browser, or another tool needs the keys, switch off **Keyboard Shortcuts** in Settings, under **Keyboard**. It is saved on this device only.
