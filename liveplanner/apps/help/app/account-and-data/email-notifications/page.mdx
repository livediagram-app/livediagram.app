Email is an optional capability of liveplanner. It only does anything when the deployment has been set up to send mail. It goes only to signed-in users, because email needs an address and a [guest identity](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/) has none. The editor itself never needs an account or an email, so this is purely additive.

## What we might send

There are three groups:

- **Tips and check-ins.** A short welcome when you first sign in, a nudge a few days later if you have not made a plan yet, a couple of getting-started notes over your first two weeks, and a friendly nudge if you have been away for about four weeks. The welcome goes straight away; everything after it can be turned off.
- **Notifications about your work.** When someone else comments on a plan you own (at most one email per plan every 15 minutes, however busy the thread), and when someone fills in one of your [forms](/help/plan/forms/) (at most one email per form every 15 minutes). Each has its own switch.
- **Account messages.** A confirmation when you delete your account, a one-time heads-up in the last week before an [API token](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) expires, and a note if one of your [forms](/help/plan/forms/) is suspended after reports. These are tied to something you did, so they always send.

Team invitations are sent by liveworkspace, which runs teams, not by liveplanner.

## What an email contains

Notification emails keep your work out of your inbox. A comment notification tells you who commented and on which plan, with a link to open it, but not what the comment said. Nothing else from the plan, and never a picture of it, goes into an email.

## Turning them off

Open **Settings**, go to **Account**, then **Notifications** beneath it (the account menu's **Account** opens Settings on **Account**, one step away). The **Email** section has one switch per optional kind: **Someone Joins My Plan**, **Someone Comments on My Plan**, **Someone Mentions Me in a Comment**, **Someone Fills In My Form**, **Someone Assigns Me an Action**, **Someone Responds to a Team Invite**, **Tips and Check-Ins**, and **Milestones**. They are all on until you turn them off. liveplanner sends nothing for **Someone Joins My Plan**, **Someone Mentions Me in a Comment**, **Someone Assigns Me an Action**, **Someone Responds to a Team Invite** or **Milestones** at the moment; the switches keep your choice. The whole row is the switch, so click anywhere on it.

<Tip>
  Every email you can turn off carries a "Manage your notifications" link in its footer that opens
  these switches, and mail apps that show a native unsubscribe button point at the same place. Only
  the welcome email has no link.
</Tip>

The switches are saved to your account, so they apply wherever you are signed in, and the change takes effect before the next email would go out. Account messages (the deletion confirmation, the token-expiry reminder and the form-suspension note) are not on this list, because they confirm something rather than nudge you.

As a guest, the same place shows a short note asking you to sign in instead of the switches (on a deployment that sends email). **In-Editor Notifications**, above them, is a different thing: it controls the brief confirmations inside the editor and never sends email.
