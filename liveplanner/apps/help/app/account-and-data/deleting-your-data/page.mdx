You own your projects, and removing one is straightforward. It helps to know how your data is scoped first, because that determines what "your data" means whether you are working as a guest or signed in.

## Deleting a project

You can delete a project from the [Explorer](/explorer/recent) or from the editor. Open the project's **⋯** menu (on its row or card, or in the Current Project section of the Explorer panel) and choose **Delete**. It goes straight away, without a question, because nothing is lost yet: a notification naming the project ("“Roadmap” deleted") appears with **Undo**, which puts it back where it was. It stays for about ten seconds, longer while your pointer is over it; after that the project is still in the Trash. Deleting the project you have open takes you to the Explorer, where the same **Undo** appears.

Deleting a project moves it to the [Trash](/help/account-and-data/trash/), where it waits for 30 days before it is removed for good, along with the content that belongs only to it, such as its tabs and comments. While it waits, its share links stop working and it disappears from your lists, and you can restore it with everything it had. To remove it straight away, delete it permanently from the Trash.

A tab the project shares with another project is not deleted with it: it stays in the other project. [Projects in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) have their own Trash in this browser.

If you have organised projects into [folders](/help/explorer/folders/), you can also tidy up by moving projects between folders or removing folders, separately from deleting the projects themselves.

## Deleting your account

When you are signed in, open **Settings**, go to **Account**, and choose **Delete Account** under **Danger Zone**. You are asked to type your email address to confirm, because this cannot be undone.

Deleting your account removes from the server, straight away:

- every project you own, including anything already in the Trash, with its tabs, comments and share links;
- your folders, saved themes and preferences;
- your stars, your history and activity;
- your [API tokens](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/), which stops any script or [connected AI tool](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/) at once;
- the account itself.

Projects in a [team's](/help/collaboration/teams/) shared library are not deleted with you: they pass to a remaining member, so the team keeps its work. A team you were the last member of is deleted, and its projects, which are all yours by then, are deleted with your account. If email is set up, you get a short confirmation once it is done.

After your data has been removed from the server, you may be asked to verify your sign-in again before the account itself is closed. [Projects in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) were never on the server, so they stay in this browser until you clear its storage.

## How your data is scoped

Where your data lives depends on how you are identified:

- **As a guest**, new projects start [offline](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/), saved only in this browser, and any you share or sync are owned on the server by your [per-browser id](/help/account-and-data/guest-identity/), stored in that one browser. Your data is scoped to that browser, so clearing the browser's storage removes the app's link to your guest content, and using a different browser or device shows a fresh, empty workspace.
- **When signed in**, your projects are owned by your account and synced across every device where you sign in. Deleting a project removes it everywhere, because the account is the single source of truth.

A guest has no account to delete. To remove what you created as a guest, delete the projects you no longer want and empty the Trash. Clearing the browser's storage deletes your projects in This Browser, but only removes this browser's record of your guest identity: projects you shared or synced stay on the server, out of reach from a fresh browser, so delete them first if you want them gone.

<Tip>
  If you plan to clear your browser, sign in first if you want to keep anything. Your guest
  projects on the server move into the account on sign-up. Ones in This Browser move only if you
  accept the offer to move them after signing in, so do that before you clear the browser.
</Tip>
