When you delete a project, it is not gone straight away. It moves to the **Trash**, where it waits for **30 days**. After that it is removed for good. Until then you can bring it back with everything it had: its tabs, its cards, its comments and history, its stars and its share links.

This works the same whether you are signed in or working as a guest.

## Opening the Trash

Choose **Trash**, the last row of the **More** group at the bottom of the Explorer sidebar, or open **Settings**, go to **Account**, and choose **Open Trash**. Both work for guests too, and on deployments without accounts. The Trash lists everything you can restore, grouped:

- **Your projects**: the ones you deleted from your own space.
- **One group per team** you belong to: team projects go to that team's Trash, and any member of the team can restore them or delete them for good, just as any member can delete them.
- **This Browser only**: [projects in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) live only in this browser, so their Trash does too.

Each project shows when it was deleted and how many days it has left. One that is past its 30 days but not yet removed reads "Removed at the next clean-up": removal runs once a day, so a project usually stays for up to a day longer.

## Restoring a project

Choose **Restore**; it needs no confirmation, because it destroys nothing. The project goes back to the folder it was in, or, if that folder has been deleted since, to the folder that held it (the root of **My Projects**, or of its team, for a top-level folder). Its share links work again, and anyone you shared it with can open it as before.

If you open a deleted project by its address and it is in your own Trash, the card that says it was deleted offers **Restore** too, with the days it has left.

## Deleting for good

Choose **Delete permanently** to remove one project now, with its history, or **Empty Trash** to remove everything in one group (only that group: emptying a team's Trash empties it for the whole team). Both ask you to confirm, and neither can be undone.

A tab that is also in another project is never lost when a project is deleted for good: it stays in the other project, exactly where it was.

## While a project is in the Trash

A deleted project disappears from your lists, Recent, Favourites, search, Home and the Inbox. Anyone opening one of its share links sees that it was deleted, and anyone who still had it open is told so, and can no longer save changes to it.

## Empty projects

A project with nothing on any of its tabs, left unchanged for **30 days**, moves to the Trash on its own. Its row in the Trash says it was moved there because it was empty, so you can tell it apart from one you deleted, and it waits the usual 30 days there before it is removed for good. Nothing is sent to tell you: there was nothing in it to lose.

Restore it like any other and it comes back as it was, with another 30 days before it could be moved again. Team projects go to the team's Trash, where any member can restore them. [Projects in This Browser](/help/privacy-and-security/offline-mode/) are never moved.

<Tip>
  Taking your own project offline is a move, not a delete, so it never goes to the Trash. (When a
  teammate takes a team project offline, it leaves the team, so for the team it goes to the team's
  Trash.) [Deleting your account](/help/account-and-data/deleting-your-data/#deleting-your-account)
  removes your own projects straight away, the Trash included.
</Tip>

## For scripts and AI tools

Deleting a project through the [API](/help/account-and-data/api-tokens/) or an [AI tool](/help/account-and-data/connect-ai-mcp/) also moves it to the Trash. An AI tool can list your Trash and restore a project from it, but it can never delete one for good; a script using the API can, when it asks for a permanent delete (see [Working with Projects](/help/developers/working-with-projects/#delete-and-restore)).
