When you open the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/) from inside a project, its scope grows to include that project's [tabs](/help/tabs/using-tabs/). That turns search into a quick way around the project you're in, not just a way to open a different one. It works on view-only links too, since it only moves you around.

## Tabs

The **Tabs** group matches the project's tabs by name, whether each holds a Board, a Sheet, a Dashboard or a Form, and marks the one you are on with **current** so you can tell a jump from a no-op. Picking a tab switches to it. On a project with many tabs (or [tab folders](/help/tabs/tab-folders/)) this is quicker than scanning the tab bar.

With an empty box the panel lists every tab in the project, so you can open it and arrow straight down to one. The Tabs group shows up to 8 matches; type more of a name to narrow a long list.

## Cards

Search doesn't look inside boards, so a card's title won't come up there. To find a card, use **Find** in the bottom-right corner (shown on a tab with a board on it): it lists every card in the project that isn't archived or in the Trash, matches what you type against a card's number (`12` or `#12`), title, description or card type, and opens the one you pick. See [Items](/help/plan/items/) for how cards are stored and found, and [Cards](/help/plan/cards/) for working with them.

To narrow what one board shows rather than find a single card, use **Filter cards** in the board's header. See [Boards](/help/plan/boards/).

## What it does not search

- **Cards, a sheet's cells, a dashboard's contents and a form's questions:** use Find for cards, and a sheet's own find (`⌘F`) for its cells. See [Sheets](/help/plan/sheets/).
- **Comments** and the names of people in the project.
- **Other projects' contents:** from anywhere, other projects are found by name only.

<Tip>
  This scope only appears inside a project. From the Explorer page, search covers your projects,
  projects shared with you, folders, teams, settings and help instead, see [Finding Projects and
  Folders](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-projects/).
</Tip>
