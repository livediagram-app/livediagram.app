When you open the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/) from inside a project, its scope grows to include that project's [tabs](/help/tabs/using-tabs/), its boards and its cards. That turns search into a quick way around the project you're in, not just a way to open a different one. It works on view-only links too, since it only moves you around.

## Tabs

The **Tabs** group matches the project's tabs by name, whether each holds a Board, a Sheet, a Dashboard or a Form, and marks the one you are on with **current** so you can tell a jump from a no-op. Picking a tab switches to it. On a project with many tabs (or [tab folders](/help/tabs/tab-folders/)) this is quicker than scanning the tab bar.

With an empty box the panel lists every tab in the project, so you can open it and arrow straight down to one. The Tabs group shows up to 8 matches; type more of a name to narrow a long list.

## Boards

The **Boards** group matches the titles of the project's boards, each marked with the tab it is on, so a board titled "Release Train" on the Sprint tab is one search away. Picking one switches to its tab. A board titled the same as its tab shows only under Tabs.

## Cards

The **Cards** group finds the project's cards that aren't archived or in the Trash. Type a card's number (`12` or `#12`) to go straight to it, or words from its title or description. The card whose number you typed comes first, then cards whose title matches, then those that match only in their description, the most recently changed first. Each shows its number, its title and the tab of the board it sits on.

Picking a card opens it in the card panel, on that tab: search switches to the tab first if you are elsewhere. A card that no board shows (its status has no column anywhere) opens where you are. The group shows up to 8 cards; type more of a title, or the number, to reach the one you want.

To browse every card, or to narrow them by type or field, use **Find** in the bottom-right corner (shown on a tab with a board on it). See [Items](/help/plan/items/) for how cards are stored and found, and [Cards](/help/plan/cards/) for working with them. To narrow what one board shows, use **Filter cards** in the board's header. See [Boards](/help/plan/boards/).

## What it does not search

- **A board's column names, a sheet's cells, a dashboard's contents and a form's questions:** use a sheet's own find (`⌘F`) for its cells. See [Sheets](/help/plan/sheets/).
- **Archived cards and cards in the Trash:** see [Items](/help/plan/items/).
- **Comments** and the names of people in the project.
- **Other projects' contents:** from anywhere, other projects are found by name only.

<Tip>
  This scope only appears inside a project. From the Explorer page, search covers your projects,
  projects shared with you, folders, teams, settings and help instead, see [Finding Projects and
  Folders](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/search-projects/).
</Tip>
