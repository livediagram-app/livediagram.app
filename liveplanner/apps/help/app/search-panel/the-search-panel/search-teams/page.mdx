When you are signed in and belong to one or more [teams](/help/collaboration/teams/), the [search panel](/help/search-panel/the-search-panel/) lists them in a **Teams** group, matched by name. Picking one opens that team's library in the [Explorer](/help/explorer/team-spaces/).

Your teams come from your sign-in, so a team you joined a moment ago on liveworkspace.app appears once your sign-in next refreshes.

To find a folder or an item inside a team's library, use the search field at the top of the [Explorer page](/help/explorer/explorer-page/#searching), which searches every library you can reach.

## Signed-in only

Teams require an account, so this group only appears when you are signed in with teams to search. Guests, and signed-in users with no teams, simply will not see it. The rest of search works the same either way.
